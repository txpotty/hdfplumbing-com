    Water Pressure Restoration in The Villages

    Low pressure at every fixture points to the service line or the regulator, not the faucets. We put a gauge on a hose bib first.     Slab Leak Water Line Repair in The Villages

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. Newer homes across Florida mostly use PEX or CPVC with PVC drains, though copper pinhole leaks are still a common repair in older homes. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water and a meter that moves with everything off. Acoustic gear and a thermal camera tell us where it is. From there you have three choices: break the slab at the spot, reroute the line through the walls or attic, or skip the old under-slab pipe and repipe overhead with PEX. You get a price on every option.

    Main Water Line Repair in The Villages

    Your main line is the pipe from the meter to the house, usually 3/4 inch or 1 inch, buried anywhere from a foot to several feet down depending on frost depth. A leak here shows up as a soggy strip of lawn and a meter dial that never rests. We start by closing the house shutoff and watching the dial. If it still moves, the leak is buried. Then a ground microphone narrows the spot, and we dig one hole instead of a trench across your yard.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in The Villages

    A trench through a paver driveway or a mature garden can cost more to restore than the pipe itself. Trenchless avoids most of that. You also get the manufacturer warranty on the new pipe.

    PEX and Copper Water Line Repair in The Villages
