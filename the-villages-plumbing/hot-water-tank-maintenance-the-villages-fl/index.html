    Standard Hot Water Tank Flushing in The Villages

    Sediment buildup is a frequent issue in hot water tanks, especially where water hardness runs high. Our standard flushing service removes these mineral deposits, which can reduce efficiency and lead to premature tank failure. We drain the tank completely, flush out all sediment and refill it, ensuring clear water flow and improved heating performance. This routine service is key for extending the life of your unit.

    Sediment Flush and Descaling in The Villages

    Hard water leaves calcium carbonate behind every time the burner heats the tank. Over a year that layer can harden into chunks that insulate the water from the flame and cause loud popping. We connect a hose to the drain valve, flush with full line pressure and agitate the bottom until the water runs clear. When scale is severe we use a descaling solution approved for potable systems. A clean tank heats faster and puts less stress on the bottom seam.
