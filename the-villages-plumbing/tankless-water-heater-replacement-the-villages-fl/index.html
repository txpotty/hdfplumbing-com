    Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Replacement in The Villages

    The cost of tankless water heater replacement in The Villages, FL usually ranges from $3,000 to $6,000, depending on the unit's capacity, brand, complexity of installation, such as converting from a tank model, and any needed electrical or gas line upgrades.     Common signs that your tankless water heater needs replacement include repeated error codes, inconsistent water temperature, reduced hot water flow or a complete lack of hot water. If your unit is over 15 to 20 years old and repairs are becoming common and costly, replacement is often the more economical choice.

    Most manufacturers offer 10 to 15 years on the heat exchanger and about 5 years on parts for residential use. We add our own labor warranty and register the unit on your behalf.

    New tankless water heaters typically come with a manufacturer's warranty, which usually covers the heat exchanger for 10 to 15 years and parts for 3 to 5 years. HDF Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work for your peace of mind. We will discuss warranty details for your chosen unit during your consultation.

    To protect the life and efficiency of your new tankless water heater, regular maintenance is crucial, especially in areas with hard water. This usually involves annual flushing to remove mineral buildup, checking the air filter, and inspecting gas and water connections. HDF Plumbing offers maintenance plans to help keep your unit in top condition.

    A standard tankless water heater replacement typically takes between 4 and 8 hours.
