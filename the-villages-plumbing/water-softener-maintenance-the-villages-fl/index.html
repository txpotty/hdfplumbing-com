    Control Valve and Timer Diagnostics in The Villages, FL

    The control valve runs every stage of regeneration, from backwash through brine draw to the final refill. We step it through each cycle by hand and watch for a motor that stalls, a drive gear that skips, a piston that drags or a drain that never shuts off. Worn pistons turn up a lot.

    Resin Cleaning and Regeneration Tuning in The Villages, FL

    Iron is the most common resin fouler we see. It coats the beads orange and blocks the exchange sites. We feed a resin cleaner through the brine well, run a regeneration and retest, and if the capacity comes back we shorten the time between regenerations so the iron does not build back up.

    Brine Tank Cleanout and Inspection in The Villages, FL

    Brine tanks collect a gray sludge of salt fines and insoluble grit, and it builds up faster with cheap rock salt. The tank gets emptied, scrubbed and rinsed. Then we look for cracks near the overflow elbow and the brine well and refill with fresh salt.

    Salt-Free Conditioner Service in The Villages

    Salt-free conditioners, usually template assisted crystallization units, do not remove hardness. A hardness test still reads high afterward.
