    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Replacement in The Villages

    A standard sump pump replacement typically costs $500 to $1,200 installed. Battery backup systems usually add $800 to $1,800. Sewage ejector pumps usually cost $1,200 to $2,500.

    We install a variety of proven sump pump brands known for their reliability and performance, including Zoeller, Wayne, Liberty Pumps and Little Giant. Our plumbers can help you pick the best brand and model for your home's requirements and budget.

    Most sump pump replacements take one to two hours. Battery backups or new discharge lines take a bit longer.

    It is recommended to test your sump pump at least every three months, and especially before and after rainy seasons. To test, simply pour a bucket of water into the sump pit. The pump should activate, drain the water and then shut off automatically. If it does not, it is time for a professional's inspection.

    Simple replacements usually do not require permits. New discharge lines or electrical work may, depending on your city.

    A primary sump pump is your main defense against basement flooding, powered by your home's electricity. A backup sump pump, usually battery-powered or water-powered, is a secondary system that kicks on if the primary pump fails, loses power or cannot keep up with heavy water inflow. Many The Villages homes benefit from a backup system due to storm risks.

    Most standard sump pump replacements can be completed within 2 to 4 hours. More complex installations, such as those requiring new pit excavation or major discharge line work, might take longer, up to a full day.

    Most sump pumps last 7 to 10 years. Pumps that sit idle for long periods can fail earlier due to stuck switches.
