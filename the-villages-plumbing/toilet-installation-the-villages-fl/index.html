    Floor-Mount and Wall-Hung Toilet Installs in The Villages, FL

    Wall-Mounted Toilet Installation in The Villages

    Wall-mounted toilets hang off a steel carrier bolted to the floor inside the wall. The tank sits behind an access plate. Because the carrier carries all the weight, we anchor its feet to the slab or framing first and check it for plumb before the drywall or tile goes up. Plan on a remodel, not a swap.

    ADA-Compliant Toilet Installation in The Villages

    ADA toilets put the seat 17 to 19 inches off the floor, and the centerline sits 16 to 18 inches from the side wall. That spacing leaves room for a grab bar. Before anything is ordered we measure the drain, because shifting it to meet the ADA centerline means cutting into the floor. Elongated bowls are standard here.

    Pressure-Assisted Toilet Installation in The Villages

    Pressure-assisted toilets have a sealed vessel inside the tank that uses line pressure to push the flush. They need at least 20 psi to work right. We check static pressure at a hose bib before recommending one, and we warn customers about the noise, since the flush is loud enough to hear across the house. They rarely clog.

    Commercial Flushometer Toilets in The Villages

    Sloan and Zurn flushometer valves are the standard on commercial toilets for a reason. There is no tank to refill. Manual and sensor valves go on both floor-mount and wall-hung bowls, with the control stop adjusted so the flush is full but does not splash. Sensor valves cut down on touch points.

    Urgent Toilet Replacement in The Villages

    A cracked tank or a bowl leaking at the base cannot wait for next week, especially in a house with one bathroom. Standard two-piece toilets ride on our trucks. Often the broken unit comes out and a 12 inch rough-in replacement goes in during the same trip, round or elongated. Close the stop until then.

    Low-Flow WaterSense Toilet Installation in The Villages, FL

    WaterSense toilets are capped at 1.28 gallons per flush, and some dual-flush models drop to around 0.8 for liquid waste. That adds up fast. Replacing a single 3.5 gallon toilet in a household of four can save several thousand gallons a year, and some water utilities have offered rebates on high-efficiency models, so it is worth asking yours. Pick a strong MaP rating.

    Rough-In Toilet Installation in The Villages, FL

    Rough-in is the plumbing that goes in before walls and floors close up. On a toilet, that is a vented 3 inch drain and a cold water stub. The drain goes 12 inches out from the finished wall line, which means we add the drywall thickness to the framing measurement. Then the inspector sees it open.

    Toilet Flange and Wax Ring Replacement in The Villages, FL

    The closet flange is the ring that ties the toilet to the drain, and it is the weak point on most old installs. On cast iron, the bolt slots rust out first. PVC and ABS flanges crack when someone overtightens the bolts, and a repair ring screwed over the top is often all it takes to restore a solid anchor. Seal goes on last.
