    Preventive Care and Longevity in The Villages, FL

    Run cold water before, during and after grinding, and let it go about 15 seconds after the motor stops so the food clears the trap.     Jam Clearing and Motor Repair in The Villages

Long stalls can overheat the motor, so we run it under load before we finish.
