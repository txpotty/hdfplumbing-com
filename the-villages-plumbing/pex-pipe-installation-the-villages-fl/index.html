    What Goes Into a PEX Job in The Villages, FL

    Remodel and Addition PEX Rough-In in The Villages

    Rough-in heights matter more than people think. A shower valve set two inches too low or a vanity stub that misses the cabinet back means opening the wall again after the tile is up, which is expensive and slow. We check heights with your contractor before drilling. Then we pressure test with the walls open.

    Full Home PEX Repiping in The Villages

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside out. Give it four or five decades and the inside is so packed with rust that a half-inch pipe barely flows, and the first draw in the morning runs brown. A full PEX repipe replaces all of it. Pressure comes back at the fixtures and the rust stops.

    PEX Repipe Cost Comparison in The Villages, FL

    Price on a repipe depends on a short list: bathroom count, square footage, how many stories, and how easy the attic and walls are to work in. PEX usually beats copper on price because the pipe costs less and a crew finishes in two to four days, not a full week. Patching is sometimes quoted on its own line. We price PEX-A, PEX-B and copper side by side when you ask.

    Uponor PEX System Installation in The Villages

    Uponor's ProPEX system is PEX-A joined with an expansion tool and a cold-expansion ring. The tech expands the pipe and ring together, pushes the fitting in, and the plastic shrinks back around the barb over the next few seconds, tighter than when it started. There is no crimp gauge to check. The fittings have a wide bore, so flow loss through each joint stays low.

    PEX for Water Line Replacement in The Villages

    Your service line runs from the meter or well to the house, often a foot and a half to three feet underground. Because PEX comes on a long coil, the whole run from the meter to the foundation can go in with zero buried fittings. A high water table across much of Florida affects drainage, septic design, and how easily crews can dig for underground repairs. It gets bedded in sand, not rocky backfill. Sharp rock against plastic pipe is how a new line fails early.

    Whole-Home PEX Repiping in The Villages

    Low pressure at every faucet, across the whole house, usually points at the pipe and not the fixtures. In older homes it is rust-choked galvanized or polybutylene with failing fittings, and swapping a faucet cartridge will not help. Repiping does fix it. Every line from the main valve to each angle stop gets replaced, and the system is tested before anything gets closed up.

    Leak Repair with PEX in The Villages, FL

    A single bad section does not always mean a repipe. For one split or pitted stretch of copper, we cut it out, transition to PEX with a listed fitting, and usually restore water within a couple of hours. That works for one leak. Once you are on the third patch, we will tell you straight whether the rest of the copper is heading the same way.

    Copper to PEX Conversions in The Villages

    Pinhole leaks in copper rarely show up alone. Aggressive water, fast flow and soil contact under slabs can pit copper from the inside, and the first few pinholes are typically followed by more. Many wells in Florida produce water with a sulfur smell or high iron, which often calls for treatment to protect fixtures and appliances. Converting to PEX stops the cycle. Where new PEX meets existing copper stubs or valves, we use listed transition fittings.
