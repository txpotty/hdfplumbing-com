    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Pembroke Pines

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. They change the minerals into crystals that do not stick to pipe walls and heating elements, so scale drops off without adding sodium or using a drain line. Maintenance is light. They suit moderate hardness.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Pembroke Pines

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. Our RO installs go under the sink with their own faucet, and we can tie in the fridge or ice maker too. It complements a softener. We can handle filter changes.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Pembroke Pines

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. Every whole-house install gets a bypass valve, and we add a pressure reducing valve if incoming pressure is too high. Every tap gets soft water. We can skip the hose bibs.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Pembroke Pines, FL

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. A carbon filter placed ahead of the softener removes those chemicals and protects the resin, since chlorine slowly breaks resin beads down over the years. Taste improves fast. Setups can be two tanks or one dual-stage tank.
