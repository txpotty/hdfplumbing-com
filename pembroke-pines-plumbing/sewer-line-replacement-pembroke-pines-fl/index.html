    Many older homes in Pembroke Pines may have sewer lines made from outdated materials like clay or cast iron, which are prone to cracking, corrosion, and root invasion.     Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Pembroke Pines

    Pipe bursting replaces the old sewer line by pulling a new pipe through the path of the old one. A bursting head breaks the old pipe outward while fused HDPE pipe follows behind. This typically needs only an entry pit and an exit pit instead of a long trench. It works well under driveways, mature trees and landscaping. The new pipe is seamless, which leaves no joints for roots to enter.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Pembroke Pines, FL

    Traditional excavation is sometimes the best option, especially when the old line has a severe belly or the slope must be corrected. We dig along the line, remove the old pipe and set new PVC on compacted bedding at the proper grade. Rocky or hard-packed soil can slow digging, and we plan for that in the estimate. Each joint is glued and checked before backfill. The inspector sees the open trench before we cover it.
