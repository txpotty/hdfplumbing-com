    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Pembroke Pines

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. The resin cures in place. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Pembroke Pines yard.

    Perma-Liner supplies the lining systems and Picote makes the cutters. Both matter. Before lining, Picote heads mill out roots and scale, and after curing they reopen the lateral connections so each drain flows. A clean pipe holds a liner best.
