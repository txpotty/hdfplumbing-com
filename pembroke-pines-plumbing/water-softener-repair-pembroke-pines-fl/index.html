    A salt bridge, a crust of salt that forms in the brine tank, can prevent your water softener from regenerating properly. This leads to hard water throughout your home. We also repair leaks or damage to the brine tank itself, frequent in older systems.

    Water Softener Leak Repair in Pembroke Pines, FL

    Leaks in a water softener can range from minor drips around fittings to significant puddles from a cracked tank or faulty valve. These leaks can cause water damage and waste water.     Control Valve Troubleshooting & Repair in Pembroke Pines, FL
