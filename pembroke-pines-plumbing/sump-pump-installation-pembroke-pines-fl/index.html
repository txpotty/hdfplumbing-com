    Sump Pump Replacement in Pembroke Pines, FL

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms.     Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Pembroke Pines, FL

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. A smart charger keeps the battery topped off and sounds an alarm if the battery weakens, the primary fails or the water rises past the backup float. Batteries need replacing every few years. They matter most below finished rooms.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Pembroke Pines, FL

    A dead pump during a storm means the water is already climbing. Our trucks carry 1/3 and 1/2 HP submersibles, check valves and fittings, so in most homes we can pull the old pump and set a new one in one visit. Most swaps take about two hours. We test it with a full pit before leaving.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Pembroke Pines

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. They fit pits where two separate pumps would crowd each other. That saves some labor. We set them in homes that want two layers without a bigger pit.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Pembroke Pines

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. Sizing comes from two measurements we take on site, the pit diameter and the vertical lift to the discharge. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. We usually pair that with a 1-1/2 inch discharge line.
