    Whole-Home PEX Replacement in Port St. Lucie, FL

    PEX-A is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, scale resistant and easy to route. We use Uponor PEX-A with expansion fittings that do not restrict flow.     Polybutylene Identification and Inspection in Port St. Lucie

    Polybutylene is usually gray, though it can be white, blue or black, and is often stamped PB2110. You may see it at the water heater, under sinks or near the main shutoff. We inspect visible lines, check the stamps and trace runs through the attic. You get a clear answer and a written replacement estimate.

    Fitting Failure and Emergency Repair in Port St. Lucie

    Acetal plastic and copper crimp ring fittings are often the first part of a poly system to fail. A burst fitting in an attic or wall can cause major damage in minutes. We respond to emergency leaks, make temporary repairs and then schedule a full replacement. Stopping the problem for good is the only permanent fix.

    Some homeowners choose Type L copper for its long history and rigid feel. We solder with lead-free solder or use pressed fittings for faster installation. Copper costs more than PEX and takes a little longer to install. We help you weigh the options for your budget and water conditions.
