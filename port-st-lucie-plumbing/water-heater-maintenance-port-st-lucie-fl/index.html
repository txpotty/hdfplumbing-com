    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Port St. Lucie

    Tankless units are particularly sensitive to hard water scale, which clogs the heat exchanger and triggers error codes. We isolate the unit with its service valves and circulate a descaling solution with a pump for the recommended time. We also clean the inlet water filter and check the unit's error history. Most manufacturers recommend descaling at least once a year in hard water areas. Regular service keeps flow rates and temperatures steady.

    Maintenance Cost Versus Replacement in Port St. Lucie, FL

    A water heater tune-up costs a fraction of a new unit, which can run well over a thousand dollars installed. Regular flushing and anode replacement can help a tank reach the high end of its rated lifespan. Tankless units in particular can last many years with proper descaling. If your heater is near the end of its life or leaking from the tank, we will tell you plainly. We help you weigh repair against replacement with actual numbers.

    Electric Water Heater Element Service in Port St. Lucie, FL

    Electric water heaters use upper and lower heating elements controlled by thermostats. Sediment can coat the lower element and cause it to fail. We test both elements and thermostats with the power off and replace parts as needed. A failed element is often the reason for lukewarm water or running out of hot water quickly. Proper testing avoids replacing parts that are still good.

    T&P Valve and Safety Testing in Port St. Lucie, FL

    The temperature and pressure relief valve keeps your water heater from building dangerous pressure. We test it by lifting the lever and checking that it opens and reseats. A valve that leaks or will not open gets replaced. We also check that the discharge pipe runs to an approved location. Expansion tanks are checked for proper air charge where installed.

    Thermostat and Heating Element Checks in Port St. Lucie

    For electric water heaters, we test the thermostat and heating elements to make sure they work correctly and hold an accurate temperature. Faulty elements or thermostats can lead to too little hot water or excessive energy use. For gas water heaters, we inspect the burner assembly and pilot light. Humidity causes condensation on cold lines and clogged AC condensate drains, a common source of hidden water damage. Properly working components are vital for both efficiency and safety.

    Gas Water Heater Burner and Venting Check in Port St. Lucie

    Gas water heaters need a clean burner and a proper vent to run safely and efficiently. We inspect the burner flame, clean the combustion chamber and check the thermocouple or flame sensor. The vent is checked for proper draft and secure connections. Blocked or loose vents can let carbon monoxide into your home. We also check the gas line and shutoff valve for any leaks.
