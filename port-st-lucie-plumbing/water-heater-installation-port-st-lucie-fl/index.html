    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Port St. Lucie

    Many plumbing codes require the installation of an expansion tank with a new water heater, especially in closed-loop systems. Plumbing work in Florida follows the Florida Building Code, whose plumbing volume is based on the International Plumbing Code. An expansion tank protects your water heater and plumbing system from excessive pressure buildup, which can be caused by the heating process. Our installation service includes correctly sizing and installing the expansion tank to ensure proper system operation and prevent premature wear on your new water heater and other plumbing fixtures in Port St. Lucie. This small component offers significant long-term protection.

    Expansion Tank Installation in Port St. Lucie, FL

    Many homes have a pressure reducing valve or check valve that creates a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure can rise and stress pipes and relief valves. An expansion tank absorbs that extra pressure. We install and pre-charge the tank to match your water pressure. This protects your new water heater and plumbing.
