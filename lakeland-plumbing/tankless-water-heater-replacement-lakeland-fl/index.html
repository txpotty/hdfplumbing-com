    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Lakeland, FL

    Many homes have tankless heaters mounted on an exterior wall with no venting at all, since the unit exhausts directly outside. These units face sun, rain and temperature swings, which can wear out plastic covers and wiring over time. When a rare freeze is forecast, covering outdoor valves and letting a faucet drip is usually enough to prevent damage. When we replace them, we check the pipe cover, freeze protection settings and the electrical connection. We can add a protective pipe cover kit to shield the water and gas lines. Exterior models are often the easiest swap when the gas line is already sized correctly.

    Commercial Tankless Replacement in Lakeland

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Lakeland, FL

    Converting from a 40 or 50 gallon tank to tankless frees up floor space and ends standby heat loss in a garage or closet. The catch is that tankless units often need a larger gas line, often three quarter inch or bigger, plus new venting. We evaluate your meter size and pipe run before quoting so there are no surprises mid-job. We also cap or reroute the old flue and seal the roof penetration if it is no longer used. Plan for a conversion to take a full day.
