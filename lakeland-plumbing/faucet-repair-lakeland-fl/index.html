    Cartridge Faucet Repair in Lakeland, FL

    Many modern single-handle faucets, often found in newer homes, use a cartridge system. When these fail, you might experience inconsistent water temperature, reduced flow, or difficulty turning the faucet on or off. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing cartridge issues and replacing them with the correct manufacturer-specific part, so your faucet works like new. We work with all major brands to provide precise repairs.

    Faucet Handle Repair & Replacement in Lakeland, FL

    A loose, broken, or stripped faucet handle makes it hard to control water flow and temperature. Over time, daily use can wear down the internal mechanisms or cause the handle itself to break. We offer expert repair for various handle types, from single-handle mixers to separate hot and cold handles. If a repair isn't feasible, we can recommend and install a suitable replacement handle that matches your existing fixture and style, adding to its lifespan.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Lakeland

    Ceramic disc faucets are known for their durability and are a popular choice in many contemporary Lakeland bathrooms. While generally reliable, they can develop leaks if the ceramic discs become scratched or misaligned, or if the inlet and outlet seals wear out. Our plumbers have the expertise to carefully disassemble these precise mechanisms, clean or replace the ceramic discs, and reseal the faucet to stop leaks and restore full performance.

    Specialty Faucet Repair in Lakeland, FL

    Beyond standard kitchen and bathroom fixtures, we handle repairs for specialty faucets such as pot fillers, utility sink faucets, and outdoor spigots found on many homes.     Ball Faucet Repair in Lakeland, FL

    Ball faucets, recognizable by their single lever that moves over a rounded cap, are common in many kitchens. They use a rotating ball joint to control water flow and temperature. If your ball faucet is leaking from the spout or base, or if the handle feels loose, it's likely time for a repair. We can replace the spring-loaded rubber seats and O-rings, which are typically the culprits, restoring smooth operation and preventing drips.

    Leaking Faucet Handle and Base Repair in Lakeland, FL

    Water seeping around the handle or pooling at the base points to failed O-rings or a loose mounting nut. If left alone, the leak can soak into the cabinet below and damage the sink base. We replace O-rings, tighten mounting hardware and reseal the base. We also check the deck for water damage.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Lakeland

    A dripping spout typically means a worn cartridge, washer or valve seat. Hard water speeds up this wear by leaving scale that scratches seals and keeps them from closing tight. We disassemble the faucet, replace the worn parts and clean the valve body. Fixing a drip right away keeps a small leak from building up on your water bill.

    Dripping Faucet Solutions in Lakeland
