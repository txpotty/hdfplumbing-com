    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Maintenance in Lakeland

    A standard sump pump inspection and cleaning typically runs $125 to $250. Replacing a float switch or check valve usually adds $75 to $250 depending on parts.

    We recommend having your sump pump professionally maintained at least once a year. If your home is in an area prone to heavy rainfall or has a high water table, twice-yearly maintenance might be more beneficial. Regular checks ensure it's ready for peak seasons.

    Routine maintenance typically does not require a permit. However, if a significant repair involves altering the discharge line or replacing the entire unit, especially if electrical work is involved, a local permit might be necessary. We handle all permit requirements for you.

    Watch for a pump that runs constantly, hums without moving water, cycles very often or makes grinding noises. Rust, a stuck float or a musty smell around the pit are also warning signs.

    A standard sump pump maintenance appointment usually takes about 30 to 60 minutes. This time allows for a complete inspection, cleaning and testing of all components. If minor repairs are needed, the visit might run slightly longer.

    Most visits take 45 minutes to an hour. Replacing a check valve or float on the same visit adds a little time.

    Watch out for unusual noises, such as grinding or rattling, that point to debris or a failing motor. If the pump runs constantly, fails to turn on or gives off a foul odor from the pit, it's definitely time for a professional check. Visible rust or corrosion are also red flags.
