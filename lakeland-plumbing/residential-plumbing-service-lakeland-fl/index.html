    Every one to two years works for most homes in Lakeland, FL. The inspection usually runs about an hour, and we check pressure, look for slow leaks at shutoffs and supply lines, test the water heater relief valve and look for corrosion on exposed pipe. Small finds cost far less to fix.

    Keep grease out of the kitchen drain. Use strainers in tubs and showers, flush only toilet paper, and find your main water shutoff now, before an emergency, so you are not hunting for it with water on the floor. Older homes benefit from a yearly check.

    A slab leak is a break in a water line running under a concrete foundation. Warm spots on the floor, a sudden jump in the water bill or the sound of water running with every fixture off are the usual signs. Do not wait on it.
