    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Lakeland

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. Because everything comes assembled on a single riser, they fit into an 18-inch pit that would be too tight for two separate pumps and two check valves. That saves some labor. We set them in homes that want two layers without a bigger pit.

    Sump Pump Replacement in Lakeland, FL

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. When we replace a pump we also check the basin for silt, look at the check valve flapper and run water through the discharge line to find any clogs. If the old one could not keep up, we size up. We fill the pit and watch it run.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Lakeland

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. Because the motor is cooled by the surrounding water, they run quieter and can push more gallons per minute up a tall lift than most pedestal pumps. Lids close flat over them. We seal the cord grommet and the lid on every install.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Lakeland, FL

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Lakeland, FL

    Two pumps on one riser cover both kinds of failure, a dead motor and a dead outlet.     Sump Pump Installation Cost in Lakeland, FL

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Lakeland, FL

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. You can reach the motor and the switch without fishing anything out of the pit, which helps in a basement where the pit is shallow or tight. Expect more motor noise than a submersible. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.
