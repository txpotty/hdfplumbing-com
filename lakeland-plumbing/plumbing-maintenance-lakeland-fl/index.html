    Gas Line and Pipe Material Checks in Lakeland, FL

    Gas leaks are the one item we never skip. Visible gas piping, connectors and shutoffs get checked, and old flex connectors get flagged. Pipe material matters too. Polybutylene or old galvanized supply lines go on the report with repipe options.

    Gas Connector and Shutoff Checks in Lakeland

    Gas feeds water heaters, furnaces, ranges and dryers. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections with leak detector solution, make sure each appliance shutoff valve turns and replace any old or kinked flexible connectors. Repairs follow local code. If you smell gas, get outside before calling the gas company.

    Water Heater Maintenance in Lakeland

    Sediment is the tank's slow killer. Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving stubborn scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. We drain and flush the tank, pull and check the anode rod, test the temperature and pressure relief valve, and look over the gas or electric connections for corrosion and wear. Flushing takes about an hour. A clean tank heats faster and lasts years longer.

    Softener Cleaning and Testing in Lakeland

    Hard water scale builds in pipes and appliances fast. We check the brine level, clean the injector screen, inspect the resin bed for fouling and confirm the regeneration cycle starts and finishes, then test the treated water hardness at a faucet. Resin cleaner helps. Older resin may need replacing.

    Sewer and Drain Camera Inspection in Lakeland, FL

    A camera shows what the sewer looks like inside. Roots, sags, cracks and grease show up on camera, especially in old clay and cast iron. You get a copy of the video. We lay out options if something needs work.

    Sump Pump Testing in Lakeland, FL

    Basements flood when the pump fails during a storm. Hurricanes and tropical storms bring flooding, power loss, and sewer backups to Florida, often overwhelming drainage systems for days. We fill the pit to test the float, check the discharge line and confirm the check valve holds. Backup pumps get tested too.

    Repipe Assessments in Lakeland, FL

    Polybutylene and old galvanized pipe leak more. During maintenance we identify your supply pipe material, look for pinhole leaks, rust stains and pressure drops, and tell you honestly whether repiping is something to plan for now or years from now. PEX and copper are the options. Work can be phased.
