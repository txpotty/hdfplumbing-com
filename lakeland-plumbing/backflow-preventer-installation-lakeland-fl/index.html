    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Lakeland

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules allow. We size them to avoid too much pressure loss on your system.

    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Lakeland

    The double check valve assembly, or DCVA, is a widely used backflow preventer for low-hazard applications where the consequences of backflow are not severe. In Lakeland, DCVAs are frequently installed on fire sprinkler systems or irrigation lines. Our certified plumbers precisely install these devices, making sure both check valves operate correctly and are properly sealed to prevent backpressure or back-siphonage into the main water supply.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Lakeland

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are often required for high-hazard applications in Lakeland. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. HDF Plumbing focuses on precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties. We work with top brands like Watts and Febco.
