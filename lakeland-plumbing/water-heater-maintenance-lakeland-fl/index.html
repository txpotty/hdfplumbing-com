    Tankless Water Heater Service in Lakeland, FL

    Tankless units need descaling. Mineral scale coats the inside of the heat exchanger, restricting flow and making the unit work harder, and eventually it starts throwing error codes or cutting out in the middle of a shower. A pump circulates descaler through it. We also check the igniter, the fan and the exhaust vent for safe operation.

    Yearly Service Plans in Lakeland, FL

    Forgetting is the main reason tanks go unflushed. A yearly plan puts the visit on the calendar, sends a reminder a week ahead, and gives you priority scheduling and repair discounts if the heater ever acts up between visits. We track it for you. It pays off over time.

    Electric Element Service in Lakeland, FL

    Two elements, two thermostats. A burned-out lower element is the usual cause of running short on hot water. We test with power off. We only replace what failed.

    Attic, Closet and Garage Units in Lakeland

    Location changes the risks. Attic units need a working drain pan and drain line so a leak does not come through the ceiling, and garage gas units often need to be raised off the floor or protected from vehicles under local code. We check the pan and drain. We check venting and access.

    Relief Valve and Safety Testing in Lakeland, FL

    The relief valve is the tank's last line of defense. We lift the lever, confirm water discharges and the valve reseats without dripping, and check that the discharge pipe runs full size to an approved spot within a few inches of the floor or outside. We swap bad ones. We check the expansion tank too.

    Water Heater Leak Checks in Lakeland, FL

    Small drips become floods. We inspect the supply connections, the relief valve outlet, the drain valve and the tank seams for moisture or rust trails, and we check the drain pan and the floor around the unit for signs of a slow leak. Rust stains are a warning. Early repairs protect floors and drywall.
