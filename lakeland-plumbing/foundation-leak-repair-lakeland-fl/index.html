    Slab Leak Detection and Repair in Lakeland

    Slab leaks are common in homes built on concrete foundations, often caused by shifting soil or corroded pipes underneath. Our technicians use low-impact methods like electronic leak detection and hydrostatic pressure testing to find these elusive leaks. Once located, we can perform targeted repairs, either by slab tunneling or direct access, minimizing disruption to your home. We work with various pipe materials, including copper, PEX, and PVC, ensuring a durable repair for your Lakeland property.

    Tunneling for Foundation Leaks in Lakeland, FL

    In some cases, the most effective way to repair a foundation leak is by tunneling beneath the slab. This method lets our plumbers reach the damaged pipe directly without breaking through your home's interior flooring. HDF Plumbing is experienced in safe and efficient tunneling, minimizing disruption to your Lakeland, FL home and delivering a complete repair of the damaged pipe section.

    Direct Foundation Leak Repair in Lakeland, FL

    When the leak is in an accessible spot and the rest of the pipe is in good condition, direct repair is often the most affordable choice. We cut a small opening in the floor, dig down to the pipe and replace the damaged section. The soil is compacted and the concrete is patched to match. Flooring can then be restored by your flooring contractor.

    Slab Leak Detection & Repair in Lakeland

    Slab leaks are known for being difficult to detect, often showing quiet signs like warm spots on the floor or a sudden jump in your water bill. HDF Plumbing uses acoustic and infrared technology to accurately locate these hidden leaks under your concrete foundation. Once found, we offer tailored repair solutions, whether it's a spot repair, re-routing, or pipe bursting, to restore your plumbing system in Lakeland without extensive damage to your property.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Repair in Lakeland

    Many newer homes are built on post-tension slabs with steel cables under high tension. Cutting into these slabs takes care and scanning to avoid hitting a cable. We use ground-penetrating radar or other scanning methods before opening the floor. When risk is too high, rerouting the plumbing is usually the safer option.

    Basement Wall Sealing and Waterproofing in Lakeland, FL

    For homes with basements in Lakeland, FL, wall leaks can be a real concern. We offer comprehensive basement waterproofing services, including interior and exterior sealing. This may involve applying waterproof coatings, sealing cracks with polyurethane or epoxy, or setting up interior drainage systems. These measures prevent water from penetrating porous concrete and masonry, keeping your basement dry and healthy.

    Advanced Leak Detection Technology in Lakeland, FL

    The ability to precisely locate a foundation leak is crucial for an effective repair. HDF Plumbing invests in modern leak detection technology, including high-frequency listening devices and specialized camera inspections, to pinpoint leaks with precision.     Water Line Reroute in Lakeland

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the foundation and runs a new line through the attic or walls. This skips breaking concrete and removes the risk of future leaks under the slab. We typically use PEX for reroutes because it is flexible and resists corrosion. Drywall openings are kept small and neat for easy patching.
