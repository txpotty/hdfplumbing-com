    What Sewer Repair Covers in Lakeland, FL

    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Lakeland

    Roots in a sewer line are not the tree's fault. The pipe was leaking first. Warm, wet, nutrient-rich air escaping from a loose joint draws roots toward the line, and once they get in they keep growing until the joint splits or the pipe cracks open. We cut them out, then talk about the long-term fix. Lining or a joint replacement keeps them from coming back.

    Pipe Burst & Collapse Repair in Lakeland, FL

    A collapsed sewer pipe stops all flow at the point of failure. The whole house loses its drains. Collapses usually come from corroded cast iron, crushed clay under a driveway or tar-paper pipe that has finally flattened, and the camera tells us exactly where the pipe gave way and how deep it sits. We dig at the mark and replace the broken length with PVC. Lining cannot fix a full collapse.

    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in Lakeland

    A self-leveling camera goes in at the cleanout or through a pulled toilet. We record the whole run. Cracks, root intrusion, offset joints and standing water all show up on the screen, and the sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot and depth on the ground above, which keeps the dig to one small pit. Older homes in Lakeland often hide two or three problems in one line, and the video catches all of them.

    Emergency Sewer Repair in Lakeland

    Raw sewage on a basement floor is not a next-week problem. We answer at night and on weekends. The first goal on an emergency call is to get the line flowing, usually with a cable or jetter, and then camera it to find out whether you have a clog or a structural failure that will back up again tomorrow. Sometimes the permanent fix waits for daylight. Until then, we tell you which fixtures are safe to use.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Lakeland

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. A cable only punches a hole. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and it is the standard prep step before any pipe lining job. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We check the pipe on video before we jet it.

    Commercial Sewer Repair in Lakeland

We repair larger diameter lines, add two-way cleanouts so future maintenance is easy, and give managers recorded footage for owners, insurers and their own files. We can work outside business hours.
