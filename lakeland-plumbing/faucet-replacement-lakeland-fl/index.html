    Corroded Faucet Replacement in Lakeland

    Green crust at the base means the faucet body is corroding. A pitted body will not let a new cartridge seal, so the drip comes back a few weeks after each repair. At that point, replace it. We pull it, even with seized nuts, and set a new faucet with a ceramic disc cartridge.

    Tub and Shower Trim Replacement in Lakeland, FL

    Tub and shower trim can be replaced without opening the wall.     Bathroom Faucet Replacement in Lakeland, FL

    Old lav faucets rarely come off clean. Lav faucet nuts hide behind the bowl in a tight gap, and after a couple of decades they tend to fuse to the threaded shank. We use a basin wrench, or cut them. We replace the pop-up drain at the same time, since the old drain gasket is almost always cracked by then.

    Water-Saving Faucet Upgrades in Lakeland

    Federal rules cap new kitchen and lav faucets at 2.2 gallons per minute. WaterSense bathroom faucets drop to 1.5 gallons per minute, and the aerated stream feels about the same in your hands. The savings show on the bill. We can swap a bath faucet for a WaterSense model in the same visit.

    Utility Sink Faucet Replacement in Lakeland

    Utility faucets in garages and laundry rooms take hard use. People hang buckets on the spout and yank hoses off the threads, which loosens the mounting and cracks the supply connections over time. Brass bodies hold up best here. We mount it tight to the wall or tub deck.

    Urgent Faucet Replacement in Lakeland, FL

    Water that will not shut off at the faucet is an urgent call. Turn the shutoff under the sink clockwise, and go to the main if that valve will not budge or starts dripping. Urgent calls get priority. A plumber stops the flow, removes the broken faucet and installs a new one from the truck.

    Touchless Faucet Upgrade in Lakeland

    Swapping a standard faucet for a touchless one takes a little more planning. We adjust the sensor range at the end.
