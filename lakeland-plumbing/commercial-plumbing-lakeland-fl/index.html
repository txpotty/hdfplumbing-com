    Commercial Leak Detection in Lakeland, FL

    Water bills that jump 30 percent with no change in business usually mean a hidden leak or a stuck fixture. Start with the meter. Zone-by-zone shutoffs and meter readings narrow it down, then acoustic sensors, thermal imaging and tracer gas pinpoint the spot. Running toilets count too. A single stuck flush valve can burn through thousands of gallons in a week.

    After-Hours Commercial Plumbing in Lakeland

    Emergencies at a business cost more the longer they last, so speed matters more than the hourly rate. Water on the floor comes first. We isolate the leak, get restrooms and kitchen lines back in service and then schedule any larger repair for a slow period so you are not paying overtime on non-urgent work. Burst pipes, sewage backups and dead water heaters all qualify.

    Emergency Commercial Plumbing in Lakeland

    A burst supply line above a drop ceiling can soak inventory and ceiling tile in under an hour. Shut the main first.     Commercial Drain Cleaning and Hydro-Jetting in Lakeland

    Commercial drains carry grease, paper towels and food waste in volumes no house ever sees. A cable just pokes a hole. We use high-pressure jetting with rotating nozzles that scrub grease off the whole circumference of 4-inch and 6-inch lines, not just the bottom. Jetting keeps the line open for months instead of days. We see the most benefit in restaurants, schools and multi-family stacks.

    Backflow Prevention Assembly Service in Lakeland

    A backflow assembly keeps water from your building, including irrigation lines, boiler water and chemical feeds, from flowing back into the public supply. Most providers want a yearly test. We test RPZ, double check and PVB assemblies with calibrated gauges and send the results straight to the water provider. Failed devices usually need a rebuild kit, not a new valve. That is why the truck carries Watts, Wilkins and Febco kits.

    Commercial Water Heater Repair and Installation in Lakeland, FL

    A commercial water heater is judged by recovery rate, the gallons it can reheat per hour, not by tank size alone. Undersized heaters run cold by mid-shift. We service AO Smith, Rheem, Bradford White and State commercial units, gas and electric, tank and tankless, and we check thermostats, gas valves, flue draft and T and P valves on every call. New installs get sized from your peak hour demand. That is how a busy kitchen stops running out of hot water during the dinner push.

    Sewer Camera Inspection for Businesses in Lakeland

    A camera inspection shows the inside of a drain line on a monitor and records it so you can share it with a landlord or contractor. Guesswork goes away. We push the camera from a cleanout, note the footage at every break, root mass or sag, and use a locator to mark depth and position on the ground above. The video is yours to keep. That record makes capital planning far easier when the board asks why the sewer needs money.

    Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Lakeland

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and crack buried water and sewer lines. We confirm it with a meter test and pressure test, then use acoustic listening gear and thermal imaging to pinpoint the leak within a foot or two. Then we decide. Some leaks are best fixed with a small cut through the slab, while others are cheaper to abandon and reroute overhead. You get a price on each option.
