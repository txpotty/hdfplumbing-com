    Our Leak Detection Equipment in Fort Myers

    Our trucks carry ground microphones and correlators, thermal cameras, line tracers, moisture meters and tracer gas kits. Each tool has a job.     PEX and Copper Leak Detection in Fort Myers, FL

    Copper leaks are loud on a ground microphone.
