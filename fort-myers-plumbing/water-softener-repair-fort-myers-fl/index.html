    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Fort Myers

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. The tank looks full, but the softener cannot make brine. After breaking up the crust, we clear the sludge and test the float and brine well. Cracked tanks get replaced.

    Water Softener Repair vs. Replacement in Fort Myers, FL

    Age and parts availability decide whether a softener is worth repairing. HDF Plumbing gives honest advice on both counts. A ten-year-old unit with a sound tank and a rebuildable valve is usually worth fixing, while an obsolete head with no parts available is a different conversation. You get a clear breakdown.

    Resin Bed Problems in Fort Myers

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads and block the exchange. We test the resin, clean it with a resin cleaner or replace it. Fresh resin brings capacity back.

    Control Valve Rebuild in Fort Myers

    Service, backwash, brine and rinse are the four cycles the control valve directs every time the softener regenerates, usually in the middle of the night.     Resin Bed Replacement in Fort Myers

    Chlorine eats resin slowly, and after enough years the beads swell, break and stop exchanging the calcium and magnesium they are supposed to hold. This is common with older systems on treated city water. HDF Plumbing removes the old resin and installs fresh, high-capacity media. The softener works like new.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Fort Myers, FL

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. A clogged injector leaves the brine tank full of water and the resin never gets recharged. We clean or replace it and check the brine line for air leaks.
