    The cost for backflow preventer services in Fort Myers, FL varies. A standard backflow test usually ranges from $75 to $150.     Backflow prevention is essential because of changing water pressures, main breaks and fire flow that can drop pressure suddenly, and the potential for cross-connections from irrigation systems or industrial processes. These factors increase the risk of contaminated water entering the public supply, making a working backflow preventer essential.

    Backflow preventers stop contaminated water from flowing backward into the public supply during pressure drops. Irrigation, fire lines and commercial equipment are frequent sources of contamination. Water providers require them to protect public health.

    A standard test usually takes 20 to 45 minutes per assembly. Water is temporarily shut off during testing. Repairs may add an hour or more.

    Constant dripping from the relief valve, low water pressure and visible cracks are common signs. Discolored water or a failed test notice also indicate a problem. Prompt repair keeps you in compliance.
