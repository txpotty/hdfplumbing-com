    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Fort Myers, FL

    Combination units put a primary AC pump and a DC backup on one discharge manifold, so you get two pumps and two floats in a single assembly. They are handy when the basin is too small for two pumps side by side. Depending on what failed, we swap one pump or the entire unit.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Replacement in Fort Myers, FL

    Zoeller cast iron pumps, the M53 and M63 in particular, are what we pull off the truck most often because they take heat and hard cycling better than plastic housings. An existing Zoeller can usually be swapped for a current model with the same 1.5-inch outlet and float style. Pump not keeping up? We can move you up to a half-horse model like the M98.

    Choosing a Backup Battery in Fort Myers

    Battery choice decides how long the backup actually runs. A sealed AGM or deep-cycle marine battery in the 75 to 100 amp-hour range will typically handle several hours of intermittent pumping during an outage. We ask how often your pump runs in a storm and size the battery to match. Batteries need replacing every three to five years.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Fort Myers

    Basement bathrooms below the sewer line drain into a sealed, vented basin, and the ejector pump in that basin has to pass 2-inch solids. It is a different pump entirely. The ejector and check valve get replaced, then we reseal the lid and check the vent connection. A bad seal shows up as sewer odor.

    Yard Drainage Pit Pumps in Fort Myers, FL

    Yard drainage pits and window well pumps can sit dry from fall to spring and then seize the first time a big storm fills them. We swap in a corrosion-resistant pump and clear out leaves and silt. Discharge goes away from the foundation. A bucket test every spring catches problems early.

    Pedestal Pumps for Shallow Pits in Fort Myers, FL

    Older basements sometimes have a narrow, shallow pit poured right into the floor, and a pedestal pump is often the only thing that fits without breaking concrete. We replace the pump and float rod and rerun the PVC up to the check valve. Cheaper than a submersible, too.

    Pedestal Pump Service in Fort Myers, FL

    Louder, yes, but a pedestal motor sits high and dry, so in a narrow pit it often outlasts a submersible by several years. Narrow pits favor pedestals. Worn units get replaced, and we reset the float rod stops so the cycle is right. A submersible is worth a look when the pit is wide enough.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Fort Myers

    Stuck floats and leaking check valves cause more sump failures than dead motors do. When the check valve leaks, the water in the pipe drops back into the basin and the pump has to move it twice, which shortens its life. Every pump we install gets a new check valve. In a tight pit we use a vertical switch, and in a wide pit a tethered float.
