    Trenchless Water Line Repair in Fort Myers

    For some water line repairs, traditional trenching can be avoided. Trenchless repair methods, like pipe bursting or pipe lining, allow us to fix or replace underground pipes with little disruption to your landscaping, driveway, or foundation. This method is often faster and less intrusive than conventional digging, making it a popular choice for homeowners in Fort Myers looking to preserve their property. We'll check whether your water line issue is a good candidate for trenchless technology.

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Fort Myers, FL

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Around Fort Myers, flushing the water heater yearly and descaling tankless units helps keep scale buildup under control. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which restricts flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe seldom holds up because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and ends repeat leaks.

    PEX Water Line Repair in Fort Myers, FL

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a flexible, durable, and corrosion-resistant option increasingly used in homes across Fort Myers, FL. While PEX is less prone to leaks than older materials, damage can still occur from improper installation, rodent chewing, or impact. Our team is fully experienced in PEX repair, using specialized crimping tools and fittings to make strong, leak-proof connections. We make sure your PEX system continues to perform well.

    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Fort Myers, FL

    Leaks often start at the compression fittings and couplings right next to the meter box. Shifting soil and heavy traffic over the box can loosen those joints over time. We determine whether the leak is on the utility side or the customer side, since the water provider is responsible for the meter and the pipe before it. When the leak is yours, we replace the fittings and the customer shutoff valve in the same visit.
