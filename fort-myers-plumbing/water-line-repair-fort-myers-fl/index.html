    Water Line Repairs We Handle in Fort Myers, FL

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Fort Myers, FL

    Galvanized service lines were common in houses built before the 1970s, and a lot of them are still in the ground. Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving heavy scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. Rust and scale close the pipe from the inside. Rusty water at the first tap in the morning is a classic sign, along with pressure that drops when two fixtures run at once. Replacing the full run with copper or PEX clears all of it up. Most homes are done in a day.

    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Fort Myers

    Pipe bursting means splitting the old line in place while the new one follows behind it. It works best on straight runs. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe outward, while new HDPE comes through right behind the head. You end up with a fresh line and two small pits, and the lawn, sidewalk and driveway in between stay where they are, which is the whole reason people pick this method over a trench.

    Underground Leak Detection in Fort Myers, FL

    A ground microphone picks up the hiss of water escaping a pressurized pipe, even through a couple of feet of dirt. Quiet leaks are harder. For those we push a hydrogen and nitrogen tracer gas into the drained line and sniff for it at the surface, which finds leaks the microphone cannot hear. Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and crack buried water and sewer lines. Once the spot is marked, you get a written repair price before we dig.

    Trenchless Water Line Repair in Fort Myers

    Trenchless work makes sense when the line runs under a driveway, patio or a row of mature shrubs. We dig a pit at the meter and another at the house. The new line travels between them without a trench. A pneumatic boring tool or a cable puller drags HDPE or PEX through the soil, or through the old pipe if it is a straight run, and most jobs wrap up in a single day.

    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Fort Myers, FL

    The meter box is where a lot of leaks start. Compression fittings there work loose over time. Before we touch anything, we figure out whether the leak is before or after the meter, since the utility is in charge of the meter and the pipe feeding it. When it is on your side, we repair it. That often means new fittings and a new customer shutoff valve on the same visit.

    Main Water Line Leak Repair in Fort Myers

    A meter that moves with every fixture off is the clearest sign you have. We confirm it in about ten minutes. The house shutoff gets closed, the dial gets watched, and if it keeps creeping we know the leak is between the meter and the house. Ground microphones find the spot. A repair coupling or a new length of pipe goes in, and then we pressure test it.

    Water Pressure Restoration in Fort Myers

    Low pressure at every fixture points to the service line or the regulator, not the faucets. We put a gauge on a hose bib first. Then we check the pressure reducing valve, the main shutoff and the curb valve for anything half closed, and look at the pipe itself. Old galvanized can be choked to half its size with rust. The fix might be a new valve or a new line.

    Emergency Water Line Repair in Fort Myers, FL

    First thing on a burst line is shutting the water off, at the house valve if it works and at the curb stop if it does not. Free-running water washes soil out from under walks and slabs fast. HDF Plumbing sends a plumber with a curb key, a pump and repair couplings. We can usually stop it within the hour. If the repair will run long, we rig a temporary bypass so the house has water while we work.
