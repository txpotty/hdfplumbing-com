    Tankless-to-Tankless Replacement in Fort Myers

    Replacing an existing tankless with a new one is usually the simpler job, since the gas line, vent path and water connections are already there. We confirm they are sized right for the new unit. Corroded service valves get swapped while we are there.

    Point-of-Use Electric Tankless in Fort Myers

    Point-of-use electric tankless heaters under a sink are a different animal from whole-house units. A small under-sink unit needs one 20 or 30 amp circuit and suits a distant bath or break room. Most take under two hours.

    Residential Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Fort Myers

    Sizing a tankless starts with how many fixtures run at once and how cold the incoming water is, not the square footage of the house.     Like-for-Like Tankless Swaps in Fort Myers

    A cracked heat exchanger or a failed control board on an older tankless is usually not worth repairing past the ten-year mark. We match the new unit to your gas line, vent type and mounting spot whenever we can. Plan on four to six hours.
