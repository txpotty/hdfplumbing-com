    Pressure-balancing valves are the baseline for any new shower. Thermostatic valves hold a set temperature and add volume control, which works well with rain heads and handhelds. Either one protects against scalding. We help you choose based on how many outlets you plan.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Fort Myers

    Steam showers need four things: a generator, a water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit.     Shower Pan Installation in Fort Myers

    Shower pans fail quietly. A pan with a pinhole in the liner or a poorly clamped drain will hold water for years while it slowly soaks the subfloor, so we flood-test every tiled pan for 24 hours before tile goes down. We level prefab pans carefully. Both kinds get slope toward the drain.
