    Frequently Asked Questions About Grease Trap Cleaning in Fort Myers

    The cost of grease trap cleaning in Fort Myers, FL varies based on the size of the trap, its accessibility, and the amount of accumulated FOG.     Local regulations in Fort Myers, FL often require commercial food service establishments to install and regularly maintain grease traps or interceptors. These regulations specify minimum cleaning frequencies, proper waste disposal, and may require permits or inspections. We make sure our services help you meet these mandates.

    The cleaning frequency depends heavily on your business's FOG output and the size of your trap. Most restaurants require cleaning every 1 to 3 months, while high-volume establishments might need it monthly. Local health codes in Fort Myers may also specify minimum frequencies.

    Neglecting commercial grease trap cleaning in Fort Myers can lead to severe drain blockages, sewage backups, foul odors impacting your business, and potential fines from the local health department or wastewater authority. It can also cause expensive plumbing emergencies and business downtime.

    FOG stands for Fats, Oils, and Grease. When poured down drains, FOG cools and solidifies, sticking to pipe walls and eventually leading to severe clogs. In grease traps, it separates from wastewater, but if not regularly removed, it overflows into the sewer system, causing blockages and environmental issues.
