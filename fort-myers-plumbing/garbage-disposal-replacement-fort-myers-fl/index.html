    Frequently Asked Questions About Garbage Disposal Replacement in Fort Myers

    The cost for garbage disposal replacement in Fort Myers, FL usually ranges from $200 to $600. This includes the new unit itself, which can vary from $100 for a basic 1/3 HP model to over $300 for a high-end 1 HP unit, plus labor costs. Factors like the disposal's horsepower, brand, and any necessary plumbing modifications can influence the final price.

    Common signs include frequent clogs, loud grinding noises without processing food, persistent leaks from the unit's housing, or if the disposal simply stops turning on. If you notice these issues, it's probably time for a replacement.

    To help it last, avoid putting fibrous or hard items like corn husks, bones, or coffee grounds down the drain. Always run cold water before and after use, and occasionally grind citrus peels to keep it fresh. Regular cold water flushing helps maintain the unit.

    New garbage disposals typically come with manufacturer warranties ranging from 1 to 10 years, depending on the model and brand. HDF Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation services for your peace of mind.

    Most replacements take about an hour. Upgrades with new electrical or drain changes may take longer.

    If your disposal is old, frequently leaking, or making grinding noises without grinding food, replacement is usually more cost-effective and provides a longer-lasting solution. Minor issues like a simple jam can usually be repaired.

    For light use in a single-person household, 1/3 HP might suffice. Most homeowners choose 1/2 HP or 3/4 HP for general use, which handles common food waste well. For large families, frequent cooking, or tougher waste like small bones, a 1 HP unit offers the best performance and durability.

    In most cases, a simple like-for-like garbage disposal replacement in Fort Myers does not require a permit. However, if significant plumbing modifications or electrical work beyond a simple swap are needed, a permit might be required. We'll advise you if this is the case.
