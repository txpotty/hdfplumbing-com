    Standard Bathtub Faucet Replacement in Fort Myers

    Single-handle and three-handle tub faucets come out differently.     Three-Handle to Single-Handle Conversion in Fort Myers

    Three-handle faucets are worth converting. We cut out the old valve, sweat or crimp in a single-handle pressure-balance valve, and cover the old handle holes with a remodel plate so the tile stays intact around it. We usually work from behind the wall. Tile around the plate stays intact.
