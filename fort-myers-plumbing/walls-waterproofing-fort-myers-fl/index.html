    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Fort Myers

    Hairline cracks in poured concrete walls leak more water than their size suggests. We inject them with low-pressure polyurethane foam, which expands to fill the crack from the inside face to the soil side, or with epoxy when the crack also needs structural bonding. Horizontal cracks are different. A horizontal crack often signals soil pressure pushing the wall in, so we check for movement with a level before we seal anything. Most vertical cracks get sealed in one visit.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Fort Myers

    A drip from a shower valve cartridge, a sweated copper stub or a loose drain slip nut can soak a wall cavity for months before paint bubbles. We find it with a moisture meter, a thermal camera that shows the cool wet spot, and a pressure test on the supply lines. Then we cut a clean access hole. We replace the leaking fitting or pipe section. We pull wet insulation and run fans until the cavity reads dry.

    Waterproofing Costs vs. Repeat Water Damage in Fort Myers, FL

    Paying once beats paying every two years. Waterproofing a shower wall usually lands in the low thousands, while repeat drywall, cabinet and floor repairs add up fast, and mold remediation on top of that can double the bill. The price for exterior block sealing depends on how much wall there is. We write up each option with its price so you can compare.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Fort Myers, FL

    Interior drain tile handles water that already got through the wall. We cut a channel in the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and run it to a sump basin so the pump can push the water outside. The wall still gets wet. It just stops puddling on your floor. This route costs less than digging outside and works where a driveway or deck blocks the wall.

    Foundation Crack Repair in Fort Myers

    Crack injection takes about two hours per crack. We set injection ports every 8 to 12 inches along the crack, seal the surface with epoxy paste, and pump in resin starting at the lowest port and working up until it pushes out the next port. Polyurethane stays flexible if the wall moves a little, and epoxy bonds the two sides back together. The surface paste gets ground off once it cures.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Fort Myers

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and often add a dehumidifier to keep relative humidity below 60 percent. Constant heat and humidity are the main plumbing stress in Florida, keeping water heaters, AC drain lines, and exterior pipes working year round. The joists stop rotting. Drier air under the floor also cuts down on the musty smell in the house.

    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Fort Myers, FL

    Rubber washing machine hoses burst. We swap them for braided stainless hoses, put a drain pan under the washer with a line to a floor drain where one is available, and install a quarter-turn shutoff or an automatic leak valve on the supply box. Water heaters get a pan too. The wall behind them can take a moisture-resistant board and sealer.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Fort Myers

    Tile and grout are not waterproof. Water passes through grout every time you shower, so the layer behind the tile, a membrane over cement board or a foam board system, is what actually keeps the studs dry. We tear out failed tile, install new backer, and apply Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban at the thickness the manufacturer calls for. Corners, niches and valve holes get extra fabric or preformed pieces.
