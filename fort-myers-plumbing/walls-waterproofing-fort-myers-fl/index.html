    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Fort Myers, FL

    Washing machine hoses, utility sinks and water heaters can all leak into nearby walls. We replace burst-prone rubber hoses with braided stainless hoses and install leak pans and shutoff valves. Walls around water heaters and laundry boxes can be sealed to resist water damage. These small upgrades head off costly drywall repairs.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Fort Myers

    Crawl space encapsulation involves sealing the crawl space entirely with heavy-duty vapor barriers and often includes a dehumidifier. This not only waterproofs the walls but also controls humidity, stopping mold growth and wood rot in the crawl space. Humidity causes condensation on cold lines and clogged AC condensate drains, a frequent source of hidden water damage. An encapsulated crawl space can noticeably improve indoor air quality and energy efficiency by keeping moisture from moving up into the home. We use durable, reinforced poly sheeting for walls and floors, sealed at all seams and penetrations.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Fort Myers, FL

    A cracked or poorly sloped shower pan often causes wet baseboards and stained ceilings below. We test the pan by plugging the drain and flooding it to confirm the leak. Then we rebuild the pan with correct pre-slope, a new liner or bonded membrane and a properly clamped drain. A second flood test proves the pan is watertight before tile.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Fort Myers

    Many homes in Florida built before the mid 1970s have cast iron drains under the slab, which commonly crack and corrode with age. Wherever a home has a basement or below-grade walls, heavy rain and snowmelt can push water through foundation walls and cracks. We seal cracks with injection products, apply interior or exterior waterproofing and install drainage where needed. A sump pump may be added if groundwater collects.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Fort Myers, FL

    Interior walls waterproofing focuses on managing water that has already passed through the foundation walls. This usually involves installing an interior drainage system, often called a French drain, along the perimeter of the basement floor. Water is collected and channeled to a sump pump, which then sends it safely away from the foundation. While not preventing water from entering the wall, it effectively stops it from pooling inside your living space. This solution is less disruptive than exterior excavation and is suitable where exterior access is limited.

    Commercial Wall Waterproofing in Fort Myers

    Restaurants, gyms, salons and offices often have restrooms, locker rooms and kitchens with constant water exposure. We waterproof commercial showers, mop sink walls and restroom wet walls with durable membranes. Work can be scheduled after hours to limit downtime. We also repair the plumbing behind those walls when leaks are discovered.

    Drainage System Integration in Fort Myers, FL

    Lasting walls waterproofing often requires a solid drainage system to divert water away from your foundation. This can include interior perimeter drains that channel water to a sump pump, or exterior French drains that collect surface water. Our team designs and installs custom drainage solutions for Fort Myers, FL properties, so water is efficiently managed and moved away from your home's vulnerable areas.
