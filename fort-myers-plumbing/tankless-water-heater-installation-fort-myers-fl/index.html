    Tankless Water Heater Relocation in Fort Myers

    Moving the heater is common on a tankless job because the old tank spot is not always the best place. Mounting near the gas meter on a garage or outside wall shortens the gas line and venting, though it can add distance to the bathrooms. We weigh both. Long hot runs may call for recirculation.

    Cost-Effective Tankless Conversions in Fort Myers, FL

    A tankless conversion in Fort Myers, FL costs more than a tank swap, mostly because of the gas line upgrade, the new venting and the unit price itself. The savings come back over time. With no standby loss and a lifespan that can reach 20 years, many homeowners see the difference on the gas bill within the first few winters. We give you real numbers.

    Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Fort Myers, FL

    Replacing an old tankless unit is usually simpler than a first-time conversion. Since the gas line and vent are already there, most swaps mean matching connections, inspecting the old vent and mounting the new heater. Vent sizes can change by brand. We confirm fit first.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Installation in Fort Myers, FL

    Rinnai has been making tankless heaters for decades, and parts are easy to get. Their condensing and non-condensing lines both fit different homes, depending on the vent route and the budget you are working with. The non-condensing ones need stainless vent pipe. Condensing models use PVC vent and a condensate drain.

    Upgrading from Tank to Tankless in Fort Myers, FL

    Switching from a tank takes more than unbolting one and hanging the other. A typical 40-gallon gas tank uses around 40,000 BTU, while a whole-home tankless unit may need 150,000 to 199,000, so the gas line from the meter almost always gets upsized. Venting changes too. Both are on the estimate.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Installation in Fort Myers

    Commercial hot water loads in Fort Myers run longer and harder than a house. A busy restaurant may run a dish machine, three hand sinks and a mop sink at once, and the tankless rack has to hold temperature through the whole lunch rush without dropping a degree. We rack units with a common manifold. One can be serviced while the rest run.

    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Fort Myers, FL

    Calcium scale is the most common reason tankless heaters fail. Many wells in Florida produce water with a sulfur smell or high iron, which often calls for treatment to protect fixtures and appliances. Once scale coats the heat exchanger, efficiency drops and error codes start, and over time it can crack the exchanger and void the warranty. A yearly flush prevents it. We install isolation valves for that.
