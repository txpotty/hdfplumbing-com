    Water Softener Failure Diagnosis in Fort Myers, FL

    Before replacing, we check whether your softener has a simple fix like a clogged injector, failed motor or wrong settings. If the valve is obsolete or parts are no longer available, replacement is typically the better value. We test hardness before and after the softener to confirm the problem. We explain the cost of repair versus replacement clearly. Call (904) 925-7356 to schedule a check.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Fort Myers, FL

    Softener resin can break down after 10 to 15 years, especially with chlorinated city water. When resin fails, you will see hard water symptoms even though the system is regenerating. Sometimes replacing resin and the tank is more economical than a whole new system. We inspect the valve and brine tank to decide. If the valve is old, a full replacement often makes better sense.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Fort Myers

    Large households and homes with very hard water can benefit from a dual tank system that provides soft water around the clock. While one tank regenerates, the other stays in service. Kinetico and similar non-electric twin tank systems are popular for this reason. We size dual tank systems to your peak demand. They cost more upfront but prevent hard water bleed-through during regeneration.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Fort Myers, FL

    For homeowners in Fort Myers, FL looking for an alternative to traditional salt-based systems, salt-free water conditioners offer a different approach. These units, sometimes called descalers, condition water to prevent mineral scale without removing the minerals or adding salt. While they do not produce soft feeling water, they effectively protect pipes and appliances from scale. We can replace your existing salt-free system or help you switch from a salt-based unit.

    While not always an emergency, a sudden water softener failure can cause immediate issues with hard water.     Brine Tank Replacement in Fort Myers

    Cracked brine tanks, salt bridges and failing float assemblies cause softeners to stop regenerating properly. We replace brine tanks and safety floats to restore steady brine draw. We also clean out mushy salt buildup that clogs the tank. A new brine tank is often part of a full system replacement. We recommend the right salt type for your system.

    Replacing Pelican Water Softeners in Fort Myers

    Pelican Water Systems are popular for their whole-house filtration and softening solutions, including both salt-based and salt-free options. If your Pelican system in Fort Myers is nearing the end of its lifespan or needs a major upgrade, our technicians are proficient in their replacement.     Salt-Free Conditioner Alternatives in Fort Myers, FL

    Some homeowners prefer a salt-free scale reduction system that uses template-assisted crystallization media. These systems do not remove hardness minerals but change them so they are less likely to stick. They need no drain and no salt. We explain the differences plainly, since they work differently than true softeners. They can be a good fit when drain access is limited or sodium is a concern.
