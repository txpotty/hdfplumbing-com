    Understanding Backflow Preventer Installation Costs in Fort Myers, FL

    Device type sets most of the price, followed by pipe size and how much piping has to change.     Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Fort Myers

    Two independent checks and a relief valve make up an RPZ. If the zone between the checks loses its pressure margin, the relief valve opens, dumping water to the floor or a drain so nothing can pass upstream, which is why we never bury one in a pit in Fort Myers. Brands include Watts and Febco.

    Backflow Prevention for Irrigation Systems in Fort Myers, FL

    Sprinkler heads sit in fertilizer and standing water, and a pressure drop on the main can siphon that water back into the house. A pressure vacuum breaker stops back-siphonage on most residential systems, and we mount it at least 12 inches above the highest sprinkler head so it can do its job in Fort Myers, FL. Mounting height is the common mistake.

    Backflow Preventer Installation Cost Comparison in Fort Myers, FL

    A residential PVB is the least expensive device we install. Commercial RPZ assemblies cost far more in parts and labor, and size, brand, enclosure and pipe changes all move the number, but replacing a worn old device often costs less than paying for a rebuild every year.     Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in Fort Myers, FL

    Commercial kitchens and chemical injection systems need an RPZ.
