    Frequently Asked Questions About Drainage Services in Fort Myers

    Drain clearing in Fort Myers usually runs $125 to $500 depending on the line, and hydro jetting runs $350 to $800. Yard drains cost more. Most residential French drain or downspout projects land between $2,000 and $8,000, depending on length, depth and how much hardscape we have to cut through or work around.

    Drain cleaning usually means a cable machine with a cutting head that bores through the clog. It is quick and cheap. Jetting scours the full pipe wall, lasting longer, but it costs more and needs sound pipe.

    Our cable machines come from Spartan Tool and RIDGID, and our cameras are RIDGID SeeSnake units with built-in locators. Jetters are trailer or cart units. Good equipment matters because a camera with a locator tells us exactly where and how deep a problem is, which keeps a sewer repair to one small excavation instead of a long trench.

    Snaking is the right first step for most single clogs. It is fast and inexpensive. Jetting is better for grease-coated kitchen lines, scaled cast iron and roots that keep coming back, because it cleans the pipe wall instead of punching a hole through the blockage.

    Most sink and toilet clogs clear in under an hour. Main lines take longer. A main line with camera work usually takes about two hours, more if the cleanout is hard to reach.

    Roots grow toward the water and nutrients inside sewer pipe. They only need a hairline gap. Once a root tip gets in through a cracked joint or loose seal, it grows into a mass that catches paper and grease, and clay and older concrete pipe with many joints are the most vulnerable.

    Yes, sewer line repairs and replacements in Fort Myers, FL usually require a plumbing permit and an inspection. Drain clearing does not. We handle the permit and locates, and the inspector sees the pipe before we backfill.

    A single fixture drain usually takes about an hour. Plan on one to two hours for a main line. Camera work adds 30 to 60 minutes, and a buried or missing cleanout adds more.
