    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Fort Myers

    Tankless heaters use flow sensors to start and thermistors to regulate temperature. A dirty inlet filter or failed sensor causes no hot water, fluctuating temperature or the unit turning off mid-shower. We clean filters, test sensor readings and replace faulty parts. Temperature becomes steady again once readings are correct.

    Condensing Tankless Repair in Fort Myers, FL

    Condensing tankless heaters, such as many Navien models, extract extra heat from exhaust and produce condensate. Clogged condensate traps, failed neutralizers and blocked drains can shut the unit down. We clean the trap, replace the neutralizer media and verify proper drainage. PVC venting is also inspected for slope and sagging.
