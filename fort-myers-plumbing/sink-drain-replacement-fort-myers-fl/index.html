    Vessel and Grid Drain Replacement in Fort Myers

    Vessel sinks use a different drain. Bowls without an overflow need an unslotted grid or umbrella drain, or water leaks out the slots. Mismatched drains are common. Swapping it takes under an hour.

    Kitchen Sink Drain Replacement in Fort Myers

    Kitchen drains start failing at the basket strainer. The putty under a 3-1/2 inch strainer dries out, the locknut rusts, and water seeps down the outside of the tailpiece where it looks like the trap is leaking even when it is not. Strainer and trap get swapped as a pair. Disposal sinks get a new flange instead.

    Pop-Up Drain Replacement in Fort Myers

    Lift-rod pop-ups are the most common bath drain. We pull the old body, clean the flange seat down to bare porcelain, reset the new flange in putty or silicone, and adjust the clevis so the lift rod opens and closes the stopper with one easy motion. We match the faucet finish. Overflow-style drains are standard.

    Bathroom Drain Upgrade in Fort Myers, FL

    Old bath drains often carry decades of hair and scum. A new pop-up and trap get rid of the rough walls hair clings to, and the bowl empties in seconds. Clogs come back slower. A hair catcher keeps it that way.

    Corroded Drain Pipe Replacement in Fort Myers, FL

    Galvanized drain arms rust from the inside. In older homes the steel stub coming out of the wall can be so thin at the threads that it crumbles when we put a wrench on it, so we cut it back and tie in new PVC with a shielded transition coupling. Cast iron gets the same treatment. Sometimes that means opening the wall.

    Leaking Drain Assembly Replacement in Fort Myers

    Water on the cabinet floor needs tracking, not guessing. We dry the drain, fill the bowl and pull the stopper, then watch the strainer and every slip joint with a dry towel until the drip shows itself. Supply leaks look similar. We check those too.

    Leaking P-Trap Replacement in Fort Myers

    Traps leak at the joints first. Slip nut washers flatten and harden with age, and a trap opened often for clogs usually has stripped threads. A new trap kit costs little. Labor is most of the bill.

    Push-Button Drain Installation in Fort Myers, FL

    Push-button drains get rid of the lift rod. They open and close with a press on the cap, and we check that the sink has an overflow before choosing a slotted body, since a slotted drain in a bowl without an overflow leaks out the slots. They clean easier too. Hair still collects, so pull the cap now and then.
