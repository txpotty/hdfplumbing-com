    Frequently Asked Questions About Garbage Disposal Repair in Fort Myers

    The cost of garbage disposal repair in Fort Myers, FL can vary depending on the issue. Simple fixes like clearing a jam might range from $100 to $200, while more complex repairs involving motor replacement or significant leaks could be $250 to $500 or more. We provide a detailed estimate after diagnosing the problem.

    Frequent jams, constant resets, leaks from the body, loud grinding and age over ten years are signs replacement is the smarter choice.

    Common signs include unusual noises like grinding or humming without grinding, a complete lack of power, persistent leaks from under the sink, or foul odors that won't go away. If you notice any of these, it's a good idea to call a licensed plumber.

    A leak from the bottom usually means the internal seal has failed. This generally cannot be repaired, and the unit needs replacing.

    While some minor issues like resetting the unit or clearing a simple jam with an Allen wrench can be DIY, more complex problems involving electrical components, significant leaks, or motor issues are best left to a professional. Improper repairs can lead to further damage, electrical hazards, or new leaks.

    To prevent future problems, avoid putting grease, coffee grounds, eggshells, fibrous vegetables, or large bones down the disposal. Always run cold water before, during, and after use for at least 30 seconds. Now and then, grind citrus peels to help clean and deodorize the unit.

    The reset button protects the motor from overheating. If it trips frequently, the unit may be overloaded, jammed or failing.

    If your garbage disposal is found to be beyond economical repair, our plumber will provide options for a new garbage disposal installation. We can help you choose a good replacement unit that meets your needs and budget, and then professionally install it.
