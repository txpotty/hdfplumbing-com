    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Fort Myers

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat speeds up corrosion and causes expansion and contraction. Signs include warm spots on the floor, a water heater that runs nonstop and higher gas or electric bills. Thermal imaging shows the heat pattern spreading through tile or concrete. We confirm with a pressure test and acoustic listening before flagging the spot.

    Non-Invasive Leak Detection in Fort Myers, FL

    Our primary goal is to locate your slab leak with the least amount of disruption possible. We prioritize non-invasive techniques like acoustic and thermal imaging before considering any intrusive methods. This approach saves your home in Fort Myers, FL from unnecessary damage, preserves your landscaping and lowers the overall cost and time of the repair process.

    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Fort Myers

    Our technicians use highly sensitive acoustic listening devices to pinpoint the exact location of a leak under your slab. This method involves listening for the subtle sound of escaping water, even through concrete. It's particularly effective for pressurized water lines, allowing us to find leaks without unnecessary excavation and minimizing disruption to your Fort Myers home.

    Repiping After Multiple Slab Leaks in Fort Myers, FL

    If your home has had two or more slab leaks, the remaining pipe is likely corroded too. Repiping replaces all supply lines with new PEX or copper run through the walls and attic. It is a bigger project, but it ends the cycle of repeat slab leaks. We can typically complete a full repipe in two to four days.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Fort Myers, FL

    Cold water slab leaks do not create warm spots, so they are harder to see. They often show up as a running water meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards. We isolate the cold side and use acoustic equipment to listen for the leak through the slab. Line tracers map the pipe route so we know where to listen.

    Slab Leak Repair Through Direct Access in Fort Myers, FL

    When a single leak is found on otherwise healthy pipe, direct access can be the most economical repair. We cut a small opening in the floor at the marked spot, repair the pipe and pressure test it. Then we patch the concrete and leave the area ready for flooring. Accurate detection keeps that opening as small as it can be.
