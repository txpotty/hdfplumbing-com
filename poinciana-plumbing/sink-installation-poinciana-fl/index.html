    Double Vanity Sink Installation in Poinciana

    A second bowl means a second drain and two more supplies. Each bowl drains through its own trap or through a continuous waste to a shared trap, and the second faucet gets new stops. Vent sizing gets checked. Both bowls should drain without gurgling.

    Drop-In Sink Installation in Poinciana, FL

    Drop-in sinks are the most forgiving style. The rim covers small flaws in the cutout, so a laminate counter can take a new self-rimming sink with only a check of the hole size and a bead of silicone or putty as the maker specifies. Mounting clips snug it down. Stainless drop-ins weigh very little.

    Vessel Sink Installation in Poinciana, FL

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. Since the rim sits higher, the faucet has to be taller or wall-mounted, and the drain must match whether the bowl has an overflow. No overflow means no slotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.

    Leak-Proof Sink Connections in Poinciana, FL

    Every joint gets tested before we leave. The bowl gets filled and drained while we watch every nut under the sink with a light and a dry towel. Paper towels catch the smallest drip. Pipe dope goes on threads, not compression fittings.

    Bathroom Sink Installation in Poinciana, FL

    Bathroom sinks are lighter but fussier. The faucet holes have to match the faucet you picked, whether that is a single hole, a 4 inch centerset or an 8 inch widespread, and the overflow has to line up with the pop-up drain. We check the spec sheet first. Mismatches get caught before the counter is cut.
