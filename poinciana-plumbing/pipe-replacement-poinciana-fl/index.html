    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Poinciana, FL

    Many older homes in Poinciana, FL still have galvanized steel pipes, which are vulnerable to rust and corrosion on the inside. HDF Plumbing provides comprehensive galvanized pipe replacement services, removing these failing pipes and installing new, modern materials. Upgrading from galvanized pipes not only improves your water quality and pressure but also protects your home from potential water damage caused by deteriorating plumbing.

    PEX Repiping in Poinciana

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale buildup and expands slightly if water freezes, which helps in colder homes. We use expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe for stronger flow. Systems can be run as a home-run manifold with a shutoff for each fixture or as a trunk and branch layout. PEX installs faster than copper with far fewer fittings in the walls. It is our most requested repipe material.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Poinciana

    Gray polybutylene pipe was installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid-1990s. It breaks down from the inside when exposed to chlorinated water, and fittings can crack with no warning. Some insurers raise premiums or decline coverage on homes that still have it. We remove polybutylene supply lines and replace them with PEX-A or copper. A completed repipe can also help when listing the home.
