    Under-Sink Grease Trap Maintenance in Poinciana, FL

    Under-sink hydromechanical traps are rated by flow, usually 10 to 50 gallons per minute, and they hold far less than people expect. In a busy kitchen, that means cleaning every week or two. The service covers grease and solids removal, a baffle scrape, a look at the inlet flow control fitting, and a new lid gasket if the old one is flattened. A bad gasket is why most of these smell.

    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Lines in Poinciana, FL

    A cable machine pokes a hole through grease. Jetting actually removes it. We run a jetter at roughly 3,000 to 4,000 psi with a rotating nozzle that cuts the grease off the full circumference of the pipe and flushes it downstream. A camera pass afterward shows whether the line is really clean.

    Grease Trap Pumping and Hauling in Poinciana

    Trap waste is regulated as a liquid waste, so in most places it has to be hauled by a licensed hauler to an approved receiving facility, not dumped down a manhole or into a dumpster. Pumping and hauling are both on us. Everything goes into a sealed tank on the truck, and each load is logged with the gallons and where it went. No barrels left behind.

    Indoor Grease Trap Cleaning in Poinciana

    Indoor traps usually sit under the three-compartment sink or in a recessed pit near the dish station. Space is tight back there. Mats go down first, the contents get pumped or scooped into sealed containers, and the floor gets wiped so prep can start right after. Gasket and flow control get checked too.

    After-Hours Grease Trap Calls in Poinciana

    Backups do not wait for business hours. We take emergency trap calls nights and weekends. Step one is pumping the trap down to stop the water, and step two is finding out whether the clog is in the trap, the outlet tee or farther down the line. Most kitchens are back open by the next service.

    Scheduled Grease Trap Maintenance in Poinciana
