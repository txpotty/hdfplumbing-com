    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Poinciana, FL

    Thermal imaging cameras allow us to visualize temperature differences on surfaces, which can indicate a hidden water leak. As water escapes a hot water line, it creates a clear thermal signature on the concrete slab. This technique is particularly useful for hot water line leaks and helps us narrow down the search area significantly without any physical disruption to your property in Poinciana.

    Video Pipe Inspection for Sewer Leaks in Poinciana, FL

    Sewer line issues under a slab can also look like slab leak symptoms. We use high-resolution video cameras to inspect the interior of your drain lines, identifying cracks, blockages or separations. This is vital if the issue isn't a pressurized water line, helping us determine if a sewer line repair is needed instead for your Poinciana home.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Poinciana

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat speeds up corrosion and causes expansion and contraction. Signs include warm spots on the floor, a water heater that runs nonstop and higher gas or electric bills. Thermal imaging shows the heat pattern spreading through tile or concrete. We confirm with a pressure test and acoustic listening before marking the spot.

    Slab Leak Repair Through Direct Access in Poinciana, FL

    When a single leak is found on otherwise healthy pipe, direct access can be the most affordable repair. We cut a small opening in the floor at the marked spot, repair the pipe and pressure test it. Then we patch the concrete and leave the area ready for flooring. Accurate detection keeps that opening as small as possible.

    Emergency Slab Leak Response in Poinciana

    A slab leak that is flooding floors or causing water to bubble up through tile needs quick attention. We respond quickly, help you shut off the water and isolate the leaking side if possible. That often lets you keep some water service until the repair. Then we locate and fix the leak.

    Water and Sewer Line Slab Leaks in Poinciana, FL

    Slab leaks can affect both freshwater supply lines and sewer drain pipes. Our detection services are equipped to identify both types of leaks, which present different challenges. Sewer line leaks often involve foul odors and slow drainage, while water line leaks can show up as wet spots or high water bills in your Poinciana, FL home. We have separate methods for each.
