    Fixing Showers in Poinciana, FL

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Poinciana, FL

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Scale packs the nozzles and the restrictor screen behind them, and the spray drops off a little at a time until one day it feels like a garden mister. A soak can bring it back. A clogged cartridge gets replaced, then we test the flow.

    Leaky Shower Valve Repair in Poinciana

    One drip every few seconds adds up to hundreds of gallons a year. In a single-handle shower the cause is almost always the cartridge or its O-rings, and on an old two-handle valve it is the stem washer and the seat. We carry the common parts. Most valves we open take a Moen, Delta, Kohler or Pfister cartridge, which means a same-visit fix.

    Mixing Valve Repair in Poinciana

    Mixing valves blend hot and cold inside the wall. Over time the cartridge or spool sticks, and you get a shower that will not stay at one temperature or a handle that turns past where it should before the water warms up. A new cartridge usually fixes it. Valves with no parts left get a replacement plan and an honest look at the tile.

    Shower Drain Body Repair in Poinciana

    Drain bodies can crack or work loose under the pan. A loose or cracked drain lets water go under the pan, which shows up as a soft floor beside the shower or a ceiling stain. We clear and test the drain. We reseal or replace the drain body as needed.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Poinciana

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. If the head drips with the valve closed, water is slipping past the cartridge, and a new head will not change that. We fix the valve. Drips from the arm threads just need fresh tape and a snug turn.

    Hot Water Pressure Problems in Poinciana, FL

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. The problem is on the hot side, either sediment in the water heater outlet, a partly closed valve at the heater or a cartridge whose hot port is packed with scale. Each side gets tested. If the sinks have good hot pressure and the shower does not, the cartridge is the first suspect.

    Shower Valve Replacement in Poinciana

    Temperature that swings or a handle that fights you points to the valve. When the body is corroded or the brand stopped making cartridges for it, we replace the valve, usually through a small access panel behind the shower wall so the tile stays put. The new valve will have scald protection. Thermostatic valves are an option if you want exact temperature control.

    Shower Handle Leak Repair in Poinciana

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water running down the inside of the trim gets behind the tile at the valve opening, and over a few months it soaks the drywall around the valve without ever showing on the shower side. The trim comes off first. Worn O-rings or a loose bonnet nut are the usual cause.
