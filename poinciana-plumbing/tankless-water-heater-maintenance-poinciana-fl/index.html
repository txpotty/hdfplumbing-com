    Home Tankless Flushing in Poinciana

    Many wells in Florida produce water with a sulfur smell or high iron, which often calls for treatment to protect fixtures and appliances. Inside a tankless heater, that water runs through a small copper or stainless coil while the burner fires, and every minute of hot water leaves a little calcium behind. A pump on the service valves pushes descaling solution through the coil for roughly an hour. The flow returns. Temperatures stop swinging, and the unit quits shutting down on high-limit.

    Flush Now or Replace the Exchanger Later in Poinciana, FL

    Step-by-Step Tankless Flushing in Poinciana

    First we shut off the gas and power, then close both isolation valves so the heater is cut off from the house plumbing.
