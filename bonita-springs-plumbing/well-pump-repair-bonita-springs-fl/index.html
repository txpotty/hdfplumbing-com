    Pressure Switch Replacement in Bonita Springs

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are frequent causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most affordable well repairs.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Bonita Springs, FL

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and relay. After a major storm, homes on wells or septic should be checked before normal use, since floodwater can foul both systems. Lightning and power surges often damage these components. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection and replace failed parts. A surge protector at the control box can help reduce repeat failures.
