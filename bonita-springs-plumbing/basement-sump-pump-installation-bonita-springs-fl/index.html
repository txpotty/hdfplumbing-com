    Sump Pump Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Bonita Springs, FL

    A high water alarm is an affordable part that gives you warning before water reaches the floor.     Commercial and Multi-Unit Sump Installations in Bonita Springs

    Office buildings, parking structures and multi-unit properties with below-grade levels often need duplex sump systems with two pumps and an alternating control panel. The panel switches lead and lag pumps on each cycle so wear stays even, and a high water alarm notifies building staff. We coordinate electrical with your electrician and follow manufacturer specs for basin size and float settings. Property managers get a clear maintenance schedule to keep both pumps exercised.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Installation in Bonita Springs

    A great pump is useless if the water it moves runs right back toward the foundation. We route rigid PVC discharge pipe out of the basement and extend it at least 10 feet from the house or into a pop-up emitter or dry well. A spring or swing check valve keeps the column of water from dropping back into the pit after every cycle, which saves wear on the motor. Frozen pipes are rare in most of Florida, and many homes have outdoor plumbing that would never hold up in a northern winter. Where lines can freeze, we add a freeze-resistant outlet so ice cannot block the line.
