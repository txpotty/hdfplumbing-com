    Slab leak detection in Bonita Springs usually costs $150 to $450. Repairs range from about $800 for a simple reroute to $3,000 or more for direct access under finished floors. We provide a written estimate after locating the leak.

    Direct repair works well for a single leak on otherwise healthy pipe in an accessible spot. Rerouting is often better under tile or hardwood and avoids future leaks on that line. We help you compare both.

    Common signs of a slab leak include unusually high water bills, warm spots on your floor, the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use, visible cracks in the foundation, damp or wet spots on carpets or flooring and a sudden drop in water pressure.     Some homeowner's insurance policies may cover the cost of accessing and repairing the slab leak, particularly if the leak was sudden and accidental.     Reroutes and repipes often require a plumbing permit, while small direct repairs may not. Requirements vary by city and county. We handle permits and inspections when needed.

    Slab leaks can cause serious damage including foundation cracks, soil erosion beneath the slab, mold and mildew growth in wall cavities or under flooring, damage to flooring materials and increased humidity levels. Over time, it can lead to structural instability and major, costly repairs.
