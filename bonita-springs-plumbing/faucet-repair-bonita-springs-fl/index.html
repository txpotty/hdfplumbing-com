    Frequently Asked Questions About Faucet Repair in Bonita Springs

    The cost of faucet repair in Bonita Springs, FL typically ranges from $150 to $400, depending on the complexity of the issue and the parts required. Minor repairs like replacing a washer or O-ring are on the lower end, while more involved issues such as cartridge replacement or internal valve repairs may cost more. We provide a clear, upfront estimate after diagnosing the problem.

    Faucet repair involves fixing the existing fixture by replacing specific worn or broken internal components like washers, O-rings, or cartridges. Faucet replacement means removing the old faucet entirely and installing a brand new one. We recommend replacement when a faucet is severely damaged, very old, or if you simply want an upgrade.

    Many brands, including Moen and Delta, offer limited lifetime warranties on cartridges and finishes for the original owner. We can help you check whether parts may be covered.

    Common signs include a persistent drip or leak, difficulty turning the handle, low water pressure, strange noises when the faucet is on, or visible corrosion around the base or spout. If you notice any of these issues, it's best to address them promptly to prevent further damage or water waste.

    Simple cartridge swaps can be a DIY job if you identify the correct part. Stuck cartridges, corroded valve bodies and leaks under the sink are best handled by a plumber.

    Most repairs take 30 to 60 minutes. If a special part must be ordered, we may need a second short visit.

    Low pressure at one faucet is typically a clogged aerator or cartridge. Low pressure everywhere could mean a failing pressure regulator, a supply issue or old corroded pipes.

    While some minor leaks, like a simple washer replacement on a compression faucet, can be a DIY project, most modern faucets involve specific tools and delicate parts like cartridges or ceramic discs. Incorrect repairs can lead to further damage, more significant leaks, or voided warranties. It's usually best to call a professional for a proper and lasting fix.
