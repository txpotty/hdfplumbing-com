    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin & Pit Installation in Bonita Springs

    Digging the pit means cutting the slab and excavating below it, deep enough to set the basin flush with the finished floor. Nearly all homes in Florida sit on concrete slabs over sandy soil, so leaks under the slab are a frequent and costly repair.     Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Bonita Springs

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps seem similar at first but do very different jobs. A sump pump moves groundwater from a perimeter drain to the yard, while an ejector moves sewage from toilets and sinks to the sewer, and the ejector basin has to be sealed and vented because of the gas. Never mix the two.
