    Common Sink Repairs in Palm Bay, FL

    Cracked or Loose Basin Repair in Palm Bay

    Undermount clips let go more often than people think. Failed epoxy lets the bowl sag a little, the caulk splits, and counter water runs right into the cabinet. New clips go in and the bowl gets pulled tight. Hairline cracks in porcelain near the drain mean replacement.

    Sink Parts Replacement in Palm Bay, FL

    Most sinks do not need replacing. Swapping a tailpiece or pop-up costs far less than a new sink, and you keep the bowl and faucet you already have. We match finish where we can. Discontinued faucets sometimes force a full faucet swap.

    Office and Restaurant Sink Repair in Palm Bay

    Office breakroom sinks get abused in ways a home sink never sees. Coffee grounds and paper towels go down all day, and nobody checks the trap until water is on the carpet. We clear it and add a strainer. For restaurants, we keep three-bowl sinks running between the lunch and dinner rush.

    Hair Clog Clearing in Palm Bay

    Hair is the bathroom sink's main problem. We pull the stopper, strip the hair mat off it, then run a small hand auger through the trap and into the wall arm instead of pouring in drain cleaner. The overflow channel gets flushed too. That slot smells when it fills with slime.

    Recurring Sink Clog Repair in Palm Bay, FL

    Monthly clogs have a cause past the trap. We look past the wall with a longer cable or a camera for a sag or for buildup where the sink line meets the tub. Now and then it is a plugged vent. A sink that gurgles as it drains is the giveaway.

    Bathroom Sink Repair in Palm Bay, FL

    Bath sinks have small parts that wear. Pop-up linkage parts sit in a damp cabinet all day, and the pivot nut is the leak we find most often on bathroom sinks. Pedestal and wall-hung sinks get the same checks. Vanity sinks just hide the leak better.

    Faucet Base Leak Repair in Palm Bay

    Water pooling around the faucet base is a different leak than a drip from the spout. That points to worn O-rings on the spout or a bad base gasket, and sometimes to a cracked faucet body that cannot be rebuilt. Every faucet type rebuilds differently. We identify yours before ordering parts.

    Overflow and Drain Flange Leak Repair in Palm Bay, FL

    Some leaks only show up when the bowl is full. A leak like that is usually the overflow gasket or the flange putty, which only get wet when the bowl fills. We pull the drain and reseal it. Marble and some granite get silicone, because putty can stain them.
