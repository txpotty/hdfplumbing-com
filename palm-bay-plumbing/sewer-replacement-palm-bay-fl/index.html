    What Goes Into a Sewer Replacement in Palm Bay, FL

    Residential Sewer Replacement in Palm Bay, FL

    Most house laterals run 30 to 100 feet. Some are two feet down, some are eight. We measure both before quoting, check whether the run passes under a driveway, porch or tree, then pick trench or trenchless based on what is in the way, so the estimate you sign is the price you pay.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement Options in Palm Bay, FL

    Bursting and pulling replace the pipe. CIPP lining coats the inside of the existing pipe instead. We use bursting when the old line is broken or undersized, because it leaves you with brand new pipe rather than a liner inside an old one, and lining when the pipe is still round and mostly intact. Each has its place.

    Commercial Sewer Line Replacement in Palm Bay

    Commercial laterals are often 6-inch pipe and carry grease or heavier flows. Restaurants usually have a grease interceptor in the line too. We schedule the work around your hours, set up temporary bypass pumping when the building needs to stay open, and coordinate with the city on any work at the tap. Weekend starts are common.

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Palm Bay

    Bursting works best on clay, cast iron and Orangeburg. A hydraulic rig at the receiving pit pulls the bursting head through the old line, splitting it into the soil, while a continuous length of fused HDPE follows behind so the new pipe has no joints at all between the pits. Roots cannot get into a joint that does not exist.

    Commercial Sewer Replacement in Palm Bay

    Bigger pipe, more flow, more rules. Most commercial lines are 6-inch or larger. We handle the permit, set up bypass pumping when the building cannot shut down, and arrange traffic control when the work reaches the road, keeping the outage as short as the job allows. Night shifts possible.

    Advanced Diagnostics for Sewer Issues in Palm Bay, FL

    Diagnosis comes before price. Video tells us the problem and the sonde tells us the spot. We sometimes add a hydro-jet cleaning first, because a line full of grease can hide a crack, then camera again so we are looking at the pipe itself. Guesswork costs money.

    Under-Slab Sewer Replacement in Palm Bay

    Sewer pipe under a slab is the hardest kind to reach. There are two options. We can cut and remove a strip of floor inside the house and patch it after, or dig a tunnel beneath the foundation from the yard, and the right choice depends on the length of the run, the flooring, and what sits above the pipe.

    A lot of older houses have no outside cleanout. Every clog then means pulling a toilet. When we replace a sewer line we add a two-way cleanout near the foundation, bringing a capped riser up to grade where you can reach it, so future cleaning or camera work is quick and cheap.
