    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Palm Bay

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. Snaking just opens a path. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and we always jet a line before lining it. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We check the pipe on video before we jet it.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection and Repair in Palm Bay, FL

    Sewer smell outside the house points to a leak. So does an extra-green strip of lawn. We confirm it on camera, look for cracks, open joints or a broken cleanout riser, and locate it on the surface to keep the excavation tight. A small crack can sometimes be fixed with a short sectional liner, with no digging at all. Larger breaks get cut out and replaced.

    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Palm Bay

    Roots in a sewer line are not the tree's fault. A leaking joint invited them in. Warm, wet, nutrient-rich air escaping from a loose joint draws roots toward the line, and once they get in they thicken until they crack the pipe or separate the joint completely. We cut them out, then talk about the long-term fix. Lining or a joint replacement keeps them from coming back.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Palm Bay, FL

    Trenchless repair means we fix the pipe without opening a trench along its full length. Usually two small pits. Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE line through the old one and splits the old pipe outward, while cured-in-place lining pushes a resin-soaked felt tube into the old pipe and hardens it into a new wall. Either way, the driveway and the trees stay put. We tell you which one your pipe can take.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Palm Bay

    Orangeburg is wood fiber soaked in coal tar. It flattens with age. Cast iron from the same era rusts from the inside, builds scale and eventually splits along the bottom where the sewage runs, and once the camera shows failures along the whole run, patching it stops being worth the money. PVC goes in, usually end to end. Trenchless bursting is often an option.

    Restaurant and Retail Sewer Repair in Palm Bay

    Restaurants, retail centers and multifamily buildings load a lateral far beyond a single house. Grease and wipes cause most of it. We jet the line, camera it, and repair the weak section with options that limit how long you are closed, including after-hours digs and bypass pumping when the business has to stay open. A two-way cleanout usually goes in at the same time.

    A self-leveling camera goes in at the cleanout or through a pulled toilet. The full length gets recorded.
