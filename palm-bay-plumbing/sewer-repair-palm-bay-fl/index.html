    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Palm Bay

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful cleaning method that uses high-pressure water streams to clear stubborn blockages, grease buildup and even tree roots from sewer lines. It's much more effective than snaking for thorough cleaning and stopping recurring clogs. This service is especially helpful for restaurants and commercial properties in Palm Bay dealing with heavy grease accumulation, as well as homes with recurring drain issues.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Palm Bay

    Clay and cast iron sewer pipe crack from soil movement, heavy vehicles and simple age. A cracked section lets sewage leak into the soil and lets dirt fall into the pipe, which leads to recurring clogs. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded couplings rated for the transition. The repair is tested before we backfill so you know it holds.

    Sewer Pipe Replacement Options in Palm Bay, FL

    When repairs are no longer sufficient due to extensive damage, age or material failure, sewer pipe replacement becomes necessary. We offer several replacement options, including traditional trenching and modern trenchless technologies like pipe bursting or pipe lining. Our experts will assess your situation and recommend the most cost-effective and least disruptive option for your Florida property, giving you a long-lasting new sewer line.

    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in Palm Bay

    Before any repair, we use advanced video camera inspection to accurately locate and assess sewer line damage. This involves inserting a flexible rod with a camera into your sewer line, allowing us to see blockages, cracks, root intrusion or pipe collapses in real time. This non-invasive method prevents unnecessary digging and helps us provide a clear diagnosis and repair plan. It's crucial for understanding issues in older homes in Palm Bay and identifying problems like bellied pipes or collapsed sections.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Palm Bay, FL

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies often form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after years of wet weather. Nearly all homes in Florida sit on concrete slabs over sandy soil, so leaks under the slab are a frequent and costly repair. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is usually a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.

    Root Intrusion Removal in Palm Bay, FL

    Tree roots are a leading cause of sewer line blockages and damage, especially with the mature trees found in many Palm Bay neighborhoods. Roots can work into tiny cracks, expand and eventually crush or clog pipes. We use specialized augers and hydro-jetting to cut through and remove roots, followed by inspection to assess pipe condition. For ongoing issues, we might recommend trenchless repair or pipe replacement to prevent future root problems.

    Sewer Line Backups and Blockages in Palm Bay

    Sewer backups are usually caused by severe blockages from grease buildup, foreign objects or heavy tree root intrusion. Our team uses advanced sewer camera inspections to identify the exact cause and location of the blockage. We then use hydro-jetting or mechanical snaking to clear the obstruction, restoring proper flow to your system and preventing further damage. This approach is very effective for the stubborn clogs often found in older Florida homes.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Palm Bay

    Every sewer repair starts with a look inside the pipe. We run a self-leveling camera from your cleanout or a pulled toilet and record the full length of the line. A sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact location and depth of every problem on the surface. You get a copy of the video and a plain-English summary of what we found.
