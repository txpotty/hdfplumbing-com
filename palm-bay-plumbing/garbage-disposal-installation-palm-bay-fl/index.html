    Disposal Installation for Double Sinks in Palm Bay, FL

    On a double sink, the disposal goes on one bowl and a continuous waste or end outlet tee on the other. The layout keeps both bowls draining and stops the disposal from pushing water into the second bowl, a problem when the tee is too close to the discharge. Heavy-wall fittings last longer.

    Commercial Disposal Setup in Palm Bay

    Restaurants and commercial kitchens in Palm Bay need disposals built for constant use. They usually need their own circuit, a bigger drain and a panel that runs water on its own, and some sewer districts restrict them, so we confirm the rules first. Every hour down hurts.

    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Palm Bay, FL

    Air switches start the disposal with a button on the sink or counter. Pressing the button sends a pulse of air through a small tube to a control box under the sink, so there is no electricity at the button and no need to drill for a wall switch. Islands use them often.

    Replacing an Old Disposal in Palm Bay, FL

    Old disposals usually come off with a twist of the mounting ring, once the power is off and the dishwasher hose and discharge tube are disconnected.
