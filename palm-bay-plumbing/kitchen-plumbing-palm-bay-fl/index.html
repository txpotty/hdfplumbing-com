    Disposal Jams and Hums in Palm Bay, FL

    Humming with no grinding means the motor is getting power but the plate is stuck. That is often ten minutes. We free the plate from below with a hex key, clear the hopper with pliers, never a hand, and reset the overload before we test it with water. No sound at all points us to the switch, the outlet or the breaker.

    Disposal Repair and Replacement in Palm Bay, FL

    A disposal that hums but will not spin is almost always jammed. We cut the power, work the flywheel loose with a 1/4 inch hex key from underneath, pull out the bottle cap or bone, and press the red reset button before we run it again. Leaks are a different story. Water from the bottom of the housing means the seals are gone and the unit needs replacing.

    Grease Trap Installation and Cleaning in Palm Bay

    Grease traps are sized by flow, and a three-compartment sink usually needs at least a 20 gallon per minute unit. We set the trap level, install the flow control fitting and vent it so it does not siphon. Cleaning schedules depend on volume. Busy fryer kitchens may need it every few weeks.

    Grease Trap Cleaning in Palm Bay

    A grease trap that smells sour is normally overdue for a pump-out.
