    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Palm Bay

    Slab Leak Repiping in Palm Bay

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Nearly all homes in Florida sit on concrete slabs over sandy soil, so leaks under the slab are a common and costly repair. Rather than jackhammer to reach one leak, we run new lines through the attic and walls and leave the old under-slab pipe out of service. No jackhammer needed. That stops the current leak and removes the chance of the next one under the same floor.

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Palm Bay

    Old galvanized lines shed rust flakes that clog aerators, faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. Clogged aerators are a clue. Replacing galvanized with PEX or copper ends the rust, brings pressure back and stops the leaks that start at the threaded joints where galvanized is thinnest. We disconnect and abandon the old pipe where removing it would mean tearing out finished walls.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Palm Bay

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. We can reroute a single hot or cold branch overhead and abandon the leaking pipe in the slab, which costs far less than a repipe and avoids breaking the floor. This works well for a first leak. If the house has had several leaks, we will walk through why a full repipe usually costs less over time.
