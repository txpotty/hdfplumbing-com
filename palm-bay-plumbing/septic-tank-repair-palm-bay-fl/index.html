    Septic Tee Repair in Palm Bay

    Sanitary tees on the inlet and outlet crack or pop loose, sometimes from a careless pump-out hose. Without the outlet tee, the floating scum layer drifts into the laterals and plugs the gravel. We glue in a new Schedule 40 tee. An effluent filter goes on if the outlet has room.

    Drain Field Repair in Palm Bay, FL

    Green stripes over the trenches and a wet lawn in dry weather are the classic signs of a field in trouble. Most of the time it is biomat, a slimy layer that seals the trench bottom. Compaction and hydraulic overload from a running toilet can do it too. Testing always comes first. Resting or jetting can bring a field back.

    Drain Field Line Jetting in Palm Bay

    Distribution laterals clog from the inside with solids and slime, especially after years without pumping. A jetter goes into each lateral from the D-box or an end cleanout, and what washes back shows how plugged it was. Roots get cut. If the soil itself has sealed, jetting will not fix it, and we say so.

    Septic Pipe Repair in Palm Bay, FL

    Old homes may still have clay tile or Orangeburg pipe running to the tank, and both fail badly with age. Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and damage buried water and sewer lines. Shifting ground and roots crack the joints and flatten the pipe. We replace the bad run with 4-inch PVC, set on sand bedding with a quarter inch per foot of fall. Short sections take an afternoon.

    Septic Tank Leak Repair in Palm Bay

    A tank that never fills to the outlet, or one that fills slowly with groundwater after rain, has a leak. After pumping, we inspect the walls and the joints with a strong light, and dye flushed down a fixture shows us where the water moves. Hairline cracks get a hydraulic cement patch. Split tanks get replaced.

    Emergency Septic Overflow in Palm Bay

    Raw sewage coming up through a tub drain carries bacteria you do not want on your skin or your floors. Keep kids and pets away from it and stop all water use. We find the blockage and get flow moving, sometimes with a jetter and sometimes with a pump truck. Cleanup guidance comes with it.

    Aerobic and Alternative System Repair in Palm Bay, FL

    Aerobic treatment units use an air pump to feed oxygen to the bacteria, and when that pump dies the unit starts to smell within days. Our techs rebuild linear aerators and swap rotary ones, plus clean out diffusers clogged with solids. Timers and panels get tested too. We also keep the service log your permit may require.
