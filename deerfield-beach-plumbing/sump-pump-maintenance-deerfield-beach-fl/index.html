    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Deerfield Beach, FL

    The float switch is the most frequent reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris. We clean or replace the switch and set the on and off levels so the pump does not short cycle. Electronic sensor switches are an option for tight pits where floats get stuck.

    Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Deerfield Beach, FL
