    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Deerfield Beach, FL

    Hot water leaking under the slab warms the floor above it. A thermal camera shows that warm bloom through tile, vinyl and even thin carpet, often spreading outward from the leak point in a recognizable shape. It only works on hot lines. We use it to narrow the search before the acoustic check.
