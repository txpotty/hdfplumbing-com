    Frequently Asked Questions About Drainage Services in Deerfield Beach

    Drain clearing in Deerfield Beach usually runs $125 to $500 depending on the line, and hydro jetting runs $350 to $800. Yard drains cost more. Most residential French drain or downspout projects land between $2,000 and $8,000, depending on length, depth and how much hardscape we have to cut through or work around.

    Roots grow toward the water and nutrients inside sewer pipe. A hairline crack is enough. Once a root tip gets in through a cracked joint or loose seal, it grows into a mass that catches paper and grease, and clay and older concrete pipe with many joints are the most vulnerable.

    Roots usually enter through an existing crack or joint, then widen it as they grow. Over years, that can split clay pipe. Roots can offset joints and, in bad cases, collapse the pipe, which then needs digging or lining.

    Call when more than one drain is slow, or when a toilet gurgles while the washer drains. That usually means the main line. In the yard, water that stands for a day or more after rain is the cue.

    Most sink and toilet clogs clear in under an hour. A main line needs more time. A main line with camera work usually takes about two hours, more if the cleanout is hard to reach.

    Our cable machines come from Spartan Tool and RIDGID, and our cameras are RIDGID SeeSnake units with built-in locators. We run cart and trailer jetters. A locating camera pinpoints the problem, so a repair means one small hole, not a long trench.

    We install and service Zoeller, Little Giant, Wayne and Liberty pumps. Cast iron models last longest. For homes that lose power in storms, we add a battery backup on its own float and a high-water alarm, so a dead primary pump or a power outage does not turn into a flooded floor.

    A typical French drain or downspout system takes one to three days. Bigger jobs take longer. Projects with dry wells, long trench runs, or concrete that has to be saw-cut for a channel drain can take most of a week, especially if the ground is too wet to dig cleanly the first day.
