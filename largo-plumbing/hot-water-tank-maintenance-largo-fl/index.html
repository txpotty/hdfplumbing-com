    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Largo, FL

    The anode rod is a sacrificial metal rod that corrodes so the steel tank does not. Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving stubborn scale on fixtures and inside water heaters.     Gas Venting Tune-Up in Largo, FL

    Atmospheric gas tanks vent by draft alone, so the vent connector needs upward slope of at least a quarter inch per foot and tight joints with three screws each. We check draft at the hood.     Street pressure over 80 psi is hard on every fixture in the house, and plumbing codes generally call for a pressure reducing valve above that number.
