    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Largo

    The cost of hydro jetting in Largo, FL usually ranges from $300 to $800, depending on the severity of the clog, the length and diameter of the pipe, and ease of access. More complex jobs, like main sewer line descaling or extensive root removal, may be on the higher end. We provide a clear quote after an initial inspection.

    It is safe for most pipes in fair condition when pressure is set correctly. We camera the line first and avoid jetting pipe that is broken or caved in.

    For most modern and well-maintained pipes, hydro jetting is very safe. However, for very old, corroded, or severely damaged pipes, the high pressure could cause further damage. That is why HDF Plumbing always performs a camera inspection first to check your pipe's condition and confirm hydro jetting is appropriate.

    While hydro jetting effectively removes existing tree roots from your sewer line, it does not prevent new roots from growing back. For a long-term solution to recurring root intrusion, we might recommend trenchless sewer repair or pipe replacement, which we can go over during our assessment.

    While small, low-pressure drain jetters are available for rent, professional hydro jetting equipment is significantly more powerful and requires training to operate safely and effectively.     Hydro jetting is very effective against almost all types of clogs. It can break through solid obstructions like tree roots, flush out heavy grease and food buildup, clear mineral scale, and remove compacted sludge and debris that traditional snaking often misses.

    Snaking uses a rotating cable to break through a clog. Jetting uses pressurized water to clean the entire pipe wall and flush debris away.
