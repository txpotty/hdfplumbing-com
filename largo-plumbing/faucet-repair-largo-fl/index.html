    Many wells in Florida produce water with a sulfur smell or high iron, which often calls for treatment to protect fixtures and appliances. Hard water contains high levels of minerals like calcium and magnesium. Over time, these minerals can build up inside your faucets, corroding internal parts, clogging aerators, and stiffening moving components. This often leads to leaks, reduced water pressure, and a shorter faucet lifespan, making regular maintenance or timely repair important.

    The most common signs include a persistent drip or leak, difficulty turning the handles, inconsistent water temperature, reduced water pressure from the faucet, or strange noises when operating it. Any of these could point to internal component wear or damage requiring attention.
