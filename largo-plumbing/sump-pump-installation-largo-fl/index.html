    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Largo, FL

    A dead pump during a storm means the water is already climbing. We carry common pumps on the truck. Most swaps take about two hours. We test it with a full pit before leaving.

    Crawl Space Sump Pump Installation in Largo, FL

    Crawl spaces with standing water breed mold and rot sill plates within a few seasons. We set a basin at the lowest point, run perforated drain pipe to it where needed and seal the vapor barrier around the lid so the space stays dry. Clearance is usually under two feet. The discharge exits through the rim joist and drains away from the house.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Largo

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. Because the motor is cooled by the surrounding water, they run quieter and can push more gallons per minute up a tall lift than most pedestal pumps. Lids close flat over them. Each install gets a sealed lid and a grommet around the cord to keep out radon and odor.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Largo

    No pit yet? We cut one at the low corner. We backfill around the basin with washed gravel so groundwater has a path in. The slab gets patched with new concrete. The pump, lid and discharge go in last.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Largo

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi monitors go further and send a phone alert when the pump runs too long, the power drops or the water climbs past a set level. Rentals need these. They can go on a new pump or one that is already working.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Largo, FL

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. The backup pump gets its own float and its own check valve, and it runs off a sealed 12-volt battery when the outlet goes dead. Most run five to seven hours on a full charge. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Largo

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go. We install exterior basins with pumps for those spots. Lots that slope toward the house need this most. We keep the outlet away from your foundation and the neighbor line.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Largo

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. They fit pits where two separate pumps would crowd each other. That saves some labor. They suit homes that want backup protection but do not want to enlarge the pit.

    Most Largo, FL sump pump jobs land between $600 and $1,800. That range covers a quality pump, a new check valve, the discharge fittings and the labor to set and test it in a pit that already exists. Digging a pit adds cost. Every line item is on the quote.

    Swapping a pump in an existing pit usually takes two or three hours. A new pit can take a full day.

    Have it cleaned and inspected once a year, ideally before the rainy season. The service covers the float switch, the check valve, the pit and a full test of the discharge line to the outside. It catches problems early.

    Most last seven to ten years. Pumps that sit idle for long stretches can fail sooner, so test yours a few times a year.
