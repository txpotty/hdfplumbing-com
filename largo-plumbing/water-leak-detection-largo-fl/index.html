    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Largo

    Leaks in underground water lines, from your meter to your home, can go unnoticed for long periods, wasting water and causing yard erosion. Signs include oddly green patches in your lawn, persistent wet spots or a constantly running water meter. HDF Plumbing employs ground-penetrating radar and tracer gas detection methods to accurately map out and pinpoint leaks in buried pipes, allowing for targeted excavation and repair rather than digging up your entire yard.

    Pool Water Leak Detection in Largo, FL

    Pools lose a lot of water to evaporation in summer heat, so a leak can hide behind normal loss. We use a bucket test to compare evaporation with actual water loss. Then we pressure test pool plumbing and dye test fittings, lights and skimmers. Finding the leak protects the pool and the soil around it.

    Leak Detection for PEX and Copper Pipes in Largo

    Whether your plumbing system uses modern PEX tubing or traditional copper pipes, HDF Plumbing has the expertise to detect leaks in both materials. PEX, while flexible, can be vulnerable to damage from rodents or improper installation, while copper can corrode over time, especially with hard water. Our tools are effective across many pipe materials, allowing accurate detection regardless of your home's plumbing type.

    Water Meter Leak Testing in Largo

    The water meter is the first and easiest leak test. With every fixture and appliance off, a moving leak indicator means water is escaping somewhere. We read the flow rate to estimate how many gallons are being lost. Then we shut off zones one at a time to see which part of the property is leaking.

    Irrigation Leak Detection in Largo

    Drip and sprinkler systems run underground across yards and often account for hidden water loss. Cracked tubing, broken emitters and stuck valves can waste huge amounts of water. We isolate the irrigation system, test each zone and trace leaking lines. Repairs restore efficient watering for your landscape.

    Pinhole Leak Detection in Largo, FL

    Pinhole leaks, often found in copper or PEX piping, are small but can cause significant damage over time, especially behind walls or under floors. We use non-invasive moisture meters to detect elevated humidity levels and thermal cameras to identify cold spots that indicate water. Our plumbers are skilled at tracing these elusive leaks without tearing apart your entire home. Swift action on pinhole leaks prevents extensive drywall damage, mold growth and compromised structural integrity. Constant heat and humidity are the main plumbing stress in Florida, keeping water heaters, AC drain lines, and exterior pipes working year round.

    Slab Leak Detection in Largo

    Slab leaks are common in homes on shifting soil and moving foundations. These leaks occur in pipes beneath your concrete slab, often signaled by warm spots on the floor, sudden spikes in your water bill or the sound of running water when all fixtures are off. HDF Plumbing uses dedicated acoustic listening devices and thermal imaging cameras to precisely locate these hidden leaks without destructive guesswork. Early detection is essential to preventing major foundation damage.

    Yard and Outdoor Leak Detection in Largo

    Leaks in outdoor plumbing, irrigation systems or main water lines buried in your yard can lead to wasted water and landscaping damage. We use ground-penetrating radar and tracer gas detection to locate buried pipes and identify leaks without extensive digging. Our approach minimizes disruption to your lawn and garden, preserving your property's appearance. Detecting these outdoor leaks early can also prevent erosion and potential foundation issues caused by saturated soil, a serious concern for homes in Largo, FL.
