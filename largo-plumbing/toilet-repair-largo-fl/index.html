    Common Toilet Repairs in Largo, FL

    Fill Valve Replacement in Largo

    Old ballcock valves with a brass arm and a big plastic float are loud and slow to refill, and they tend to stick open when scale builds up in the ports. We swap them for an adjustable fill valve from Fluidmaster or Korky and set the height so the critical level mark sits at least an inch above the overflow. It is a twenty-minute job. The tank gets noticeably quieter.

    Toilet Brands We Service in Largo

    Kohler, American Standard, TOTO, Gerber and Mansfield make up most of the toilets we open, and each one uses its own flapper size or flush valve design. Put the wrong flapper on one and it will leak within weeks. We read the model number cast inside the tank wall and stock the common ones on every truck.

    Commercial Toilet Repair in Largo

    Office and retail restrooms take more flushes in a day than a house sees in a month, so the parts wear faster and fail harder. Most commercial toilets use flushometer valves instead of tanks, and those need diaphragm kits and vacuum breakers from time to time. We carry Sloan and Zurn kits. If downtime is a problem, we can come in before you open or after you lock up.

    Toilet Tank and Bowl Crack Repair in Largo

    Hairline cracks in a tank can sit for years and then split open when someone sets a heavy lid down or a bolt is tightened too far. Epoxy does not hold under water pressure for long. We replace it. If the tank model is still made, a matching tank is often cheaper than a new toilet.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Repair in Largo

    Restaurant restrooms get the hardest use we see, and wall-hung bowls there can loosen on their carriers until the seal leaks behind the wall. The fix is to tighten or rebuild the carrier, then reseal the bowl. That one needs care. Leave a loose wall-hung bowl alone long enough and the china can crack at the mounting holes.

    Flush Valve Seat Repair in Largo

    Scale on the flush valve seat is the reason a brand-new flapper sometimes keeps leaking, because the rubber cannot seal against a rough, crusted ring. Feel the seat with a finger. A scrub pad and some vinegar clean most seats, and if it is pitted we install a seat repair kit or a new flush valve. Then we set the float so the water stops about an inch below the overflow.

    Running Toilet Repair in Largo

    A tank that keeps hissing and refilling every few minutes is usually leaking past the flapper or overfilling into the overflow tube. It adds up fast. We check the chain, the float height, the flapper and the seat before touching a part, then replace only what failed. Figure on thirty minutes for a typical running toilet, longer if the flush valve seat is pitted.

    Toilet Gasket and Seal Replacement in Largo, FL

    The rubber parts in a toilet, the flapper, the tank-to-bowl gasket, the bolt washers and the wax ring at the floor, all break down with age and chlorine. They get stiff. A stiff gasket stops sealing and lets water seep out slowly where you will not see it until the floor stains. When one tank seal has failed, we replace the full set so the others do not follow next month.
