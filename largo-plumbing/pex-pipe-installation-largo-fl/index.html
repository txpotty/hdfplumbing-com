    What Goes Into a PEX Job in Largo, FL

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Largo, FL

    With PEX-A, the tech slips an expansion ring onto the pipe and stretches both with a battery tool. The fitting is pushed in while the pipe is open, and the plastic's memory shrinks it back around the barb, sealing tighter during the first minute. In the cold, it takes longer to shrink back. Joints get time to set before we pressurize.

    PEX for Water Line Replacement in Largo

    Your service line runs from the meter or well to the house, usually 18 to 36 inches down. A PEX main comes off a coil in one piece, so there are no buried joints between the meter and the foundation wall. Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and crack buried water and sewer lines. We bed it in sand. Rock pressing on the pipe is the usual cause of an early failure.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Largo, FL

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A bends the easiest and uses expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and uses crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C handles more like B. Every one is listed for potable water. Pipe, fittings and tools come from the same maker on our jobs, which keeps the warranty valid.

    Whole-Home PEX Repiping in Largo

    Low pressure at every faucet, not just one, usually points at the pipe and not the fixtures. In an older house that usually means rust-choked galvanized or polybutylene with tired fittings, and a new faucet cartridge will not touch it. Repiping does fix it. We replace every supply line from the main valve to each angle stop and test the system before closing anything.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Largo

    PEX-B is stiffer, costs less per foot, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. A crimp joint is only as good as the tool, so we check every ring with a go/no-go gauge, and a ring that does not pass gets cut out and redone. It is a five-second check. Skipping it causes most crimp leaks.

    Leak Repair with PEX in Largo, FL

    A single bad section is not always a reason to repipe. We can cut out a split or corroded stretch of copper, transition to PEX with a listed fitting, and have the water back on in an hour or two. That works for one leak. Once you are on the third patch, we will tell you straight whether the rest of the copper is heading the same way.

    Uponor PEX System Installation in Largo

    Uponor's ProPEX system is PEX-A joined with an expansion tool and a cold-expansion ring. The tech expands the pipe and ring together, pushes the fitting in, and the plastic shrinks back around the barb over the next few seconds, tighter than when it started. No crimp tool to calibrate. The fittings have a wide bore, so flow loss through each joint stays low.

    Full Home PEX Repiping in Largo

A full PEX repipe replaces all of it. You get real pressure back, and the discolored water goes away with the old pipe.
