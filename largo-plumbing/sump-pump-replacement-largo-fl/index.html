    Which Sump Pump Fits Your Largo, FL Home

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Largo, FL

    Combination units put a primary AC pump and a DC backup on one discharge manifold, so you get two pumps and two floats in a single assembly. They are handy when the basin is too small for two pumps side by side. We replace the whole assembly or just the failed side.

    Pedestal Pumps for Shallow Pits in Largo, FL

    Older basements sometimes have a narrow, shallow pit poured right into the floor, and a pedestal pump is often the only thing that fits without breaking concrete. We swap the pump, replace the float rod and stops, and run new 1.5-inch PVC to the check valve. Cheaper than a submersible, too.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Largo, FL

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the pit, out of the water, which is why a good one can run fifteen years in a dry basement. They are louder, no question. The new column gets set plumb and braced, and we set the float rod stops so the pump turns on and off at the right water levels. They fit pits as narrow as 10 inches.

    Choosing a Backup Battery in Largo

    Battery choice decides how long the backup actually runs. A sealed AGM or deep-cycle marine battery in the 75 to 100 amp-hour range will typically handle several hours of intermittent pumping during an outage. We size it to how often your pump runs on a wet day. Batteries need replacing every three to five years.

    Backup Pump Options in Largo, FL

    After a major storm, homes on wells or septic should be checked before normal use, since floodwater can foul both systems. Outages tend to hit right when the rain is heaviest and the pit is filling fastest. A backup takes over when the main pump quits or loses power. Where water pressure is strong, a water-powered backup runs off the supply line with no battery at all, protected by a backflow preventer. Wells cannot run them.

    Commercial Sump Pump Service in Largo

    Commercial sumps run harder than residential ones, and a restaurant or office basement often has a duplex system with two pumps that alternate on a controller.
