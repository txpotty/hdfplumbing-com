    Leaks We Locate in Ocala, FL

    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Ocala, FL

    The service line from the meter to the house can leak for months without surfacing, especially in old galvanized pipe. A green stripe in a dry yard is a hint. We trace the line with an electronic locator, then use a ground microphone to hear the leak, and on plastic pipe we inject tracer gas and sniff for it at the surface.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection in Ocala, FL

    A cracked drain line leaks wastewater into the soil, which can show up as a sewer smell in the yard, a soggy patch or slow drains. We use a camera for these. The camera goes in at the cleanout and runs to the main, recording cracks, offset joints and root entry in PVC, clay and cast iron, so you see the exact distance and depth of the break.

    Preventive Leak Inspections in Ocala, FL

    A yearly leak check catches small problems early. We test static pressure, read the meter with everything off and look at supply lines, water heater fittings and shutoff valves. Older homes with galvanized pipe or original water heater connectors get the most out of this, because those parts often fail together. It is a short visit.

    Slab Leak Detection in Ocala

    Concrete slab homes have supply lines buried in sand below the floor, and a pinhole there can run for months before anyone notices. A warm tile is the classic sign. We pressure test the hot and cold sides separately, then listen through the slab with a ground microphone and confirm with a thermal camera when the leak is on the hot side.

    Irrigation Leak Detection in Ocala, FL

    Sprinkler laterals crack and zone valves stick open, and either one can waste thousands of gallons before the bill shows it. We start outside. Our tech tests the irrigation system separately from the house, runs each zone and traces buried lines, because many water providers say to rule out irrigation first when a summer bill jumps.

    Slab Leaks From Ground Movement in Ocala

    Pipe set in concrete cannot flex when the ground moves, so it cracks at elbows and where it rises through the slab. Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and crack buried water and sewer lines. Isolating the line comes first. Then acoustic listening and thermal imaging find the leak so nobody has to break the floor open to search, and you get a marked spot plus repair options before any concrete gets cut.

    PEX and Copper Leak Detection in Ocala, FL

    On a ground microphone, copper leaks are easy to hear. PEX is quieter, since plastic pipe does not carry sound well, so we lean more on thermal scans, moisture readings and tracer gas when the house is plumbed in it. Both show up on our schedule every week. Pinholes in copper and failed crimp fittings in PEX top the list of what we find.
