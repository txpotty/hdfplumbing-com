    New Sump Pit Excavation in Ocala

    If your basement has never had a pump, we start by finding the low point where water collects. Gravel goes around the liner to help water flow in while keeping fine soil out. We then patch the slab flush with new concrete so the floor remains clean and level.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Sump Pump Installation in Ocala

    Replacing a pump in an existing pit usually runs $500 to $1,200 including the pump and check valve. Installing a new pit through the slab generally falls between $1,500 and $3,500, and adding a battery backup adds about $600 to $1,500.

    Replacing a pump usually does not need a permit, but new pits, new discharge routes or new electrical circuits may. We check local requirements and pull permits when they apply.

    Watch for a pump that runs constantly, cycles on and off rapidly, makes grinding noises or never seems to turn on. Rust stains, a stuck float and water sitting above the switch level are also red flags.

    Newer homes across Florida mostly use PEX or CPVC with PVC drains, though copper pinhole leaks are still a frequent repair in older homes. Homes built on slabs without basements seldom need one. Homes that do have basements or below-grade rooms can take on water fast during heavy storms, especially where the soil sheds rain instead of absorbing it.

    We recommend having your sump pump inspected and maintained annually, ideally before the rainy season. Regular maintenance includes checking the float switch, cleaning debris out of the pit, testing the check valve and making sure the discharge line is clear. This helps prevent unexpected failures and extends the life of your pump.

    Discharge should go to your yard, a dry well or an approved drainage area at least 10 feet from the foundation. Connecting a sump pump to the sanitary sewer is usually not allowed by local codes.

    Most residential basements need a 1/3 or 1/2 horsepower pump, but the right answer depends on how fast water enters the pit and how high it must be lifted. We measure both before recommending a model.

    HDF Plumbing offers a warranty on our labor for basement sump pump installations, typically for one year. The sump pump itself comes with a manufacturer's warranty, which can range from 1 to 5 years depending on the brand and model. We will provide you all warranty details at the time of installation.
