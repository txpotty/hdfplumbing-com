    What We Fix on Toilets in Ocala, FL

    Flush Mechanism Repair in Ocala, FL

    A weak flush usually means the tank is not filling to the fill line, the chain has too much slack, or the flapper is closing before the tank empties. We bring the water up to the line stamped on the overflow tube and adjust the chain to about half an inch of slack. Handles break too. A loose or cracked handle is a five-minute swap.

    Older Toilet Brands We Service in Ocala, FL

    Older toilets from Eljer, Briggs, Crane and Universal Rundle still turn up in homes built before the 1990s, and their flush valves often take parts nobody stocks anymore. We carry adapters for some of them. For the rest, we will tell you if parts are available or if replacement is the honest answer.

    Cracked Tank and Supply Line Repair in Ocala, FL

    Braided supply lines last years, but the plastic nut on the toilet end can crack and a bulge in the braid means the inner hose is about to burst. We check every supply line on every visit. Bulges get replaced on the spot. Old vinyl or solid plastic lines get swapped for braided stainless with metal nuts.

    Toilet Gasket and Seal Replacement in Ocala, FL

    The rubber parts in a toilet, the flapper, the tank-to-bowl gasket, the bolt washers and the wax ring at the floor, all break down with age and chlorine. They get stiff. Hardened rubber stops sealing and lets water seep out slowly where you will not see it until the floor stains. We replace the whole set of tank seals at once when one is gone.

    Running Toilet Repair in Ocala

    A tank that keeps hissing and refilling every few minutes is usually leaking past the flapper or overfilling into the overflow tube. It adds up fast. We check the chain, the float height, the flapper and the seat before touching a part, then replace only what failed. Figure on thirty minutes for a typical running toilet, longer if the flush valve seat is pitted.

    Flush Valve Seat Repair in Ocala

    Scale on the flush valve seat is the reason a brand-new flapper sometimes keeps leaking, because the rubber cannot seal against a rough, crusted ring. Feel the seat with a finger. A scrub pad and some vinegar clean most seats, and if it is pitted we install a seat repair kit or a new flush valve. The float gets set so the water level sits roughly one inch under the top of the overflow tube.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Repair in Ocala

    Restaurant restrooms get the hardest use we see, and wall-hung bowls there can loosen on their carriers until the seal leaks behind the wall. We tighten or rebuild the carrier and reseal the bowl. That one needs care. A loose wall-hung bowl can crack the china if it is ignored.

    Toilet Tank and Bowl Crack Repair in Ocala

    Hairline cracks in a tank can sit for years and then split open when someone sets a heavy lid down or a bolt is tightened too far. Epoxy does not hold under water pressure for long. We replace it. If the tank model is still made, a matching tank is often cheaper than a new toilet.
