    Pilot Light and Ignition Service in Ocala

    Gas Water Heater Noise Troubleshooting in Ocala

    Rumbling or popping from the bottom of the tank is water boiling under a layer of sediment. Hard water and years without a flush both make it worse. Whistling points somewhere else. That usually means a relief valve or a partly closed supply valve.

    Pilot Light and Burner Issues in Ocala

    A pilot that lights, holds for ten seconds and then goes out is almost always a thermocouple or a weak magnet in the gas valve. No flame at all usually means a plugged pilot orifice or a bad piezo. Burner trouble looks different. The pilot stays lit but the main flame never comes on, which points to the thermostat side of the valve, a tripped thermal cutoff or a flame arrestor so clogged with lint that the burner starves for air. Each part gets tested in that sequence.

    Gas Valve and Thermostat Repair in Ocala, FL

    Lukewarm showers and a tank that never catches up often trace back to the gas control. The tech checks the dial, the immersion sensor and the outlet pressure with a manometer. That swap takes about an hour. The new valve gets a leak test at every joint.
