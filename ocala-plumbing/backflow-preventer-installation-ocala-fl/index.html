    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Ocala

    Two independent checks and a relief valve make up an RPZ. When pressure between the checks gets too close to supply pressure, the relief opens, dumping water to the floor or a drain so nothing can pass upstream, which is why we never bury one in a pit in Ocala. Brands include Watts and Febco.

    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Ocala

    A double check valve assembly, or DCVA, uses two spring-loaded checks in series with four test cocks. It is the usual pick for low-hazard lines such as many fire sprinklers, and because it does not discharge water, it can go in a vault where local rules allow it, sized so it does not rob your system of pressure in Ocala. We leave room for the tester.

    Backflow Prevention for Irrigation Systems in Ocala, FL

    Sprinkler heads sit in fertilizer and standing water, and a pressure drop on the main can siphon that water back into the house. On most homes, a pressure vacuum breaker handles the back-siphonage risk, and we mount it at least 12 inches above the highest sprinkler head so it can do its job in Ocala, FL. Mounting height is the common mistake.
