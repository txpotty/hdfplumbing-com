    Constant Pressure System Repair in Ocala, FL

    Variable speed drives like the Grundfos SQE and Franklin SubDrive keep pressure steady by changing motor speed. When they fault, the drive shows a code that guides diagnosis. The drive, sensor and wiring get tested, and the failed part gets repaired or replaced before we verify the setpoint. These need a smaller tank.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Ocala, FL

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up through a suction line with an ejector. Air leaks on the suction side cause most failures. HDF Plumbing checks the foot valve, the ejector nozzle and every suction fitting, then reprimes the pump and watches it reach cut-out pressure. A jet pump that runs nonstop without reaching pressure has almost always lost prime or sucked air through a cracked fitting.

    Well Pump Maintenance and Repair Cost in Ocala, FL

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Ocala

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Most switches fail from pitted contacts, a plugged sensing tube or insects in the housing. After swapping the switch and clearing the tube, we set cut-in and cut-out to match the tank, usually 40 and 60 psi. One of the cheaper well repairs.

    Well Pump Diagnostic Services in Ocala, FL

    Voltage, amperage, pressure and flow rate tell us most of what is happening down the well without pulling a single length of pipe. High amp draw points to a weak motor. A clogged intake or a dropping water level shows up as low flow with normal amps, and that changes the repair entirely. Testing first saves a pull.

    Well Pump Repair vs Replacement Cost in Ocala, FL
