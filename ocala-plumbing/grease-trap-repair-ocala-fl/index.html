    Why Choose HDF Plumbing for Grease Trap Repair in Ocala, FL?

    Missing outlet tees are the single most common repair we make. They get knocked loose during pumping more than you would think. Without the tee, the outlet draws from the top of the tank, right where the grease floats, and the trap sends grease to the sewer while looking perfectly fine from above. A new tee takes about thirty minutes.

    • Inspection Ready, We fix the items inspectors cite most, like missing tees and loose lids, under Florida rules.
    • Specialized Tools, Jetters and sewer cameras let us find the blockage and clear it instead of guessing.
    • Longer Trap Life, Early repairs keep a small failed part from taking the whole trap with it.

    Citywide Coverage

    Both trap types, hydromechanical and gravity, show up on most of our weekly schedules. That mix keeps the crew sharp on each.     Damaged Baffle and Lid Repair in Ocala, FL

    Grease Line Clog Repair in Ocala, FL

    Lines downstream of the trap should stay clean if the trap is working. When we find grease past the outlet, the trap is not doing its job. We jet the downstream run to the property line or the sewer tap, then camera it to confirm the pipe is clear and to document any cracked or sagging sections for a repair quote. If we only clear the line and skip the trap, it clogs again.

    Grease Trap Drain Line Repair in Ocala

    Sometimes the trap is not the problem. Lines between the sinks and the trap clog first, because that is where hot water cools. A bellied pipe will keep clogging no matter how often you jet it.

    Leaking Grease Trap Repair in Ocala, FL

    A wet floor around an under-sink trap usually points to a failed lid gasket or a loose inlet coupling. Older steel bodies rust out along the bottom seams, too. We dry the area, fill the trap and watch where the water shows up. Some days the fix is a cheap gasket and ten minutes. If the seam itself has opened, patching rarely holds and we will tell you so before you pay for it.

    Damaged Baffle Repair in Ocala

    Baffles are the walls inside the trap that slow the water down long enough for grease to float and solids to sink. Once one cracks or falls loose, the trap is just a box that grease passes through. We pump it out and pull the old baffle. On plastic units we order the factory part for that model number. Steel traps with a rusted-out baffle may need a fabricated piece, and sometimes a new trap is the cheaper answer.

    Lid, Gasket and Riser Repair in Ocala, FL

    Outdoor risers take a beating from garbage trucks and the occasional forklift. A cracked riser lets surface water and grit into the interceptor. We replace damaged risers and set traffic-rated covers, H-20 rated where vehicles cross, and seal the riser joints with butyl rope so the tank stays tight. A light-duty lid in a drive aisle will break eventually.

A jetter actually takes it out of the line.
