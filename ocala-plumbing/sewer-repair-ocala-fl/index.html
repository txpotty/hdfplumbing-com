    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Ocala, FL

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies often form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after years of wet weather. A high water table across much of Florida affects drainage, septic design, and how easily crews can dig for underground repairs. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is typically a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.

    Root Intrusion Removal in Ocala, FL

    Tree roots are a common cause of sewer line blockages and damage, especially with the mature trees found in many Ocala neighborhoods. Roots can infiltrate tiny cracks, expand and eventually crush or clog pipes. We use specialized augers and hydro-jetting to cut through and remove roots, followed by inspection to assess pipe condition. For ongoing issues, we might recommend trenchless repair or pipe replacement to prevent future root problems.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Ocala, FL

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair technique where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is effective for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without extensive digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an excellent option for preserving landscapes in Ocala.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Ocala

    Clay and cast iron sewer pipe crack from soil movement, heavy vehicles and simple age. A cracked section lets sewage leak into the soil and lets dirt fall into the pipe, which leads to repeat clogs. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded couplings rated for the transition. The repair is tested before we backfill so you know it holds.

    Pipe Burst & Collapse Repair in Ocala, FL
