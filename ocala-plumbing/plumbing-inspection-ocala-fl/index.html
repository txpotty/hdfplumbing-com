    What a Plumbing Inspection Covers in Ocala, FL

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Ocala, FL

    Many older homes have clay, cast iron or Orangeburg sewer lines that can crack, sag or fill with roots. The camera starts at a cleanout. Every offset, belly, root intrusion and break shows up with its depth and location, and the recording is yours to keep.

    Water Heater Inspections in Ocala

    Water heaters give clues long before they fail. The serial number tells us the age, and we check for rust, look at the vent and test the relief valve. We check tankless units for scale. On gas units, we look at the burner and flame, check for a sediment trap and confirm the vent is drafting so combustion gases go outside.

    Whole-Home Plumbing Inspections in Ocala

    Our whole-home inspections focus on the problems we see most in Florida houses. That list starts with old supply pipe, worn angle stops, aging water heaters, slow drains and sewer lines with roots or sags. We test each one directly. A corroded fitting or partial blockage found now will not become a ceiling stain or a holiday backup later.

    Leak Detection and Slab Screening in Ocala

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. Warm spots are another clue. Meter tests, pressure loss checks and a look for damp baseboards come first, and slab homes get acoustic and thermal follow-up. Catching a slab leak early limits damage to floors and foundations.

    Preventive Maintenance Checks in Ocala, FL

    A maintenance check is a lighter version of a full inspection for owners who want to stay ahead of problems. The visit covers flushing the heater, working the shutoffs, reading pressure and checking for slow drains and small leaks. It takes about an hour. Yearly or every-other-year checks stretch heater life and keep valves from seizing.

    Water Heater and Softener Inspection in Ocala

    We check the water heater's age by serial number, look for rust, leaks and sediment and test the relief valve. Venting and gas connections get checked. We also look at the expansion tank, drain pan and softener, and a hardness test shows whether the softener is working or just eating salt.

    Pre-Purchase Inspection With Sewer Video in Ocala

    Plumbing problems are some of the most expensive surprises after closing. We check supply pipe, record the sewer, date the water heater and test every fixture. We send the report to your agent too. Many buyers use our findings to ask for repairs or credits, and the sewer video alone has saved buyers from five-figure surprises.

    Seasonal Plumbing Checks in Ocala, FL

    Constant heat and humidity are the main plumbing stress in Florida, keeping water heaters, AC drain lines, and outdoor pipes working year round. A seasonal check looks at the parts of the system that weather affects most. We look at hose bibbs, exposed pipe in garages and crawlspaces, irrigation backflow devices and the water heater's relief valve and drain. Insulation gets checked too. Swapping a cracked outdoor faucet or insulating a pipe costs little next to a burst line in the wall.
