    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Ocala

    Gray polybutylene pipe was installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid-1990s. It breaks down from the inside when exposed to chlorinated water, and fittings can crack with no warning. Some insurers raise premiums or decline coverage on homes that still have it. We remove polybutylene supply lines and replace them with PEX-A or copper. A completed repipe can also help when selling the home.

    Burst Pipe Repair & Replacement in Ocala, FL

    Northern parts of Florida do get occasional freezing nights, which can crack exposed pipes, irrigation lines, and backflow devices. Cold snaps can lead to burst pipes, especially in exposed areas or unheated spaces. When a pipe bursts in your Ocala, FL home, it causes instant water damage and requires urgent attention. Our team provides emergency service to shut off water, remove damaged sections, and replace them with new, more freeze-resistant piping, often PEX, to prevent future occurrences. We work fast to minimize disruption and water loss.

    PEX Repiping in Ocala

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale buildup and expands slightly if water freezes, which helps in colder homes. We use expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe for stronger flow. Systems can be run as a home-run manifold with a shutoff for each fixture or as a trunk and branch layout. PEX installs faster than copper with far fewer fittings in the walls. It is our most popular repipe material.

    Slab leaks, common in homes built on concrete foundations, can be damaging and difficult to detect.
