    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads so they cannot grab hardness. We test the resin, clean it with a resin cleaner or replace it. Fresh resin brings capacity back.

    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Ocala, FL

    A crust of salt across the brine tank stops regeneration, and hard water shows up at every tap within a day or two. The bridge gets broken up and the tank cleaned out. Older brine tanks can crack, and we repair or replace those too. Keep the salt about half full.
