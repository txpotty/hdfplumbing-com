    Ejector Pump Replacement in Ocala

    Ejector pumps tend to run 7 to 10 years, depending on how often they cycle and what gets flushed. Signs of trouble include a pump that runs constantly, a grinding or humming noise, an alarm that keeps tripping and sewage odor near the pit, and any of those means it is time to call before the basement floor tells you.     Liberty Pumps Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Ocala, FL

    Liberty builds single and dual pump packages that arrive with the basin, pump, lid and fittings matched. Matched packages speed up the install, and Liberty's float design is less likely to snag on the basin wall, a common reason older pumps fail to start. Switch failures cause many calls.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Ocala

    Liberty ejector pumps show up on a lot of our installs, with cast iron bodies and two-inch solids handling on most sewage models. A full-port valve gets added.

    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Ocala

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps look alike in a catalog but do very different jobs. Sump pumps push groundwater out to the yard, ejectors push sewage to the sewer, and only the ejector pit must be sealed and vented against gas. They are not interchangeable.

    Zoeller Ejector Pump Installation in Ocala, FL

    Zoeller has made ejector pumps for a very long time, and its cast iron models are the ones we see still running in older basements. We install Zoeller for both new basins and drop-in replacements, and its standard models often fit existing pits and discharge piping without changes. Floats and switches are stocked locally.

    Ejector Pump Alarms and Backup Options in Ocala

    A high water alarm belongs on every ejector system, since a pump failure can lead to a sewage backup quickly. Audible alarms on their own power or Wi-Fi alarms that text you give warning while the pit is still filling, not after waste reaches the floor. After a major storm, homes on wells or septic should be checked before normal use, since floodwater can foul both systems. Battery backup is an option.

    Cost-Effective Sewage Ejector Pump Upgrades in Ocala, FL

    Upgrading an old ejector does not always mean a new basin. With a sound basin, a new pump, fresh check valve, shutoff and alarm give you most of a new system for far less. Cracked basins get replaced.
