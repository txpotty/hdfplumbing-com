    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Ocala, FL

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. A jet scours the entire pipe wall. For a single clog in an otherwise clean line, snaking is cheaper and works fine, but for a line that clogs every few months, jetting removes the buildup that keeps catching debris.

    Preventive Hydrojet Maintenance in Ocala

    Preventive jetting makes sense when the same line clogs more than once a year. Camera findings set the interval. Grease-heavy lines may need six-month service, while root-prone sewers can often go 18 to 24 months.
