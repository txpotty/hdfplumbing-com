    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Ocala

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE or Rinnai Sensei pass 90 percent efficiency by pulling extra heat out of the exhaust. They vent through PVC. The condensate is acidic, so we install a neutralizer cartridge and run the line to an approved drain. Salt air in coastal parts of Florida corrodes outdoor fittings and exposed metal pipe faster than in inland areas. The savings carry through every season because hot water use does.

    Navien Venting and Install in Ocala

    Navien allows 2-inch or 3-inch PVC venting on its condensing units, with maximum lengths that drop as you add elbows. We stay inside those limits. Warranty depends on it.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Ocala, FL

    Electric tankless water heaters skip the gas line entirely, but a whole-house model can need 120 to 160 amps on two to four double-pole breakers. Many panels cannot spare that. Panel size and wire gauge get checked first.

    Leaking Tankless Replacement in Ocala

    A leaking tankless can drip inside the cabinet for weeks before anyone notices the puddle. Shut the gas and cold isolation valve now. Then we look at whether the leak is a fitting or the heat exchanger itself, because one is a $20 fix and the other means a new unit.

    Commercial Tankless Replacement in Ocala

    Cascade controls on a commercial rack decide which unit fires first, and after one unit is replaced they need rebalancing. Skip that and the new unit wears out first. We also set a descaling schedule.

    Recirculation Tankless Upgrades in Ocala, FL

    Waiting a minute for hot water at the far bathroom wastes thousands of gallons a year in a long single-story house. Built-in pumps on Navien A and some Rinnai models push water through a crossover valve or a dedicated return. The pump runs on a timer. Usually just the morning and evening peaks.

    Point-of-Use Electric Tankless in Ocala

    Point-of-use electric tankless heaters under a sink are a different animal from whole-house units. A small under-sink unit needs one 20 or 30 amp circuit and suits a distant bath or break room. Swaps take an hour or two.

    Tank to Tankless Conversion in Ocala, FL

    Converting from a tank to tankless takes a full day in most homes. The work usually includes a larger gas line, new venting through a wall or roof, and a new spot on the wall for the unit. We cap or remove the old flue.
