    Flange Repair During Bowl Replacement in Ocala, FL

    The flange is the ring that anchors the bowl to the drain, and the only time anyone sees it is when the old bowl comes off. That is the moment to check it. A cracked or sunken flange gets repaired before the new bowl goes down, since a new bowl on a bad flange will rock and leak within months.

    Commercial Toilet Bowl Replacement in Ocala

    Floor-mount commercial bowls take a beating in high-traffic restrooms, and the ones that crack usually do it at the bolt holes from years of being kicked or overtightened. The new bowl has to match the outlet and the spud size. We reconnect the flushometer and test it before the restroom reopens.

    Emergency Toilet Bowl Replacement in Ocala, FL

    A bowl that splits open can dump the tank and every refill onto the floor until someone closes the angle stop. Shut the valve behind the toilet first. Once the water is off, we remove the broken bowl in pieces if needed, wrap the shards and set a replacement or a full new toilet as fast as we can get one.

    Brands We Match in Ocala

    American Standard, Kohler, TOTO and Gerber sell bowls separately for many two-piece models, but the tank bolts, gasket style and bolt spacing have to match exactly. A near match will leak. We confirm the pairing from the model number before ordering, which avoids a second trip.

    Bowl Replacement Versus Full Toilet Replacement in Ocala, FL

    Bowl-only swaps make sense when the tank is newer and the bowl is still made, since a matched bowl often costs $100 to $350 while a new basic toilet starts around $200. The labor is about the same for both. If the tank is old or the model is discontinued, a full replacement usually wins, and we show you both prices.

    Toilet Bowl Replacement for Remodels in Ocala

    Remodels are a good time to change the bowl shape or height, as long as the rough-in stays the same and the new footprint covers the old flooring cutout. We check that before you order. We coordinate with your contractor or tile crew so the flange ends up at the right height above the finished floor.

    Cracked Toilet Bowl Replacement in Ocala

    Most bowl cracks start near the bolt holes or at the base where the porcelain meets the floor, and a crack below the waterline leaks whether you can see it or not. Some leak slowly, some all at once. We drain the tank, lift the tank off and remove the bowl, and the matching bowl goes in on a fresh seal with new tank gaskets and bolts.

    Flange Repair and Toilet Reset in Ocala, FL

    A wobble at the base usually means the bowl has been rocking on a loose or broken flange, and every rock breaks the wax seal a little more. Cast iron flanges rust thin. We repair the flange with a stainless ring, or replace it, before the new bowl goes down on a fresh seal.
