    Gravity Grease Interceptor Installation in Ocala, FL

    Concrete or polyethylene, a buried gravity interceptor is a two-compartment tank with tees on the inlet and outlet that reach down below the grease layer. Concrete is heavier and holds up under traffic. Poly is easier to set. Plastic tanks in wet soil need hold-down straps or a concrete collar so they do not float when pumped.

    Large Outdoor Grease Interceptor Installation in Ocala

    Outdoor gravity interceptors run from 750 gallons to well past 2,000, and setting one means an excavator on site and a traffic-rated lid if it sits in the drive lane. Before anything ships, we check that the tank and covers carry an H-20 traffic rating where trucks will roll over them. Most go in over two to four days. The inlet invert has to sit a couple of inches above the outlet, and we shoot both with a laser level before backfill.

    In-Ground Gravity Interceptor Installation in Ocala, FL

    Buffets, grocery delis and kitchens doing several hundred covers a night usually end up with an in-ground gravity interceptor of 1,000 gallons or more. The work is excavation and a level gravel bed, then the tank and its inlet and outlet piping. Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and damage buried water and sewer lines. We plan the dig and the backfill around what the soil report and the open hole actually show us. Risers bring the lids to grade.

    Size comes from flow, and flow comes from your fixtures. Outdoor tanks get sized by the sewer authority's formula, which we run for you.

    Watch the pump-out bills. A trap that needs service far more often than it used to is either too small or broken inside. Smell is another clue. Slow drains near the trap and failed inspections point to replacement too.

    Coverage comes from two places. Your trap carries a factory warranty that varies by brand and material. Our written labor warranty covers the joints we made and how we set the unit. Damage from skipped cleanings falls outside it.
