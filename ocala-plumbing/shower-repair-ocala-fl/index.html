    Commercial Shower Repair in Ocala, FL

    Mixing Valve Repair in Ocala

    Mixing valves blend hot and cold inside the wall. A sticking cartridge or spool shows up as temperature that drifts or a handle that has to go much farther than it used to. We replace the cartridge. Valves with no parts left get a replacement plan and an honest look at the tile.

    Leaky Shower Valve Repair in Ocala

    One drip every few seconds adds up to hundreds of gallons a year. Single-handle showers drip from a worn cartridge or O-rings, while old two-handle valves drip from a washer and a pitted seat. The parts are on the truck. Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister cartridges cover most of the valves we open, so the repair is usually done that same visit.

    Shower Handle Leak Repair in Ocala

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water running down the inside of the trim gets behind the tile at the valve opening, and over a few months it soaks the drywall around the valve without ever showing on the shower side. We pull the trim and look. It is usually a loose bonnet nut or a worn O-ring.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Ocala, FL

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. A worn spout gate or a tired diverter stem lets water keep running from the spout, and the head pressure drops off. Usually a new spout fixes it. Spout replacements take about twenty minutes.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Ocala

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. If the head drips with the valve closed, water is slipping past the cartridge, and a new head will not change that. The fix is in the valve. Leaks at the arm threads are different, and those get new thread tape and a snug turn.

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Ocala

    Showerheads clog from the inside out. Scale builds behind the face plate and in the flow restrictor, so the spray goes uneven, shoots sideways or drops to a trickle even when the valve is wide open. A vinegar soak can work. Otherwise we put on a new head with rubber nozzles that rub clean with your thumb.

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Ocala, FL

    Stains on the ceiling below are often the first clue. A meter on the walls and a flood test on the pan show whether the leak is the valve, the pan or the drain. The source gets repaired. On slab homes, these leaks can spread into the flooring of the next room before anyone sees them.
