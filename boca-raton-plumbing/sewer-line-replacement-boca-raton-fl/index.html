    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair & Replacement in Boca Raton, FL

    Trenchless technology, such as pipe bursting or pipe lining, offers a less invasive option to traditional methods. Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one, fracturing the old pipe in place. Pipe lining installs a new, durable resin liner inside the existing pipe. Both methods greatly reduce the need for excavation, preserving your yard and landscaping in Boca Raton, FL while providing a long-lasting solution.

    Traditional Trench Sewer Line Replacement in Boca Raton

    For significant damage or certain pipe layouts, traditional trenching may be the most effective method. This involves digging a trench to access and replace the damaged sewer line directly. Our team plans the excavation to minimize disruption to your landscape, using the right equipment for efficient, precise digging. We make sure of proper grading and backfilling to prevent future issues and restore your property.

    Sewer Line Inspection and Diagnostics in Boca Raton

    Before any replacement, a thorough video inspection is essential. We use high-resolution cameras to travel through your sewer lines, identifying the exact location and nature of the problem, whether it is cracks, root intrusion, or collapsed sections. This precision lets us recommend the most appropriate and cost-effective replacement plan. That way we address the real cause of your sewer issues in Boca Raton.

    Partial Sewer Line Repair in Boca Raton, FL

    Not every damaged sewer needs full replacement. If the camera shows a single break or one root-damaged joint, a spot repair may solve the problem. We locate the exact spot and depth, excavate a small area and replace that section. This costs far less than replacing the whole lateral. We only recommend it when the rest of the line is in sound condition.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Boca Raton

    Cast iron drains in older homes corrode from the inside, particularly along the bottom of the pipe. Over time this leaves cracks, holes and a rough surface that catches waste. Replacing the cast iron sewer outside the home is often the first step. We can also evaluate under-slab cast iron and discuss lining or rerouting choices. New PVC or HDPE will not rust or scale the same way.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Boca Raton, FL

    Traditional excavation is sometimes the best option, especially when the old line has a severe belly or the slope must be corrected. We dig along the line, remove the old pipe and set new PVC on compacted bedding at the correct grade. Rocky or hard-packed soil can slow digging, and we account for that in the estimate. Each joint is glued and checked before backfill. The inspector sees the open trench before we cover it.
