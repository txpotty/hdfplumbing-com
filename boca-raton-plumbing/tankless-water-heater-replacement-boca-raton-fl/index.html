    Gas Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Boca Raton

    A gas tankless unit at full fire can pull 199,000 BTU, roughly five times what a 40 gallon tank burner uses. The gas line usually has to be 3/4-inch or larger. Pipe length and meter capacity get checked against the sizing tables, and we confirm pressure at full fire. An undersized line causes ignition faults.

    Commercial Tankless Racks in Boca Raton, FL

    Restaurants and salons run tankless racks hard. A rack of four units can draw more than 600,000 BTU, so we confirm gas pressure at the far unit with every burner firing. Installs can happen after closing.

    Tankless vs. Tank Replacement Cost in Boca Raton, FL

    Tankless costs more up front than a tank, often twice as much installed. Over 20 years it can come out close, since a tankless can outlast two tanks and skips standby loss, but the payback depends on how much hot water your house uses. You see both quotes.

    Tankless-to-Tankless Replacement in Boca Raton

    Replacing an existing tankless with a new one is usually the simpler job, since the gas line, vent path and water connections are already there. We confirm they are sized right for the new unit. Old service valves often get replaced too.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Boca Raton

    Rinnai units we pull are usually 12 to 18 years old, and the failure tends to be a leaking heat exchanger or a dead control board. Newer Rinnai models usually fit the old mounting pattern. That keeps wall patching small. We match the vent type or convert to PVC on a condensing model.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Boca Raton, FL

    Exterior-mount tankless units hang on an outside wall and vent straight out the front, so there is no vent pipe to run. Covers and wiring take a beating outdoors. Frozen pipes are rare in most of Florida, and many homes have outdoor plumbing that would never hold up in a northern winter. On every outdoor swap, we check freeze protection and the pipe cover. They are usually the easiest swap.

    Gas Line Testing on Tankless Installs in Boca Raton, FL

    Every gas connection on a tankless gets a soap test and a manometer reading at full fire before we leave the house. A line that holds pressure at rest can still sag under full load. We test both.

    Rinnai Sensei condensing models replace older non-condensing Rinnai units in a lot of homes, but the venting changes from stainless to PVC. We handle that conversion. Most of the plumbing lines up already.
