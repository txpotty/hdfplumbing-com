    Ejector Pump Alarms and Backup Options in Boca Raton

    A high water alarm should be on any ejector basin, since a pump failure can lead to a sewage backup quickly. We install audible alarms with their own power source, or Wi-Fi alarms that text your phone, so you hear about a stuck float while the basin is still filling and not after waste has reached the basement floor. After a major storm, homes on wells or septic should be checked before normal use, since floodwater can foul both systems. Battery backup is an option.

    Basement Bathroom Ejector Systems in Boca Raton

    Basement bathrooms sit below the building sewer, so every fixture drains to a sealed pit instead of flowing by gravity. We cut the slab, trench the drain lines to the basin, set it, run a two-inch vent up through the house to the roof and pipe the discharge to the building drain with a check valve and shutoff.
