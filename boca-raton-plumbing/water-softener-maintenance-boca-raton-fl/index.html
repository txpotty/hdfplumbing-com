    Control Valve and Injector Service in Boca Raton, FL

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw. We disassemble the injector, clean the screens and replace worn seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1. A clean valve gives you a complete regeneration every time.

    Bypass Valve and Drain Line Repair in Boca Raton, FL

    A leaking bypass valve can let hard water slip past the softener without you noticing. We test and rebuild bypass valves and replace cracked fittings. We also inspect the drain line for kinks and proper air gap and the brine overflow line for correct routing. These small fixes prevent leaks and backflow problems.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Regeneration Optimization in Boca Raton, FL

    The resin beads are responsible for ion exchange, removing hard minerals from your water. Over time, these beads can become fouled with iron or other contaminants, reducing their capacity. Our maintenance includes cleaning the resin bed and adjusting the regeneration cycle settings for full softening capacity. We confirm your system is regenerating efficiently for your water quality, providing truly soft water.

    Routine Water Softener Tune-Ups in Boca Raton

    Our routine tune-ups involve a thorough inspection of your entire water softener system. We check for proper salt levels, inspect the brine tank for salt bridges and ensure the resin bed is working effectively. This preventive service is vital wherever hard water minerals can quickly affect system performance if not regularly addressed.

    Routine Water Softener Inspections in Boca Raton

    Regular inspections are the foundation of effective water softener maintenance. Our technicians will examine your entire system, including the brine tank, resin tank, control valve and all connecting pipes. We check for leaks, corrosion, proper salt levels and any signs of wear that could lead to future problems. This preventive approach helps extend the life of your unit and keeps performance consistent, especially since water hardness varies from place to place.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Boca Raton

    Softener resin can foul with iron, manganese or sediment and lose capacity over time. Chlorine and chloramine in city water also slowly break resin down. We run a resin cleaner through the system and test performance afterward. If the resin is exhausted, we rebed the tank with fresh high-capacity resin, which costs far less than a new system.
