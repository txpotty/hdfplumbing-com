    Residential Expansion Tank Installation in Boca Raton

    Closed systems are the reason most homes in Boca Raton need an expansion tank. A pressure reducing valve or a check valve at the meter blocks the water from expanding back into the main, so every heating cycle raises the pressure in your pipes until something gives. Usually the relief valve gives first.
