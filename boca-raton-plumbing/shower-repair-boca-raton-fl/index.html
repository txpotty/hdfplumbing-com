    Shower Repairs We Handle in Boca Raton, FL

    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Boca Raton, FL

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. Because everything is linked, a weak diverter starves the head and a leaky valve drips from the spout and the head alike. We check all of it. The overflow gasket gets a look too, since it is a common hidden leak.

    Shower Handle Leak Repair in Boca Raton

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water leaking behind the trim slips into the wall cavity at the valve opening and soaks the drywall while the tile face stays dry. We pull the trim and look. It is usually a loose bonnet nut or a worn O-ring.

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Boca Raton

    Showerheads clog from the inside out. Scale builds behind the face plate and in the flow restrictor, so the spray goes uneven, shoots sideways or drops to a trickle even when the valve is wide open. Soaking helps sometimes. Otherwise we put on a new head with rubber nozzles that rub clean with your thumb.

    Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Boca Raton, FL

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. A hand auger through the strainer clears most of them, and a drum machine handles clogs that have moved past the trap. Drain cleaner usually misses the clog. We recommend a hair catcher once it is clear.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Boca Raton

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. Water that keeps coming out after the handle is off has to be getting past a worn cartridge or seat, so replacing the showerhead alone will not stop it. The fix is in the valve. Drips from the arm threads just need fresh tape and a snug turn.

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Boca Raton, FL

    Stains on the ceiling below are often the first clue. We use a moisture meter on the walls and a flood test on the pan to tell a leaking valve from a cracked pan, a failed grout line or a loose drain body. The source gets repaired. With slab floors, the water can travel under the flooring of nearby rooms.

    Hot Water Pressure Problems in Boca Raton, FL

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. It comes down to the hot side, whether that is a sediment-choked heater outlet or a cartridge with scale packed in the hot port. Each side gets tested. Good hot flow at the sinks points us straight at the shower cartridge.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Boca Raton, FL

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open. Moen Posi-Temp showers take the 1222, and Delta Monitor 13 and 14 showers take the RP46074, and both wear out on a fairly regular clock. We stock both. Original owners can often get the cartridge free under warranty.
