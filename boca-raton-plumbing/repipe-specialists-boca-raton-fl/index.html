    Copper Repiping in Boca Raton, FL

    Efficient PEX Repiping in Boca Raton, FL

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and very popular choice for repiping projects in Boca Raton, FL. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points and making installation quicker and less intrusive than traditional rigid pipes. PEX is also very resistant to freezing, scaling, and chlorine, common issues in many water systems. We use high-quality PEX-A or PEX-B tubing, installed with durable crimp or expansion fittings, to create a dependable and efficient plumbing network throughout your home.

    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Boca Raton

    Slab leaks are a costly and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Nearly all homes in Florida sit on concrete slabs over sandy soil, so leaks under the slab are a frequent and costly repair. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the damaged pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use advanced detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.
