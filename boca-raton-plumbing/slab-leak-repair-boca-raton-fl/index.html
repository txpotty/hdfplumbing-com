    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Boca Raton, FL

    When a slab leak is localized and accessible, direct repair is often the most practical solution. This involves carefully jackhammering a small section of your foundation to expose the damaged pipe, then repairing or replacing the compromised section. We use durable materials designed to hold up to soil movement under the slab.

    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Boca Raton

    For more extensive damage, multiple leaks, or pipes that are too old and corroded to repair reliably, pipe rerouting is a long-term solution. This involves abandoning the leaking line under the slab and installing a new pipe through the walls or attic. This method bypasses the problem area entirely, providing a fresh, durable plumbing line. It is a common solution for aging homes in Boca Raton with original copper or galvanized plumbing.
