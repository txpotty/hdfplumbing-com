    Grease Trap Cleaning in Boca Raton

    A grease trap that smells sour is normally overdue for a pump-out.     Disposal Repair and Replacement in Boca Raton, FL

    A disposal that hums but will not spin is almost always jammed. We cut the power, work the flywheel loose with a 1/4 inch hex key from underneath, pull out the bottle cap or bone, and press the red reset button before testing it again. Leaks are a different story. A drip from the bottom of the housing means the internal seal failed, and that unit has to be replaced.

    Dishwasher and Ice Maker Lines in Boca Raton

    A dishwasher needs a hot supply with its own shutoff, a drain to the disposal or a wye tailpiece, and an air gap on the sink deck or a high loop fastened under the counter, depending on local code.     Frequently Asked Questions About Kitchen Plumbing in Boca Raton

    Repair covers jams, tripped resets, loose mounts and leaking sink flanges. Replacement is the answer when the motor burns out, the grinding chamber cracks, or water leaks from the bottom of the housing, because those parts are not sold separately. You will get a straight answer.

    Humming without grinding, water under the sink, a bad smell that cleaning does not fix, constant clogs or no sound at all. Check the reset button first. That red button sits on the bottom of the disposal, and if it keeps tripping the motor is likely overloaded or on its way out.

    Replacing a faucet or clearing a clog normally does not require a permit. Moving a sink, adding a new drain line or changing the waste and vent piping usually does, and the rules vary by city and county. We check Boca Raton rules ahead of the job.

    A jammed unit under eight years old is worth repairing. If it leaks from the housing or the motor is failing, replacement is the better money.
