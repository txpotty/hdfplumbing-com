    Pool Heater, Fire Pit and Grill Lines in St. Cloud, FL

    Backyard gas lines for pool heaters, fire pits and built-in grills face sun, weather and irrigation water that shorten their life. Pool heaters are the big load, often 250,000 to 400,000 BTU, so a line that was fine for a grill can starve the heater and the house appliances at the same time. Sizing is the first thing we check. New and repaired lines get tested, buried to the required depth and laid with tracer wire.

    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in St. Cloud

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, crack and leak with age. Furnaces, water heaters, ranges and dryers each need a sound connection, and most need a drip leg before the appliance valve.

    Propane Gas Line Service in St. Cloud, FL

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. Our work covers propane yard lines, regulator replacement and fitting repairs, plus a check that every appliance has its propane orifices installed.     Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in St. Cloud, FL

    A kitchen remodel or a new tankless water heater can push a gas system past what its original pipe can deliver. Tankless units need a lot of gas.     Gas Leak Detection in St. Cloud

    CSST Repair and Bonding in St. Cloud, FL

    Newer homes often have corrugated stainless steel tubing running through the attic. Damaged runs get spliced with the manufacturer's fittings, and we confirm the bond on the way out. After a major storm, homes on wells or septic should be checked before normal use, since floodwater can contaminate both systems.
