    Dependable Water Line Repair Services in St. Cloud, FL

    Main Water Line Leak Repair in St. Cloud

    Most main line leaks show up as a soggy strip of yard, a sudden jump in your water bill or a meter dial that moves with every fixture off. We confirm the leak by isolating the house shutoff and watching the meter. Then we use ground microphones to locate the exact spot. A single section is cut out and replaced with repair couplings or a new length of pipe, and the line is pressure tested before backfill.

    Emergency Water Line Break Repair in St. Cloud, FL

    A full break can flood a yard, wash out soil under walkways and leave your home without water. Our emergency crews shut off the supply, locate the break and set up a temporary bypass when the repair will take more than a few hours. Most emergency breaks are repaired the same day. We also document the damage with photos for your insurance or HOA.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in St. Cloud

    If your service line runs under a driveway, pool deck or mature landscaping, trenchless replacement can save a lot of restoration cost. We dig small pits at each end and pull new HDPE or PEX pipe through using a pneumatic boring tool or pipe pulling equipment. The process usually takes one day. You keep your pavers and trees, and the new line carries a lengthy manufacturer warranty.

    Water Line Repair at the Meter in St. Cloud, FL

    Leaks often start at the compression fittings and couplings right next to the meter box. Shifting soil and heavy traffic over the box can loosen those joints over time. We determine whether the leak is on the utility side or the customer side, since the water provider is in charge of the meter and the pipe before it. When the leak is yours, we replace the fittings and the customer shutoff valve on the same trip.

    Underground Water Line Leak Repair in St. Cloud

    Underground water line leaks are often hard to detect until serious damage has occurred. Common signs include sudden spikes in your water bill, damp spots in your yard, or a noticeable drop in water pressure. Our team uses acoustic leak detection and ground-penetrating radar to precisely locate these hidden leaks in St. Cloud, minimizing the need for extensive excavation. We repair breaks caused by shifting soil, tree root intrusion, or aging pipes, restoring your water service quickly.

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in St. Cloud, FL

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Many wells in Florida produce water with a sulfur smell or high iron, which often calls for filtration to protect fixtures and appliances. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which restricts flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe seldom holds up because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and stops repeat leaks.

    Underground Leak Detection in St. Cloud, FL

    Many water line leaks occur underground, making them difficult to detect without specialized equipment. Our technicians use modern acoustic and thermal imaging tools to accurately locate hidden leaks without unnecessary digging. Early detection saves you money on water bills and prevents expensive damage to your foundation.
