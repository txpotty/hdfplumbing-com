    Frequently Asked Questions About Underground Pipe Repair in St. Cloud

    Simple buried pipe repairs usually run $500 to $2,500. Sewer replacements cost more. Extensive trenchless sewer replacements can be $5,000 to $15,000 or more, depending on where the pipe runs, how bad the damage is and the method used to fix it. We quote after inspection.

    Acoustic ground microphones pick up the sound of escaping water. We also use tracer gas. Hydrogen-nitrogen gas pushed into the line rises through the soil at the leak, and electronic locators trace the exact pipe route so we know where to listen. Together they narrow it to a few feet.

    Wet spots or extra-green grass are the classic signs. So is a jump in the bill. A sudden drop in pressure, a sewage or sulfur smell in the yard, or gurgling drains may mean a broken pipe underground on the supply or drain side. Check the meter with everything off.

    Main water service and sewer replacements usually do. Spot repairs vary. The St. Cloud building department typically wants a permit for any major buried line replacement, which allows an inspection before backfill. The permit is our job.

    Most point repairs take a few hours to a day. Full lines run longer. A full service line replacement typically takes one to two days, depending on length, depth and how much of the route can be done with a pull-through method. We give you the timeline first.

    Every repair is warrantied. We write the warranty down. Our workmanship warranty covers the repair, while the parts carry separate manufacturer coverage. We explain it before we start.

    Our labor warranty is written into the quote. Length depends on the job. A spot repair and a full service replacement carry different coverage because the scope, materials and risks are different from one job to the next. Pipe makers add their own coverage.

    Many utilities offer a one-time leak adjustment. It is worth asking. Most want proof that the leak was repaired promptly, and we give you an invoice and repair documentation that shows the date, location and fix. Rules differ by provider.
