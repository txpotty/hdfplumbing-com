    Water Meter Leak Testing in St. Cloud

    The water meter is the first and easiest leak test. With every fixture and appliance off, a moving leak indicator means water is escaping somewhere. We read the flow rate to estimate how many gallons are being lost. Then we shut off zones one at a time to see which part of the property is losing water.

    Advanced Leak Detection Technology in St. Cloud

    HDF Plumbing invests in modern leak detection technology to provide the most accurate and non-invasive service possible in St. Cloud. This includes high-resolution thermal cameras for detecting temperature changes caused by hidden moisture, sensitive acoustic sensors that listen for the faint sounds of escaping water, and video pipe inspection cameras for internal pipe diagnostics. These tools allow us to find leaks with minimal disruption to your property.

    Hidden Wall & Ceiling Leak Detection in St. Cloud, FL

    Leaks behind walls or in ceilings can cause serious damage before they become visible, leading to stained drywall, peeling paint or a musty odor. These often stem from faulty pipe connections, old galvanized pipes or issues with shower pans and drains. Our technicians use moisture meters and infrared cameras to scan these areas, identifying elevated moisture levels and temperature differences that indicate a hidden leak, minimizing the need for extensive wall demolition.

    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in St. Cloud

    Leaks in underground water lines, from your meter to your home, can go unnoticed for extended periods, wasting water and causing yard erosion. Signs include unusually green patches in your lawn, persistent wet spots or a constantly running water meter. HDF Plumbing employs ground-penetrating radar and tracer gas detection methods to accurately map out and pinpoint leaks in buried pipes, allowing for targeted excavation and repair rather than digging up your entire yard.

    Leak Detection for PEX and Copper Pipes in St. Cloud

    Whether your plumbing system uses modern PEX tubing or traditional copper pipes, HDF Plumbing has the expertise to detect leaks in both materials. PEX, while flexible, can be vulnerable to damage from rodents or improper installation, while copper can corrode over time, especially with hard water. Our tools are effective across various pipe materials, ensuring accurate detection regardless of your home's plumbing type.

    Pool Water Leak Detection in St. Cloud, FL

    Pools lose a lot of water to evaporation in summer heat, so a leak can hide behind normal loss. We use a bucket test to compare evaporation with actual water loss. Then we pressure test pool plumbing and dye test fittings, lights and skimmers. Finding the leak safeguards the pool and the soil around it.

    Water Heater and Softener Leak Detection in St. Cloud

    Water heaters and softeners can leak at fittings, valves and drain lines. A softener stuck in regeneration can send water down the drain continuously, and a dripping relief valve on a water heater wastes water too. We check these units carefully and repair or replace failed parts. That often explains a mystery bill in homes that have water softeners.
