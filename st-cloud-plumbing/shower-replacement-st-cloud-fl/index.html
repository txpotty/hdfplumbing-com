    Frequently Asked Questions About Shower Replacement in St. Cloud

    Shower replacement in St. Cloud, FL runs from about $1,500 for a basic unit swap to over $10,000 for a custom tiled walk-in.     For most homes, Moen or Delta valves are hard to beat. Sterling and American Standard make solid units, and Kohler covers valves and bases well. We fit it to your budget.

    Our workmanship warranty covers the installation. Manufacturer warranties cover the valve, the fixtures and the unit itself, and we go over both in detail during your consultation. Keep your paperwork.

    Many wells in Florida produce water with a sulfur smell or high iron, which often calls for treatment to protect fixtures and appliances. Scale builds on heads, handles and glass doors over time.     Replacement keeps the footprint. A replacement stays in place, and a remodel can move walls and change the layout. Replacement costs less.

    Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common jobs. The tub comes out and a new pan, walls and fixtures go in, with the drain moved as needed. It is easier to use.

    Watch for leaks that return after repairs. Cracked pans or units, soft floors near the shower, mold that keeps coming back and old valves without scald protection are the other signs.
