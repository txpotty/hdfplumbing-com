    Tune-Up Versus Replacement in Titusville, FL

    A tune-up costs a fraction of a new heater. Installed tanks often run well over a thousand dollars, and regular flushing and anode replacement help a tank reach the high end of its rated lifespan, while tankless units can last many years with proper descaling. Sometimes replacement makes sense. You see both prices side by side.

    Anode Rod Checks in Titusville

    The anode rod rusts so the tank does not. It is a long metal rod screwed into the top of the tank, and once it has corroded down to bare wire, the water starts eating the steel tank lining instead. We pull it and look. Replacing the rod is much cheaper than replacing the heater.

    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Titusville

    Electric tanks fail at the lower element. Sediment buries the lower element until it overheats and fails, leaving you with a short supply of hot water. Both elements get tested. Thermostats and wiring get checked with the power off.

    Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in Titusville, FL

    Gas units need a clean burner and a good draft. Leak checks, flame inspection, burner cleaning and a draft test are part of every gas visit. CO is the risk. The thermocouple or flame sensor gets checked as well.

    Relief Valve and Safety Testing in Titusville, FL

    The relief valve is the tank's last line of defense. We lift the lever, confirm water discharges and the valve reseats without dripping, and check that the discharge pipe runs full size to an approved spot within a few inches of the floor or outside. We swap bad ones. We check the expansion tank too.

    Flushing a Tank Water Heater in Titusville

    Popping and rumbling mean sediment. Scale on the tank bottom traps heat and causes water to pop and rumble as it heats. The tank drains until the water is clean. Bad tanks need a few rounds.
