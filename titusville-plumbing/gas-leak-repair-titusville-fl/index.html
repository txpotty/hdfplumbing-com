    Residential Gas Leak Detection in Titusville

    A gas leak in a house rarely sits where the smell is strongest, because gas drifts along joists and collects in closets and basements. We start with a gauge on the system to confirm there is a leak at all. Then the sniffer comes out. A combustible gas detector follows the reading from fitting to fitting until it spikes. Bubble solution on the suspect joint gives the final answer.

    Commercial Gas Leak Services in Titusville

    Restaurant kitchens and small shops run more appliances on bigger pipe, often 1-1/4 inch or larger, fed from a meter with its own regulator. When the leak is small enough, we plan the shutdown for after close. Fryers on quick-disconnect hoses fail often. We test every hose and manifold joint and leave tags on anything we had to shut off. New pipe still needs the commercial sign-off.

    Gas Line Replacement and Upgrades in Titusville, FL

    Adding a gas range or a tankless heater can overload pipe that was sized years ago for just a furnace and a water heater.     Gas Meter to House Line Repair in Titusville, FL

    The run from the utility meter to the house shutoff is the homeowner's pipe in most service areas, so the repair falls to a plumber and not the gas company. That stretch sees more weather than any other pipe in the system. Risers rust at grade. We replace the riser or the buried run, pressure test it and leave the meter connection ready for the utility to open the meter. Expect a half day for most riser swap.

    Propane Gas Leak Repair in Titusville, FL

    Propane behaves differently from natural gas because it is heavier than air, so a leak settles into basements and crawlspaces instead of rising. We check both regulators, the one at the tank and the one at the house. Low spots get sniffed first.     Outdoor Gas Line Repair in Titusville

The outdoor branch gets isolated and tested by itself, then the bad run is replaced with polyethylene and a tracer. Above-ground stub-ups get a new riser and a shutoff valve.

    Red Tag Gas Repairs in Titusville

    A red tag on your meter or appliance means the utility found a leak or unsafe condition and will not restore gas until a licensed contractor fixes it.
