    Frequently Asked Questions About Garbage Disposal Installation in Titusville

    The cost for garbage disposal installation in Titusville, FL usually ranges from $200 to $500, not including the unit itself. This price can vary based on the complexity of the installation, such as needing new wiring or plumbing adjustments, and the type of disposal. We provide clear, upfront pricing before any work begins.

    HDF Plumbing installs all major garbage disposal brands, including InSinkErator, Waste King, Moen, Whirlaway, and KitchenAid. Our plumbers are familiar with the specific installation requirements for each manufacturer and can help you choose the best unit for your needs.

    Most installs take one to two hours. Adding electrical or reworking drains can take a bit longer.

    For light use or single-person households, a 1/3 or 1/2 horsepower unit might suffice. For average families, a 3/4 horsepower unit is a good balance of power and cost. Large families or those who cook frequently might benefit from a 1 horsepower disposal to handle more food waste effectively without jamming.

    To maintain your new garbage disposal, always run cold water before, during, and after grinding food waste. Avoid putting hard items like bones, fibrous vegetables, or grease down the drain. Occasionally grind citrus peels or ice cubes to help clean the blades and freshen the unit.

    Continuous feed disposals operate with a wall switch and can run while you're adding food waste. Batch feed disposals operate only when a special stopper is inserted, making them a popular choice for safety reasons, especially in homes with young children. We install both types, catering to your preference.

    While some homeowners attempt DIY installation, it's usually recommended to hire a professional. Improper installation can lead to leaks, electrical hazards, and damage to your plumbing system. Our licensed plumbers ensure a safe, code-compliant, and reliable installation.

    Most standard kitchen sinks are compatible with garbage disposals, provided they have a drain opening of 3 1/2 to 4 inches. Some sinks, particularly older cast iron models or those with unusual drain configurations, may require adapters or modifications. Our plumbers can assess your sink's compatibility.
