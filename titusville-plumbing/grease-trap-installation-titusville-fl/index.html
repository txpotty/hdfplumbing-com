    Grease Trap Sizing and Placement in Titusville, FL

    For indoor traps we lean on Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura, because their parts are easy to find and their units carry PDI or ASME certification.     As close to the grease-producing fixtures as the space allows, so grease does not cool and coat the pipe before it reaches the trap.     An under-sink hydromechanical trap with good access is usually a half-day job, including the flow control and a leak test.     The common rule is to clean before grease and solids fill 25 percent of the trap's depth. Busy kitchens with small indoor traps often hit that mark every week. Outdoor tanks go longer.
