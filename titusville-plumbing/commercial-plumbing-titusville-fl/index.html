    Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Titusville

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. A high water table across much of Florida affects drainage, septic design, and how easily crews can dig for underground repairs.
