    Burner Cleaning and Repair in Titusville, FL

    Venting and Draft Repair in Titusville, FL

    Atmospheric tanks rely on hot exhaust rising through a metal flue with an upward slope of at least a quarter inch per foot.     A thermocouple is a small copper-sheathed rod that sits in the pilot flame and makes a tiny electric current. The current keeps a magnet in the gas valve pulled open. No flame, no current. When the pilot goes out, the magnet lets go and the valve shuts off gas to both the pilot and the burner, which is the safety that keeps raw gas from filling a closet. Weak thermocouples cause more pilot outages than anything else.
