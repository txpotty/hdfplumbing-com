    PEX resists scale and corrosion, flexes with temperature changes and has far fewer joints. Copper is very durable but prone to pinhole leaks in some water conditions and costs more to install.

    PEX piping is designed to be highly durable and has an estimated lifespan of 50 years or more. Its resistance to corrosion, scaling and freeze damage adds to its long-term reliability, making it a sound investment for your Titusville home.

    PEX can be used for outdoor applications like irrigation lines or outdoor spigots, but it needs to be protected from direct UV light, which can degrade the material over time. When used outdoors, PEX should consistently be buried or otherwise shielded.

    PEX offers several advantages over copper, including lower material and installation costs, greater flexibility and resistance to corrosion and freezing. Unlike copper, PEX does not form pinhole leaks over time due to water chemistry, making it a more durable choice for many Titusville homes. It also cuts down on water hammer noise.
