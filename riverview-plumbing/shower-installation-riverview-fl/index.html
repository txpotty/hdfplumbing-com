    Shower Valve and Fixture Installation in Riverview, FL

    Pressure-balancing valves are the baseline for any new shower. Thermostatic valves hold a set temperature and add volume control, which works well with rain heads and handhelds. Either one protects against scalding. The outlet count usually decides it.

    Custom Shower System Installation in Riverview, FL

    Multi-head showers depend on valve capacity. One head works fine off a standard valve, while multiple outlets need a thermostatic valve, volume controls and larger 3/4-inch supply lines. Layout comes first. Then we check whether the water heater can keep up.

    Setting a tub is mostly about level and support. Acrylic and steel tubs get mortar or foam underneath, while cast iron rests on a level ledger, which keeps the caulk line intact. The valve goes in next. We set the spout and head heights to your layout and test the overflow gasket with water.
