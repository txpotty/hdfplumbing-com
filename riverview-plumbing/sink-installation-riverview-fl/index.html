    Wall-Mount and ADA Sink Installation in Riverview, FL

    Wall-hung lavatories need a carrier or heavy blocking. ADA layouts call for a rim at or under 34 inches, open knee space, and insulated pipes below. Offset drains help with knee space. Lever or sensor faucets finish it off.

    Vessel Sink Installation in Riverview, FL

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. Since the rim sits higher, the faucet has to be taller or wall-mounted, and the drain must match whether the bowl has an overflow. No overflow means no slotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.

    Drop-In Sink Installation in Riverview, FL

    Drop-in sinks are the most forgiving style. The rim hides small cutout flaws, so laminate tops take a drop-in with just a size check and the right sealant. Mounting clips snug it down. Stainless drop-ins weigh very little.

    Double Vanity Sink Installation in Riverview

    A second bowl means a second drain and two more supplies. Each bowl drains through its own trap or through a continuous waste to a shared trap, and the second faucet gets new stops. Vent sizing gets checked. Both bowls should drain without gurgling.

    Leak-Proof Sink Connections in Riverview, FL

    Every joint gets tested before we leave. We fill the bowl to the overflow, pull the stopper, and watch every nut under the cabinet at full flow with a flashlight and a dry paper towel. A towel shows a weep fast. Pipe dope goes on threads, not compression fittings.

    Undermount Sink Installation in Riverview

    Undermount sinks hang from the countertop. The bowl hangs on clips with a full bead of silicone, and it stays braced overnight while the adhesive cures. Glue alone is not enough. Plumbing goes on the next day.

    Kitchen Sink Replacement in Riverview

    Pulling the old kitchen sink is half the job. Strainers are often fused on, the disposal has to come off, and the dishwasher hose needs a fresh high loop. After that, the new bowl goes in. We reuse the disposal if it still runs well.

    Faucet and Drain Assembly for New Sinks in Riverview, FL

    Faucet and drain go on before the sink is set, when possible. Mounting the faucet and the pop-up on a sink that is still on sawhorses takes a fraction of the time and gets a tighter seal than working upside down inside the cabinet. Undermounts are the exception. Those get plumbed from below.
