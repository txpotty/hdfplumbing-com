    We check the water heater's age by serial number, look for rust, leaks and sediment and test the relief valve. Venting and gas connections get checked.     Sewer Camera Inspection in Riverview, FL

    Many older homes have clay, cast iron or Orangeburg sewer lines that can crack, sag or fill with roots. The camera starts at a cleanout.     Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Riverview, FL

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. High pressure ages fixtures fast. Anything over 80 psi cuts the life of hoses, fixtures and heaters, and we note the supply pipe type, from copper and CPVC to poly, Kitec and galvanized.

    Gas Connection Safety Check in Riverview, FL

    During a full inspection, we look at the visible gas piping and the connectors to each appliance. Most of our gas findings are in older homes, often at original appliance connectors.

    Preventive Maintenance Checks in Riverview, FL

    A maintenance check is a lighter version of a full inspection for owners who want to stay ahead of problems. We flush the water heater, exercise the main shutoff and angle stops, check pressure and look for slow drains and weeping fittings. It takes about an hour. Yearly or every-other-year checks stretch heater life and keep valves from seizing.

    Water Heater Safety Inspection in Riverview

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. Our checks include the relief valve and its discharge line, the expansion tank and the drain pan. We check gas units for draft. Backdrafting heaters can push combustion gases indoors, which is why we test the vent and report problems before we leave.

    Leak Detection and Slab Screening in Riverview

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. Warm spots are another clue.
