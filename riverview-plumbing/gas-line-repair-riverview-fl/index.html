    Gas Appliance Connector Repair in Riverview, FL

    Permits and Code Compliance for Gas Work in Riverview, FL

    Most gas piping work beyond a simple connector swap needs a permit in Riverview, FL.     Buried Yard Line Repair and Replacement in Riverview, FL

    Many older homes have steel gas lines running from the meter to the house or out to a pool heater. The wrap fails eventually. The new line is polyethylene with tracer wire, or coated steel where it has to be exposed, and we backfill and clean up the yard when the test passes.

    Natural Gas Leak Detection and Repair in Riverview

    Finding a gas leak starts at the meter. With every appliance off, a moving test dial on the meter tells us gas is escaping somewhere, and then we isolate branches to narrow it down. An electronic detector does the rest. Soap solution at the suspect fitting confirms it with bubbles, and then we repair the joint, replace the section or swap the connector before testing again.
