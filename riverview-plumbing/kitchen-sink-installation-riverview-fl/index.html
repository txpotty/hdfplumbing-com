    How We Install Kitchen Sinks in Riverview, FL: Style by Style

    Farmhouse Sink Installation in Riverview, FL

    A fireclay apron sink sits on a frame inside the cabinet, not on the countertop. We build that frame from 2x4s or a steel bracket, set the sink dead level and leave the right reveal for the stone shop. Drain placement is planned early. Many farmhouse sinks drain at the back, and the trap has to work around that.

    Undermount Sink Installation in Riverview, FL

    Undermounts hang from clips anchored into the underside of the stone, plus a bead of silicone along the rim. We support the bowl from below with a brace while the silicone cures, typically for a full day, before loading it with water. Skipping the cure time leads to sagging. A sink that drops even an eighth of an inch opens a gap where water and crumbs collect along the counter edge.

    Workstation Sink Installation in Riverview, FL

    Workstation sinks have a ledge for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks, and most are single deep bowls with an offset drain. The trap layout follows the offset drain, which lets the disposal fit and leaves storage room on one side. Check the faucet reach as well.

    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Riverview

    An undermount depends on its clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. We mount the bowl, seal the rim with silicone and brace it while the adhesive sets, which usually takes overnight before the sink can hold a full load of water. Then come the strainers and trap.

    Sink and Disposal Installed Together in Riverview, FL

    Adding a disposal costs least when the sink is already out. The disposal mounts to the new flange, the trap is set to its outlet height and the dishwasher plug gets knocked out for the hose. Power is a separate question. There needs to be a switched outlet under the sink.

    Restaurant Sink Installation in Riverview

    Restaurants, cafes and commissary kitchens in Riverview get their commercial sinks installed by HDF Plumbing. Hand sinks need hot and cold. Three-compartment, prep and hand sinks go in with the floor sinks, indirect drains and grease trap tie-ins the inspector checks.

    Top-mount Sink Installation in Riverview

    Drop-in sinks are common in Florida homes with laminate or tile counters. The rim holds the sink up. We check the cutout, run a bead of silicone under the lip and tighten the mounting clips evenly around the bowl so the rim seats flat. Next we connect and test the faucet and drain.
