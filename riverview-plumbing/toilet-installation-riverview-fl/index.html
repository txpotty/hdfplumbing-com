    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Riverview, FL

    High-efficiency toilets flush on 1.28 gallons or less, against 3.5 gallons or more for toilets from before the early 1990s. Check for the WaterSense label. A MaP rating of 800 grams or better is what we look for, since a weak low-flow toilet that needs double flushing saves no water at all. Dual-flush models add a lighter half flush.

    Wall-Mounted Toilet Installation in Riverview

    Wall-mounted toilets hang off a steel carrier bolted to the floor inside the wall. An access plate hides the tank. Because the carrier carries all the weight, we anchor its feet to the slab or framing first and check it for plumb before the drywall or tile goes up. Plan on a remodel, not a swap.

    Pressure-Assisted Toilet Installation in Riverview

    Pressure-assisted toilets have a sealed vessel inside the tank that uses line pressure to push the flush. They need at least 20 psi to work right. We check static pressure at a hose bib before recommending one, and we warn customers about the noise, since the flush is loud enough to hear across the house. They rarely clog.

    Toilet Replacement and Upgrades in Riverview, FL

    Swapping an old 3.5 gallon toilet for a modern one is the easiest water savings in the house. The old unit comes out in about twenty minutes. The rest is flange cleanup and shimming the new bowl level on a floor that has settled with age. We haul the old one away.

    Commercial Flushometer Toilets in Riverview

    Sloan and Zurn flushometer valves are the standard on commercial toilets for a reason. They recover instantly between flushes. Manual and sensor valves go on both floor-mount and wall-hung bowls, with the control stop adjusted so the flush is full but does not splash. Sensor valves cut down on touch points.

    Comfort-Height and ADA Toilets in Riverview

    Aging in place usually starts in the bathroom. A taller toilet and a grab bar go a long way. If the drywall is already off, blocking gets added between studs so a grab bar can screw into wood, not hollow-wall anchors. We also center the bowl to ADA spacing.

    Toilet Brands We Install in Riverview

    We install Kohler, TOTO, American Standard, Gerber and most other brands sold at supply houses and home centers. Every brand has its quirks. Some one-piece TOTO models mount with a Unifit adapter, and certain Kohler toilets use a tank-to-bowl gasket that most hardware stores do not carry, so we stock both. We keep those parts on hand.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in Riverview, FL

    Wall-hung toilets need a wall at least 2 by 6 deep, or a furred-out chase built in front of an existing 2 by 4 wall. The floor underneath stays open. That makes mopping easier and lets you set the bowl height anywhere within the carrier's range, which is handy for tall households or anyone who wants a comfort-height seat without a tall bowl. Service happens through the flush plate.
