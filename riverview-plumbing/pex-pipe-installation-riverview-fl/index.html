    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Riverview

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the distinct advantages of each. Our experts in Riverview can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.

    Copper to PEX Conversions in Riverview

    Copper lines can develop pinhole leaks from water chemistry, high velocity and sometimes soil contact under slabs. Many wells in Florida produce water with a sulfur smell or high iron, which often calls for treatment to protect fixtures and appliances. Once a home has had several pinhole leaks, more often follow. Converting to PEX stops the cycle and reduces the risk of hidden water damage. We use approved transition fittings wherever new PEX connects to existing copper stubs or valves.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Riverview

    PEX-B is stiffer and more affordable, and it is joined with copper crimp rings or stainless steel cinch clamps over brass or poly fittings.
