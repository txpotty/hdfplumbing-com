    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in Riverview

    Hydromechanical grease traps sit under or near sinks and use baffles and flow control to separate grease from water. They are common in small cafes, coffee shops and quick-service restaurants with tight space. We install units from Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura sized by gallons per minute. Each install includes a flow control fitting and proper venting to keep the trap working as designed. These units need frequent cleaning, so we place them where staff can reach the lid easily.

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in Riverview

    Hydro-mechanical grease interceptors, often called HGI units, are compact and designed for indoor use, usually under a sink or on the floor. These units use baffles to slow down wastewater flow, allowing grease to separate and accumulate. HDF Plumbing expertly installs various sizes of HGI units, ensuring proper venting, leveling, and connection to your drainage system in Riverview for the best efficiency.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Grease Trap Installation in Riverview

    The cost of grease trap installation in Riverview, FL varies significantly based on the size, type, and complexity of the system. Small under-sink units might range from $1,000 to $3,000, while large outdoor interceptors requiring excavation can cost anywhere from $5,000 to $20,000 or more. We provide transparent, upfront pricing after a thorough on-site assessment.

    The cleaning frequency for a new grease trap depends on its size, the volume of FOG produced by your kitchen, and local regulations in Riverview. Typically, grease traps require cleaning every 1 to 3 months. Regular cleaning is essential to maintain efficiency and prevent backups.

    Signs you might need a new grease trap include frequent drain clogs, slow drainage in multiple fixtures, persistent foul odors coming from drains, or if your current unit is old, corroded, or no longer meeting local code requirements. An inspection by a professional plumber can confirm the need for replacement.

    The primary purpose of a grease trap is to intercept fats, oils, and greases (FOG) from commercial kitchen wastewater before they enter the public sewer system. This prevents pipe blockages, protects municipal infrastructure, and helps businesses comply with environmental regulations.

    We install all common types of grease traps, including gravity grease interceptors, hydro-mechanical grease traps (automatic grease removal units), and compact under-sink grease traps. Our team helps you determine the best system for your commercial kitchen's specific needs and local code requirements.

    Most food service businesses that cook, wash dishes or prepare food with fats, oils or grease are required to have one. Local pretreatment programs set the specific rules, and we can help you confirm what applies to your site.

    The timeline for grease trap installation depends on the project's scope. A simple under-sink unit might take a few hours. Larger outdoor gravity interceptors, which involve excavation and more extensive plumbing, can take anywhere from one to several days. We always aim to limit disruption to your business operations.
