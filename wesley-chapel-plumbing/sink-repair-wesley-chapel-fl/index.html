    Bathroom Sink Repair in Wesley Chapel, FL

    Bath sinks have small parts that wear. The pop-up linkage sits in a damp cabinet for years, and the pivot nut where the rod enters the drain is the most common leak we find on a bathroom sink. Pedestal and wall-hung sinks get the same checks. Vanity sinks just hide the leak better.

    Garbage Disposal Repair in Wesley Chapel

    A disposal that hums but will not spin is usually jammed.     Loose or Wobbly Sink Repair in Wesley Chapel

    A sink that moves when you lean on it is pulling on the drain. Pedestals get blocking behind the wall when needed.

    Slow or Clogged Sink Drain Repair in Wesley Chapel, FL

    Slow drains almost always clog at the trap or the first few feet past the wall. Hydro-jetting is for main lines, not sink arms.

    Low Water Pressure at the Sink in Wesley Chapel, FL

    Unscrew the aerator before anything else. A clogged aerator screen causes most low flow at a single faucet, and if cleaning it does not help we check the cartridge and the angle stop for debris. Whole-house low pressure is another call. We test the pressure regulator in that case.

    Recurring Sink Clog Repair in Wesley Chapel, FL

    Monthly clogs have a cause past the trap. We look past the wall with a longer cable or a camera for a sag or for buildup where the sink line meets the tub. Sometimes the vent is blocked. A sink that gurgles as it drains is the giveaway.

    The trap holds a plug of water that keeps sewer gas out of the room. Chrome traps that have rusted thin get swapped for PVC or brass.
