    Gas Line Repair & Replacement in Wesley Chapel, FL

    Black iron pipe with threaded fittings is still the most common interior gas pipe, and the leak is nearly always at a thread, not in the middle of a length.     Interior Gas Pipe Repair in Wesley Chapel, FL

    Inside the house, gas leaks hide at unions in the basement and at the threaded nipple behind the furnace, places nobody looks. We work from the meter inward, testing each section as we go.     Corrugated Stainless Steel Tubing Repair in Wesley Chapel, FL

    CSST is the yellow-jacketed flexible tubing used in many newer homes, and it can be damaged by a nearby lightning strike if it was never bonded. Bonding means a 6 AWG copper wire from the gas piping to the electrical grounding system. Plenty of older installs lack it. We repair CSST only with the matching brand's fittings, because TracPipe and Gastite parts do not interchange. A splice that skips that rule fails the inspection.

    Underground Gas Line Repair in Wesley Chapel

    Yard lines run from the meter to the house, and often on to a detached garage, a pool heater, a grill or an outdoor kitchen. We call 811 for locates, dig at the suspected spot and replace the bad run with yellow polyethylene pipe and tracer wire.
