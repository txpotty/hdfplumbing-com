    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Wesley Chapel, FL

    Interior drain tile handles water that already got through the wall. It just stops puddling on your floor. This route costs less than digging outside and makes sense when a patio or driveway sits against the foundation.

    Basement Wall Sealing in Wesley Chapel, FL

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Salt air in coastal parts of Florida corrodes outdoor fittings and exposed metal pipe faster than in inland areas. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then seal the face with a masonry waterproofer that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. Most homeowners notice the musty smell is gone within a few weeks. Those weep holes empty into the interior drain if the house has one.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Wesley Chapel

    Tile and grout are not waterproof. Water passes through grout every time you shower, so the layer behind the tile, a membrane over cement board or a foam board system, is what actually keeps the studs dry. We tear out failed tile, install new backer, and apply Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban at the thickness the manufacturer calls for. Corners, niches and valve holes get extra fabric or preformed pieces.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Wesley Chapel, FL

The new pan gets its own flood test before tile.
