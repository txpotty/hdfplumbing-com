    Most standard toilet repairs, such as replacing a flapper, fill valve, or wax ring, can be completed within one to two hours by an skilled plumber. More complex issues like clearing a deep clog or diagnosing intermittent problems might take a bit longer, but we aim for efficiency.

    Most standard toilet repairs, such as fixing a running toilet or replacing internal tank components, can be completed within 1 to 2 hours. More complex issues like clearing a tough main line clog or replacing a wax ring might take longer, potentially 2 to 4 hours.
