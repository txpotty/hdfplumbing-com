    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Repair in Wesley Chapel

    A single spot repair typically runs $1,500 to $4,500 depending on depth and access. Trenchless lining usually costs $80 to $250 per foot, and full replacement of a typical residential line ranges from $5,000 to $15,000. We give you a written quote after the camera inspection.

    Many cities require a permit for sewer line replacement and for any work in the public right-of-way. Small repairs on private property may not, depending on local rules. We check with your city and pull the permit when it is required.

    The duration of a sewer repair depends heavily on the scope of work. Simple spot repairs or hydro-jetting might take a few hours. Trenchless repairs like pipe lining can often be completed in one to two days. Full sewer line replacements involving excavation can take anywhere from two to five days, depending on the length and complexity of the line.

    Trenchless sewer repair is a method of repairing or replacing sewer lines with minimal digging, often requiring only one or two access points. It involves techniques like pipe bursting, where a new pipe is pulled through the old one, or pipe lining, where a resin-impregnated liner is inserted and cured inside the existing pipe. This preserves your landscaping and is less disruptive than traditional methods.

    For sewer line replacements, we mainly use durable, code-compliant materials such as PVC for its longevity and resistance to corrosion, or HDPE for trenchless applications. We select the best material based on your needs and local soil conditions.

    We use Schedule 40 PVC or ABS that meets local code, joined with approved fittings and shielded no-hub couplings at transitions. For lining, we use epoxy-saturated liners rated for many years of service.

    Standard policies often exclude wear, tear and root damage to the sewer line itself, though they may cover resulting water damage inside the home. Some insurers offer a service line endorsement. We provide video and written documentation if you file a claim.

    Most spot repairs are completed in one day, including excavation and backfill. Larger replacements or jobs that need a city right-of-way permit can take two to four days. We give you a timeline before we begin.
