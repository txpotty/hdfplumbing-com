    Water Heater Maintenance in Kendale Lakes

    Sediment is the tank's slow killer. Around Kendale Lakes, flushing the water heater yearly and descaling tankless units helps keep scale buildup under control.     Softener Upkeep in Kendale Lakes, FL

    Softeners fail quietly. A unit that stopped regenerating or ran out of salt keeps sending water to the house, it just is not soft anymore, and you will not notice until scale shows up on the faucets and the water heater starts popping. Bypass and drain get tested. We measure hardness after.

    Gas Connector and Shutoff Checks in Kendale Lakes

    Gas feeds water heaters, furnaces, ranges and dryers. Visible piping and connections get leak tested, shutoffs get turned, and old flex connectors get replaced. Repairs follow local code. If you smell gas, get outside before calling the gas company.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Kendale Lakes

    Tankless units need descaling. Scale builds up inside the heat exchanger, especially with hard water, and the unit starts throwing error codes, running lukewarm or cutting out mid-shower long before it actually wears out. We pump descaler through the service valves. We also clean the inlet screen and check venting and gas supply.

    Sewer and Drain Camera Inspection in Kendale Lakes, FL

    A camera shows what the sewer looks like inside.     Water Heater Flush and Inspection in Kendale Lakes

    Popping or rumbling means sediment. Minerals settle on the tank bottom, trap heat and cause the popping you hear when it fires. Flushing clears it. Tankless units get a descaling flush through the service valves with a pump and descaler. Records support warranty claims.
