    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Kendale Lakes, FL

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat expands and contracts the pipe against concrete and gravel. Mineral scale and constant recirculation can wear the pipe wall faster. We repair the leak or reroute the hot line, and we can adjust recirculation pumps to limit wear. Warm floors and higher gas or electric bills are common signs.

    PEX Repiping for Slab Leaks in Kendale Lakes, FL

    If multiple slab leaks point to a systemic issue with your home's original plumbing, a full PEX repipe might be the most cost-effective and durable solution. PEX tubing is flexible, corrosion-resistant, and less prone to leaks caused by foundation shifts, making it a strong choice for many homes. We can replace all the water supply lines in your Kendale Lakes home, providing a brand-new, leak-free plumbing system that will last for decades.

    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Kendale Lakes, FL

    When the leak is isolated and the rest of the pipe is in good shape, a spot repair through the slab is a sensible option. We cut a small opening, excavate to the pipe and replace the damaged section. The trench is compacted and the concrete is patched. Flooring is then restored by your preferred contractor.

    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Kendale Lakes, FL

    When a slab leak is localized and accessible, direct repair is often the most efficient solution. This involves carefully jackhammering a small section of your foundation to expose the damaged pipe, then repairing or replacing the compromised section. Our technicians are skilled in precise concrete cutting and restoration, so the repair is effective and the area is professionally restored. We use durable materials designed to hold up to soil movement under the slab.

    Tunneling Slab Leak Repair in Kendale Lakes, FL

    In some cases, especially when the leak is under a load-bearing wall or in a hard-to-reach area, tunneling beneath the foundation is the preferred method. This allows our plumbers to reach the damaged pipe from outside your home, avoiding disruption to your interior floors and finishes. Tunneling spares the interior but requires skilled excavation and shoring to protect structural integrity throughout the repair process in Kendale Lakes, FL.

    Emergency Slab Leak Repair in Kendale Lakes, FL

    A large slab leak can push water up through flooring or into walls quickly. We help you shut off the water and send a technician to isolate the leaking line. Often we can restore water to the rest of the house while we plan the repair. Prompt action limits flooring damage and soil saturation.

    Electronic Slab Leak Detection in Kendale Lakes

    Locating a hidden slab leak takes specialized tools. This precision detection reduces the need for extensive concrete removal, saving you time and money. We can detect leaks in both hot and cold water lines, as well as sewer lines, for a complete diagnosis of your Kendale Lakes home.

    Sewer Line Slab Leak Repair in Kendale Lakes, FL
