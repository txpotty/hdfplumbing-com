    Galvanized Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Kendale Lakes, FL

    Older homes may still have galvanized steel pipe that rusts from the inside out. Low pressure, brown water and leaks at threaded joints are the typical signs. We remove or abandon the galvanized lines and replace them with PEX or copper, and your water pressure and water quality usually improve the same day the new lines go into service.

    PEX Repiping in Kendale Lakes, FL

    PEX is flexible plastic tubing that can run long distances without a fitting, which means fewer joints hidden in your walls. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. PEX shrugs off scale and chlorine, survives freezing better than copper and installs faster, so it is our most common repipe material. Our PEX-A installs use expansion fittings that do not shrink the flow path. That keeps pressure up at the far fixtures.

    PEX Versus Copper Repipe Pricing in Kendale Lakes, FL

    PEX repipes usually cost less than copper because the material is cheaper and installation goes faster. Copper costs more. For a typical house, copper often runs 20 to 40 percent above PEX, though some buyers and homeowners like the look and feel of metal pipe and are willing to pay for it. Both prices include permits, fittings, patching and cleanup.

    Slab Leak Repiping in Kendale Lakes

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and crack buried water and sewer lines. Rather than jackhammer to reach one leak, we run new lines through the attic and walls and leave the old under-slab pipe out of service. No jackhammer needed. It ends this leak and takes the rest of the slab plumbing out of the picture.

    Type L Copper Repiping in Kendale Lakes

    Copper has been plumbing homes for generations, and done right it lasts a long time. We use Type L, not the thinner Type M, and we support it with the right hangers so it does not rub or rattle in the walls. All joints use lead-free solder. Copper can pinhole in aggressive water, so we ask about your water and any past leaks before recommending it over PEX.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Kendale Lakes

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water and long attic runs. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open the pipe end and let it shrink back over the fitting for a full-bore joint. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. Most homes are repiped with PEX-A in two to three days.
