    What Goes Into a PEX Install in Kendale Lakes, FL

    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Kendale Lakes, FL

    Gray polybutylene went into millions of homes from the late 1970s to the mid 1990s. Chlorine in treated water attacks the plastic and the acetal fittings from the inside, so it can look fine from outside right up to the day a joint lets go. Insurers ask about it. Swapping it for PEX can help with insurance and with resale.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Kendale Lakes, FL

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A bends the easiest and uses expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and uses crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C handles more like B. Every one is listed for potable water. We match the pipe to the fittings and tools from one maker so the warranty stays intact.

    Copper to PEX Conversions in Kendale Lakes

    Pinhole leaks in copper tend to come in clusters. Water chemistry, high flow velocity and contact with soil under a slab can all pit the pipe wall from the inside, and once a few pinholes appear, more are usually on the way. Around Kendale Lakes, flushing the water heater yearly and descaling tankless units helps keep scale buildup under control. Converting to PEX stops the cycle. Where new PEX meets existing copper stubs or valves, we use listed transition fittings.

    PEX Main Water Line Installation in Kendale Lakes

    The main from the meter to the house feeds every fixture you own. A leaking old service line shows up as a soggy patch in the yard, a higher water bill and weak pressure everywhere. We trench and lay new PEX. At the foundation we install a new full-port ball valve as the main shutoff.

    Uponor PEX System Installation in Kendale Lakes

    Uponor's ProPEX system is PEX-A joined with an expansion tool plus a ring slipped over the pipe end. We stretch the pipe and ring, seat the fitting, and within seconds the plastic shrinks back around the barb into a tight seal. There is no crimp gauge to check. The fittings have a wide bore, so flow loss through each joint stays low.

    Emergency PEX Pipe Repair & Installation in Kendale Lakes, FL

    PEX rarely fails on its own. Most failures trace back to a nail or screw through the pipe, a rodent working on an attic run, or a fitting that was not crimped fully. Our trucks stock half-inch to one-inch pipe and fittings. Most emergency PEX repairs in Kendale Lakes, FL are a cut-and-splice job that takes under an hour once the drywall is open.

    PEX Repipe Cost Comparison in Kendale Lakes, FL

    Price on a repipe depends on a short list: bathroom count, square footage, how many stories, and how easy the attic and walls are to work in. PEX almost always comes in below copper because the material is cheaper and a crew can run a house in two to four days instead of a week. Patching is sometimes quoted on its own line. Ask and we will price all three, PEX-A, PEX-B and copper, side by side.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Kendale Lakes

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and the honest answer depends on the job. PEX-A makes tighter bends, can be heat-fixed if it kinks, and its fittings flow better, while PEX-B is cheaper and its crimps hold just as well when each one is gauged. Either one lasts for decades. For tight attics we lean toward PEX-A.
