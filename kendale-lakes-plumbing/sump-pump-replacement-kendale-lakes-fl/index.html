    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Replacement in Kendale Lakes

    The cost of sump pump replacement in Kendale Lakes, FL typically ranges from $500 to $1,500, depending on the type of pump, submersible or pedestal, its horsepower and any necessary pit changes. Battery backup systems will add to this cost, usually between $300 and $800. We provide a written, upfront quote after assessing your needs.

    We install Zoeller, Liberty and Wayne pumps. Cast iron models are our pick for durability.

    To maintain your new sump pump, regularly check the pit for debris, test the pump monthly by pouring water into the pit, and make sure the discharge pipe is clear. An annual professional inspection by HDF Plumbing can also help extend its lifespan.

    Constant running, loud grinding, frequent cycling or failure to start are common signs. Rust, age and a burning smell also indicate trouble.

    While it might seem like a simple task, sump pump replacement involves electrical connections, proper plumbing and making sure the pump is correctly sized and installed to prevent future flooding. DIY replacement can lead to improper operation, electrical hazards or system failure. It is best to hire a professional plumber for a safe and effective installation.

    In many areas, minor like-for-like sump pump replacements typically do not require a permit. However, if the replacement involves major changes to the electrical system, discharge piping or the sump pit itself, a permit might be necessary. Our team will advise you on local code requirements in Kendale Lakes.

    It is recommended to test your sump pump at least every three months, and especially before and after rainy seasons. To test, simply pour a bucket of water into the sump pit. The pump should activate, drain the water and then shut off automatically. If it does not, it is time for a professional's inspection.

    Most standard sump pump replacements can be completed within 2 to 4 hours. More complex installations, such as those requiring new pit excavation or extensive discharge line work, might take longer, up to a full day.
