    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Kendale Lakes, FL

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. That makes service easy. They are louder than submersibles. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Kendale Lakes, FL

    Two pumps on one riser cover both kinds of failure, a dead motor and a dead outlet. The primary handles daily water. Finished basements are the usual reason. We install them in homes where a flood would ruin flooring and drywall.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Kendale Lakes

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi monitors go further and send a phone alert when the pump runs too long, the power drops or the water climbs past a set level. Landlords like them. We add them to new installs and to pumps already in place.

    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in Kendale Lakes

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume. For those jobs we install duplex systems where two pumps alternate, with a control panel and a high water alarm that can tie into the building monitoring. Downtime costs money. Work is scheduled around your operating hours.

    Sump Pump Replacement in Kendale Lakes, FL

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. We pull the old pump and inspect the pit. If the old one could not keep up, we size up. Testing is done with a full pit.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost in Kendale Lakes, FL

    Price depends mostly on whether a pit already exists. Swapping a pump in an existing pit is the cheapest job, while a new pit means saw cutting, digging, gravel and a concrete patch, which roughly doubles the labor. Backups add cost. We list each option on a written quote.

    Crawl Space Sump Pump Installation in Kendale Lakes, FL

    Crawl spaces with standing water breed mold and rot sill plates within a few seasons. We set a basin at the lowest point, run perforated drain pipe to it where needed and seal the vapor barrier around the lid so the space stays dry. Access is often tight. We route the discharge out through the rim joist and away from the foundation.
