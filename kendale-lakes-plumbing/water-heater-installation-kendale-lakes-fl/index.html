    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Kendale Lakes

    Closed plumbing systems trap the extra volume water gains when it heats. A small expansion tank on the cold line absorbs that extra volume, which keeps the relief valve from weeping and saves wear on faucets and fittings.     Gas Water Heater Replacement in Kendale Lakes

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Kendale Lakes, FL

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match voltage and breaker size to the panel, and since there is no vent, an electric tank can sit in spots a gas unit cannot. A drain pan goes under closet installs. Quiet and simple.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Kendale Lakes

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Each one gets a new gas connector, a sediment trap and a shutoff valve, plus a vent connector pitched up toward the flue at a quarter inch per foot. We test draft before leaving. Gas suits busy households.

    Expansion Tank Installation in Kendale Lakes, FL

    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system.
