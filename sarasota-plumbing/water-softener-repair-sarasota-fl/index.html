    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Sarasota

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. The tank looks full, but the softener cannot make brine. After breaking up the crust, we clear the sludge and test the float and brine well. A cracked brine tank gets swapped for a new one.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Sarasota, FL

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Its small ports clog with sediment and salt. A clogged injector leaves the brine tank full of water and the resin never gets recharged. The injector and screen get cleaned or replaced, and the brine line gets checked.
