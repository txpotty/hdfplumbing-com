    Slab Leak Detection in Sarasota

    Slab leaks are common in homes on shifting soil and moving foundations. These leaks occur in pipes beneath your concrete slab, often indicated by warm spots on the floor, sudden spikes in your water bill or the sound of running water when all fixtures are off. HDF Plumbing uses dedicated acoustic listening devices and thermal imaging cameras to precisely locate these hidden leaks without destructive guesswork. Early detection is essential to preventing major foundation damage.

    Preventative Leak Inspections in Sarasota, FL

    Water Heater and Softener Leak Detection in Sarasota

    Water heaters and softeners can leak at fittings, valves and drain lines. A softener stuck in regeneration can send water down the drain nonstop, and a dripping relief valve on a water heater wastes water too. We check these units carefully and repair or replace failed parts. That often explains a mystery bill in homes that have water softeners.

    Yard and Outdoor Leak Detection in Sarasota

    Leaks in outdoor plumbing, irrigation systems or main water lines buried in your yard can lead to wasted water and landscaping damage. Detecting these outdoor leaks early can also prevent erosion and potential foundation issues caused by saturated soil, a serious concern for homes in Sarasota, FL.

    Pool Water Leak Detection in Sarasota, FL

    Pools lose a lot of water to evaporation in summer heat, so a leak can hide behind normal loss. We use a bucket test to compare evaporation with actual water loss.
