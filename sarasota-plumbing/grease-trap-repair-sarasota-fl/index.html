    Lid, Gasket and Riser Repair in Sarasota, FL

    Damaged lids and failed gaskets let odors escape and create safety hazards. Outdoor risers can crack from vehicle traffic or ground movement. We replace lids, install new gaskets and repair or replace risers. Traffic-rated lids are installed where trucks or cars drive over the interceptor. These repairs keep odors in and keep people safe.

    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Sarasota

    Baffles slow incoming water so grease can rise and solids can settle. When baffles crack, corrode or break loose, grease passes straight through the trap. We remove damaged baffles and install new ones made for your trap model. In concrete interceptors, we can repair or rebuild baffle walls. Restoring baffles brings back proper separation and helps you stay compliant.

    Flow Control Device Repair in Sarasota

    Hydromechanical traps rely on a flow control fitting to keep water moving at the rated speed. If this fitting is missing or clogged, the trap can be overwhelmed and grease escapes. We inspect, clean or replace flow control devices during repair visits. We also check the air intake that allows the device to work properly. Correct flow control is necessary for a trap to pass inspection.

    Grease Trap Leak Repair in Sarasota

    Leaks can come from cracked trap bodies, failed gaskets or loose pipe connections. Indoor leaks can damage floors and create slip hazards in the kitchen. We locate the source and replace gaskets, seal fittings or repair cracks when possible. Outdoor leaks can contaminate soil and lead to odors around the building. If the body is badly damaged, we suggest replacement.

    Grease Trap Drain Line Repair in Sarasota

    Beyond the trap itself, the drain lines leading to and from the grease trap can also suffer damage or blockages. These lines are especially susceptible to grease build-up. In Sarasota, we diagnose and repair issues within these connecting pipes, including clearing FOG accumulation, fixing collapsed sections, or repairing root intrusions. Making sure these lines are clear is vital for the overall efficiency of your grease trap system.

    Damaged Baffle and Lid Repair in Sarasota, FL

    The internal baffles of a grease trap are essential for separating FOG from wastewater. If these are damaged or dislodged, the trap becomes inefficient, allowing grease to escape into the sewer system. We repair or replace broken baffles and cracked lids in Sarasota, FL, making sure your trap functions as designed. A secure lid also prevents odors and keeps pests out, maintaining hygiene around the unit.

    Grease Trap Repair vs Replacement Cost in Sarasota, FL

    Simple repairs like gasket or tee replacements usually cost far less than a new trap. Major damage such as a cracked tank body, failing concrete or a badly corroded steel unit may make replacement the better value. We compare repair cost, remaining life and compliance risk for you. Older steel traps often rust through and are good candidates for upgrade. We give you options and pricing in writing so you can decide.

    Grease Line Clog Repair in Sarasota, FL

    Grease that hardens in the lines before or after the trap can cause backups even when the trap itself is clean. We use hydro jetting to clear the pipe and remove buildup. A camera inspection helps us confirm the line is clear and check for damage. If we find broken or sagging pipe, we can repair the section. This keeps water and waste moving smoothly.
