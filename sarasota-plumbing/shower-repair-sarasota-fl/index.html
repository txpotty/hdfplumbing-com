    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Sarasota, FL

    Low pressure at the shower with good pressure at the sink narrows it down fast. That points to the head, the flow restrictor or the cartridge, not the main or the water heater, and it is usually something we can fix within an hour. We start with the head. If every fixture is weak, we check the regulator and the main.

    Commercial Shower Service in Sarasota

    Hotels and dorms cannot wait days on a shower. We carry commercial cartridges and thermostatic parts, and we can stage repairs room by room so only one unit is down at a time. We can work nights. Each repaired valve gets its limit stop set and tested before we move on.

    Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Sarasota, FL

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. A hand auger through the strainer clears most of them, and a drum machine handles clogs that have moved past the trap. Drain cleaner usually misses the clog. We recommend a hair catcher once it is clear.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Sarasota

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. If the head drips with the valve closed, water is slipping past the cartridge, and a new head will not change that. We fix the valve. Leaks at the arm threads are different, and those get new thread tape and a snug turn.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Sarasota, FL

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Scale packs the nozzles and the restrictor screen behind them, and the spray drops off a little at a time until one day it feels like a garden mister. Cleaning often works. If the cartridge is clogged too, we replace it and test the flow again.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Sarasota, FL

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. When the gate in a lift-style spout wears or a three-handle diverter stem loses its washer, water keeps pouring from the spout and the shower gets weak. We replace the spout or stem. Spout replacements take about twenty minutes.

    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Sarasota, FL

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. That means a worn diverter steals pressure from the head, a clogged tub drain slows the shower and a leaky valve shows up at both the spout and the head. We check all of it. We also check the overflow gasket, a frequent hidden leak.
