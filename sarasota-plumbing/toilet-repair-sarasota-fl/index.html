    Clogged Toilet Clearing in Sarasota, FL

    Most clogs sit in the trapway, the curved channel cast into the porcelain, and a closet auger with a rubber-sleeved head clears them without scratching the bowl. Plungers miss some.     Flush Valve Seat Repair in Sarasota

    Scale on the flush valve seat is the reason a brand-new flapper sometimes keeps leaking, because the rubber cannot seal against a rough, crusted ring. Feel the seat with a finger. We clean it with a scrub pad, and if it is pitted we install a seat repair kit or a new flush valve. The float gets set so the water level sits roughly one inch under the top of the overflow tube.

    Pressure-Assist and Dual-Flush Repair in Sarasota

    Pressure-assist toilets use a sealed vessel inside the china that compresses air with incoming water pressure, and the cartridge inside that vessel eventually wears out. Hardware stores rarely stock those parts. We carry Flushmate cartridges and dual-flush seals for the common TOTO and American Standard models, and we check house pressure, since these units need steady line pressure to flush properly. Low pressure makes them sound weak.

    Leaking Toilet Base Repair in Sarasota, FL

    Toilet Tank and Bowl Crack Repair in Sarasota

    Hairline cracks in a tank can sit for years and then split open when someone sets a heavy lid down or a bolt is tightened too far.     Weak Flush and Clog Repair in Sarasota, FL
