    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Sarasota

    The heat exchanger in a tankless heater is a coil of small tubes that water races through while the burner fires. Hard water drops calcium and magnesium on those hot surfaces, narrowing the passages over time. We connect a submersible pump and hoses to the service valves and circulate descaling solution for 45 to 60 minutes. Then we flush with fresh water to rinse the system completely. Restored flow means steadier temperatures and fewer shutdowns.

    Noritz Tankless Water Heater Tune-Ups in Sarasota, FL

    Noritz tankless water heaters are robust systems, but they still require annual tune-ups to maintain their performance through every season.     Inlet Filter and Air Intake Cleaning in Sarasota

    Every tankless unit has an inlet water screen that catches grit and scale flakes. Those screens can clog with sand and debris, especially after municipal line work. We pull and clean the screen and inspect the air intake filter, which can load up with dust and dryer lint. Clean filters keep the flow sensor reading accurately and the burner breathing properly.

    Navien Tankless Heater Tune-Ups in Sarasota

    Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Sarasota

    The cost of tankless water heater maintenance in Sarasota usually ranges from $150 to $300, depending on the unit's condition, age and brand.     With regular descaling, many tankless heaters last 15 to 20 years. Neglected units in hard water can fail in under half that time.

    Rinnai shows LC codes, Navien may show E003 or E030 and Noritz often shows 90 or 99 when scale or airflow is a problem. These codes typically clear after proper cleaning.

    Many manufacturers exclude scale damage from heat exchanger warranties. Regular, documented maintenance is the best way to keep your coverage intact.

    Many tankless water heater manufacturers require proof of regular, professional maintenance to keep your unit's warranty valid. Skipping annual service can void the warranty, leaving you responsible for costly repairs that would otherwise be covered. Always check your specific warranty terms.

    The descaling process involves shutting off the unit, isolating the water lines and connecting a circulating pump to flush a mild acidic solution through the heat exchanger.
