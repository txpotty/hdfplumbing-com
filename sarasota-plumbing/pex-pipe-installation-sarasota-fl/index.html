    Copper to PEX Conversions in Sarasota

    Copper lines can develop pinhole leaks from water chemistry, high velocity and sometimes soil contact under slabs. Around Sarasota, flushing the water heater yearly and descaling tankless units helps keep mineral buildup under control. Once a home has had several pinhole leaks, more usually follow. Converting to PEX stops the cycle and reduces the risk of hidden water damage. We use approved transition fittings wherever new PEX connects to existing copper stubs or valves.

    PEX for Water Line Replacement in Sarasota

    When your main water line from the street to your home needs replacement, PEX is an excellent option. Its flexibility allows for easier trenchless or minimal-dig installations, limiting disruption to your yard in Sarasota. PEX main lines handle ground movement and resist corrosion better than rigid pipe. Nearly all homes in Florida sit on concrete slabs over sandy soil, so leaks under the slab are a common and costly repair.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Sarasota

    PEX-B is stiffer and more affordable, and it is joined with copper crimp rings or stainless steel cinch clamps over brass or poly fittings. It is a solid option for smaller projects and budget-conscious repipes. Brands like Viega, Zurn and SharkBite offer dependable PEX-B systems. We use a go/no-go gauge on every crimp to confirm a proper seal.

    Remodel and Addition PEX Rough-In in Sarasota

    Kitchen and bathroom remodels are a good time to replace old supply lines with PEX. We rough in new lines to the correct heights for vanities, showers, tubs and appliances, then pressure test before the walls are closed. Additions and guest suites get correctly sized lines tied into your existing system. We coordinate with your contractor to keep the project schedule moving.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Sarasota, FL

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a tight seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can be repaired from kinks with a heat gun. It is our preferred choice for many full repipes.

    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Sarasota, FL

    Gray polybutylene pipe was common in homes built from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s and is known for sudden failures. Chlorine in municipal water breaks down the plastic and its fittings over time. Plenty of insurers now ask about polybutylene when writing a policy. Replacing it with PEX removes that risk and can make your home easier to insure and sell.
