    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Sarasota, FL

    Exterior-mount tankless units hang on an outside wall and vent straight out the front, so there is no vent pipe to run. Covers and wiring take a beating outdoors. Northern parts of Florida do get occasional freezing nights, which can crack exposed pipes, irrigation lines, and outdoor devices.     Commercial Tankless Racks in Sarasota, FL

    Restaurants and salons run tankless racks hard. A rack of four units can draw more than 600,000 BTU, so we confirm gas pressure at the far unit with every burner firing. Installs can happen after closing.

    Navien NPE units are condensing, and the A models have a built-in recirculation pump and buffer tank that cut down on cold-water sandwiches. Their combi boilers handle both space heat and domestic water. We set up both.

    Emergency Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Sarasota

    No hot water from a tankless usually shows up as an error code on the controller, and the code tells us half of what we need to know. A photo of the error code helps a lot. If the heat exchanger is leaking, replacement is the fix, and we can often have a new unit running the same or next day.

    Navien Venting and Install in Sarasota
