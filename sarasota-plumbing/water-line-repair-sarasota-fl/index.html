    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Sarasota

    Pipe bursting means splitting the old line in place while the new one follows behind it. It works best on straight runs. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe outward, and a length of HDPE trails behind it in the same path. You end up with a fresh line and two small pits, and the lawn, sidewalk and driveway in between stay where they are, which is the whole reason people pick this method over a trench.

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Sarasota, FL

    Galvanized service lines were common in houses built before the 1970s, and a lot of them are still in the ground. Many wells in Florida produce water with a sulfur smell or high iron, which often calls for treatment to protect fixtures and appliances. Scale builds up inside and the opening shrinks. Rusty water at the first tap in the morning is a classic sign, and so is pressure that falls off when the shower and the washer run together. Replacing the full run with copper or PEX clears all of it up. Usually a one-day job.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Sarasota

    A trench through a paver driveway or a mature garden can cost more to restore than the pipe itself. Trenchless avoids most of that. We dig a pit at each end and pull HDPE or PEX through with a boring tool or pipe puller, following the old line or cutting a new path beside it. Most replacements finish in a day. The new pipe carries its own manufacturer warranty.

    Emergency Water Line Repair in Sarasota, FL

    First thing on a burst line is shutting the water off, at the house valve if it works and at the curb stop if it does not. Water pouring into a yard undermines everything around it. HDF Plumbing sends a plumber with a curb key, a pump and repair couplings. We can usually stop it within the hour. If the repair will run long, we rig a temporary bypass so the house has water while we work.

    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Sarasota

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. Newer homes across Florida mostly use PEX or CPVC with PVC drains, though copper pinhole leaks are still a common repair in older homes. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water and a meter that moves with everything off. We pinpoint the leak with acoustic gear and thermal imaging. From there you have three choices: break the slab at the spot, reroute the line through the walls or attic, or skip the old under-slab pipe and repipe overhead with PEX. You get a price on every option.

    Underground Water Line Leak Repair in Sarasota

    Underground leaks hide well. Many people find out from the bill before they see anything in the yard. Others notice a patch of grass that grows greener than the rest, or ground that stays soft long after the sprinklers shut off. We confirm the leak at the meter, trace the pipe route and listen along it. We dig where the noise is loudest.

    Main Water Line Leak Repair in Sarasota

    A meter that moves with every fixture off is the clearest sign you have. Confirming it takes about ten minutes. The house shutoff gets closed, the dial gets watched, and if it keeps creeping we know the leak is between the meter and the house. Ground microphones find the spot. A repair coupling or a new length of pipe goes in, and then we pressure test it.
