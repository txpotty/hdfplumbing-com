    Residential Grease Interceptor Setup in North Port, FL

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in North Port

    Hydro-mechanical grease interceptors, often called HGI units, are compact and designed for indoor use, usually under a sink or on the floor. These units use baffles to slow down wastewater flow, allowing grease to separate and accumulate. HDF Plumbing expertly installs various sizes of HGI units, ensuring proper venting, leveling, and connection to your drainage system in North Port for the best efficiency.

    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in North Port

    Hydromechanical grease traps sit under or near sinks and use baffles and flow control to separate grease from water. They are common in small cafes, coffee shops and quick-service restaurants with tight space. We install units from Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura sized by gallons per minute. Each install includes a flow control fitting and proper venting to keep the trap working as designed. These units need frequent cleaning, so we place them where staff can reach the lid easily.

    Grease Trap Installation for Schools and Institutions in North Port

    School cafeterias, hospitals, senior living facilities and churches with kitchens often need commercial interceptors. These sites usually have large dish machines and steam kettles that push high flow. We size interceptors to handle peak lunch or dinner loads without bypassing grease. Our crews schedule work around school breaks or off hours. We also provide documentation for facility managers and code officials.

    Grease Trap Installation Cost in North Port, FL

    Small under-sink hydromechanical traps generally cost much less to install than large outdoor interceptors. Indoor units are typically a few thousand dollars installed, while in-ground interceptors can run well into five figures with excavation and concrete work. Pricing depends on unit size, location, soil conditions and whether we need to cut and patch floors. We give you a written estimate that includes permits and inspection.     Gravity Grease Interceptor Installation in North Port, FL

    Gravity grease interceptors, or GGI units, are larger, typically located outdoors, and rely on the natural difference in specific gravity between FOG and water. These are common for high-volume commercial kitchens. Our team handles the involved excavation, concrete work, and plumbing connections required for proper GGI installation, maintaining compliance with all local Florida ordinances for waste management.

    In-Ground Gravity Interceptor Installation in North Port, FL

    Larger restaurants and high-volume kitchens often need an outdoor gravity interceptor. These concrete or polyethylene tanks usually range from several hundred to over 1,500 gallons. We excavate, set the tank on a stable base, connect inlet and outlet piping and install manhole risers. A high water table across much of Florida affects drainage, septic design, and how deeply crews can dig for underground repairs.
