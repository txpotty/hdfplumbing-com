    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in North Port, FL

    The anode rod, often called the sacrificial rod, is essential for preventing corrosion inside your hot water tank. Replacing a worn anode rod is a cost-effective way to protect your tank from rust and leaks, sparing you a full tank replacement.
