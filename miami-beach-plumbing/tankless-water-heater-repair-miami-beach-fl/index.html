    Tankless Water Heater Descaling Service in Miami Beach

    Scale is calcium and magnesium carbonate that comes out of hard water as it heats up, and a tankless exchanger heats water fast through narrow passages.     Igniter & Flame Rod Repair in Miami Beach, FL

    Gas tankless heaters light with a hot surface or spark igniter, then prove flame with a flame rod. We clean or replace the rod, look for a cracked igniter or a lazy spark, and confirm the gas valve opens on call. Five-minute fix sometimes.
