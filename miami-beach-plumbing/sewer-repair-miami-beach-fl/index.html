    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Miami Beach, FL

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies usually form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after years of wet weather. Nearly all homes in Florida sit on concrete slabs over sandy soil, so leaks under the slab are a frequent and costly repair. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is typically a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.

    Sewer Line Backups and Blockages in Miami Beach

    Sewer backups are usually caused by severe blockages from grease buildup, foreign objects or extensive tree root intrusion.     Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Miami Beach

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful cleaning method that uses high-pressure water streams to clear stubborn blockages, grease buildup and even tree roots from sewer lines. It's much more effective than snaking for thorough cleaning and preventing recurring clogs. This service is especially helpful for restaurants and commercial properties in Miami Beach dealing with heavy grease accumulation, as well as homes with recurring drain issues.

    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Miami Beach

    Tree roots are a common culprit for sewer line damage in Florida, drawn to the moisture and nutrients inside pipes. They can infiltrate tiny cracks, expand and eventually crush or clog the line. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing root intrusion using camera technology. We can remove roots with specialized cutting tools and recommend preventive measures or durable pipe relining options to prevent future invasions, protecting your home's foundation.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Miami Beach

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg pipe, a tar-paper material that flattens and blisters over time. Cast iron from the same era corrodes from the inside and scales up until little flow remains. When a camera shows widespread failure, patching is a waste of money. We replace the line in sections or end to end with PVC, and we can often use trenchless methods to avoid tearing up the whole yard.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Miami Beach, FL

    Tree and shrub roots go straight for the moisture and nutrients leaking from old sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense mats that catch paper and waste. We cut the roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro-jetter, then repair or seal the joint so they cannot come right back. For lines with many rooted joints, we will show you when lining or replacement makes more sense than ongoing cleaning.
