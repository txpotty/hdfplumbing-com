    Pilot Light Repair in Miami Beach

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most common gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and service or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are finished in about an hour.

    Burner Cleaning and Repair in Miami Beach, FL

    A yellow, lazy or noisy flame signals a dirty burner or poor combustion air. We remove and clean the burner, check the orifice size for natural gas or propane and adjust as needed. A clean blue flame heats more quickly and produces less carbon monoxide. We also check for rust flakes falling from the flue.

    Tank Leaks and Pressure Problems in Miami Beach

    Even gas water heaters can develop tank leaks, often due to corrosion from hard water or high water pressure. Many wells in Florida produce water with a sulfur smell or high iron, which often calls for treatment to protect fixtures and appliances. While some leaks may call for a replacement, minor leaks or pressure relief valve issues can often be repaired. Our team assesses the extent of the damage, inspects the anode rod and addresses pressure imbalances to prevent further problems in your Miami Beach home.

    Thermostat and Gas Valve Repair in Miami Beach, FL

    If your gas water heater isn't heating water to the correct temperature, or if it is running too hot, the thermostat or gas valve might be to blame. These components regulate the gas supply and temperature. Our plumbers in Miami Beach, FL can test the thermostat for accuracy and inspect the gas valve for any blockages or faults. We carry a range of replacement parts for various brands, allowing for quick and efficient repairs.

    Gas Water Heater Noise Troubleshooting in Miami Beach

    Strange noises coming from your gas water heater, such as rumbling, popping or whistling, often indicate sediment buildup in the tank or a burner problem. Hard water can add to sediment accumulation. Our repair service includes flushing the tank and inspecting for sediment, addressing pressure issues or checking other components that might be causing the noise, restoring efficiency and quiet operation.
