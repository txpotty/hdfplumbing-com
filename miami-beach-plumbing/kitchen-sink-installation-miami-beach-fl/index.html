    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Miami Beach

    Double bowl sinks need a continuous waste or end outlet layout to drain both sides through one trap. We plan the layout around your disposal and dishwasher connections. Proper slope keeps both sides draining freely. We use heavy-wall fittings that resist leaks.

    Under-mount Sink Installation in Miami Beach, FL

    Under-mount sinks provide a sleek, seamless look, making countertop cleaning easier. Their installation is more complex than top-mount sinks, requiring precise measurements and strong adhesive or clips for secure attachment to the underside of the counter. HDF Plumbing is skilled at under-mount sink installation, ensuring a strong bond and a perfectly sealed edge to prevent water infiltration. We ensure the cutout is smooth and the sink is level for proper drainage.

    Disposal and Dishwasher Hookup with Sink Installation in Miami Beach, FL

    Most new kitchen sinks are paired with a disposal and dishwasher. We mount the disposal, connect the dishwasher drain through an air gap or high loop, and install the dishwasher supply. Doing it all at once saves time and money. We test every connection under real use.

    Workstation Sink Installation in Miami Beach, FL

    Workstation sinks include ledges for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks. They are usually deep, single-bowl designs with offset drains. We position the drain to fit your cabinet and disposal and confirm the faucet reach. These sinks are popular in modern kitchen remodels.
