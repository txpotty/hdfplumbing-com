    Exterior Waterproofing Solutions in Miami Beach, FL

    For comprehensive basement leak prevention, exterior waterproofing involves excavating around your foundation and applying waterproof membranes or coatings. This creates a strong barrier against moisture intrusion from the outside. While more involved, this method offers excellent long-term protection against water damage, especially for older homes in Miami Beach.

    Water Heater and Appliance Leak Repair in Miami Beach, FL

    Water heaters, softeners and laundry machines are often installed in basements, and their leaks pool quickly. A dripping T&P valve, a corroded tank bottom or a split washer hose are frequent culprits. We repair or replace the failed part and install drain pans and leak sensors to catch future problems early. If a tank has rusted through, we can replace it the same day in most cases.

    Interior Drainage Systems in Miami Beach

    When exterior excavation isn't practical, interior drainage systems provide an effective solution for managing basement leaks. We install perimeter drains along the basement walls, collecting water before it can surface and directing it to a sump pump for removal. This system is less disruptive and can be a cost-effective way to dry out a wet basement.

    Drain and Sewer Pipe Leak Repair in Miami Beach, FL

    Cast iron drain stacks in older homes crack and scale with age, and plastic joints can separate if a line was poorly supported. These leaks usually show up only when an upstairs fixture runs. We run water through each drain while watching the basement, then confirm the crack with a camera. Repairs range from a single section swap to replacing an entire stack with PVC or ABS.

    Clogged Drain Tile Repair in Miami Beach

    Interior or exterior drain tile systems can become clogged with sediment or tree roots over time, leaving them useless.     Sump Pump Repair and Installation in Miami Beach, FL
