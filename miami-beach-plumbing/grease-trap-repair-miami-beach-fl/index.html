    Lid, Gasket and Riser Repair in Miami Beach, FL

    Damaged lids and failed gaskets let odors escape and create safety hazards. Outdoor risers can crack from vehicle traffic or ground movement. We replace lids, install new gaskets and repair or replace risers. Traffic-rated lids are installed where trucks or cars drive over the interceptor. These repairs keep odors in and keep people safe.

    Grease Line Clog Repair in Miami Beach, FL

    Grease that hardens in the lines before or after the trap can cause backups even when the trap itself is clean. We use hydro jetting to clear the pipe and remove buildup. A camera inspection helps us confirm the line is clear and check for damage. If we find broken or sagging pipe, we can repair the section. This keeps water and waste moving smoothly.

    Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in Miami Beach, FL

    Tees direct flow into and out of the trap at the correct depth. Missing or broken tees allow floating grease to escape into the sewer line. We replace damaged tees with durable PVC or other approved materials. We also check that they are set at the right depth for your unit. This is one of the most common and necessary grease trap repairs.

    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Miami Beach

    Baffles slow incoming water so grease can rise and solids can settle. When baffles crack, corrode or break loose, grease passes straight through the trap. We remove damaged baffles and install new ones made for your trap model. In concrete interceptors, we can repair or rebuild baffle walls. Restoring baffles brings back proper separation and helps you stay up to code.
