    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Miami Beach, FL

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. That means a worn diverter steals pressure from the head, a clogged tub drain slows the shower and a leaky valve shows up at both the spout and the head. Each part gets tested. The overflow gasket gets a look too, since it is a common hidden leak.

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Miami Beach, FL

    Stains on the ceiling below are often the first clue. A meter on the walls and a flood test on the pan show whether the leak is the valve, the pan or the drain. The source gets repaired. With slab floors, the water can travel under the flooring of nearby rooms.

    Shower Handle Leak Repair in Miami Beach

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water running down the inside of the trim gets behind the tile at the valve opening, and over a few months it soaks the drywall around the valve without ever showing on the shower side. The trim comes off first. Worn O-rings or a loose bonnet nut are the usual cause.

    Shower Temperature Swing Repair in Miami Beach

    Cold blasts when a toilet flushes mean the pressure-balance spool is stuck. That spool is supposed to slide back and forth to keep the hot and cold mix even when pressure drops on one side, and scale can freeze it in place. Replacing the cartridge solves it. Water that never gets hot enough may only need the limit stop turned.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Miami Beach

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. If the head drips with the valve closed, water is slipping past the cartridge, and a new head will not change that. The fix is in the valve. Leaks at the arm threads are different, and those get new thread tape and a snug turn.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Miami Beach, FL

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. A worn spout gate or a tired diverter stem lets water keep running from the spout, and the head pressure drops off. We replace the spout or stem. Spout replacements take about twenty minutes.

    Same-Day Shower Leak Repair in Miami Beach, FL

    Shut off the main first if water is getting where it should not. For a sudden wall leak or a valve that will not close, the main valve near the meter is the first move. Call us next. We stop the leak, make the repair or a safe temporary fix and check for anything that needs drying.

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Miami Beach, FL

    Low pressure at the shower with good pressure at the sink narrows it down fast. With good pressure elsewhere, the problem is in the head, the restrictor or the cartridge, and that is typically a short repair. The head comes off first. Low pressure everywhere sends us to the pressure regulator and main valve instead.
