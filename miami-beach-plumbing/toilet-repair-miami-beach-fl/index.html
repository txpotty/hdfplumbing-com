    Common Toilet Repairs in Miami Beach, FL

    Toilet Brands We Service in Miami Beach

    Kohler, American Standard, TOTO, Gerber and Mansfield make up most of the toilets we open, and each one uses its own flapper size or flush valve design. The wrong flapper will leak. We match the part to the model number stamped inside the tank and stock the common ones on every truck.

    Running Toilet Repair in Miami Beach

    A tank that keeps hissing and refilling every few minutes is almost always losing water past the flapper or overfilling into the overflow tube. It adds up fast. We look at the chain, float, flapper and seat before touching a part, then replace only what failed. Most running toilets are quiet again in about thirty minutes.

    Flush Valve Seat Repair in Miami Beach

    Scale on the flush valve seat is the reason a brand-new flapper sometimes keeps leaking, because the rubber cannot seal against a rough, crusted ring. Feel the seat with a finger. A scrub pad and some vinegar clean most seats, and if it is pitted we install a seat repair kit or a new flush valve. The float gets set so the water level sits roughly one inch under the top of the overflow tube.

    Leaking Toilet Repair in Miami Beach

    Water on the floor can come from four places: the wax ring, the tank-to-bowl gasket, the supply line or sweat running off a cold tank. Each one needs a different fix. We wipe it all dry and watch where the first drop forms, which takes about ten minutes and saves pulling a toilet that did not need it. A cracked bowl gets a replacement quote, not a patch.

    Toilet Flush Repair in Miami Beach, FL

    Two or three tries to get a full flush points to a flapper closing too early, a chain hooked in the wrong hole, scale in the rim jets, or a cracked overflow tube letting water bypass the bowl. Each one has a tell. We watch a full cycle with the lid off and replace the flush valve if the overflow tube is cracked, since it cannot be patched.

    Toilet Tank and Bowl Crack Repair in Miami Beach

    Hairline cracks in a tank can sit for years and then split open when someone sets a heavy lid down or a bolt is tightened too far. Epoxy does not hold under water pressure for long. We replace it. A matching replacement tank is often an option, and it can cost less than a whole new toilet.

    Toilet Leaking Through the Ceiling in Miami Beach, FL

    Water coming through the ceiling under an upstairs bathroom usually traces back to a failed wax ring or a cracked supply nut, and the drywall below holds the evidence. Stop using that toilet. The toilet comes up, the flange gets checked, and the bowl goes back on a new seal, then we show you how far the water traveled so you can decide about the ceiling.

    Sensor and Flushometer Repair in Miami Beach

    Sensor flush valves fail in their own ways, from dead batteries and dirty sensor eyes to solenoids that stick open and keep a bowl running. Before replacing parts, we test the sensor and the solenoid. Batteries are the cheap fix. Ghost flushing on a sensor valve usually means the eye needs adjusting or cleaning.
