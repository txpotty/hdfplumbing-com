    Home Tankless Flushing in Port Charlotte

    Many wells in Florida produce water with a sulfur smell or high iron, which often calls for treatment to protect fixtures and appliances. We hook a pump to the service valves and circulate descaling solution for about an hour. The flow returns. Temperatures stop swinging, and the unit quits shutting down on high-limit.

    Tankless Vent Inspection in Port Charlotte

    Gas tankless heaters push exhaust out through a vent that has to stay sealed from the heater all the way to the termination outside the house. Condensing units use PVC or polypropylene vent pipe, pitched back toward the heater so condensate drains. Every joint gets checked, and we test for carbon monoxide by the heater. Wasp nests in vent caps are common.

    Rinnai Air Filter and Flame Rod Care in Port Charlotte

    Rinnai cabinets are tight. On these units we pull the air intake filter, wash it and check the flame rod for carbon buildup. On recirculating models we also check the pump schedule, because a wrong setting burns gas for nothing.

    Noritz Tankless Tune-Ups in Port Charlotte, FL

    Noritz builds a solid heater, but solid does not mean maintenance-free. We start a Noritz tune-up at the exhaust, checking for soot and any blocked termination. Next comes the combustion air intake and the fan. Last, we check the temperature setting and run a few fixtures at once, which shows whether the unit holds temperature between back-to-back showers.

    Navien Condensing Tune-Ups in Port Charlotte

    Navien NPE units are condensing heaters with a second heat exchanger that pulls extra heat out of the exhaust before it leaves the house. That second exchanger makes acidic condensate. The condensate trap gets cleared and the neutralizer media checked. On A-series models with the built-in recirculation pump, we test the pump and the buffer tank too.
