    Main Water Line Repair in Port Charlotte

    The main water line brings water from the municipal supply to your home. When this line leaks or breaks, it can cause significant water loss, foundation damage, and low water pressure.     PEX Water Line Repair in Port Charlotte, FL

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a flexible, durable, and corrosion-resistant option more and more used in homes across Port Charlotte, FL. While PEX is less prone to leaks than older materials, damage can still occur from improper installation, rodent chewing, or impact. Our team is fully experienced in PEX repair, using specialized crimping tools and fittings to make strong, leak-proof connections. We make sure your PEX system continues to perform well.

    PEX and Copper Water Line Repair in Port Charlotte

    Whether your home has modern PEX piping or traditional copper lines, our technicians are skilled in repairing both.     Trenchless Water Line Repair in Port Charlotte

    For some water line repairs, traditional trenching can be avoided. Trenchless repair methods, like pipe bursting or pipe lining, allow us to fix or replace underground pipes with minimal disruption to your landscaping, driveway, or foundation. This method is often faster and less intrusive than traditional digging, making it a popular choice for homeowners in Port Charlotte looking to preserve their property.     Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Port Charlotte

    Many water line problems are made worse by high incoming pressure. A failed pressure reducing valve can push well over 80 psi into your plumbing, which stresses the service line and every fixture. We test static pressure with a gauge and replace worn regulators from brands like Watts and Zurn Wilkins. We also swap seized gate valves for full-port ball valves so you can shut the water off fast in an emergency.

    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Port Charlotte, FL

    Leaks often start at the compression fittings and couplings right next to the meter box. Shifting soil and heavy traffic over the box can work loose those joints over time. We determine whether the leak is on the utility side or the customer side, since the water provider is in charge of the meter and the pipe before it. When the leak is yours, we replace the fittings and the customer shutoff valve on the same trip.

    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Port Charlotte

    Slab leaks, where water lines running beneath your home's concrete foundation leak, are a especially challenging issue. Many homes in Florida built before the mid 1970s have cast iron drains under the slab, which commonly crack and corrode with age. These leaks can undermine your foundation, leading to major structural damage. We use specialized listening devices and camera inspections to locate slab leaks precisely and offer repair options including direct slab penetration, re-routing, or pipe lining. Addressing a slab leak promptly in Port Charlotte is crucial to prevent extensive, costly foundation damage.

    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Port Charlotte, FL

Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars, while a full replacement costs more but resets the clock for decades.
