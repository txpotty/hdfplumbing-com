    No-water calls often end at the control box, not the pump. Three-wire pumps have a control box with a start capacitor and relay, and those parts fail far more often than the motor itself. We test them first. Swapping a capacitor costs a fraction of pulling a pump.

    Jet Pump Seal and Motor Replacement in Port Charlotte, FL

    Single-stage and two-stage jet pumps wear out at the seal and impeller, and a leaking shaft seal shows up as a puddle under the motor. New pump, primed and tested. After that, we watch the pressure gauge cycle from cut-in to cut-out.

    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Port Charlotte

    Jet Pump Replacement in Port Charlotte, FL
