    Condensing Tankless Repair in Port Charlotte, FL

    Condensing tankless heaters recover heat from the flue gas and make acidic condensate that has to drain somewhere. That condensate runs through a trap and, where code or the manual calls for it, a neutralizer filled with limestone or marble chips before it reaches the drain. A plugged trap will lock the heater out. We clean the trap, refill the neutralizer and check the PVC vent for sag or the wrong slope.

    Tankless Error Code Diagnostics in Port Charlotte, FL

    Every tankless brand flashes its own codes, and the same number can mean different things on two units. A Rinnai 11 is no ignition, a Navien E003 is ignition failure, and a Noritz 11 is also an ignition fault, yet the checks behind each one are not the same. We read the code, pull the history and test the part tied to it. Guessing gets expensive.

    Noritz Tankless Water Heater Repair in Port Charlotte, FL

    Noritz heaters hold up well, though flow sensors and vent joints still fail with age. A code 90 points to combustion trouble, often a dirty fan or a restricted intake. Our tech cleans the burner and fan, checks every vent joint, and compares the flow sensor reading to the spec sheet. Gaskets get replaced if they leak.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Repair in Port Charlotte, FL

    Rinnai units are common, and scale causes most of their service calls. The LC code is the unit's own warning that the exchanger needs a flush. We also see dirty flame rods behind a code 11 or 12 and tired combustion fans behind a code 61, and each part gets tested against Rinnai's published values. Parts come from the factory list.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Repair in Port Charlotte

    Navien condensing units use a secondary stainless exchanger to recover heat from the flue gas. On NPE-A models we also test the built-in recirculation pump, after clearing the trap and checking vent pitch. Then we fire it at low and high flow.
