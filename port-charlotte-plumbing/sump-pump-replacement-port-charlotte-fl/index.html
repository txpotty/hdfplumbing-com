    Sizing a Submersible Sump Pump in Port Charlotte

    Horsepower matters less than people think. For most houses a 1/3 HP pump is enough, provided the water only has to climb 10 to 15 feet to get out. We check the flow chart against your actual lift before we pick a size. Oversize it and it short cycles.

    Cast Iron Submersible Pumps in Port Charlotte

    Cast iron sheds motor heat into the water around it far better than a plastic housing does, which is a big part of why a Zoeller M53 or Liberty 250 outlasts a hardware-store pump.     High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Port Charlotte

    If your pump runs every 30 seconds during a storm and still loses ground, it is undersized for the water coming in. We time the cycles and measure how fast the pit refills.     Zoeller Sump Pump Replacement in Port Charlotte, FL

    Zoeller cast iron pumps, the M53 and M63 in particular, are what we pull off the truck most often because they take heat and hard cycling better than plastic housings.     Backup Pump Options in Port Charlotte, FL

    After a major storm, homes on wells or septic should be checked before normal use, since floodwater can foul both systems.     Choosing a Backup Battery in Port Charlotte

    Battery choice decides how long the backup actually runs. Most backups use a sealed AGM or deep-cycle battery around 75 to 100 amp-hours, which covers several hours of on-and-off pumping. We size it to how often your pump runs on a wet day. Plan on a new battery every three to five years.
