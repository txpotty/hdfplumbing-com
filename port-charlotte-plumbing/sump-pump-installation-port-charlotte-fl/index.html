    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Port Charlotte, FL

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. The backup pump gets its own float and its own check valve, and it runs off a sealed 12-volt battery when the outlet goes dead. Most run five to seven hours on a full charge. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Port Charlotte, FL

    Liberty builds quiet, well-made submersible pumps with good switch options.     Crawl spaces with standing water breed mold and rot sill plates within a few seasons. A small sump basin at the low point fixes that. Clearance is usually under two feet. The discharge exits through the rim joist and drains away from the house.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Port Charlotte, FL

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water.
