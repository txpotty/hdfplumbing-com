    Water Heater Expansion Tank Service in Clearwater, FL

    Many modern plumbing systems include an expansion tank connected to the water heater. This tank absorbs excess pressure created during the heating cycle, protecting your hot water tank and other plumbing fixtures. Our maintenance includes checking the expansion tank's air pressure and overall condition.     Attic and Garage Tank Checkups in Clearwater, FL

    Many homes put the water heater in a garage or attic where temperatures can swing widely. Heat speeds up the wear on plastic drain valves, gas valve seals and wiring insulation. We check drain pans, pan drain lines, platform height for gas units in garages and clearances around the tank. A leak in an attic tank without a working pan can ruin a ceiling, so we confirm that protection is in place.

    Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in Clearwater, FL
