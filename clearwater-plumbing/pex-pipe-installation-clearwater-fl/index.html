    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Clearwater, FL

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a tight seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can recover from kinks with a heat gun. It is our preferred choice for many full repipes.

    PEX Main Water Line Installation in Clearwater

    The main water line brings water into your home, and a failing one can cause serious issues. We provide expert PEX main water line installation, replacing old, damaged lines with durable PEX.     PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Clearwater

    PEX-B is stiffer and more affordable, and it is joined with copper crimp rings or stainless steel cinch clamps over brass or poly fittings. It is a good option for smaller projects and budget-conscious repipes. Brands like Viega, Zurn and SharkBite offer dependable PEX-B systems. We use a go/no-go gauge on every crimp to confirm a proper seal.
