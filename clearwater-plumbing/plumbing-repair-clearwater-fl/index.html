    Water Heater Valve and Element Repair in Clearwater

    Rumbling or popping from a tank means sediment on the bottom. A rotten-egg smell in the hot water usually means the anode rod is reacting with the water. We replace thermocouples, thermostats, elements, gas valves, anode rods and relief valves from truck stock, which covers the large majority of tank repairs we see in a year. A tank leaking at the seam is a different story. No part fixes that, and we tell you plainly.

    Water Softener Repair in Clearwater, FL

    Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving heavy scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. White crust on faucets and spotty glasses are the first signs a softener has stopped working. The usual causes are a salt bridge in the brine tank or a plugged injector screen. HDF Plumbing repairs the common brands and tests the water hardness before and after the fix so you can see the number drop. Resin lasts ten to fifteen years.

    Drain Line Repair in Clearwater

    Slow drains that come back a week after snaking usually mean the pipe itself is damaged. Cracked cast iron or a sagging run will hold debris every time.     Gas Leak Repair in Clearwater

    Tank and Tankless Water Heater Repair in Clearwater
