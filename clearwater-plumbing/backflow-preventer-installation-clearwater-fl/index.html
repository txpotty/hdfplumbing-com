    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Clearwater

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules allow. We size them to avoid excessive pressure loss on your system.

    Backflow Testing and Certification in Clearwater

    Most water providers require testable backflow assemblies to be certified at installation and then annually.     Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Device Installation in Clearwater, FL

    For high-hazard applications in Clearwater, FL where toxic substances could contaminate the water supply, a reduced pressure zone, or RPZ, device is usually required. RPZ backflow preventers offer the highest level of protection, using a relief valve to discharge water if the pressure drops. HDF Plumbing specializes in RPZ installation, making sure all components are correctly sized, positioned and set up according to state and local plumbing codes.
