    Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Tamarac, FL

    The float switch is a critical component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off.     Battery Backup Pump Maintenance in Tamarac

    Severe storms often bring power outages along with the rain. A battery backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or fails. We load test the battery, check the charger and alarm and run the backup pump on its own. Most backup batteries need replacing every three to five years.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Tamarac

    The discharge line carries water away from your basement, and the check valve prevents pumped water from flowing back into the pit. We inspect these components for blockages, leaks or damage. A faulty check valve can cause the pump to short-cycle, leading to premature wear.     Commercial and Elevator Pit Pumps in Tamarac

    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Tamarac, FL

We clean or replace the switch and set the on and off levels so the pump does not short cycle. Electronic sensor switches are an option for tight pits where floats get stuck.
