    Standard InSinkErator Installation in Tamarac

    A standard install replaces the unit under an existing sink. The old disposal comes down, the flange gets scraped clean and a new one goes in on fresh putty. Then the mount ring goes on. We hang the unit, turn the lower ring until it locks and connect the discharge tube to the trap with a new slip-joint washer so nothing weeps. Power comes from a cord kit or the existing hardwire, checked for ground before the breaker goes back on.

    Disposal Installs for New Sinks in Tamarac, FL

    A new sink changes the drain math. Deeper undermount or farmhouse bowls are common in remodels. With the bowl lower, the disposal drops too, and the discharge can land below the old trap arm, which means lowering the stub. Better to sort that out mid-remodel than after the tile is up.

    First-Time Disposal Installation in Tamarac

    Kitchens that never had a disposal need some rework. The sink needs a disposal flange. The tailpiece and trap have to be reworked, and the cabinet needs a switched outlet, which means an electrician if there is no power under the sink already. We also check the drain slope to the stack.

    Badger Series Installation in Tamarac, FL

    The Badger line runs from the 1/3 HP Badger 1 up to the Badger 5XP. Every Badger shares one quick-lock mount. That shared mount is why a Badger-to-Badger swap is often a 45-minute job, since the upper ring and flange can stay put if they are not corroded. No cord comes in the box, so the cord kit is on us.

    Batch Feed vs Continuous Feed in Tamarac

    Continuous feed is the type most kitchens have. Flip the switch and it runs until you flip it off. Batch feed only runs with its stopper turned in place, so nobody can reach into a running unit, which some families with small kids prefer and which needs no wall switch at all. We wire each one to match.
