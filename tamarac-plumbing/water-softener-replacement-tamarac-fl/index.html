    Metered Softener Replacement in Tamarac

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly exhausted. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first few weeks.

    Softener Loop Installation in Tamarac, FL

    Many homes have a softener loop near the main water line that allows easy installation. When a home lacks one, we can add a loop with shutoff valves and a bypass. We also route a drain line to a laundry box, floor drain or approved location. A good loop keeps outdoor hose bibs on hard water to save salt. This makes future service simpler.

    Traditional Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Tamarac

    Salt-based water softeners are a common and effective solution for hard water in Tamarac. These systems use ion exchange to remove calcium and magnesium minerals, which are notorious for causing scale buildup. We replace old, inefficient salt-based units with modern, high-efficiency models that use less salt and water.
