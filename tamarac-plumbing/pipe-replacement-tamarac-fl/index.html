    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Tamarac

    Gray polybutylene pipe was installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid-1990s. It breaks down from the inside when exposed to chlorinated water, and fittings can crack with no warning. Some insurers raise premiums or decline coverage on homes that still have it. We remove polybutylene supply lines and replace them with PEX-A or copper. A completed repipe can also help when listing the home.

    Slab Leak Pipe Replacement in Tamarac

    Slab leaks, common in homes built on concrete foundations, can be damaging and difficult to detect. HDF Plumbing uses modern leak detection technology to locate the exact source, then expertly repairs or reroutes the compromised pipe, often using durable PEX to bypass the slab entirely, preventing future issues in the same spot.

    PEX Repiping in Tamarac

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale buildup and expands slightly if water freezes, which helps in colder homes. PEX installs faster than copper with far fewer fittings in the walls. It is our most popular repipe material.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Tamarac, FL

    Pinhole leaks in copper usually start as a small green stain or a damp spot on drywall. They are caused by a mix of water chemistry, high velocity and thin Type M pipe, and once they begin they tend to keep coming back. Patching each leak costs $300 to $800 per visit plus drywall repair. We can replace the affected branch or repipe the home with PEX, which does not develop pinholes.
