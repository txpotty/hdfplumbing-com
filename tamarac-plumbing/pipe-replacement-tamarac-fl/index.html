    What Goes Into a Repipe in Tamarac, FL

    Copper Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Tamarac, FL

    Older homes sometimes have copper that is thin from decades of use. Pinholes start showing up. We replace it with new Type L copper or PEX, and fixing awkward runs from past remodels. Better flow.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Tamarac, FL

    Drain lines fail too. Old cast iron rusts through. We replace cracked cast iron, failing ABS or old galvanized drains with PVC, checking slope and venting so the new drains flow freely. Camera first.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Tamarac

    PEX bends around corners and comes in long rolls, so a typical repipe needs fewer fittings than copper would on the same layout. When a rare freeze is forecast, covering outdoor valves and letting a faucet drip is usually enough to prevent damage. It often survives freezing where copper would crack. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings where possible, keeping flow strong at each joint. Faster install.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Tamarac, FL

    Corrosion shows up as green or white crust on copper, rust on steel, or pinholes that drip. Water chemistry plays a role. We replace corroded sections or the whole system depending on how widespread the damage is, and we check the water heater connections and shutoffs since they often corrode too. Look at the joints.

    Emergency Pipe Replacement in Tamarac, FL

    A burst pipe needs fast action. Shut off the main valve first. Then call, because water spreading through walls and floors does damage every minute, and we can usually get there, stop the leak and replace the failed section the same day. Drying comes next.

    PEX Repiping in Tamarac

    PEX repipes go faster than copper. Flexible lines snake through walls with fewer openings, and home-run systems from a manifold let you shut off individual fixtures without killing water to the house. Popular choice.

    Slab Leak Pipe Replacement in Tamarac

    Rerouting is often better than digging. Jackhammering floors is a last resort. We run new pipe through the attic or walls to bypass the leaking line under the concrete, and usually costs less than repairing in place. Fast too.
