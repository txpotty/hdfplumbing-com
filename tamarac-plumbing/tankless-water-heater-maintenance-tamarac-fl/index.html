    Tankless Banks for Restaurants and Apartments in Tamarac

    Cascade systems link several tankless units to one controller that fires them in turn. Heavy use means scale builds faster than in a single home unit. Each heater gets descaled, and we check that the controller spreads the load evenly. Quarterly service is common.

    Service Valve Kit Installs in Tamarac, FL

    Plenty of tankless heaters went in without service valves, and without them there is no clean way to hook up a descaling pump.     Rinnai Tankless Service in Tamarac

    Rinnai units tell you a lot through the controller. Code 11 means the burner never lit, and code 12 means the flame went out after it did. On a Rinnai visit we clean the air intake filter and check the flame rod for soot or cracks. The LC codes are scale warnings, and they usually clear once the exchanger has been flushed and the unit is reset.
