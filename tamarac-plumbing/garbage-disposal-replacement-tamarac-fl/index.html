    Frequently Asked Questions About Garbage Disposal Replacement in Tamarac

    A standard disposal replacement with the unit usually costs $275 to $650. Premium models like InSinkErator Evolution usually run $500 to $1,000 installed.

    For light use, a 1/3 HP or 1/2 HP unit is usually sufficient. For regular use and tough food scraps, a 3/4 HP or 1 HP unit is recommended. Our plumbers can help you assess your needs and choose the best fit for your Tamarac home.

    Most garbage disposal replacements can be completed within 1 to 2 hours. This timeframe includes disconnecting the old unit, installing the new one, and testing it thoroughly. More complex situations, such as significant plumbing modifications or electrical work, might extend the duration slightly.

    Leaks from the body, frequent resets, constant jams, loud grinding and age over ten years are common signs.

    New garbage disposals typically come with manufacturer warranties ranging from 1 to 10 years, depending on the model and brand. HDF Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation services for your peace of mind.

    If your current disposal jams often or struggles with food waste, a 3/4 or 1 HP model can help.

    A continuous feed disposal operates when you flip a wall switch, allowing you to add food waste while it's running. A batch feed disposal only operates when a special stopper is inserted into the drain, making it usually safer as it prevents accidental activation while hands are near the opening.
