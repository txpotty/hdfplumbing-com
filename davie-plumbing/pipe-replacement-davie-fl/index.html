    PEX Pipe Replacement in Davie

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping has become a common choice for residential pipe replacement due to its flexibility, affordability, and resistance to freezing. When a rare freeze is forecast, covering outdoor valves and letting a faucet drip typically does the job to prevent damage. Unlike rigid pipes, PEX can expand, reducing the risk of burst pipes during sudden cold snaps. HDF Plumbing specializes in PEX installation, which often allows for less invasive replacement methods compared to traditional materials. This modern solution offers excellent performance and water quality for your home.

    PEX Repiping in Davie

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale buildup and expands slightly if water freezes, which helps in colder homes. PEX installs faster than copper with many fewer fittings in the walls. It is our most requested repipe material.
