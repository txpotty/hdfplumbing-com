    Rooting uses a spinning cable with cutting blades to break up clogs and roots. Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scrub the pipe walls. Many tough lines benefit from both.

    Homes with tree roots or older clay pipe often need rooting every 12 to 24 months. Homes with newer PVC lines may go many years without it. A camera inspection can help set the best schedule.

    For most standard drain cleaning or rooter services within your property's existing plumbing system, permits are generally not required in Davie, FL.     Rooting is safe when done by trained technicians using the correct cable and blade. Badly cracked or collapsed pipe can be harmed by any cleaning method. We watch for warning signs and stop if the pipe shows signs of damage.
