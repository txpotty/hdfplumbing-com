    Electric Water Heater Element Service in Davie, FL

    Electric water heaters use upper and lower heating elements controlled by thermostats. Sediment can coat the lower element and cause it to fail. We test both elements and thermostats with the power off and replace parts as needed. A failed element is often the reason for lukewarm water or running out of hot water fast. Proper testing avoids replacing parts that are still good.

    Water Heater Leak Detection and Prevention in Davie, FL

    Leaks are a common issue that can lead to significant water damage if not caught early. Our maintenance service includes a thorough inspection of all water heater connections, pipes, and the tank itself for any signs of leaks or corrosion. We examine the drain pan and surrounding area for moisture. Early detection and repair of small leaks can prevent larger, more destructive problems, protecting your home's floors and interior from water damage.

    Gas Water Heater Burner and Venting Check in Davie

    Gas water heaters need a clean burner and a proper vent to run safely. We inspect the burner flame, clean the combustion chamber and check the thermocouple or flame sensor. The vent is checked for proper draft and secure connections. Blocked or loose vents can let carbon monoxide into your home. We also check the gas line and shutoff valve for any leaks.

    Tank Water Heater Flushing in Davie

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium sediment at the bottom of the tank. This layer insulates the water from the burner or lower element, wasting energy and causing popping sounds. We connect a hose to the drain valve and flush the tank until the water runs clear. For heavy buildup, we may stir and flush in several cycles. A clean tank heats faster and runs more quietly.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Davie

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part in your water heater designed to corrode instead of the tank itself. Depending on your water chemistry, these rods can wear down faster than expected. During maintenance, we inspect the anode rod's condition and recommend replacement if necessary. A healthy anode rod is crucial for preventing rust and extending the lifespan of your water heater tank, saving you from a costly full replacement.
