    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Davie

    Cold leaks don't make warm spots. Watch the bill instead. Look for a rising bill, a meter that spins with nothing on or damp carpet, since a cold leak wets the soil as much as a hot one. Your Davie home is no exception. We isolate the cold line and repair or reroute it.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Davie, FL

    Hot lines leak under slabs more than cold lines. Heat is the reason. Each time hot water runs, the pipe expands against concrete and gravel, and recirculation pumps keep that cycle going all day, which grinds the pipe wall thin at the rub points. We repair or reroute the line and can set the recirculation pump on a timer.

    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Davie, FL

    Spot repairs fit leaks in otherwise good pipe. It is a single small opening. The floor gets opened, the pipe replaced and tested, and the trench compacted and patched, ready for your flooring contractor.

    Direct Slab Penetration Repair in Davie, FL

    Cutting through the slab is the shortest path to a localized leak. We wet-cut to keep dust down. Once the pipe is fixed, the fill is compacted and the slab patched to carry normal load, all done to code. Florida rules apply throughout. Your Davie home gets swept out at the end.

    Electronic Slab Leak Detection in Davie

    Locating comes before cutting. We use a ground mic and a thermal camera. The mic picks up the hiss of water escaping through concrete, and the camera shows warm water spreading from a hot line, so together they narrow the leak to a spot about a foot across. We test hot lines, cold lines and drains in your Davie home.

    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Davie

    A reroute abandons the leaking pipe under the slab. New line goes overhead. For older Davie homes, this works well with aging copper or galvanized pipe, since it bypasses every foot of buried line on that run. We keep drywall cuts small at fixture walls.

    Thermal and Acoustic Tools in Davie
