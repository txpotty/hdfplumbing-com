    Water Softener Replacement in Davie, FL

    Older softeners lose ground as resin wears out, valve seals leak or injectors clog. While the old system is out, we inspect the loop and the drain, then install a newer metered unit. Salt use drops. You will notice soft water the same day.

    Water Softener Installation Cost in Davie, FL

    Cost depends on the system type, its capacity and whether a loop already exists. An existing loop keeps the price down, and adding a loop adds labor and materials. Carbon tanks cost extra. We put equipment, labor and setup on the quote.

    Fleck Water Softener Installation in Davie, FL

    Fleck valves, especially the 5600SXT and 7000SXT, are on a lot of the softeners we install. Their parts are easy to find and the valves can be rebuilt instead of replaced, so the system lasts. Settings match your test. We confirm the setup before leaving.

    Commercial Water Softener Installation in Davie, FL

    Restaurants, hotels and laundromats feel hard water in their equipment first. Commercial softeners we install use larger resin tanks or twin-tank alternating systems so soft water is available around the clock, even during regeneration. Equipment lasts longer. We schedule around your hours.

    Culligan Water Softener Installation in Davie

    Culligan systems are common in homes around here, and we install and connect them. Each install follows the brand's specs for drain, bypass and power, then we program it to your water test. Connections and setup included. Existing loops make it faster.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Davie

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. They change the minerals into crystals that do not stick to pipe walls and heating elements, so scale drops off without adding sodium or using a drain line. Maintenance is light. They work best on moderate water.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Davie

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. Every whole-house install gets a bypass valve, and we add a pressure reducing valve if incoming pressure is too high. Every tap gets soft water. Outdoor taps can be left off.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Davie

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. Compared with an older timer-based unit, a modern metered softener can use roughly half the salt and noticeably less regeneration water each month. That pays back over time. Several models are available.
