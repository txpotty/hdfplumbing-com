    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Horizon West

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by capturing heat from exhaust gases. They vent through inexpensive PVC and produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized and drained properly. We install a neutralizer cartridge and route the condensate line to an approved drain. Salt air in coastal parts of Florida corrodes outdoor fittings and exposed metal pipe faster than in inland areas. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes. We explain the cost difference so you can decide if the efficiency gain is worth it.

    Like-for-Like Tankless Swaps in Horizon West

    When your existing tankless has a cracked heat exchanger or a failed control board that is not worth repairing, a direct replacement is often the fastest fix.     Recirculation Tankless Upgrades in Horizon West, FL
