    Picking a Tankless for Your Horizon West, FL House

    Whole-House Electric Tankless Replacement in Horizon West

    Whole-house electric tankless performance drops when the incoming water is cold, since the unit has to raise the temperature further at the same amperage. We replace Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart units. A heat pump water heater is sometimes the better fit, and we will tell you.

    Rinnai Condensing Upgrades in Horizon West, FL

    Rinnai Sensei condensing models replace older non-condensing Rinnai units in a lot of homes, but the venting changes from stainless to PVC. The vent swap is part of our job. Gas and water connections usually line up.

    Point-of-Use Electric Tankless in Horizon West

    Point-of-use electric tankless heaters under a sink are a different animal from whole-house units. They run on a single 20 or 30 amp circuit and work well for a far bathroom or an office sink. Swaps take an hour or two.

    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Horizon West

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE or Rinnai Sensei pass 90 percent efficiency by pulling extra heat out of the exhaust. The vent is plain PVC. The condensate is acidic, so we install a neutralizer cartridge and run the line to an approved drain. Humidity causes condensation on cold lines and clogged AC condensate drains, a common source of hidden water damage. The savings carry through every season because hot water use does.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Horizon West, FL

    Pulling a 50 gallon tank out of a garage frees up floor space and ends standby heat loss. Gas is the catch, since tankless units usually need 3/4-inch pipe or larger and a new vent. We check the meter size and pipe run before quoting, and we cap or reroute the old flue and seal the roof penetration if it is no longer needed. Expect a full day.

    Residential Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Horizon West

    Sizing a tankless starts with how many fixtures run at once and how cold the incoming water is, not the square footage of the house.     Tankless-to-Tankless Replacement in Horizon West

    Replacing an existing tankless with a new one is usually the simpler job, since the gas line, vent path and water connections are already there. We still check sizing against the new model. Old service valves often get replaced too.
