    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Horizon West, FL

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through 2 or 3-inch PVC, so they can sit far from a chimney. A standard outlet has to be within reach of the cord. We run the vent within the maker's length and elbow limits.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Service in Horizon West, FL

    Rheem tanks and their Ruud twins share most parts, which helps when a gas valve fails a few years in. Either one works. We set them up per the factory manual and test draft.

    A.O. Smith Gas Water Heater Service in Horizon West

    Thermal Expansion Tank Installation in Horizon West

    Closed systems are the reason expansion tanks exist. With a check or regulator on the main, expanding hot water has nowhere to go and the pressure spikes each heating cycle. Many codes require a thermal expansion tank there. We set its air charge to match house pressure.
