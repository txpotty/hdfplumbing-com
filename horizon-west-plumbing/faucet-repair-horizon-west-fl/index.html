    What We Fix on Faucets in Horizon West, FL

    Leaking Faucet Handle and Base Repair in Horizon West, FL

    Water seeping around the handle or pooling at the base usually means failed O-rings on the spout or cartridge, or a mounting nut that has worked loose under the sink. Check the cabinet floor. We replace the O-rings and tighten the mounting hardware, then reseal the base so water on the counter cannot get under it.

    Delta and Moen Faucet Repair in Horizon West, FL

    Delta and Moen are the faucets we repair most, and both back their cartridges with lifetime warranties for the original owner on many models. Ask us to check. Kohler, Pfister and American Standard each use proprietary cartridges, and we stock the common ones for each brand.

    Outdoor Hose Bib Repair in Horizon West

    Outdoor spigots take a beating from sun and weather. Northern parts of Florida do get occasional freezing nights, which can crack exposed pipes, irrigation lines, and backflow devices. We replace packing washers and seat washers on standard hose bibs, and we install frost-free sillcocks with the valve seat inside the warm wall when an old one has split. A vacuum breaker goes on every new one.

    Kitchen Sprayer and Diverter Repair in Horizon West, FL

    Spray heads on kitchen faucets get clogged with scale, and side sprayers lose pressure when the diverter inside the faucet body wears out. A diverter costs very little. Touch and touchless faucets add a solenoid and batteries to the list, and we test those before replacing anything.

    Specialty Faucet Repair in Horizon West, FL

    Pot fillers, laundry tub faucets and wall-mount utility faucets each have their own valve styles, and many use compression stems or quarter-turn cartridges that hardware stores do not stock. The common stems ride on the truck. For filtered water faucets, we replace the cartridge or the check valve as needed.

    Compression Faucet Repair in Horizon West

    Two-handle faucets with separate hot and cold handles are often compression faucets, which seal by pressing a rubber washer down onto a brass seat inside the valve. Washers harden and seats get pitted. New washers go in and the seat gets dressed or replaced with a seat wrench, and the packing gets redone if the stem leaks.

    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Repair in Horizon West

    Pull-down kitchen faucets wear at the hose, the spray head and the docking magnet or counterweight that pulls the hose back into the spout. Hoses split near the quick-connect. We replace hoses, spray heads and weights with brand parts, then check for drips under the sink where the hose connects.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Horizon West

    Ceramic disc faucets last a long time, but grit from a water main repair or scale from hard water can scratch the discs or wear out the rubber inlet seals underneath. Inlet seals cost very little. If the discs are scratched, the whole cartridge gets replaced, since the two discs are lapped to fit each other and cannot be resurfaced.
