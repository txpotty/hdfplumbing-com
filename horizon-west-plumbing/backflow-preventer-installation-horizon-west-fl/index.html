    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Horizon West

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules allow. We size them to avoid too much pressure loss on your system.

    Backflow Enclosures and Freeze Protection in Horizon West, FL

    Above-ground assemblies sit in direct sun in summer and can freeze on cold nights. We install insulated enclosures, freeze blankets or heated cabinets based on your local climate. Enclosures also discourage theft, since brass backflow assemblies are a common target for scrap thieves. We choose enclosures with enough room for testing and repairs.
