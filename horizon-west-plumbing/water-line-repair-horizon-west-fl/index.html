    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Horizon West, FL

    One leak on a newer copper or PEX line is a spot repair. Two or more leaks on galvanized is a replacement. Age matters too, since most service lines past 40 years old are living on borrowed time.     Copper Water Line Repair in Horizon West

    Buried copper is tough, but it is not forever. Rocks pressing against the pipe wear pinholes over time, and acidic soil or high water velocity can thin the wall from the inside.     Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Horizon West

    A trench through a paver driveway or a mature garden can cost more to restore than the pipe itself. Trenchless avoids most of that.     A ground microphone picks up the hiss of water escaping a pressurized pipe, even through a couple of feet of dirt. Some leaks barely make a sound.     Main Water Line Repair in Horizon West

    Your main line is the pipe from the meter to the house, usually 3/4 inch or 1 inch, buried anywhere from a foot to several feet down depending on frost depth. When it fails you tend to see wet ground, a spinning meter and weak pressure at every tap. We start by closing the house shutoff and watching the dial. A moving dial with the house valve closed means an underground leak. Then a ground microphone narrows the spot, so the crew opens one small pit rather than trenching the whole run.

    PEX and Copper Water Line Repair in Horizon West

We cut out the bad section of copper and sweat or press in a new length, and for PEX we use the matching crimp or expansion tool so the joint is made the way the manufacturer intends.
