    Frequently Asked Questions About Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Horizon West

    A standard flush and inspection usually runs $125 to $250 depending on tank size and access. Anode replacement typically adds $75 to $200 in parts and labor, and powered anodes cost more.

    Around Horizon West, flushing the water heater yearly and descaling tankless units helps keep mineral buildup under control. Mineral content adds to sediment buildup in hot water tanks, which reduces efficiency and can damage heating elements or tank linings over time. Regular flushing is especially important in hard water areas.

    The anode rod, also known as a sacrificial rod, is a metal rod inside your hot water tank designed to corrode before the tank itself. It protects the tank lining from rust and corrosion, which matters most when water carries a lot of minerals, and it extends the lifespan of your unit.

    Regular, professional maintenance can often be a condition for keeping your manufacturer's warranty valid. Check your warranty documentation, but generally, proof of annual service can help keep your warranty intact.

    A typical hot water tank maintenance service generally takes about 1 to 2 hours. This includes draining, flushing, inspection and any minor adjustments or part replacements like an anode rod.

    Most flushes and inspections take 45 minutes to 90 minutes. Heavily scaled tanks or anode replacements in tight closets can take a bit longer.

    Routine hot water tank maintenance, such as flushing and inspections, typically does not require a permit in Horizon West. However, if major components are replaced or the entire unit is swapped out, a plumbing permit may be necessary. Our team handles all necessary permit applications.
