    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Horizon West, FL

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through PVC or other approved pipe. Without needing a chimney, the vent can exit through a side wall within the allowed length. A 120-volt outlet is required. Termination clearances follow the manual.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Horizon West, FL

    Gas tankless units fire at up to 199,000 BTU, far more than a 40,000 BTU tank burner. The higher input means a bigger gas line and a dedicated vent, often PVC on condensing models.     Proper Sizing and Venting in Horizon West, FL

    Sizing and venting decide whether a gas heater runs safe and efficient. We size the heater from your peak use, then match the vent to the manufacturer table and code. A small vent can backdraft. Draft gets tested on every job.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Horizon West

    Atmospheric vent heaters rely on hot exhaust rising through a metal flue, with no fan. They cost the least, but the vent has to be the right size, slope upward and keep its clearances. Old vents get checked. Corroded or undersized pipe gets replaced.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Installation in Horizon West, FL

    High-efficiency gas heaters use condensing technology to pull extra heat out of the exhaust. Because the exhaust comes out cool and wet, they vent through PVC and need a condensate drain, which changes where the heater can go and what the install involves. Rebates may apply.
