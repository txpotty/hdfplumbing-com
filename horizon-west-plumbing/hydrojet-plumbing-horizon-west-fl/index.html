    Sewage in a tub or floor drain means the main is blocked. Hold off on running water. Our technician opens the line, then jets it to remove the cause, and a camera pass after jetting shows whether the backup came from buildup alone or from a broken or root-filled section that needs repair.

    Tree Root Removal with Hydrojetting in Horizon West

    Roots find their way into sewer lines through cracked joints, and older clay pipe has a joint every few feet. We use a spinning root-cutter nozzle. After cutting, the jet flushes the root pieces down to the main, and the camera shows which joints let the roots in so you know where a future repair or liner would go.

    Hydrojetting for Cast Iron Pipes in Horizon West, FL

    Older cast iron drains corrode and build scale on the inner wall, leaving a rough surface that grabs debris. Jetting can remove that buildup. Thin or cracked cast iron gets gentler settings or a lining recommendation instead.
