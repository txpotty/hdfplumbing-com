    Galvanized Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Horizon West, FL

    Older homes may still have galvanized steel pipe that rusts from the inside out. Low pressure, brown water and leaks at threaded joints are the typical signs. We remove or abandon the galvanized lines and replace them with PEX or copper, and your water pressure and water quality usually improve the same day the new lines go into service.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Horizon West

    Copper is the traditional choice, and Type L copper has a thicker wall than the Type M that went into many older homes. It holds up well, does not mind sunlight in an exposed run and has a long track record behind it. It costs more. Our plumbers solder joints with lead-free solder or use press fittings, support the pipe at the spacing the code calls for and pressure test the system before any wall is closed.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Horizon West, FL

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside, and over decades the inside diameter closes down to a fraction of what it was. Pressure drops slowly. Brown water after the house sits overnight, weak flow at upstairs showers and leaks at threaded joints are the usual signs that galvanized is near the end. New PEX or copper takes its place. Owners usually notice stronger flow the first day.

    Copper Repiping in Horizon West, FL

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes, with lead-free solder or ProPress fittings. Copper takes a bit longer. Copper repipes usually cost more and take a day longer than PEX, mostly because every turn needs a fitting.

    Repiping for Hard Water in Horizon West, FL

    Around Horizon West, flushing the water heater yearly and descaling tankless units helps keep mineral buildup under control. Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside pipe, and over years it narrows the bore and speeds up corrosion in metal lines. Pressure suffers. For homes with hard water, we usually recommend PEX-A, which resists scale and does not corrode, and we can add a softener loop or a whole-house filter during the repipe. Adding the loop now costs less than later.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Horizon West

    Kitec, an orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe with brass fittings, and gray polybutylene are both known for failures. Many homes built from the 1980s through the early 2000s have one or the other. The pipe at the water heater usually tells us which one you have, and we replace the full system so the home is easier to insure and to sell.

    Residential Repiping Plans in Horizon West
