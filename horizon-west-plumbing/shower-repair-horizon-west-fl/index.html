    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Horizon West

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. Water that seeps from the arm connection runs back along the pipe into the wall, and you only find out when the paint on the other side starts to bubble. Fresh thread tape fixes it. Handheld hoses and slide bars can go in during the same visit.

    Same-Day Shower Leak Repair in Horizon West, FL

    Shut off the main first if water is getting where it should not.     Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Horizon West, FL

    Stains on the ceiling below are often the first clue. We use a moisture meter on the walls and a flood test on the pan to tell a leaking valve from a cracked pan, a failed grout line or a loose drain body. Then we fix the source.     Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Horizon West, FL

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. That means a worn diverter steals pressure from the head, a clogged tub drain slows the shower and a leaky valve shows up at both the spout and the head. We check all of it. We also check the overflow gasket, a frequent hidden leak.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Horizon West, FL

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open. The Moen 1222 runs the Posi-Temp line, and the Delta RP46074 runs the Monitor 13 and 14 series, and both wear out on a schedule you can almost set a calendar by. We stock both. Many of these carry a lifetime warranty to the original owner.

    Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Horizon West, FL

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. Drain cleaner usually misses the clog. A hair catcher over the strainer keeps the next one from forming.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Horizon West, FL

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Minerals fill the nozzles and the screen behind them, and the spray fades so gradually you barely notice until it is a mist. Cleaning often works. A clogged cartridge gets replaced, then we test the flow.
