    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Jetting in Kendall

    Most jetting jobs in Kendall run $300 to $800. The spread comes down to clog severity, pipe length and size, and access. Mains cost more. We confirm the price on site before starting.

    Done right, jetting is safe on PVC, cast iron and clay in decent shape. Badly rusted or broken pipe can fail under pressure, so we scope questionable lines first. Pressure is adjustable.

    Jetting with a root-cutting nozzle shreds root masses and flushes them out. That clears the line for now, but the roots came through a crack or joint and will grow back, so a camera look afterward is worth doing. Fixing the joint ends it.

    A snake bores a hole through the clog. A jetter sends pressurized water through a nozzle that scours the whole inside of the pipe, taking grease and scale off the walls instead of leaving them there to catch the next clog. Results hold longer.

    Every year or two works for most homes in Kendall. Big trees or a grease history can mean yearly jetting, and restaurants more often. Your tech can advise.

    Usually one to three hours. Main lines and heavy root jobs can take longer, especially if we need to run a camera before and after.
