    Commercial Drain Cleaning in Kendall, FL

    Tub and Shower Drain Cleaning in Kendall, FL

    Showers clog slowly, then all at once. Hair builds a mat under the strainer.     Kitchen Drain Cleaning in Kendall

    Grease is the big one in kitchens. It goes down warm and sets up like candle wax in the pipe. Bits of food and soap catch on that coating until the 1-1/2 inch line is down to the size of a pencil, and we clear it with a drum machine that is safe for PVC and old cast iron alike.

    Drain Cleaning Cost Compared to Repairs in Kendall, FL

    Cleaning almost always costs less than digging. We try the cheap fix first. If the same line clogs every few months, the camera may show a belly, a crushed section or roots that cleaning only delays, and at that point we lay out the price of repeat cleanings next to a spot repair or a liner so you can compare.

    Hydro Jetting for Drains in Kendall

    Jetting does what a cable cannot. Water at around 3,000 to 4,000 PSI scours the whole pipe wall. Cabling opens a path, but jetting strips the buildup off the walls. It works best on a kitchen or main line that keeps clogging every few months no matter how often it gets cabled.

    Outdoor & Storm Drain Cleaning in Kendall

    Yard drains and downspout lines fill with leaves, grit and roots. Hurricanes and tropical storms bring flooding, power loss, and sewer backups to Florida, often swamping drainage systems for days. Puddles against the slab after rain mean the line is not moving water. We jet or cable the line to the outlet and pull the debris out of the catch basin, so the next storm runs off instead of soaking into the soil next to the house.

    Kitchen Sink Drain Cleaning in Kendall

    Coffee grounds and eggshells are a sink's worst enemies. Both sink to the bottom of the pipe and pack down hard. Our tech feeds a cable through the trap arm and works the clog until the line takes full flow, then checks the disposal and the dishwasher connection, since either one can push food into the line. A standard sink clog is usually under an hour.
