    Dishwasher Connection and Air Gap Setup in Kendall

    Most InSinkErator units have a dishwasher inlet with a factory knockout plug inside. We knock that plug out, clear the debris and connect the dishwasher drain hose with a proper clamp. We then install or verify an air gap or high loop so dirty sink water cannot siphon back into the dishwasher. Forgetting the knockout is one of the most common DIY mistakes and leads to a dishwasher that will not drain. We test both appliances together before we go.

    Insinkerator Batch Feed vs. Continuous Feed Installation in Kendall

    We install both batch feed and continuous feed Insinkerator models in Kendall homes. Batch feed disposals operate only when the stopper is in place, offering a safety advantage, while continuous feed models run as long as the switch is on. Our plumbers can advise on the best option for your lifestyle and kitchen usage, then perform the installation with precision, making sure the appropriate electrical connections and switch mechanisms are correctly integrated for your chosen model.
