    What Goes Into a Sewer Line Replacement in Kendall, FL

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Kendall

    A collapsed run or a long belly usually means we dig. We open a trench along the old line, pull the broken pipe and lay new PVC on compacted bedding at a steady fall of an eighth to a quarter inch per foot. Where we can, the sod is cut and set aside before the digging starts. The inspector sees open pipe. We compact the backfill in layers, which keeps a dip from forming along the trench.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Replacement in Kendall, FL

    Orangeburg is tar-soaked wood fiber. It was installed in a lot of homes in the decades after the war, and over time it softens, blisters and squashes into an oval that catches everything going by. Clay holds up better, except at the joints. Several bad spots on one run usually make full replacement the better buy.

    Emergency Sewer Collapse Repair in Kendall

    Every drain in the house stops when a lateral collapses. The tech scopes the line, marks the break and lets you know whether the dig can start next day or has to wait on locates. Utility marking takes a few days in most areas. We can often set up a bypass so one bathroom stays usable.

    Cured-in-Place Sewer Lining in Kendall

    Lining builds a new pipe inside the old one. After cutting roots and jetting the line, we pull in a resin-filled liner and inflate it until it cures against the old pipe wall. It needs a pipe that still holds its shape. It is a strong choice under slabs and finished rooms. You will get a straight answer on whether yours is a candidate.

    Commercial Sewer Lateral Work in Kendall, FL

    Apartment buildings, offices and restaurants in Kendall, FL need lateral work too. Bigger flow and heavier grease mean careful sizing, extra cleanouts for jetting and attention to the grease interceptor tie-in. Some jobs need traffic control. Tenant notices and work windows get planned ahead.

    Fixing a Bellied Sewer by Trench in Kendall, FL

    A belly is a low spot that holds water. Bursting and lining follow the old path, so they keep the belly, and the only way to correct the fall is to dig, regrade the bedding and lay new pipe at a steady slope. Rock adds time. We price that in after the locate shows depth. Each joint gets checked before the inspector sees it.
