    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Kendall

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. Instead of removing hardness, they convert it into a form that resists sticking, which reduces scale without salt or brine waste. Little upkeep is needed. They suit moderate hardness.

    Fleck Water Softener Installation in Kendall, FL

    Fleck valves, especially the 5600SXT and 7000SXT, are on a lot of the softeners we install. They are simple to program, easy to rebuild and parts are stocked almost everywhere, which matters when a seal fails ten years in. We program yours to your water. We confirm the setup before leaving.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Kendall

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. We install RO units under the kitchen sink with a dedicated faucet, and many can also feed a refrigerator water line or an ice maker. Pairs well with a softener. Filters get changed on schedule.

    Culligan Water Softener Installation in Kendall

    Culligan systems are common in homes around here, and we install and connect them. Each install follows the brand's specs for drain, bypass and power, then we program it to your water test. We handle all of it. A loop saves time.

    Water Softener Replacement & Upgrades in Kendall, FL

    Upgrading an old timer-based softener is one of the easiest ways to cut salt use. Modern units meter water use, send salt reminders by app and come in larger capacities for growing families. The savings add up. We swap it out in a few hours.

    Water Softener Installation Cost in Kendall, FL

    Cost depends on the system type, its capacity and whether a loop already exists. An existing loop keeps the price down, and adding a loop adds labor and materials. Filters add cost. Your written quote lists equipment, labor and setup.

    Pelican Water Softener Installation in Kendall, FL

    Pelican sells both salt-based softeners and salt-free conditioners, often direct to homeowners. When a homeowner has already bought one, we handle the plumbing, the bypass and the startup flush the manual requires. Skipping the flush causes trouble. Every step gets done.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Kendall, FL

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. HDF Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. Most effective option.
