    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Repair in Kendall

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. Salt sits there unused, and regeneration fails. After breaking up the crust, we clear the sludge and test the float and brine well. A cracked brine tank gets swapped for a new one.

    Control Valve Repair in Kendall

    Water that runs to the drain all day usually means the control valve is stuck between cycles. HDF Plumbing technicians rebuild these valves with the correct seal and spacer kit for your model. Mechanical or electronic, we fix the timing. Parts for the common valves are stocked, which keeps most repairs to one visit.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Kendall

    Hard water coming back usually means the softener has stopped regenerating. A failed timer motor, a bad circuit board or a stuck meter turbine are the usual causes, along with settings lost in a power outage. Each part gets tested. After the repair, the valve is programmed for your household size and hardness so you have soft water by the next cycle.

    Control Valve Troubleshooting and Repair in Kendall, FL

    Stuck pistons, worn seals and failed drive motors cause most control valve trouble, and the symptoms are hard water, constant regeneration or a leak at the head. We diagnose the valve on site. Our plumbers carry seal kits and pistons for Fleck, Clack and Autotrol heads, which covers a big share of the softeners installed in homes around here.

    Water Softener Leak Detection & Repair in Kendall, FL

    Cracked tanks, loose fittings and failed O-rings are behind most softener leaks. Even a slow drip wastes water and soaks the floor. Once we find the source, from the bypass to the drain connection, the failed seal or fitting gets replaced. Then it gets a pressure test.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Kendall, FL

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. A clogged injector leaves the brine tank full of water and the resin never gets recharged. We clean or replace it and check the brine line for air leaks.

    Water Softener Leak Repair in Kendall, FL

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads and block the exchange. Depending on the test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced. New resin restores full capacity.
