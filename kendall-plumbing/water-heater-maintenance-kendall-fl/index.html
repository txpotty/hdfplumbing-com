    T&P Valve and Safety Testing in Kendall, FL

    The temperature and pressure relief valve keeps your water heater from building dangerous pressure. We test it by lifting the lever and checking that it opens and reseats. A valve that leaks or will not open gets swapped out. We also check that the discharge pipe runs to an approved location. Expansion tanks are checked for proper air charge where installed.

    Electric Water Heater Element Service in Kendall, FL

    Electric water heaters use upper and lower heating elements controlled by thermostats. Sediment can coat the lower element and cause it to fail. We test both elements and thermostats with the power off and replace parts as necessary. A failed element is often the reason for lukewarm water or running out of hot water quickly. Proper testing avoids replacing parts that are still good.

    Standard Tank Water Heater Maintenance in Kendall

    Our standard tank water heater maintenance includes flushing the tank to remove sediment buildup. Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving heavy scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. We also inspect the anode rod, test the temperature and pressure relief valve, and check for any leaks or corrosion on the tank and connections. This service helps prevent premature failure and improves heating efficiency, keeping your energy bills in check.

    Gas Water Heater Burner and Venting Check in Kendall

    Gas water heaters need a clean burner and a proper vent to run safely. We inspect the burner flame, clean the combustion chamber and check the thermocouple or flame sensor. The vent is checked for proper draft and secure connections. Blocked or loose vents can let carbon monoxide into your home. We also check the gas line and shutoff valve for any leaks.

    Emergency Water Heater Inspection in Kendall, FL

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Kendall

    Tankless units are especially sensitive to hard water scale, which clogs the heat exchanger and triggers error codes. We isolate the unit with its service valves and circulate a descaling solution with a pump for the recommended time. We also clean the inlet water filter and check the unit's error history. Most manufacturers recommend descaling at least once a year in hard water areas. Regular service keeps flow rates and temperatures consistent.
