    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Kendall, FL

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match the voltage and breaker size to your panel, and because electric tanks need no vent, they can go in a closet, an attic or a laundry room where a gas unit could not. A drain pan goes under closet installs. Quiet and simple.

    Gas Water Heater Installation in Kendall, FL

    Gas heaters recover faster than electric, often refilling a 50 gallon tank in under an hour. We install the gas line, the sediment trap and the vent connector, then check draft at the hood with a match or a smoke pencil to confirm exhaust is going up and out. Then we check for leaks. We add an expansion tank if code calls for it.

    Commercial Water Heater Installation in Kendall

    Restaurants, salons and offices burn through hot water at rates no household tank can keep up with. We install commercial tanks over 100,000 BTU and racked tankless systems, with the venting and gas supply those loads require. We schedule around your hours. Night installs are available.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Kendall, FL

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Gas tankless models usually need a bigger gas line than the old tank had, plus a dedicated vent run through the wall or roof. Upfront cost is higher. We confirm your meter and line can handle it first.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Kendall

    Closed plumbing systems trap the extra volume water gains when it heats. Insurance companies in Florida often ask about plumbing age and material, so permitted work by a licensed plumber can help with coverage. A small expansion tank on the cold line absorbs that extra volume, which keeps the relief valve from weeping and saves wear on faucets and fittings. We pre-charge it to your house pressure. It mounts on the cold inlet.

    Major Brand Water Heater Installation in Kendall, FL

    Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith and Noritz are the brands we install most. Every manufacturer sets its own clearance, venting and relief valve rules, and following them is what keeps the warranty good. Our crews know each one. You can compare warranty length and recovery rate with us before you buy.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Kendall

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. Every attic heater needs a drain pan with a line to the outside or a drain, a relief line routed properly and a clear path for the next tank to come in and out. Ceilings are at stake. If moving it makes sense, we can relocate the heater.

    Brand-Specific Water Heater Installation in Kendall

    Each manufacturer publishes its own install rules, and they are not identical. Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White and Navien each spell out clearances, vent lengths and relief valve placement, and a warranty claim can be denied if those were ignored. Our crews install to spec. That protects your coverage.
