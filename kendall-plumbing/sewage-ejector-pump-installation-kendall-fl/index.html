    Zoeller Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Kendall

    Zoeller's sewage line covers home and heavier-duty use, in cast iron with tethered or vertical float switches. Tall lifts need bigger pumps.

    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Kendall

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps look alike in a catalog but do very different jobs. A sump pump moves groundwater from a perimeter drain to the yard, while an ejector moves sewage from toilets and sinks to the sewer, and the ejector basin has to be sealed and vented because of the gas. They are not interchangeable.

    Laundry and Wet Bar Ejector Pumps in Kendall, FL

    Below-grade laundry rooms and wet bars produce gray water that still needs to be lifted to the sewer. A smaller laundry tray pump or a compact ejector can handle it, sized for the 15 or more gallons a washer dumps at once, with a lint filter so fibers do not wrap the impeller. Lint kills small pumps.

    Duplex and Commercial Ejector Systems in Kendall, FL

    Commercial buildings, multi-unit properties and busy homes often need duplex systems with an alternating control panel. Alternating the lead pump spreads wear, and when one fails or flow spikes, the second starts on its own and the panel sounds an alarm. Redundancy matters here.

    Basement Bathroom Ejector Systems in Kendall

    Basement bathrooms sit below the building sewer, so the toilet, shower and sink drain to a sealed basin instead of flowing by gravity. We saw the floor, trench drains to the basin, set it, carry a two-inch vent to the roof and run discharge to the main drain through a check valve and shutoff. The slab gets repoured.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin Installation in Kendall, FL

    The basin is where the system lives, and a 30-gallon size is common for a single basement bathroom. A larger basin means longer, less frequent cycles that spare the motor and switch, so we go bigger when the pit takes a laundry or several fixtures. Lids must be gas-tight.

    Emergency Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Kendall

    An ejector failure usually announces itself with the alarm, followed by slow drains and a smell near the pit. Stop all use of the basement fixtures and call, because once a plumber is on site most emergency replacements take just a few hours. Trucks stock popular models.
