    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Kendall

    While both sump pumps and sewage ejector pumps remove water, their applications are distinct. HDF Plumbing provides clear guidance and installation for both in Kendall. A sump pump handles groundwater and storm runoff, typically at a basement's lowest point to prevent flooding. A sewage ejector pump, however, is designed for wastewater containing solids from toilets, sinks and showers in below-grade plumbing. We make sure you get the correct pump for your drainage needs, preventing costly mistakes.

    Laundry and Wet Bar Ejector Pumps in Kendall, FL

    Below-grade laundry rooms and wet bars produce gray water that still needs to be lifted to the sewer line. A smaller ejector or laundry tray pump can handle washer discharge and sink drainage. We install a lint filter where needed to guard the pump. These systems are compact and can usually be installed without major floor demolition.

    Duplex and Commercial Ejector Systems in Kendall, FL

    Commercial buildings, multi-unit properties and homes with heavy below-grade use often need duplex systems with two pumps and an alternating control panel. If one pump fails, the other keeps working while an alarm alerts staff. We size the basin and pumps for peak flow and install controls to code. Maintenance schedules are provided to keep both pumps in good condition.

    Basement Bathroom Ejector Systems in Kendall

    Basement bathrooms sit below the building sewer, so toilets, showers and sinks drain into a sealed basin instead of flowing by gravity. We excavate the floor, set a basin typically 30 to 50 gallons and connect each fixture drain. A solids-handling pump lifts waste up and into the main drain line. The basin lid is sealed and vented to keep odors out.
