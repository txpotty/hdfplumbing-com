    What Our Commercial Plumbers Do in Kendall, FL

    Commercial Pipe Repair and Repiping in Kendall, FL

    Older commercial buildings often have galvanized supply lines that are rusted nearly shut, or copper that has pinholed along hot water runs. Patching works for a while. When pinholes keep moving around the building, repiping the worst zones in Type L copper or PEX usually beats paying for more emergency visits. The first step is mapping the risers and branches. Then we phase the work by floor or by tenant so the building stays open.

    Backflow Assembly Testing and Rebuilds in Kendall

    Most water providers require annual tests on commercial backflow assemblies, and many send late notices that come with fines. Test early. Our testers handle RPZ, double check and PVB devices with annually calibrated gauges and submit results the way your provider wants them. If a device fails, we usually rebuild it the same day.

    Backflow Testing and Certification in Kendall

    Each backflow test takes about 30 minutes per assembly and requires a short water shutoff to that line. Plan for a brief outage. We read each check valve and the relief valve on a calibrated gauge and record the numbers on the standard test form. Passing devices get tagged. We file the report with your water provider and keep a copy on our side for next year.

    Commercial Leak Detection in Kendall, FL

    Water bills that jump 30 percent with no change in business usually mean a hidden leak or a stuck fixture. Start with the meter. Zone-by-zone shutoffs and meter readings narrow it down, then acoustic sensors, thermal imaging and tracer gas pinpoint the spot. Sometimes the culprit is just a running toilet. One stuck flush valve can waste several thousand gallons a week.

    Tenant Improvement and Build-Out Plumbing in Kendall, FL

    New tenants usually need restrooms moved, a break room added or a kitchen built in a shell space. Our crew installs from the plans, works around the framers and electricians and books inspections early so the opening date holds. Old lines get capped. Abandoned drains and supplies are cut back to the main and capped properly, which prevents dead legs and sewer gas later.

    Grease Trap Installation in Kendall

    A new restaurant or a kitchen expansion usually needs a grease interceptor sized from the fixtures that drain to it. Small kitchens can get by with an under-sink trap, but bigger operations often need an in-ground interceptor of 500 gallons or more. Sizing is set by code. We work from your kitchen plan, set the unit at the right depth and vent it so it passes the pretreatment inspection on the first try.

    Commercial Drain Cleaning and Hydro-Jetting in Kendall

    Commercial drains carry grease, paper towels and food waste in volumes no house ever sees. A cable just pokes a hole. Our jetters push water at 3,000 to 4,000 psi through a spinning nozzle that scours the full pipe wall, which clears 4-inch and 6-inch lines back to bare pipe. Jetting keeps the line open for months instead of days. We see the most benefit in restaurants, schools and multi-family stacks.

    Grease Interceptor and Line Service in Kendall, FL

    Grease does not stay in the trap if the trap is full or the baffles are broken. It ends up in your line. We inspect interceptors, replace failed baffles and outlet tees and jet the kitchen lines that feed them, which removes hardened grease that a cable just slides past. A jetted line holds up much better on a busy night.
