    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in Kendall

    Rooting a sink, tub or toilet usually costs $125 to $300. Main sewer rooting through a cleanout usually runs $200 to $500. Adding a jetting pass brings the total to about $450 to $1,000, depending on line length and how much grease is in it. After-hours calls cost more.

    Rental machines are easy to get, but they are dangerous in untrained hands. Cables can kink, whip and wrap around a wrist in a second. They also get stuck. A rental cable stuck in an old clay line can turn a $300 rooter call into a dig to retrieve it.

    We run RIDGID, Spartan and Gorlitz augers, plus sewer cameras and jetters from the same tier of commercial makers. These machines take daily abuse and keep working. Brand matters less than setup. A good machine with the wrong cutter can still damage a pipe, so we spend as much care picking the head as we do the machine.

    A limited guarantee for a set number of days comes with most rooter work. If the line clogs again within that time, we return at no extra charge.

    Routine drain cleaning in Kendall, FL does not usually need a permit. Major repair, sewer replacement or excavation in the yard generally does. We handle that paperwork. If the rooter work turns up a problem that needs a permit, we tell you before any repair gets scheduled.

    Snaking runs a spinning cable with a cutting head through the clog. A jetter uses water at thousands of psi to scour the inside of the pipe from wall to wall. Jetting leaves the pipe cleaner. Snaking handles one-time clogs, while jetting is for grease lines and scaled mains that keep coming back.

    Keep grease, coffee grounds and wipes out of drains. Hair strainers in tubs and showers help. After using the disposal, run hot water for a bit and scrape food into the trash first to keep the kitchen line clearer. Older lines with roots need scheduled rooting every 12 to 24 months.
