    Grinding Noise Repair in Delray Beach

    Jam Clearing and Motor Repair in Delray Beach

    Bones, fruit pits and bottle caps lodge between the grind ring and the impeller lugs, and the motor stalls.     The InSinkErator lineup runs from Badger units to the Evolution series, and we repair both.     Noise and Odor Diagnosis in Delray Beach

    A rattle that stops after a few seconds is usually a spoon or a pit bouncing in the chamber. A constant high whine means something else. That whine often comes from worn motor bearings, and once bearings start to fail the noise only gets worse until the motor seizes. Odors usually trace to the splash guard. We pull it, clean the chamber walls and replace the guard if it is torn.

    Noisy Disposal Repair in Delray Beach

    Grinding, rattling or a hum that sounds rough usually mean trouble inside the unit. A loose mount will rattle. We check the mount, then look for debris, then run the motor empty, since a whine with an empty chamber means the bearings are going.
