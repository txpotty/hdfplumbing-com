    Two pumps on one riser cover both kinds of failure, a dead motor and a dead outlet.     Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Delray Beach, FL

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. That makes service easy. They are louder than submersibles. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Delray Beach, FL

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. The charger keeps the battery topped off. Batteries need replacing every few years. They matter most below finished rooms.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Delray Beach

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. They fit pits where two separate pumps would crowd each other. That saves some labor. They suit homes that want backup protection but do not want to enlarge the pit.
