    Sewer Repair Methods for West Palm Beach, FL Homes

    Root Intrusion Repair in West Palm Beach, FL

    Cutting roots treats the symptom. Fixing the joint treats the cause. After clearing the line, we look at each rooted joint on camera and decide between a short sectional liner, a full liner or cutting in new pipe, based on the number of bad joints and the spacing between them. One or two joints is a small job. A line rooted end to end is a lining or replacement job.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in West Palm Beach

    Orangeburg is wood fiber soaked in coal tar. It flattens with age. Cast iron from the same era rusts from the inside, builds scale and eventually splits along the bottom where the sewage runs, and once the camera shows failures along the whole run, patching it stops being worth the money. PVC goes in, usually end to end. Trenchless bursting is often an option.

    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in West Palm Beach

    A self-leveling camera goes in at the cleanout or through a pulled toilet. We record the whole run. Cracks, root intrusion, offset joints and standing water all show up on the screen, and the sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot and depth on the ground above, which keeps the dig to one small pit. Older homes in West Palm Beach often hide two or three problems in one line, and the video catches all of them.

    Pipe Burst & Collapse Repair in West Palm Beach, FL

    A collapsed sewer pipe stops all flow at the point of failure. Every drain backs up. Collapses usually come from corroded cast iron, crushed clay under a driveway or tar-paper pipe that has finally flattened, and we locate the break and its depth before we start digging. We dig at the mark and replace the broken length with PVC. Lining cannot fix a full collapse.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in West Palm Beach

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. Snaking just opens a path. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and it is the standard prep step before any pipe lining job. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We check the pipe on video before we jet it.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in West Palm Beach, FL

    Trenchless repair means we fix the pipe without opening a trench along its full length. Usually two small pits. Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE line through the old one and splits the old pipe outward, while cured-in-place lining pushes a resin-soaked felt tube into the old pipe and hardens it into a new wall. Both save driveways and mature trees. Not every line qualifies, and we will say so.

    Sewer Pipe Replacement Options in West Palm Beach, FL

    Replacement makes sense when the camera shows failures every few feet. Spot repairs will not keep up. We price open trench replacement, pipe bursting and full-length lining side by side for your Florida property, with the footage, depth and surface restoration spelled out for each one. The right pick depends on depth and access.
