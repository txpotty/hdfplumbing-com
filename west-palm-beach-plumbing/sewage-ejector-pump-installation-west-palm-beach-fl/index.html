    Commercial Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in West Palm Beach, FL

    Businesses in West Palm Beach, FL with restrooms, kitchens or break rooms below grade need ejector systems that keep up with heavier, steadier use than a house. Commercial setups often run duplex pumps with an alternating panel, so one pump can fail and the other keeps the restrooms open. Closed restrooms cost business. Each pump can carry the load alone.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin Installation in West Palm Beach, FL

    The basin holds everything, and a 30-gallon size is common for a single basement bathroom. Bigger basins let the pump run longer, fewer times a day, which is easier on the motor and switch, so we size up when the pit serves a laundry or several fixtures. Lids must be gas-tight.

    Zoeller Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in West Palm Beach

    Zoeller's sewage line covers home and heavier-duty use, in cast iron with tethered or vertical float switches. We pick the model by horsepower and pump curve for your lift, and a typical basement bathroom lands on a four-tenths or half horsepower unit with two-inch solids handling. Tall lifts need bigger pumps.

    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in West Palm Beach

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps seem similar at first but do very different jobs. A sump pump moves groundwater from a perimeter drain to the yard, while an ejector moves sewage from toilets and sinks to the sewer, and the ejector basin has to be sealed and vented because of the gas. They are not interchangeable.

    New Construction Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in West Palm Beach, FL

    On a new build, the basin goes in before the slab is poured. That is the cheapest time, since there is no concrete to cut, and we stub the drains, the vent and the discharge to the plans so the rough-in inspection goes smoothly. Builders like the timing.

    Basement Bathroom Ejector Systems in West Palm Beach

    Basement bathrooms sit below the building sewer, so every fixture drains to a sealed pit instead of flowing by gravity. We cut the slab, trench the drain lines to the basin, set it, run a two-inch vent up through the house to the roof and pipe the discharge to the building drain with a check valve and shutoff. Then we patch the concrete.

    Liberty Pumps Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in West Palm Beach, FL

    Liberty builds single and dual pump packages that arrive with the basin, pump, lid and fittings matched. That speeds up new installs, and their wide-angle float switches are less likely to hang up on the basin wall than older tethered floats, which is one of the most common reasons a pump fails to start. Switch failures cause many calls.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin & Pit Installation in West Palm Beach

    Digging the pit means cutting the slab and excavating below it, deep enough to set the basin flush with the finished floor. Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and damage buried water and sewer lines. We bed the basin on gravel and backfill around it so it cannot float when groundwater rises, then pour new concrete to seal the floor and lock the basin in place. A floating basin breaks pipe.
