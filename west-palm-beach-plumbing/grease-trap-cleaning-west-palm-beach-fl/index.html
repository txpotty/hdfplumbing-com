    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Lines in West Palm Beach, FL

    A cable machine pokes a hole through grease. Jetting actually removes it. We run a jetter at roughly 3,000 to 4,000 psi with a rotating nozzle that cuts the grease off the full circumference of the pipe and flushes it downstream. Afterward a camera pass confirms the pipe is clean.

    Grease Trap Hydro-Jetting in West Palm Beach, FL

    Some traps have years of grease baked onto the walls and inlet piping. Pumping alone leaves that crust in place. Jetting the empty tank and its inlet line strips it back to bare walls and gets back the capacity that crust was taking. Plan on about an extra hour.
