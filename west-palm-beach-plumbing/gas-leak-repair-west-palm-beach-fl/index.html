    Gas Line Repair and Replacement in West Palm Beach, FL

    Black iron pipe with threaded fittings is still the most common interior gas pipe, and the leak is nearly always at a thread, not in the middle of a length.     Appliance Connector Replacement in West Palm Beach

    Old uncoated brass connectors, the kind made before the mid-1970s, are the subject of consumer safety warnings because the flare nut can separate from the tube.
