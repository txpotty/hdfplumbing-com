    Hidden Wall and Ceiling Leaks in West Palm Beach

    Bubbling paint, soft drywall and a musty smell come up on most of our calls. All of them mean water. Thermal cameras find the wet area, and a moisture meter confirms it.     Sewer Line Leak Detection in West Palm Beach, FL

    A cracked drain line leaks wastewater into the soil, which can show up as a sewer smell in the yard, a soggy patch or slow drains. We use a camera for these. The camera goes in at the cleanout and runs to the main, recording cracks, offset joints and root entry in PVC, clay and cast iron, with a locator giving the exact depth and distance.

    Slab Leak Signs in West Palm Beach, FL

    Warm floor spots, cracked tile and a meter that never stops are the common signs. So is the sound of water with everything off.     Our Leak Detection Equipment in West Palm Beach

    Our trucks carry acoustic correlators and ground microphones, thermal cameras, line tracers, moisture meters and tracer gas kits. Each tool has a job. Acoustic gear hears pressurized water escaping, thermal cameras show temperature differences from hot water lines and wet drywall, and tracer gas finds leaks that make no sound at all on plastic pipe.

    Leak Testing for Multifamily and Offices in West Palm Beach

    Multifamily buildings and office parks have long supply systems that let leaks hide for months. Early detection means fewer tenant complaints and fewer insurance claims building-wide.

    Commercial Building Leaks in West Palm Beach, FL

    Restaurants, offices and apartment complexes in West Palm Beach lose money every day a leak runs.     Slab Leak Detection in West Palm Beach

    Concrete slab homes have supply lines buried in sand below the floor, and a pinhole there can run for months before anyone notices. A warm tile is the classic sign. We pressure test the hot and cold sides separately, then use a ground microphone to hear the leak through the concrete and confirm with a thermal camera when the leak is on the hot side.

Older homes with galvanized pipe or original water heater connectors benefit most from a yearly check, since those parts tend to fail all at once. It is a short visit.
