    Picking the Right Softener for West Palm Beach, FL Homes

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in West Palm Beach, FL

    Resin beads break down after 10 to 15 years, faster on chlorinated city water, and the softener keeps regenerating while the water slowly turns hard again. New resin in the old tank sometimes beats a full replacement on price. It depends on the valve. New resin under a tired valve rarely lasts.

    Replacing Pelican Water Systems in West Palm Beach

    Pelican systems usually combine a carbon filter with either a salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner in one setup on the main line. We replace the media or the whole system, depending on age. Installed to spec, the warranty stays valid.

    Softener Loop Installation in West Palm Beach, FL

    Softener loops are the pair of stubbed-out lines near the garage wall or water heater where a softener ties into the main. We can add a loop with shutoff valves and a bypass. Outdoor spigots stay on hard water. We run the drain to a standpipe or floor drain through an air gap.

    Upsizing a Whole-Home Softener in West Palm Beach

    Undersized softeners are common in homes that added a bathroom or a couple of teenagers since the original system went in. A 32,000-grain unit that regenerated weekly for a family of three may be running dry every three days now. Sizing is based on current usage.

    Urgent Softener Problems in West Palm Beach

    Not every softener failure is urgent. Water on the floor from the valve or an overflowing brine tank does count, and those calls get moved up. Bypass it until we arrive.

    Salt-Free Alternatives in West Palm Beach, FL

    Some homeowners do not want salt or have no drain near the main line. A TAC conditioner is the honest alternative there, and we tell people plainly that it prevents scale but will not give them the slick feel of soft water. Low-sodium diets are another reason.
