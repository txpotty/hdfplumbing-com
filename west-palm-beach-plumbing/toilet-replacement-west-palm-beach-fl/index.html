    Toilet Rough-In Adjustments in West Palm Beach, FL

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, usually 12 inches, sometimes 10 or 14 in older homes. Measure to the closet bolts, not the baseboard. If the new toilet's rough-in does not match, it either sits too far out or will not fit. Offset flanges can fix a small mismatch.

    High-Efficiency Toilet (HET) Upgrades in West Palm Beach, FL

    High-efficiency toilets carry a WaterSense label and a MaP score, which tells you how many grams of waste the toilet clears in one flush. Look for 800 or higher. A high score means fewer double flushes and fewer clogs, and that is where the real water savings come from.

    Rental and Multi-Unit Toilet Replacement in West Palm Beach, FL

    Landlords with several units do better standardizing on one toilet model with common parts, because the next flapper or fill valve is then the same part in every unit.     Wall-Hung Toilet Replacement in West Palm Beach

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, so replacing one means checking the carrier bolts, the gasket on the outlet and the condition of the wall finish around it. The carrier itself sometimes needs new bolts or a coupling. The new bowl gets matched to the carrier already in the wall whenever possible, which avoids opening the wall.
