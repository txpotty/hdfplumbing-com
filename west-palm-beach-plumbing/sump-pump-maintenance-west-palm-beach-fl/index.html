    Battery Backup System Testing in West Palm Beach, FL

    Storms knock out power when the pit fills fastest. That is the moment a backup pump earns its keep. We check the battery date, clean the terminals and confirm the charger is actually charging, since a dead charger quietly drains the battery to nothing over a few months.

    Commercial Building Sump Pumps in West Palm Beach, FL

    Parking garages, mechanical rooms and loading docks often drain to large pits with pumps rated at a horsepower or more. Those units need more frequent checks than a house pump.     Elevator Pit and Garage Pumps in West Palm Beach

    Elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms all collect water that has nowhere to go by gravity, so a pump has to lift it out.     Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in West Palm Beach, FL

    Not every sump sits in a basement. Crawlspaces and deep window wells often have small pumps that clear runoff after a storm. Our plumber clears the screen, tests the float and traces the discharge to daylight or an approved drain, since a line that ends under the house only relocates the water. When a rare freeze is forecast, covering outdoor valves and letting a faucet drip is usually enough to prevent damage.

    Commercial Sump Pump Maintenance in West Palm Beach, FL

    Commercial pits are often bigger and deeper, and many have two pumps on an alternating controller. We test both pumps, the alternator and the high-water alarm. You get a written record of each reading, which comes in handy for insurance renewals and inspections. Quarterly visits are common.

    Sump Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in West Palm Beach

    Silt and gravel wash into a pit through the drain tile every time it rains, and over a few years that layer can bury the intake screen. Our plumber drains the basin and digs the sediment out by hand. A clean pit lets the float move freely. The pump runs cooler too.

    Emergency Sump Pump Repair in West Palm Beach

    Float Switch Checks in West Palm Beach, FL

    The float switch tells the pump when to start and stop, and a float with too little travel between on and off makes the pump short cycle dozens of times an hour. We watch the float through a full cycle. Then we set the on and off points so the pump runs longer, less often.
