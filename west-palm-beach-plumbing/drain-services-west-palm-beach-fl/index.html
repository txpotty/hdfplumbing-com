    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Services in West Palm Beach

    The cost of drain cleaning in West Palm Beach can vary widely based on the complexity of the clog and the method required. Simple drain snaking might range from $150 to $350, while hydro jetting or main sewer line clearing could be $400 to $800 or more. We provide a detailed estimate after diagnosing the issue.

    Hydro jetting is a powerful drain cleaning method that uses high-pressure water to clear blockages and scour the inside of pipes. It's often necessary for severe clogs, tree root intrusions, grease buildup, or when traditional snaking isn't effective, particularly for main sewer lines.

    For most homes, a professional drain cleaning every one to two years is sufficient for preventative maintenance. However, homes with older plumbing, frequent hair or grease buildup, or known tree root issues may benefit from annual cleaning. Businesses, especially restaurants, often require more frequent service.

    Routine drain cleaning and jetting do not require permits. Sewer line repair or replacement usually does, and we handle those permits.

    Avoid pouring grease down drains, use drain screens in sinks and showers to catch hair and food particles, and be mindful of what gets flushed down toilets. Regular preventative maintenance, like an annual professional drain cleaning, can also be very effective.

    Hydro jetting is safe for pipe in sound condition, including PVC and most cast iron. We camera the line first so we do not jet a pipe that is currently cracked or collapsing.

    While readily available, we generally advise against using chemical drain cleaners. They can be corrosive, potentially damaging your pipes, especially older ones, and are often ineffective against tough clogs like tree roots or heavy grease. They also pose a risk to your health and the environment. Professional methods are safer and more effective.
