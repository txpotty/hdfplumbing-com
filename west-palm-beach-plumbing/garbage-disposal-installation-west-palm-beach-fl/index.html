    Standard Disposal Installation in West Palm Beach

    High-Horsepower Disposal Installation in West Palm Beach

    A 3/4 or 1 HP unit is bigger and heavier than a starter model, so we measure the cabinet first.     Disposals in Remodels and New Builds in West Palm Beach

    On new builds and remodels in West Palm Beach, we coordinate with the general contractor so the disposal, the sink and the dishwasher are planned together.     Same-Day Disposal Installation in West Palm Beach, FL

    Batch-Feed Disposal Installation in West Palm Beach, FL

    Batch-feed units only run with the lid in place, and the lid holds a magnet that closes a switch inside the unit. No wall switch is needed, which makes them handy in kitchens where running a new switch would mean opening finished walls. You grind one load at a time. Families with small kids often like the trade.

    Disposal Installation on an Existing Sink in West Palm Beach

    For a common 1/2 or 3/4 HP disposal, we remove the old unit if there is one and install the new one on the existing sink. We connect it to the drain, set the P-trap and wire it, then check the clearance under the sink, which can be tight in older West Palm Beach homes with shallow cabinets or a water filter mounted on the back wall. Tight cabinets need planning. A compact unit can solve it.

    Switching From Batch-Feed to Continuous-Feed in West Palm Beach, FL

    Going from batch-feed to continuous-feed means adding a way to switch it on. That can be a wall switch, an air switch button on the sink deck or a switched outlet under the sink, and the choice depends on how hard it is to run wire to the spot you want. An air button skips the wall wiring. The drain usually stays the same.

    High-Capacity Disposal Installation in West Palm Beach, FL

We check that the circuit can handle the motor and that the discharge path is sized for the heavier flow, since a big unit feeding a narrow or poorly sloped trap arm still clogs. Bigger units are heavier. They get supported during mounting.
