    Workstation Sink Installation in West Palm Beach, FL

    Workstation sinks have a ledge for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks, and many are deep single bowls with the drain off center. The trap layout follows the offset drain, which lets the disposal fit and leaves storage room on one side. Faucet reach matters too.

    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in West Palm Beach

    Both bowls of a double sink share one trap, using either a center outlet continuous waste or an end outlet tee. The layout depends on your disposal and dishwasher, because the disposal side changes where the tee sits and how much slope each bowl gets. We use heavy-wall fittings.

    Granite and Quartz Composite Sinks in West Palm Beach

    Composite sinks outweigh stainless bowls and usually thicker at the drain, so standard strainers sometimes need a longer threaded body. We check the strainer fit first. When the strainer is too short for the sink thickness, we replace it instead of cranking it down and chipping the drain opening.

    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in West Palm Beach

    An undermount depends on its clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. The bowl is mounted, the rim sealed with silicone and the sink braced while the adhesive sets, normally overnight before it can take a full bowl of water. Then come the strainers and trap.

    Drop-In Sink Replacement in West Palm Beach

    Pulling an old drop-in starts with cutting the caulk line and backing off the clips from below. Old clips are often rusted solid, so we bring a reciprocating saw and cut them off rather than prying on the counter. Laminate chips if you pry. Your new rim has to fully cover the old hole.

    Kitchen Sink Installation Cost Factors in West Palm Beach, FL

    Undermount and farmhouse installs cost more than a drop-in swap. Adding a disposal, moving the drain, lowering a wall stub or replacing old galvanized stubs all add labor, and we list each one separately on the quote. The countertop is priced separately.

    Farmhouse Sink Installation in West Palm Beach, FL

    Apron-front sinks rest on a support frame built into the cabinet, not on the countertop.
