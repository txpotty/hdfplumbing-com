    How We Keep Water Out of Walls in West Palm Beach, FL

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in West Palm Beach, FL

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries. The usual cause is a sprinkler head aimed at the house instead of the bed, or soil graded toward the foundation. We fix the water first. Then we clean the wall, let it dry and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in West Palm Beach

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and usually add a dehumidifier to hold humidity under 60 percent. Humidity causes condensation on cold lines and clogged AC condensate drains, a frequent source of hidden water damage. The joists stop rotting. Drier air under the floor also cuts down on the musty smell in the house.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in West Palm Beach, FL

    Houses built before modern drainage rules often have no footing drain at all, or a clay tile drain that collapsed decades ago. Houses from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s may have polybutylene supply pipe, which many insurers dislike and owners choose to replace. Fieldstone and old brick foundations need breathable products. A rigid coating on soft brick traps moisture and makes the face spall off over a few seasons, so we use lime-compatible or vapor-open coatings and pair them with a new interior drain. We repoint failed mortar joints before sealing.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in West Palm Beach

    A drip from a shower valve cartridge, a sweated copper stub or a loose drain slip nut can soak a wall cavity for months before paint bubbles. We find it with a moisture meter, a thermal camera that picks up the cooler wet area, and a pressure test on the supply lines. Then we cut a clean access hole. The bad fitting or section of pipe gets replaced. Wet insulation comes out, and fans dry the cavity before we close it up.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in West Palm Beach, FL

    Interior drain tile handles water that already got through the wall. We cut a channel in the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and run it to a sump basin so the pump can push the water outside. The wall still gets wet. The difference is that the water no longer ends up on your floor. This route costs less than digging outside and works where a driveway or deck blocks the wall.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in West Palm Beach

    Houses from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s may have polybutylene supply pipe, which many insurers dislike and owners choose to replace. Any wall below grade sits in soil that gets saturated after heavy rain or snowmelt, and that water pushes against the wall with steady hydrostatic pressure. We inject cracks, coat the wall inside or out, and add drain tile wherever water collects at the footing. A sump goes in if groundwater sits high. Some basements never need a sump.

    Basement Wall Sealing in West Palm Beach, FL

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Humidity causes condensation on cold lines and clogged AC condensate drains, a common source of hidden water damage. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then coat the face with a masonry waterproofing product that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. The musty smell usually fades within weeks. Those weep holes empty into the interior drain if the house has one.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in West Palm Beach

    Tile and grout are not waterproof. Water passes through grout every time you shower, so the layer behind the tile, a membrane over cement board or a foam board system, is what actually keeps the studs dry. We tear out failed tile, install new backer, and apply Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban at the thickness the manufacturer calls for. Corners, niches and valve holes get extra fabric or preformed pieces.
