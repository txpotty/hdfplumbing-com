    Resin Rebedding in West Palm Beach

    Chlorine and chloramine in treated water slowly swell and soften resin beads, and iron or sediment can coat them until capacity drops off. We try a resin cleaner first. If the bed is spent, we empty out the old resin and refill the tank with new high-capacity media, which runs far less than a new softener.

    Commercial Softener Service in West Palm Beach

    Commercial units work harder. Restaurants and laundromats can burn through a softener's capacity in a single day, so a twin-tank system keeps one tank online while the other recharges. We set visits around your business hours so the water never goes hard during a rush.

    Salt Bridge Removal and Brine Tank Cleaning in West Palm Beach, FL

    Humidity and cheap salt cause most salt bridges. The top layer fuses into a crust with an empty pocket below it. The softener keeps regenerating on schedule, but it draws plain water instead of brine, which leaves the resin without a recharge and hard water flows to every tap.

    Breaking Up Salt Bridges in West Palm Beach

    To check for a bridge, we push a broom handle straight down through the salt. A hard stop, then a drop, means a bridge. We break the crust, vacuum out the salt mush below it, then check the brine well, safety float, brine line and grid plate before refilling to about half full.

    Softener Maintenance Plans and Pricing in West Palm Beach, FL

    The plan is one flat yearly fee. It covers the service visit and both hardness tests, and parts installed on a plan visit come at a reduced price. For most homes it pays for itself the first time it catches a stuck float.

    Resin Cleaning and Regeneration Tuning in West Palm Beach, FL

    Iron is the most common resin fouler we see. It coats the beads orange and blocks the exchange sites. We feed a resin cleaner through the brine well, run a regeneration and retest, and if the capacity comes back we set the valve to regenerate more often so iron cannot load the bed again.

    Regeneration Programming and Hardness Testing in West Palm Beach, FL

    Plenty of softeners still run factory settings. The installer never changed them, or the household grew and nobody updated the valve. We enter your raw hardness and household size, then set a metered valve to regenerate by gallons used instead of every few days on a timer.
