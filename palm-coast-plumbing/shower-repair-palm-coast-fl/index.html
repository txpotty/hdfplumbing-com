    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Palm Coast, FL

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open. Moen Posi-Temp showers take the 1222, and Delta Monitor 13 and 14 showers take the RP46074, and both wear out on a fairly regular clock. We stock both. Many of these carry a lifetime warranty to the original owner.

    Commercial Shower Repair in Palm Coast, FL

    Gyms and apartment buildings run showers dozens of times a day. Commercial valves get heavy-duty cartridges, and we schedule around your busy hours so the showers stay open when people need them. Vandal-resistant heads are available. Limit stops are set to code on each valve.

    Slow Shower Drain Repair in Palm Coast, FL

    Standing water at your ankles is the first sign. Minerals bind soap scum and hair into a tough mat that clings to the pipe walls and gets thicker with every shower. An auger cuts through it. Heavy buildup further down gets cleared with a water jetter instead of harsh chemicals.

    Shower Handle Leak Repair in Palm Coast

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water leaking behind the trim slips into the wall cavity at the valve opening and soaks the drywall while the tile face stays dry. We pull the trim and look. It is usually a loose bonnet nut or a worn O-ring.

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Palm Coast, FL

    Low pressure at the shower with good pressure at the sink narrows it down fast. That points to the head, the flow restrictor or the cartridge, not the main or the water heater, and it is usually something we can fix within an hour. We start with the head. If every fixture is weak, we check the regulator and the main.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Palm Coast

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. If the head drips with the valve closed, water is slipping past the cartridge, and a new head will not change that. The fix is in the valve. Drips from the arm threads just need fresh tape and a snug turn.

    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Palm Coast

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. A leak at the arm threads follows the pipe into the wall, and it shows up as bubbling paint in the next room. Fresh thread tape fixes it. We can add a handheld or slide bar while we are there.

    Mixing Valve Repair in Palm Coast

    Mixing valves blend hot and cold inside the wall. Over time the cartridge or spool sticks, and you get a shower that will not stay at one temperature or a handle that turns past where it should before the water warms up. A new cartridge usually fixes it. Valves with no parts left get a replacement plan and an honest look at the tile.
