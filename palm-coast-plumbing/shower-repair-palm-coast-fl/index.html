    Leaking Shower Valve Repair in Palm Coast

    A shower that drips after it is turned off typically has a worn cartridge or seals. Hard water scale scratches cartridge surfaces and breaks down O-rings faster than soft water does. We replace the cartridge with a genuine part and lubricate the valve with silicone grease. This stops the drip and makes the handle turn easily again.

    Emergency Shower Leak Repair in Palm Coast

    A severe shower leak can quickly cause extensive water damage to walls, floors, and ceilings. If you have a sudden, uncontrollable shower leak in your Palm Coast home, do not wait.     Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Palm Coast

    A malfunctioning showerhead can spray erratically, have reduced pressure, or even leak. Often, mineral deposits from the water supply can clog the nozzles, or internal components can wear out.     Shower Temperature Problem Repair in Palm Coast

    If your shower turns cold when someone flushes, the pressure-balance spool may be stuck. Your shower will hold a steady, comfortable temperature.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Palm Coast, FL

    A diverter sends water from the tub spout to the showerhead. When it wears out, water keeps running from the spout and shower pressure drops. We replace diverter spouts, three-handle diverter stems and multi-function diverter valves. This brings back full flow to the showerhead.
