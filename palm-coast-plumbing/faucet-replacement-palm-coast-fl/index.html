    Water-Saving Faucet Upgrades in Palm Coast

    Federal rules cap new kitchen and lav faucets at 2.2 gallons per minute. WaterSense lav faucets go lower, to 1.5 gallons per minute, and most people do not notice the difference because the aerator mixes air into the stream. It adds up over a year. A WaterSense lav faucet is an easy swap.

    Crusty Bathroom Faucet Replacement in Palm Coast, FL

    Crusty lav faucets are the most common swap we do. We pull the old centerset or widespread faucet, scrape the deck down to bare porcelain and install a new faucet with a matching pop-up drain so the finishes line up. It takes about an hour. The handles get set to close at the same angle.

    Urgent Faucet Replacement in Palm Coast, FL

    Water that will not shut off at the faucet is an urgent call. Turn the shutoff under the sink clockwise, and go to the main if that valve will not budge or starts dripping. We take urgent calls. A plumber stops the flow, removes the broken faucet and installs a new one from the truck.

    Tub and Shower Trim Replacement in Palm Coast, FL

    Tub and shower trim can be replaced without opening the wall. If your valve is a Moen Posi-Temp or a Delta Monitor, a trim kit from the same brand bolts right onto it and gives you new handles, a new spout and a new showerhead. Brand matters here. An outdated valve may need replacing instead.

    Outdoor and Utility Faucet Replacement in Palm Coast

    Hose bibs and laundry faucets get ignored until they leak. Frozen pipes are rare in most of Florida, and many homes have outdoor plumbing that would never survive a northern winter. A split sillcock body can leak inside the wall for weeks before the stain shows up on the drywall in the basement or garage. We replace them with frost-free models where freezing is a risk. Laundry tub faucets get a vacuum breaker.

    Touchless Faucet Upgrade in Palm Coast

    Swapping a standard faucet for a touchless one takes a little more planning. Touchless models need a dry spot on the cabinet wall for the control box and either batteries or an outlet within reach of the cord. We check that first. We adjust the sensor range at the end.

    Most swaps take one to three hours. Drilling a new hole in stone, replacing corroded stubs or cutting out a fused faucet adds time, sometimes most of a morning. We stay out of your way.

    A basic swap is doable with the right tools. Fused nuts and corroded stops are where DIY swaps go wrong, often leaving a cracked sink or a wet cabinet floor. That is a common call for us.
