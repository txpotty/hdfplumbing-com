    What We Fix on Toilets in Palm Coast, FL

    Running Toilet Fixes in Palm Coast

    Phantom refills, the tank topping itself off for a few seconds in the middle of the night, almost always mean a slow leak at the flapper. The level falls a little until the fill valve opens back up. Sometimes the cause is a refill tube pushed too far down the overflow, which siphons the tank. We clip it above the water line.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Repair in Palm Coast

    Restaurant restrooms get the hardest use we see, and wall-hung bowls there can loosen on their carriers until the seal leaks behind the wall. We tighten or rebuild the carrier and reseal the bowl. That one needs care. A loose wall-hung bowl can crack the china if it is ignored.

    Commercial Toilet Repair in Palm Coast

    Office and retail restrooms take more flushes in a day than a house sees in a month, so the parts wear faster and fail harder. Commercial bowls usually run on flushometer valves rather than tanks, and those need diaphragm kits and vacuum breakers from time to time. We carry Sloan and Zurn kits. Work can be scheduled before opening or after close.

    Fill Valve Replacement in Palm Coast

    Old ballcock valves with a brass arm and a big plastic float are loud and slow to refill, and they tend to stick open when scale builds up in the ports. We swap them for an adjustable fill valve from Fluidmaster or Korky and set the height so the critical level mark sits at least an inch above the overflow. It is a twenty-minute job. You will notice the difference the first night, because the tank stops hissing.

    Leaking Toilet Repair in Palm Coast

    Water on the floor can come from four places: the wax ring, the tank-to-bowl gasket, the supply line or sweat running off a cold tank. Each one needs a different fix. We dry everything and watch where the water shows up first, which takes about ten minutes and saves pulling a toilet that did not need it. A cracked bowl gets a replacement quote, not a patch.

    Toilet Brands We Service in Palm Coast

    Kohler, American Standard, TOTO, Gerber and Mansfield make up most of the toilets we open, and each one uses its own flapper size or flush valve design. Put the wrong flapper on one and it will leak within weeks. We match the part to the model number stamped inside the tank and stock the common ones on every truck.

    Clogged Toilet Clearing in Palm Coast, FL

    Most clogs sit in the trapway, the curved channel cast into the porcelain, and a closet auger with a rubber-sleeved head clears them without scratching the bowl. Plungers miss some. When the auger finds nothing and the bowl still fills, the blockage is further down the drain or in the main line. At that point we run a cable from the nearest cleanout or send a camera down to see it.

    Toilet Gasket and Seal Replacement in Palm Coast, FL

    The rubber parts in a toilet, the flapper, the tank-to-bowl gasket, the bolt washers and the wax ring at the floor, all break down with age and chlorine. They get stiff. A stiff gasket stops sealing and lets water seep out slowly where you will not see it until the floor stains. We replace the whole set of tank seals at once when one is gone.
