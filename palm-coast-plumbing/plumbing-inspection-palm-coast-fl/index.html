    What a Plumbing Inspection Covers in Palm Coast, FL

    Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Palm Coast, FL

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. Too much pressure wears parts. Anything over 80 psi cuts the life of hoses, fixtures and heaters, and we note the supply pipe type, from copper and CPVC to poly, Kitec and galvanized.

    Leak Detection and Slab Screening in Palm Coast

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. Warm spots are another clue. Meter tests, pressure loss checks and a look for damp baseboards come first, and slab homes get acoustic and thermal follow-up. Catching a slab leak early limits damage to floors and foundations.

    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Palm Coast, FL

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. Our plumber identifies the supply pipe, whether copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, poly or Kitec, and explains how each usually fails. Certain types need a plan. Polybutylene and Kitec are known for sudden failures, and galvanized slowly closes up with rust, so those get flagged with replacement options.

    Rental and Commercial Property Inspections in Palm Coast

    Landlords and property managers use our inspections between tenants or before buying an investment property. Every unit gets its own notes. Water heaters, fixtures and sewer lines get recorded unit by unit, and commercial inspections can add backflow assemblies and grease trap lines, which helps owners budget for maintenance and larger repairs.

    Water Heater Inspections in Palm Coast

    Water heaters give clues long before they fail. We read the serial number for age, look for rust at the fittings and the base, check the venting and test the T and P relief valve. We check tankless units for scale. On gas units, we look at the burner and flame, check for a sediment trap and confirm the vent is drafting so combustion gases go outside.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Palm Coast, FL

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. Shutoffs get tested too. Stops and the main valve tend to seize with age, and gurgling drains can mean a vent problem, both of which are simple fixes once found.

    Whole-Home Plumbing Inspections in Palm Coast

    Our whole-home inspections focus on the problems we see most in Florida houses. That list starts with old supply pipe, worn angle stops, aging water heaters, slow drains and sewer lines with roots or sags. We test each one directly. Catching a corroded fitting or a partial sewer blockage now keeps it from becoming a ceiling stain or a backup on a holiday weekend.

    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Palm Coast

    Many wells in Florida produce water with a sulfur smell or high iron, which often calls for treatment to protect fixtures and appliances. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. Signs include crusted aerators, clogged showerheads, slow toilet fill and heater sediment, and we can test hardness during the visit. You get options, not a pitch. Softeners, filters and scale reducers all help, and we suggest the one that matches the reading we took.
