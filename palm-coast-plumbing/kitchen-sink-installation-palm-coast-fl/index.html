    How We Install Kitchen Sinks in Palm Coast, FL: Style by Style

    Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in Palm Coast

    Sitting on the counter by their rim, drop-in sinks are a natural fit for tile, laminate and butcher block counters. It is often the quickest swap. After checking the cutout, we seal the rim and draw the clips down evenly, and in older homes and rentals it is often a two-hour job.

    Drop-In Sink Replacement in Palm Coast

    Pulling an old drop-in starts with cutting the caulk line and backing off the clips from below. Old clips are often rusted solid, so we bring a reciprocating saw and cut them off rather than prying on the counter. Laminate chips if you pry. Your new rim has to fully cover the old hole.

    Undermount Sinks on Stone in Palm Coast, FL

    Every fabricator handles undermount clips a bit differently. Some epoxy threaded studs into the stone, others use a rail system, and we work with whichever one your countertop shop prefers. The bowl is braced regardless. The trap does not get connected until the adhesive cures.

    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Palm Coast

    An undermount depends on its clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. We mount the bowl, seal the rim with silicone and brace it while the adhesive sets, which usually takes overnight before the sink can hold a full load of water. Strainers and traps go in after.

    Composite Sink Installation in Palm Coast

    Granite and quartz composite sinks resist scratches on the surface but can chip at the drain hole if a strainer is overtightened. We tighten by hand first. Composite makers usually call for silicone instead of plumber's putty under the strainer, since some putties stain the material, and we follow that sheet.

    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Palm Coast

    A double bowl drains both sides through one trap, using either a center outlet continuous waste or an end outlet tee. We pick the layout around your disposal and dishwasher, since a disposal on one side changes where the tee can go and how much fall each side gets. Heavy-wall fittings resist leaks.

    Commercial Kitchen Sink Installation in Palm Coast, FL

    Three-compartment sinks, prep sinks and hand sinks carry separate health code requirements. Prep sinks usually need an indirect drain to a floor sink with an air gap, while the three-compartment sink often runs through the grease trap. The local rule gets checked first. We work before opening or after close so service is not interrupted.

    Apron-Front Sinks in Palm Coast, FL

    Apron sinks need a cabinet cut to fit the front panel, and the bowl must be set before the counter gets measured. We set the sink and frame early, so the fabricator measures from the actual bowl instead of a spec sheet. That avoids an uneven reveal. Cast iron versions can top 150 pounds, so we bring two people.
