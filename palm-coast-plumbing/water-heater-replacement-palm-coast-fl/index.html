    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Palm Coast, FL

    Gas water heaters reheat quicker than electric tanks, and in Palm Coast, FL homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. Gas gets shut off and capped, the old tank drained and removed, and the new one set with a fresh connector, sediment trap and properly sloped vent. Every joint gets checked.

    Upsizing Your Water Heater in Palm Coast

    Running out of hot water often means the family has outgrown it. A larger tank or a model with a higher first-hour rating can keep up with more showers back to back, and in some homes a 50-gallon high-recovery gas unit does the job of a 75-gallon standard tank without taking up any more floor space. Recovery rate counts too.

    Commercial Water Heater Replacement in Palm Coast

    Businesses in Palm Coast put far more hours on a heater than a house, so commercial replacements need more capacity and faster recovery. Restaurants, salons and offices all draw differently, so we size by peak gallons per hour and required temperature, which may mean a larger tank, a booster or a tankless rack. We schedule around your hours.

    Attic Water Heater Replacement in Palm Coast

    Many homes in Florida built before the mid 1970s have cast iron drains under the slab, which commonly crack and corrode with age. Attic water heaters take more work to replace than ones in a garage or closet. Getting a 50-gallon tank up a pull-down ladder or through a narrow scuttle takes two people and care, and code calls for a drain pan with a line to the outside or a safe drain, a service platform and a light near the unit. Two-person crew, always.

    Rheem and Ruud Water Heater Replacement in Palm Coast, FL

    Ruud and Rheem come out of the same company and share many parts. We install both, and because parts overlap, a thermostat or element is easy to find when repairing a newer tank beats replacing it. We will say when repair works.

    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Palm Coast

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is common, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. Since tankless can pull four times the gas of a tank, the meter line usually gets bigger, and the old vent is swapped for stainless or PVC. We price both options.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Palm Coast, FL

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters move heat from the surrounding air into the tank, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need space and airflow, often a room of about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they work best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. They also cool the room.

    Conventional Tank Water Heater Replacement in Palm Coast

    Standard tank heaters are still in most homes, and they usually fail from age, rust or sediment. A leak at the base, loud popping or rusty hot water means it is time, and a like-for-like or resized swap usually takes two to four hours. Hot water by evening.
