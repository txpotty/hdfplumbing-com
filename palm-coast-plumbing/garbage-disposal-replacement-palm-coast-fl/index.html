    Frequently Asked Questions About Garbage Disposal Replacement in Palm Coast

    A standard disposal replacement with the unit typically costs $275 to $650. Premium models like InSinkErator Evolution usually run $500 to $1,000 installed.

    Most garbage disposal replacements can be completed within 1 to 2 hours. This timeframe includes disconnecting the old unit, installing the new one, and testing it thoroughly.     We replace all major brands of garbage disposals, including InSinkErator, Waste King, Moen, GE, KitchenAid, and Whirlaway. Our plumbers are familiar with the specific installation requirements and features of each brand, delivering a proper and efficient replacement every time.

    While it's possible for a handy homeowner to replace a garbage disposal, it involves working with both electricity and plumbing, which can be hazardous if not done correctly.     To extend the life of your new disposal, avoid putting fibrous foods like celery, corn husks, or onion skins down it. Also, do not dispose of grease, coffee grounds, or non-food items.
