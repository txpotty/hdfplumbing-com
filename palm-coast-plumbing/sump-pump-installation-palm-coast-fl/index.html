    Sump Pump Replacement in Palm Coast, FL

    Sump pumps often sit idle for long stretches between storms, which can lead to stuck floats and seized motors. We replace old pumps with new models and inspect the pit, check valve and discharge line. Upgrading to a higher capacity pump may help if your old one could not keep up. We test the new pump by filling the pit with water. Replacement before the rainy season is a smart move.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Palm Coast

    If your basement or crawl space has no pit, we can build one. We cut the slab, excavate through soil, hardpan or rock and set a durable basin. We add gravel around the basin to help water flow in. Then we patch the concrete and install the pump and cover. A new pit gives you a reliable place to collect and remove water.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Palm Coast

    A high water alarm lets you know when water rises above normal levels. Smart monitors can send alerts to your phone when the pump runs or fails. We install alarms and Wi-Fi monitors on new and existing pumps. These devices are particularly useful for vacation homes and rental properties. Early warnings can prevent major water damage.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Palm Coast, FL

    Strong storms can knock out power right when your pump is needed most. Battery backup pumps take over automatically when the main pump fails or power goes out. We install backup systems with sealed batteries and charging units. Many include alarms that warn you about problems. Backups add an extra layer of protection for finished basements.
