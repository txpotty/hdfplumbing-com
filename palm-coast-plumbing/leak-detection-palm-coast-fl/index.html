    Main Water Line Leak Detection in Palm Coast

    A soft wet patch near the sidewalk or a sinking spot over the line can mean the service pipe is leaking. Old galvanized and copper lines fail most.     Slab Leaks From Ground Movement in Palm Coast

    Pipe set in concrete cannot flex when the ground moves, so it cracks at elbows and where it rises through the slab. Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and crack buried water and sewer lines. Isolating the line comes first. Then acoustic listening and thermal imaging find the leak so nobody has to break the floor open to search, and you get a marked spot plus repair options before the saw comes out.

    Our Leak Detection Equipment in Palm Coast

    Our trucks carry acoustic correlators and ground microphones, thermal cameras, line tracers, moisture meters and tracer gas kits. Every tool covers a different case. Acoustic gear hears pressurized water escaping, thermal cameras show temperature differences from hot water lines and wet drywall, and tracer gas finds leaks that make no sound at all on plastic pipe.

    Slab Leak Signs in Palm Coast, FL

    A meter that never rests, cracked tile and warm floor spots are the common signs. So is the sound of water with everything off. We isolate hot and cold lines with pressure tests, then trace and listen through the slab, and once it is located we go over spot repair, reroute and repipe options with prices for each.

    Pool and Spa Leak Detection in Palm Coast

    Heat and wind can pull a lot of water out of a pool, so it is hard to tell evaporation from a leak. A bucket test settles it. We then pressure test the plumbing lines and dye test around lights, skimmers and return fittings, since a leak left alone can wash out the soil behind the shell.
