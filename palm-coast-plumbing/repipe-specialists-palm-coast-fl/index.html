    What Our Repipe Crews Do in Palm Coast, FL

    Residential Repiping Plans in Palm Coast

    Every house is piped a little differently, so a repipe starts with a walk-through. We count fixtures, check attic and crawlspace access, look at where the main comes in and ask about plans for future remodels before we draw a route. Then we size the lines. A three-bath house with a tankless heater may need 1-inch main trunk lines to keep showers steady when the washer kicks on.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Palm Coast

    Copper is the traditional choice, and Type L copper has a thicker wall than the Type M that went into many older homes. It holds up well, does not mind sunlight in an exposed run and has a long track record behind it. It costs more. We sweat joints with lead-free solder or press them, hang the pipe at proper support intervals and test the whole system before patching.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Palm Coast, FL

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside, and over decades the inside diameter closes down to a fraction of what it was. Pressure drops slowly. Rust-colored water in the morning, poor pressure upstairs and dripping threaded joints all point to failing galvanized. We replace it with PEX or copper. Most homeowners notice the pressure difference the first time they turn on the shower.

    Drywall Patching and Restoration in Palm Coast, FL

    Repiping needs small openings in walls and ceilings to reach fixtures and pull pipe. Our crew cuts neat rectangles, covers nearby surfaces and patches each opening after the pressure test. We match the texture. We match knockdown, orange peel or smooth walls, so the patches are ready to prime and paint.

    Urgent Repipes After Repeated Failures in Palm Coast, FL

    A widespread pipe failure sometimes forces a repipe sooner than you planned. We make the house safe and usable first, with temporary repairs that get you water, then schedule the full repipe as soon as the crew and permit allow. We move fast. Even urgent repipes usually take just two to four days after we begin.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Palm Coast

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water and long attic runs. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open the pipe end and let it shrink back over the fitting for a full-bore joint. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. Most homes are repiped with PEX-A in two to three days.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Palm Coast

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. We can reroute a single hot or cold branch overhead and abandon the leaking pipe in the slab, which costs far less than a repipe and avoids breaking the floor. This works well for a first leak. After multiple leaks, we will show you why a full repipe may cost less over the next ten years.
