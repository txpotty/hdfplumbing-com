    Grease Line Clog Repair in Palm Coast, FL

    Preventative Grease Trap Maintenance & Repair in Palm Coast, FL

    Most trap repairs we do trace back to pumping intervals that stretched too long. Pretreatment programs commonly set the limit at 25 percent of the liquid depth for grease and solids combined. After a repair, we measure the grease cap and solids with a sludge judge and suggest a pumping schedule based on how fast your trap actually fills. Busy fryers fill a trap fast.

    Grease Trap Odor Control & Repair in Palm Coast

    A sewer smell near the trap means gas is getting out somewhere it should not. The usual suspects are a torn lid gasket or a floor drain trap that has dried out. Pumping the trap helps for a week, but it will not fix a gasket that no longer seals.

    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Palm Coast

    Inside a concrete gravity interceptor the baffle is usually a poured or precast wall with a crossover opening. A crumbling wall lets grease slip into the second compartment and out the outlet. We can rebuild the wall with form work and a sulfate-resistant mix or set a polyethylene baffle panel, depending on the tank. Either way, it needs a full pump-out first.

    Damaged Baffle and Lid Repair in Palm Coast, FL

While the lid is off, we look at the baffles too.
