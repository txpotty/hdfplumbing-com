    Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Palm Harbor, FL

    The float switch is a key component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. A stuck or improperly adjusted float switch can lead to the pump running continuously or failing to activate at all. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to ensure reliable operation for your home in Palm Harbor, FL.

    Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Palm Harbor, FL

    The check valve stops pumped water from falling back into the pit when the pump shuts off. A failed valve makes the pump run twice as often and wears out the motor. We test and replace check valves and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose joints. We also make sure water exits away from the house so it does not soak into the soil near the slab or foundation.

    Annual Sump Pump Check-ups in Palm Harbor

    Regular annual maintenance is key for the longevity and reliability of your sump pump. Our technicians perform a thorough inspection, checking the motor, impeller, float switch and discharge lines for any signs of wear or blockage. We also test the system's operation by filling the pit with water to ensure it activates correctly and pumps water away efficiently, protecting your Palm Harbor home from unexpected flooding.
