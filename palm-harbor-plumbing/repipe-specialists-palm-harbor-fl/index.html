    Slab Leak Reroutes in Palm Harbor

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step.     Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Palm Harbor, FL

    Polybutylene, a gray or blue plastic pipe installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s, breaks down with exposure to chlorine and other disinfectants in tap water. We replace the full system with PEX or copper. Insurers often ask about it at renewal.
