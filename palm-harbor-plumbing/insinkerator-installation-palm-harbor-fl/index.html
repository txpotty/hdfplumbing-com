    Hard Water and Mineral Considerations in Palm Harbor

    Many wells in Florida produce water with a sulfur smell or high iron, which often calls for filtration to protect fixtures and appliances. Hard water leaves scale on disposal grind rings, sink flanges and drain tailpieces. Over time that buildup can make a disposal noisy and cause slow drainage. We inspect the drain line for scale and debris before connecting a new unit. If heavy buildup is present, we recommend cleaning or replacing the trap and tailpiece at the same time. Stainless steel grind components on Evolution models resist corrosion better in these conditions.

    Insinkerator Batch Feed vs. Continuous Feed Installation in Palm Harbor

    We install both batch feed and continuous feed Insinkerator models in Palm Harbor homes. Batch feed disposals operate only when the stopper is in place, offering a safety advantage, while continuous feed models run as long as the switch is on. Our plumbers can advise on the best option for your lifestyle and kitchen usage, then perform the installation with precision, ensuring the appropriate electrical connections and switch mechanisms are correctly integrated for your chosen model.

    Dishwasher Connection and Air Gap Setup in Palm Harbor

    Most InSinkErator units have a dishwasher inlet with a factory knockout plug inside. We knock that plug out, clear the debris and connect the dishwasher drain hose with a proper clamp. We then install or verify an air gap or high loop so dirty sink water cannot siphon back into the dishwasher. Forgetting the knockout is one of the most common DIY mistakes and leads to a dishwasher that will not drain. We test both appliances together before we leave.
