    What Goes Into a Repipe in Palm Harbor, FL

    Whole-Home Re-Piping in Palm Harbor

    A whole-home repipe replaces every supply line from the main shutoff to each fixture, including the water heater connections. Most single-story homes take two to three days. We run new trunk lines through the attic or crawlspace, branch down to fixtures through small wall openings, with service restored each evening when possible. Then patching.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Palm Harbor, FL

    Drain lines fail too. Cast iron cracks and scales. We replace cracked cast iron, failing ABS or old galvanized drains with PVC, checking slope and venting so the new drains flow freely. Camera first.

    Water Line Replacement vs. Repair in Palm Harbor, FL

    A single bad spot can usually be fixed. Several leaks in different rooms, rusty water throughout, or pipe that crumbles when we cut it tells us the system is failing everywhere, and patching just moves the next leak down the line. We show you what we find.

    Pipe Replacement for Multi-Unit Buildings in Palm Harbor

    Apartments and condos share risers. Shared lines mean shared problems. We plan riser replacement in phases, one stack at a time, keeping each unit informed. Organized work.

    Emergency Pipe Replacement in Palm Harbor, FL

    A burst pipe needs fast action. Shut off the main valve first. Then call, because water spreading through walls and floors does damage every minute, and our crews aim to stop it fast. Drying comes next.

    Full Repipes for Older Homes in Palm Harbor

    Homes from the 1950s and 1960s often still have original galvanized pipe. It is near the end. A full repipe brings new PEX or copper to every fixture, improves pressure, clears up discolored water, and removes the leak risk from aging threads. Worth it.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Palm Harbor

    PEX bends around corners and comes in long rolls, so a typical repipe needs fewer fittings than copper would on the same layout. When a rare freeze is forecast, covering outdoor valves and letting a faucet drip typically does the job to prevent damage. PEX can stretch a little in a freeze instead of splitting. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings where possible, which give a full-bore connection and hold up well over time. Faster install.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Palm Harbor, FL

    Pinhole leaks start small. A drip, then a stain. They usually mean the copper wall has thinned from corrosion, so finding one often means others are close. Repipe or patch, we explain.
