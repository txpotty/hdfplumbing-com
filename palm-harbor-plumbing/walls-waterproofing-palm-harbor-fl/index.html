    How We Keep Water Out of Walls in Palm Harbor, FL

    Foundation Crack Repair in Palm Harbor

    Crack injection takes about two hours per crack. We set injection ports every 8 to 12 inches along the crack, seal the surface with epoxy paste, and pump in resin from the bottom port up until it pushes out the next port. Polyurethane stays flexible if the wall moves a little, and epoxy bonds the two sides back together. The surface paste gets ground off once it cures.

    Commercial Wall Waterproofing in Palm Harbor

    Restaurant kitchens, gym locker rooms and salon wash stations put water on walls all day. We waterproof commercial showers, mop sink walls and restroom wet walls with membranes rated for constant water exposure, and we repair the supply and drain piping behind them when we find a leak during demo. After-hours scheduling is available.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Palm Harbor, FL

    Interior drain tile handles water that already got through the wall. We cut a channel in the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and tie it into a sump pit so the pump can push the water outside. The wall still gets wet. The difference is that the water no longer ends up on your floor. This route costs less than digging outside and works where a driveway or deck blocks the wall.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Palm Harbor

    Bare dirt in a crawl space releases gallons of water vapor a day into the air under your floor. A 10 mil or heavier polyethylene barrier, overlapped a foot at every seam and taped, cuts that off and keeps the joists and subfloor dry. We run it up the foundation walls and hold it in place with termination strip. It is a cheap fix that makes a big difference.

    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Palm Harbor, FL

    A sump pump is the part of an interior system that actually moves the water. We set a lidded basin below slab level, install a cast iron pump sized for the inflow, and add a battery backup or water-powered backup for the night the power goes out during a storm. Check valves matter. Without one, the discharge water runs back into the pit and the pump short-cycles. The discharge line gets routed at least 10 feet from the house.

    Commercial Walls Waterproofing in Palm Harbor

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and mechanical rooms in Palm Harbor take on water the same way houses do, just with more at stake. One wet electrical room can close a business for days. We schedule around operating hours, work with property managers on access, and meet the waterproofing specs in the building plans for below-grade walls. We keep downtime short.

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Palm Harbor

    Hairline cracks in poured concrete walls leak more water than their size suggests. We inject them with low-pressure polyurethane foam, which expands to fill the crack from the inside face to the soil side, or with epoxy when the crack also needs structural bonding. Horizontal cracks are different. They can mean the wall is bowing from soil pressure, so we check for movement with a level before we seal anything. Most vertical cracks get sealed in one visit.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Palm Harbor

    Tile and grout are not waterproof. Water passes through grout every time you shower, so the layer behind the tile, cement board with a membrane or a foam board system, is what actually keeps the studs dry. We tear out failed tile, install new backer, and apply Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban at the thickness the manufacturer calls for. Corners, niches and valve holes get extra fabric or preformed pieces.
