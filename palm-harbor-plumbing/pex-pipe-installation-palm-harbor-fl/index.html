    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Palm Harbor

    PEX-B is stiffer, costs less per foot, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. Since a crimp is only as good as the tool that made it, every ring gets a go/no-go gauge check, and any ring that fails gets cut off and redone. It is a five-second check. Skipping it causes most crimp leaks.

    Copper to PEX Conversions in Palm Harbor

    Pinhole leaks in copper tend to come in clusters. Many wells in Florida produce water with a sulfur smell or high iron, which often calls for filtration to protect fixtures and appliances. Converting to PEX stops the cycle. We use listed transition fittings anywhere PEX ties into copper that stays.
