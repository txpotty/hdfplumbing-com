    Picking a Tankless for Your Palm Harbor, FL Home

    Recirculation Tankless Upgrades in Palm Harbor, FL

    Waiting a minute for hot water at the far bathroom wastes thousands of gallons a year in a long single-story house. Navien A models and Rinnai units with internal pumps can recirculate through a return line or a crossover valve under the farthest sink. We set a schedule. Usually just the morning and evening peaks.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Palm Harbor, FL

    Navien NPE units are condensing, and the A models have a built-in recirculation pump and buffer tank that cut down on cold-water sandwiches. Their combi boilers handle both space heat and domestic water. We set up both.

    Navien Venting and Install in Palm Harbor

    Navien allows 2-inch or 3-inch PVC venting on its condensing units, with maximum lengths that drop as you add elbows. Our vent runs stay within spec. Go past them and the warranty suffers.

    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Palm Harbor

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE or Rinnai Sensei pass 90 percent efficiency by pulling extra heat out of the exhaust. The vent is plain PVC. The condensate is acidic, so we install a neutralizer cartridge and run the line to an approved drain. Humidity causes condensation on cold lines and clogged AC condensate drains, a frequent source of hidden water damage. Showers and laundry run all year, so the gas savings do too.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Palm Harbor, FL

    Pulling a 50 gallon tank out of a garage frees up floor space and ends standby heat loss. Gas is the catch, since tankless units usually need 3/4-inch pipe or larger and a new vent. We check the meter size and pipe run before quoting, and we cap or reroute the old flue and seal the roof penetration if it is no longer needed. Plan on a full day.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Palm Harbor

    Rinnai units we pull are usually 12 to 18 years old, and the failure tends to be a leaking heat exchanger or a dead control board. A current Rinnai often mounts on the same bracket. Less drywall work that way. We match the vent type or convert to PVC on a condensing model.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Palm Harbor

    A gas tankless unit at full fire can pull 199,000 BTU, roughly five times what a 40 gallon tank burner uses. Most need at least a 3/4-inch line. We check meter capacity and pipe length against the manufacturer's sizing chart, then confirm pressure with the unit running. An undersized line causes ignition faults.
