    Shower Diverter Repair in Palm Harbor, FL

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. When the gate in a lift-style spout wears or a three-handle diverter stem loses its washer, water keeps pouring from the spout and the shower gets weak. We replace the spout or stem. It is a short job.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Palm Harbor, FL

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Scale packs the nozzles and the restrictor screen behind them, and the spray drops off a little at a time until one day it feels like a garden mister. Cleaning often works. A clogged cartridge gets replaced, then we test the flow.

    Leaky Shower Valve Repair in Palm Harbor

    One drip every few seconds adds up to hundreds of gallons a year.     Shower Drain Body Repair in Palm Harbor

    Drain bodies can crack or work loose under the pan. A loose or cracked drain lets water go under the pan, which shows up as a soft floor beside the shower or a ceiling stain. We clear and test the drain. We reseal or replace the drain body as needed.

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Palm Harbor, FL

    Stains on the ceiling below are often the first clue. A meter on the walls and a flood test on the pan show whether the leak is the valve, the pan or the drain. The source gets repaired. On slab homes, these leaks can spread into the flooring of the next room before anyone sees them.

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Palm Harbor, FL

    Low pressure at the shower with good pressure at the sink narrows it down fast.
