    Frequently Asked Questions About Backflow Preventer in Palm Harbor

    Backflow preventer testing in Palm Harbor, FL typically costs between $75 and $150. This fee covers the technician's visit, the test itself, and the submission of required reports to your local water authority. Complex commercial systems or multiple devices may carry higher costs.

    Most water providers require testing at least once per year. Some require testing after installation, repair or relocation as well. Your provider's notice will list the deadline.

    Constant dripping from the relief valve, low water pressure and visible cracks are common signs. Discolored water or a failed test notice also indicate a problem. Prompt repair keeps you compliant.

    A standard test usually takes 20 to 45 minutes per assembly. Water is briefly shut off during testing. Repairs may add an hour or more.

    An RPZ, or Reduced Pressure Zone assembly, offers the highest level of protection, suitable for high-hazard cross-connections. A DCVA, or Double Check Valve Assembly, provides moderate protection and is typically used for low to medium hazard situations, like irrigation systems. The choice depends on the application and local code requirements.

    Backflow prevention is essential because of changing water pressures, main breaks and fire flow that can drop pressure suddenly, and the potential for cross-connections from irrigation systems or industrial processes. These factors increase the risk of contaminated water entering the public supply, making a working backflow preventer a must.

    We service Watts, Febco, Zurn Wilkins, Apollo and other common brands. Our trucks carry rebuild kits for common models. Less common parts can usually be ordered quickly.

    Signs of a failing backflow preventer include discolored water, unusual tastes or odors in your water, water leaking from the relief valve, or a sudden drop in water pressure. If you notice any of these issues, it is important to have your device inspected right away.
