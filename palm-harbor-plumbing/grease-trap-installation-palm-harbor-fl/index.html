    Under-Sink Grease Trap Setup in Palm Harbor, FL

    Smaller establishments or specific kitchen stations in Palm Harbor, FL may benefit from compact under-sink grease traps. These units are well suited for capturing FOG at the source, preventing it from entering the main plumbing lines.     Gravity Grease Trap Installation in Palm Harbor

    Gravity grease traps are a common and effective solution for many commercial kitchens in Palm Harbor. These passive systems use the natural separation of FOG from water due to density differences. Our plumbers are experienced with gravity grease traps, ensuring proper sizing, correct inlet and outlet piping, and convenient access for future cleaning and maintenance to get the most from their efficiency.

    Large Outdoor Grease Interceptor Installation in Palm Harbor

    For high-volume commercial kitchens in Palm Harbor, large outdoor grease interceptors are often required by code. These large units are installed underground and designed to manage significant amounts of FOG before wastewater enters the municipal sewer system. We handle the entire process, including excavation, precise placement, connection, and final inspection, ensuring a robust and compliant installation.

    Grease Trap Sizing and Placement in Palm Harbor, FL

    Correct sizing and strategic placement are critical for a grease trap's effectiveness. Installing a unit that is too small will lead to frequent clogs and overflow, while an oversized one can be an avoidable expense. Our plumbers meticulously calculate the required capacity based on your kitchen's water flow, fixture count, and operating hours, delivering optimal performance for your Palm Harbor, FL business.

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in Palm Harbor

    Hydro-mechanical grease interceptors, often called HGI units, are compact and designed for indoor use, usually under a sink or on the floor. These units use baffles to slow down wastewater flow, allowing grease to separate and accumulate. HDF Plumbing expertly installs various sizes of HGI units, ensuring proper venting, leveling, and connection to your drainage system in Palm Harbor for the best efficiency.

    Automatic Grease Removal Units in Palm Harbor

    Automatic grease removal devices skim grease from the water and collect it in a container your staff empties. Brands like Thermaco Big Dipper are popular in kitchens with limited space and frequent cleaning needs. We install these units with the correct power supply and drain connections. They can reduce the need for frequent pump-outs in smaller kitchens. We explain daily maintenance so your staff keeps the unit running well.
