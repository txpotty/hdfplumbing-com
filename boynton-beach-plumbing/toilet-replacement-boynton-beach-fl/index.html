    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Replacement in Boynton Beach

    Most toilet replacements, including a quality standard toilet, labor and disposal, cost $450 to $950. Premium one-piece or smart toilets can raise the total to $1,200 to $2,500. Flange repairs, if needed, typically add $100 to $250.

    We often recommend and install reliable brands like American Standard, Kohler, Toto, and Delta. These brands offer a range of durable, water-efficient models that perform well across a wide range of water conditions and are known for their longevity.

    A like-for-like toilet replacement in the same location usually does not require a permit. If drains are moved or a new bathroom is added, a permit is usually required.

    Key signs include persistent leaks that are hard to pinpoint or fix, a cracked toilet bowl or tank, frequent clogs despite proper use, excessive rocking or instability, or a toilet that is very old and inefficient, using too much water per flush. If repair costs approach the price of a new toilet, replacement is often the better choice.

    Most standard toilet replacements can be completed by a professional plumber in about 2 to 4 hours. This includes the removal of the old unit, preparation of the flange, installation of the new toilet, and testing for leaks. More complex installations might take a bit longer.

    Older toilets can use 3.5 to 7 gallons per flush, while WaterSense toilets use 1.28 gallons or less. For a typical household, that can mean thousands of gallons saved each year.

    Signs of failing internal toilet parts include a toilet that runs constantly, a weak or incomplete flush, a handle that needs jiggling, or visible wear and tear on components like the flapper, fill valve, or flush valve. While some parts can be replaced one at a time, an older toilet with multiple failing components might benefit more from a full replacement.

    Manufacturers typically warranty parts for 1 to 5 years and porcelain longer. We guarantee our workmanship and will return if a leak is caused by the installation.
