    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Boynton Beach

    A whole-house water softener system is installed directly on your main water line, treating all the water entering your Boynton Beach home. This means every faucet, shower and appliance receives soft water. Our installation process involves carefully integrating the system into your existing plumbing, including needed bypass valves and pressure regulators, to provide comprehensive protection against hard water throughout your property.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Boynton Beach

    Reverse osmosis systems remove dissolved solids, which can be high in some water supplies. We install RO units under the kitchen sink with a dedicated faucet. Some systems also connect to refrigerators or ice makers. RO pairs well with a softener for complete water treatment. We change filters on a schedule so the system keeps performing.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Boynton Beach

    Salt-based (ion exchange) water softeners are the most common and effective solution for truly softening water. These systems use a resin bed to exchange hard minerals like calcium and magnesium with sodium ions. HDF Plumbing expertly installs many models of salt-based softeners, ensuring proper connection to your main water line, correct drainage setup and accurate programming for regeneration cycles in your Boynton Beach home.

    We offer a warranty on our workmanship, and the water softener units themselves come with manufacturer warranties, which usually range from 5 to 10 years for the tank and control valve. We will provide you with all warranty details for your specific system after installation.

    Maintenance primarily involves periodically adding salt to salt-based systems, usually every 4 to 8 weeks depending on water usage and hardness.     Control valves usually carry 5 years of coverage, and tanks often carry 10 years or more. We also warranty our installation work.
