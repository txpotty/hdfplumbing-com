    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Boynton Beach

    The cost of water heater installation in Boynton Beach, FL can range from $1,200 to $4,000 or more, depending on the type of unit (traditional tank vs. tankless), its capacity, fuel type (gas or electric) and any needed plumbing or electrical upgrades.     Manufacturer warranties typically range from 6 to 12 years on the tank and parts. We also warranty our installation labor.

    If your home has a pressure reducing valve or check valve, an expansion tank is usually required. It protects your water heater and pipes from pressure spikes.

    An expansion tank absorbs excess water pressure created as water heats up inside the tank. Many homes in Boynton Beach, FL, especially those with closed-loop plumbing systems, are required by code to have an expansion tank to protect the water heater and plumbing system from damage.

    New water heaters usually come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and components, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. HDF Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work, ensuring peace of mind. We will discuss specific warranty details for your chosen unit during the consultation.

    Many tank water heaters last 8 to 12 years, though hard water can shorten that. Regular flushing and anode rod checks help lengthen life.

    The decision to repair or replace often depends on the age of your unit, the cost of the repair and its energy efficiency. If your water heater is over 10 years old, frequently breaks down, or the repair cost is more than 50% of a new unit, replacement is often the more cost-effective choice.
