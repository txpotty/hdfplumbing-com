    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Boynton Beach, FL

    Cold water slab leaks do not create warm spots, so they are harder to see. They often show up as a running water meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards. We isolate the cold side and use acoustic equipment to listen for the leak through the slab. Line tracers map the pipe route so we know exactly where to listen.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Boynton Beach

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the slab and runs new pipe through the walls or attic. It avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than direct access under finished floors. We usually use PEX for reroutes because it handles heat and resists hard-water corrosion. This option is a good fit when the slab is covered in tile or hardwood.

    Pressure Testing & Isolation in Boynton Beach

    When a leak is suspected, we can perform pressure tests on individual water lines. By isolating sections of your plumbing system and monitoring pressure gauges, we can confirm the presence of a leak and often determine which particular line is compromised. This step-by-step approach is crucial for homes with complex plumbing layouts, ensuring an accurate diagnosis before any excavation in Boynton Beach, FL.

    Water and Sewer Line Slab Leaks in Boynton Beach, FL

    Slab leaks can affect both freshwater supply lines and sewer drain pipes. Our detection services are set up to identify both types of leaks, which present different challenges. Sewer line leaks often involve foul odors and slow drainage, while water line leaks can appear as wet spots or high water bills in your Boynton Beach, FL home. We have separate methods for each.

    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Boynton Beach, FL

    Thermal imaging cameras allow us to visualize temperature differences on surfaces, which can indicate a hidden water leak. As water escapes a hot water line, it creates a clear thermal signature on the concrete slab. This technique is particularly useful for hot water line leaks and helps us narrow down the search area quickly without any physical disruption to your property in Boynton Beach.

    Video Pipe Inspection for Sewer Leaks in Boynton Beach, FL

    Sewer line issues under a slab can also look like slab leak symptoms. We use high-resolution video cameras to inspect the interior of your drain lines, identifying cracks, blockages or separations. This is vital if the issue isn't a pressurized water line, helping us determine if a sewer line repair is needed instead for your Boynton Beach home.
