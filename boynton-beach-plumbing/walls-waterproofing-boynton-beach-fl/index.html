    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Boynton Beach, FL

    Block and stem walls often show efflorescence, which is the white mineral crust left when water passes through masonry. Irrigation and sprinklers aimed at the house are common culprits. We adjust or reroute irrigation, clean the wall and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating. Proper grading and drip line placement help keep the wall dry.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Boynton Beach

    Exterior foundation waterproofing involves excavating around the foundation to apply a waterproof membrane or coating directly to the wall. We use high-quality asphalt-modified polyurethane membranes or polymer-based coatings that resist hydrostatic pressure and soil movement. Nearly all homes in Florida sit on concrete slabs over sandy soil, so leaks under the slab are a common and costly repair.     Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Boynton Beach

    Even minor cracks in foundation walls can allow significant water intrusion. Our services include crack repair using epoxy or polyurethane injection to seal these entry points permanently.
