    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Winter Haven

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. A leak at the arm threads follows the pipe into the wall, and it shows up as bubbling paint in the next room. Fresh thread tape fixes it. Handheld hoses and slide bars can go in during the same visit.

    Emergency Shower Repair in Winter Haven, FL

    A shower that will not shut off is an emergency. Shut the main, or use the shower's integral stops if your valve has them behind the trim or an access panel. Next, call us. HDF Plumbing sends a plumber with cartridges on the truck, and we can often get your water back on within the hour.

    Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in Winter Haven

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Mineral grit scores the sealing faces and flattens the O-rings until the cartridge can no longer stop the flow. A new cartridge fixes it. We use genuine parts and coat the new one with silicone grease so the handle turns smoothly.

    Slow Shower Drain Repair in Winter Haven, FL

    Standing water at your ankles is the first sign. Minerals bind soap scum and hair into a tough mat that clings to the pipe walls and gets thicker with every shower. We cut it out with an auger. For stubborn buildup in the branch line, we jet the line with water instead of using acid cleaners.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Winter Haven

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. Leaks at the arm threads are different, and those get new thread tape and a snug turn.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Winter Haven, FL

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open.
