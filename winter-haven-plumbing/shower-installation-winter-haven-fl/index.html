    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Winter Haven, FL

    Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common jobs. We pull the tub, move the drain from the end of the tub to where the shower pan needs it and raise the valve to shower height, usually around 48 inches off the floor. Slab homes need concrete work. No more stepping over a tub wall.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Winter Haven

    Steam showers need four things: a generator, a water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. We install the generator within the maker's distance limits, plumb its supply and drain and run the steam line to the head inside the enclosure. An electrician runs the circuit. A sealed enclosure with a sloped ceiling keeps drips off you.

    Emergency Shower System Replacement in Winter Haven, FL

    Water under a failed pan can rot a wood subfloor in a season. When a pan is cracked or a valve is leaking into the framing, we pull the damaged shower, check the joists and subfloor and install a new unit as quickly as the framing allows. The framing must be dry. Then the new shower goes in.

    Barrier-Free Walk-In Showers in Winter Haven, FL

    Barrier-free entries need careful drain placement. The membrane and slope get planned with the tile setter, so water reaches the drain instead of spilling out the open side. Humidity causes condensation on cold lines and clogged AC condensate drains, a common source of hidden water damage. Pan material follows from that.

    Prefab Shower Unit Installation in Winter Haven, FL
