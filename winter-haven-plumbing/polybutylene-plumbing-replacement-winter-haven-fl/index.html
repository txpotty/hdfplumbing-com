    PEX Pipe Installation in Winter Haven, FL

    PEX is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, resists scale and corrosion and installs faster than copper. Labor runs lower with PEX.     Full Home Repiping with PEX in Winter Haven

    Most poly replacements we do use PEX-A, a flexible tubing that runs long distances with few fittings. Expansion fittings keep the bore full size, which helps pressure at the last fixture on the line.
