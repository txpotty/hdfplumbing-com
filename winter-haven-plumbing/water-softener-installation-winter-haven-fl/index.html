    Softener Loop Installation in Winter Haven

    A softener loop is the plumbing connection that routes your main water line through a softener. Many newer homes include a loop, but older homes often do not. We cut into the main line, install the loop and add a bypass valve. We keep outdoor hose bibs and irrigation on hard water when possible to save salt. A proper loop makes future service easy.

    Water Softener Replacement in Winter Haven, FL

    Older softeners can lose efficiency due to worn resin, failing valves or clogged injectors. We remove the old unit and install a new, more efficient model.     Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Winter Haven, FL

    Municipal water often contains chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. A carbon filter paired with a softener removes those chemicals and protects resin.     Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Winter Haven
