    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in Winter Haven

    The cost of water heater replacement in Winter Haven, FL usually ranges from $1,200 to $3,500, depending on the type of unit (gas, electric, tankless or hybrid), its capacity and any needed plumbing modifications.     Manufacturer tank warranties typically range from 6 to 12 years depending on the model. We also warranty our installation labor.

    New water heaters typically come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and parts, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. HDF Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation, giving you added peace of mind. Specific warranty details vary by brand and unit.

    Choosing the right size depends on the number of people in your household, their hot water usage habits and peak demand times.     Tankless offers endless hot water and a longer lifespan but costs more upfront. We can compare both options based on your household and budget.

    A traditional water heater stores and heats a limited amount of water in a tank, whereas a tankless water heater heats water on demand as it flows through the unit.     Common signs include a lack of hot water, rust-colored water, a metallic taste in hot water, strange noises like rumbling or popping from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit or a water heater that is over 10 to 12 years old.
