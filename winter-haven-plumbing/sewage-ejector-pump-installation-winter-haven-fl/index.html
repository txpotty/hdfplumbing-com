    Ejector Pump Alarms and Backup Options in Winter Haven

    A high water alarm should be on any ejector basin, since a pump failure can lead to a sewage backup quickly. We install audible alarms with their own power source, or Wi-Fi alarms that text your phone, so you hear about a stuck float while the basin is still filling and not after waste has reached the basement floor. After a major storm, homes on wells or septic should be checked before normal use, since floodwater can contaminate both systems. Battery backup is an option.

    Basement Bathroom Ejector Systems in Winter Haven

    Basement bathrooms sit below the building sewer, so the toilet, shower and sink drain to a sealed basin instead of flowing by gravity. We saw the floor, trench drains to the basin, set it, carry a two-inch vent to the roof and run discharge to the main drain through a check valve and shutoff. Then we patch the concrete.

    Grinder Pump Installation in Winter Haven

    Grinder pumps chop waste into slurry before pumping it, which lets them push through small pipe over long distances. A grinder makes sense when the sewer is distant, uphill or pressurized, such as a pressure sewer where each home pumps into a shared main. They cost more. For a basement bath near the drain, a standard ejector usually works.

    Upflush and Macerating Toilet Systems in Winter Haven

    Macerating systems like Saniflo let you add a toilet and sink without breaking the concrete floor. It sits behind the toilet, grinds waste and pumps it through a small line, often three-quarter or one inch, to a drain above, which suits a small half bath. Venting still applies.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin & Pit Installation in Winter Haven

    Digging the pit means cutting the slab and excavating below it, deep enough to set the basin flush with the finished floor. Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and damage buried water and sewer lines. We bed the basin on gravel and backfill around it so it cannot float when groundwater rises, then pour new concrete to seal the floor and lock the basin in place. A floating basin breaks pipe.
