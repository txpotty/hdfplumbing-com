    Long-Lasting PEX Pipe Installation Services in Winter Haven, FL

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Winter Haven

    PEX-B is stiffer and more affordable, and it is joined with copper crimp rings or stainless steel cinch clamps over brass or poly fittings. It is a solid option for smaller projects and budget-conscious repipes. Brands like Viega, Zurn and SharkBite offer dependable PEX-B systems. We use a go/no-go gauge on every crimp to verify a proper seal.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Winter Haven, FL

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a secure seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can recover from kinks with a heat gun. It is our preferred choice for many full repipes.

    Copper to PEX Conversions in Winter Haven

    Copper lines can develop pinhole leaks from water chemistry, high velocity and sometimes soil contact under slabs. Around Winter Haven, flushing the water heater yearly and descaling tankless units helps keep mineral buildup under control. Once a home has had several pinhole leaks, more usually follow. Converting to PEX stops the cycle and lowers the risk of hidden water damage. We use approved transition fittings wherever new PEX connects to existing copper stubs or valves.

    Leak Repair with PEX in Winter Haven, FL

    For specific pipe leaks, PEX can be used to repair or replace sections of damaged piping. Our plumbers can isolate the compromised section and tie in PEX, providing a durable and flexible fix that holds up to future pressures. This targeted approach is cost-effective for localized issues and brings the benefits of PEX to troubled areas of your existing plumbing in Winter Haven, FL.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Winter Haven

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the different advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, usually using crimp or compression fittings. Our experts in Winter Haven can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.

    PEX Repipe Cost Comparison in Winter Haven, FL

    PEX repipes usually cost less than copper repipes because the material is less expensive and installs faster. The total depends on the number of bathrooms, the home's size and how accessible the attic and walls are. Drywall patching and texture matching are sometimes included or quoted separately. We give you a detailed estimate so you can compare PEX-A, PEX-B and copper options together.
