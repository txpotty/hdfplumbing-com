    Expansion Tank Sizing for Large Water Heaters in Winter Haven

    A standard 2 gallon tank works for many 40 and 50 gallon heaters at moderate pressure. Larger heaters, higher incoming pressure or high-capacity systems may need a 4.5 gallon tank or larger. We use manufacturer sizing charts that factor in heater volume, supply pressure and temperature setting. Correct sizing keeps the tank from over-flexing and wearing out early.

    Tank Sizing and Placement in Winter Haven

    Correct sizing and placement are key to an effective water heater expansion tank in Winter Haven. An undersized tank will not provide adequate protection, while an oversized one is an unnecessary expense. We accurately calculate the required tank volume based on your water heater's capacity, system pressure and average water temperature. Placement matters too, usually near the water heater on the cold water inlet line, with easy access for future maintenance or inspection.
