    Natural Gas and Propane Lines in Winter Haven, FL

    Natural gas and propane run at different pressures, and the pipe sizing changes with them.     PEX Repiping in Winter Haven

    Copper repipes cost more and take longer, which is why most whole-house repipes today use PEX-A or PEX-B tubing with brass or poly fittings. You also hear less of it, since PEX softens water hammer. It also handles a freeze better. Frozen pipes are rare in most of Florida, and many homes have outdoor plumbing that would never hold up in a northern winter. Fittings are matched to the tubing manufacturer.

    Sump Pump Installation in Winter Haven, FL
