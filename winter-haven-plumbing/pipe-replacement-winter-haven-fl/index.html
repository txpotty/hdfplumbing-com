    Repiping and Pipe Replacement Choices in Winter Haven, FL

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Winter Haven, FL

    Galvanized steel was common before the 1960s. Inside, rust builds once the coating fails. Over decades the opening shrinks, pressure drops and the water turns brown after sitting, while threaded joints corrode through first. Replacing it is the only fix.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Winter Haven, FL

    Corrosion shows up as green or white crust on copper, rust on steel, or pinholes that drip. Water chemistry plays a role. We replace corroded sections or the whole system depending on how widespread the damage is, and we check the water heater connections and shutoffs since they often corrode too. Look at the joints.

    Slab Leak Pipe Replacement in Winter Haven

    Rerouting is often better than digging. Jackhammering floors is a last resort. We run new pipe through the attic or walls to bypass the leaking line under the concrete, which leaves the floors alone and prevents the same line from failing again. Fast too.

    Slab Leak Pipe Repair and Replacement in Winter Haven

    Water under a slab shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a sound of running water, or a meter that spins with everything off. We locate the leak first. Then you have a choice between breaking the slab to fix that spot or rerouting the line through walls or the attic, which skips the jackhammer. We explain both.

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Winter Haven, FL

    Removing every piece is rarely needed. It is disconnected and capped, while new lines run beside it, which keeps drywall damage low. Only accessible pipe comes out.

    Whole-Home Re-Piping in Winter Haven

    A whole-home repipe replaces every supply line from the main shutoff to each fixture, including the water heater connections. Most single-story homes take two to three days. We run new trunk lines through the attic or crawlspace, branch down to fixtures through small wall openings, with service restored each evening when possible. Then patching.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Winter Haven

    PEX bends around corners and comes in long rolls, so a typical repipe needs fewer fittings than copper would on the same layout. Frozen pipes are rare in most of Florida, and many homes have outdoor plumbing that would never survive a northern winter. It often survives freezing where copper would crack. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings where possible, which give a full-bore connection and hold up well over time. Faster install.

    PEX Repiping in Winter Haven

    PEX repipes go faster than copper. Flexible lines snake through walls with fewer openings, and home-run systems from a manifold let you shut off individual fixtures without killing water to the house. Popular choice.
