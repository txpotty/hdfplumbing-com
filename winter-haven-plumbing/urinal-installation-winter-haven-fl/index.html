    Commercial Urinal Installation in Winter Haven

    Offices and restaurants want urinals that survive heavy use.     Residential Urinal Plumbing in Winter Haven, FL

    Home urinals use the same rules as commercial ones. The drain is usually 2 inch, the trap is built into the china, the fixture needs a vent within code distance, and the flush valve needs either a 3/4 inch supply or a lower-flow residential valve on 1/2 inch. Your city may want a permit. We check that first.

    ADA-Compliant Urinal Installation in Winter Haven

    At least one urinal in a public restroom with more than one usually has to be accessible. ADA units need a rim at or below 17 inches, a 13-1/2 inch projection and 30 by 48 inches of clear floor. Flush controls must be reachable. We mark it all during rough-in.

    Flush Valve Urinal Installation in Winter Haven, FL

    Flush valve urinals are the commercial standard. Tankless flushometers deliver a set volume from a 3/4 inch line and recover instantly for the next user. The volume matches the fixture rating. Wrong settings stain bowls.

    Sloan Flush Valve Installation in Winter Haven, FL

    Sloan Royal valves are on most commercial urinals we see. Each Sloan Royal gets the right inside parts for the bowl, a tuned control stop and a rebuild kit on hand. Optima sensor valves fit the same spud. Battery models need no wiring.

    Waterless Cartridge Urinals in Winter Haven

    Each waterless brand uses its own cartridge. Falcon, Waterless Co, Kohler and Zurn units all trap odor differently, and the cartridge or sealing liquid has to be replaced after a set number of uses, which in a busy restroom can mean every few months. A replacement schedule comes with the install. Spare cartridges should stay on site.
