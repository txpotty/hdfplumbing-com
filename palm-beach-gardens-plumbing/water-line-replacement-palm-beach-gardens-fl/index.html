    Water Line Materials We Install in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    Our trucks carry Type K copper, PEX-a and HDPE. Each one fits a different job. All of it is listed for drinking water, meeting NSF 61.

    Main Water Line Repair vs. Replacement in Palm Beach Gardens

    One leak on a twenty-year-old copper line? A repair coupling is often the right call. Three leaks on a galvanized line from the 1950s is different, because the pipe is thin everywhere and the next leak is already forming, so the full line costs less than a string of repairs. We tell you which camp you are in and show you the pipe we cut out.

    Galvanized Main Line Replacement in Palm Beach Gardens

    Galvanized steel was common in homes built before the 1960s. Inside, the pipe rusts once the zinc is gone. After a few decades the opening can be half its original size, and that shows up as weak pressure and orange water, and no amount of cleaning brings it back. It has to come out.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    Pipe pulling works like this. We dig a pit at each end of the old line. A cable or bursting head goes through the old pipe and drags new HDPE or PEX back through the same path, splitting the old pipe if it is brittle, so a driveway, a row of shrubs or a concrete walk can stay where it is.
