    Replacing Pressure-Assisted Toilets in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    Pressure-assisted toilets use a sealed vessel in the tank that stores air under line pressure and pushes water through the bowl hard, which helps in houses with long, low-slope drain runs.     Choosing a Toilet Brand in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    One-Piece Toilet Replacement in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    One-piece toilets have no tank-to-bowl joint, so there are fewer leak points and fewer crevices to clean. They are heavier and pricier than two-piece models.     Toilet Brands We Replace in Palm Beach Gardens

    Brand matters less than model, because every major maker sells a great toilet and a mediocre one at nearly the same price. Our picks come down to flush valve size and the MaP score.     Standard Toilet Replacement in Palm Beach Gardens

    Two-piece toilets are what most houses have, and after twenty or thirty years the tank bolts corrode, the porcelain gets crazed and the flush never quite clears the bowl.     Gravity-Flush Toilet Replacement in Palm Beach Gardens

    Gravity-flush toilets are the simplest and most forgiving, and a well-made one with a 3 inch flush valve will outflush most of the older toilets it replaces.     Wall-Hung Toilet Replacement in Palm Beach Gardens

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, so replacing one means checking the carrier bolts, the gasket on the outlet and the condition of the wall finish around it. Some carriers need new bolts. The new bowl gets matched to the carrier already in the wall whenever possible, which avoids opening the wall.
