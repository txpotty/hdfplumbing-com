    Reliable Water Softener Replacement Services in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    Softener Loop Installation in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    Many homes have a softener loop near the main water line that makes for easy installation. When a home lacks one, we can add a loop with shutoff valves and a bypass. We also route a drain line to a laundry box, floor drain or approved location. A good loop keeps outdoor hose bibs on hard water to save salt. This makes future service simpler.

    Traditional Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Palm Beach Gardens

    Salt-based water softeners are a common and effective solution for hard water in Palm Beach Gardens. These systems use ion exchange to remove calcium and magnesium minerals, which are notorious for causing scale buildup. We replace old, inefficient salt-based units with modern, high-efficiency models that use less salt and water. Our plumbers confirm proper brine tank installation and regeneration settings for strong performance.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Palm Beach Gardens

    Large households and homes with very hard water can benefit from a dual tank system that provides soft water all day and night. While one tank regenerates, the other stays in service. Kinetico and similar non-electric twin tank systems are popular for this reason. We size dual tank systems to your peak demand. They cost more upfront but prevent hard water bleed-through during regeneration.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Palm Beach Gardens

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly exhausted. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first few weeks.

    Water Softener Failure Diagnosis in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    Before replacing, we check whether your softener has a simple fix like a clogged injector, failed motor or wrong settings. If the valve is obsolete or parts are no longer available, replacement is usually the better value. We test hardness before and after the softener to confirm the problem. We explain the cost of repair versus replacement clearly. Call (904) 925-7356 to schedule a check.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    For homeowners in Palm Beach Gardens, FL looking for an alternative to traditional salt-based systems, salt-free water conditioners offer a different approach. These units, sometimes called descalers, condition water to prevent mineral scale without removing the minerals or adding salt. While they do not produce soft feeling water, they effectively protect pipes and appliances from scale. We can replace your existing salt-free system or help you switch from a salt-based unit.

    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

If your Fleck-controlled softener is beyond repair or simply outdated, HDF Plumbing can expertly replace the entire unit.
