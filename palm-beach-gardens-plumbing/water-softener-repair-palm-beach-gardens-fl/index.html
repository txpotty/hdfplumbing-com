    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Service in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    The tank looks full but makes no brine. A hard crust has formed and is keeping the salt from dissolving. Bridges get broken up, mushy salt gets cleaned out and the float, brine well and safety valve get checked. In a hot or damp garage, switching to pellets cuts down on bridging.

    Resin Testing and Replacement in Palm Beach Gardens

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Treated city water slowly breaks resin down into mush. After a capacity test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced, and a good tank and valve with new resin performs like new. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    No-salt conditioners do not remove minerals, they change how scale forms, but they still need service. If scale comes back or pressure drops, the media or the prefilter is usually the problem. HDF Plumbing repairs these units in Palm Beach Gardens, FL. The media wears out in a few years.

    Bypass and Fitting Leak Repair in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    Bypass valves, the O-rings where the valve meets the tank, cracked fittings and the drain line are the usual leak points. A minor drip still wastes water and damages floors and cabinets. We replace O-rings or fittings and test under pressure. Mineral tank cracks cannot be fixed.

    Control Valve Repair in Palm Beach Gardens

    Water that runs to the drain all day usually means the control valve is stuck between cycles. HDF Plumbing rebuilds stuck valves with the correct seal and spacer kit for your model.     Control Valve Troubleshooting and Repair in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    Stuck pistons, worn seals and failed drive motors cause most control valve trouble, and the symptoms are hard water, constant regeneration or a leak at the head. We diagnose the valve on site.
