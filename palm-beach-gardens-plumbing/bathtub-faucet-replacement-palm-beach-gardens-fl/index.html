    Standard Bathtub Faucet Replacement in Palm Beach Gardens

    Single-handle and three-handle tub faucets come out differently. Single-handle valves can often be updated from the front, while three-handle valves usually get cut out and replaced. We check before quoting. Two-handle models fall in between.

    Three-Handle to Single-Handle Conversion in Palm Beach Gardens

    Three-handle faucets are worth converting. The old valve comes out, a pressure-balance valve goes in, and a remodel plate covers the extra holes. Most need an access panel or a back-wall opening. Tile around the plate stays intact.

    Cartridge and Stem Replacement in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    Thermostatic and Pressure-Balance Valves in Palm Beach Gardens, FL
