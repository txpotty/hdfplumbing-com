    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    A sump pump is the part of an interior system that actually moves the water. We set a lidded basin below slab level, install a cast iron pump sized to the water coming in, and add a battery backup or water-powered backup for the night the power goes out during a storm. Check valves matter. Without one, the discharge water runs back into the pit and the pump short-cycles. The discharge line gets routed at least 10 feet from the house.

    Cost-Effective Waterproofing Solutions in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    Not every wet wall needs a $15,000 excavation. Sometimes the fix is a downspout extension and regrading the soil so it slopes away from the house, six inches of drop over the first ten feet, and that costs a few hundred dollars. We start with the cheapest fix that will actually work. Every option comes written out with its price.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Palm Beach Gardens

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and often add a dehumidifier to keep relative humidity below 60 percent. Constant heat and humidity are the main plumbing stress in Florida, keeping water heaters, AC drain lines, and exterior pipes working year round. Wood rot slows to a stop. Drier air under the floor also cuts down on the musty smell in the house.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Palm Beach Gardens

    A drip from a shower valve cartridge, a sweated copper stub or a loose drain slip nut can soak a wall cavity for months before paint bubbles. We find it with a moisture meter, a thermal camera that shows the cool wet spot, and a pressure test on the supply lines. Then we cut a clean access hole. We replace the leaking fitting or pipe section. Wet insulation comes out, and fans dry the cavity before we close it up.

    Paying once beats paying every two years.     Foundation Crack Repair in Palm Beach Gardens

    Crack injection takes about two hours per crack. We set injection ports every 8 to 12 inches along the crack, seal the surface with epoxy paste, and pump in resin starting at the lowest port and working up until it pushes out the next port. Polyurethane stays flexible if the wall moves a little, and epoxy bonds the two sides back together. The surface paste gets ground off once it cures.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Walls Waterproofing in Palm Beach Gardens

    Most jobs fall between $3,000 for interior crack sealing and $15,000 or more for exterior excavation with new footing drains. The size of the wall, how wet it gets, which method fits and how hard the wall is to get to all move the number. Access matters most. Nothing starts until you have the quote in writing.

    It removes the water mold needs, so new growth slows down. It does not kill mold that is already there. Any mold already growing needs to be remediated before or alongside the waterproofing, and once the space is dry and sealed it should stay that way.

    Every install gets a written warranty on materials and workmanship. The coverage and the term are spelled out in your proposal, and when a manufacturer offers its own warranty on a membrane or resin we register it in your name. Store the paperwork with your home documents.

    Crack repairs take a few hours. Interior drain tile usually takes one to three days, and a full exterior dig around the whole house can run three to seven days or more depending on weather and how deep the footing sits. We give you a timeline at the first visit.

    Waterproofing takes away the moisture mold needs to grow. Existing mold should be removed and the area dried before we seal anything, and for larger growth, generally more than about ten square feet, we recommend a remediation company. Small patches we cut out and replace ourselves.

    Interior systems manage water after it has come through the wall, with drain tile along the footing and a sump to pump it out. Exterior waterproofing stops water before it reaches the wall, which means digging down to the footing and applying a membrane to the outside face. The exterior route costs more.

    Xypex, Tremco, Sika and Henry products show up on a lot of our jobs, from crystalline admixtures to elastomeric membranes and sealants. The product depends on the wall. Crystalline coatings work well on poured concrete, but block walls usually call for an elastomeric membrane or drainage board.

    Check the basement after a heavy rain. Wet spots on the walls, mold or mildew, a musty smell in the crawl space, efflorescence on concrete, new cracks in the foundation or standing water on the floor all mean water is getting in somewhere. Those signs usually get worse, not better.
