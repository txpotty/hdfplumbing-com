    Standard Disposal Installation in Palm Beach Gardens

    Most households in Palm Beach Gardens do well with a 1/3 to 1/2 horsepower continuous-feed unit.     High-Horsepower Disposal Installation in Palm Beach Gardens

    A 3/4 or 1 HP unit is bigger and heavier than a starter model, so we measure the cabinet first. We also check the circuit. Bigger motors draw more at startup.

    High-Capacity Disposal Installation in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    Busy kitchens get the most from a 1 HP unit, like an InSinkErator Evolution or a Waste King Legend model. These units weigh more. They get supported during mounting.

    On new builds and remodels in Palm Beach Gardens, we work with your contractor so the disposal, the sink and the dishwasher are planned together.
