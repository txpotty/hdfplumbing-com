    No Hot Water Tankless Repair in Palm Beach Gardens

    No hot water at all usually points to ignition, gas supply or a heat exchanger choked with scale. The service menu's error history is where we start, then test the igniter, flame rod and gas pressure in that order, because a weak flame signal and low pressure can look identical on the display. We look at the vent run as well. Common igniters and flame rods ride on the truck.

    Noritz Tankless Water Heater Repair in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    Noritz heaters hold up well, though flow sensors and vent joints still fail with age. A code 90 points to combustion trouble, often a dirty fan or a restricted intake. We clean the fan and burner, check the vent joints for gaps, and test the flow sensor's output against the spec sheet. Gaskets get replaced if they leak.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Repair in Palm Beach Gardens

    Pressure at full fire matters on a gas tankless burner, not just at the moment it lights. We put a manometer on the inlet test port and run every hot tap in the house, because pressure that drops below the manufacturer's minimum under load causes flame loss and cold slugs. Then we check the exhaust. Codes come off when the numbers are right.

    Recirculation Pump Repair in Palm Beach Gardens

    Recirculation cuts the wait for hot water at a far bathroom from a minute or more to a few seconds. A dead pump or a stuck check valve means you get long waits again or lukewarm water in the cold line. We test the pump, look for a thermal bypass valve under the farthest sink, and set the schedule so it runs around your mornings and evenings. Two settings, mostly.

    Rinnai Error Code Repair in Palm Beach Gardens

    Rinnai owners usually call about a code 10, which points to an air supply or exhaust blockage. We check the vent terminal for bird nests and dryer lint, then pull the front cover to inspect the intake screen and the combustion fan. A code 10 can also come from a vent run that is too long for the model. We measure.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Repair in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    Rinnai units are common, and scale causes most of their service calls. The LC code is the unit's own warning that the exchanger needs a flush. We also see dirty flame rods behind a code 11 or 12 and tired combustion fans behind a code 61, and we test every one against Rinnai's published values. Parts come from the factory list.

    Condensing Tankless Repair in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    Condensing tankless heaters pull extra heat out of the exhaust and make acidic condensate that has to drain somewhere. That condensate runs through a trap and, where code or the manual calls for it, a neutralizer filled with limestone or marble chips before it reaches the drain. Clogged traps shut the unit down. We clean the trap, refill the neutralizer and check the PVC vent for sag or the wrong slope.

    Tankless Error Code Diagnostics in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    Every tankless brand flashes its own codes, and the same number can mean different things on two units. A Rinnai 11 is no ignition, a Navien E003 is ignition failure, and a Noritz 11 is also an ignition fault, but the tests behind them differ. We read the code, pull the history and test the part tied to it. Guessing gets expensive.
