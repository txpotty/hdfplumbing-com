    Sewer Line Leak Detection and Repair in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    Undetected sewer line leaks can lead to unpleasant odors, wet spots in your yard and even structural damage to your home's foundation. HDF Plumbing uses precise leak detection methods to pinpoint the source of the leak, whether it's a minor crack or a significant break. Once found, we provide targeted repairs, from patching small leaks to replacing compromised sections of pipe, ensuring a watertight system.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Palm Beach Gardens

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful cleaning method that uses high-pressure water streams to clear stubborn blockages, grease buildup and even tree roots from sewer lines. It's much more effective than snaking for thorough cleaning and stopping recurring clogs. This service is especially helpful for restaurants and commercial properties in Palm Beach Gardens dealing with heavy grease accumulation, as well as homes with persistent drain issues.

    Sewer Line Backups & Blockages in Palm Beach Gardens

    Sewer backups are often caused by severe blockages from grease buildup, foreign objects or extensive tree root intrusion. Our team uses advanced sewer camera inspections to identify the exact cause and location of the blockage. We then use hydro-jetting or mechanical snaking to clear the obstruction, restoring proper flow to your system and preventing further damage. This approach is highly effective for the stubborn clogs often found in older Florida homes.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies often form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after years of wet weather. Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and damage buried water and sewer lines. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is usually a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair technique where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is effective for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without major digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an great option for preserving landscapes in Palm Beach Gardens.

    Sewer Pipe Replacement Options in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    When repairs are no longer enough due to extensive damage, age or material failure, sewer pipe replacement becomes necessary. We offer various replacement options, including traditional trenching and modern trenchless technologies like pipe bursting or pipe lining. Our experts will assess your situation and recommend the most cost-effective and least disruptive solution for your Florida property, giving you a long-lasting new sewer line.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Palm Beach Gardens

    When trenchless repair isn't possible due to severe damage or specific pipe configurations, traditional dig-and-replace methods are necessary. This involves excavating a trench to access and replace the damaged section of the sewer line. While more intrusive, it is sometimes the only way to address badly collapsed pipes, significant offsets or certain pipe materials common in older Florida properties. Our team makes sure of careful excavation and efficient replacement, minimizing the impact on your property in Palm Beach Gardens.
