    What Goes Into a Tankless Install in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    Electric tankless heaters make sense in Palm Beach Gardens, FL homes without gas, or for a single sink or a guest bath far from the main heater. A whole-home electric unit draws heavy amperage. Some need two or three 40 to 60 amp double-pole breakers, and a 100-amp panel often cannot carry that on top of the air conditioner and range. Panel capacity gets checked up front.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Palm Beach Gardens

    Gas tankless units in Palm Beach Gardens are the common pick for whole-house hot water because a single 199,000 BTU burner can feed two showers and a sink at the same time without losing temperature. Gas is the catch. Most homes need the line upsized from the meter, often from half-inch to three-quarter inch or larger. We test the new gas piping before first fire.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Installation in Palm Beach Gardens

    Commercial hot water loads in Palm Beach Gardens run longer and harder than a house. A busy restaurant may run a dish machine, three hand sinks and a mop sink at once, and the tankless rack has to hold temperature through the whole lunch rush without dropping a degree. Units share a header manifold. Service one, keep the others going.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Services in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    Businesses in Palm Beach Gardens, FL with steady hot water use, like salons, restaurants and small gyms, often get more out of two or three tankless units racked together than one big tank. One unit can fail and the rest still carry the load. Peak demand sets the rack size. Commercial installs usually need a larger gas service and a mechanical permit.

    Tankless vs Tank Cost Comparison in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    Upfront, a tankless install in Palm Beach Gardens, FL costs more than a standard tank because of the unit price, the gas line upgrade and the venting changes. The gap shrinks over time. Lower standby loss, a lifespan of up to 20 years against about 10 for a tank, and parts that can be replaced instead of the whole unit all count toward the total cost of ownership. Ask for both prices.

    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    Calcium scale is what kills most tankless units early. Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving stubborn scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. Scale coats the heat exchanger, cuts efficiency and triggers error codes, and left alone it can crack the exchanger and end the warranty claim before it starts. Flush it yearly. We install isolation valves for that.

    Rinnai, Navien and Noritz Installation in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    We install Rinnai, Navien, Noritz and Rheem in Palm Beach Gardens, FL homes all year long. Rinnai brings range and parts support, Navien offers built-in recirc, Noritz makes compact condensing units for tight spots, and Rheem rounds out the list. Warranty terms vary. We explain the differences.

    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in Palm Beach Gardens

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to get hot water to a far bathroom, because the unit has to sense flow and fire first. A recirculation pump on a timer or a motion sensor keeps the hot line primed, so the wait at the farthest sink drops from a minute to a few seconds. It saves water too.
