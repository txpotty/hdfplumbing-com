    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. We test angle stops and the main shutoff valve, which often seize over time. We also check for proper venting and gurgling drains. Small fixes like these are easy to plan for once you know they exist.

    Leak Detection and Slab Screening in Palm Beach Gardens

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a strong sign of a hidden leak. We test the meter, check pressure loss and inspect for warm spots, damp baseboards and mildew.
