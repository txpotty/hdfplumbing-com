    Double check assemblies work for low-hazard connections such as many fire lines and some irrigation.     Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Palm Beach Gardens

    A double check valve assembly, or DCVA, uses two spring-loaded checks in series with four test cocks.     Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Palm Beach Gardens

    Two independent checks and a relief valve make up an RPZ. When pressure between the checks gets too close to supply pressure, the relief opens, dumping water to the floor or a drain so nothing can pass upstream, which is why we never bury one in a pit in Palm Beach Gardens. We install Watts and Febco units.
