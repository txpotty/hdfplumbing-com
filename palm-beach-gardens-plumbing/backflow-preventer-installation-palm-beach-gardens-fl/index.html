    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Palm Beach Gardens

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules allow. We size them to avoid excessive pressure loss on your system.

    Backflow Testing and Certification in Palm Beach Gardens

    Most water providers require testable backflow assemblies to be certified at installation and then once a year. Our certified testers check each valve's opening point, relief valve operation and differential pressure. If a device fails, we can repair or rebuild it right away with genuine parts. We submit passing results so you avoid compliance letters or service interruption.

    Irrigation Backflow Preventer Installation in Palm Beach Gardens

    Landscape irrigation is the most common reason homeowners need a backflow device. Fertilizer, herbicides and stagnant water in sprinkler and drip lines can be siphoned into your drinking water if pressure drops. We install pressure vacuum breakers or double check assemblies based on local requirements and the design of your system. Above-ground installs are set at least 12 inches above the highest sprinkler head when a PVB is used.

    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Palm Beach Gardens

    The double check valve assembly, or DCVA, is a common backflow preventer for low-hazard applications where the consequences of backflow are not severe. In Palm Beach Gardens, DCVAs are often installed on fire sprinkler systems or irrigation lines. Our certified plumbers carefully install these devices, making sure both check valves operate correctly and are properly sealed to prevent backpressure or back-siphonage into the main water supply.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Palm Beach Gardens

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are commonly required for high-hazard applications in Palm Beach Gardens. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. HDF Plumbing focuses on precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties. We work with top brands like Watts and Febco.
