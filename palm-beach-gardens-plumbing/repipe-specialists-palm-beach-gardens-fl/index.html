    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Palm Beach Gardens

    Slab leaks are a costly and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and crack buried water and sewer lines. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the compromised pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use modern detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Palm Beach Gardens

    Copper piping offers excellent durability, corrosion resistance, and a long lifespan, making it a premium choice for many homeowners in Palm Beach Gardens. While the upfront cost is higher than PEX, copper provides superior rigidity and resistance to UV light if exposed. Copper also inherently inhibits bacterial growth, contributing to cleaner water.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Palm Beach Gardens
