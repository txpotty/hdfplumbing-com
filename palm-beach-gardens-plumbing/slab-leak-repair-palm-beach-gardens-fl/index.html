    Sewer Line Slab Leak Repair in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    Slab leaks aren't always about clean water pipes, since sewer lines can also leak under your foundation. These leaks pose significant health risks and can cause extensive damage. We are experienced in detecting and repairing sewer line slab leaks, using camera inspections to identify the exact problem area. Our methods ensure proper waste removal and prevent contamination in your Palm Beach Gardens, FL property, following health and safety codes.

    Slab Leak Detection in Palm Beach Gardens

    We start by shutting off fixtures and watching the meter to confirm a leak. Then we isolate the hot and cold lines to find out which one is leaking. Using acoustic ground microphones, we listen for the sound of escaping water through the concrete. Thermal imaging shows hot water leaks as warm areas on the floor surface.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Palm Beach Gardens

    Cold water slab leaks are harder to feel but still waste water and wet the soil. They often show up as a high water bill or a meter that moves with all fixtures off. We isolate the cold line, find the leak and repair or reroute it. Fixing cold leaks promptly helps protect your foundation.

    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    When the leak is isolated and the rest of the pipe is in good shape, a spot repair through the slab is a practical option. We cut a small opening, excavate to the pipe and replace the damaged section. The trench is compacted and the concrete is patched. Flooring is then restored by your preferred contractor.

    Slab Leak Repair Cost Comparison in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    Spot repairs usually have a lower upfront cost but involve breaking concrete and repairing flooring. Reroutes cost a bit more but avoid concrete work and future leaks in that line. Whole-house repipes are the best choice when you have had multiple slab leaks or old copper throughout. We give you written prices for each option.

    Advanced Leak Detection Technology in Palm Beach Gardens

    Our approach to effective slab leak repair includes investing in the latest diagnostic technology. We use equipment such as thermal imaging cameras to detect temperature changes caused by leaking hot water lines, and highly sensitive acoustic sensors to listen for the sound of escaping water.     Electronic Slab Leak Detection in Palm Beach Gardens

    Locating a hidden slab leak requires specialized tools. Our plumbers use advanced electronic listening devices and thermal imaging cameras to pinpoint the exact location of the leak under your concrete foundation. This precise detection reduces the need for extensive concrete removal, saving you time and money.     Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Palm Beach Gardens

    For more complex or recurring slab leaks, or when the original piping is badly deteriorated, pipe rerouting is an excellent long-term solution. This method involves abandoning the leaky pipe under the slab and installing new plumbing lines through walls or the attic, bypassing the foundation entirely. This removes future slab leak risks from that particular line and is often preferred for older homes in Palm Beach Gardens with aging copper or galvanized pipes.
