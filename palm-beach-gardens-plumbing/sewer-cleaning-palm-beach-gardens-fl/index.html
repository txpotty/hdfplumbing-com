    Cast iron rots from the inside. Many homes in Florida built before the mid 1970s have cast iron drains under the slab, which often crack and corrode with age.     Jetting a Sewer Line in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    Grease and scale are jetting work. A cable only scratches them. The rear jets drive the hose forward while the spray scours the wall, so the line ends up much closer to its original size.
