    A standard shower installation, such as a tub-shower replacement, can often be completed within one to two days.     Pressure-balance valves keep the hot and cold ratio steady when pressure changes. Thermostatic valves control the actual temperature and allow separate volume control, which suits multi-head showers.

    Large rain heads and multiple outlets use more hot water. We check your water heater size and recovery rate and recommend upgrades when needed.

    A pressure-balancing valve maintains a consistent temperature by balancing incoming hot and cold water pressure, preventing sudden temperature changes. A thermostatic valve maintains a precise temperature setting regardless of pressure fluctuations, offering a higher level of comfort and safety, especially in homes with children or elderly residents.
