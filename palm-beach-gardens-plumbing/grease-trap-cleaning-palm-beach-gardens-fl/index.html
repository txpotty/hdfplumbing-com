    Commercial Grease Trap Cleaning in Palm Beach Gardens

    Restaurants and hospital kitchens in Palm Beach Gardens run the same basic setup, a trap or interceptor sitting between the sinks and the sewer, sized by flow rate or by gallons.     Large Outdoor Grease Interceptor Cleaning in Palm Beach Gardens

    Gravity interceptors in the parking lot often hold 750 to 2,000 gallons across two or three compartments. Each compartment gets pumped separately. On big tanks we break the grease cap with a pressure wand first, since a cap that has set up hard will plug the suction hose and leave half the grease behind. No one goes inside without confined-space gear.

    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Lines in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    A cable machine pokes a hole through grease. Jetting actually removes it. We run a jetter at roughly 3,000 to 4,000 psi with a rotating nozzle that cuts the grease off the full circumference of the pipe and flushes it downstream. A camera pass afterward shows whether the line is really clean.

    Outdoor Grease Interceptor Pumping in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    In-ground interceptors need a vacuum truck and usually a pressure wand to break up the cap. We open every compartment and pump each one to the floor. With the tank empty, we inspect the tees, baffle wall, risers and lid seals, since a cracked riser lets dirt and groundwater into the tank. Lids go back on tight.

    Scheduled Grease Trap Maintenance Plans in Palm Beach Gardens

    Most pretreatment programs use the 25 percent rule: pump before floating grease and settled solids together take up a quarter of the trap's liquid depth. Other programs simply set a fixed interval, commonly 90 days for large interceptors. Whichever rule your program uses, we schedule to it. Changes are easy.

    Indoor Grease Trap Cleaning in Palm Beach Gardens

    Indoor traps usually sit under the three-compartment sink or in a recessed pit near the dish station. Space is tight back there. We also check the gasket and the flow control.
