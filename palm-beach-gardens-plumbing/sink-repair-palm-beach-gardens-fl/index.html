    Hair Clog Clearing in Palm Beach Gardens

    Hair is the bathroom sink's main problem. We pull the stopper, strip the hair mat off it, then run a small hand auger through the trap and into the wall arm instead of pouring in drain cleaner. We flush the overflow channel while we are there. That slot smells when it fills with slime.

    Commercial Sink Repair in Palm Beach Gardens

    Restaurants and salons cannot wait a week for a sink. A down hand sink can cost you an inspection, so we work around your hours and carry commercial faucets and basket strainers. Most commercial clogs are grease. We also check the grease interceptor connection when the drain backs up.

    Leaking P-Trap Fix in Palm Beach Gardens

    Check the slip nuts first. Overtightened plastic slip nuts crack, and the beveled washers inside them flatten until each joint weeps. The whole trap gets replaced as a kit. A 1-1/4 inch kit for bath sinks, 1-1/2 inch for kitchens.

    Burst Supply Line Repair in Palm Beach Gardens

    A burst line under a sink can push out several gallons a minute. If the angle stop will not stop it, the main shutoff will, and the sooner it is closed the less water ends up inside the vanity floor and the subfloor below. Then lay towels down. We replace the line and the stop and moisture-check the cabinet.

    Garbage Disposal Repair in Palm Beach Gardens

    A disposal that hums but will not spin is usually jammed. We free the flywheel from underneath with a 1/4 inch hex key, press the red reset button, and check for a bent impeller or a broken grinding plate. Leaks are a different story. A cracked housing means a new disposal, usually an InSinkErator or a Waste King.

    Cracked or Loose Basin Repair in Palm Beach Gardens

    Undermount clips let go more often than people think. When the epoxy under the counter fails, the bowl drops a quarter inch, the caulk line splits, and water from the counter runs straight down into the cabinet. We reset the bowl on new clips. Hairline cracks in porcelain near the drain mean replacement.

    Overflow and Drain Flange Leak Repair in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    Some leaks only show up when the bowl is full. A leak like that is usually the overflow gasket or the flange putty, which only get wet when the bowl fills. We pull the drain and reseal it. Marble and some granite get silicone, because putty can stain them.

    Outdoor and Utility Sink Repair in Palm Beach Gardens

    Outdoor kitchen sinks and garage utility tubs sit in the weather. When a rare freeze is forecast, covering outdoor valves and letting a faucet drip is usually enough to prevent damage. Sun breaks down plastic traps and supply lines left outside, and yard dirt and grass clippings clog the drain faster than kitchen scraps ever do. We fix leaks at the hose bib too. A frost-free sillcock is worth it on exposed walls.
