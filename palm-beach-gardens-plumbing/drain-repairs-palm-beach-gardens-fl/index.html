    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Repairs in Palm Beach Gardens

    A simple clog clearing starts around $150. Main line work costs more. Complex main line repairs can run past $3,000, with the type of drain, how bad the damage is and how hard the pipe is to reach setting most of the price. We price it after we look.

    Unclogging a drain does not need one. Bigger changes might. Replacing a stack, relocating a drain or replacing a section of the building drain in Palm Beach Gardens may require a plumbing permit, depending on local rules and how much of the system is being changed. We pull it when one applies.

    Tightening a slip nut or replacing a plastic trap is fair game. Hand snakes are fine too. Anything inside a wall, under a floor or involving cast iron, or a leak that keeps returning after you have tightened it, is a job for a licensed plumber with the right tools. Skip the caustic cleaners.

    Cleaning removes a blockage from a sound pipe. Repair fixes the pipe. A crack, a sag, a broken fitting or a missing vent will keep causing clogs no matter how often the line gets cleaned, which is why recurring clogs usually call for a fix instead of another cleaning. We check on video.

    Snugging a slip nut or replacing a plastic trap is a fair DIY job. Do not crank it down. If the leak comes from a cracked tailpiece, a corroded metal drain or somewhere inside the wall, a plumber can make a lasting repair and check the cabinet floor and subfloor for hidden damage. Water damage under sinks spreads quietly.

    Clearing a simple clog takes an hour or two. Repairs run longer. Replacing a section of main line or opening a wall to swap a cast iron stack can take a full day or more, depending on access and whether a permit inspection is needed. The quote includes a time estimate.

    Hydro-jetting scours the pipe with water at several thousand psi. It cleans wall to wall. A snake only punches a hole through a clog, but a jet nozzle strips buildup off the entire pipe wall. We camera first on old or brittle pipe.

    Slow drains, gurgling and sewer smell are the big three warnings. Drips in the cabinet count too. A drain that clogs again a few weeks after it was cleared, or water spots below a bathroom, point to a pipe problem instead of a simple clog. Catch it early.
