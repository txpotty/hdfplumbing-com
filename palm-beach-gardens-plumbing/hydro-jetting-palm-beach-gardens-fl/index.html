    HDF Plumbing uses professional-grade hydro jetting equipment from trusted manufacturers like Spartan Tool and MyTana. These brands are known for their reliability, powerful performance, and safety features, so we can deliver the most effective cleaning for your pipes.

    Drain snaking uses a cable with an auger that punches a hole through a blockage to restore some flow. Hydro jetting, however, uses high-pressure water to completely scour the inside of the pipe, removing all buildup and restoring the pipe to a near-new condition. Hydro jetting offers a more complete and longer-lasting clean.

    Hydro jetting is highly effective against a wide range of stubborn clogs that traditional snaking cannot fully clear. This includes heavy grease buildup, mineral scale, sludge, soap scum, hair clogs, and even invasive tree roots that have gotten into your sewer lines. It is designed to scour the entire pipe surface.

    Repeat clogs in the same line, slow drains after snaking, sewer odors and known grease or root problems are good reasons to consider jetting.

    Hydro jetting is exceptionally effective against almost all types of clogs. It can break through solid obstructions like tree roots, flush out heavy grease and food buildup, clear mineral scale, and remove compacted sludge and debris that traditional snaking often leaves behind.
