    Pedestal Sump Pump Service in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the water, so the wear shows up in the shaft, the bearings and the float rod. We check the rod for bends and the stops for slipping. Bearings get a listen. A motor that whines or rattles on startup is usually close to done.

    Home Sump Pump Service in Palm Beach Gardens

    A typical house pit is about 18 inches across and two feet deep, and most of what goes wrong happens inside it. We pull the pump, clear the intake screen and scoop out the silt. After that, the pump gets a real water test. Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and damage buried water and sewer lines.

    Backup Pump Battery Service in Palm Beach Gardens

    Outages and heavy rain tend to arrive together. The backup takes over when the primary loses power or simply stops. Our plumber load tests the battery, tests the charger and alarm, then runs the backup alone with the primary unplugged. Expect a new battery every three to five years.

    Submersible Sump Pump Maintenance in Palm Beach Gardens

    Submersible pumps sit at the bottom of the pit, which keeps them quiet but hides the problems. We lift the unit out to look at the seals and the cord. A cracked cord usually means a new pump. The intake screen gets cleaned, and we look over the housing for corrosion, since pumps sitting in standing water for years rust from the bottom up.

    Annual Sump Pump Inspections in Palm Beach Gardens

    Once a year is the minimum. Each yearly visit covers the motor, the impeller, the float and the discharge line. Then we fill the pit and time how long the pump takes to empty it, because a slow drawdown is the earliest sign of a worn impeller or a partly blocked line.

    Maintenance Versus Replacement Cost in Palm Beach Gardens, FL

    A new sump pump, installed, often runs $400 to $1,200 depending on horsepower, pit work and whether you add a battery backup. A maintenance visit costs a small slice of that number. With regular service, seven to ten years is typical. You get a straight answer on whether your pump has life left or should be replaced.

    Sump Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Palm Beach Gardens

    Silt and gravel wash into a pit through the drain tile every time it rains, and over a few years that layer can bury the intake screen. Our plumber drains the basin and digs the sediment out by hand. A clean pit lets the float move freely. The pump runs cooler too.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Palm Beach Gardens

    Heavy afternoon thunderstorms are common in summer, and saturated ground can push water into drain lines and slow every fixture in the house. A few weeks ahead of the wet season, we run a hose into the pit until the float trips and the pump starts. Then we time how fast it pumps out and listen to the bearings. A struggling pump can then be replaced on a calm day instead of in the middle of a storm.
