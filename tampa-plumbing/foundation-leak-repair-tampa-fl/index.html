    Slab Leak Detection & Repair in Tampa

    Slab leaks are notorious for being difficult to detect, often showing subtle signs like warm spots on the floor or a sudden jump in your water bill. HDF Plumbing uses acoustic and infrared technology to precisely locate these hidden leaks under your concrete foundation.     Water Line Reroute in Tampa

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the foundation and runs a new line through the attic or walls. This avoids breaking concrete and removes the risk of future leaks under the slab. We typically use PEX for reroutes because it is flexible and resists corrosion. Drywall openings are kept small and neat for simple patching.

    Tunneling for Foundation Leaks in Tampa, FL

    In some cases, the best way to repair a foundation leak is by tunneling beneath the slab. HDF Plumbing is experienced in safe and efficient tunneling, minimizing disruption to your Tampa, FL home and ensuring a complete repair of the damaged pipe section.
