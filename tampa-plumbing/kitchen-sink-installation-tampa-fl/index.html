    How We Install Kitchen Sinks in Tampa, FL: Style by Style

    Drop-In Sink Replacement in Tampa

    Removing an old drop-in begins by slicing through the old caulk and backing off the clips from below. Old clips are often rusted solid, so we bring a reciprocating saw and cut them off rather than prying on the counter. Laminate edges chip easily. Your new rim has to fully cover the old hole.

    Restaurant Sink Installation in Tampa

    Restaurants, cafes and commissary kitchens in Tampa get their commercial sinks installed by HDF Plumbing. Hand sinks need tempered or hot and cold water.     Commercial Kitchen Sink Installation in Tampa, FL

    Three-compartment sinks, prep sinks and hand sinks carry separate health code requirements. Prep sinks usually need an indirect drain to a floor sink with an air gap, while the three-compartment sink often runs through the grease trap. We confirm the rules first. We work before opening or after close so service is not interrupted.

    Workstation Sink Installation in Tampa, FL

    Workstation sinks have a ledge for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks, and most are single deep bowls with an offset drain. We plan the trap around that offset drain so the disposal fits and the cabinet keeps usable space on one side. Check the faucet reach as well.

    Apron-Front Sinks in Tampa, FL

    Apron sinks need a cabinet cut to fit the front panel, and the bowl must be set before the counter gets measured. We set the sink and frame early, so the fabricator measures from the actual bowl instead of a spec sheet. It prevents a crooked reveal. Cast iron versions can top 150 pounds, so we bring two people.

    Stainless Steel Sink Installation in Tampa

    Stainless sinks come in gauges from 16 to 22, and the lower the number, the thicker the steel. Thin 22 gauge bowls flex and drum when the disposal runs, so we add sound pads or suggest an 18 gauge or heavier sink if you have not bought one yet. Mounting style changes the plumbing. Either way, we hook up the faucet, strainers and disposal.

    Composite Sink Installation in Tampa

    Granite and quartz composite sinks resist scratches on the surface but can chip at the drain hole if a strainer is overtightened. Hand tight comes first. Composite makers usually call for silicone instead of plumber's putty under the strainer, since some putties stain the material, and we follow that sheet.

    Stainless Sinks With Sound Pads in Tampa

    Budget stainless sinks ring like a drum when dishes hit them or the disposal runs. Better sinks come with rubber pads and an undercoating, and if yours does not, we can add a coat of spray undercoating before it goes in. It is a small thing. Once the bowl is mounted, the space underneath is hard to reach, so this is a job worth doing while the sink is still on the floor.
