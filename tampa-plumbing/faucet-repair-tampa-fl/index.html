    Compression Faucet Repair in Tampa

    Compression faucets, often found in older Tampa homes, are known for their separate hot and cold handles. When these faucets leak, it's usually due to worn-out rubber washers or packing. Our team is skilled in disassembling these classic fixtures, replacing the necessary seals and washers, and reassembling them to stop leaks for good. We respect the character of older homes and make sure these repairs are done carefully.

    Ball Faucet Repair in Tampa, FL

    Ball faucets, recognizable by their single lever that moves over a rounded cap, are common in many kitchens. They use a rotating ball joint to control water flow and temperature. If your ball faucet is leaking from the spout or base, or if the handle feels loose, it's likely time for a repair. We can replace the spring-loaded rubber seats and O-rings, which are typically the culprits, restoring smooth operation and preventing drips.

    Dripping Faucet Solutions in Tampa

    That constant drip, drip, drip isn't just annoying, it's a sign of a deeper problem. Most dripping faucets are caused by internal component wear, like a deteriorated rubber washer or a cracked cartridge. Our team provides precise solutions, replacing the specific parts that are failing. We work with all types of faucets, including compression, cartridge, disc, and ball varieties, ensuring a quiet and drip-free operation once again.

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Tampa

    Weak or sputtering flow is usually caused by mineral buildup in the aerator or cartridge. We remove and clean or replace the aerator, flush the lines and check the cartridge for scale blockage. On pull-down faucets, the spray head and check valves can also clog. If flow is low at every fixture, we test whole-house water pressure.

    Leaking Faucet Handle and Base Repair in Tampa, FL

    Water seeping around the handle or pooling at the base points to failed O-rings or a loose mounting nut. If left alone, the leak can soak into the cabinet below and damage the sink base. We replace O-rings, tighten mounting hardware and reseal the base. We also check the deck for water damage.

    Outdoor Hose Bib Repair in Tampa

    Outdoor spigots take a beating from sun and weather. Frozen pipes are rare in most of Florida, and many homes have outdoor plumbing that would never hold up in a northern winter. We repair leaking packing nuts, replace worn washers and install new frost-free hose bibs when needed. We also add vacuum breakers to meet backflow prevention requirements. A leaking hose bib can waste a lot of water over a long season of yard use.

    Faucet Repair Versus Replacement in Tampa, FL

    A cartridge repair often costs well under the price of a new faucet and can add years of life.
