    Burner Assembly and Venting System Repair in Tampa

    A malfunctioning burner assembly can result in poor heating or unusual noises. We carefully inspect the burner for blockages, corrosion or wear. We also check the venting system for proper draft and obstructions, which is vital for safe operation and preventing carbon monoxide buildup in your Tampa home. Proper venting is a key safety consideration.

    Gas Control Valve Replacement in Tampa, FL

    The gas control valve regulates temperature and shuts off gas if the pilot goes out. Newer valves from Honeywell and White-Rodgers use status lights that blink codes when something fails. If the valve itself has failed, we replace it with an exact match or approved universal model. We leak test and confirm temperature control afterward.

    Venting and Draft Repair in Tampa, FL

    Atmospheric gas water heaters depend on a properly sloped metal flue to carry exhaust out of the home. Disconnected sections, rusted draft hoods and poor slope can let combustion gases spill back. We repair or replace vent sections and confirm draft with a test. Proper venting is critical for your family's safety.

    Cost-Effective Gas Water Heater Solutions in Tampa, FL

    Pilot Light Repair in Tampa

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most common gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and clean or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are completed in about an hour.
