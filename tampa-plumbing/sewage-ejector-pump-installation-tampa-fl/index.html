    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Tampa

    While both sump pumps and sewage ejector pumps remove water, their applications are distinct. HDF Plumbing provides expert guidance and installation for both in Tampa. A sump pump handles groundwater and storm runoff, typically at a basement's lowest point to prevent flooding. A sewage ejector pump, however, is designed for wastewater containing solids from toilets, sinks and showers in below-grade plumbing. We make sure you get the correct pump for your drainage needs, preventing costly mistakes.

    Laundry and Wet Bar Ejector Pumps in Tampa, FL

    Below-grade laundry rooms and wet bars produce gray water that still needs to be lifted to the sewer line. A smaller ejector or laundry tray pump can handle washer discharge and sink drainage.
