    Leaks We Locate in Tampa, FL

    PEX and Copper Leak Detection in Tampa, FL

    Copper leaks are loud on a ground microphone. PEX is quieter, since plastic pipe does not carry sound well, so we lean more on thermal scans, moisture readings and tracer gas when the house is plumbed in it. We handle both every week. Pinholes in copper and failed crimp fittings in PEX are the two leaks we find most.

    Irrigation Leak Detection in Tampa, FL

    Sprinkler laterals crack and zone valves stick open, and either one can waste thousands of gallons before the bill shows it. Check the yard first. Our tech tests the irrigation system separately from the house, runs each zone and traces buried lines, because many water providers say to rule out irrigation first when a summer bill jumps.

    Pool and Spa Leak Detection in Tampa

    Heat and wind can pull a lot of water out of a pool, so it is hard to tell evaporation from a leak. A bucket test settles it. We then pressure test the plumbing lines and dye test around lights, skimmers and return fittings, since a leak left alone can wash out the soil behind the shell.

    Commercial Leak Detection in Tampa

    In Tampa, large buildings have long supply runs where a leak can hide behind finished walls for a long time. We test zone by zone. Our techs isolate risers, mains and fire lines with pressure gauges, then locate leaks with acoustic and thermal tools, working after hours when tenants or customers would otherwise be in the way.

    Pool Leak Detection in Tampa

    A pool losing more than a quarter inch a day beyond evaporation probably has a leak. We start with a bucket test. Then we pressure test the suction and return lines, use an underwater hydrophone to listen, and dye test around the skimmer, lights and main drain to find the exact crack or fitting that is losing water.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection in Tampa, FL

    A cracked drain line leaks wastewater into the soil, which can show up as a sewer smell in the yard, a soggy patch or slow drains. Cameras find these. The camera goes in at the cleanout and runs to the main, recording cracks, offset joints and root entry in PVC, clay and cast iron, so you see the exact distance and depth of the break.

    Preventive Leak Inspections in Tampa, FL

    A yearly leak check catches small problems early. We test static pressure, read the meter with everything off and look at supply lines, water heater fittings and shutoff valves. Older homes with galvanized pipe or original water heater connectors benefit most from a yearly check, since those parts tend to fail all at once. It is a short visit.

    Our Leak Detection Equipment in Tampa

    Our trucks carry ground microphones and correlators, thermal cameras, line tracers, moisture meters and tracer gas kits. Every tool covers a different case. Acoustic gear hears pressurized water escaping, thermal cameras show temperature differences from hot water lines and wet drywall, while tracer gas catches silent leaks on PEX and PVC.
