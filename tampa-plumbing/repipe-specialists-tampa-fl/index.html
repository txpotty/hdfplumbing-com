    Repiping Work We Handle in Tampa, FL

    Commercial Repiping in Tampa, FL

    Commercial buildings in Tampa, FL often have larger pipe, risers that serve several floors and tenants who cannot lose water for a full day. We phase commercial repipes by floor, suite or riser so most of the building stays in service while we work. Night work is possible. We use Type L copper or commercial-grade PEX sized from fixture units and installed to commercial code.

    Copper Repiping in Tampa, FL

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes, with lead-free solder or ProPress fittings. Copper takes a bit longer. Most copper jobs run a day longer than PEX, since each turn needs its own fitting.

    Repiping for Hard Water in Tampa, FL

    Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving heavy scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside pipe, and over years it narrows the bore and speeds up corrosion in metal lines. Pressure suffers. For homes with hard water, we usually recommend PEX-A, which resists scale and does not corrode, and we can add a softener loop or a whole-house filter during the repipe. Planning it now saves a second visit later.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Tampa, FL

    Older homes may still have galvanized steel pipe that rusts from the inside out. Low pressure, brown water and leaks at threaded joints are the typical signs. We remove or abandon the galvanized lines and replace them with PEX or copper, and your water pressure and water quality usually improve the same day the new lines go into service.

    Condo, Townhome and Multi-Family Repipes in Tampa

    Shared walls and HOA rules make repiping attached homes more involved. We coordinate with the HOA, schedule water shutoffs that may affect neighbors and protect adjoining units from dust and noise as much as we can. Property managers get phased schedules. Big buildings get repiped by stack or wing, which lets residents stay home.

    PEX Versus Copper Repipe Pricing in Tampa, FL

    PEX repipes usually cost less than copper because the material is cheaper and installation goes faster. Copper costs more. Copper commonly costs 20 to 40 percent more than PEX, and some owners prefer it anyway for its long history. Both prices include permits, fittings, patching and cleanup.

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Tampa, FL

    Polybutylene, a gray or blue plastic pipe installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s, breaks down with exposure to chlorine and other disinfectants in tap water. It fails without warning. Brittle pipe walls and cracked fittings mean one polybutylene leak usually signals risk across the whole house. We replace the full system with PEX or copper. Insurers often ask about it at renewal.

The first step is temporary repairs so you have water, and then we book the repipe as quickly as the permit and schedule allow. We move fast.
