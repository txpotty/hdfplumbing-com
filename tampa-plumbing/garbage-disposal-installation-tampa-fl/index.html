    Replacing an Old Disposal in Tampa, FL

    Old disposals usually come off with a twist of the mounting ring, once the power is off and the dishwasher hose and discharge tube are disconnected. If the new unit is the same brand and mount style, the swap is faster, but we still replace the flange gasket and putty when the old seal has hardened. Seals harden with age. The old disposal leaves with us.

    Disposal Installation for Double Sinks in Tampa, FL

    On a double sink, the disposal goes on one bowl and a continuous waste or end outlet tee on the other. We lay out the drain so both bowls drain freely and the disposal outlet does not push water into the other bowl, which happens when the tee sits too close to the disposal discharge. We use heavy-wall fittings.

    Disposal Installation on an Existing Sink in Tampa

    For a common 1/2 or 3/4 HP disposal, we remove the old unit if there is one and install the new one on the existing sink. We connect it to the drain, set the P-trap and wire it, then check the clearance under the sink, which can be tight in older Tampa homes with shallow cabinets or a water filter mounted on the back wall. Cramped cabinets take some planning. Sometimes a compact model is the better fit.

    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Tampa, FL

    An air button on the sink deck or counter turns the disposal on. Pressing the button sends a pulse of air through a small tube to a control box under the sink, so there is no electricity at the button and no need to drill for a wall switch. Islands use them often.

    Standard Disposal Installation in Tampa

    Most households in Tampa do well with a 1/3 to 1/2 horsepower continuous-feed unit. We remove the old unit if there is one, set a new flange in fresh plumber's putty, hang the disposal on a new mount and connect it to the trap with a new discharge tube and gasket. Wiring and testing come last. We listen for a smooth spin and watch the drain clear.

    Disposal Upgrades in Tampa, FL

    Stepping up from an old 1/3 HP unit to a quieter model is a common request. We disconnect and remove the old disposal, check the trap and drain arm for corrosion, and install the new unit with a new mount, so the upgrade does not inherit the old unit's worn parts. Old drain arms often need replacing. Rust works from inside galvanized pipe.

    Same-Day Disposal Installation in Tampa, FL

    When the old disposal dies and the sink is full of water, we can usually install a new one the same day. Our trucks carry common 1/2 and 3/4 HP models, mounting hardware and cord kits, so we do not need a second trip to the supply house before the sink drains again. Figure on roughly an hour.

    Batch-Feed Disposal Setup in Tampa

    The stopper lid acts as the switch on a batch-feed unit. We install the unit, then test the lid in every position to make sure the motor only runs when the lid is fully seated and stops the moment it is lifted out. It is the safety feature. They are popular with families who have small children and people who prefer no open hopper in Tampa homes.
