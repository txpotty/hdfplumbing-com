    Flush Valve Urinal Installation in Tampa, FL

    Flush valve urinals are the commercial standard. Tankless flushometers deliver a set volume from a 3/4 inch line and recover instantly for the next user. We set the volume to the bowl. Wrong settings stain bowls.

    Commercial Urinal Installation in Tampa

    Offices and restaurants want urinals that survive heavy use. Each unit goes on a carrier with new gaskets and stainless bolts, paired with a matched Sloan or Zurn valve. Partitions get spaced to code. Most codes want 15 inches from centerline to the side.

    ADA Urinal Installation in Tampa

    Accessible urinals start at the carrier. We set the carrier so the finished rim lands at 17 inches or lower, keep the clear floor space free of partitions and door swings, and mount the flush handle or sensor where a seated user can reach it. Measurements get checked twice. Inspectors measure too.

    ADA-Compliant Urinal Installation in Tampa

    At least one urinal in a public restroom with more than one usually has to be accessible. That means a rim no higher than 17 inches above the floor, an elongated bowl projecting at least 13-1/2 inches from the wall, and a 30 by 48 inch clear floor space in front. Flush controls must be reachable. We mark it all during rough-in.

    Sloan Flush Valve Installation in Tampa, FL

    Sloan Royal valves are on most commercial urinals we see. We install them with the correct inside parts kit for the bowl's rated volume, set the control stop so the flush is strong without splashing, and keep rebuild kits on the truck for later service. Optima sensor valves fit the same spud. Battery models need no wiring.

    Old Urinal Removal and Replacement in Tampa

    Old urinals come off harder than they went on. Rusted bolts and seized valves often have to be cut, so we protect the tile while working. New gaskets always go in. The flange gets cleaned to bare metal.

    Urinal Upgrades and Water Savings in Tampa, FL

    Older urinals often use 1.5 gallons or more per flush. Replacing them with 0.5 or 0.125 gallon models on the existing carrier, and swapping the flush valve to match, cuts water use sharply in any restroom that sees regular traffic. Ask your water utility about rebates. We provide the model numbers you need.
