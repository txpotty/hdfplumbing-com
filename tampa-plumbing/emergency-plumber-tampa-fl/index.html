    Slab Leak Detection & Repair in Tampa, FL

    Slab leaks can cause serious structural damage and high water bills. Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and crack buried water and sewer lines. Our emergency team uses acoustic leak detection and thermal imaging to pinpoint the exact spot of the leak under your Tampa home's foundation.
