    Cracked Bowl or Tank Replacement in Tampa

    Sometimes only the tank is cracked, and if the model is still in production, a matching tank costs less than a whole new toilet. We check the model number first. A cracked bowl always means a full replacement, since the bowl carries the seal to the floor and cannot be patched.

    One-Piece Toilet Replacement in Tampa, FL

    One-piece toilets have no tank-to-bowl joint, so there are fewer leak points and fewer crevices to clean. They cost more and weigh more.     High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Tampa, FL

    Toilets made before 1994 often use 3.5 gallons a flush, and some from the 1970s use five or more.
