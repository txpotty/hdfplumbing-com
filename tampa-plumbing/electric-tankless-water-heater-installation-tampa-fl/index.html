    HDF Plumbing stands behind our work with a full labor warranty on all electric tankless water heater installations. Additionally, the units themselves come with manufacturer warranties, usually ranging from 5 to 10 years on the heat exchanger. We'll provide full details for your specific unit.

    Many wells in Florida produce water with a sulfur smell or high iron, which often calls for filtration to protect fixtures and appliances. Hard water can lead to mineral buildup (scale) inside electric tankless water heaters, which can reduce efficiency and lifespan. Regular flushing and descaling, often recommended annually, are key maintenance steps to keep your unit running well.

    Sizing an electric tankless water heater correctly is essential and depends on factors like the number of hot water fixtures in your home, your incoming groundwater temperature and your peak hot water demand. Our technicians will perform a careful assessment to recommend the ideal size for your Tampa household.

    Installation requires plumbing and high-amperage electrical work. Professional installation ensures safety and protects your warranty.

    Fluctuating water temperature, error codes and reduced flow are common signs. Hard water scale is a frequent cause.
