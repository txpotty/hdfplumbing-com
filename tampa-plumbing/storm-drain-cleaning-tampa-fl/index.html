    Root Removal from Storm Drains in Tampa

    Tree roots are a common cause of severe storm drain blockages in Tampa, especially in older neighborhoods with mature trees. Roots seek out moisture and can work into drain pipes through tiny cracks or loose joints, growing and eventually completely blocking water flow. HDF Plumbing uses specialized root-cutting attachments on our drain snakes and hydro jetting equipment to cut through and remove root intrusions. After removal, we can suggest further steps to prevent future root problems.

    Storm Drain Cleaning Cost Versus Water Damage in Tampa, FL

Cleaning once a year, ideally before the rainy season, prevents nearly all problems. If a pipe is crushed or disconnected, we can locate it with a camera and repair just that section.     Channel and Trench Drain Cleaning in Tampa

    Channel drains across driveways and garage entries catch water before it reaches the house. Their narrow channels fill quickly with sand, leaves and debris. We remove the grates, clean the channel and jet the outlet pipe. Cracked or loose grates can be replaced while we are there. A clean trench drain keeps water out of your garage during heavy rain.

    Downspout and Roof Drain Lines in Tampa, FL

    Many homes connect downspouts to buried pipe that carries water away from the foundation. These lines clog with roof granules, leaves and nests, then back up and dump water at the wall. We jet them clear and test with a hose to confirm flow at the outlet. For flat roofs with scuppers, we check that the discharge path is open too. Keeping water away from the slab helps protect against soil movement and cracking.
