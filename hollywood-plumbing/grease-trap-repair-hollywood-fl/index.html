    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Trap Repair in Hollywood

    A cable machine punches a hole through grease. A jetter scrubs the whole pipe wall instead.     Grease Trap Drain Line Repair in Hollywood

    Sometimes the trap is not the problem. Lines between the sinks and the trap clog first, because that is where hot water cools.     Damaged Baffle Repair in Hollywood

    Baffles are the walls inside the trap that slow the water down long enough for grease to float and solids to sink. Once one cracks or falls loose, the trap is just a box that grease passes through. We pump it out and pull the old baffle. On plastic units we order the factory part for that model number. Steel ones often need a fabricated baffle or a new trap.

    Flow Control Device Repair in Hollywood

    Hydromechanical traps are rated by flow, like 20 or 50 gallons per minute, and the flow control fitting is what holds the water to that rate. Take it out and a three-compartment sink dump blows grease right past the baffles. Inspectors look for it. The air intake gets cleared too, because the fitting needs air to work.

    Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in Hollywood, FL

    Tees are cheap parts with an outsized job. On the inlet side, the tee directs incoming water down under the floating grease. The outlet tee draws water from low in the tank, well under the grease cap, so if it breaks off or someone removes it during pumping, floating grease walks right out to the sewer. New tees go in as Schedule 40 PVC, cut to the drop length the manufacturer or local code specifies.
