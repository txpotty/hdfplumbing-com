    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Hollywood, FL

    Cold-side leaks leave no warm spot. That makes them harder to spot. They usually show up as a running meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards, which is why we isolate the cold line, trace its route and listen along it with acoustic gear.

    Supply and Drain Leaks Under the Slab in Hollywood, FL

    Supply leaks and drain leaks act differently. Supply leaks are pressurized, so they move the meter, warm the floor on the hot side and run whether or not anyone uses water. Drain leaks only leak when water goes down, showing up as smells, sluggish drains or wet spots near bathrooms. We test both kinds in your Hollywood, FL home.

    Pressure Testing and Isolation in Hollywood

    Pressure isolation is always the first step. Our tech closes the main, caps or valves off the hot and cold sides, and puts a gauge on each, then watches which side loses pressure over a set time. That tells us where to look. It also rules out a toilet or faucet leak masquerading as a slab leak in your Hollywood, FL home.

    Why Locating First Saves Money in Hollywood

    Detection usually costs a few hundred dollars. Guessing wrong costs far more. Breaking up a floor in the wrong place means paying for concrete work, flooring and a second opening, while a precise mark keeps the job to one opening or a reroute that leaves the slab alone in your Hollywood property.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Hollywood

    A reroute abandons the leaking line under the floor. New pipe runs through the walls or attic instead, which avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than opening a finished floor. We usually use PEX because it handles heat and resists corrosion. It suits tile and hardwood homes.

    Direct Access Slab Leak Repair in Hollywood, FL

    One leak on healthy pipe is a good spot-repair candidate. We cut a small opening at the marked spot, dig down to the pipe, replace the bad section and hold a pressure test. Then we backfill and patch the concrete level, ready for flooring. Accurate marks keep that hole small.

    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Hollywood

    Water escaping a pressurized pipe makes a hiss that carries up through concrete. Our ground microphones hear it. We move the pickup across the floor in a grid over the traced pipe route, listening for the spot where the sound peaks and then checking it again from a different angle, which works best on copper and less well on PEX.
