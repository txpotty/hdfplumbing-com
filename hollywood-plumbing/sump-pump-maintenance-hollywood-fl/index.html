    Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Hollywood, FL

    The float switch is a critical component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. A stuck or improperly adjusted float switch can lead to the pump running continuously or failing to activate at all. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to ensure reliable operation for your home in Hollywood, FL.

    Battery Backup Pump Maintenance in Hollywood

    Severe storms often bring power outages along with the rain. A battery backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or fails. We load test the battery, check the charger and alarm and run the backup pump on its own. Most backup batteries need replacement every three to five years.

    Zoeller and Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Hollywood

    HDF Plumbing is proficient in maintaining all major sump pump brands, including popular models from Zoeller and Wayne.     Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Hollywood

    After a major storm, homes on wells or septic should be checked before normal use, since floodwater can contaminate both systems. We test your pump ahead of the rainy season by filling the pit until the float trips. We watch the pump empty the basin, listen for grinding bearings and measure how fast it cycles. If the pump struggles, you still have time to fix it before the first big storm.

    Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Hollywood, FL

    For Wayne sump pump owners in Hollywood, FL, HDF Plumbing offers expert maintenance. Our technicians are familiar with the features of Wayne pumps, including their top-suction design and specific float switch mechanisms.     Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Hollywood, FL

    The check valve stops pumped water from falling back into the pit when the pump shuts off. A failed valve makes the pump run twice as often and wears out the motor. We test and replace check valves and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose joints. We also make sure water exits away from the house so it does not soak into the soil around the slab or foundation.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Maintenance in Hollywood

    HDF Plumbing is experienced in servicing Zoeller sump pumps, a widely trusted brand known for its durability. Our maintenance includes specific checks for Zoeller models, such as inspecting the cast iron housing for cracks, confirming the motor is running smoothly and testing the float switch assembly for correct activation. We stock common replacement parts for quick repairs, keeping your Zoeller pump in peak condition.

    Commercial and Elevator Pit Pumps in Hollywood

    Commercial buildings often have sump pumps in elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms. These pumps may need oil-minder controls or alarms to meet code and insurance requirements. We test duplex systems, alternating controls and high-water alarms on a regular basis. Maintenance logs help property managers stay on top of inspections.
