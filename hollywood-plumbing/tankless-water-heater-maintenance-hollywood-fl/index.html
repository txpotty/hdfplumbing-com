    Step-by-Step Tankless Flushing in Hollywood

    First we shut off the gas and power, then close both isolation valves so the heater is cut off from the house plumbing. The pump pulls solution from a bucket, through the cold port, out the hot port and back again. Once the cycle ends, we rinse with clean water and pull the inlet screen. Then we check for leaks.

    Noritz Descaling in Hollywood, FL

    Noritz exchangers scale up fast on hard water. The descaler is one Noritz approves, and the cycle stays within the manual's time limit so the metal does not etch. While the cycle runs, we check the fan motor and exhaust path.

    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Hollywood

    Picture a bundle of thin tubes with a gas flame under them and cold water racing through at three or four gallons a minute. That is the exchanger. Calcium and magnesium drop out on the hottest tube walls first and narrow the passages. We dissolve it with 45 to 60 minutes of pump-driven descaling solution.

    Emergency Tankless Repair in Hollywood, FL

    No hot water before work is a rough start. Most no-heat calls come down to a failed igniter or an exchanger so scaled that the high-limit switch keeps tripping. The truck carries igniters and descaling gear, so we can usually fix it that day.

    Tankless Vent Inspection in Hollywood

    Gas tankless heaters push exhaust out through a vent that has to stay sealed from the heater all the way to the termination outside the house. Condensing units use PVC or polypropylene vent pipe, pitched back toward the heater so condensate drains. We check every joint and test for carbon monoxide near the unit. Wasp nests in vent caps are common.

    Service Valve Kit Installs in Hollywood, FL

    Plenty of tankless heaters went in without service valves, and without them there is no clean way to hook up a descaling pump. We add a pair of isolation valves with purge ports on the hot and cold lines. The kit usually has a relief valve port too. After that, every flush takes minutes to set up.

    Noritz Tankless Tune-Ups in Hollywood, FL

    Noritz builds a solid heater, but solid does not mean maintenance-free. A Noritz tune-up starts at the exhaust, where we look for soot and blocked terminations. Next comes the combustion air intake and the fan. Last, we check the temperature setting and run a few fixtures at once, which shows whether the unit holds temperature between back-to-back showers.

    What Tankless Maintenance Saves in Hollywood

    Scale on an exchanger acts like insulation. A heavily scaled unit burns more gas per shower than a clean one, and that adds up each month. We lay out the maintenance price next to the likely repair cost so you can decide.
