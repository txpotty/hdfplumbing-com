    How We Install Kitchen Sinks in Hollywood, FL: Style by Style

    Workstation Sink Installation in Hollywood, FL

    Workstation sinks have a ledge for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks, and most are single deep bowls with an offset drain. The trap layout follows the offset drain, which lets the disposal fit and leaves storage room on one side. Check the faucet reach as well.

    Stainless Steel Sink Installation in Hollywood

    Stainless sinks come in gauges from 16 to 22, and a lower gauge means thicker steel. Thin 22 gauge bowls flex and drum when the disposal runs, so we add sound pads or suggest an 18 gauge or heavier sink if you have not bought one yet. Top-mount and undermount plumb differently. We connect the strainers, faucet and disposal either way.

    Disposal and Dishwasher Hookup with Sink Installation in Hollywood, FL

    Most new kitchen sinks get paired with a disposal and a dishwasher. We mount the disposal, run the dishwasher drain through an air gap or high loop and install a dedicated hot supply with its own shutoff. Doing it all at once saves money. Every connection is tested under real use.

    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Hollywood

    An undermount depends on its clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. We mount the bowl, seal the rim with silicone and brace it while the adhesive sets, which usually takes overnight before the sink can hold a full load of water. Strainers and traps go in after.

    Apron-Front Sinks in Hollywood, FL

    Apron sinks need a cabinet cut to fit the front panel, and the bowl must be set before the counter gets measured. We set the sink and frame early, so the fabricator measures from the actual bowl instead of a spec sheet. It prevents a crooked reveal. A cast iron apron sink can weigh 150 pounds or more, so two of us show up.

    Stainless Sinks With Sound Pads in Hollywood

    Budget stainless sinks ring like a drum when dishes hit them or the disposal runs. Better sinks come with rubber pads and an undercoating, and if yours does not, we can add a coat of spray undercoating before it goes in. It is a small upgrade. Once the bowl is mounted, the space underneath is hard to reach, so this is a job worth doing while the sink is still on the floor.

    Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in Hollywood

    Sitting on the counter by their rim, drop-in sinks are a natural fit for tile, laminate and butcher block counters. Usually the fastest upgrade. We check the cutout size, seal the rim and tighten the clips evenly, and in most rentals and older homes the job is done in a couple of hours.
