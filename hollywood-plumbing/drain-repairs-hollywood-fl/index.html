    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Repairs in Hollywood

    Trap and slip-joint repairs typically cost $150 to $350. Pipe sections run higher. A section of drain pipe usually runs $400 to $1,200, and cast iron stack replacement can range from $2,000 to $6,000 with drywall, flooring and access driving most of the spread. We write it up before we start.

    Most trap and fixture drain repairs take one to two hours. Wall work takes longer. Replacing drain pipe inside a wall or under a floor can take half a day to a full day, depending on how much finish material has to come out. We give you a time with the quote.

    A trap swap or a fixture drain fix is usually an hour or two. Bigger jobs run past a day. Replacing a section of main line can take a full day or several days, especially if permits, inspections or excavation under a slab are involved. The schedule is in the quote.

    Age and corrosion lead the list. Soil movement is close behind. Tree roots, temperature swings, heavy loads over buried pipe, plus bad installation such as skipped primer or unsupported runs, can all break a drain early. Most failures have more than one cause.

    Repeat clogs come from something in the pipe catching debris. Grease or scale is common. Poor pipe slope, a missing vent or a cracked fitting with a rough edge catches debris over and over, even after the line is cleared. We find it on video.

    Fixing the cause instead of the clog. Slope matters most. After we correct a sag, replace a rough fitting or add a missing vent, the line stays clear with normal use and a little care about grease. Old metal pipe may need periodic jetting.

    Slow drains, gurgling and sewer smell are the big three warnings. Drips in the cabinet count too. A drain that clogs again a few weeks after it was cleared, or a stain on the ceiling below a tub or shower, means the pipe or a fitting needs repair rather than another cleaning. Catch it early.

    We install PVC and ABS for most new drain work. Cast iron where needed. Cast iron still makes sense in some multi-story walls where noise matters, and we repair existing copper, galvanized and cast iron drains with materials that match local code. Every part meets code.
