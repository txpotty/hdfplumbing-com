    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Repairs in Hollywood

    The cost of drain repairs in Hollywood, FL can vary a lot, typically ranging from $150 for a simple clog removal to over $3,000 for complex main sewer line repairs. Factors like the type of drain, severity of the issue and access to the pipes all influence the final price. We provide a detailed estimate after a full inspection.

    Harsh chemical cleaners can soften older ABS glue joints and corrode metal drain parts with repeated use. They also rarely clear grease or scale fully. A mechanical auger or hydro-jet is safer and more effective.

    Minor clogs can sometimes be resolved with plungers or enzymatic drain cleaners. However, for stubborn blockages, recurring issues or suspected pipe damage, it is best to call a professional. DIY attempts with harsh chemical cleaners can sometimes damage pipes or mask a larger problem.

    You should call a professional if your DIY efforts with a plunger or basic snake haven't cleared the clog, or if you have recurring clogs, foul odors or multiple slow drains. These issues usually point to a deeper problem that requires specialized tools and expertise to resolve effectively.

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful drain cleaning method that uses high-pressure water to clear out stubborn blockages, grease buildup, mineral deposits and even tree roots from your pipes. It is often used for severe clogs or as a preventive measure to thoroughly clean drain lines, leaving them almost like new.

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful drain cleaning method that uses high-pressure water to scour the inside of pipes, thoroughly removing stubborn clogs, grease buildup and even tree roots. It's usually used when traditional snaking isn't enough, or for preventive maintenance on main sewer lines, especially in older homes with buildup issues.

    We work with all common types of drain pipe found in Florida homes, including PVC, ABS, cast iron and copper. Our technicians are skilled in repairing and replacing sections of various pipe materials for compatibility and longevity with your existing plumbing system.

    While chemical drain cleaners are widely available, we usually advise against using them. They often contain harsh chemicals that can damage your pipes, especially older ones, and are typically only a temporary fix. For effective and safe clog removal, professional methods are always the better choice.
