    Electric Element and Thermostat Service in Hollywood

    Electric tanks rely on upper and lower heating elements that get crusted with scale in hard water. We test each element with a meter, check the thermostats and look for scorched wires at the terminal block. If an element has failed we replace it with the matching wattage and voltage. Many families who think they need a new heater just need a lower element and a flush.

    Bradford White & Rheem Tank Maintenance in Hollywood

    We are skilled in maintaining all major hot water tank brands, including popular models from Bradford White and Rheem, commonly found in Hollywood homes. Our technicians are familiar with the specific components and maintenance requirements for these brands, making sure your unit receives brand-specific care. We use appropriate parts and techniques to keep your system running well.

    Pressure Relief Valve Testing in Hollywood

    A properly functioning temperature and pressure relief (T&P) valve is a crucial safety device for any hot water tank. Our technicians will test this valve to confirm it's not stuck or leaking, preventing dangerous pressure buildup within the tank. We verify it opens and closes correctly, and if it fails, we will recommend immediate replacement to keep your Hollywood home safe and compliant with plumbing codes.

    Sediment Flush and Descaling in Hollywood

    Hard water leaves calcium carbonate behind every time the burner heats the tank. Over a year that layer can harden into chunks that insulate the water from the flame and cause loud popping. We connect a hose to the drain valve, flush with full line pressure and agitate the bottom until the water runs clear. When scale is severe we use a descaling solution rated for potable systems. A clean tank heats faster and puts less stress on the bottom seam.

    Attic and Garage Tank Checkups in Hollywood, FL

    Many homes put the water heater in a garage or attic where temperatures can swing hard. Heat speeds up the wear on plastic drain valves, gas valve seals and wiring insulation. We check drain pans, pan drain lines, platform height for gas units in garages and clearances around the tank. A leak in an attic tank without a working pan can destroy a ceiling, so we confirm that protection is in place.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Hollywood, FL

    The anode rod, often called the sacrificial rod, is essential for preventing corrosion inside your hot water tank. Water chemistry varies from one neighborhood to the next, and it changes the rod's lifespan. Our technicians inspect the anode rod for wear and recommend replacement if it's badly corroded, typically every 3 to 5 years. Replacing a worn anode rod is a cost-effective way to protect your tank from rust and leaks, sparing you a full tank replacement.

    Sediment Flushing & Cleaning in Hollywood, FL

    Over time, minerals and sediment settle at the bottom of hot water tanks, reducing efficiency and possibly leading to premature failure. Our maintenance includes a thorough flushing of the tank to remove these deposits. This process helps your tank heat water more efficiently and quietly, a vital step for any hot water tank in Hollywood, FL, where mineral content in the water is a common concern.

    Electric Hot Water Tank Element Check in Hollywood

    Electric hot water tanks rely on heating elements to warm the water. During maintenance, our plumbers inspect these elements for signs of wear, corrosion or burnout. We also check the thermostat settings and wiring connections to ensure they are functioning correctly and safely. This preventive check can identify failing elements before they stop heating altogether, avoiding unexpected cold showers for your family in Hollywood.
