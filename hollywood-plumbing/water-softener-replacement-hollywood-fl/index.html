    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Hollywood

    With proper maintenance, most quality water softeners can last between 10 and 15 years, and sometimes even longer. Factors like water hardness, usage volume and the quality of the original installation can affect its lifespan. Regular servicing helps extend the life of your unit.

    Most softeners last 10 to 15 years. Resin and valves wear out sooner with very hard or chlorinated water.

    Spots on dishes, scale on faucets, dry skin and increased soap use can point to a failing softener. A softener that no longer uses salt or keeps flooding the brine tank may also need replacement.

    In many areas, a plumbing permit is usually not required for a direct like-for-like water softener replacement if no significant changes to the existing plumbing are made. However, if new water lines or drain lines are being installed or relocated, a permit may be necessary. We will advise you on any permitting requirements.

    Requirements vary by city. Simple replacements may not require one, but new plumbing connections may. We check local rules for you.
