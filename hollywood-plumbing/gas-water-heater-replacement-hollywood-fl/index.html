    Forty and fifty gallon gas tanks are the workhorse in most Florida homes, and a leak at the bottom seam means the tank is finished. Newer tanks can run taller than the old one. We check clearance before we order. Plan on about two to three hours.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Replacement in Hollywood

    Atmospheric vent heaters last 10 to 12 years on average, and the end usually shows up as rusty hot water or a slow drip from the bottom.     Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Hollywood, FL

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. A natural gas unit does not go on propane unless the manufacturer allows a conversion. We confirm pressure with a manometer.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Service in Hollywood, FL

    Rheem tanks and their Ruud twins share most parts, which helps when a gas valve fails a few years in. We install both. Setup follows the factory manual, including the draft test.
