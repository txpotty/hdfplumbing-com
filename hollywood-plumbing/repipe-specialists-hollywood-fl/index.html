    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Hollywood

    Slab leaks are a costly and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Nearly all homes in Florida sit on concrete slabs over sandy soil, so leaks under the slab are a common and costly repair. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the damaged pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use modern detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Hollywood

    PEX-A tubing is flexible, resists scale buildup and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open up the full pipe diameter. Long continuous runs mean fewer joints in walls and attics. Most homes are repiped with PEX in two to three days.

    Drain Line Repiping in Hollywood

    While often overlooked, your home's drain lines are just as important as your supply lines. Old cast iron or corroded drain pipes can lead to frequent clogs, slow drainage, and even sewage backups. HDF Plumbing provides comprehensive drain line repiping in Hollywood, replacing failing waste pipes with new, smooth PVC or ABS lines. This ensures efficient waste removal and prevents future drainage problems, protecting your home from foul odors and unsanitary conditions.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Hollywood

    Copper piping offers excellent durability, corrosion resistance, and a long lifespan, making it a premium choice for many homeowners in Hollywood. While the upfront cost is higher than PEX, copper provides superior rigidity and resistance to UV light if exposed. Our team meticulously solders each joint, ensuring a leak-proof and robust plumbing system that can withstand the test of time, including changing temperatures and local water conditions. Copper also inherently inhibits bacterial growth, contributing to cleaner water.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Hollywood, FL

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and very durable piping material that's gaining popularity for repiping projects. HDF Plumbing specializes in PEX pipe replacement in Hollywood, FL, offering a system that resists freezing, corrosion, and scale buildup. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points. PEX also noticeably dampens water hammer noise, providing a quieter plumbing system for your home.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Hollywood

    Kitec, with its orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe and brass fittings, and gray polybutylene pipe are both known for failures. Many homes built in the 1980s through early 2000s contain one or the other. We replace these systems completely so your home is easier to insure and sell. Visible pipe at the water heater often tells us what you have.

    Repiping for Hard Water Areas in Hollywood, FL

    Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving stubborn scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. Water in many areas contains minerals that cause scale buildup inside pipes over time.
