    Repiping Work We Handle in Hollywood, FL

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Hollywood

    Copper is the traditional choice, and Type L copper has a thicker wall than the Type M that went into many older homes. Type L tolerates UV, stays rigid and has decades of field history. It costs more. We sweat joints with lead-free solder or press them, hang the pipe at proper support intervals and test the whole system before patching.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Hollywood, FL

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Flexible PEX soaks up some of the pressure spike behind water hammer, and its long fitting-free runs leave fewer weak points. Attic PEX gets kept away from flues and out of UV.

    Drain Line Repiping in Hollywood

    Drain pipe wears out too, especially old cast iron that cracks and scales up inside. Slow drains in several fixtures, sewer odors and repeated clogs in the same line are common signs of a failing drain system. A camera shows the condition. New PVC or ABS, sloped and vented properly, replaces the bad sections and drains better than the old pipe.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Hollywood

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. We can reroute a single hot or cold branch overhead and abandon the leaking pipe in the slab, which costs far less than a repipe and avoids breaking the floor. This works well for a first leak. If the house has had several leaks, we will walk through why a full repipe usually costs less over time.

    Repiping for Hard Water in Hollywood, FL

    Many wells in Florida produce water with a sulfur smell or high iron, which often calls for treatment to protect fixtures and appliances. Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside pipe, and over years it narrows the bore and speeds up corrosion in metal lines. Pressure suffers. For homes with hard water, we usually recommend PEX-A, which resists scale and does not corrode, and we can add a softener loop or a whole-house filter during the repipe. Adding the loop now costs less than later.

    Slab Leak Repiping in Hollywood

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Nearly all homes in Florida sit on concrete slabs over sandy soil, so leaks under the slab are a frequent and costly repair. Rather than jackhammer to reach one leak, we run new lines through the attic and walls and leave the old under-slab pipe out of service. No jackhammer needed. That stops the current leak and removes the chance of the next one under the same floor.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Hollywood, FL

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside, and over decades the inside diameter closes down to a fraction of what it was. Pressure drops slowly. Rust-colored water in the morning, poor pressure upstairs and dripping threaded joints all point to failing galvanized. New PEX or copper takes its place. Most homeowners notice the pressure difference the first time they turn on the shower.
