    Water Softener Service in Hollywood

    A softener only helps if it regenerates. Salt level, brine tank, injector and a test regeneration all get checked. We test the water afterward. A softener that quietly stopped working lets scale back into every pipe and appliance.

    Scheduled Service for Businesses in Hollywood

    Commercial plumbing takes a beating. We service large water heaters, restroom fixtures, backflow preventers and kitchen drain lines on a set schedule, so failures are caught on a quiet Tuesday morning instead of a busy Friday night. Health inspectors notice. You get records for every visit.

    Gas Connector and Shutoff Checks in Hollywood

    Gas feeds water heaters, furnaces, ranges and dryers. Visible piping and connections get leak tested, shutoffs get turned, and old flex connectors get replaced. Repairs follow local code. If you smell gas, get outside before calling the gas company.

    Backflow Preventer Testing in Hollywood

    Backflow devices protect the drinking water. Many water providers require yearly testing on irrigation and commercial backflow preventers, and our certified testers check each check valve and relief valve and file the results with your utility. We repair failures. Weather protection gets checked too. Late tests can mean fines.

    Sewer and Drain Camera Inspection in Hollywood, FL

    A camera shows what the sewer looks like inside. We look for roots, bellies, cracks and grease buildup in the main and branch drains, which matters most in older homes with clay or cast iron lines that have been in the ground for decades. The recording is yours. We lay out options if something needs work.

    Whole-Home Plumbing Inspection in Hollywood, FL

    Room by room, fixture by fixture. Under-sink checks, toilet valve tests, fixture runs, washer hose inspection and a pressure reading cover the house. Washers and flappers get replaced that day. Bigger items go on the report.

    Sump Pump Testing in Hollywood, FL

    Basements flood when the pump fails during a storm. Hurricanes and tropical storms bring flooding, power loss, and sewer backups to Florida, often swamping drainage systems for days. We pour water into the pit to make sure the float lifts and the pump kicks on, check the discharge line for clogs or freezing risk, and inspect the check valve so water does not drain back in. Battery backups get load tested.

    Drain Camera Checks During Maintenance in Hollywood

    Sewer problems hide underground. The camera finds roots, grease, sags and offsets before they back up into the house. Nearly all homes in Florida sit on concrete slabs over sandy soil, so leaks under the slab are a common and costly repair. We show you the video. Catching a cracked joint early can mean a spot repair instead of a full replacement.
