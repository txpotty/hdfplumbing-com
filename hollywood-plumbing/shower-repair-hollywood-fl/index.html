    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Hollywood

    Showerheads clog from the inside out. Scale builds behind the face plate and in the flow restrictor, so the spray goes uneven, shoots sideways or drops to a trickle even when the valve is wide open. Soaking helps sometimes.     Dripping Showerhead Repair in Hollywood

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. If the head drips with the valve closed, water is slipping past the cartridge, and a new head will not change that. The fix is in the valve. Drips from the arm threads just need fresh tape and a snug turn.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Shower Repair in Hollywood

    Most shower repairs, like a cartridge or diverter, cost $150 to $375. Valve replacements that require opening the wall usually run $450 to $1,200, depending on the valve and how much wall has to come open. Shower pans run higher.

    Cartridge and showerhead swaps usually need no permit. Bigger work like moving pipes or a full remodel may need a permit under local rules. We sort that out first.

    Start with the showerhead. Mineral buildup in the head is the most common cause, followed by a clogged cartridge, a stuck pressure-balance spool or sediment in the water heater outlet. We test each one. If every tap is weak, we check the regulator.

    Mineral scale in the head or the cartridge is the usual cause. A sticking balance valve or a partly closed main can also choke the flow. We check them in order.

    Musty smells and loose tile are the quiet ones. The obvious ones are a dripping head, temperature that will not hold, weak spray and water that stands around your feet long after the shower is off. Any of them is worth a call.

    Moen, Delta, Kohler, American Standard, Pfister and Grohe are most of what we see. Symmons and Sterling show up too, along with older brands whose parts take some hunting. Rare parts may take a day.

    Worn cartridge or seals, almost always. Hard water speeds that wear, which is why drips are so common, and a new cartridge with fresh silicone grease usually stops it for years.

    A drain screen catches most of the hair. Clean the showerhead every few months with a vinegar soak, skip the harsh chemical drain cleaners and fix small drips before they score the cartridge.
