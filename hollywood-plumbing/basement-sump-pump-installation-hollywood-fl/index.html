    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Hollywood

    Outages and heavy rain tend to show up together. Without house power, a battery backup keeps a second pump going, and a fully charged 75 amp-hour battery can run a typical backup pump for several hours of on-and-off cycling, longer on a light night. We test it under load.

    Sump Pit Installation in Hollywood, FL

    Pit size controls how often the pump starts. Too small a pit means the pump short cycles in heavy flow, so we usually install an 18 by 22 inch or 18 by 30 inch basin with a solid bottom and perforated sides wrapped in filter fabric. Fewer starts mean a longer life.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Systems in Hollywood, FL

    Some lots sit in a high water table and fill a standard pit every few minutes during spring. For those, we step up to a 1/2 or 3/4 horsepower unit in a bigger basin, all sized after we time how fast your pit refills. Timing the refill comes first.

    Quiet Submersible Pumps for Finished Basements in Hollywood

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Hollywood, FL

    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Sump Pump Installation in Hollywood

    Replacing a pump in an existing pit usually runs $500 to $1,200 including the pump and check valve. A brand new pit through the slab falls between $1,500 and $3,500, and a battery backup adds roughly $600 to $1,500. The backup is a common add.

    Discharge belongs in the yard, a dry well, or an approved drainage area at least 10 feet from the foundation. Most local codes do not allow sump water in the sanitary sewer, and it can draw a fine.

    Zoeller, Wayne, Little Giant and Liberty Pumps cover most of our installs. We pick the model by horsepower and lift, then by features like a vertical float or a built-in alarm, so the pump fits your pit, your budget and the amount of water your Hollywood basement actually takes on. We default to cast iron.

    Plumbing work in Florida follows the Florida Building Code, whose plumbing volume is based on the International Plumbing Code. New pits, new circuits and new discharge routes are the parts most likely to need a plumbing permit. We check the local rules before the job starts, and when a permit applies we pull it and schedule the inspection ourselves. No trips to the permit office.

    Labor on our sump pump installs is warrantied, typically for one year. Each pump also has a factory warranty, usually one to five years depending on the brand and model, and we write the exact coverage on your invoice so you know which part to call about if something fails. Keep the receipt.

    Once a year is enough for most pumps, ideally before the wet season. We check the float, clean the pit and test the check valve, and we confirm the discharge outlet outside is open, since a clogged outlet makes the pump run hard against a blocked line. The visit is short.

    Grinding or rattling during a cycle is the first warning. Other signs are rust on the housing and rapid cycling, along with a pump that does not start during a heavy rain even though the pit is full to the float. After about ten years, plan on replacing it.

    Permit rules vary by town, including Hollywood. A straight pump replacement usually does not, but a new pit, a new outlet or a new exit through the foundation wall often does, and we sort that out before any concrete gets cut. The paperwork is on us.
