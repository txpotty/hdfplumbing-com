    Copper Pinhole Leak Repair in Hollywood

    Pinholes form when the inner wall of copper pipe erodes or pits. Hot water speeds it up. They often appear in thin Type M pipe and on hot water lines where velocity and temperature are higher, so we cut out the affected section and replace it with Type L copper or a press fitting.     Copper Slab Leak Repair in Hollywood

    Preventive Copper Pipe Inspection in Hollywood, FL

    An inspection catches thin spots before they leak. Plan on an hour.
