    HDF Plumbing replaces well pressure tanks, the steel tank that holds water under air pressure between the pump and your faucets. Inside is a rubber bladder or diaphragm that separates air from water. Once that bladder tears, the tank fills with water, the air cushion disappears, and the pump begins short cycling.     Pressure Switch Replacement in Hollywood, FL

    Switch contacts pit and burn over time. Worn contacts can fail to open or close. When we swap a tank we look at the contacts and the sensing port underneath, since a new tank paired with a tired switch is asking for trouble. Cheap part.

    Large Capacity Tanks for Ranch and Commercial Wells in Hollywood

    Big properties need big drawdown. Stock tanks, irrigation and barns add up. We install large capacity tanks or banks of tanks on a manifold, so heavy-use systems still keep the pump from short cycling. Floor space matters.

    Well Pressure Tank Leak Repair vs. Replacement in Hollywood

    Leaks at the tank tee, a gauge or a fitting can be fixed. A leak from the tank itself cannot. Bladder tanks with a fixed bladder cannot be rebuilt, and a pinhole in the shell means replacement, while a leaking tee or relief valve is a simple part swap we can usually do in under an hour.

    Preventative Well Pressure Tank Maintenance in Hollywood, FL

    Check the air charge once a year. Shut off the pump, drain the system to zero, and put a tire gauge on the valve at the top of the tank, then add air with a small compressor if it has dropped. Water out of the valve means a torn bladder.

    Constant Pressure System Conversion in Hollywood, FL

    Constant pressure systems use a variable speed drive that adjusts pump speed to demand. Pressure stays steady even with two showers running. These systems can run on a smaller tank, though the drive and wiring add to the price. We explain the trade-off.

    Well-X-Trol Pressure Tank Installation in Hollywood, FL

    Amtrol's Well-X-Trol line uses a diaphragm design. It is one of the most common tanks we install. Its stainless or polypropylene water connection keeps water off the steel shell, limiting internal corrosion, and the factory pre-charge still gets checked and adjusted to your switch on install.

    Flex-Lite Pressure Tank Replacement in Hollywood

    Flex-Lite tanks have a composite shell. They will not rust. That makes them a good pick for damp basements, crawlspaces and well houses where steel tanks tend to corrode from the bottom up, and they are lighter to carry into tight spaces. Same plumbing as steel.

    Bladder Pressure Tank Replacement in Hollywood

    Rapid clicking at the switch is the classic sign. That fast on-off pattern means the tank has no usable air, and the pump is now taking every hit of demand directly, so each glass of water starts the motor. We swap the tank and the cycling stops.
