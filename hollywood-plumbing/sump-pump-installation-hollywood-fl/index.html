    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Hollywood

    A primary sump pump is your first defense against basement flooding. We install sturdy, electrically powered pumps designed to turn on automatically when water levels rise in the sump pit. Our plumbers will assess your basement's water intrusion risks, considering local water tables and soil types in Hollywood, to recommend the right pump size and horsepower for effective water removal.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Hollywood

    If your basement or crawl space has no pit, we can build one. We cut the slab, excavate through soil, compacted clay or rock and set a durable basin. We add gravel around the basin to help water flow in. Then we patch the concrete and install the pump and cover. A new pit gives you a dependable place to collect and remove water.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost in Hollywood, FL

    A pump replacement in an existing pit usually costs much less than a new pit installation. New pits require slab cutting, excavation and concrete patching, which adds labor. Battery backups and alarms add to the cost but provide extra protection. We give you a written quote that explains each option. That way you can choose the right level of protection for your budget.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Hollywood, FL

    Power outages during heavy storms are a serious concern, which is why a battery backup sump pump is a smart addition. This system takes over on its own if your primary pump loses power, providing continuous flood protection. We install reliable backup units that can operate for hours, giving you peace of mind even when the electricity goes out in Hollywood.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Hollywood

    Some homes collect water in sunken patios, below-grade driveways or window wells rather than a basement. We install exterior sump basins with pumps that move storm water to a safe discharge point. These systems are useful for lots that slope toward the house. We route discharge lines so water drains away from the foundation and neighboring lots. This helps prevent siding damage and foundation problems.

    Sump Pump Replacement in Hollywood, FL

    Sump pumps often sit idle for long stretches between storms, which can lead to stuck floats and seized motors. We replace old pumps with new models and inspect the pit, check valve and discharge line. Upgrading to a higher capacity pump may help if your old one could not keep up. We test the new pump by filling the pit with water. Replacement before storm season is a smart move.
