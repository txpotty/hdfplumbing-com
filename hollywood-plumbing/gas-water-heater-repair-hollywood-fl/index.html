    Restoring Hot Water for Older Homes in Hollywood

    Many homes in Hollywood have older gas water heater models that require specialized knowledge for repair.     Gas Control Valve Replacement in Hollywood, FL

    The gas control valve controls temperature and shuts off gas if the pilot goes out. Newer valves from Honeywell and White-Rodgers use status lights that blink codes when something fails. If the valve itself has failed, we replace it with an exact match or approved universal model. We leak test and confirm temperature control afterward.

    Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Hollywood

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust out through PVC pipe. Failures often involve the blower motor, pressure switch, hot surface igniter or condensate in the vent. We read the error codes, test each part and repair the failure. Correct vent slope and termination are checked before we leave.

    Pilot Light and Burner Issues in Hollywood

    A common problem with gas water heaters is a pilot light that won't stay lit or a burner that fails to ignite. This can be caused by a faulty thermocouple, a dirty pilot tube or issues with the gas control valve. Our technicians in Hollywood are skilled at diagnosing these components and performing the needed repairs, whether it is cleaning, adjusting or replacing parts. We confirm proper gas flow and ignition for consistent hot water.

    Venting and Draft Repair in Hollywood, FL

    Atmospheric gas water heaters depend on a properly sloped metal flue to carry exhaust out of the home. Disconnected sections, rusted draft hoods and poor slope can let combustion gases spill back. We repair or replace vent sections and confirm draft with a test. Proper venting is critical for your family's safety.

    Water Heater Tank Leaks and Pressure Issues in Hollywood, FL

    While gas water heaters are sturdy, tank leaks or pressure problems can occur. We can often fix small fitting leaks or replace relief valves if pressure is too high. If the tank itself is failing due to rust or old age, a replacement might be the more economical long-term solution. We will provide an honest assessment of the repair options for your Hollywood, FL home.

    Pilot Light Repair in Hollywood

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most common gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and clean or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are completed in about an hour.

    Gas Water Heater Repair Cost in Hollywood, FL

    Thermocouple and pilot repairs usually cost $150 to $350. Gas control valve replacement often runs $350 to $700, and power vent blower repairs can range from $400 to $800. A new gas tank installed usually costs $1,600 to $3,000 depending on size and venting. We help you decide based on tank age and condition.
