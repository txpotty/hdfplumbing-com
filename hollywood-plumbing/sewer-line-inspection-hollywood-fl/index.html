    Camera Inspection for Blockages in Hollywood

    A drain that slows and then stops usually has something sitting in the line. Our camera tells us what.     Root Intrusion Inspection in Hollywood, FL

    Roots from shade trees, ornamental trees and thirsty shrubs go after the moisture at sewer joints, and once a hair-thin root gets through a gap it can thicken into a mat that fills the pipe. Over time they fill the line. We see on video where they enter and how much of the line they fill. Next comes a plan: cutting, jetting or a liner.

    Sewer Line Locating and Depth Marking in Hollywood, FL

    Before any repair, you need to know where the pipe runs. Depth matters too. Our camera carries a sonde transmitter that we trace from above ground with a receiver, then we flag or paint the route and problem spots so the dig or liner crew starts in the exact right place.

    Sewer Scopes for Slow Drains in Hollywood

    Drains that stay slow after every cleaning often point to something a snake cannot reach. Bellies are common culprits. We see on video if it is a blockage, a belly or a partial collapse, which saves you from paying for cleanings that never stick.

    Clay and Cast Iron Line Inspections in Hollywood, FL

    Clay and cast iron fail in their own ways. Clay cracks and separates at joints, letting roots in, while cast iron rusts, builds scale and wears a channel along the bottom of the pipe. We scope both regularly. We tell you honestly how many years the line likely has.

    Root Intrusion Detection in Hollywood, FL

    Roots find their way into sewer joints wherever mature trees grow near the line, and they grow fast once they reach water. Clay joints are the usual entry. Our HD camera shows the root mass and where it enters, while the sonde lets us mark the spot above ground so you know which tree to watch.
