    Main Sewer Line Hydrojetting in Hialeah

    The main sewer carries every drain in the house, so a blockage there backs up the lowest fixture first. Jetting the main clears roots, grease and sludge all the way to the city connection.     Grease Trap Line Cleaning in Hialeah, FL

    Restaurant kitchen lines send warm grease downstream where it cools and sticks. Much of it coats the pipe before it ever reaches the interceptor. Short jetting passes keep loosened grease from plugging the line downstream. Quarterly works for most.

    Chemical-Free Drain Cleaning in Hialeah, FL

    Hydrojetting uses only water, so nothing caustic goes into your pipes or septic tank. Chemical cleaners can damage pipe. Jetting removes the buildup physically, and the debris flushes to the sewer or septic tank as normal waste, so there is nothing left in the pipe to eat at joints or harm the bacteria a septic system depends on.

    Storm and Yard Drain Jetting in Hialeah, FL
