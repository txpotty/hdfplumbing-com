    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Hialeah

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. A high water table across much of Florida affects drainage, septic design, and how deeply crews can dig for underground repairs. We replace corroded sections with polyethylene pipe laid with tracer wire, using an approved transition riser where the line comes up out of the ground at the house. Above-ground runs stay in steel. Everything is tested before the trench is backfilled, so a bad joint gets caught while it is still easy to reach.

    Interior Black Iron Pipe Repair in Hialeah

    Threaded black iron pipe can leak at joints after years of vibration or settling. Our plumbers reseal or replace the leaking fittings and sections using new pipe and approved sealant. Rusty runs get swapped out. Heavily rusted pipe in garages, crawlspaces or outside walls is replaced before it fails, and we pressure test the full system afterward.

    Propane Gas Line Service in Hialeah, FL

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. Our work covers propane yard lines, regulator replacement and fitting repairs, plus a check that every appliance has its propane orifices installed. Wrong orifices make a burner run dirty and unsafe. The test procedure is the same as for natural gas.

    Natural Gas Leak Detection and Repair in Hialeah
