    Tile Shower Replacement in Hialeah, FL

    Grout cracks and failed waterproofing are how tile showers die. Our job is the valve, drain and pan, and we coordinate with the tile installer on the waterproofing membrane. Schluter Kerdi is a common choice. It goes on over the backer board.

    Leaking pans soak the subfloor and the ceiling below. Whether it is a prefab base with a crack or a tiled pan with a failed liner, we tear it out, replace any rotted subfloor and install a new pan that gets flood-tested for a full day. We replace the drain as well. It ends the cycle.

    Emergency Shower Leak Replacement in Hialeah

    A sudden leak from a shower can soak a ceiling in an hour.     Custom Tile Shower Replacement in Hialeah, FL

    Custom tile replacement starts with careful demolition. Demo goes to the studs, damaged wood gets replaced and a new waterproofed pan goes in with the valve set to tile depth. The slope has to be right. We flood-test before tile.
