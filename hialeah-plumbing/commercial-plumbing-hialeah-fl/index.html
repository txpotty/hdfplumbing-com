    Grease Trap and Interceptor Repair in Hialeah, FL

    Food service sites need a grease trap or interceptor to keep fats, oils and grease out of the public sewer, and pretreatment inspectors check them. Odor is the first warning. We repair broken baffles, replace cracked lids and outlet tees, jet the inlet lines and confirm the unit is sized for your fixture count and flow rate.     Commercial Water Heater Service in Hialeah, FL

    Big tank heaters fail in a few common ways: a failed gas valve, a cracked flue baffle, a stuck T and P valve or a tank full of scale. Each sounds different. We check draft and combustion, test water temperature at the far end of the building and flush the tank, then tell you whether repair or replacement makes more sense. Tankless units get a descaling flush. Recirculation pumps on long runs get tested as part of the visit.

    Backflow Assembly Testing and Rebuilds in Hialeah

    Most water providers require annual tests on commercial backflow assemblies, and many send late notices that come with fines. Test early. Our testers handle RPZ, double check and PVB devices with annually calibrated gauges and submit results the way your provider wants them. If a device fails, we usually rebuild it the same day.

    Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Hialeah

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and crack buried water and sewer lines. Meter and pressure tests confirm it, and acoustic gear plus thermal imaging narrow the spot down to a foot or so. Then we decide. Some leaks are best fixed with a small cut through the slab, while others are cheaper to abandon and reroute overhead. You get a price on each option.

    Backflow Prevention Assembly Service in Hialeah

    A backflow assembly keeps water from your building, including irrigation lines, boiler water and chemical feeds, from flowing back into the public supply. Most providers want a yearly test. Our certified testers check reduced pressure zone assemblies, double check valves and pressure vacuum breakers with calibrated differential gauges, then file the report with your water provider. Failed devices usually need a rebuild kit, not a new valve. We carry Watts, Wilkins and Febco kits for that reason.

    Commercial Pipe Repair and Repiping in Hialeah, FL

    Older commercial buildings often have galvanized supply lines that are rusted nearly shut, or copper that has pinholed along hot water runs. Patching works for a while. Once leaks start showing up in different spots every few months, a sectional repipe in Type L copper or commercial-grade PEX usually costs less than the next three emergency calls. We map the system first. Then we phase the work by floor or by tenant so the building stays open.

    Commercial Sewer Line Repair in Hialeah, FL
