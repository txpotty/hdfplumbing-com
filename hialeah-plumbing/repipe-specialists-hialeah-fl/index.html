    Copper Repiping in Hialeah, FL

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes. Joints are soldered with lead-free solder or pressed with ProPress fittings. Copper repipes usually cost more and take a bit longer than PEX.

    Efficient PEX Repiping in Hialeah, FL

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and very popular choice for repiping projects in Hialeah, FL. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points and making installation quicker and less intrusive than traditional rigid pipes. PEX is also very resistant to freezing, scaling, and chlorine, common issues in many water systems. We use quality PEX-A or PEX-B tubing, installed with durable crimp or expansion fittings, to create a reliable and efficient plumbing network throughout your home.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Hialeah

    Copper piping offers exceptional durability, corrosion resistance, and a long lifespan, making it a premium choice for many homeowners in Hialeah. While the upfront cost is higher than PEX, copper provides superior rigidity and resistance to UV light if exposed. Our team meticulously solders each joint, ensuring a leak-proof and robust plumbing system that can withstand the test of time, including changing temperatures and local water conditions. Copper also inherently inhibits bacterial growth, contributing to cleaner water.

    Condo, Townhome and Multi-Family Repipes in Hialeah

    Shared walls and HOA rules make repiping attached homes more complex. We coordinate with HOAs, schedule water shutoffs and protect neighboring units.     Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Hialeah, FL

    Many older homes in Hialeah, FL still have galvanized steel pipes, which are prone to corrosion, rust, and mineral buildup. This leads to low water pressure, discolored water, and eventual leaks. Our team specializes in the complete removal and replacement of these outdated systems with modern PEX or copper piping.     PEX-A tubing is flexible, resists scale buildup and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water. Most homes are repiped with PEX in two to three days.
