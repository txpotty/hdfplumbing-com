    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Hialeah

    Slab leaks, where water lines running beneath your home's concrete foundation leak, are a especially challenging issue. Newer homes across Florida mostly use PEX or CPVC with PVC drains, though copper pinhole leaks are still a common repair in older homes. These leaks can undermine your foundation, leading to major structural damage. We use specialized listening devices and camera inspections to pinpoint slab leaks precisely and offer repair options including direct slab penetration, re-routing, or pipe lining. Addressing a slab leak promptly in Hialeah is crucial to prevent extensive, costly foundation damage.

    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Hialeah, FL

    Leaks often start at the compression fittings and couplings right next to the meter box. Shifting soil and heavy traffic over the box can work loose those joints over time. We determine whether the leak is on the utility side or the customer side, since the water provider is responsible for the meter and the pipe before it. When the leak is yours, we replace the fittings and the customer shutoff valve on the same trip.

    A single leak on a newer copper or PEX line is usually a quick spot repair. Repeat leaks, galvanized pipe or a line older than about 40 years usually justify full replacement.
