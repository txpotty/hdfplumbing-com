    Most gas water heater repairs cost $150 to $700. Thermocouple and pilot work is at the low end, while gas control valves and power vent components are at the higher end. We quote before starting.

    Because of the dangers that come with natural gas and possible carbon monoxide poisoning, gas water heater repairs should always be handled by a licensed professional. DIY attempts can be dangerous and may void warranties or break local Hialeah, FL plumbing codes.

    A thermocouple is a safety device in a gas water heater that senses if the pilot light is lit. If it doesn't sense heat, it shuts off the gas supply to prevent gas leaks. A faulty thermocouple is a leading reason for a pilot light not staying lit and is a critical safety component.

    Generally, minor repairs like replacing a thermocouple or a thermostat do not require a permit. If the repair involves significant changes to the gas line or venting system, or if the unit needs to be replaced, a permit from the Hialeah building department might be necessary.
