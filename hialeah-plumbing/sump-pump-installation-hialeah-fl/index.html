    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Hialeah

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi monitors go further and send a phone alert when the pump runs too long, the power drops or the water climbs past a set level. Rentals need these. We add them to new installs and to pumps already in place.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Hialeah, FL

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get. We install their M53 and M98 models often. Backup and combo units are available too. We help you pick the model that fits the pit.

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Hialeah, FL

    Two pumps on one riser cover both kinds of failure, a dead motor and a dead outlet. The primary handles daily water. Finished basements are the usual reason. They are worth it anywhere a few inches of water would mean new drywall.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Hialeah

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. Because everything comes assembled on a single riser, they fit into an 18-inch pit that would be too tight for two separate pumps and two check valves. Install time is shorter too. They suit homes that want backup protection but do not want to enlarge the pit.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Hialeah, FL

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. A battery backup pump sits beside the primary and runs on its own float. Most run five to seven hours on a full charge. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Hialeah, FL

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. A smart charger keeps the battery topped off and sounds an alarm if the battery weakens, the primary fails or the water rises past the backup float. Expect a battery swap every four to five years. Finished basements get the most out of them.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Hialeah, FL

    Liberty builds quiet, well-made submersible pumps with good switch options. Their 250 and 280 series cover most residential lifts, and the vertical float version fits narrow pits where a wide tethered float would rub. Parts are easy to find. We drill a 3/16 inch weep hole in the discharge to stop air lock.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Hialeah

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go. We install exterior basins with pumps for those spots. Lots that slope toward the house need this most. The outlet sits well clear of the foundation and the property line.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Hialeah

    Most Hialeah, FL sump pump jobs land between $600 and $1,800. That range covers a quality pump, a new check valve, the discharge fittings and the labor to set and test it in a pit that already exists. New pits cost more. Every line item is on the quote.

    Most standard installs take three to six hours. A new pit can stretch it to a full day. A simple swap is quicker.

    A straight replacement often needs no permit. We let you know if your job needs one, usually only for new pits or wiring.
