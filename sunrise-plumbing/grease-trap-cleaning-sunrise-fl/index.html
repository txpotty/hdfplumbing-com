    Why Choose HDF Plumbing for Grease Trap Cleaning in Sunrise, FL?

    Skipped pump-outs usually announce themselves at the floor drain first, with gray water pooling around the grate after the dish machine drains. Then comes the smell. A rancid, sour odor means the grease cap has started to break down and give off hydrogen sulfide. The same gas corrodes concrete, which shortens the life of an in-ground interceptor.

    • Odor Control, A pumped trap stops the rotten-egg odor that decaying grease gives off.

    • Prevent Backups, Pumping on schedule keeps grease from slipping past the outlet and setting up in the line.

    Residential Grease Trap Cleaning in Sunrise, FL

    Homes rarely have a grease trap, though a few large custom kitchens and some home-based catering setups in Sunrise, FL put a small unit under the sink. These are usually 10 to 20 gallon units.     Under-Sink Grease Trap Maintenance in Sunrise, FL

    Under-sink hydromechanical traps are rated by flow, usually 10 to 50 gallons per minute, and they hold far less than people expect. In a busy kitchen, that means cleaning every week or two. The service covers grease and solids removal, a baffle scrape, a look at the inlet flow control fitting, and a new lid gasket if the old one is flattened. A bad gasket is why most of these smell.

    Grease Trap Hydro-Jetting in Sunrise, FL

    Some traps have years of grease baked onto the walls and inlet piping. That crust survives a normal pump-out. Jetting the empty tank and its inlet line strips it back to bare walls and gets back the capacity that crust was taking. Plan on about an extra hour.

    Large Outdoor Grease Interceptor Cleaning in Sunrise

    Gravity interceptors in the parking lot often hold 750 to 2,000 gallons across two or three compartments. Each compartment gets pumped separately. On big tanks we break the grease cap with a pressure wand first, since a cap that has set up hard will plug the suction hose and leave half the grease behind. No one goes inside without confined-space gear.

    Preventive Maintenance Programs in Sunrise, FL

    A maintenance plan is just a pumping interval plus someone who tracks it. Your manager does not have to remember it, because we track the dates. If the sludge judge shows the trap hitting 20 percent between visits, we pull the next date in, and if it reads 10 percent, we push it out and save you a pump-out. Adjustments happen at every visit.

    Commercial Grease Trap Cleaning in Sunrise

    Restaurants and hospital kitchens in Sunrise run the same basic setup, a trap or interceptor sitting between the sinks and the sewer, sized by flow rate or by gallons. We pump it empty and scrape it. Before the lid goes back on, we look over both tees and the baffle wall for cracks or missing pieces.     Grease Trap Inspection and Repair in Sunrise

    An empty trap is the only time anyone can see what is wrong with it. We look for cracked baffles and missing outlet tees. A missing outlet tee lets the floating grease cap pour straight into the sewer, and it is one of the most common failures we find on older concrete interceptors. Small fixes can often happen the same visit.

The originals stay on site.
