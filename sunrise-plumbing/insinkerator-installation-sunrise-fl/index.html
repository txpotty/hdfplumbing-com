    Upgrading Your InSinkErator in Sunrise, FL

    Moving up from a 1/3 HP builder unit to a 3/4 HP Evolution changes more than the grind. Expect the new unit to sit lower in the cabinet. Because of that height, the discharge can end up below the trap arm, and we measure first so we can lower the trap if needed. We also confirm the circuit can carry the larger motor.

    Dishwasher Connection and Air Gap Setup in Sunrise

    Nearly every InSinkErator has a dishwasher inlet with a factory knockout plug inside. We drive the plug out and fish it out of the chamber. Then the dishwasher hose goes on with a proper clamp, routed through an air gap or up into a high loop under the counter so sink water cannot siphon back into the dishwasher tub. Both appliances get run together.

    Disposal Installs for New Sinks in Sunrise, FL

    A new sink changes the drain math. Undermount and farmhouse bowls are often deeper than the old drop-in. With the bowl lower, the disposal drops too, and the discharge can land below the old trap arm, which means lowering the stub. Better to sort that out mid-remodel than after the tile is up.

    Batch Feed vs Continuous Feed in Sunrise

    Continuous feed is the type most kitchens have. Flip the switch and it runs until you flip it off. A batch feed unit needs its stopper locked in to run, keeping hands out of the chamber, and it needs no wall switch. We wire each one to match.
