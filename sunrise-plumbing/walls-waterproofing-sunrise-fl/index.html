    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Sunrise, FL

    Interior drain tile handles water that already got through the wall. We cut a channel in the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and tie it into a sump pit so the pump can push the water outside. The wall still gets wet. The difference is that the water no longer ends up on your floor. This route costs less than digging outside and makes sense when a patio or driveway sits against the foundation.

    Waterproofing Costs vs. Repeat Water Damage in Sunrise, FL

    Paying once beats paying every two years. Waterproofing a shower wall usually lands in the low thousands, while replacing drywall, cabinets and flooring again and again costs far more, and mold remediation on top of that can double the bill. The price for exterior block sealing depends on how much wall there is. We write up each option with its price so you can compare.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Sunrise

    Newer homes across Florida mostly use PEX or CPVC with PVC drains, though copper pinhole leaks are still a frequent repair in older homes. Any wall below grade sits in soil that gets saturated after heavy rain or snowmelt, and that water pushes against the wall with steady hydrostatic pressure. We inject cracks, coat the wall inside or out, and install drain tile where water pools along the footing. A sump goes in if groundwater sits high. Not every basement needs one.

    Commercial Walls Waterproofing in Sunrise

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and mechanical rooms in Sunrise take on water the same way houses do, just with more at stake. A wet electrical room can shut a business down. We schedule around operating hours, work with property managers on access, and meet the waterproofing specs in the building plans for below-grade walls. Downtime stays short.

    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Sunrise, FL

    A sump pump is the part of an interior system that actually moves the water. We set a lidded basin below slab level, install a cast iron pump sized for the inflow, and add a battery backup or water-powered backup for the night the power goes out during a storm. Check valves matter. A missing check valve lets the discharge line drain back into the pit and the pump short-cycles. The discharge line gets routed at least 10 feet from the house.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Sunrise

    Tile and grout are not waterproof. Water passes through grout every time you shower, so the layer behind the tile, cement board with a membrane or a foam board system, is what actually keeps the studs dry. We tear out failed tile, install new backer, and apply Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban at the thickness the manufacturer calls for. Corners, niches and valve holes get extra fabric or preformed pieces.

    Cost-Effective Waterproofing Solutions in Sunrise, FL

    Not every wet wall needs a $15,000 excavation. Sometimes the fix is a downspout extension and regrading the soil so it slopes away from the house, a drop of about six inches in the first ten feet, and that costs a few hundred dollars. We start with the cheapest fix that will actually work. You get the options in writing, side by side.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Sunrise

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. It is the most reliable fix for a leaking basement, because the water never touches the block or concrete. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing so water has somewhere to go. Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and crack buried water and sewer lines. We backfill after the drain is tested.
