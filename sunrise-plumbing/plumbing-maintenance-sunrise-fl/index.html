    Softener Cleaning and Testing in Sunrise

    Hard water scale builds in pipes and appliances fast. We check the brine level, clean the injector screen, inspect the resin bed for fouling and confirm the regeneration cycle starts and finishes, then test the treated water hardness at a faucet. Fouled resin can be cleaned. Worn resin gets swapped.

    Water Softener Service in Sunrise

    A softener only helps if it regenerates. We check the salt level, break up any salt bridge in the brine tank, clean the brine well and injector, and run a manual regeneration to confirm the valve cycles correctly. Hardness gets tested after. A softener that quietly stopped working lets scale back into every pipe and appliance.

    Water Heater Maintenance in Sunrise

    Sediment is the tank's slow killer. Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving heavy scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. We drain and flush the tank, pull and check the anode rod, test the temperature and pressure relief valve, and look over the gas or electric connections for corrosion and wear. Flushing takes about an hour. A clean tank heats faster and lasts years longer.

    Hot Water Tank Service in Sunrise, FL

    Gas and electric tanks need different checks. Gas tanks get a burner, flue and leak check, and electric tanks get element and thermostat tests. Sediment comes out either way. Mineral buildup on the bottom wastes fuel and can lead to early tank failure.

    Softener Upkeep in Sunrise, FL

    Softeners fail quietly. A unit that stopped regenerating or ran out of salt keeps sending water to the house, it just is not soft anymore, and you will not notice until scale shows up on the faucets and the water heater starts popping. Bypass and drain get tested. We measure hardness after.

    Repipe Assessments in Sunrise, FL

    Polybutylene and old galvanized pipe leak more. During maintenance we identify your supply pipe material, look for pinhole leaks, rust stains and pressure drops, and tell you honestly whether repiping is something to plan for now or years from now. PEX and copper are the options. Work can be phased.

    Hidden Leak Checks in Sunrise, FL

Newer homes across Florida mostly use PEX or CPVC with PVC drains, though copper pinhole leaks are still a frequent repair in older homes. Catching it early limits damage.
