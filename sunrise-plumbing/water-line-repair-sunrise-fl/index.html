    Water Pressure Restoration in Sunrise

    Low water pressure can be a frustrating symptom of an underlying water line problem, such as blockages, leaks, or a failing pressure regulator. In Sunrise, if your water pressure has dropped suddenly, it's crucial to have your main water line inspected.     Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Sunrise

    Many water line problems are made worse by high incoming pressure. A failed pressure reducing valve can push well over 80 psi into your plumbing, which stresses the service line and every fixture. We test static pressure with a gauge and replace worn regulators from brands like Watts and Zurn Wilkins. We also swap seized gate valves for full-port ball valves so you can shut the water off quickly in an emergency.

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Sunrise, FL

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving heavy scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which restricts flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe seldom holds up because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and stops repeat leaks.
