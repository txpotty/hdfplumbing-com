    Frequently Asked Questions About Clogged Drain Repair in Sunrise

    Clearing a single sink, tub or shower drain typically costs $125 to $300. Harder clogs that need a larger machine or access through a roof vent run $250 to $450. Hydro jetting a heavily scaled or greasy line typically costs $350 to $800.

    Most single fixture clogs are cleared in 30 to 90 minutes. Lines with heavy buildup or limited access can take longer. We tell you if the job will run beyond the usual time.

    Caustic drain cleaners can damage ABS fittings, corrode older metal pipes and sit in the trap if they do not clear the clog. They also create a hazard for anyone working on the drain afterward. Mechanical clearing is safer and more reliable.

    We rely on professional equipment from trusted brands like Spartan Tool, RIDGID, and Gorlitz. These tools are known for their reliability and effectiveness in diagnosing and clearing various types of drain clogs, ensuring a high standard of service.

    Hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to clear stubborn blockages and completely clean the inside of your pipes. It's often necessary for severe clogs caused by tree roots, heavy grease buildup, or heavy mineral deposits from hard water, which traditional snaking might not fully clear.

    You should call a professional plumber for clogged drain repair if DIY methods like plungers are ineffective, if you experience multiple clogged drains, or if you suspect a main sewer line blockage. Recurring clogs or slow drains also indicate a need for professional diagnosis to prevent bigger issues.

    While chemical drain cleaners are widely available, we generally advise against them. They often contain harsh chemicals that can corrode pipes, especially older ones, and can be hazardous to your health and the environment. Professional plumbers use safer, more effective methods.

    Most standard clogged drain repairs, such as those in a sink or bathtub, can be completed within 1 to 2 hours. More complex issues, like main sewer line clogs or those requiring camera inspection and hydro-jetting, might take 3 to 4 hours or longer, depending on the difficulty.
