    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Replacement in Sunrise

    Well pump replacement usually costs $1,800 to $4,500 depending on well depth and pump size. Deep wells or constant pressure systems can cost $4,000 to $8,000.

    It is often a good idea to consider replacing your pressure tank at the same time as your well pump, especially if both are of similar age. A new pressure tank can improve the efficiency and lifespan of your new pump by reducing cycling. It also cuts labor costs to do both jobs at once.

    Most well pump replacements are finished in one day. Deep wells or complex systems may take longer.

    Manufacturer warranties typically range from 1 to 5 years. We also provide a labor warranty.

    Permit requirements vary by county. We follow local requirements for all well work.

    In many areas, a plumbing or well permit is required for well pump replacement to confirm compliance with local building and health codes. HDF Plumbing handles all required permitting on your behalf, so your installation is fully compliant and safe.

    Common signs of a failing well pump include no water, sputtering faucets, reduced water pressure, discolored water or surprisingly high electricity bills. If your pump runs constantly or cycles on and off too frequently, these are also signs that replacement may be necessary.

    The lifespan of a well pump varies greatly depending on usage, water quality and pump type, but most last between 10 and 20 years. Regular maintenance and proper sizing can extend its life, but eventually wear and tear will call for replacement.
