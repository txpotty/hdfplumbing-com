    Gas Line Installation for New Appliances in Sunrise, FL

    Water heaters, ranges, dryers, fireplaces and pool heaters all pull from the same gas service.     Water Softener Installation in Sunrise, FL

    Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving stubborn scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. Scale shows up first as white crust on faucet aerators and a rumbling water heater. A hardness test at the kitchen tap, in grains per gallon, tells us whether a salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner fits your usage. The bypass valve is not optional. It lets you shut the unit off for service without losing water to the house.

    Backflow Assembly Testing and Installation in Sunrise

    Fire lines and irrigation systems account for most of the backflow assemblies we install. A certified tester checks each assembly on install day and the results go to the water provider. That starts the annual clock. Most jurisdictions want a retest every 12 months, and we can put you on a reminder list so it does not slip.

    PEX Repiping in Sunrise

    Copper repipes cost more and take longer, which is why most whole-house repipes today use PEX-A or PEX-B tubing with brass or poly fittings. It runs quieter than copper, with less water hammer banging in the walls. It also handles a freeze better. When a rare freeze is forecast, covering outdoor valves and letting a faucet drip is usually enough to prevent damage. We use fittings matched to the tubing brand.

    Water Heater Installation in Sunrise

    Tank or tankless, gas or electric, the first question is how many people shower in the morning. We size the heater to that peak demand and your fuel source, then check the venting and gas supply before quoting. Gas heaters need the closest look. Bad flue pitch or a spilling draft hood can send carbon monoxide into the living space. We haul the old tank away the same day.

    Natural Gas and Propane Lines in Sunrise, FL

    Natural gas and propane run at different pressures, and the pipe sizing changes with them. We size every run from the appliance BTU ratings and total pipe length, and use black iron or CSST depending on the path. CSST must be bonded. Without that bond, a lightning strike nearby can arc through the tubing and start a fire.

    Expansion Tank and Sewage Ejector Installs in Sunrise, FL

    A closed water system has nowhere for heated water to expand, so pressure climbs every single time the heater cycles. An expansion tank absorbs that swing and keeps the relief valve from dripping. A PRV usually means you need one. Fixtures below the sewer line get an ejector system with a sealed basin, a check valve and a vent.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Sunrise, FL

    A basement bathroom sits below the sewer line, so gravity cannot carry the waste out. The fix is a sealed basin with an ejector pump that lifts waste to the main. Pump and basin are sized to the fixtures, the basin is vented to code, and the discharge gets a check valve and a shutoff so the pump can come out for service. We usually install Liberty or Zoeller pumps.
