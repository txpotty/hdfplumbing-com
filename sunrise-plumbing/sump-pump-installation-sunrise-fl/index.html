    Sump Pump Replacement in Sunrise, FL

    Sump pumps often sit idle for long stretches between storms, which can lead to stuck floats and seized motors. We replace old pumps with new models and inspect the pit, check valve and discharge line. Upgrading to a higher capacity pump may help if your old one could not keep up. We test the new pump by filling the pit with water. Replacement before the rainy season is a smart move.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost in Sunrise, FL

    A pump replacement in an existing pit usually costs much less than a new pit installation. New pits require slab cutting, excavation and concrete patching, which adds labor. Battery backups and alarms add to the cost but provide extra protection. We give you a written quote that explains each option. That way you can choose the right level of protection for your budget.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Sunrise

    If your basement or crawl space has no pit, we can create one. We cut the slab, excavate through soil, hardpan or rock and set a durable basin. We add gravel around the basin to help water flow in. Then we patch the concrete and install the pump and cover. A new pit gives you a reliable place to collect and remove water.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Sunrise, FL

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and repair. They are often a good choice for shallow sump pits or areas where space is tight. Our team is skilled in installing these pumps, ensuring proper pit depth and discharge line setup for optimal performance in Sunrise, FL.
