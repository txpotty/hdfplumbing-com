    Softener Not Regenerating in Sunrise

    Hard water coming back usually means the softener has stopped regenerating. A failed timer motor, a bad circuit board or a stuck meter turbine are the usual causes, along with settings lost in a power outage. Each part gets tested. Then we program the valve for your household and hardness, and soft water returns by the next cycle.

    Bypass and Fitting Leak Repair in Sunrise, FL

    Bypass valves, the O-rings where the valve meets the tank, cracked fittings and the drain line are the usual leak points. A minor drip still wastes water and damages floors and cabinets. We replace O-rings or fittings and test under pressure. A cracked mineral tank usually means replacement.

    Water Softener Leak Repair in Sunrise, FL

    Drips around fittings waste water. Our plumbers find the source, whether seals, O-rings, hoses or internal parts. We replace the failed part with silicone-lubricated O-rings rated for potable water in your Sunrise, FL home and test it under line pressure.

    Emergency Water Softener Repair in Sunrise

    A softener stuck in regeneration can run water to the drain for hours or flood a utility room. Urgent calls like that get priority. The crew in Sunrise shuts the unit into bypass first, then finds out whether the valve, the timer or the drain line caused it. Usually a stuck piston or a dead motor is to blame, and both can be replaced the same day.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Sunrise, FL

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. Once it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying, and the softener regenerates with plain water that does nothing to the resin. We clean or replace it and check the brine line for air leaks.

    Control Valve Rebuild in Sunrise

    Service, backwash, brine and rinse are the four cycles the control valve directs every time the softener regenerates, usually in the middle of the night. Over the years, seals, spacers and pistons wear down. Leaks at the valve or water running to the drain are common signs. The valve gets rebuilt with the kit made for it.
