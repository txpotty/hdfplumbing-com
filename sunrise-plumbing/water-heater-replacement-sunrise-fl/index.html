    Attic Water Heater Replacement in Sunrise

    Houses from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s may have polybutylene supply pipe, which many insurers refuse to cover and owners choose to replace. Attic water heaters are harder to swap than ones in a garage or closet. Moving a 50-gallon tank through a pull-down ladder or small opening takes two people, and code wants a pan with a drain line, a platform and a light. Two-person crew, always.

    Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith in Sunrise, FL

    We install Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith on the majority of our swaps. Bradford White is sold only through plumbers and known for durability, Rheem has a wide lineup and good parts support, and AO Smith offers many sizes and efficiency options, so the right pick usually depends on fit, warranty and stock. All three hold up.

    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Sunrise, FL

    A leaking tank can put 40 or 50 gallons on the floor faster than most people expect. If water is coming from the heater, shut off the cold supply valve on top, turn the gas valve to off or flip the breaker, and open a hot tap to relieve pressure, then call and we will try to have a plumber and a new tank at your home that day. Do not wait it out.

    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Sunrise

    Electric water heaters in Sunrise usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. A bad element can be replaced. A leaking tank cannot, so if water is coming from the tank body itself, we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to what your panel already supplies. Most swaps take a few hours.

    Rheem and Ruud Water Heater Replacement in Sunrise, FL

    Ruud and Rheem are built by the same maker and share many parts. Our technicians install both, and since the parts overlap, a Ruud thermostat or element is easy to source when a repair makes more sense than a full replacement on a newer tank. We will say when repair works.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Sunrise, FL

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from the air around them, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need space and airflow, often a room of about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they work best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. The space runs cooler.
