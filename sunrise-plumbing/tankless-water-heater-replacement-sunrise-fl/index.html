    Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Sunrise

    The cost of tankless water heater replacement in Sunrise, FL typically ranges from $3,000 to $6,000, depending on the unit's capacity, brand, complexity of installation, such as converting from a tank model, and any necessary electrical or gas line upgrades. This price usually includes the unit itself and labor. We provide a written, upfront quote after a thorough assessment of your home.

    New tankless water heaters typically come with a manufacturer's warranty, which usually covers the heat exchanger for 10 to 15 years and parts for 3 to 5 years. HDF Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work for your peace of mind. We will go over warranty details for your chosen unit during your consultation.

    With proper installation and regular maintenance, a new tankless water heater usually lasts 20 to 25 years. This is much longer than traditional tank water heaters, which usually last 10 to 15 years. Regular flushing and descaling can help extend the life of your tankless unit, especially in areas with hard water.

    DIY tankless water heater replacement is generally not recommended due to the complexity of gas lines, high-voltage electricity, water connections and venting requirements. Improper installation can lead to serious gas leaks, carbon monoxide poisoning, water damage or electrical hazards. It is best to hire a licensed and experienced plumber like HDF Plumbing.

    Yes, you can absolutely replace a traditional tank water heater with a tankless unit. This is a common upgrade for homeowners seeking better energy efficiency and endless hot water. However, it often requires changes to gas lines, venting and possibly electrical wiring, making professional installation important for safety and performance.

    To prepare for tankless water heater replacement, make sure the area around your existing unit is clear and easy to reach for our plumbers. If possible, clear a path to the installation site and keep pets or children safely out of the work area. We will handle draining the old unit and all other technical preparations.

    A standard tankless water heater replacement, if you are upgrading from an existing tankless unit, typically takes 4 to 8 hours. If you are converting from a traditional tank water heater, the process can take longer, possibly a full day, due to extra plumbing, gas or electrical changes required for proper installation and venting.

    Well-maintained tankless units can last 15 to 20 years. In hard water areas without regular descaling, heat exchangers can fail much sooner. Annual flushing makes the largest difference.
