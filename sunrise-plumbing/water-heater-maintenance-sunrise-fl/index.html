    Tankless Descaling in Sunrise, FL

    Scale is the tankless unit's main enemy. Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving heavy scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. We close the isolation valves, connect a small pump and circulate descaling solution through the heat exchanger for the time the manufacturer recommends, then flush it with clean water. The inlet screen gets cleaned too. We also check the burner and venting.

    Flushing a Tank Water Heater in Sunrise

    Popping and rumbling mean sediment. Calcium and magnesium settle on the bottom of the tank and form a layer that traps heat under it, so water boils in pockets beneath the scale and pops every time the burner or lower element fires. The tank drains until the water is clean. Bad tanks need a few rounds.

    Relief Valve and Safety Testing in Sunrise, FL

    The relief valve is the tank's last line of defense. We test the valve, confirm it reseats and check that the discharge pipe is routed correctly. We swap bad ones. Expansion tanks get an air charge check.

    Thermostat, Element and Burner Checks in Sunrise

    Wrong temperature means a part is off. On electric units we test the thermostats and elements for proper resistance and accurate shutoff, and on gas units we inspect the burner, the pilot or igniter and the gas control valve. Salt air in coastal parts of Florida corrodes outdoor fittings and exposed metal pipe faster than in inland areas. Bad parts cost money. They also cut your hot water short.
