    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Sunrise

    For electric water heaters, our maintenance service focuses on checking heating elements, thermostats, and electrical connections. We confirm proper wiring and test for any short circuits or power problems that could affect performance. Sediment flushing is also performed, just like with gas models, to prevent buildup and protect the lower heating element from burning out prematurely in Sunrise.

    Water Heater Anode Rod Inspection in Sunrise

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part that protects your water heater tank from corrosion. During maintenance in Sunrise, we inspect the condition of the anode rod and recommend replacement if it's heavily depleted. Replacing a worn anode rod can extend the life of your water heater by several years, preventing expensive tank leaks.

    T&P Valve and Safety Testing in Sunrise, FL

    The temperature and pressure relief valve keeps your water heater from building unsafe pressure. We test it by lifting the lever and checking that it opens and reseats. A valve that leaks or will not open gets swapped out. We also check that the discharge pipe runs to an approved location. Expansion tanks are checked for proper air charge where installed.

    Electric Water Heater Element Service in Sunrise, FL

    Electric water heaters use upper and lower heating elements controlled by thermostats. Sediment can coat the lower element and cause it to fail. We test both elements and thermostats with the power off and replace parts as needed. A failed element is often the reason for lukewarm water or running out of hot water quickly. Proper testing avoids replacing parts that are still good.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Sunrise

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part in your water heater designed to corrode instead of the tank itself. Depending on your water chemistry, these rods can wear down faster than expected. During maintenance, we inspect the anode rod's condition and recommend replacement if necessary. A healthy anode rod is key for preventing rust and extending the lifespan of your water heater tank, saving you from a costly full replacement.
