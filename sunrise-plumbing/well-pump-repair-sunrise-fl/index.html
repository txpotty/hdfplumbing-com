    Pressure Tank Repair in Sunrise, FL

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. Fast cycling kills pumps young. We check the air charge, inspect the bladder and recharge or replace the tank, then confirm the pump runs at least a minute per cycle. Steadier pressure follows.

    Grundfos Well Pump Repair in Sunrise

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have built-in electronics that protect the motor and report faults. Most Grundfos calls involve a sensor or a drive fault. HDF Plumbing reads the fault code, checks the wiring and tests the pressure sensor before replacing anything in a Sunrise, FL well. Usually the electronics are fine.

    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Sunrise, FL

    Electrical faults cause a big share of well calls, from a tripped breaker to a burned contact in the pressure switch. The control box capacitor and relay are next on the list. Our technicians test circuits with the power locked out. All wiring and splices meet Florida electrical code before the pump restarts.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Sunrise, FL

    Capacitors, relays and corroded terminals inside the control box cause many pump failures. A tripped breaker is often the first sign. Licensed technicians test these parts safely with the power locked out, replace what failed and tighten every connection in the box. Wiring meets Florida code when the job is done.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Sunrise, FL

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up through a suction line with an ejector. Air leaks on the suction side cause most failures. HDF Plumbing checks the foot valve, the ejector nozzle and every suction fitting, then reprimes the pump and watches it reach cut-out pressure. A jet pump that runs nonstop without reaching pressure has almost always lost prime or sucked air through a cracked fitting.

    Above-Ground Jet Pump Repair in Sunrise

    Lost prime is the number one jet pump complaint. Shaft seal leaks and worn impellers come next. The pump gets reprimed and resealed, and the foot valve and suction line get checked, because a single air leak will undo the work. Everything is above ground.

    Deep Well Pump Repair in Sunrise

    Deep wells get submersible pumps. They push water up instead of pulling it, and they run quiet for years, but motors, impellers and wire splices still fail eventually. We pull the pump with the right equipment, test it and replace only what failed. Heat-shrink splice kits and a torque arrestor protect the new work in your Sunrise, FL well.
