    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Apopka

    Tankless water heaters need specialized maintenance, mainly descaling to remove mineral buildup from the heat exchanger. This is especially important where the water is hard. Our technicians use approved descaling solutions to keep your tankless unit running at peak efficiency, preventing reduced hot water flow and possible breakdowns. We also check gas lines, ventilation, and water connections.

    Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Apopka, FL

    Whether gas or electric, your hot water tank benefits greatly from annual maintenance. We check burner operation, flue venting, and potential gas leaks on gas models, and inspect electrical connections and heating elements on electric units. Sediment flushing is a key part of our service. It removes mineral deposits that can reduce heating efficiency and lead to early tank failure.

    Drainage Services and Hydrojet Plumbing in Apopka

    Preventative drain maintenance is key to avoiding clogs and sewer backups.
