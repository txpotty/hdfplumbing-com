    PEX Repiping in Apopka, FL

    PEX is flexible plastic tubing that can run long distances without a fitting, which means fewer joints hidden in your walls. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. PEX shrugs off scale and chlorine, survives freezing better than copper and installs faster, so it is our most common repipe material. We use PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe open. Flow stays strong at the last shower.

    Commercial Repipe Services in Apopka

    Older commercial buildings in Apopka often have galvanized risers, copper that has pinholed or plastic pipe that no longer meets code.     Type L Copper Repiping in Apopka

    Copper has been plumbing homes for generations, and done right it lasts a long time. Our copper repipes use Type L with proper hangers and isolators so the pipe does not wear against framing. Lead-free solder only. Because some water chemistries eat copper, we look at your leak history before recommending it over PEX.

Copper repipes usually cost more and take a day longer than PEX, mostly because every turn needs a fitting.
