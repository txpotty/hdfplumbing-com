    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Apopka

    A submersible pump sits at the bottom of the pit, sealed in an oil-filled housing, and it runs quieter than anything else you can put down there.     Pedestal Pump Service in Apopka, FL

    Louder, yes, but a pedestal motor sits high and dry, so in a narrow pit it often outlasts a submersible by several years. Narrow pits favor pedestals. We replace worn units and set the float rod stops for a proper cycle. A submersible is worth a look when the pit is wide enough.

    Cast Iron Submersible Pumps in Apopka

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Apopka

    Stuck floats and leaking check valves cause more sump failures than dead motors do. A check valve that leaks lets the discharge column fall back into the pit, so the pump runs again for the same water and wears itself out early. New valve every time. Vertical switches suit tight pits, and tethered floats suit wide ones.
