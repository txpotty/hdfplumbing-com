    Jet Pump System Repair in Apopka, FL

    Jet pumps, usually found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Typical problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. HDF Plumbing can effectively troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in Apopka operates at peak efficiency. We also check for proper priming, which is essential for jet pump function.

    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Apopka

    Submersible pumps are common in residential wells, designed to operate submerged within the well casing. When these pumps fail, symptoms often include no water, low water pressure, or strange noises from the well. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing issues like motor burnout, damaged impellers, or faulty check valves that can affect submersible pump performance. We use specialized equipment for safe and efficient pump extraction and reinstallation, ensuring the integrity of your well system.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Apopka

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are common causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most budget-friendly well repairs.

    Well Pump Pressure Tank Issues in Apopka

    The pressure tank works in conjunction with your well pump to maintain consistent water pressure and reduce pump cycling. A waterlogged tank, a ruptured diaphragm, or a faulty air valve can cause rapid pump cycling, fluctuating water pressure, or even no water at all. Our team carefully diagnoses pressure tank problems and performs necessary repairs or replacements. We make sure your tank is properly sized and charged for optimal system performance in Apopka.
