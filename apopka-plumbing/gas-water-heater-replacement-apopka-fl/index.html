    40 and 50 Gallon Tank Replacement in Apopka

    The 40 and 50 gallon gas tank is the workhorse of most Florida homes. When yours starts leaking from the bottom seam or produces rusty water, replacement is the only real fix.     Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Apopka, FL

    For homes in Apopka, FL where traditional venting is not possible, or for those wanting higher efficiency, power vent gas water heaters are an excellent choice. These units use a powered fan to exhaust combustion gases, offering more flexibility in installation location. Our technicians are skilled in installing these more complex systems. That includes running new PVC vent piping and making proper electrical connections for the blower motor, all while following local codes.

    Thermal Expansion Tank Installation in Apopka

    Many water utilities install pressure regulators or backflow devices that create a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure spikes can strain your tank and fixtures. Code commonly requires a thermal expansion tank on these systems. We install a correctly sized expansion tank and pre-charge it to match your water pressure. This simple add-on can help your new heater last longer.

    Standard Gas Water Heater Replacement in Apopka

    Most homes in Apopka use a conventional tank-style gas water heater. We carefully remove your old unit, inspect the existing gas and water lines, and install a new, energy-efficient model.     Atmospheric Vent Tank Upgrades in Apopka, FL

    Most older homes have atmospheric vent water heaters that rely on natural draft up a metal flue. We inspect the flue pipe for rust, proper slope and correct connector size during replacement. A loose or undersized vent can spill exhaust into the garage or closet. We replace damaged vent sections and draft hoods as needed. Then we test draft with the burner running before we leave.

    Large Capacity 75 Gallon Gas Heaters in Apopka, FL

    Big families, soaking tubs and multiple showers running at once often need a 75 gallon tank or a high-recovery 50 gallon model. These heaters use larger burners, sometimes 75,000 BTU, and may need a bigger gas line and flue. We check your meter and pipe size to make sure the larger tank gets the gas it needs. A high-recovery unit can be a good middle ground when space is tight. We walk you through recovery rates so you pick the right fit.

    Power Vent Water Heater Replacement in Apopka

    Power vent gas water heaters use an electric blower to push exhaust through PVC pipe, which lets them sit far from a chimney or roof flue. When the blower motor or pressure switch fails again and again, replacing the whole unit can be the better choice. We install new power vent models and confirm a dedicated outlet is available. Venting length and number of elbows must stay within the manufacturer's limits. Power vent tanks cost more but solve tricky placement problems.
