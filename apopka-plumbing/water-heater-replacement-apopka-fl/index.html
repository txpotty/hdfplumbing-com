    Most replacements take two to four hours. Attic installs or relocations can take longer.

    A traditional water heater stores and heats a limited amount of water in a tank, whereas a tankless water heater heats water on demand as it flows through the unit.     HDF Plumbing installs and services all major brands, including Bradford White, Rheem, AO Smith, Rinnai, Navien and State Water Heaters. We can help you choose the best brand and model that suits your budget and hot water demands.

    Choosing the right size depends on the number of people in your household, their hot water usage habits and peak demand times.     Tank water heaters often last about 8 to 12 years, and hard water and sediment buildup can shorten that.
