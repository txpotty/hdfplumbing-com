    Shower Pan Installation in Apopka

    Shower pans fail quietly. A pan with a pinhole in the liner or a poorly clamped drain will hold water for years while it slowly soaks the subfloor, so we flood-test every tiled pan for 24 hours before tile goes down. Prefab pans get a level check. Either way, the slope runs to the drain.

    Tiled Walk-In Shower Installation in Apopka

    Frameless glass walk-ins are popular, and the plumbing behind them has to be dead on. Valve, head, handheld and drain go in first, then the pan is sloped a quarter inch per foot to the drain. Then we test. We pressure-test the valve and flood the pan for a day before tile.

    Barrier-Free Walk-In Showers in Apopka, FL

    Barrier-free entries need careful drain placement. The membrane and slope get planned with the tile setter, so water reaches the drain instead of spilling out the open side. Humidity causes condensation on cold lines and clogged AC condensate drains, a common source of hidden water damage. We pick the pan system to match.

    Walk-In Shower Installation in Apopka

    Walk-in showers depend on floor slope. With no curb, the floor needs a steady quarter-inch-per-foot pitch to the drain and waterproofing that extends past the opening. We set that slope. We add blocking for grab bars while the studs are bare.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Apopka

    Steam showers need four things: a generator, a water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. The generator goes within the maker's distance limit, and we plumb its supply, drain and steam line to the head. The circuit is an electrician's job. A sealed enclosure with a sloped ceiling keeps drips off you.

    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Apopka, FL

    Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common jobs. The tub comes out, the drain shifts to the pan location and the valve moves up to about 48 inches. On a slab, we cut concrete. No more stepping over a tub wall.
