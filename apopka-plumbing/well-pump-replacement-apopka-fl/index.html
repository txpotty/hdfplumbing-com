    What We Fix on Well Systems in Apopka, FL

    Jet Pump Replacement in Apopka, FL

    Jet pumps sit above ground, in a well house or basement, and pull water up by suction from wells where the water level is within about 25 feet. Deep-well jets with a two-pipe setup can reach further, but they work harder. Lost prime is the classic complaint. Usually a leaking foot valve or a cracked suction line lets the water fall back down the well, so we check both before we blame the pump.

    Agricultural and Ranch Well Pumps in Apopka

    Irrigation and stock wells often run 3 to 10 HP pumps, sometimes on three-phase power with a motor starter and overload protection at the panel. We replace those pumps and the starters that protect them. We schedule around watering times.

    Submersible Well Pump Replacement in Apopka

    A submersible pump hangs on drop pipe inside a 4-inch or 6-inch casing, often 100 to 400 feet down, so pulling one is a two-person job with a pump hoist. Most fail from sand chewing up the impellers or a lightning surge that cooks the windings. Before pulling, we test the windings with a megohmmeter. The reading shows whether the fault is the motor or the wire.

    Grundfos Well Pump Replacement in Apopka, FL

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have a built-in soft start and dry-run protection, and they run on two wires with no control box at the surface. They are pricier than a standard pump. The SQE pairs with a CU 301 controller for constant pressure.

    Constant Pressure Well Pump Installation in Apopka, FL

    Constant pressure systems hold the house at one pressure, say 50 psi, whether one faucet is open or the shower and the irrigation are running together. A variable frequency drive speeds the pump up and slows it down to match demand. No more pressure dip mid-shower.

    Well Pump Brands We Install in Apopka, FL

    Goulds, Grundfos, Franklin Electric and Sta-Rite cover nearly every well pump we install. We pick by the well, not the logo. Parts for Goulds and Franklin are easy to get, which matters when a control box dies on a weekend.

    No-Water Calls in Apopka

    Pump trouble rarely gives notice. One morning the faucet coughs air and then nothing, and by then the motor has often been struggling for weeks. We test the control box and pressure switch before pulling anything.

    Well Pump Efficiency Upgrades in Apopka, FL

    Older 1 HP pumps running against a 60 psi switch use more power than most people expect, especially on a well with a long horizontal run to the house. Right-sizing the pump or adding a variable-speed drive often trims the electric bill. The math does not always work. We run the numbers with you.
