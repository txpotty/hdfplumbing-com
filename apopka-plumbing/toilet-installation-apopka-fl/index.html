    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Installation in Apopka

    Many wells in Florida produce water with a sulfur smell or high iron, which often calls for filtration to protect fixtures and appliances.     Common signs you might need a new toilet include frequent clogs, cracks in the porcelain bowl or tank, constant running water, a wobbly base, or a toilet that is very old and inefficient. Upgrading can save water and refresh the look of your bathroom.

    A one-piece toilet has the bowl and tank integrated into a single unit, offering a sleek look and simpler cleaning. A two-piece toilet has a separate bowl and tank, which are bolted together during installation. Both types are widely available and perform well.
