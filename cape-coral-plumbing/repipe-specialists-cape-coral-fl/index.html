    PEX Pipe Replacement in Cape Coral, FL

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Flexible PEX soaks up some of the pressure spike behind water hammer, and its long fitting-free runs leave fewer weak points. We keep PEX out of sunlight and away from water heater flues.

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Cape Coral, FL

    Polybutylene, a gray or blue plastic pipe installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s, breaks down with exposure to chlorine and other disinfectants in tap water. It fails without warning. Brittle pipe walls and cracked fittings mean one polybutylene leak usually signals risk across the whole house. We replace the full system with PEX or copper. Many insurers ask about it.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Cape Coral, FL

    Older homes may still have galvanized steel pipe that rusts from the inside out. Weak flow and rusty water are common. We remove or abandon the galvanized lines and replace them with PEX or copper, and your water pressure and water quality usually improve the same day the new lines go into service.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Cape Coral

    Kitec, an orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe with brass fittings, and gray polybutylene are both known for failures. Many homes built from the 1980s through the early 2000s have one or the other. We can usually identify it from the pipe at the water heater, and full replacement makes the house easier to insure and sell.
