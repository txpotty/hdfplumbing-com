    Backflow Prevention for Irrigation Systems in Cape Coral, FL

    Irrigation systems in Cape Coral, FL are a common source of backflow, as stagnant water and lawn chemicals can be drawn back into the potable water supply. Installing a dedicated backflow preventer for your sprinkler system is often a local code requirement. We specialize in selecting and installing the correct device, such as a pressure vacuum breaker or atmospheric vacuum breaker, to protect your home's main water line from contaminants coming from your landscape irrigation.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Cape Coral

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules allow. We size them to avoid excessive pressure loss on your system.

    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in Cape Coral, FL

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of protection and are required for high hazard connections like commercial kitchens, medical facilities, chemical injection and car washes. They include two independent check valves with a relief valve that dumps water if either check fails. Because the relief valve can discharge, we plan for drainage and install the assembly above grade where it can be tested easily. We carry proven brands like Watts, Febco, Wilkins and Apollo.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Backflow Preventer Installation in Cape Coral

    The cost for backflow preventer installation in Cape Coral, FL usually ranges from $400 to $1,500, depending on the type of device, its size and the complexity of the installation. A basic residential PVB for an irrigation system is usually on the lower end, while a commercial-grade RPZ assembly costs more because of material and labor requirements.

    Backflow preventers are installed at cross-connection points where non-potable water could enter the potable water supply. Common locations include irrigation system connections, fire sprinkler lines, boiler systems and connections to auxiliary water supplies, usually just after the main water meter or at the point of hazard.

    You probably need a backflow preventer if you have an irrigation system, a fire sprinkler system, a swimming pool or any auxiliary water source that connects to the main water supply. Local regulations in Cape Coral often require these devices for certain applications to protect public health.

    We install backflow preventers from leading manufacturers known for quality and reliability. Our preferred brands include Watts, Febco, Zurn and Apollo, so you receive a durable, effective device that meets all industry standards.

    Most residential installs take two to four hours including testing. Large commercial assemblies can take a full day, particularly if piping must be reconfigured.

    We install all common types of backflow preventers, including reduced pressure zone assemblies, pressure vacuum breakers and double check valve assemblies. Our team will recommend the right device based on your property's hazard level and local plumbing codes in Cape Coral.

    Backflow is when water flows the wrong way through a pipe and carries contaminants into the clean water supply. It can happen from back siphonage during a pressure drop or back pressure from pumps and other equipment.

    Most municipalities require annual testing of backflow preventers by a licensed, certified backflow tester. This confirms the device is working correctly and continues to protect your potable water supply from contamination. We can also assist with scheduling these annual tests.
