    Plumbing Rough-In Services in Cape Coral

    The rough-in phase is the foundation of your new construction plumbing system. This involves installing all underground and in-wall piping before concrete is poured and walls are closed. We precisely lay out and connect all water supply, drain, and vent pipes according to architectural plans and local Cape Coral codes. Using high-quality PEX or copper piping, we ensure secure, leak-free connections that will serve your property for decades.

    Water Heater Installation for New Builds in Cape Coral

    Selecting and installing the right water heater is crucial for any new construction. We provide professional installation of traditional tank-style and tankless water heaters, considering your property's hot water demands and energy efficiency goals. Our plumbers confirm correct sizing, venting, and gas or electrical connections, adhering to all safety regulations for new homes in Cape Coral.

    Water Heater and Recirculation Installation in Cape Coral, FL

    New homes commonly use tank water heaters in the garage, basement or utility room, or tankless units on an exterior wall. We size the heater to your bathroom count and install gas or electric connections, venting, an expansion tank and drain pan where required.     Underground and Under-Slab Plumbing in Cape Coral

    The underground phase sets the drain, waste and vent layout that every fixture in the house will rely on. We trench, bed and slope PVC or ABS drain lines at a minimum quarter inch per foot and protect every pipe that passes through the slab or foundation with sleeves. Nearly all homes in Florida sit on concrete slabs over sandy soil, so leaks under the slab are a common and costly repair. We compact bedding carefully and avoid rigid points that could stress the pipe as the ground moves. Each line is water tested and left visible for the inspector before your concrete contractor pours.

    Fixture Installation and Trim-Out in Cape Coral, FL

    Once the rough-in is complete and walls are up, our team returns for fixture installation and trim-out. This includes connecting sinks, toilets, bathtubs, showers, dishwashers, and water heaters. We make sure all fixtures are properly seated, sealed, and functional, providing a clean finish to your new construction plumbing. We work with many brands like Moen, Kohler, and American Standard to match your design specifications.
