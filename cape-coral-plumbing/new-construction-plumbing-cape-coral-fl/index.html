    Sewer and Drainage Systems in New Builds in Cape Coral

    The building drain under a new house usually runs 4-inch PVC at a quarter inch per foot out to the sewer or septic connection. Nearly all homes in Florida sit on concrete slabs over sandy soil, so leaks under the slab are a common and costly repair. We bed the pipe in sand or gravel so it holds grade as the backfill settles. Cleanouts go where code calls for them. We also set a two-way cleanout near the foundation so a future drain call never requires pulling a toilet.

    Fixture Installation and Trim-Out in Cape Coral, FL

    Gas Line Installation for New Construction in Cape Coral, FL
