    Outdoor & Storm Drain Cleaning in Cape Coral

    Yard drains and downspout lines fill with leaves, grit and roots. Hurricanes and tropical storms bring flooding, power loss, and sewer backups to Florida, often swamping drainage systems for days. Standing water near the foundation is the warning sign. We jet or cable the line to the outlet and pull the debris out of the catch basin, so the next storm runs off instead of soaking into the soil next to the house.

    Jetting Versus Cabling in Cape Coral, FL

    Cabling and jetting solve different problems. A cable cuts through a blockage. A jetter scrubs the pipe wall clean and flushes the debris downstream to the city main or septic tank, so we cable first on a hard root mass and jet afterward when the line has years of buildup on it.

    Main Line Drain Cleaning in Cape Coral

    Toilets bubbling while the washer drains? The main line is the likely spot. Every fixture shares that one pipe, so the lowest drain in the house is where water comes up, and the usual causes are roots, a crushed section of old pipe or a heap of wipes caught on a bad joint, which we find with a camera and clear with a sectional cable or a jetter.

    Clogged Toilet Repair in Cape Coral, FL

    Toilets clog at the trapway, a tight bend inside the porcelain. We use a closet auger with a rubber guard on the bowl end. If the toilet clears but water still rises in the tub, the clog is past the toilet in the branch or main, and we move to a bigger machine at that point. Kids' toys come out too.

    Main Sewer Drain Cleaning in Cape Coral
