    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Cape Coral

    The cost of hydro jetting in Cape Coral, FL can range from $300 to $800, depending on the severity of the clog, the length and diameter of the pipe, and whether camera inspection is required. More complex jobs, like removing extensive tree roots or cleaning long commercial lines, may be on the higher end. We provide a written estimate after assessing your situation.

    No permit is needed for jetting. If the inspection reveals damage that needs repair, permits may be required and we handle them.

    Most residential jobs take 1 to 3 hours including inspection. Long commercial lines or heavy grease can add time.

    A thorough jetting often keeps a line clear for a year or more, depending on use and roots. Lines with heavy root intrusion may require maintenance or a repair.

    Snaking uses a rotating cable to break through a clog. Jetting uses pressurized water to clean the entire pipe wall and flush debris away.

    While hydro jetting effectively removes existing tree roots from your sewer line, it does not prevent new roots from growing back. For a long-term solution to recurring root intrusion, we might recommend trenchless sewer repair or pipe replacement, which we can discuss during our assessment.

    For most modern and well-maintained pipes, hydro jetting is very safe. However, for very old, corroded, or severely damaged pipes, the high pressure could cause further damage.
