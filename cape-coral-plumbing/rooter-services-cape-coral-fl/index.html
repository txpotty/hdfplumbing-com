    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in Cape Coral

    To prevent future clogs, avoid pouring grease down drains, use drain screens in sinks and showers to catch hair and food particles, and dispose of fibrous materials like coffee grounds and wipes in the trash. Consider periodic professional drain maintenance or hydro-jetting, especially for older homes or those with a history of recurring blockages.

    Rooting uses a spinning cable with cutting blades to break up clogs and roots. Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scrub the pipe walls. Many tough lines benefit from both.

    Snaking uses an auger to break through and pull out blockages, while hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the entire inside of the pipe, removing buildup like grease and mineral deposits. Hydro-jetting provides a much more thorough cleaning, often recommended for recurring clogs or main sewer lines.

    Slow drains in several fixtures, gurgling toilets and sewage odors are common signs. Water backing up into a tub when you flush is another clue. Frequent clogs in the same line also point to a deeper blockage.

    Homes with tree roots or older clay pipe often need rooting every 12 to 24 months. Homes with newer PVC lines may go many years without it. A camera inspection can help set the right schedule.
