    Channel and Trench Drains in Cape Coral, FL

    Driveways and patios that slope toward the house can send runoff straight into a garage or a door threshold. A trench drain cut into the concrete stops it. The channel goes into a saw-cut in the slab with a traffic-rated grate and solid pipe to an outlet.

    French Drain Installation in Cape Coral

    A French drain is a gravel-filled trench with perforated pipe that collects groundwater and carries it away. We wrap the gravel in filter fabric so silt does not clog it. The pipe needs at least a 1 percent slope to a daylight outlet, a dry well or a sump, and the outlet end needs a grate so animals do not nest in it.

    Commercial Drainage Services in Cape Coral

    Retail centers and office buildings need floor drains, roof drains and parking lot catch basins that keep working. We set up service schedules for property managers. A catch basin that fills with silt can flood a parking lot in one storm and send water into ground-floor suites, so we clean basins before the wet season and jet the lines that tie them together.

    Hydro Jet Line Cleaning in Cape Coral

    Jetting uses water at high pressure to strip a pipe back to its original diameter. Around Cape Coral, flushing the water heater yearly and descaling tankless units helps keep mineral buildup under control. Mineral scale and grease build up on pipe walls over years and catch everything that goes down the drain, so a cleaned pipe stays clear much longer than one that was only cabled. Most jetting jobs take two hours.

    Outdoor and Storm Drain Cleaning in Cape Coral

    Area drains, catch basins and driveway trench drains fill with leaves and silt. A buried grate sends water across the yard, and the pipe under it fills with mud. We vacuum the basins and jet the lines. Fall cleaning helps most.

    Main Line and Sewer Drainage in Cape Coral, FL

    Water coming up in the tub when the washer drains is a classic main line symptom. The lowest drain in the house shows the backup first.     Dry Well Installation in Cape Coral, FL

    Where there is no place to discharge on the surface, a dry well lets water soak into deeper soil. Drainage depends on the soil. We run a percolation check before digging, size the well for the roof or yard area feeding it, and keep it at least ten feet from the foundation and well away from any septic field.
