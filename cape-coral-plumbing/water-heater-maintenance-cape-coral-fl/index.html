    Commercial Tank and Tankless Service in Cape Coral, FL

    Tank Flush and Inspection in Cape Coral

    Sediment forms from minerals in the water. On a standard tank visit we flush that sediment out, test the temperature and pressure relief valve, check the anode for corrosion and inspect every fitting for leaks, which protects one of the more expensive appliances in the house. Efficiency comes back. Recovery speeds up.

    Tankless Descaling in Cape Coral, FL

    Scale is the tankless unit's main enemy. Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving heavy scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. A pump runs descaler through the exchanger for the recommended time, followed by a clean water flush. We clean the inlet filter. We also check the burner and venting.

    Yearly Service Plans in Cape Coral, FL

    Forgetting is the main reason tanks go unflushed. Plans schedule the visit, send reminders and add priority booking and repair discounts. We track it for you. Long-term savings add up.

    Electric Element Service in Cape Coral, FL

    Two elements, two thermostats. Sediment coats the lower element first, it runs hot and burns out, and that is the most common reason an electric tank suddenly gives you only half a tank of hot water. Power gets shut off first. We only replace what failed.

    Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in Cape Coral, FL

    Gas units need a clean burner and a good draft. We check for gas leaks at the valve and connector, watch the flame, clean the burner and confirm the flue pulls exhaust up and out instead of spilling it into the room. Carbon monoxide is the concern. We also test the thermocouple.
