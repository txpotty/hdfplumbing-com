    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Replacement in Cape Coral

    A standard 40 or 50 gallon gas water heater replacement usually runs $1,800 to $3,200 installed, including haul-away and basic code upgrades. Larger 75 gallon or power vent units usually cost $3,000 to $4,800. We give a firm price before starting.

    Most standard gas water heater replacements can be finished within 3 to 5 hours. More complex installations, such as converting to a power vent unit or relocating the water heater, may take longer, usually a full day.

    Atmospheric vent water heaters use natural convection to vent combustion gases up a chimney or flue. Power vent models use an electric fan to force gases out, allowing for more flexible venting options and often higher efficiency due to more complete combustion.

    Most gas tanks last 8 to 12 years. Sediment from hard water and extreme garage temperatures can cut that. Annual flushing and anode checks help them last longer.

    Replacing a gas water heater involves working with gas lines, water connections and venting, which can be dangerous if not done correctly. It is strongly recommended to hire a licensed professional like HDF Plumbing for safety and code compliance.

    We install all major reputable brands of gas water heaters, including Rheem, A.O. Smith, Bradford White and Noritz. We can help you choose the best brand and model to suit your budget and hot water demands.

    A typical gas water heater has a lifespan of 10 to 12 years with proper maintenance. Factors like water quality in Cape Coral, such as hard water causing mineral buildup, and usage patterns can affect its longevity.
