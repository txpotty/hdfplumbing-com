    Conventional Tank Water Heater Replacement in Cape Coral

    Standard tank heaters are still in most homes, and they usually fail from age, rust or sediment. A leak at the base, loud popping or rusty hot water means it is time, and a like-for-like or resized swap usually takes two to four hours. You shower tonight.

    Attic Water Heater Replacement in Cape Coral

    Houses from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s may have polybutylene supply pipe, which many insurers refuse to cover and owners choose to replace. Attic water heaters take more work to replace than ones in a garage or closet. Getting a 50-gallon tank up a pull-down ladder or through a narrow scuttle takes two people and care, and code calls for a drain pan with a line to the outside or a safe drain, a service platform and a light near the unit. Two-person crew, always.

    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Cape Coral

    Electric water heaters in Cape Coral usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. A bad element can be replaced. A leaking tank cannot, so if water is coming from the tank body itself, we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to what your panel already supplies. Most swaps take a few hours.
