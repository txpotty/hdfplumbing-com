    Perma-Liner Lining Equipment in Cape Coral

    Perma-Liner makes inversion drums and epoxy systems for lining 2- to 6-inch drain lines, which covers most of what we see in houses. The equipment fits in a pickup bed. Compact gear matters on tight lots. Resin gets mixed on site and timed to the length of the run, so it does not start curing before the liner is in place.

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow Trenchless Systems in Cape Coral

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow both make lining systems we use, each with its own resin chemistry and installation equipment. Both are built to ASTM standards for cured-in-place pipe. The brand matters less than the prep, because a liner bonded to a dirty, scaly pipe wall will not hold the way the manufacturer rates it. We clean to bare pipe first.

    Cost Comparison of Trenchless vs Traditional in Cape Coral, FL

    Depth changes the math. A shallow line in open lawn is cheap to dig, but a lateral eight feet deep that runs under the driveway and a porch costs far more to excavate than to burst or line from two pits. We quote both ways. Then you pick.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Cape Coral, FL

    CIPP stands for cured-in-place pipe. We wet out a felt tube with epoxy resin, invert it into the cleaned line with air pressure, and hold it against the old pipe wall until the resin hardens. Ambient cure takes a few hours, and hot water or steam shortens it. The finished liner seals cracks and leaking joints in the original pipe.

    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Cape Coral

    Orangeburg is bituminous fiber pipe that was laid in houses mostly from the late 1940s into the early 1970s. It goes oval and blisters, so a liner has nothing round to bond to and bursting is usually the better fix. Clay is different. Clay that still holds its round shape is a good candidate for a liner.

    Commercial Trenchless Sewer Lining in Cape Coral, FL

    Office and retail buildings often have a cast iron building drain under the slab that has rotted along the bottom. Lining it avoids cutting the floor. Work gets scheduled around tenant hours, nights included. A typical commercial lining job runs one to three days, depending on footage and how many branch connections need to be reinstated with a cutter.

    Root Intrusion Fixes in Cape Coral, FL

    Roots enter at the joints, where clay bells or cast iron hubs have a small gap or a failed gasket. Once inside, they branch into a mat that fills the pipe. We cut them out with a mechanical root cutter or a hydro jet, then line the pipe so those joints are sealed for good and the line stops needing a cleaning every few months. With no joints left, there is nowhere for them to return.
