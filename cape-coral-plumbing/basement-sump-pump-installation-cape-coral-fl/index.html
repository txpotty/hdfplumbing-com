    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Sump Pump Installation in Cape Coral

    Replacing a pump in an existing pit usually runs $500 to $1,200 including the pump and check valve. Cutting a new pit through the slab typically costs $1,500 to $3,500, and a battery backup adds roughly $600 to $1,500. The backup is a common add.

    Changing out a pump in an existing pit takes about two to three hours. Plan on most of a day for a new pit with a slab cut and patch, since the concrete needs a little time to firm up before the pump goes into service.

    Labor on our sump pump installs is warrantied, typically for one year. Each pump also has a factory warranty, usually one to five years depending on the brand and model, and we write the exact coverage on your invoice so you know which part to call about if something fails. Save the paperwork.

    Quality cast iron pumps usually carry three to five years of factory coverage. We back our installation labor separately, and we walk you through the exact terms for the model you pick before we install it.

    Discharge belongs in the yard, a dry well, or an approved drainage area at least 10 feet from the foundation. Most local codes do not allow sump water in the sanitary sewer, and it can draw a fine.

    Look for a pump that runs nonstop, short cycles, or does not start when the pit fills. Corrosion on the housing and a float that hangs up are warning signs, and if your basement flooded even though you have a pump, the pump or its switch was not up to the job. Over seven years old is suspect.

    We strongly recommend professional installation. The job combines electrical, plumbing and concrete work, and a bad seal on the lid, an undersized pit or a discharge that drains toward the house can leave you worse off than having no pump at all. Mistakes show up in storms.

    Most standard installs finish within 4 to 8 hours. Long discharge runs or a new pit can add several hours, and if we need a new circuit from the panel, an electrician may need to come out the same day, which can push the job into the evening. We give a timeline at the visit.
