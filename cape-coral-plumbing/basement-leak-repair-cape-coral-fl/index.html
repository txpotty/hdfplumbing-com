    How We Fix Basement Leaks in Cape Coral, FL

    Exterior Waterproofing Solutions in Cape Coral, FL

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing, cleaning the wall, and applying a membrane with a drainage board over it. It is the most thorough fix and the most disruptive one. Plan on several days. Our crew handles the footing drain and its outlet, and a waterproofing partner applies the membrane.

    Groundwater Pressure Relief in Cape Coral

    High groundwater pushes up through the slab as well as in through the walls. You may see water coming up at the floor cracks or around the floor drain during a long wet week. Under-slab drain pipe tied to a sump lowers the water level beneath the floor. It is not a quick job.

    Sump Pit and Pump Installation in Cape Coral

    Installing a new sump means breaking out a section of slab, setting a lined pit, and running the discharge out through the rim joist. Most of our installs get a cast iron pump, a check valve and 1-1/2 inch discharge. Discharge cannot go into the sewer in most places. It usually ends outside, well away from the foundation, at a spot your local rules allow.

    Clogged Drain Tile Repair in Cape Coral

    Footing drains clog with silt and roots over the years, and once they stop flowing the water table at the wall rises. A camera run from the sump inlet shows us where the tile is plugged. A jetter usually opens it. Crushed clay tile has to be dug up and replaced with perforated PVC or corrugated pipe in a sock.

    Exterior Membrane Waterproofing in Cape Coral, FL

    Excavating the outside of a foundation lets us fix the leak where the water starts. Salt air in coastal parts of Florida corrodes outdoor fittings and exterior metal pipe faster than in inland areas. We pick a membrane rated for that exposure, lay new perforated drain pipe at the footing in clean stone, and backfill so the grade slopes away from the house at least six inches over the first ten feet. We extend the downspouts while the yard is open.

    Waterproofing for Older Florida Homes in Cape Coral, FL

    Older basements usually went in with block walls and little more than tar on the outside. Many homes in Florida built before the mid 1970s have cast iron drains under the slab, which commonly crack and corrode with age. Water collects inside hollow block cores, and weep holes at the base let it drain into a perimeter channel. Clay tile drains fail too.

    Wall and Slab Seepage Repair in Cape Coral

    Seepage at the wall or the slab joint is usually surface water pooling against the foundation. Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and damage buried water and sewer lines. Before anyone cuts the floor, we check the gutters, downspout extensions and the grade along that wall, because fixing those outside often stops seepage for a fraction of the cost. We always try the cheap fixes first.

    Hydrostatic Pressure Management in Cape Coral, FL

    Water pressing against the outside of a basement wall finds the weakest point, usually the cove joint where wall meets floor. Heavy afternoon thunderstorms are common in summer, and saturated ground can push water into sewer lines and slow every fixture in the house. An interior drain channel along that joint relieves the pressure by giving the water a path to the sump. No pressure, no seepage.
