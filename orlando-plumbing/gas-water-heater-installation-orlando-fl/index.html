    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Installation in Orlando

    A standard 40 to 50 gallon gas tank usually costs $1,800 to $4,000 installed. Power vent and tankless units run higher due to vent and gas work.

    Fuel and burner setup are the difference. Natural gas comes from a utility line at lower pressure, while propane comes from a tank and needs a different orifice and regulator setting. We install both.

    Flush the tank once a year and check the anode rod every few years. Sediment over the burner makes the tank run hot and noisy, and a spent anode lets the steel rust from the inside. We offer yearly maintenance.

    Rusty water, leaks, pilot trouble and slow recovery are common signs. Past ten years old, replacement may be the smarter option.

    Peak demand decides it, which comes down to how many people and fixtures use hot water at the same time. Our technicians look at that during the visit and recommend a gallon size or flow rate for your home.
