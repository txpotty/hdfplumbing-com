    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Orlando, FL

    Cast iron drains and sewer lines corrode from the inside, leaving rough scale, cracks and channel rot along the bottom of the pipe. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or cutters, then line it to restore a smooth, sealed interior. This is especially useful for cast iron running under slabs where excavation is costly. Lining can add decades of service.

    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Orlando, FL

    Sewer lines in Orlando, FL can suffer damage from several factors, including shifting soils that cause pipes to crack or misalign. A high water table across much of Florida affects drainage, septic design, and how easily crews can dig for underground repairs. Tree root intrusion is another major cause, as roots seek out moisture in pipes and grow, causing blockages and structural damage. Age and corrosion, especially in older cast iron or clay pipes, also contribute to deterioration. Our trenchless solutions address these common issues effectively.

    Sewer Line Video Inspection in Orlando

    Before any trenchless repair, we conduct a thorough video inspection of your sewer line. This involves inserting a high-resolution camera into the pipe to accurately diagnose the problem's exact location and nature. This crucial step makes sure we choose the most appropriate and effective trenchless method for your Orlando property, avoiding guesswork.

    Sectional Point Repair in Orlando

    When only one section of a line is damaged, a point repair can fix it without lining the whole length. We position a short resin liner at the damaged spot using the camera and inflate it until it cures. Point repairs are a cost-effective fix for a single crack, hole or root entry. They are a good fit on otherwise healthy lines.

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow Trenchless Technologies in Orlando

    HDF Plumbing uses proven trenchless technologies from trusted brands like Perma-Liner and NuFlow. These systems are known for their durability, effectiveness and adherence to plumbing standards. Using these materials and equipment means your trenchless sewer line repair in Orlando is not only low impact but also a high-quality, long-lasting solution, backed by manufacturer warranties.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Orlando

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one while fracturing the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene pipe, which is very resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in older Orlando homes. This method is well suited when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.

    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Orlando

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg fiber pipe or vitrified clay sewer laterals. Orangeburg flattens and blisters over time, while clay cracks and separates at joints. Pipe bursting is often the best option for Orangeburg, and lining can work well for clay that still holds its shape. We determine the material on camera before recommending a method.
