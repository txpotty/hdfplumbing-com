    PEX for Water Line Replacement in Orlando

    When your main water line from the street to your home needs replacement, PEX is an excellent option. Its flexibility allows for easier trenchless or minimal-dig installations, limiting disruption to your yard in Orlando. PEX main lines handle ground movement and resist corrosion better than rigid pipe. Nearly all homes in Florida sit on concrete slabs over sandy soil, so leaks under the slab are a common and costly repair.

    Copper to PEX Conversions in Orlando

    Copper lines can develop pinhole leaks from water chemistry, high velocity and sometimes soil contact under slabs. Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving heavy scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. Once a home has had several pinhole leaks, more usually follow. Converting to PEX stops the cycle and lowers the risk of hidden water damage. We use approved transition fittings wherever new PEX connects to existing copper stubs or valves.

    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Orlando, FL

    Gray polybutylene pipe was common in homes built from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s and is notorious for sudden failures. Chlorine in municipal water breaks down the plastic and its fittings over time. Plenty of insurers now ask about polybutylene when writing a policy. Replacing it with PEX removes that risk and can make your home easier to sell and insure.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Orlando, FL

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a secure seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can recover from kinks with a heat gun. It is our favored choice for many full repipes.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Orlando

    PEX-B is stiffer and more affordable, and it is joined with copper crimp rings or stainless steel cinch clamps over brass or poly fittings. It is a solid option for smaller projects and budget-conscious repipes. Brands like Viega, Zurn and SharkBite offer dependable PEX-B systems. We use a go/no-go gauge on every crimp to verify a proper seal.

    Frequently Asked Questions About PEX Pipe Installation in Orlando

    The cost of PEX pipe installation in Orlando can range from $2,500 to $15,000 or more, depending on the size of your home, accessibility and whether it is a full repipe or a partial replacement. Minor repairs might be a few hundred dollars. We provide a detailed quote after an on-site assessment.

    A full home PEX repipe for an average-sized home in Orlando typically takes 3 to 5 days. Smaller projects or single-line replacements can often be completed in a single day. We aim to minimize disruption and communicate timelines clearly.

    PEX can be used for outdoor applications like irrigation lines or outdoor spigots, but it needs to be protected from direct UV light, which can break down the material over time. When used outdoors, PEX should consistently be buried or otherwise shielded.

    Most whole-home PEX repipes take two to four days. Larger homes or difficult attic access can take a bit longer.

    PEX resists scale and corrosion, flexes with temperature changes and has fewer joints. Copper is very durable but can develop pinhole leaks in some water conditions and costs more to install.

    While PEX is simpler to install than copper, professional installation is highly recommended. Correct sizing, routing and secure connections are crucial for preventing leaks and ensuring system longevity. Improper DIY installation can lead to costly water damage and code violations.

    PEX must be protected from sunlight because UV light degrades it. Outdoors it needs to be buried, sleeved or completely covered.
