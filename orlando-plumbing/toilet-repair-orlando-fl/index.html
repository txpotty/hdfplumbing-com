    The gaskets and seals within your toilet tank and at its base are essential for preventing leaks. Over time, these rubber components can degrade, crack, or become brittle due to age and exposure to water, especially where the water is hard. We specialize in replacing worn-out flapper seals, tank-to-bowl gaskets, and the wax ring at the toilet's base, which is essential for preventing leaks onto your bathroom floor and maintaining a watertight seal.

    Running Toilet Repair in Orlando

    A running toilet is not just annoying, it can waste hundreds of gallons of water a day, leading to significantly higher water bills. This common issue is usually caused by a faulty flapper, a misaligned fill valve, or an improperly adjusted float. Our plumbers promptly identify the exact cause, whether it's mineral buildup from hard water affecting the flapper seal or a worn-out component, and replace the necessary parts to stop the continuous flow and restore efficiency.

    Toilet Tank and Bowl Crack Repair in Orlando

    Cracks in a toilet tank or bowl in Orlando can lead to significant water leakage and require immediate attention.     Leaking Toilet Base Repair in Orlando, FL
