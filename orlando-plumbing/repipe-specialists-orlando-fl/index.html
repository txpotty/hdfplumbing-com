    Residential Repiping Experts in Orlando

    For any residential property in Orlando experiencing plumbing issues due to old, failing pipes, HDF Plumbing offers tailored repiping services.     Repipe Costs Versus Leak Repairs in Orlando, FL

    A whole-home PEX repipe for an average home often runs $5,000 to $12,000, with copper usually costing more. A single slab leak repair can run $1,000 to $4,000 including access and restoration. After two or three leaks, a repipe often pays for itself. We give you a side-by-side comparison so you can decide.

    Efficient PEX Repiping in Orlando, FL

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and highly popular choice for repiping projects in Orlando, FL. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points and making installation quicker and less intrusive than traditional rigid pipes. PEX is also highly resistant to freezing, scaling, and chlorine, common issues in many water systems. We use high-quality PEX-A or PEX-B tubing, installed with durable crimp or expansion fittings, to create a dependable and efficient plumbing network throughout your home.

    Drywall Patching and Restoration in Orlando, FL

    Repiping requires small openings in walls and ceilings to reach fixtures. Our crews cut clean openings, protect surrounding areas and patch every hole when the plumbing is done. We match common textures like knockdown and orange peel. Your walls are ready for paint when we finish.

    Drain Line Repiping in Orlando

    While often overlooked, your home's drain lines are just as important as your supply lines. Old cast iron or corroded drain pipes can lead to frequent clogs, slow drainage, and even sewage backups. HDF Plumbing provides complete drain line repiping in Orlando, replacing failing waste pipes with new, smooth PVC or ABS lines. This ensures efficient waste removal and prevents future drainage problems, protecting your home from foul odors and unsanitary conditions.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Orlando

    PEX-A tubing is flexible, resists scale buildup and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open up the full pipe diameter. Long continuous runs mean fewer joints in walls and attics. Most homes are repiped with PEX in two to four days.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Orlando

    Kitec, with its orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe and brass fittings, and gray polybutylene pipe are both known for failures. Many homes built in the 1980s through early 2000s contain one or the other. We replace these systems completely so your home is easier to insure and sell. Visible pipe at the water heater often tells us what you have.

    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Orlando

    Slab leaks are a costly and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. A high water table across much of Florida affects drainage, septic design, and how easily crews can dig for underground repairs. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the compromised pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use advanced detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.
