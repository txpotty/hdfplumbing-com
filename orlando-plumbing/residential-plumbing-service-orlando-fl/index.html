    Pipe Repair and Repiping in Orlando, FL

    Older homes often have galvanized or polybutylene pipes prone to corrosion and leaks. Houses from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s may have polybutylene supply pipe, which many insurers dislike and owners choose to replace. Our residential plumbing service provides expert pipe repair for isolated issues or full home repiping using durable materials like PEX or copper. We assess your existing system, recommend the best long-term solution and complete the repipe with minimal disruption, providing a reliable and safe water supply for decades to come.

    Faucet, Toilet and Fixture Repair in Orlando, FL

    Hard water wears out faucet cartridges, toilet fill valves and flappers faster than in soft water areas. We replace cartridges in Moen, Delta and Kohler faucets, rebuild running toilets and fix leaking shower valves. When a fixture is beyond repair, we install new ones and haul the old ones away. Small drips add up, so fixing them saves water and money.

    Drain Cleaning & Clog Removal in Orlando, FL

    Clogged drains are a common nuisance in Orlando homes, often caused by grease, hair and soap scum buildup. We use professional-grade tools like augers, hydro-jetting equipment and drain snakes to clear even the toughest blockages in kitchen sinks, bathroom drains and main sewer lines. Our methods are safe for your pipes and effective at removing obstructions that DIY solutions can't handle. We can also inspect lines with cameras to identify recurring problems, such as tree root intrusion or collapsed pipes, which are sometimes a factor in older Orlando properties.

    Repiping and Pipe Replacement in Orlando

    Homes with failing polybutylene, corroded galvanized or pinhole-prone copper often need a repipe. We replace supply lines with PEX or copper, routing new lines through the attic and walls to avoid breaking the slab. Most homes are repiped in two to four days with water restored each night. We patch the drywall openings so the house is ready for painting.

    Leak Detection and Repair in Orlando, FL

    Hidden leaks can cause significant damage to your home's foundation and structure. Nearly all homes in Florida sit on concrete slabs over sandy soil, so leaks under the slab are a frequent and costly repair. Our residential plumbing service includes advanced leak detection using acoustic sensors and thermal imaging to locate leaks behind walls, under floors or in slab foundations without destructive methods. Once found, our experienced technicians perform precise repairs, whether it's a simple pipe patch or a full section replacement, protecting your home from costly water damage.
