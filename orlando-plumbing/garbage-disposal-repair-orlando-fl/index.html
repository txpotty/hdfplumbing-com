    Noisy Disposal Repair in Orlando

    Grinding, rattling or a hum that sounds rough all point to something inside. A loose mount will rattle. We check the mount first, then the chamber for debris, then listen to the motor with the chamber empty, because a motor that whines with nothing in it has bad bearings.

    Leak Detection and Repair in Orlando, FL

    There are four usual leak points on a disposal: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. We dry everything, run water with a little food coloring and watch to see which joint shows color first, since water runs down the outside of the unit and hides the real source. Three of the four are fixable. When the housing leaks, the unit has to go.

    Noise and Odor Diagnosis in Orlando

    A rattle that stops after a few seconds is usually a spoon or a pit bouncing in the chamber. A steady whine is different.
