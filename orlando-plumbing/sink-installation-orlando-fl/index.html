    Undermount Clips and Cure Time in Orlando

    Undermount bowls need overnight to cure. The silicone bead under the rim reaches handling strength in a few hours but needs about a day before it carries the weight of a full basin of water and a pot leaning on the edge. We brace it and come back. Plumbing goes in on the second visit.

    Undermount Sink Installation in Orlando

    Undermount sinks hang from the countertop. We set the bowl with the clips or anchors the stone shop provides, run a continuous bead of 100 percent silicone around the rim, and leave it braced while the adhesive cures overnight. Adhesive by itself will not hold it. Plumbing goes on the next day.

    Faucet and Drain Assembly for New Sinks in Orlando, FL

    Faucet and drain go on before the sink is set, when possible. Mounting the faucet and the pop-up on a sink that is still on sawhorses takes a fraction of the time and gets a tighter seal than working upside down inside the cabinet. Undermounts are the exception. Those get plumbed from below.

    Bathroom Sink Installation in Orlando, FL

    Bathroom sinks are lighter but fussier. The faucet holes have to match the faucet you picked, whether that is a single hole, a 4 inch centerset or an 8 inch widespread, and the overflow has to line up with the pop-up drain. The spec sheet gets read first. Mismatches get caught before the counter is cut.

    Pedestal Sink Mounting in Orlando

    The pedestal is mostly for looks. The basin itself hangs on a bracket lagged into 2x blocking behind the drywall, and if the old wall has no blocking we open it, add a board between the studs, and patch it back. Drywall anchors do not cut it. A loose basin cracks the trap.

    Commercial Sink Installation in Orlando, FL

    Commercial kitchens need sinks the health inspector will sign off on. Three-compartment sinks usually drain indirectly through an air gap to a floor sink, and hand sinks need tempered water within easy reach of every prep station on the line. Plan review comments come first. Mop basins and grease interceptor ties are part of it.

    Kitchen Sink Installation in Orlando

    Kitchen sinks come in four mounts: drop-in, undermount, farmhouse and flush-set. A cast iron farmhouse bowl can top a hundred pounds empty, so the base cabinet often gets reinforced before it goes in. We check the cabinet before the sink arrives. Disposal and dishwasher connections are part of the job.

    Wall-Mount and ADA Sink Installation in Orlando, FL

    Wall-hung lavatories need a carrier or heavy blocking. ADA layouts call for a rim at or under 34 inches, open knee space, and insulated pipes below. An offset tailpiece frees up knee room. Lever or sensor faucets finish it off.
