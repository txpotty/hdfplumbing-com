    What Goes Into a Water Heater Swap in Orlando, FL

    Conventional Tank Water Heater Replacement in Orlando

    Standard tank heaters remain the most common type, and they usually fail from age, rust or sediment. A leak at the base, loud popping or rusty hot water means it is time, and a like-for-like or resized swap usually takes two to four hours. You shower tonight.

    Rheem and AO Smith Water Heater Replacement in Orlando, FL

    Rheem and AO Smith make tanks in nearly every size, from 30-gallon short tanks to 80-gallon tall models. Both are solid, and the pick usually depends on what fits the space, the warranty length you want and what the supply house has in stock. We help you compare.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Orlando, FL

    Gas water heaters reheat quicker than electric tanks, and in Orlando, FL homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. Gas gets shut off and capped, the old tank drained and removed, and the new one set with a fresh connector, sediment trap and properly sloped vent. Then we leak test.

    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Orlando

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is a popular move, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. A whole-home tankless unit can draw four times the gas of a 40-gallon tank, so the gas line from the meter usually has to be upsized, and the old metal vent gets replaced with stainless or PVC run to the maker's specs. You see both numbers.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Orlando, FL

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters move heat from the surrounding air into the tank, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need space and airflow, often a room of about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they work best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. They also cool the room.

    Bradford White Water Heater Replacement in Orlando

    Bradford White sells only through the trade, so you will not find one at a big-box store. Their tanks have a good reputation for durability, and parts and warranty support run through the plumber who installed it, which makes it simple for us to handle a warranty claim for a homeowner down the road. Supply houses stock them.

    Upsizing Your Water Heater in Orlando

    Running out of hot water usually means the house outgrew the tank. A bigger tank or a higher first-hour rating handles more back-to-back showers, and sometimes a 50-gallon high-recovery unit matches a 75 in the same footprint. Recovery rate counts too.

    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Orlando

    Electric water heaters in Orlando usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. A bad element can be replaced. Tank leaks are the end, so we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to your existing circuit. Most swaps take a few hours.
