    How We Repair Basement Leaks in Orlando, FL

    Sump Pump Installation & Repair in Orlando

    A sump pump sits in a pit below the slab and kicks on when a float rises. Stuck floats, worn motors and failed check valves cause most of the service calls we run on sumps. We test yours with a bucket. A battery backup is worth adding where power drops in storms.

    Sump Pit and Pump Installation in Orlando

    Installing a new sump means breaking out a section of slab, setting a lined pit, and running the discharge out through the rim joist. We use a cast iron pump in most homes and a 1-1/2 inch discharge with a check valve. Discharge cannot go into the sewer in most places. It has to discharge outside, far enough from the foundation that it does not cycle back into the pit, often ten feet or more.

    Wall Leak Solutions in Orlando, FL

    Damp walls without a crack usually mean water moving through porous concrete or block. A simple foil test tells you whether it is seepage or condensation: tape a square of foil to the wall for a day and see which side gets wet. Condensation needs a dehumidifier. Real seepage calls for better drainage or an interior channel.

    Wall and Slab Seepage Repair in Orlando

    Seepage at the wall or the slab joint is usually surface water pooling against the foundation. Nearly all homes in Florida sit on concrete slabs over sandy soil, so leaks under the slab are a common and costly repair. Before anyone cuts the floor, we check the gutters, downspout extensions and the grade along that wall, because fixing those outside often stops seepage for a fraction of the cost. We always try the cheap fixes first.

    Emergency Basement Leak Repair in Orlando, FL

    A pipe that lets go in a basement can put an inch of water on the floor in under an hour. Shut the main valve first. Our plumber stops the leak and repairs the pipe, then restores water so the drying can start. We can point you to a drying company too.

    Interior Drainage Systems in Orlando

    Where digging outside is not practical, an interior drain system does the same job from inside. A strip of slab comes out along the walls, perforated pipe goes in on stone, and it all drains to a sump. The wall gets a dimpled membrane. Seepage runs down behind it and into the pipe.

    Clogged Drain Tile Repair in Orlando

    Footing drains clog with silt and roots over the years, and once they stop flowing the water table at the wall rises. A camera run from the sump inlet shows us where the tile is plugged. A jetter usually opens it. If the old clay tile has collapsed, it gets replaced with new perforated pipe.

    Urgent Basement Leak Service in Orlando, FL

    Basement leaks rarely wait for business hours. Tanks tend to split at night when nobody is watching. The on-call truck carries couplings, valves and a wet vac, so the first visit usually stops the leak and restores water to the rest of the house.

    Wet Basement Repair Near Me in Orlando, FL

    Speed matters with basement leaks because water spreads under carpet and up into drywall fast. Water can climb a foot up drywall in a single day. Calling a nearby plumber the morning you notice it can mean replacing a strip of baseboard instead of a whole wall of drywall plus insulation and carpet pad.

    Hiring someone local means a plumber who knows Orlando's housing stock. Older neighborhoods tend to have cast iron stacks and clay sewer laterals. Newer homes usually have PVC and sump pits that can fall behind in a wet season. We stock parts for both.
