    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Line Installation in Orlando

    The cost of water line installation in Orlando, FL varies widely depending on pipe material, PEX or copper, the length of the line, excavation depth and site access. A basic main water line replacement can range from $2,500 to $8,000, while new construction installations might be part of a larger plumbing package. We provide a clear estimate after a site assessment.

    In most communities the homeowner owns the line from the meter to the home, while the utility owns the meter and the line on the street side. We verify responsibility with your provider before work begins.

    Most residential main water line installations or replacements in Orlando can be completed within one to three days. This timeline can vary based on the length of the line, the complexity of the excavation, weather and permit processing times.

    Trenching does disturb a narrow path, but we backfill, compact and regrade when we finish. If the route crosses concrete or mature landscaping, we can talk through trenchless options.

    Pipe manufacturers offer long warranties on PEX and HDPE, and copper has a long track record. We also warranty our installation labor and will go over the specific terms with you before work starts.

    HDF Plumbing stands behind the quality of our work. We offer a comprehensive warranty on our water line installations, covering both parts and labor. Specific warranty details will be provided with your estimate, giving you peace of mind that your investment is covered.

    We focus on high-quality, reputable brands for our water line installations. For PEX, we often use Uponor Wirsbo or Viega PureFlow. For copper, we use Type L or Type K copper from trusted manufacturers. Our material selection focuses on durability and performance in your local conditions.
