    Floor-Mount and Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in Orlando, FL

    Wall-Mounted Toilet Installation in Orlando

    Wall-mounted toilets hang off a steel carrier bolted to the floor inside the wall. The tank sits behind an access plate. The carrier holds the whole load, so its feet get anchored to slab or framing and it gets plumbed before any wall finish goes on. Plan on a remodel, not a swap.

    Standard Toilet Installation in Orlando

    A standard floor-mount toilet is a two-hour job when the flange is sound. We shut off the stop, drain the tank and pull the old bowl. Once the old wax is scraped off, the flange gets checked for cracks, new stainless bolts go in, and the new bowl is lowered straight onto a fresh seal. The old one leaves with us.

    Wall-Hung Carrier Systems in Orlando

    In-wall carriers from Geberit and Grohe each mount a little differently, and the instructions matter. The frame height chart from the maker gets followed to the letter. Set the frame too low and the bowl ends up at child height, too high and nobody's feet touch the floor, and neither can be fixed once the wall is tiled. Measure twice here.

    Two-Piece Toilet Installation in Orlando

    Two-piece toilets come with the tank and bowl separate, joined by a rubber gasket and two or three bolts. The tank goes on before the bowl is set. Tightening the tank bolts evenly, side to side, matters more than how hard they go, because an uneven pull cracks the china or leaves a slow drip at one bolt. It should not wobble.

    Pressure-Assisted Toilet Installation in Orlando

    Pressure-assisted toilets have a sealed vessel inside the tank that uses line pressure to push the flush. Below about 20 psi, they flush poorly. We check static pressure at a hose bib before recommending one, and we warn customers about the noise, since the flush is loud enough to hear across the house. They rarely clog.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Orlando, FL

    Comfort height bowls stand about two inches taller than standard ones. Think about who will use it before you buy. Kids and shorter adults may not like their feet off the floor, but people with sore knees or a new hip usually love the extra height. Installation is the same as a standard bowl.

    Toilet Flange and Wax Ring Replacement in Orlando, FL

    The closet flange is the ring that ties the toilet to the drain, and it is the weak point on most old installs. On cast iron, the bolt slots rust out first. PVC and ABS flanges crack when someone overtightens the bolts, and a repair ring screwed over the top is often all it takes to restore a solid anchor. Seal goes on last.

    Customer-Supplied Toilet Installation in Orlando, FL

    Bought the toilet yourself? First, look at the rough-in printed on the carton. Before the old toilet comes out, we inspect the new one for shipping cracks, because cracked china goes back to the store, not onto your flange. We bring the seal and stainless bolts.
