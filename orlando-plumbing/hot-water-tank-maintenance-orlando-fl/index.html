    Anode Rod Inspection & Replacement in Orlando, FL

    The anode rod is your hot water tank's sacrificial protector against corrosion. Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving heavy scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. During our maintenance, we pull and inspect the anode rod.     Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Orlando

    The temperature and pressure relief valve is the part that keeps a tank from becoming dangerous. We lift the lever, confirm it opens and reseats and check the discharge line for proper material and termination. We also check your thermal expansion tank pressure with a gauge, since a waterlogged expansion tank lets pressure jump every heating cycle. High street pressure is common in many service areas, so we test static pressure at a hose bib too.

    Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in Orlando, FL

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber. We check the draft hood and vent connector for proper slope and secure joints. Dust and lint from garages can clog the flame arrestor screen on newer FVIR models, which causes the pilot to drop out. A clean burner gives a stable blue flame and lower gas bills.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Orlando

    A standard flush and inspection usually runs $125 to $250 depending on tank size and access. Anode replacement usually adds $75 to $200 in parts and labor, and powered anodes cost more.

    Common signs include reduced hot water supply, strange noises like rumbling or popping, rust-colored water, a sulfur or rotten egg smell, or water leaking from the tank. These indicate issues that maintenance can often resolve.

    Many manufacturers, including Rheem, A.O. Smith and Bradford White, expect regular maintenance and can reject claims for sediment damage. Keeping service records safeguards your warranty.

    The anode rod, also known as a sacrificial rod, is a metal rod inside your hot water tank designed to corrode before the tank itself. It protects the tank lining from rust and corrosion, which counts most when water carries a lot of minerals, and it lengthens the lifespan of your unit.

    Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving stubborn scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. Mineral content contributes to sediment buildup in hot water tanks, which reduces efficiency and can damage heating elements or tank linings over time. Regular flushing is particularly important in hard water areas.

    Neglecting maintenance can lead to poor operation, higher energy bills, premature tank failure from corrosion or sediment buildup, and potential safety hazards from a malfunctioning pressure relief valve. Regular service prevents these costly and dangerous issues.

    While some basic steps like draining a small amount of water can be done by a homeowner, professional hot water tank maintenance involves professional tools and experience to properly inspect electrical or gas components, anode rods and pressure relief valves. It is best left to a professional to ensure safety and code compliance.

    Regular, professional maintenance can frequently be a condition for keeping your manufacturer's warranty valid. Check your warranty documentation, but in most cases, proof of annual service can help keep your warranty intact.
