    Faucet Handle Repair & Replacement in Orlando, FL

    A loose, broken, or stripped faucet handle makes it hard to control water flow and temperature. Over time, daily use can wear down the internal mechanisms or cause the handle itself to break. We offer expert repair for various handle types, from single-handle mixers to separate hot and cold handles. If a repair isn't feasible, we can recommend and install a suitable replacement handle that matches your existing fixture and style, extending its lifespan.

    Leaking Faucet Handle and Base Repair in Orlando, FL

    Water seeping around the handle or pooling at the base points to failed O-rings or a loose mounting nut. If left alone, the leak can soak into the cabinet below and damage the sink base. We replace O-rings, tighten mounting hardware and reseal the base. We also check the deck for water stains.

    Low Water Pressure Faucet Fix in Orlando, FL

    Experiencing weak water flow from your faucet? This could be due to mineral buildup in the aerator, a faulty cartridge, or issues with your home's main water line. Around Orlando, flushing the water heater yearly and descaling tankless units helps keep mineral buildup under control. Our technicians thoroughly inspect the faucet and associated plumbing to pinpoint the cause. We then clean or replace the necessary components to restore full water pressure, ensuring a steady and strong flow for daily use.

    Compression Faucet Repair in Orlando

    Compression faucets, often found in older Orlando homes, are known for their separate hot and cold handles. When these faucets leak, it's typically due to worn-out rubber washers or packing. Our team is skilled in disassembling these classic fixtures, replacing the necessary seals and washers, and reassembling them to stop leaks for good. We respect the character of older homes and make sure these repairs are done carefully.

    Dripping Faucet Solutions in Orlando
