    Drain Jetting for Hard Water Scale in Orlando

    Hard water leaves mineral deposits inside drain pipes, especially in older galvanized and cast iron. That rough scale traps hair, grease and soap, leading to frequent clogs. A jetting nozzle designed for scale can break up and wash away much of this buildup. We use the camera to judge how much wall remains before and after. Clean pipe drains better and clogs less frequently.

    Mineral Scale and Sludge Removal in Orlando

    Around Orlando, flushing the water heater yearly and descaling tankless units helps keep scale buildup under control. Over time, minerals in the water can deposit as hard scale on the inside of pipes, narrowing the passage and catching debris. Sludge from detergents, soaps, and organic matter also contributes to blockages. Our drain jetting service scrubs off these hard mineral deposits and thick sludge layers, restoring the full diameter of your pipes and improving drainage in your Orlando home or business.

    Kitchen Drain Jetting in Orlando

    Kitchen lines are where grease, food particles and dish soap collect and harden. Over time the pipe narrows until the sink drains slowly or backs up into the other basin. We access the line through a cleanout or the trap arm and feed a small jetting hose toward the main. Rear-facing jets scrub the walls while the stream flushes residue away. Your sink and dishwasher drain at full speed once more.

    Laundry Drain Jetting in Orlando, FL

    Washing machines discharge lint, fabric softener residue and fine sand or grit. These build into a gray sludge that slows the standpipe and overflows during the spin cycle. Jetting clears the standpipe and branch line completely. We also check the trap, vent and drain size to make sure they can handle the washer flow. A lint filter on the discharge hose helps keep the line clear.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Jetting in Orlando

    Drain jetting for an interior branch line in Orlando typically costs $250 to $550. Commercial kitchen lines and multiple drains may cost more and are quoted in person.

    We use professional compact jetters from established drain equipment makers, paired with nozzles designed for grease, scale and debris.

    Drain snaking typically involves pushing an auger through a clog to create a small opening for water to pass. Drain jetting, or hydro jetting, uses high-pressure water to scour the entire pipe wall, removing buildup and leaving the pipe much cleaner. Jetting is better for stubborn blockages and preventative maintenance.

    For most homes, preventative drain jetting every 1 to 2 years is enough, especially if you experience frequent minor clogs. Businesses, particularly those in food service, may benefit from quarterly or semi-annual jetting to prevent grease buildup. We can recommend a schedule based on your specific needs.

    A typical residential drain jetting service can take anywhere from 1 to 3 hours, depending on the complexity and location of the clog. More severe blockages or main sewer line jetting may require additional time for preparation and thorough cleaning.

    Key signs include frequently recurring clogs, multiple slow-draining fixtures, gurgling sounds from drains or toilets, unpleasant odors coming from drains, or sewage backups. These symptoms often point to a significant blockage that snaking alone cannot resolve.

    While drain jetting focuses on clearing existing pipes, we work with trusted brands for replacement parts if needed, such as Charlotte Pipe for PVC, Cast Iron Soil Pipe Institute (CISPI) standards for cast iron, and several manufacturers for cleanout assemblies. Our focus is on the integrity of your existing drain system.
