    Frequently Asked Questions About Grease Trap Installation in Orlando

    Small indoor hydromechanical traps typically cost $1,500 to $5,000 installed. Large outdoor gravity interceptors often run $8,000 to $30,000 or more with excavation, concrete and site restoration. We provide a written estimate after a site visit.

    We install a variety of reputable grease trap brands, including Zurn, Big Dipper, Schier, and Rockford Separators. Our plumbers are familiar with the specifications and installation requirements of numerous manufacturers, ensuring you get a high-quality, durable system tailored to your needs.

    We recommend and install high-quality, durable grease trap brands such as Zurn, Big Dipper, Schier, and Rockford Separators. These manufacturers are known for their reliability and compliance with industry standards, offering various solutions to meet diverse commercial kitchen needs.

    The main purpose of a grease trap is to intercept fats, oils, and greases (FOG) from commercial kitchen wastewater before they enter the public sewer system. This prevents pipe blockages, protects municipal infrastructure, and helps businesses comply with environmental regulations.

    While the terms are often used interchangeably, 'grease trap' usually refers to smaller, indoor hydro-mechanical units, often found under sinks. 'Grease interceptor' usually refers to larger, outdoor gravity-fed units with higher capacities. Both serve the same purpose of separating FOG from wastewater.

    An indoor under-sink trap can often be installed in a day. Outdoor interceptors typically take several days to a week or more, depending on excavation, concrete work and inspection scheduling.

    The timeline for grease trap installation depends on the project's scope. A simple under-sink unit might take a few hours. Larger outdoor gravity interceptors, which involve excavation and more extensive plumbing, can take anywhere from one to several days. We always aim to limit disruption to your business operations.

    Grease trap installation is a complex commercial plumbing task that requires specialized knowledge, tools, and adherence to strict local codes. Attempting a DIY installation can lead to costly errors, code violations, and potential health hazards. It is always best to hire licensed and experienced plumbers like HDF Plumbing.
