    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Orlando, FL

    For many sewer line issues, especially those caused by tree roots or pipe corrosion, trenchless repair offers a less invasive solution. This method, which includes pipe bursting or pipe lining, requires minimal excavation, saving your yard and landscaping from major disruption. We use specialized equipment to either pull a new pipe through the old one or insert a resin-coated liner that cures in place, creating a new, durable pipe inside the old one. It's an effective and popular choice for protecting property appearance in Orlando.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Orlando

    When trenchless repair isn't feasible due to severe damage or specific pipe configurations, traditional dig-and-replace methods are necessary. This involves excavating a trench to access and replace the damaged section of the sewer line. While more intrusive, it is sometimes the only way to address badly collapsed pipes, significant offsets or certain pipe materials common in older Florida properties. Our team ensures careful excavation and efficient replacement, minimizing the impact on your property in Orlando.

    Sewer Line Backups & Blockages in Orlando

    Sewer backups are usually caused by severe blockages from grease buildup, foreign objects or extensive tree root intrusion. Our team uses HD sewer camera inspections to identify the exact cause and location of the blockage. We then use hydro-jetting or mechanical snaking to clear the obstruction, restoring proper flow to your system and preventing further damage. This approach is very effective for the stubborn clogs often found in older Florida homes.

    Sewer Vent Pipe Repair in Orlando, FL

    While often overlooked, the sewer vent pipe is essential for proper drainage and preventing sewer gas buildup. Issues with vent pipes, such as blockages from animal nests or damage from storms, can lead to slow drains, gurgling noises and foul odors. HDF Plumbing diagnoses and repairs vent pipe problems, making sure your entire drainage system works correctly and safely and protecting good indoor air quality.

    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Orlando

    Tree roots are a common culprit for sewer line damage in Florida, drawn to the moisture and nutrients inside pipes. They can work into tiny cracks, expand and eventually crush or clog the line. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing root intrusion using camera technology. We can remove roots with specialized cutting tools and recommend preventive measures or durable pipe relining solutions to prevent future invasions, protecting your home's foundation.

    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in Orlando

    Before any repair, we use advanced video camera inspection to accurately locate and assess sewer line damage. This involves inserting a flexible rod with a camera into your sewer line, allowing us to see blockages, cracks, root intrusion or pipe collapses in real time. This no-dig method prevents unnecessary digging and helps us provide a precise diagnosis and repair plan.
