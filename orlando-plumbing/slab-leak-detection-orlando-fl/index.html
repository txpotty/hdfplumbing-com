    Water and Sewer Line Slab Leaks in Orlando, FL

    Slab leaks can affect both freshwater supply lines and sewer drain pipes. Our detection services are equipped to identify both types of leaks, which present different challenges. Sewer line leaks often involve foul odors and slow drainage, while water line leaks can appear as wet spots or high water bills in your Orlando, FL home. We have specific methods for each.

    Repiping After Multiple Slab Leaks in Orlando, FL

    If your home has had two or more slab leaks, the remaining pipe is likely corroded too. Repiping replaces all supply lines with new PEX or copper run through the walls and attic. It is a bigger project, but it ends the cycle of repeat slab leaks. We can typically complete a full repipe in two to four days.

    Emergency Slab Leak Detection in Orlando

    A sudden drop in water pressure, visible dampness or the sound of running water can signal an emergency slab leak. HDF Plumbing offers emergency detection services in Orlando to address urgent situations quickly. We focus on rapid response to limit water damage and prevent further structural compromise to your property.

    Non-Invasive Leak Detection in Orlando, FL

    Our primary goal is to locate your slab leak with the least amount of disruption possible. We favor non-invasive techniques like acoustic and thermal imaging before considering any intrusive methods. This approach saves your home in Orlando, FL from unnecessary damage, preserves your landscaping and lowers the overall cost and time of the repair process.

    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Orlando, FL

    Thermal imaging cameras allow us to visualize temperature differences on surfaces, which can indicate a hidden water leak. As water escapes a hot water line, it creates a clear thermal signature on the concrete slab. This technique is particularly useful for hot water line leaks and helps us narrow down the search area significantly without any physical disruption to your property in Orlando.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Orlando, FL

    Cold water slab leaks do not create warm spots, so they are harder to see. They often show up as a running water meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards. We isolate the cold side and use acoustic equipment to listen for the leak through the slab. Line tracers map the pipe route so we know exactly where to listen.

    Electronic Leak Detection in Orlando

    For hard-to-find leaks, our electronic leak detection equipment sends a signal through your pipes and measures changes that indicate a breach. This technology allows us to locate leaks in many pipe materials, including copper, PEX and PVC, commonly found in Orlando homes. It helps prevent exploratory digging, saving you time and money.

    Slab Leak Repair Through Direct Access in Orlando, FL

    When a single leak is found on otherwise healthy pipe, direct access can be the most economical repair. We cut a small opening in the floor at the marked spot, repair the pipe and pressure test it. Then we patch the concrete and leave the area ready for flooring. Accurate detection keeps that opening as small as it can be.
