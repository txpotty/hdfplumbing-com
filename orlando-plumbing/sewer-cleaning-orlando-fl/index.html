    Dealing with Grease Buildup in Orlando, FL

    Grease hardens as it cools. It shrinks the opening a little every week. Kitchen grease coats the sewer line the same way plaque coats an artery, and over a few years the opening can close to the size of a finger, which is when drains start to back up, and jetting is the right fix.

    Scheduled Sewer Cleaning in Orlando, FL

    History predicts the next backup. We set a cleaning interval. A line that has backed up before can be cleaned on a set schedule, often every year or two, so it never stops again without warning.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Orlando

    Older properties may still have clay or Orangeburg pipe. Both have weak spots. Clay joints invite roots, and Orangeburg, a tar-paper pipe from the mid-1900s, flattens under the weight of the soil over time, so we clean these lines carefully and map the joints and soft spots on camera.

    Preventative Sewer Maintenance in Orlando

    Roots come back after cutting. Usually within a year or so. Putting a root-prone main on a yearly cleaning schedule keeps the growth small enough that it never stops the line, and it costs far less than an after-hours emergency call.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Orlando

    Jetting cleans the whole pipe wall. Cabling just opens a hole. Water at 3,000 to 4,000 PSI strips grease, scale and fine roots off the inside of the line and flushes it all toward the street, which leaves the pipe close to its original diameter.

    Apartment and Strip Center Sewers in Orlando, FL

    Shared laterals carry heavy loads. Many tenants use a single line. Scheduled jetting between grease interceptor pump-outs keeps solids from building up, and we can work after hours so tenants and customers are not bothered by the noise or the truck, with a written report and a video file on request.

    Emergency Sewer Backup Cleaning in Orlando

    Sewage coming up through a shower or floor drain? Shut off the washer and dishwasher. A tech arrives ready to clear the main and start cleanup, with the goal of restoring flow before anything else, then we disinfect the area and take photos for an insurance claim.

    After-Hours Sewer Backups in Orlando

    Backups happen at bad times. We answer anyway. The after-hours rate is quoted on the phone before we dispatch, and the tech arrives ready to clear the main, set up a wet vac and photograph the damage for your insurance company.
