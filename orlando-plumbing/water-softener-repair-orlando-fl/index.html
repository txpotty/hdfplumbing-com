    Control Valve Rebuild in Orlando

    Service, backwash, brine and rinse are the four cycles the control valve directs every time the softener regenerates, usually in the middle of the night. Hard water and chlorine wear out the seals, spacers and pistons. Leaks at the valve or water running to the drain are common signs. The valve gets rebuilt with the kit made for it.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Repair in Orlando

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. The tank looks full, but the softener cannot make brine. We break the bridge, clean out the mushy salt and check the float and brine well. Cracked tanks get replaced.

    Drips around fittings waste water. We trace it to the seal, O-ring, hose or internal part that failed. We replace the failed part with silicone-lubricated O-rings rated for potable water in your Orlando, FL home and pressure test the connection.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Orlando, FL

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. Once it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying, and the softener regenerates with plain water that does nothing to the resin. The injector and screen get cleaned or replaced, and the brine line gets checked.
