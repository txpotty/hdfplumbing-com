    Frequently Asked Questions About Bathroom Plumbing in Orlando

    The cost of bathroom plumbing in Orlando, FL varies widely depending on the specific service.     HDF Plumbing services and installs all major brands of bathroom fixtures. This includes well-known names like Kohler, American Standard, Moen, Delta, TOTO, and Sterling. We can also work with less common or specialty brands and find the right parts for your specific needs.

    Fixture repairs and like-for-like replacements usually do not need a permit. Moving drains, adding fixtures or remodeling often does. We handle permits when required.

    A standard bathroom faucet installation typically takes 1 to 3 hours, assuming no complications. Factors like existing plumbing condition, fixture type, and accessibility can affect the total time. Our plumbers work efficiently to limit disruption.

    If the toilet is cracked, uses more than 1.6 gallons per flush or needs frequent repairs, replacement is often better. A new WaterSense toilet can reduce your water bill.

    To prevent clogs, avoid flushing anything but toilet paper down the toilet. For sinks and showers, use drain strainers to catch hair and soap scum.     Low water pressure can stem from several issues, including mineral buildup in aerators or showerheads, a failing pressure regulator, leaks in the water line, or issues with your water heater. Our plumbers can diagnose the exact cause.
