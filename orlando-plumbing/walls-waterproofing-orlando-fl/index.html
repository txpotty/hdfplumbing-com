    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Orlando

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and usually add a dehumidifier to hold humidity under 60 percent. Salt air in coastal parts of Florida corrodes outdoor fittings and exterior metal pipe faster than in inland areas. Wood rot slows to a stop. Drier air under the floor also cuts down on the musty smell in the house.

    Basement Wall Sealing in Orlando, FL

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Salt air in coastal parts of Florida corrodes outdoor fittings and exterior metal pipe faster than in inland areas. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then coat the face with a masonry waterproofing product that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. The musty smell usually fades within weeks. The weep holes empty into interior drain tile when there is one.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Orlando

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. It is the most reliable fix for a leaking basement, because the water never touches the block or concrete. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing to carry the water away from the wall. Nearly all homes in Florida sit on concrete slabs over sandy soil, so leaks under the slab are a common and costly repair. We backfill after the drain is tested.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Orlando, FL

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries. The usual cause is a sprinkler head aimed at the house instead of the bed, or soil graded toward the foundation. We fix the water first. After that the wall gets cleaned, dried and coated with a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Orlando

    Bare dirt in a crawl space releases gallons of water vapor a day into the air under your floor. A 10 mil or heavier polyethylene barrier, overlapped a foot at every seam and taped, cuts that off and keeps the joists and subfloor dry. We run it up the foundation walls and hold it in place with termination strip. Cheap fix, big difference.

    Commercial Walls Waterproofing in Orlando

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and mechanical rooms in Orlando take on water the same way houses do, just with more at stake. A wet electrical room can shut a business down. We schedule around operating hours, work with property managers on access, and meet the waterproofing specs in the building plans for below-grade walls. Downtime stays short.

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Orlando

    Hairline cracks in poured concrete walls leak more water than their size suggests. We inject them with low-pressure polyurethane foam, which expands to fill the crack from the inside face to the soil side, or with epoxy when the crack also needs structural bonding. Horizontal cracks are different. A horizontal crack often signals soil pressure pushing the wall in, so we check for movement with a level before we seal anything. Most vertical cracks get sealed in one visit.

    Cost-Effective Waterproofing Solutions in Orlando, FL

We start with the cheapest fix that will actually work. Every option comes written out with its price.
