    Finding and Repairing Foundation Leaks in Orlando, FL

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Repair in Orlando

    Many newer homes sit on post-tension slabs with steel cables under thousands of pounds of tension. Cutting one is dangerous. Before we open the floor, we scan with ground-penetrating radar to map the cables, and if the leak sits too close to a tendon, rerouting the line is the safer call.

    Advanced Leak Detection Technology in Orlando, FL

    A ground microphone picks up the hiss of water leaving a pipe under four inches of concrete. It does most of the work. HDF Plumbing pairs it with a line tracer that follows the pipe route and a thermal camera that shows hot water spreading, so in most homes we can mark the leak within a foot or two, in Orlando, FL or anywhere else.

    Slab Leak Detection and Repair in Orlando

    Slab leaks show up quietly. Sometimes all you get is a warm spot on the tile or a bill that creeps up. HDF Plumbing isolates the hot and cold sides, then uses acoustic and infrared gear to pin down the break under your concrete. For Orlando homes, we choose between a spot repair, an attic reroute and a tunnel dug from outside, depending on where the leak sits and how old the pipe is.

    Drain Line Leak Under Foundation in Orlando, FL

    Drain leaks under a slab are harder to find. Drains carry no pressure. A hydrostatic test shows whether the line holds water, and a camera run shows us the cracked fitting, offset joint or roots causing it. Fixes include tunneling, slab cuts or a pipe liner, and we lay out the trade-offs.

    PEX and PVC Foundation Leak Repair in Orlando

    Newer houses use PEX or PVC under the slab. It still leaks. Most failures we find are at a fitting that was crimped short or a joint that pulled apart when the soil moved, not in the pipe wall itself. HDF Plumbing replaces the fitting with a proper crimp, expansion or solvent-weld connection so the line under your Orlando foundation is sound again.

    Crawl Space Foundation Leak Repair in Orlando, FL

    Crawl spaces hide leaks from supply lines, drains and AC condensate lines. Sagging, wet insulation is a common clue. Water sitting under the house rots sill plates and floor joists and feeds mold on the subfloor, so we find the source, fix the pipe and check whether the vapor barrier needs replacing. A sump pump may make sense in a Orlando, FL crawl space that floods from outside.

    Tunneling for Foundation Leaks in Orlando, FL

    Tunneling means digging under the slab from outside the house. Your flooring stays put. We dig a pit beside the foundation and hand-tunnel to the leak, which works well when the break sits under cabinets, a tiled shower or hardwood you do not want torn up. HDF Plumbing backfills and compacts the tunnel when the Orlando, FL repair is done.

    Foundation Leak Detection in Orlando

    Locating comes before anything else. First we isolate hot from cold. Each side gets its own pressure test, and once we know which side is losing water, we follow the pipe route with a tracer and listen along it with the ground mic until the sound peaks. Thermal imaging helps confirm hot water leaks by showing warm spots on the floor.
