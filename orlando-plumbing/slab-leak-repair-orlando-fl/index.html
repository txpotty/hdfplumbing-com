    Slab Leak Detection and Repair in Orlando, FL

    Slab Leak Detection in Orlando

    The first test is the water meter. We shut off everything first. If the meter still turns, we close the water heater inlet and pressure test hot and cold separately, then walk the losing line with a ground mic until the hiss is loudest. Thermal imaging shows hot water leaks as warm areas on the floor.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Orlando

    Cold leaks don't make warm spots. Watch the bill instead. Look for a rising bill, a meter that spins with nothing on or damp carpet, since a cold leak wets the soil as much as a hot one. Your Orlando home is no exception. We isolate the cold line and fix it or run a new one.

    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Orlando, FL

    Direct repair is often the most practical fix for a single leak. A small section of slab gets cut. With the pipe exposed, the damaged length is cut out, new pipe is joined in, and the line is pressure tested before the soil goes back in and gets compacted in layers. The concrete is patched flush with the floor.

    Thermal and Acoustic Tools in Orlando

    A thermal camera shows the warm streak a hot water leak leaves on the floor. Cold leaks need the mic. On cold lines we rely on an acoustic sensor that listens through the slab, marking the point where the sound is loudest. Both tools ride on every Orlando truck.

    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Orlando

    Old galvanized or thin copper is hard to patch reliably. Rerouting skips it. The bad run stays in the ground unused while a new line goes overhead or in the walls, where it can be reached later. Orlando homes with original plumbing see this often.

    Emergency Slab Leak Repair in Orlando, FL

    A large slab leak can push water up through flooring or into walls quickly. Shut off the main. On the phone, we help you close the shutoff, then a technician arrives to isolate the leaking line so the rest of the house has water while we plan the permanent repair.

    Direct Slab Penetration Repair in Orlando, FL

    Cutting through the slab is the shortest path to a localized leak. We wet-cut to keep dust down. After repairing the pipe, we compact the fill and patch the opening so the floor carries load like it did before, and the whole job follows plumbing code from cut to cure. Florida rules apply throughout. Your Orlando home stays clean at the end.
