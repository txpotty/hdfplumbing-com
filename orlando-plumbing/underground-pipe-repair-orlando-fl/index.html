    Underground Water Line Repair in Orlando, FL

    A leaking water service runs at full street pressure around the clock. The meter never stops. Low pressure, a wet spot that never dries and a meter that turns with everything off are the usual clues, so we verify it with a pressure test and then pinpoint it acoustically. Repairs use approved fittings.

    Storm Drain and Lateral Repair in Orlando, FL

    Storm laterals carry roof and yard water away from the foundation. When they fail, water pools. We jet the line, camera it for crushed or separated sections and repair broken runs so runoff gets carried off instead of sitting next to the house. We check the discharge end.

    Water Main Underground Repair in Orlando

    Your water main is the pipe between the meter and the house. You own that part. When it breaks, we locate the leak without digging, expose the pipe and splice in new PEX or copper with fittings approved for direct burial, then pressure test the repair. Old galvanized usually needs full replacement.

    Buried Sewer Lateral Repair in Orlando, FL

    Buried laterals crack from roots, settling and age. Video locates the break. We excavate at the mark, cut out the damaged length and replace it with Schedule 40 PVC, joining it to the existing pipe with shielded couplings sized for the outside diameter of each material. Fall gets checked before we close it.

    HOA and Multifamily Underground Repair in Orlando

    HOA common areas and apartment complexes have long buried runs under parking and sidewalks. Tenants still need to get in. We locate carefully to avoid unnecessary concrete cutting, schedule work around residents, and document every repair with photos and test results that property managers can hand to the board or the insurer. We work with your schedule.

    Sewer Line Underground Repair in Orlando, FL

    Roots and settling soil cause most buried sewer failures. Age does the rest. We offer open trench repair, pipe bursting and cured-in-place lining, picking the method after the video shows the depth and extent of the damage. All work meets Florida plumbing code.

    Irrigation and Yard Line Repair in Orlando

    Sprinkler supply lines sit shallow and break easily. Landscapers hit them a lot. A broken irrigation main can run up a water bill fast in the growing season, so we repair PVC and poly mains, replace failed backflow connections and fix the supply line to the valve manifold. We can reroute lines away from trees.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Orlando, FL

    Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one while breaking the old pipe outward. The new line takes its place. A bursting head on a cable splits clay, cast iron or brittle plastic, while a continuous HDPE pipe is pulled in behind at the same diameter or bigger. It works on badly damaged lines. Lining cannot do that.
