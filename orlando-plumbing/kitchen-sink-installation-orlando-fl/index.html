    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Orlando

    Undermount sinks need strong clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. We mount the bowl, seal the rim with silicone, and support it while the adhesive sets. Then we install strainers, traps and supply lines. A proper undermount install prevents gaps and leaks along the counter edge.

    Farmhouse Sink Installation in Orlando, FL

    Farmhouse or apron-front sinks add a striking look to any kitchen, but their installation requires specific expertise due to their weight and unique mounting requirements. HDF Plumbing provides careful farmhouse sink installation, ensuring the sink is adequately supported within your cabinetry and properly aligned with your countertop. We carefully manage the plumbing connections, which can differ from standard sinks, to prevent leaks and ensure efficient drainage. This focused service gives you a beautiful and functional focal point for your kitchen.

    Top-mount Sink Installation in Orlando

    Top-mount, or drop-in, sinks are a common and straightforward option for many Florida homes. While simpler to install, proper sealing and alignment are still crucial to prevent water damage and ensure stability. Our plumbers in Orlando efficiently install top-mount sinks, applying durable sealant around the rim to create a watertight barrier. We make sure the sink is securely fastened and all plumbing connections, including the faucet and drain, are properly tightened and tested for leaks.

    Under-mount Sink Installation in Orlando, FL

    Under-mount sinks provide a sleek, seamless look, making countertop cleaning easier. Their installation is more complex than top-mount sinks, requiring precise measurements and strong adhesive or clips for secure attachment to the underside of the counter. HDF Plumbing excels in under-mount sink installation, ensuring a strong bond and a perfectly sealed edge to prevent water infiltration. We ensure the cutout is smooth and the sink is level for optimal drainage.

    Stainless Steel Sink Installation in Orlando

    Stainless steel sinks are a popular choice for their durability and ease of cleaning, especially in busy kitchens.     Workstation Sink Installation in Orlando, FL

    Workstation sinks include ledges for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks. They are usually deep, single-bowl designs with offset drains. We position the drain to fit your cabinet and disposal and confirm the faucet reach. These sinks are a common pick in modern kitchen remodels.

    Composite Sink Installation in Orlando

    Composite sinks, made from materials like granite or quartz composites, offer excellent durability and a wide range of colors to match your kitchen decor.
