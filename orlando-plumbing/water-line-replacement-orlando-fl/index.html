    Copper Water Line Installation in Orlando

    PEX Water Line Installation in Orlando, FL

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a modern, flexible, and increasingly popular choice for water line replacement. It resists corrosion, scaling, and chlorine, making it highly durable for most water conditions. Frozen pipes are rare in most of Florida, and many homes have outdoor plumbing that would never survive a northern winter. PEX can expand and contract without bursting, which is a significant advantage when a cold snap hits Orlando. Its flexibility allows for fewer fittings and quicker installation.

    Polybutylene Water Line Replacement in Orlando, FL

    Polybutylene pipe was used in some homes built in the 1980s and 1990s. It can become brittle and fail with no warning. Many insurers flag it as a risk. We replace polybutylene service lines with durable materials. This reduces the chance of sudden leaks.

We also replace old gate valves with full-port ball valves at the house. This makes it easier to shut off water in an emergency. These upgrades protect your plumbing system.
