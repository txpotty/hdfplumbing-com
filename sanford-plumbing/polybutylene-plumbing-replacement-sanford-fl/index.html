    Understanding Polybutylene Failure in Sanford

    Polybutylene's inherent weakness stems from its chemical reaction with oxidants like chlorine, commonly used in municipal water treatment. Over time, this reaction causes the pipes and their plastic fittings to become brittle, leading to hairline cracks and eventual rupture. Around Sanford, flushing the water heater yearly and descaling tankless units helps keep scale buildup under control. Hard water can also add stress to pipe that is already weakened.
