    Tankless Water Heater Repair in Sanford

    Scale is the main enemy of a tankless heater. It coats the heat exchanger and trips the unit into an error code. We descale with a pump and approved solution, clean the inlet filter and check the igniter and flow sensor while the unit is open. All major brands.

    Gas Burner and Venting Repair in Sanford, FL

    Combustion is the part of a gas heater that matters most for safety. Yellow flame tips or soot at the draft hood mean the burner is not getting enough air to burn cleanly. The burner gets cleaned, and draft gets tested at the hood. Backdrafting is dangerous.

    Pilot Repair in Sanford

    A pilot that lights and then dies when you let go of the knob almost always means a weak thermocouple. Sometimes the cause is a clogged flame arrestor screen starving the burner of air. The thermocouple gets a millivolt test. A good reading points us to the gas valve instead.

    Electric Water Heater Element Repair in Sanford

    Lukewarm water or hot water that runs out in five minutes usually means a burned-out element or a stuck thermostat. Each element gets an ohm reading. Readings tell us whether an element is open or shorted, which explains the breaker trips. Common elements and thermostats are stocked.

    Sediment Flush and Noise Repair in Sanford

    Popping, rumbling or crackling usually means a thick layer of sediment is trapping water and overheating the steel at the tank bottom. We drain the tank, break up scale with repeated fills and check the drain valve for blockage. Flushing restores capacity. On hard water, plan on one flush a year.

    Burst tanks and relief valves stuck open cannot wait until Monday. HDF Plumbing answers emergency calls in Sanford, FL and puts these at the top of the list. The plumber shuts off the fuel and the cold supply, then drains the tank to stop the water. Repair or replace comes next.
