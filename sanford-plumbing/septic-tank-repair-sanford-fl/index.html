    Leach Field Repair in Sanford, FL

    Soggy ground, strong odors or lush grass over the field can point to a struggling disposal area. Causes include clogged distribution pipes, a crushed distribution box or soil sealed by solids.     Effluent Pump Repair in Sanford, FL

    Homes with uphill or distant fields often use an effluent pump in a pump tank. Failed pumps, stuck floats or tripped breakers trigger high water alarms. We test the pump, float switches and control panel and replace failed parts. A working alarm gives you warning before sewage backs up.

    Septic Tank Leak Repair in Sanford

    Septic tank leaks can lead to foul odors, soggy spots in your yard and potential groundwater contamination. Our technicians use dye testing and camera inspections to locate the source of the leak, whether it is a crack in the tank or a failed pipe connection. We provide durable repair solutions using purpose-made sealants and patching materials designed for the harsh environment of a septic tank in Sanford, ensuring a watertight seal and preventing future issues.

    Emergency Septic Repair in Sanford

    Septic Repair Cost Comparison in Sanford, FL

    Septic Baffle and Tee Repair in Sanford

    Baffles and tees keep scum and solids in the tank so they do not flow out into the leach field. Concrete baffles crumble from hydrogen sulfide gas, and plastic tees can break off. When they fail, solids clog the field quickly. We replace damaged baffles with durable PVC tees and add an effluent filter where it makes sense.
