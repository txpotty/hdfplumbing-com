    Wall-Hung Toilet Replacement in Sanford

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, so replacing one means checking the carrier bolts, the gasket on the outlet and the condition of the wall finish around it. The carrier itself sometimes needs new bolts or a coupling. The new bowl gets matched to the carrier already in the wall whenever possible, which avoids opening the wall.

    Urgent Toilet Replacement in Sanford, FL

    Late-night failures happen, usually a tank that splits along a seam or a bowl that cracks at the base and keeps seeping no matter how many towels go down. Closing the angle stop stops the water. A replacement can usually go in first thing the next morning, and we will tell you if the floor needs to dry before we set the new one.

    Toilet Brands We Replace in Sanford

    Brand matters less than model, because every major maker sells a great toilet and a mediocre one at nearly the same price. We look at the flush valve size and MaP score.     Emergency Toilet Replacement in Sanford

    Replacing Pressure-Assisted Toilets in Sanford, FL

    Pressure-assisted toilets use a sealed vessel in the tank that stores air under line pressure and pushes water through the bowl hard, which helps in houses with long, low-slope drain runs. They are noticeably louder than gravity toilets. When we replace one, we pick a vessel brand with parts that are easy to get later, and we check that house pressure is in the range the unit needs.

    Rental and Multi-Unit Toilet Replacement in Sanford, FL

    Landlords with several units do better standardizing on one toilet model with common parts, because the next flapper or fill valve is then the same part in every unit. We can replace several toilets in one visit. Tenants keep a working bathroom. Entry gets coordinated with each tenant directly, so you do not have to be there.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Sanford

    Chair height toilets put the seat about 17 to 19 inches off the floor, roughly two inches higher than a standard bowl, and that makes a real difference for knees and hips. No plumbing changes are needed. Many people pair it with an elongated bowl and a soft-close seat, and the swap takes the same couple of hours as any other.

    Water-Saving Toilet Upgrades in Sanford, FL

    Older toilets are one of the largest indoor water users in a house, and replacing a 3.5 gallon model with a 1.28 gallon one cuts toilet water use by more than half. Dual-flush toilets add a lighter flush for liquids. We recommend models with high MaP scores so the savings do not come with double flushing.
