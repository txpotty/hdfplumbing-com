    Tunneling Slab Leak Repair in Sanford, FL

    PEX Repiping for Slab Leaks in Sanford, FL

    Two or three slab leaks in a few years point to the pipe, not bad luck. A repipe may be cheaper long term. PEX bends with the structure, won't corrode and runs overhead, so a whole-house repipe leaves nothing under the slab. It suits many Sanford homes.

    Slab Leak Detection in Sanford

    The first test is the water meter. Every fixture gets shut off. A meter that keeps turning tells us to isolate hot from cold, test each side and walk the losing line with a ground mic. Thermal imaging shows hot water leaks as warm areas on the floor.

    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Sanford

    Old galvanized or thin copper is hard to patch reliably. Rerouting skips it. We abandon the leaking run in place and install new pipe through walls or the attic, so the problem section is gone from service and the water travels through fresh line that you can reach later. Sanford homes with original plumbing are good candidates.

    Commercial Slab Leak Repair in Sanford, FL

    Commercial slabs in Sanford carry longer pipe runs and more branches. Locating takes longer.     Spot Repair Through the Slab in Sanford, FL

    Spot repairs fit leaks in otherwise good pipe. One cut, one fix. We open the floor over the leak, dig to the pipe, replace the bad section and pressure test it, then compact the trench and patch the concrete so your flooring contractor can finish the surface.

    Thermal and Acoustic Tools in Sanford

    A thermal camera shows the warm streak a hot water leak leaves on the floor. Cold lines show no heat.
