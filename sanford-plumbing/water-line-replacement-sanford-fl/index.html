    Damage Assessment Before Replacement in Sanford

    We start at the meter. If the little dial on the meter is spinning with every fixture off, water is leaving somewhere.     Polybutylene Water Line Replacement in Sanford, FL

    Polybutylene is the gray or blue plastic pipe installed from roughly the late 1970s to the mid 1990s. Chlorine in the water breaks it down from the inside. The failures tend to be sudden splits rather than slow drips, and some home insurers refuse to cover homes that still have it. Worth replacing.

    Copper Water Line Installation in Sanford

    Type K copper is the thick-wall grade made for burial. Street pressure does not bother it. We bed it in sand, keep it away from sharp rock, and sleeve it where it passes through a foundation wall, because concrete and copper rubbing together for twenty years eventually wear a hole. PEX is cheaper.

    PEX Service Lines for Long Runs in Sanford

    PEX comes in coils up to several hundred feet. That makes it the easy pick when the meter sits out at the road and the house is set far back, because one coil covers the whole run without a joint. Fewer joints underground means fewer places a leak can start, and it is the joints that usually fail first.

    Water Line Upgrades and Code Compliance in Sanford, FL

When we replace the line we size it to your fixture count, often stepping up to 1-inch pipe, and bring the shutoff, depth and backflow pieces up to what code asks for today. Better pressure upstairs.
