    Pressure Relief Valve Testing in Pompano Beach

    A properly working temperature and pressure relief (T&P) valve is a crucial safety device for any hot water tank. Our technicians will test this valve to ensure it's not stuck or leaking, preventing dangerous pressure buildup within the tank. We verify it opens and closes correctly, and if it fails, we will suggest immediate replacement to keep your Pompano Beach home safe and compliant with plumbing codes.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Pompano Beach, FL

    The anode rod, often called the sacrificial rod, is essential for preventing corrosion inside your hot water tank. Water chemistry varies from one neighborhood to the next, and it changes the rod's lifespan. Our technicians inspect the anode rod for wear and recommend replacement if it's badly corroded, typically every 3 to 5 years. Replacing a worn anode rod is a cost-effective way to protect your tank from rust and leaks, sparing you a full tank replacement.

    Commercial and Rental Tank Maintenance in Pompano Beach

    Property managers, restaurants and small offices often run several tanks that work much harder than a home unit. We set up scheduled maintenance for landlords and businesses, flushing each tank and logging the anode condition and valve test date. Records like these help with insurance and with planning replacements before a tank fails during business hours. We service 50 to 100 gallon commercial models as well as residential tanks in rental units.

    Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Pompano Beach

    The temperature and pressure relief valve is the part that keeps a tank from becoming dangerous. We lift the lever, confirm it opens and reseats and check the discharge line for proper material and termination. We also check your thermal expansion tank pressure with a gauge, since a waterlogged expansion tank lets pressure jump every heating cycle. High street pressure is not unusual in many service areas, so we test static pressure at a hose bib too.
