    Sewer Lateral Lining in Pompano Beach

    Laterals are the most common candidates for trenchless repair. Old materials crack and sag. Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe crack, sag or fill with roots over time, so we jet the line clean, inspect it by camera and choose lining or bursting based on what we see. The new line restores full flow and resists roots.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Pompano Beach

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. It hardens inside the old pipe. Once inside the cleaned pipe, it gets inflated against the wall and cured with heat or UV, forming a new pipe with no joints. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Pompano Beach yard.

    Sectional Point Repair in Pompano Beach

    Sometimes only one joint or a few feet of pipe is bad. A spot liner fixes that. Guided by camera, a short liner is placed over the bad section and cured, so you pay for a few feet instead of the full line. It suits isolated damage in Pompano Beach laterals.

    Cost-Effective Underground Pipe Repair in Pompano Beach, FL

    People are often surprised at the final number. It's often cheaper in the end. Per foot, lining can look pricey, but once you add the cost of tearing out and replacing a driveway, a patio and the landscaping above an open trench, the trenchless bid usually comes out ahead.

    Pipe Bursting in Pompano Beach, FL

    Bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. You'll see an entry and exit pit. A bursting head is pulled through, fracturing the old pipe outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind, and that HDPE is fused into one continuous length with no joints at all.

    Lateral and Spot Lining in Pompano Beach

    Not every pipe needs lining end to end. Short damage gets a short liner. When damage is limited to a few joints or a short section, a sectional liner costs less than a full-length one and goes in quickly, but we confirm the rest of the line is sound by camera before recommending it.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Pompano Beach, FL

    Main water lines can also be replaced without a trench. Bursting or boring works. New HDPE or PEX goes in along the old path by bursting, or along a new one by directional boring, leaving lawns and drives alone. The line is pressure tested before service is turned back on.
