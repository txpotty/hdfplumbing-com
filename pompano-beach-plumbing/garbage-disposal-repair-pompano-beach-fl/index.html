    Jammed Disposal Repair in Pompano Beach

    A humming disposal that will not spin almost always has a jam. Bones, fruit pits, glass or utensils can wedge the flywheel. We cut power, use a hex wrench on the bottom of the unit to free the flywheel and remove the object. Then we test the motor and press the reset button when needed.

    Slow Draining Disposal Repair in Pompano Beach, FL

    A disposal that grinds but drains slowly often has a clogged discharge tube or trap arm. Grease, rice and coffee grounds are the usual culprits. We clear the line with the right tools and check for proper slope. We also explain what not to put the disposal.

    Jam Clearing and Motor Repair in Pompano Beach

    A common issue with garbage disposals is a jam, often caused by hard food items like bones or fibrous vegetables.     Leaking Disposal Repair in Pompano Beach, FL

    Leaks can come from the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube or the disposal body itself. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty, and replace gaskets or tubes when needed. A leak from the bottom of the unit usually means the internal seal has failed. In that case, replacement is the smarter option.
