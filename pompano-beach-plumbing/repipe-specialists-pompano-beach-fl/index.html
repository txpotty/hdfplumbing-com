    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Pompano Beach

    Old galvanized lines shed rust flakes that clog aerators, faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. That is often the first sign. Replacing galvanized with PEX or copper ends the rust, brings pressure back and stops the leaks that start at the threaded joints where galvanized is thinnest. Old pipe stays in walls when pulling it would wreck finished surfaces.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Pompano Beach

    Kitec, an orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe with brass fittings, and gray polybutylene are both known for failures. Both still turn up in older homes. We can usually identify it from the pipe at the water heater, and full replacement makes the house easier to insure and sell.

    Slab Leak Repiping in Pompano Beach

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. A high water table across much of Florida affects drainage, septic design, and how deeply crews can dig for underground repairs. Rather than jackhammer to reach one leak, we run new lines through the attic and walls and leave the old under-slab pipe out of service. No jackhammer needed. It ends this leak and takes the rest of the slab plumbing out of the picture.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Pompano Beach, FL

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Flexible PEX soaks up some of the pressure spike behind water hammer, and its long fitting-free runs leave fewer weak points. We keep PEX out of sunlight and away from water heater flues.

    Commercial Repipe Services in Pompano Beach

    Older commercial buildings in Pompano Beach often have galvanized risers, copper that has pinholed or plastic pipe that no longer meets code.     Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Pompano Beach, FL

    Polybutylene, a gray or blue plastic pipe installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s, breaks down with exposure to chlorine and other disinfectants in tap water. It fails without warning. Brittle pipe walls and cracked fittings mean one polybutylene leak usually signals risk across the whole house. We replace the full system with PEX or copper. Insurers often ask about it at renewal.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Pompano Beach, FL

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside, and over decades the inside diameter closes down to a fraction of what it was. Pressure drops slowly. Brown water after the house sits overnight, weak flow at upstairs showers and leaks at threaded joints are the usual signs that galvanized is near the end. We replace it with PEX or copper. Owners usually notice stronger flow the first day.

    Copper Repiping in Pompano Beach, FL

Our copper work uses thicker Type L pipe, joined with lead-free solder or ProPress fittings, rather than the Type M found in many older houses. Expect a slightly longer job. Most copper jobs run a day longer than PEX, since each turn needs its own fitting.
