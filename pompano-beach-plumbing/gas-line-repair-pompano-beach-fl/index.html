    Natural Gas Leak Detection and Repair in Pompano Beach

    Finding a gas leak starts at the meter. With every appliance off, a moving test dial on the meter tells us gas is escaping somewhere, and then we isolate branches to narrow it down. A combustible gas detector narrows it further. A spray of soap solution on the fitting shows bubbles at the leak, and we fix that joint or section and retest the whole system.

    Buried Yard Line Repair and Replacement in Pompano Beach, FL

    Many older homes have steel gas lines running from the meter to the house or out to a pool heater. The wrap fails eventually. After the coating breaks down, corrosion starts, and constant moisture from irrigation or poor drainage makes it worse. Utilities get marked first. The new line is polyethylene with tracer wire, or coated steel where it has to be exposed, and we backfill and clean up the yard when the test passes.

    Interior Black Iron Pipe Repair in Pompano Beach

    Threaded black iron pipe can leak at joints after years of vibration or settling. We tighten, reseal or replace fittings and sections with new pipe and gas-rated thread sealant. Rusted pipe goes too. Heavily rusted pipe in garages, crawlspaces or outside walls is replaced before it fails, and we pressure test the full system afterward.

    Honest Gas Line Repair Pricing in Pompano Beach, FL

    Gas leaks are usually a surprise expense, so the price should not be another surprise. You get a written estimate covering the work, the parts and any permit fees before anything begins. The number holds. When a spot repair and a full replacement are both reasonable, we price both so you can compare the up-front cost against the age of the rest of the pipe.

    Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in Pompano Beach, FL

    A kitchen remodel or a new tankless water heater can push a gas system past what its original pipe can deliver. Tankless units need a lot of gas. Many tankless heaters are rated at 150,000 to 199,000 BTU, which can be more than double what an old tank heater used, so the line feeding it often has to be upsized back toward the meter. We calculate the full load first. Then we size the new piping so every appliance in the house still gets full pressure at the same time.

    Propane Gas Line Service in Pompano Beach, FL

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. Our work covers propane yard lines, regulator replacement and fitting repairs, plus a check that every appliance has its propane orifices installed. Wrong orifices make a burner run dirty and unsafe. The test procedure is the same as for natural gas.

    Permits and Code Compliance for Gas Work in Pompano Beach, FL

    Most gas piping work beyond a simple connector swap needs a permit in Pompano Beach, FL. Inspection covers sizing, approved materials, pipe supports, shutoffs, drip legs and a witnessed or documented pressure test. The permit is on us. A permitted repair also gives you a paper trail for insurance and for any future sale.

    Gas Leak Repair at Fittings and Valves in Pompano Beach

    Most residential gas leaks are at a threaded joint, a valve stem or a flexible connector, not in the middle of a pipe. That keeps repairs small. We back out the leaking joint, clean the threads, apply gas-rated thread sealant and reassemble it, or replace the fitting or valve if the threads are damaged. Every repair gets a soap test and a system pressure test before we leave.
