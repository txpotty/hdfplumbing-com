    Where We Find Leaks in Pompano Beach, FL

    Slab Leak Detection in Pompano Beach

    Concrete slab homes have supply lines buried in sand below the floor, and a pinhole there can run for months before anyone notices. A warm tile is the classic sign. We pressure test the hot and cold sides separately, then listen through the slab with a ground microphone and confirm with a thermal camera on hot water lines.

    Leaks in Walls and Ceilings in Pompano Beach, FL

    A brown ring on the ceiling under a bathroom usually points to a shower valve, a drain joint or a toilet wax ring. We figure out which. Thermal cameras show the cool wet area, a moisture meter confirms it and a borescope through a small hole lets us see the actual pipe before any big opening is made.

    Slab Leaks From Ground Movement in Pompano Beach

    Pipe set in concrete cannot flex when the ground moves, so it cracks at elbows and where it rises through the slab. Nearly all homes in Florida sit on concrete slabs over sandy soil, so leaks under the slab are a frequent and costly repair. We isolate the line first. Then acoustic listening and thermal imaging find the leak so nobody has to break the floor open to search, and you get a marked spot plus repair options before any concrete gets cut.

    Leak Detection Price Ranges in Pompano Beach, FL

    For a standard home, leak detection typically runs $150 to $450. Pool leak detection runs about $250 to $500. Repairs vary a lot, anywhere from $150 for a toilet or faucet to several thousand for rerouting a slab line overhead, so a precise mark keeps the repair cost down.

    PEX and Copper Leak Detection in Pompano Beach, FL

    On a ground microphone, copper leaks are easy to hear. PEX is quieter, since plastic pipe does not carry sound well, so we lean more on thermal scans, moisture readings and tracer gas when the house is plumbed in it. Both show up on our schedule every week. Pinholes in copper and failed crimp fittings in PEX are the two leaks we find most.

    Irrigation Leak Detection in Pompano Beach, FL

    Sprinkler laterals crack and zone valves stick open, and either one can waste thousands of gallons before the bill shows it. Check the yard first. Our tech tests the irrigation system separately from the house, runs each zone and traces buried lines, because many water providers say to rule out irrigation first when a summer bill jumps.

    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Pompano Beach, FL

    The buried line from your meter to the house can leak for months without surfacing, especially in old galvanized pipe. A green stripe in a dry yard is a hint. We trace the line with an electronic locator, then use a ground microphone to hear the leak, switching to tracer gas on plastic lines that do not carry sound.

    Fixture and Appliance Leaks in Pompano Beach, FL

    A silent toilet leak can lose hundreds of gallons a day. Worn faucet cartridges, softener valves and water heater connectors add more. We dye test tanks, check flappers and fill valves, and inspect supply lines and water heater fittings, and most of these repairs take under an hour with parts from the truck. They are inexpensive repairs.
