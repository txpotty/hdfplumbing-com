    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Pompano Beach

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Pompano Beach, FL

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. The backup pump gets its own float and its own check valve, and it runs off a sealed 12-volt battery when the outlet goes dead. Most run five to seven hours on a full charge. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Pompano Beach

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. Because the motor is cooled by the surrounding water, they run quieter and can push more gallons per minute up a tall lift than most pedestal pumps. The pit lid sits flush. Each install gets a sealed lid and a grommet around the cord to keep out radon and odor.

    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in Pompano Beach

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume.     Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Pompano Beach, FL

    A dead pump during a storm means the water is already climbing. Our trucks carry 1/3 and 1/2 HP submersibles, check valves and fittings, so in most homes we can pull the old pump and set a new one in one visit. Most swaps take about two hours. We test it with a full pit before leaving.

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Pompano Beach, FL
