    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in Pompano Beach

    The cost of water heater replacement in Pompano Beach, FL usually ranges from $1,200 to $2,500 for a standard tank unit, including installation. Factors like unit type, capacity, brand and labor complexity influence the final price.

    Manufacturer tank warranties typically range from 6 to 12 years depending on the model. We also warranty our installation labor.

    Generally, if your water heater is over 10 years old, frequently breaks down or has a major issue like a leaking tank, replacement is often more cost-effective in the long run. For newer units with minor, easily fixable issues, repair might be a viable option. Our technicians can provide an honest assessment.

    Yes, in many areas of Pompano Beach and Florida, a plumbing permit is required for water heater replacement to make sure the installation meets local safety and building codes. HDF Plumbing handles all necessary permitting on your behalf, so you do not have to worry about compliance.

    Common signs include a lack of hot water, rust-colored water, a metallic taste in hot water, strange noises like rumbling or popping from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit or a water heater that is over 10 to 12 years old. If you notice any of these, it is time to call a professional.

    Most standard tank-style water heater replacements can be completed in 3 to 5 hours, assuming no major complications or plumbing reconfigurations. Tankless or hybrid installations might take a bit longer, often 5 to 8 hours, due to more complex venting or gas line changes.

    Flushing a traditional tank water heater annually is recommended in most homes. Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving stubborn scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. This helps remove sediment buildup, which can reduce efficiency and shorten the lifespan of the unit. Regular flushing can also prevent scale from damaging heating elements.
