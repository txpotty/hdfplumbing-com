    Control Valve and Injector Service in Pompano Beach, FL

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw.     Regeneration Settings and Hardness Testing in Pompano Beach, FL

    Many softeners are set incorrectly from day one or never updated when household size changes. We test your raw water hardness and program the valve to match your actual use.     Brine Tank Cleaning and Inspection in Pompano Beach, FL

    Over time, sludge and insoluble materials can build up in your brine tank, hindering the softening process. Our maintenance includes thoroughly cleaning the brine tank, inspecting for any cracks or leaks and ensuring the float valve operates correctly.     Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Pompano Beach

    Softener resin can foul with iron, manganese or sediment and lose capacity over time. Chlorine and chloramine in city water also slowly break resin down. We run a resin cleaner through the system and test performance afterward. If the resin is exhausted, we rebed the tank with fresh high-capacity resin, which costs much less than a new system.
