    Many plumbing codes require the installation of an expansion tank with a new water heater, especially in closed-loop systems. Our installation service includes correctly sizing and installing the expansion tank to ensure proper system operation and prevent early wear on your new water heater and other plumbing fixtures in Pompano Beach.
