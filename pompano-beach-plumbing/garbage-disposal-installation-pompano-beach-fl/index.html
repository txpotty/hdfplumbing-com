    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Pompano Beach, FL

    Air switches start the disposal with a button on the sink or counter. The button pushes air through a tube to a control box in the cabinet, which keeps electricity away from the button and avoids a new wall switch. Islands use them often.

    Disposal Upgrades in Pompano Beach, FL

    Upgrading from an old 1/3 HP unit to something quieter is a common request. Corroded drain arms get swapped. Galvanized arms rust from the inside.

    Disposal Installation for Double Sinks in Pompano Beach, FL

    Double bowl sinks put the disposal on one side and a continuous waste or end outlet tee on the other. We lay out the drain so both bowls drain freely and the disposal outlet does not push water into the other bowl, which happens when the tee sits too close to the disposal discharge. Heavy-wall fittings last longer.

    High-Horsepower Disposal Installation in Pompano Beach

    A 3/4 or 1 HP unit takes up more room and weighs more than a basic unit, so we measure the cabinet first. Taller units can push the discharge outlet below the wall stub, and when that happens we either lower the drain at the wall or pick a model with a higher outlet so water does not sit in the chamber. Power needs a check too. A larger motor pulls more current when it starts.

    InSinkErator Model Selection in Pompano Beach, FL

    We help you pick between the Badger and Evolution lines based on how you cook, how much noise bothers you and how much cabinet room you have.     Switching From Batch-Feed to Continuous-Feed in Pompano Beach, FL

An air button skips the wall wiring. The plumbing side is usually a straight swap.
