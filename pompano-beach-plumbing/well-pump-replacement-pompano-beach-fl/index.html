    Well Pump Sizing in Pompano Beach

    Sizing starts with the well log. We get depth and static level from the well log or a sounder, then a draw-down test shows what the well can really produce in gallons per minute. Pumps bigger than the well can supply run dry.

    Well Pump Brands We Install in Pompano Beach, FL

    Goulds, Grundfos, Franklin Electric and Sta-Rite cover nearly every well pump we install. Each brand has strengths, and we choose based on your well. Goulds and Franklin parts are on every supply shelf, which helps when a control box fails late on a Saturday afternoon.

    Control Box and Pressure Switch Replacement in Pompano Beach, FL

    Not every dry faucet means a dead pump. A pressure switch with pitted contacts or a plugged sensing tube can stop the pump even though nothing is wrong down the well. The switch itself is a cheap part. We show you the burned contacts.

    Submersible Well Pump Replacement in Pompano Beach

    A submersible pump hangs on drop pipe inside a 4-inch or 6-inch casing, often 100 to 400 feet down, so pulling one is a two-person job with a pump hoist. Sand in the impellers and lightning surges are the usual culprits. Before pulling, we test the windings with a megohmmeter. The reading shows whether the fault is the motor or the wire.

    Grundfos Well Pump Replacement in Pompano Beach, FL

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have a built-in soft start and dry-run protection, and they run on two wires with no control box at the surface. They cost more up front. The SQE pairs with a CU 301 controller for constant pressure.

    No-Water Calls in Pompano Beach

    Pump trouble rarely gives notice. One morning the faucet coughs air and then nothing, and by then the motor has often been struggling for weeks. Before we pull the pump, we test the switch and control box.

    Drop Pipe and Wire Replacement in Pompano Beach

    Galvanized drop pipe from the 1970s and 1980s rusts from the inside, and a pinhole at 150 feet will bleed pressure every time the pump shuts off. Depending on depth and pump weight, it gets replaced with schedule 120 PVC or poly. The wire gets checked too. Nicked jackets and old tape splices go.

    Pump and Pressure Tank Matching in Pompano Beach

    Pump and tank work as a pair. An undersized or waterlogged tank forces dozens of extra starts a day, and each start puts heat into the motor windings. Every pump job includes a tank check.
