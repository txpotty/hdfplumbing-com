    Anode Rod Inspection & Replacement in Weston, FL

    The anode rod is your hot water tank's sacrificial defense against corrosion. Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving stubborn scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. During our maintenance, we pull and inspect the anode rod. If it's significantly depleted, we recommend and perform a replacement to extend the life of your tank in Weston, FL, preventing rust from eating away at the tank lining.

    Maintenance Versus Replacement Costs in Weston, FL

    Sediment Flushing & Cleaning in Weston, FL

    Over time, minerals and sediment settle at the bottom of hot water tanks, reducing efficiency and possibly leading to premature failure. Our maintenance includes a thorough flushing of the tank to remove these deposits. This process helps your tank heat water more evenly and quietly, a vital step for any hot water tank in Weston, FL, where mineral content in the water is a frequent concern.

    Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Weston

    The temperature and pressure relief valve is the part that keeps a tank from becoming dangerous. We lift the lever, confirm it opens and reseats and check the discharge line for proper material and termination. We also check your thermal expansion tank pressure with a gauge, since a waterlogged expansion tank lets pressure jump every heating cycle. High street pressure is common in many service areas, so we test static pressure at a hose bib too.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Service in Weston, FL

    Many modern plumbing systems include an expansion tank connected to the water heater. This tank absorbs excess pressure created during the heating cycle, protecting your hot water tank and other plumbing fixtures. Our maintenance includes checking the expansion tank's air pressure and overall condition. Proper expansion tank function is key to preventing premature wear on your hot water tank and protecting system integrity.
