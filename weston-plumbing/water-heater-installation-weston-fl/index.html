    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Weston

    Many plumbing codes require the installation of an expansion tank with a new water heater, particularly in closed-loop systems. Plumbing work in Florida follows the Florida Building Code, whose plumbing volume is based on the International Plumbing Code. An expansion tank protects your water heater and plumbing system from excessive pressure buildup, which can be caused by the heating process. Our installation service includes correctly sizing and installing the expansion tank to support proper system operation and prevent early wear on your new water heater and other plumbing fixtures in Weston. This small component offers significant long-term protection.
