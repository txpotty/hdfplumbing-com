    Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in Weston, FL

    Tees direct flow into and out of the trap at the correct depth. Missing or broken tees allow floating grease to escape into the sewer line. We replace damaged tees with durable PVC or other approved materials. We also check that they are set at the right depth for your unit. This is one of the most common and important grease trap repairs.

    Grease Trap Leak Repair in Weston

    Leaks can come from cracked trap bodies, failed gaskets or loose pipe connections. Indoor leaks can damage floors and create slip hazards in the kitchen. We locate the source and replace gaskets, seal fittings or repair cracks when possible. Outdoor leaks can contaminate soil and lead to odors around the building. If the body is badly damaged, we suggest replacement.

    Grease Trap Drain Line Repair in Weston

    Beyond the trap itself, the drain lines leading to and from the grease trap can also suffer damage or blockages. These lines are particularly susceptible to grease build-up. In Weston, we diagnose and repair issues within these connecting pipes, including clearing FOG accumulation, fixing collapsed sections, or repairing root intrusions. Ensuring these lines are clear is vital for the overall efficiency of your grease trap system.

    Clogged Grease Trap Repair in Weston

    Grease traps often clog due to excessive accumulation of fats, oils, and grease (FOGs) or food solids. Our technicians use high-pressure hydro-jetting to break down tough blockages and thoroughly clean the internal baffles and lines.     Baffle Repair and Replacement in Weston

    Baffles slow incoming water so grease can rise and solids can settle. When baffles crack, corrode or break loose, grease passes straight through the trap. We remove damaged baffles and install new ones made for your trap model. In concrete interceptors, we can repair or rebuild baffle walls. Restoring baffles brings back proper separation and helps you stay compliant.

    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Trap Repair in Weston

    When traditional snaking isn't enough, hydro-jetting is our go-to method for severe grease trap blockages. This powerful technique uses high-pressure water streams to fully scour the interior of pipes and the grease trap itself, removing years of FOG buildup, scale, and debris. It's a highly effective, non-invasive method that not only clears clogs but also cleans the lines, significantly reducing the likelihood of future blockages in your Weston business.
