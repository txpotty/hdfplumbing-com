    Flange and Inlet Leak Repair in Weston, FL

    Leaks show up at the flange, dishwasher inlet, discharge tube or body. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty and replace gaskets or tubes as needed. A leak from the bottom of the unit usually means the internal seal has failed, and in that case replacement is the better option. It is not a repairable part.

    Jammed Disposal Repair in Weston

    A jam usually comes from one hard object, like a peach pit, a chicken bone or a twist tie wrapped around the impeller. We free the flywheel from below with the hex key and pull the object with pliers, never a hand, then run it with water to make sure nothing else is stuck. The reset button may need a press. Repeat jams point us to the trap arm.

    After-Hours Disposal Service in Weston

    A dead disposal during dinner prep is a real problem, especially if the sink is full of water. Do not try drain chemicals. Drain chemicals can eat at the seals and splash on whoever opens the trap, so leave the water where it is and give us a call.

    InSinkErator and Other Brand Repairs in Weston, FL

    Every brand, from InSinkErator to Waste King and Moen, mounts differently, so a flange leak is fixed with that brand's kit. InSinkErator uses a three-bolt or Quick Lock style ring, while Waste King uses an EZ Mount twist ring that comes off without tools. We stock the common kits. It means most repairs need only one trip.

    Noise and Odor Diagnosis in Weston

    A rattle that stops after a few seconds is usually a spoon or a pit bouncing in the chamber. A constant high whine means something else. A steady whine usually points to failing motor bearings, and the noise keeps building until the motor locks up. Most smells start at the splash guard. We pull it, clean the chamber walls and replace the guard if it is torn.

    Commercial Disposal Repair in Weston

    Restaurant disposals run 1 to 5 horsepower and usually include a control box and a solenoid valve for water. When one stops, the dish line backs up fast. We check the controls first. A bad solenoid or tripped overload in the panel kills the unit as fully as a jam does, but it gets fixed far faster.

    Disposal Odor and Buildup Cleaning in Weston, FL

    Food buildup and grease on the chamber walls and under the splash guard cause most disposal smells. Many wells in Florida produce water with a sulfur smell or high iron, which often calls for filtration to protect fixtures and appliances. Mineral scale traps the food. Cleaning the guard and chamber, checking the drain and swapping a torn guard often solves a smell that lemon peels never fixed.
