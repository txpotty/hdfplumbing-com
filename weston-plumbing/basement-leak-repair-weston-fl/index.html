    How We Fix Basement Leaks in Weston, FL

    Floor Drain and Backwater Valve Repair in Weston

    A floor drain that backs up when the washer drains or during a storm usually points to a blockage downstream or a failed backwater valve. After clearing the line, we run a camera to confirm it is open. Where the city sewer surcharges in storms, a backwater valve on the building drain keeps sewage from coming up through the floor drain, and it needs its flapper cleaned once a year. Dry traps smell.

    Crack Injection for Basements in Weston

    Injection works on poured concrete walls, not block. We set ports along the crack, seal the face with epoxy paste, and pump in polyurethane or epoxy from the bottom port up until resin shows at the next port. Epoxy bonds the two sides. Polyurethane is better for active leaks because it foams on contact with water.

    Interior Drainage Systems in Weston

    Where digging outside is not practical, we handle the water from inside instead. We cut the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and slope it to a new or existing sump pit before patching the floor with fresh concrete. The wall gets a dimpled membrane. Water then runs behind it into the channel.

    Commercial Basement Waterproofing in Weston

    Commercial basements often hold stock, files or equipment rooms that cannot sit in water. We look at floor drains, elevator pits and sump systems as one system, since a single failed check valve or a clogged pit inlet can flood the whole lower level. Duplex pumps help. A high-water alarm warns staff before the floor gets wet.

    Groundwater Pressure Relief in Weston

    High groundwater pushes up through the slab as well as in through the walls. Water rising through floor cracks or around a floor drain during wet weather is the usual sign. Under-slab drain pipe tied to a sump lowers the water level beneath the floor. It is not a quick job.

    Emergency Basement Leak Repair in Weston, FL

    A pipe that lets go in a basement can put an inch of water on the floor in under an hour. First, close the main water shutoff. Our plumber stops the leak and repairs the pipe, then restores water so the drying can start. We can point you to a drying company too.

    Emergency Basement Flooding Response in Weston

    A burst pipe or a failed sump during a storm can put inches of water on the floor in minutes. Call us first, then move belongings off the floor. Stay away from wet outlets. If water is near the furnace, the water heater or any outlet, keep out of the room until the power to that area is off.
