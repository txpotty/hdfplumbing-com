    Septic Tank Leak Repair in Weston

    A tank that never fills to the outlet, or one that fills slowly with groundwater after rain, has a leak.     Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Weston, FL

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones get eaten by sulfuric acid from sewer gas and eventually fall off into the tank. With no outlet baffle, sludge flows right out to the leach lines. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Drain Field Line Jetting in Weston

    Distribution laterals clog from the inside with solids and slime, especially after years without pumping. A jetter goes into each lateral from the D-box or an end cleanout, and what washes back shows how plugged it was. Roots get cut. If the soil itself has sealed, jetting will not fix it, and we say so.
