    Around Weston, flushing the water heater yearly and descaling tankless units helps keep mineral buildup under control. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters.     During a full inspection, we look at the visible gas piping and the connectors to each appliance.     Water Heater Safety Inspection in Weston

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. A heater that backdrafts can spill combustion gases into the house, so we test the draft at the vent hood and note any problems right away.

    Water Heater Inspections in Weston
