    Slab Leak Detection in Weston

    Slab leaks are common in homes on shifting soil and moving foundations. These leaks occur in pipes beneath your concrete slab, often signaled by warm spots on the floor, sudden spikes in your water bill or the sound of running water when all fixtures are off. HDF Plumbing uses specialized acoustic listening devices and thermal imaging cameras to precisely locate these hidden leaks without destructive guesswork. Early detection is key to preventing major foundation damage.

    Water Heater and Softener Leak Detection in Weston

    Water heaters and softeners can leak at fittings, valves and drain lines. A softener stuck in regeneration can send water down the drain continuously, and a dripping relief valve on a water heater wastes water too. We check these units carefully and repair or replace failed parts. That often explains a mystery bill in homes that have water softeners.

    Water Meter Leak Testing in Weston

    The water meter is the first and simplest leak test. With every fixture and appliance off, a moving leak indicator means water is escaping somewhere. We read the flow rate to estimate how many gallons are being lost. Then we shut off zones one at a time to see which part of the property is losing water.

    Leak Detection for PEX and Copper Pipes in Weston

    Whether your plumbing system uses modern PEX tubing or traditional copper pipes, HDF Plumbing has the expertise to detect leaks in both materials. PEX, while flexible, can be susceptible to damage from rodents or improper installation, while copper can corrode over time, especially with hard water. Our tools are effective across many pipe materials, allowing accurate detection regardless of your home's plumbing type.

    Main Service Line Leak Detection in Weston, FL

    The line from the meter to your house carries full pressure and can leak underground for a long time. Wet soil, soft spots or unusually green patches of yard may be the only clues. We trace the line, listen for the leak and mark it for a targeted repair. Replacing old galvanized or polybutylene service lines is often recommended.

    Smart Leak Detection Device Installation in Weston
