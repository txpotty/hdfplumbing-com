    Which Sump Pump Fits Your Weston, FL Home

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Weston, FL

    Combination units put a primary AC pump and a DC backup on one discharge manifold, so you get two pumps and two floats in a single assembly. They suit pits too tight for two separate pumps. We replace the whole assembly or just the failed side.

    Sump Basin and Pit Replacement in Weston, FL

    Old clay tile or 18-inch plastic basins crack and fill with silt, and a pump in a basin that small cycles every few minutes during a heavy rain. We break out the concrete, set a larger poly basin with a sealed lid, and pour the floor back. A bigger basin means longer pump cycles. Fewer starts, longer motor life.

    Sizing a Submersible Sump Pump in Weston

    Horsepower matters less than people think. A third-horse pump moves plenty of water for most homes, as long as the vertical lift to the discharge is under about 15 feet. Before we choose, we read the pump curve at your real discharge height. A pump that is too big empties the pit too fast and wears itself out.

    Commercial Sump Pump Replacement in Weston

    Commercial sumps run harder than residential ones, and a restaurant or office basement often has a duplex system with two pumps that alternate on a controller. We replace the pumps and the alternating relay. Work can be scheduled early or after closing so nobody is stepping around an open basin.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Weston

    Hurricanes and tropical storms bring flooding, power loss, and sewer backups to Florida, often swamping drainage systems for days. A primary pump on household current does nothing once the breaker panel goes dark. We add a 12-volt backup pump on its own float, mounted a few inches above the primary, with a deep-cycle battery and a charger that tests itself. It also covers a stuck primary switch. A high-water alarm lets you know the backup has taken over.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Replacement in Weston, FL

    Zoeller cast iron pumps, the M53 and M63 in particular, are what we pull off the truck most often because they take heat and hard cycling better than plastic housings. An existing Zoeller can usually be swapped for a current model with the same 1.5-inch outlet and float style. Need more lift? We can move you up to a half-horse model like the M98.

    Choosing a Backup Battery in Weston

    Battery choice decides how long the backup actually runs. Most backups use a sealed AGM or deep-cycle battery around 75 to 100 amp-hours, which covers several hours of on-and-off pumping. We size it to how often your pump runs on a wet day. Batteries need replacing every three to five years.

    Pedestal Pumps for Shallow Pits in Weston, FL

    Older basements sometimes have a narrow, shallow pit poured right into the floor, and a pedestal pump is often the only thing that fits without breaking concrete. We swap the pump, replace the float rod and stops, and run new 1.5-inch PVC to the check valve. They usually cost less than a submersible.
