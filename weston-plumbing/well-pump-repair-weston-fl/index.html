    Pressure Tank Repair in Weston, FL

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. That rapid cycling burns out pumps early. We check the air charge, inspect the bladder and recharge or replace the tank. Correct tank sizing protects the pump and gives more even pressure at the tap.

    Well Pressure Tank Repair in Weston

    The pressure tank works with your well pump to maintain consistent water pressure and reduce pump cycling. Issues like a waterlogged tank, sudden pressure drops, or continuous pump operation often point to a faulty pressure tank. We inspect the air bladder, pressure switch, and tank connections to find the problem. In Weston, we repair or replace tanks to improve your well system's performance and extend pump life.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Weston

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are frequent causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most budget-friendly well repairs.

    Well Pump Repair vs Replacement Cost in Weston, FL

    Control and pressure switch repairs often cost $150 to $500, while a pressure tank replacement typically runs $600 to $1,500. Pulling and replacing a submersible pump can range from $1,800 to $5,000 or more, depending on depth, horsepower and wire. If your pump is older and already needs pulling, replacing it at the same time can save a second pull later. We lay out both options with honest numbers.
