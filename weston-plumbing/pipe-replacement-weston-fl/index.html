    PEX Repiping in Weston

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale buildup and expands slightly if water freezes, which helps in colder homes. PEX installs faster than copper with far fewer fittings in the walls. It is our most requested repipe material.

    Pinhole leaks in copper usually start as a small green stain or a damp spot on drywall.
