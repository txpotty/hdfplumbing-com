    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Weston, FL

    Many homes have tankless heaters mounted on an exterior wall with no venting at all, since the unit exhausts directly outside. These units face sun, rain and temperature swings, which can break down plastic covers and wiring over time. When we replace them, we check the pipe cover, freeze protection settings and the electrical connection. We can add a protective pipe cover kit to guard the water and gas lines.     Converting from a 40 or 50 gallon tank to tankless frees up floor space and ends standby heat loss in a garage or closet.
