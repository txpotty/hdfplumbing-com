    Emergency Shower Leak Replacement in Weston

    A sudden leak from a shower can soak a ceiling in an hour. A cracked pan, split valve or loose drain gets located quickly and either repaired or made safe until replacement. Turn off the main. Next, call us and stay out from under the wet spot.

    Walk-In Shower Conversion in Weston, FL

    Walk-in conversions are one of the most requested bathroom jobs we do. We relocate the drain to fit the new pan, raise the valve to shower height and set the floor slope so water stays in the shower instead of running out the entry. We add blocking for grab bars. ADA layouts are available.

    Aging-in-Place Shower Replacement in Weston

    Aging in place often starts with getting rid of the tub. We remove it, adjust the drain and install a new shower valve at the right height, with blocking for grab bars and a handheld shower on a slide bar so the shower works seated or standing. Thresholds stay low. It makes a real difference.

    Replacing Showers Damaged by Hard Water in Weston, FL

    Many wells in Florida produce water with a sulfur smell or high iron, which often calls for filtration to protect fixtures and appliances. Scale shows up first as a weak showerhead and a stiff handle. Given time, scale seizes cartridges, pits valve bodies and etches glass beyond cleaning, which is when we replace. A softener helps the new one. Ask us about options.

    Shower Valve and Fixture Upgrades in Weston

    Sometimes the shower only needs its guts replaced. When the pan and walls are fine, a new valve, diverter and trim fix drips and swings without demo. The difference is real. Half a day, usually.

    Custom Tile Shower Replacement in Weston, FL

    Custom tile replacement starts with careful demolition. We take the old tile and pan down to the studs and subfloor, replace any damaged wood and install a new waterproofed pan and walls, then set the valve and drain to the finished tile depth. The slope has to be right. A flood test comes before tile.

    Accessible Shower Replacement in Weston, FL

    Barrier-free showers need low or flat entries and controls within reach. We install the valve where someone seated can reach it, add blocking for grab bars and set a handheld on a slide bar, all following ADA guidelines when you ask for them. We keep the entry wide. We plan for a seat.

    Shower Unit Replacement in Weston

    Cracked fiberglass units leak at the base first. Cracks tend to start where the base was not supported, and each shower flexes it until water slips through to the subfloor. We pull the old unit. Then we check the subfloor and set the new base on a full mortar bed.
