    PEX for Remodels & Additions in Weston, FL

    Copper to PEX Conversions in Weston

    Pinhole leaks in copper tend to come in clusters. Water chemistry, high flow velocity and contact with soil under a slab can all pit the pipe wall from the inside, and once a few pinholes appear, more are usually on the way. Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving heavy scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. A switch to PEX ends it. We use listed transition fittings anywhere PEX ties into copper that stays.

    Full Home PEX Repiping in Weston

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside out. Give it four or five decades and the inside is so packed with rust that a half-inch pipe barely flows, and the first draw in the morning runs brown. We replace every foot of it with PEX. You get real pressure back, and the discolored water goes away with the old pipe.

    Hydronic Heating with PEX in Weston

    Radiant floor loops are PEX too, though not the same pipe you drink from. Heating work calls for oxygen-barrier PEX, coated so oxygen cannot seep through the pipe wall and corrode the boiler, the circulator and the iron parts. Loops have length limits. Too long and the far end runs cold.

    Remodel and Addition PEX Rough-In in Weston

    Rough-in heights matter more than people think. If the shower valve sits two inches low or the vanity stubs miss the cabinet, the wall gets reopened after tile, and that costs time and money. We check heights with your contractor before drilling. Then we pressure test with the walls open.

    Uponor PEX System Installation in Weston

    Uponor's ProPEX system is PEX-A joined with an expansion tool plus a ring slipped over the pipe end. The tech expands the pipe and ring together, pushes the fitting in, and the plastic shrinks back around the barb over the next few seconds, tighter than when it started. There is no crimp gauge to check. Because the fittings are wide inside, each joint costs very little flow.

    PEX Main Water Line Installation in Weston

    The main from the meter to the house feeds every fixture you own.
