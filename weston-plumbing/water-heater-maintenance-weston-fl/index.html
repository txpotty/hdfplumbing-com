    Flushing a Tank Water Heater in Weston

    Popping and rumbling mean sediment. Calcium and magnesium settle on the bottom of the tank and form a layer that traps heat under it, so water boils in pockets beneath the scale and pops every time the burner or lower element fires. We flush until it runs clear. Bad tanks need a few rounds.

    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Weston

    Electric tanks fail at the lower element. Sediment piles up around the lower element, it runs hotter than it should, and eventually it burns out, which is why you suddenly get only a few minutes of hot water. We test both elements. With power off, we check thermostats and connections.

    Anode Rod Replacement in Weston

    Some water eats anode rods fast. Depending on your water chemistry, a rod that should last five years might be spent in three, so we pull it on every visit instead of assuming it is fine because the tank is still young. We swap worn ones. A healthy rod keeps the tank from rusting through.

    Tankless Descaling in Weston, FL

    Scale is the tankless unit's main enemy. Around Weston, flushing the water heater yearly and descaling tankless units helps keep scale buildup under control.
