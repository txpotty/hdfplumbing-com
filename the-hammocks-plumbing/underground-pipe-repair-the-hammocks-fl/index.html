    Frequently Asked Questions About Underground Pipe Repair in The Hammocks

    Simple buried pipe repairs usually run $500 to $2,500. Sewer replacements cost more. Extensive trenchless sewer replacements can be $5,000 to $15,000 or more, depending on where the pipe runs, how bad the damage is and the method used to fix it. The price comes after we look.

    It gets worse and more expensive. Soil washes out. A leaking service can undermine a driveway, soak a foundation, grow mold in a crawlspace and run up the water bill every month, and a cracked sewer lateral can let sewage into the soil around your home. Fix it sooner.

    Check the meter with every fixture off. Watch the dial. If it moves, water is going somewhere, with soggy ground or a hiss near the meter often pointing to the main line. A locate visit settles it.

    Small repairs often do not. Replacements usually do. Full water service or sewer line replacement usually requires a permit, and any work in the public right-of-way always needs coordination with the city. The permit is on us.

    It depends on your policy and the cause. Check what is excluded. Sudden and accidental damage is sometimes covered, but normal aging and long-term leaks generally fall outside coverage. Ask your insurer directly. We provide documentation for the claim.

    A spot repair is usually a few hours. Trenchless jobs take about a day. A full sewer replacement with excavation can take two to five days, with footage, depth and inspections setting the pace. We put the schedule in writing.

    We use PEX from major manufacturers like Uponor and Viega. Pipe meets ASTM standards. PVC and ABS products meet the ASTM specs for buried water or drain service, while every fitting we bury is listed for underground use. No off-brand fittings.

    For water lines we use PEX or Type K copper. Both are rated for burial. Sewer and drain work uses Schedule 40 PVC, and trenchless jobs use fused HDPE, all of which resist corrosion and handle ground movement better than the old materials they replace. Local code decides the final pick.
