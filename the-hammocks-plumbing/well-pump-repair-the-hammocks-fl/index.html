    Jet Pump System Repair in The Hammocks, FL

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up through a suction line with an ejector. Air leaks on the suction side cause most failures. HDF Plumbing checks the foot valve, the ejector nozzle and every suction fitting, then reprimes the pump and confirms it builds pressure. A jet pump that runs nonstop without reaching pressure has almost always lost prime or sucked air through a cracked fitting.

    Well Pump Brands We Service in The Hammocks, FL

    Low Water Pressure Diagnosis in The Hammocks

    Weak pressure on a well is not always the pump. Common causes include a plugged sediment filter, a low switch setting or a yard line leak. In some cases the pump is fine and the well just cannot keep up with demand. We test each one.

    Grundfos Well Pump Repair in The Hammocks

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have built-in electronics that protect the motor and report faults. Most Grundfos calls involve a sensor or a drive fault. HDF Plumbing reads the fault code, checks the wiring and tests the pressure sensor before replacing anything in a The Hammocks, FL well. Electronics fail less often than people think.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in The Hammocks

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, a clogged sensing tube and ant nests are the usual reasons one fails. After swapping the switch and clearing the tube, we set cut-in and cut-out to match the tank, usually 40 and 60 psi. This is among the least expensive well repairs.

    Control Box and Surge Repair in The Hammocks, FL

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and a relay. Heavy afternoon thunderstorms are common in summer, and saturated ground can push water into drain lines and slow every fixture in the house. Surges from lightning are the usual cause of control box failures. After testing the capacitor, relay and overload, we replace the bad part and suggest a surge arrestor if the box lacks one. An arrestor costs little.
