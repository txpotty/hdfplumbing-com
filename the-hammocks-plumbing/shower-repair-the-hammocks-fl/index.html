    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in The Hammocks, FL

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open. Moen Posi-Temp showers take the 1222, and Delta Monitor 13 and 14 showers take the RP46074, and both wear out on a fairly regular clock. We stock both. Original owners can often get the cartridge free under warranty.

    Mixing Valve Repair in The Hammocks

    Mixing valves blend hot and cold inside the wall. A sticking cartridge or spool shows up as temperature that drifts or a handle that has to go much farther than it used to. We replace the cartridge. If the valve is too old for parts, we talk through a replacement valve and what it means for the tile.

    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in The Hammocks

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. A leak at the arm threads follows the pipe into the wall, and it shows up as bubbling paint in the next room. We reseal the threads. We can add a handheld or slide bar while we are there.

    Leaky Shower Valve Repair in The Hammocks

    One drip every few seconds adds up to hundreds of gallons a year. Single-handle showers drip from a worn cartridge or O-rings, while old two-handle valves drip from a washer and a pitted seat. We carry the common parts. Most valves we open take a Moen, Delta, Kohler or Pfister cartridge, which means a same-visit fix.

    Hot Water Pressure Problems in The Hammocks, FL

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. It comes down to the hot side, whether that is a sediment-choked heater outlet or a cartridge with scale packed in the hot port. We test both sides. Good hot flow at the sinks points us straight at the shower cartridge.

    Emergency Shower Repair in The Hammocks, FL

    A shower that will not shut off is an emergency. Close the main valve where the water line enters the house, or the shower's own stops if it has them behind an access panel. Then call us. HDF Plumbing sends a plumber with cartridges on the truck, and we can often get your water back on within the hour.

    Shower Handle Leak Repair in The Hammocks

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water leaking behind the trim slips into the wall cavity at the valve opening and soaks the drywall while the tile face stays dry. The trim comes off first. It is usually a loose bonnet nut or a worn O-ring.

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in The Hammocks, FL

    Low pressure at the shower with good pressure at the sink narrows it down fast. With good pressure elsewhere, the problem is in the head, the restrictor or the cartridge, and that is typically a short repair. The head comes off first. If every fixture is weak, we check the regulator and the main.
