    Commercial Water Heater Replacement in The Hammocks

    Businesses in The Hammocks put far more hours on a heater than a house, so commercial replacements need more capacity and faster recovery.     Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in The Hammocks

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is common, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. A whole-home tankless unit can draw four times the gas of a 40-gallon tank, so the gas line from the meter usually has to be upsized, and the old metal vent gets replaced with stainless or PVC run to the maker's specs. You see both numbers.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in The Hammocks, FL

    Gas water heaters recover faster than electric, and in The Hammocks, FL homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. We shut off the gas, disconnect and cap the line, drain the old tank and set the new one, then install a new flex connector, a sediment trap and a vent connector sloped up toward the flue. Then we leak test.
