    Copper to PEX Conversions in The Hammocks

    Copper lines can develop pinhole leaks from water chemistry, high velocity and sometimes soil contact under slabs. Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving heavy scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. Once a home has had several pinhole leaks, more often follow. Converting to PEX stops the cycle and lowers the risk of hidden water damage. We use approved transition fittings wherever new PEX connects to existing copper stubs or valves.

    Frequently Asked Questions About PEX Pipe Installation in The Hammocks

    A whole-home PEX repipe typically costs $4,500 to $12,000 depending on home size, bathroom count and access. Smaller jobs like a single bathroom remodel rough-in usually run $800 to $2,500.

    PEX piping is designed to be highly durable and has an estimated lifespan of 50 years or more. Its resistance to corrosion, scaling and freeze damage adds to its long-term reliability, making it a sound investment for your The Hammocks home.

    PEX resists scale and corrosion, flexes with temperature changes and has fewer joints. Copper is very durable but prone to pinhole leaks in some water conditions and costs more to install.

    We make small, targeted openings to run new pipe and patch them when done. Most homeowners are surprised by how little drywall work is needed.

    Signs you might need PEX repiping include persistently low water pressure, discolored or rusty water, frequent leaks, or old galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes. These issues are typical in older The Hammocks homes and point to a failing plumbing system.

    PEX offers several advantages over copper, including lower material and installation costs, greater flexibility and resistance to corrosion and freezing. Unlike copper, PEX does not form pinhole leaks over time due to water chemistry, making it a more durable choice for many The Hammocks homes. It also reduces water hammer noise.

    Whole-home repipes usually require a plumbing permit and inspection. We handle the permit process as part of the job.

    We mainly use high-quality, reputable PEX brands such as Uponor (PEX-A) and Viega (PEX-B), which are known for their durability and reliability.
