    Slab Leak Reroutes in The Hammocks

    Rerouting leaves the failed pipe in the slab. New pipe runs through the walls or attic instead, which avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than opening a finished floor. We usually use PEX because it handles heat and resists corrosion. Tile and hardwood floors make this the favorite.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in The Hammocks, FL

    Cold-side leaks leave no warm spot. That makes them harder to spot. They usually show up as a running meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards, so we isolate the cold side, map the pipe with a line tracer and listen along the route with a ground microphone.

    Supply and Drain Leaks Under the Slab in The Hammocks, FL

    Supply leaks and drain leaks act differently. Supply leaks are pressurized, so they move the meter, warm the floor on the hot side and run whether or not anyone uses water. Drain leaks only leak when water goes down, showing up as smells, sluggish drains or wet spots near bathrooms. We test both kinds in your The Hammocks, FL home.

    Pressure Testing and Isolation in The Hammocks

    We always start with isolation. Our tech closes the main, caps or valves off the hot and cold sides, and puts a gauge on each, then watches which one drops over fifteen to thirty minutes.
