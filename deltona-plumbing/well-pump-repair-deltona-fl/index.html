    Franklin Electric Well Pump Service in Deltona, FL

    Franklin Electric motors and control boxes are in a lot of local wells. Our technicians know them well, from the QD control boxes to the SubDrive constant pressure units. We carry the common Franklin parts. Replacing a failed motor usually costs less than a rewind once shipping is counted.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Deltona

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Most switches fail from pitted contacts, a plugged sensing tube or insects in the housing. After swapping the switch and clearing the tube, we set cut-in and cut-out to match the tank, usually 40 and 60 psi. This is among the least expensive well repairs.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Deltona, FL

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up through a suction line with an ejector. Air leaks on the suction side cause most failures. HDF Plumbing checks the foot valve, the ejector nozzle and every suction fitting, then reprimes the pump and watches it reach cut-out pressure. A jet pump that runs nonstop without reaching pressure has almost always lost prime or sucked air through a cracked fitting.

    Well Pump Diagnostic Services in Deltona, FL

    Voltage, amperage, pressure and flow rate tell us most of what is happening down the well without pulling a single length of pipe. A weak motor draws high amps. A clogged intake or a dropping water level shows up as low flow with normal amps, and that changes the repair entirely. Testing first saves a pull.

    Pressure Tank Repair in Deltona

    Short cycling is the classic pressure tank symptom, with the pump clicking on and off every few seconds while a faucet runs. A waterlogged tank or a ruptured bladder causes most of it. We check the precharge with the tank drained. It should sit about 2 psi below the cut-in pressure, so a 40/60 switch wants roughly 38 psi in the tank.
