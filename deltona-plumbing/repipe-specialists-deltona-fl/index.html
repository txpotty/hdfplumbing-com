    PEX Pipe Replacement in Deltona, FL

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Because PEX flexes, it absorbs some of the shock that causes water hammer, and long runs with few fittings mean fewer places for a leak to start inside a wall. Attic PEX gets kept away from flues and out of UV.

    Type L Copper Repiping in Deltona

    Copper has been plumbing homes for generations, and done right it lasts a long time. Our copper repipes use Type L with proper hangers and isolators so the pipe does not wear against framing. All joints use lead-free solder. Copper can pinhole in aggressive water, so we ask about your water and any past leaks before recommending it over PEX.

    Slab Leak Repiping in Deltona

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Nearly all homes in Florida sit on concrete slabs over sandy soil, so leaks under the slab are a frequent and costly repair. Rather than jackhammer to reach one leak, we run new lines through the attic and walls and leave the old under-slab pipe out of service. No jackhammer needed. It ends this leak and takes the rest of the slab plumbing out of the picture.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Deltona, FL

    Older homes may still have galvanized steel pipe that rusts from the inside out. Low pressure, brown water and leaks at threaded joints are the typical signs. We remove or abandon the galvanized lines and replace them with PEX or copper, and your water pressure and water quality usually improve the same day the new lines go into service.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Deltona

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water and long attic runs. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open the pipe end and let it shrink back over the fitting for a full-bore joint. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. Most homes are repiped with PEX-A in two to three days.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Deltona

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. We can reroute a single hot or cold branch overhead and abandon the leaking pipe in the slab, which costs far less than a repipe and avoids breaking the floor. This works well for a first leak. After multiple leaks, we will show you why a full repipe may cost less over the next ten years.

    Repiping for Hard Water in Deltona, FL

    Around Deltona, flushing the water heater yearly and descaling tankless units helps keep scale buildup under control. Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside pipe, and over years it narrows the bore and speeds up corrosion in metal lines. Pressure suffers. For homes with hard water, we usually recommend PEX-A, which resists scale and does not corrode, and we can add a softener loop or a whole-house filter during the repipe. Planning it now saves a second visit later.
