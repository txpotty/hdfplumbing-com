    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Port Orange, FL

    Copper to PEX Conversions in Port Orange

    Copper lines can develop pinhole leaks from water chemistry, high velocity and sometimes soil contact under slabs. Converting to PEX stops the cycle and reduces the risk of hidden water damage. We use approved transition fittings wherever new PEX connects to existing copper stubs or valves.
