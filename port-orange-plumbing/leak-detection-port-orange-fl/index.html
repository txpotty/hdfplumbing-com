    Frequently Asked Questions About Leak Detection in Port Orange

    Leak detection in Port Orange typically costs $150 to $450 for a typical home, depending on the size of the system and the number of areas tested. Pool leak detection runs about $250 to $500. We tell you the price before we start.

    We use acoustic ground microphones, thermal imaging cameras from brands like FLIR, line tracers, moisture meters and pressure test gauges. Each tool helps confirm a different type of leak. Using several together gives reliable results.

    You can check your meter, test toilets with food coloring in the tank and look for damp spots. Finding leaks under slabs or inside walls requires specialized equipment. That is where professional detection saves time and money.

    To prevent future leaks, consider regular plumbing inspections, especially for older homes. Keep water pressure in a safe range, address any signs of corrosion and maintain your plumbing system. For homes with known issues, proactive repiping or pipe upgrades can be a lasting solution.

    Most standard leak detection services can be completed within 1 to 3 hours. More complex cases, such as multiple leaks or hard-to-access areas like under a slab, might take longer to precisely pinpoint.
