    Emergency Storm Drain Clearing in Port Orange

    Catch Basin Cleaning in Port Orange, FL

    Catch basins are critical parts of any storm drainage system in Port Orange, FL, designed to collect runoff and trap debris before it enters the main drain lines. Regular cleaning of catch basins is essential to prevent them from becoming overloaded and causing blockages further down the system. Our service includes removing built-up leaves, sediment, trash, and other debris from catch basins, so they work correctly and protect your entire drainage system. This preventative maintenance is key to avoiding larger issues.

    Downspout and Roof Drain Lines in Port Orange, FL

    Many homes connect downspouts to buried pipe that carries water away from the foundation. These lines clog with roof granules, leaves and nests, then back up and dump water at the wall. We jet them clear and test with a hose to verify flow at the outlet. For flat roofs with scuppers, we check that the discharge path is open too. Keeping water away from the slab helps protect against soil movement and cracking.

    Root Removal from Storm Drains in Port Orange

    Tree roots are a common cause of severe storm drain blockages in Port Orange, especially in older neighborhoods with mature trees. Roots seek out moisture and can work into drain pipes through tiny cracks or loose joints, growing and eventually completely blocking water flow. HDF Plumbing uses dedicated root-cutting attachments on our drain snakes and hydro jetting equipment to cut through and remove root intrusions. After removal, we can suggest further steps to prevent future root problems.

    Hydro Jetting for Storm Drains in Port Orange, FL

    Hydro jetting is a powerful solution for deeply clogged storm drains in Port Orange, FL. This method uses high-pressure water streams to blast away stubborn obstructions like compacted soil, heavy silt, roots, and grease buildup. It is very effective for thoroughly cleaning the inside of the pipe walls, restoring the drain to its full capacity and preventing a quick return of blockages, a common issue with older drainage systems.

    Dry Well Cleaning and Inspection in Port Orange, FL

    Dry wells let storm water soak into the ground instead of flowing to a street. They are found on commercial sites, HOA common areas and some residential lots. Silt slowly seals the settling chamber and the drainage rock, slowing how fast water drains. We vacuum the chamber, inspect the inlet and outlet, and check drain-down time. If the dry well no longer drains properly, we explain options for rehabilitation or replacement.
