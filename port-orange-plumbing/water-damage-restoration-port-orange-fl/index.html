    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Damage Restoration in Port Orange

    Small single-room restorations with extraction and drying often cost $1,200 to $4,000. Larger jobs with drywall, cabinet and flooring replacement can run $5,000 to $15,000 or more, especially when the water spread across several rooms before anyone found it. Policies often cover it.

    Drying and minor drywall repairs usually do not need permits. Water heater swaps, repiping and structural work often require them. Permits are handled by us.

    Most homeowner's policies cover sudden and accidental water damage, like a burst pipe or a failed appliance. Outside flooding typically requires flood insurance, and long-running leaks are usually treated as maintenance. Check your coverage.

    Bubbling paint, warped baseboards and soft spots in floors are typical signs. Stains on ceilings and walls also point to hidden leaks, especially below bathrooms or near exterior walls where pipes run. Rising bills are a clue.

    Our drying setup includes high-velocity air movers, LGR and desiccant dehumidifiers, and pin and pinless moisture meters. Cavity drying systems blow air through small holes near the floor and can often save the drywall. We also use thermal imaging.
