    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Port Orange

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the water. The motor stays dry and you can reach it without pulling the pump, which is why they suit narrow pits under 14 inches across, and why they often outlast cheap submersibles in Port Orange homes. Expect more noise.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Port Orange, FL

    Submersible units sit down in the pit, sealed, with the water around them cooling the motor. You hear far less of them than a pedestal, and in Port Orange, FL we set them on a couple of bricks so the intake stays above the silt that settles on the bottom of every liner. The cord seal matters.
