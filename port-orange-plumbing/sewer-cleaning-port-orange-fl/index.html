    What Our Port Orange, FL Sewer Techs Handle

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Port Orange

    Jetting cleans the whole pipe wall. A cable only makes a path. A jetter sends high-pressure water around the full circumference of the pipe, removing buildup that a cable would leave behind.

    Shop and Restaurant Sewers in Port Orange

    Busy buildings clog more often. More use means more buildup. Our commercial setup handles bigger pipe, and we schedule around your hours so customers never notice.

    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Port Orange, FL

    Cast iron rots from the inside. Newer homes across Florida mostly use PEX or CPVC with PVC drains, though copper pinhole leaks are still a frequent repair in older homes. Scale builds on top while the bottom channels out. Aggressive cutters can break through thin spots, so we use descaling chains and careful jetting made for older iron, which restores flow and gives a clear view of the remaining pipe wall on camera.

    Emergency Sewer Backup Cleaning in Port Orange

    Sewage coming up through a shower or floor drain? Stop using water. A tech arrives ready to clear the main and start cleanup, with the goal of restoring flow before anything else, then we disinfect the area and take photos for an insurance claim.

    Preventative Sewer Maintenance in Port Orange

    Roots come back after cutting. Plan on regrowth in 12 to 18 months. A scheduled cleaning every year or two keeps roots and grease from closing the line and lets you plan around it.

    Grease and Scale Buildup Cleaning in Port Orange, FL

    Grease bonds with mineral scale into a hard crust. Many wells in Florida produce water with a sulfur smell or high iron, which often calls for filtration to protect fixtures and appliances. Over years the opening shrinks. A cable can punch a hole through the crust, but a jetter cleans the walls back to full size and leaves less for new grease to grab onto, and the method depends on pipe material and condition.

    Root Masses in Port Orange Sewer Laterals

    Mature trees and old clay laterals do not mix. Clay joints let roots in. We cut roots in several passes, then look on camera to judge the damage and talk about lining or a spot repair.

    Sewer Cleaning Versus Sewer Repair in Port Orange, FL

    Cleaning cannot fix a broken pipe. It only removes what is inside. If your sewer backs up every few months, the camera usually shows a belly, a crack or a collapsed section, and we compare the yearly cost of cleanings to a spot repair, a liner or a replacement, with the numbers in writing.
