    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Port Orange, FL

    The float switch is the most frequent reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris. We clean or replace the switch and set the on and off levels so the pump does not short cycle. Electronic sensor switches are an option for tight pits where floats get stuck.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Check-Ups in Port Orange, FL

    For homes equipped with pedestal sump pumps, HDF Plumbing provides specialized maintenance services in Port Orange, FL. We focus on inspecting the external motor, ensuring proper lubrication and checking the impeller for any obstructions. This type of pump, with its motor above the water, requires specific attention to its shaft and bearings to prevent premature wear and support reliable performance during heavy rains.

    Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Port Orange, FL

    Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Port Orange, FL

    The check valve stops pumped water from falling back into the pit when the pump shuts off. A failed valve makes the pump run twice as often and wears out the motor. We test and replace check valves and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose joints. We also make sure water exits away from the house so it does not soak into the soil near the slab or foundation.

    Battery Backup System Testing in Port Orange, FL

We check the battery charge, verify the charger's function and ensure the backup pump activates when the main power is interrupted.
