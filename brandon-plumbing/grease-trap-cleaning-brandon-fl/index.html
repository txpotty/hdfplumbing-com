    Grease Trap Hydro-Jetting in Brandon, FL

    Some traps have years of grease baked onto the walls and inlet piping. Pumping alone leaves that crust in place. We jet the inside of the empty trap and the line feeding it, which takes the unit back to bare walls and restores the capacity the crust was eating up. It adds about an hour.

    Odor Control and Trap Inspections in Brandon

    A trap that stinks between pump-outs usually has a flattened lid gasket or a vent problem. We check both on every visit. Gaskets are cheap to swap, but a broken vent can push sewer gas into the kitchen and slow the drain too, so it is worth chasing. Most smells get fixed that visit.

    Commercial Grease Trap Pumping in Brandon

    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Lines in Brandon, FL

    A cable machine pokes a hole through grease. Jetting actually removes it. We run a jetter at roughly 3,000 to 4,000 psi with a rotating nozzle that cuts the grease off the full circumference of the pipe and flushes it downstream. Afterward a camera pass confirms the pipe is clean.
