    ADA Urinal Installation in Brandon

    Accessible urinals start at the carrier.     Office and Restaurant Urinal Installation in Brandon

    Restaurant restrooms see a lot of traffic in short bursts. Busy bars can see hundreds of flushes a night, so we install valves that recover fast and carriers that stay tight. We can fit tamper-resistant valves. Night installs keep you open.

    ADA-Compliant Urinal Installation in Brandon

    At least one urinal in a public restroom with more than one usually has to be accessible. That means a rim no higher than 17 inches above the floor, an elongated bowl projecting at least 13-1/2 inches from the wall, and a 30 by 48 inch clear floor space in front. Flush controls must be reachable. We mark it all during rough-in.

    Urinal Permits and Compliance in Brandon, FL

    New urinals usually need a permit. Adding a fixture means new drain, vent and supply work that most cities inspect at rough-in and again at final, and commercial jobs often need plans that show fixture counts and accessible layouts. We pull the permit. Like-for-like swaps may not need one.

    Urinal Installation Cost Factors in Brandon, FL

    Carrier work is the biggest cost swing. A replacement on an existing carrier with good supply and drain lines costs far less than a new install that needs a carrier, a new 2 inch drain and a 3/4 inch supply run through finished walls. Touchless valves cost more than manual. We itemize each piece.

    Waterless Urinal Installation in Brandon

    Waterless urinals save the most water and need the most discipline. They use a cartridge or a layer of sealing liquid that lets urine pass while blocking sewer gas, and the cartridge has to be swapped on schedule or the restroom starts to smell. Your custodial team needs training. We show them how.

    Waterless Cartridge Urinals in Brandon

    Each waterless brand uses its own cartridge. Falcon, Waterless Co, Kohler and Zurn units all trap odor differently, and the cartridge or sealing liquid has to be replaced after a set number of uses, which in a busy restroom can mean every few months. A replacement schedule comes with the install. Spare cartridges should stay on site.
