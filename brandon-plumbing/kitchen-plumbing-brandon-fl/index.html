    Replacing an Old Kitchen Sink in Brandon

    Pulling an old cast iron sink takes two people and a putty knife, since they are heavy and usually caulked in tight. The counter cutout gets measured first. Your new sink must fit the existing opening or the countertop needs cutting, so we confirm the size before you order. The drain gets rebuilt to suit the new bowl depth.

    Kitchen Sink Installation in Brandon

    Undermount sinks hang from clips and epoxy under the stone, so we check the mounting before anything else. Drop-in sinks get a bead of silicone under the rim, new strainer baskets and a fresh 1-1/2 inch trap set to meet the drain in the wall without any strain on the joints. Supply lines are new every time. Both bowls get filled and drained while we watch every joint.

    Dishwasher and Ice Maker Lines in Brandon

    A dishwasher needs a hot supply with its own shutoff, a drain to the disposal or a wye tailpiece, and either an air gap at the sink or a high loop under the counter, depending on local code. Ice makers need a cold line. We run braided stainless or copper to the fridge with a quarter-turn shutoff, never the thin plastic tubing that splits behind the fridge.

    Workstation and Farmhouse Sinks in Brandon

    An apron-front sink needs cabinet support built to hold it, since a 30 inch fireclay bowl can weigh over 100 pounds before water. We check the frame first. Many workstation sinks have a drain set off to the back or side, so we plan the trap and disposal layout around it before the counter crew arrives.

    Disposal Repair and Replacement in Brandon, FL

    A disposal that hums but will not spin is almost always jammed. We cut the power, work the flywheel loose with a 1/4 inch hex key from underneath, pull out the bottle cap or bone, and press the red reset button before testing it again. Leaks are a different story. A drip from the bottom of the housing means the internal seal failed, and that unit has to be replaced.

    Burst Supply Lines in Brandon, FL

    Supply lines with plastic coupling nuts split more often than braided stainless, especially after years of pressure swings. A failed line under the sink can put gallons per minute into the cabinet and the floor below before anyone notices it. Both supply lines get replaced. The second line is just as old as the one that failed.

    Grease Trap Repair in Brandon, FL

    Rusted inlet tees, cracked lids and missing baffles all let grease pass straight through a trap. We inspect each part during cleaning and replace what has failed, usually on the same visit. Without a baffle, a trap is only a box. For larger interceptors we arrange pumping first, then go in and inspect the structure, the outlet and the sample port.

    Kitchen Drain Clog Removal in Brandon, FL

    Water rising in the second bowl when the first drains tells us the clog is past the trap, in the branch line behind the cabinet. We run a 1/4 or 3/8 inch cable from the cleanout or the trap arm, and on grease-packed lines we follow up with a jetter. Chemical cleaners rarely touch grease. They sit in the trap and soften PVC glue joints over time.
