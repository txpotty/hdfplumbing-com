    Septic Alarm Troubleshooting in Brandon

    A septic alarm sounding is the system's way of saying the water is too high in the pump chamber or the aerobic unit. Hit the mute switch and go easy on the water. Our tech confirms the pump runs and looks for a float snagged on its cord. A tripped breaker is the cheapest answer.

    Aerobic and Alternative System Repair in Brandon, FL

    Aerobic treatment units use an air pump to feed oxygen to the bacteria, and when that pump dies the unit starts to smell within days.     Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Brandon, FL

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.
