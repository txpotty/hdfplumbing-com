    Toilet Gasket and Seal Replacement in Brandon, FL

    The rubber parts in a toilet, the flapper, the tank-to-bowl gasket, the bolt washers and the wax ring at the floor, all break down with age and chlorine. They get stiff.     Pressure-Assist and Dual-Flush Repair in Brandon

    Pressure-assist toilets use a sealed vessel inside the china that compresses air with incoming water pressure, and the cartridge inside that vessel eventually wears out.
