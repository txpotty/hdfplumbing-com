    What a Tank Tune-Up Covers in Brandon, FL

    Electric Element Check in Brandon

    Electric tanks have two elements, usually 4,500 watts at 240 volts, and the lower one does most of the work and takes most of the scale. We test each one with a meter. A failed lower element shows up as hot water that runs out halfway through a shower. We replace it with the matching wattage and voltage.

    T&P Valve Replacement in Brandon

    Replacing a relief valve is a short job, but it has to be done right. The new valve must match the tank's BTU or kilowatt rating. We drain the tank below the valve opening, thread in the new one with pipe dope rated for potable water, and reconnect a discharge pipe in copper, CPVC or another approved material. Never plug or cap that pipe.

    Rheem and Bradford White Tank Service in Brandon

    Rheem, Bradford White, AO Smith and State make most of the tanks in local garages, and each has quirks a tech should know before opening a drain valve. Some models hide the anode under the top cover. Bradford White often uses a combo anode in the hot outlet nipple. We carry the right sockets for each brand.

    Gas Venting Tune-Up in Brandon, FL

    Atmospheric gas tanks vent by draft alone, so the vent connector needs upward slope of at least a quarter inch per foot and tight joints with three screws each. Draft at the hood gets checked first. On newer FVIR models, garage dust and dryer lint can clog the flame arrestor screen at the bottom, which starves the burner and makes the pilot drop out. We vacuum it clean.

    Rental and Commercial Tank Logs in Brandon

    Landlords and property managers with a dozen units need to know which tank is next to fail. Each tank we touch goes into a log. The log shows the serial date, anode condition, the last relief valve test and a rough estimate of years left, so you can plan replacements during a vacancy instead of on a Saturday night. We service 30 to 100 gallon tanks in rentals.

    Expansion Tank Service in Brandon, FL

    A thermal expansion tank is the small steel tank on the cold line above the heater, and it gives heated water somewhere to go when a check valve or pressure reducer closes the system. We test the air side with a tire gauge. The precharge needs to match static pressure in the house. A waterlogged expansion tank sounds solid when you knock on it, and it lets pressure spike every time the burner fires.

    Maintenance Versus Replacement Costs in Brandon, FL

    A yearly flush costs a small fraction of a new installed tank, which often runs $1,400 to $3,000 or more depending on size and venting. You get a straight answer on which one is worth it. A tank over 10 to 12 years old with rust at the base is a replacement. Younger tanks with a sound liner are worth maintaining.

    Relief Valve Testing in Brandon

    Temperature and pressure relief valves on residential tanks are typically rated to open at 150 psi or 210 degrees, and they can stick shut after years of mineral buildup. We lift the test lever and watch it reseat. If it keeps dripping, we replace it. We also confirm the discharge pipe runs full size to within six inches of the floor or to an approved drain, which is what plumbing codes ask for.
