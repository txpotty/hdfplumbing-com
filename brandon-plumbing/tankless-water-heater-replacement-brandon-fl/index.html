    Tankless vs. Tank Replacement Cost in Brandon, FL

    Tankless costs more up front than a tank, often twice as much installed. Across 20 years the gap narrows, because one tankless may outlast two tanks, but your usage decides the payback. You see both quotes.

    Tank to Tankless Conversion in Brandon, FL

    Converting from a tank to tankless takes a full day in most homes. The work usually includes a larger gas line, new venting through a wall or roof, and a new spot on the wall for the unit. The old flue gets capped.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Brandon

    Rinnai units we pull are usually 12 to 18 years old, and the failure tends to be a leaking heat exchanger or a dead control board. Newer Rinnai models usually fit the old mounting pattern. Less drywall work that way. Venting stays the same, or switches to PVC if you move to a condensing unit.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Brandon, FL

    Electric tankless water heaters skip the gas line entirely, but a whole-house model can need 120 to 160 amps on two to four double-pole breakers. Plenty of panels do not have room. We check panel capacity and wire gauge before quoting.

    Navien Venting and Install in Brandon

    Navien allows 2-inch or 3-inch PVC venting on its condensing units, with maximum lengths that drop as you add elbows. We stay inside those limits. Warranty depends on it.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Brandon, FL

    Commercial tankless setups in restaurants and offices often use two or more units on a manifold, with a controller that splits the load. One bad unit can come out while the others keep running. Health code sets the hot water temperature at the hand sinks and dish areas.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Brandon, FL

    Exterior-mount tankless units hang on an outside wall and vent straight out the front, so there is no vent pipe to run. Covers and wiring take a beating outdoors. Northern parts of Florida do get occasional freezing nights, which can crack exposed pipes, irrigation lines, and backflow devices. We check the freeze protection settings and the pipe cover kit on every outdoor swap. Often the simplest job we do.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Brandon, FL

    Navien NPE units are condensing, and the A models have a built-in recirculation pump and buffer tank that cut down on cold-water sandwiches. Their combi boilers handle both space heat and domestic water. We set up both.
