    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Brandon

    Slab leaks are a costly and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and crack buried water and sewer lines. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the compromised pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use advanced detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    Efficient PEX Repiping in Brandon, FL

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and highly popular choice for repiping projects in Brandon, FL. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points and making installation quicker and less intrusive than traditional rigid pipes. PEX is also very resistant to freezing, scaling, and chlorine, common issues in many water systems. We use high-quality PEX-A or PEX-B tubing, installed with durable crimp or expansion fittings, to create a reliable and efficient plumbing network throughout your home.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Brandon

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. We can reroute a single hot or cold branch overhead and abandon the leaking pipe in the slab.
