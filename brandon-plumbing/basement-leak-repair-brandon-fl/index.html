    Groundwater Pressure Relief in Brandon

    High groundwater pushes up through the slab as well as in through the walls. Water rising through floor cracks or around a floor drain during wet weather is the usual sign. Under-slab drain pipe tied to a sump lowers the water level beneath the floor. It is not a quick job.

    Exterior Waterproofing Solutions in Brandon, FL

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing, cleaning the wall, and applying a membrane with a drainage board over it. It is the most thorough fix and the most disruptive one.     Emergency Basement Flooding Response in Brandon

    A burst pipe or a failed sump during a storm can put inches of water on the floor in minutes. Get valuables up off the floor while we are on the way. Stay away from wet outlets. Water near an outlet or a gas appliance means stay out until the power is shut off.

    Waterproofing for Older Florida Homes in Brandon, FL

    Older basements were often built with block walls and no exterior membrane. Many homes in Florida built before the mid 1970s have cast iron drains under the slab, which commonly crack and corrode with age. Water collects inside hollow block cores, and weep holes at the base let it drain into a perimeter channel. Clay tile drains fail too.

    Sump Pit and Pump Installation in Brandon

    Installing a new sump means breaking out a section of slab, setting a lined pit, and running the discharge out through the rim joist. Most of our installs get a cast iron pump, a check valve and 1-1/2 inch discharge. Discharge cannot go into the sewer in most places. It usually ends outside, well away from the foundation, at a spot your local rules allow.

    Water Heater and Appliance Leak Repair in Brandon, FL

    Water heaters, softeners and washing machines often sit in basements, and their leaks pool on the floor out of sight. Once the tank itself leaks, it needs replacing. Valve and connector leaks are usually a same-visit fix, and an automatic shutoff sensor is worth adding.

    Sump Pump Installation & Repair in Brandon

    A sump pump sits in a pit below the slab and kicks on when a float rises. Stuck floats, worn motors and failed check valves cause most of the service calls we run on sumps. We test yours with a bucket. A battery backup is worth adding where power drops in storms.
