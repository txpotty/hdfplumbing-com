    Humming Disposal Repair in Brandon

    Humming with no spin almost always means a jam. Spoons, glass, bones and pits cause most of them. We cut power, work the flywheel free with a hex wrench on the bottom of the unit, remove the object and then test the motor and the reset button with water running.

    InSinkErator and Other Brand Repairs in Brandon, FL

    InSinkErator, Waste King and Moen each use different mounts, so a flange leak is fixed with that brand's kit. InSinkErator uses a three-bolt or Quick Lock style ring, while Waste King uses an EZ Mount twist ring that comes off without tools. We stock the common kits. It means most repairs need only one trip.

    Electrical Issues and Motor Repair in Brandon

    A disposal that hums but will not grind has power but cannot turn, while one that is silent has no power at all. They are different problems. For a silent unit we test the breaker, reset and switch, and for a humming one we check the flywheel and motor before deciding if it is worth repairing.

    Disposal Repair vs Replacement Costs in Brandon, FL

    Most jams and small leaks are much cheaper than a replacement. If the disposal is past eight to ten years old, leaks from the body or has a failing motor, replacement is usually the better value, and we give you both prices when it is close. The choice is yours.

    Leaking Disposal Fix in Brandon, FL

    A drip at the top usually means the sink flange putty has dried out. We drop the unit, pull the flange, clean off the old putty and reset it with fresh putty and new gaskets, then hang the disposal back up. That fixes most top leaks. Side leaks are usually the discharge gasket or the dishwasher hose clamp.
