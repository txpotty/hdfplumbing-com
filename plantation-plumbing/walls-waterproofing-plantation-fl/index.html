    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Plantation, FL

    A cracked or poorly sloped shower pan often causes wet baseboards and stained ceilings below. We test the pan by plugging the drain and flooding it to confirm the leak. Then we rebuild the pan with correct pre-slope, a new liner or bonded membrane and a properly clamped drain. A second flood test proves the pan is watertight before tile.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Plantation

    Crawl space encapsulation involves sealing the crawl space entirely with heavy-duty vapor barriers and often includes a dehumidifier. This not only waterproofs the walls but also controls humidity, stopping mold growth and wood rot in the crawl space. Constant heat and humidity are the main plumbing stress in Florida, keeping water heaters, AC drain lines, and exterior pipes working year round. An encapsulated crawl space can significantly improve indoor air quality and energy efficiency by keeping moisture from moving up into the home.     Frequently Asked Questions About Walls Waterproofing in Plantation

    Shower wall waterproofing with a membrane system usually runs $1,200 to $3,500 including demolition and backer board, not counting new tile. Exterior block wall sealing often costs $800 to $3,000 depending on size. Below-grade foundation waterproofing generally ranges from $2,500 to $8,000.

    Key signs you need walls waterproofing include damp or wet basement walls, visible mold or mildew growth, a musty odor in your basement or crawl space, efflorescence on concrete walls, cracks in the foundation, or standing water after heavy rains. Don't ignore these warning signs, as they often point to ongoing water intrusion.

    A shower wall waterproofing project usually takes two to four days, including drying time between coats. Exterior block sealing can often be completed in a day. Larger foundation jobs may take a few days.

    Skilled DIYers can roll on liquid membranes, but mistakes at seams, corners and drains often lead to leaks. Shower pans also require correct slope and flood testing. Professional installation reduces the risk of hidden water damage.

    We commonly install Schluter Kerdi, Custom RedGard, Laticrete Hydro Ban and similar systems. The right choice depends on your shower design and tile. We follow each manufacturer's installation steps to keep the warranty valid.

    Damp proofing is a less durable solution, typically a spray-on asphalt coating that resists soil moisture but isn't designed to withstand hydrostatic pressure. Waterproofing uses stronger membranes, coatings, or drainage systems built to prevent liquid water from entering, even under pressure. For basements or crawl spaces in areas with high water tables or heavy rainfall, waterproofing is the right choice.

    While minor DIY fixes like patching small cracks are possible, complete walls waterproofing is best left to professionals. It involves specialized knowledge of drainage, foundation mechanics, and proper material application. Improper DIY work can lead to poor results, wasted money, and worse water damage over time.

    Walls waterproofing is generally not recommended as a DIY project due to its complexity and the critical importance of a sound foundation. It often involves heavy excavation, specialized equipment, precise material application, and knowledge of drainage principles and local building codes. Improper waterproofing can lead to more severe and costly damage down the line, so professional help is highly advisable.
