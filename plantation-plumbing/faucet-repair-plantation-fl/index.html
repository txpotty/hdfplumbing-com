    Dripping Faucet Solutions in Plantation

    Every drip has a source, and on a faucet that has dripped for years there is usually more than one worn part. We replace what failed and check the rest. Compression, cartridge, ball and disc faucets all fail differently, so the repair is matched to the valve inside yours.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Plantation

    Ceramic disc faucets last a long time, but grit from a water main repair or scale from hard water can scratch the discs or wear out the rubber inlet seals underneath. Inlet seals cost very little. A scratched disc means a new cartridge, since the two discs are lapped to fit each other and cannot be resurfaced.

    Kitchen Sprayer and Diverter Repair in Plantation, FL

    Spray heads on kitchen faucets get clogged with scale, and side sprayers lose pressure when the diverter inside the faucet body wears out. A diverter costs very little. Touch and touchless faucets add a solenoid and batteries to the list, and we test those before replacing anything.

    Tub Spout and Diverter Repair in Plantation, FL

    Water still running from the tub spout while the shower is on means the diverter is worn, either the gate inside the spout or a diverter stem in the valve. Spouts install two ways. We measure the pipe stub and bring the right style, and the fix gives you full pressure at the showerhead again.

    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Repair in Plantation

    Pull-down kitchen faucets wear at the hose, the spray head and the docking magnet or counterweight that pulls the hose back into the spout. Hoses split near the quick-connect. Brand-matched hoses, heads and weights go in, then check for drips under the sink where the hose connects.

    Emergency Faucet Repair in Plantation

    A faucet that will not shut off, or one with a cracked body spraying under the sink, needs the water off before anything else happens. Turn the two small valves under the sink clockwise. If they will not turn, use the main shutoff for the house. We come fast for these.

    Bathroom Pop-Up Drain Repair in Plantation

    Pop-up drains on bathroom sinks fail at the pivot ball, the clevis strap, the lift rod or the stopper itself, and a slow drain often turns out to be hair wrapped around the stopper. Try lifting out the stopper first. A new pop-up assembly goes in, matched to your faucet finish when the old one is corroded or the pivot leaks.

    Specialty Faucet Repair in Plantation, FL

The common stems ride on the truck. Filtered water faucets at the kitchen sink get new cartridges or check valves as needed.
