    Frequently Asked Questions About Backflow Preventer Installation in Plantation

    Most installs in Plantation, FL run $400 to $1,500. A residential PVB for irrigation sits at the low end, while a commercial-grade RPZ costs more because the assembly itself is bigger and the labor, supports and drain work add up quickly. The first test is part of the quote.

    Two to four hours covers most residential installs. Large commercial work or major repiping takes longer, sometimes a full day, especially when the water provider requires the shutoff to happen outside business hours. We give you a time window.

    Manufacturers usually offer one to five years depending on the model. We also warranty our labor and go over the coverage with you.

    Our backflow installs carry a warranty on labor and the installed device. Specific details come with your quote, along with the manufacturer's coverage, which differs between models and sizes and which we explain before you choose. Ask us to compare models.

    Water running from the relief valve on an RPZ is the clearest sign. Other signs include leaks at the body and low pressure past the device, and discolored or foul-smelling water means you should call for an inspection in Plantation the same day. Bad-smelling water is urgent.

    Most water providers require an annual test by a certified backflow tester. Each check and the relief valve get verified, and we can put you on a reminder list so next year's test happens before the provider sends a notice. Missing the test can mean a shutoff.

    Backflow is water flowing the wrong way through a pipe, carrying contaminants into the clean supply. It happens from back-siphonage during a pressure drop or back pressure from pumps and boilers.
