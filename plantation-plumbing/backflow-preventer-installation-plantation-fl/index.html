    Frequently Asked Questions About Backflow Preventer Installation in Plantation

    The cost for backflow preventer installation in Plantation, FL can range from about $300 to $1,500, depending on the type of device required, such as a DCVA or an RPZ, the complexity of the installation and any changes needed to existing plumbing. Commercial installations with larger devices may cost more. We provide a detailed, upfront quote after an on-site assessment.

    An RPZ, or reduced pressure zone device, provides the highest level of protection and is used for high-hazard applications where contaminants could be toxic. A DCVA, or double check valve assembly, is used for low-hazard applications where contaminants are non-toxic. The choice comes down to the risk of contamination at the cross-connection point.

    Most residential installs take two to four hours including testing. Large commercial assemblies can take a full day, especially if piping must be reconfigured.

    Constant dripping or discharge from an RPZ relief valve, low pressure downstream and a failed annual test are common signs. Visible corrosion or a cracked body from freezing also means service is needed.

    Many jurisdictions require a permit for new backflow assemblies, especially on commercial properties. We check local rules and pull the permit when required.
