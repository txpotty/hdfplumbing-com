    Water Softener Maintenance in Plantation

    Water softeners fight hard water, protecting your pipes and appliances from mineral scale. Our maintenance service includes checking salt levels, cleaning the brine tank, confirming the regeneration cycle works correctly, and inspecting valves and resin beds. Proper softener maintenance extends its life and keeps soft water flowing to your home, preventing costly scale damage.

    Gas Line Inspection and Repair in Plantation

    Gas lines feed water heaters, furnaces, ranges, dryers and outdoor grills or fire pits. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections for leaks and check that appliance shutoffs work. Flexible connectors that are old or kinked get replaced. Any gas repair is done to local code with pressure testing where required. If you ever smell gas, leave the home and call your gas utility right away.

    Preventative Leak Detection in Plantation, FL

    Early leak detection is a key part of our plumbing maintenance. We use advanced tools to find hidden leaks in pipes, especially those under slabs or behind walls, which are common in older homes. Houses from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s may have polybutylene supply pipe, which many insurers refuse to cover and owners choose to replace. Catching these leaks early prevents serious water damage, mold growth, and wasted water, saving homeowners in Plantation from extensive repairs down the line.

    Sewer Line and Drain Camera Inspection in Plantation, FL

    A camera inspection shows the condition of your sewer line and main drains. We look for roots, bellies, cracks and buildup before they cause a blockage. Older homes with clay or cast iron lines benefit most from regular checks. We record the inspection and give you a copy. If cleaning or repair is needed, we explain your choices.

    Repiping and Polybutylene Replacement in Plantation, FL

    Homes with polybutylene or aging galvanized pipe face a higher risk of leaks and low water pressure. During maintenance, we check pipe materials and look for signs of failure. If repiping makes sense, we explain PEX and copper options and give a detailed estimate. Repiping can be done in stages to fit your budget. New pipe brings back strong pressure and confidence.

    Drainage Services and Hydrojet Plumbing in Plantation

    Preventive drain maintenance is key to avoiding clogs and sewer backups. We offer routine drain camera inspections to identify grease buildup, roots, or other obstructions before they cause a problem. Our hydrojet plumbing service can clear out stubborn blockages, keeping your drainage system clear and free-flowing, which is especially important for homes with mature trees near the sewer line.

    Backflow Preventer Testing in Plantation

    Backflow preventers protect the drinking water supply from contamination, and many water providers require annual testing for irrigation and commercial devices. Our certified testers check each valve and file results with your water utility as required. If a device fails, we repair or replace the internal parts. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where needed. Keeping up with testing avoids fines and water shutoffs.

    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Plantation

    Tankless water heaters need specific maintenance, mainly descaling to remove mineral buildup from the heat exchanger. This is particularly important where the water is hard. Our technicians use approved descaling solutions to keep your tankless unit running at peak efficiency, preventing reduced hot water flow and potential breakdowns. We also check gas lines, ventilation, and water connections.
