    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Plantation, FL

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get. We install their M53 and M98 models often. Backup and combo units are available too. Pit size and lift decide which model we recommend.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Plantation

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. They fit pits where two separate pumps would crowd each other. Install time is shorter too. We set them in homes that want two layers without a bigger pit.

    Sump Pump Replacement in Plantation, FL

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. We pull the old pump and inspect the pit. If the old one could not keep up, we size up. Testing is done with a full pit.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Plantation, FL

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. A battery backup pump sits beside the primary and runs on its own float. A good battery carries it through most outages. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Plantation

    No pit yet? We cut one at the low corner. Washed gravel goes around the basin so water can reach it. The slab gets patched with new concrete. Last come the pump, the lid and the discharge line.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Plantation, FL

    Liberty builds quiet, well-made submersible pumps with good switch options. Their 250 and 280 series cover most residential lifts, and the vertical float version fits narrow pits where a wide tethered float would rub. Replacement switches are easy to get. We drill a 3/16 inch weep hole in the discharge to stop air lock.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Plantation, FL

    A dead pump during a storm means the water is already climbing. We carry common pumps on the truck. Most swaps take about two hours. We test it with a full pit before leaving.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Plantation, FL

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. A smart charger keeps the battery topped off and sounds an alarm if the battery weakens, the primary fails or the water rises past the backup float. Expect a battery swap every four to five years. Finished basements get the most out of them.
