    Perma-Liner and NuFlow Lining Systems in Plantation

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow both make lining systems we use, each with its own resin chemistry and installation equipment.     Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Plantation

    Orangeburg is bituminous fiber pipe that was laid in houses mostly from the late 1940s into the early 1970s. It goes oval and blisters, so a liner has nothing round to bond to and bursting is usually the better fix. Clay is different. Vitrified clay that still holds shape lines well.

    Causes of Sewer Line Failure in Plantation, FL

    Shifting soil is one of the main reasons sewer joints separate, crack or drop out of grade over the years. Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and crack buried water and sewer lines. Roots are the other big one, since a hairline gap at a clay joint is enough for a feeder root to get in and then swell into a mat that catches paper. Then age finishes the job. Cast iron scales and thins, and clay bells crack under the weight of soil and traffic.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacements in Plantation

    A bursting head is a steel cone, slightly wider than the old pipe, pulled through the line by a hydraulic unit or a winch cable. It cracks the old pipe outward into the soil while a string of new HDPE pipe follows right behind it. Fused joints, no gaps. We use it when the old line is too broken or flattened to hold a liner.

    Root Intrusion Fixes in Plantation, FL

    Roots enter at the joints, where clay bells or cast iron hubs have a small gap or a failed gasket. Once in, they fill the pipe. We cut them out with a mechanical root cutter or a hydro jet, then line the pipe so those joints are sealed for good and the line stops needing a cleaning every few months. The roots cannot get back in.
