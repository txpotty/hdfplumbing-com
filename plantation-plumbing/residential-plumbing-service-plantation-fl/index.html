    Water Heater Repair and Replacement in Plantation, FL

    Thermocouples, gas valves, elements and relief valves are the common water heater repairs. We service Rheem, Bradford White, Navien and Rinnai units, and when a tank is leaking from the shell we replace it with proper venting, an expansion tank and a drain pan, usually the same day. Old tanks get hauled off.

    Pressure Regulators and Shutoff Valves in Plantation, FL

    Water pressure over 80 psi wears out hoses and valves early. A gauge on the hose bib tells us your static pressure in a minute, and if it reads high we install or replace a pressure reducing valve and set it in the 50 to 70 psi range most fixtures are rated for. Seized main shutoffs get replaced too.

    Drain Cleaning and Clog Clearing in Plantation, FL

    Grease is the big one in kitchen lines. In bathrooms it is hair wrapped in soap scum, and we clear both with hand augers, sectional cable machines or a hydro jetter, depending on how far down the blockage sits and what the pipe is made of. When a clog keeps coming back, we run a camera to look for roots or a cracked hub.

    Faucet, Toilet and Shower Valve Repair in Plantation, FL

    Hard water chews through faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. We rebuild shower valves, replace stems and cartridges and fix leaking tub spouts, and if a fixture is too corroded to save, we install the replacement and haul the old one away so it is not sitting in your garage. Even a slow drip shows up on the water bill.

    Repiping and Supply Line Replacement in Plantation

    Polybutylene, the gray plastic pipe used for years in home construction, fails from the inside without warning. It gets replaced with PEX or copper, same as rusted galvanized and pinholed copper, routing new lines through the attic and walls so the slab stays intact and the house keeps water each night. We patch the access holes, ready for paint.

    Slab Leak Location and Repair in Plantation

    Slab leaks hide under concrete until something gives them away. Nearly all homes in Florida sit on concrete slabs over sandy soil, so leaks under the slab are a frequent and costly repair. Our techs find them with ground microphones and thermal imaging, so the only concrete we break is the small patch right over the leak, not half the kitchen floor. Then you pick the fix. Some leaks get a spot repair, others a reroute overhead that skips the slab entirely.

    Water Heater Service and Installation in Plantation, FL

    Tank or tankless, the failures repeat. A tank heater starts leaking at the bottom or rumbling from sediment, and tankless units throw error codes when scale builds up in the heat exchanger or the venting is wrong. We flush, descale and repair both types.

    Faucet and Fixture Repair in Plantation

    Most dripping faucets need a cartridge, not a new faucet. We carry cartridges for Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister, and when you do want new fixtures we install them, connect new braided supply lines, and check the angle stops under the sink while we are there. Old angle stops often break when turned.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Residential Plumbing Service in Plantation

    Most minor repairs run $150 to $450. Water heater replacement costs $1,400 to $3,000 depending on fuel type and size. You approve a flat price before any work begins, so the bill at the end matches what you agreed to.

    One to two hours covers many repairs. A water heater install takes about half a day, and a whole-house repipe runs a few days, with the water turned back on each evening so the family can shower and cook. We tell you up front.

    Swapping a showerhead or a toilet flapper is fine for most people. Gas lines, water heaters and anything behind a wall belong with a licensed plumber, for safety and because those jobs usually need a permit and an inspection that a homeowner repair can fail.     Emergencies go to the top of the board. We aim to have a plumber at the house within a few hours, and for a burst pipe or sewage backup we will walk you through closing the main shutoff on the phone so the damage stops while we drive. Grab towels and a bucket while you wait.

    Two leaks in a year is the tipping point for most homes. Polybutylene and galvanized pipe also tilt the answer toward a repipe, since patching one spot on a failing system just moves the next leak a few feet down the line. We price both side by side.

    Moen, Delta, Kohler, American Standard, Pfister and TOTO make up most of our installs.     Most repairs take one to three hours. A leaky pipe or clogged drain is often done in under two, while water heater installs take most of a day and full repipes can stretch over several days depending on the size of the house and access. We give you a timeline up front.
