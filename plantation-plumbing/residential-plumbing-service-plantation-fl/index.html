    Pressure Regulators and Shutoff Valves in Plantation, FL

    Water pressure above 80 psi stresses pipes, fixtures and appliance hoses. We test your pressure and install or replace pressure reducing valves set to a safe range.     Slab Leak Detection & Repair in Plantation

    Slab leaks are a serious concern for homes built on concrete foundations. Nearly all homes in Florida sit on concrete slabs over sandy soil, so leaks under the slab are a frequent and costly repair. Once located, we provide efficient repair options, which may include direct access, rerouting or pipe bursting, always choosing the least disruptive method for your home.
