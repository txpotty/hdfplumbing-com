    Frequently Asked Questions About Leak Detection in Plantation

    Most leak detection in Plantation, FL runs $250 to $800, depending on where the leak is and how many lines we have to test. Slab and buried leaks land on the high end. You get a written estimate after the first assessment.

    Checking the meter and dye testing a toilet are easy DIY steps. Past that, call us. A professional with acoustic sensors or tracer gas finds the source without random holes in the drywall, which saves money on the repair.

    Pipe material matters more than brand. Galvanized steel corrodes at the threads. Old polybutylene fails early, and even copper or PEX can leak from bad installation, too much pressure or foundation movement.
