    Slab Leak Reroutes in Kissimmee

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. We can reroute a single hot or cold branch overhead and abandon the leaking pipe in the slab, which costs far less than a repipe and avoids breaking the floor. This works well for a first leak.     Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Kissimmee, FL

    Polybutylene, a gray or blue plastic pipe installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s, breaks down with exposure to chlorine and other disinfectants in tap water. It fails without warning. Brittle pipe walls and cracked fittings mean one polybutylene leak usually signals risk across the whole house. We replace the full system with PEX or copper. Many insurers ask about it.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Kissimmee, FL

    Older homes may still have galvanized steel pipe that rusts from the inside out. Low pressure, brown water and leaks at threaded joints are the typical signs. We remove or abandon the galvanized lines and replace them with PEX or copper, and your water pressure and water quality usually improve the same day the new lines go into service.

    Commercial Repiping in Kissimmee, FL

    Commercial buildings in Kissimmee, FL often have larger pipe, risers that serve several floors and tenants who cannot lose water for a full day. We phase commercial repipes by floor, suite or riser so most of the building stays in service while we work. Night work is possible. Materials are usually Type L copper or commercial PEX, sized to the fixture count and installed to commercial code.

    Copper Repiping in Kissimmee, FL

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes, with lead-free solder or ProPress fittings. Expect a slightly longer job. Copper repipes usually cost more and take a day longer than PEX, mostly because every turn needs a fitting.

    Type L Copper Repiping in Kissimmee

    Copper has been plumbing homes for generations, and done right it lasts a long time. We use Type L, not the thinner Type M, and we support it with the right hangers so it does not rub or rattle in the walls. Lead-free solder only. Copper can pinhole in aggressive water, so we ask about your water and any past leaks before recommending it over PEX.
