    Tub Faucets in Older Homes in Kissimmee

    Older homes hide odd tub plumbing. We find galvanized supplies that rust shut, valves soldered to copper with no stops anywhere, and spouts threaded onto steel nipples that snap when you try to turn them. We bring transition fittings. Replacing a short run of galvanized is common.

    Dripping Tub Faucet Replacement in Kissimmee

    Drips at the spout come from inside the valve. Single-handle drips mean worn cartridge seals, and multi-handle drips mean worn washers and seats. New internals end the drip. Cracked bodies get a new valve.

    Moen Tub Faucet Replacement in Kissimmee

    Moen Posi-Temp valves use the 1222 cartridge. A worn 1222 causes drips or weak hot water, and replacing it takes about an hour with a puller. Moentrol valves use a different part. We carry both.

    Brand Tub Faucet Replacement in Kissimmee

    Brand matters more on tub valves than anywhere else. Moen, Delta, Kohler, American Standard and Pfister each use their own cartridges and trim threads, and a new trim kit from the same maker as the valve usually bolts right on without touching the plumbing. Mixing brands rarely works. We find the logo or the casting mark first.

    Cartridge and Stem Replacement in Kissimmee, FL

    Most dripping tub faucets need internals, not a new valve. We swap cartridges or stems and seats, greasing the O-rings so handles turn easily. Old seats need a seat wrench. About an hour, usually.
