    A single repair on a buried water line usually costs $800 to $2,500 including locating and excavation. Full main water service replacement typically runs $2,500 to $6,500, and sewer lateral repairs range from $1,500 to $5,000 depending on depth.

    In most cities, the homeowner owns and maintains the water line from the meter to the house. The utility is usually responsible for the meter and the line on the street side. We can help confirm which side the leak is on.

    Small repairs often do not need a permit, but full water service or sewer line replacement usually does. Work in the public right-of-way always requires coordination with the city. We handle permits when needed.

    The duration of an underground pipe repair depends heavily on the severity and location of the damage, as well as the repair method chosen.     HDF Plumbing uses high-quality, industry-approved materials from reputable brands known for durability and longevity. This includes PEX piping from manufacturers like Uponor or Viega, and various PVC and ABS pipe products that meet ASTM standards.

    Look for unusually wet spots in your yard, unexplained increases in your water bill, foul odors, decreased water pressure or gurgling sounds from drains. These can all point to a hidden leak or blockage in your underground plumbing system.

    Common signs include a water bill that jumps without explanation, a meter that moves with all water off, low pressure and wet or sinking spots in the yard. A hissing sound near the meter box can also point to a leak.
