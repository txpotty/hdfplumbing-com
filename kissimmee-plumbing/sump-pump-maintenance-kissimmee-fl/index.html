    Sump Pump Maintenance in Kissimmee, FL: What We Check

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Maintenance in Kissimmee, FL

    Backup pumps sit in the pit next to the main pump and run off a 12-volt battery when the power drops or the primary fails. Our plumber load tests the battery instead of just reading its voltage. Then we unplug the main pump and let the backup empty the pit on its own. Alarms get tested too.

    Backup Pump Battery Service in Kissimmee

    Outages and heavy rain tend to arrive together. A backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or quits. We load test the battery, check the charger and the alarm, and run the backup by itself with the main pump unplugged. Expect a new battery every three to five years.

    Float Switch Inspection in Kissimmee, FL

    The float switch tells the pump when to start and stop, and a float with too little travel between on and off makes the pump short cycle dozens of times an hour. Our plumber watches the float rise and drop through a complete cycle. Then we set the on and off points so the pump runs longer, less often.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Service in Kissimmee

    Zoeller pumps like the M53 and M63 use cast iron housings and an integral float switch, and they tend to outlast cheaper plastic units. Even so, we look for cracks in the casting and make sure the float moves freely. We carry the common Zoeller parts on the truck.

    Submersible Sump Pump Service in Kissimmee

    Submersible pumps sit at the bottom of the pit, which keeps them quiet but hides the problems. Our plumber pulls the unit out so we can see the cord and the shaft seal. A cracked cord usually means a new pump. The intake screen gets cleaned, and we look over the housing for corrosion, since pumps sitting in standing water for years rust from the bottom up.

    Pit Cleaning and Liner Checks in Kissimmee

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, leaves and gravel settle at the bottom of the basin. Sooner or later the pump sucks it into the screen and locks up the impeller. We pump the basin dry, rinse the screen and check the plastic liner for cracks, since a cracked liner lets soil wash in from the sides. Takes about twenty minutes.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Kissimmee

    The check valve sits on the discharge pipe a foot or so above the pump and keeps the water in the line from falling back into the pit. A failed valve sends that water back down for the pump to move again. Motors wear out faster. We test the valve, look for a weep hole between the pump and the valve, and check every joint up to the wall.

    Emergency Sump Pump Repair in Kissimmee

    A pump that runs nonstop, hums without moving water or will not start at all needs a plumber the same day, before the pit overflows. We send a plumber with floats, check valves and spare pumps on the truck. Most jammed impellers clear in minutes. A burned motor means we swap in a new pump on the spot and leave the pit protected.
