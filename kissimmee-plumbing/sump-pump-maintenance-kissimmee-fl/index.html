    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Kissimmee

    The discharge line carries water away from your basement, and the check valve prevents pumped water from flowing back into the pit. We inspect these components for blockages, leaks or damage. A faulty check valve can cause the pump to short-cycle, leading to early wear. We make sure these vital parts are functioning correctly to maintain an effective water removal system for your Kissimmee residence.

    Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Kissimmee, FL

    The float switch is a critical component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. A stuck or improperly adjusted float switch can lead to the pump running continuously or failing to activate at all. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to support reliable operation for your home in Kissimmee, FL.

    Commercial and Elevator Pit Pumps in Kissimmee

    Commercial buildings often have sump pumps in elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms. These pumps may need oil-minder controls or alarms to meet code and insurance requirements. We test duplex systems, alternating controls and high-water alarms on a scheduled basis. Maintenance logs help property managers stay on top of inspections.

    Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Kissimmee, FL

    The check valve stops pumped water from falling back into the pit when the pump shuts off. A failed valve makes the pump run twice as often and wears out the motor. We test and replace check valves and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose joints. We also make sure water exits away from the house so it does not soak into the soil near the slab or foundation.
