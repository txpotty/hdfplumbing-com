    Power Vent Water Heater Replacement in Kissimmee

    Power vent gas water heaters use an electric blower to push exhaust through PVC pipe, which lets them sit far from a chimney or roof flue. When the blower motor or pressure switch fails repeatedly, replacing the whole unit can be the better choice. We install new power vent models and confirm a dedicated outlet is available. Venting length and number of elbows must stay within the manufacturer's limits. Power vent tanks cost more but solve tricky placement problems.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Kissimmee, FL

    Atmospheric Vent Tank Upgrades in Kissimmee, FL

    Thermal Expansion Tank Installation in Kissimmee

    Many water utilities install pressure regulators or backflow devices that create a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure spikes can strain your tank and fixtures. Code commonly requires a thermal expansion tank on these systems. We install a properly sized expansion tank and pre-charge it to match your water pressure. This simple add-on can help your new heater last longer.

    40 and 50 Gallon Tank Replacement in Kissimmee
