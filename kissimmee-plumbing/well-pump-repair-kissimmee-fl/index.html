    Diagnosing Low Water Pressure in Kissimmee

    Low water pressure from a well system can be a frustrating problem, often indicating issues beyond just the pump itself. This could be due to a failing pressure switch, a partially clogged well screen, a leak in the supply line, or even a pump that is no longer meeting demand. Our team conducts thorough diagnostics to pinpoint the exact cause of low pressure in your Kissimmee home, delivering a targeted and effective repair solution.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Kissimmee

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are common causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most budget-friendly well repairs.

    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Kissimmee

    Submersible pumps are widely used in residential wells, designed to operate submerged within the well casing. When these pumps fail, symptoms usually include no water, low water pressure, or strange noises from the well. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing issues like motor burnout, damaged impellers, or faulty check valves that can affect submersible pump performance. We use dedicated equipment for safe and efficient pump extraction and reinstallation, protecting the integrity of your well system.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Kissimmee, FL

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and relay. Hurricanes and tropical storms bring flooding, power loss, and sewer backups to Florida, often overwhelming drainage systems for days. Lightning and power surges frequently damage these components. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection and replace failed parts. A surge protector at the control box can help prevent repeat failures.
