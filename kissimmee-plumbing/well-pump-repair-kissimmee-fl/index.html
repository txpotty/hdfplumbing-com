    Well Pressure Tank Repair in Kissimmee

    Bladder tanks hold a cushion of air that lets the pump rest between cycles. A failed bladder lets the tank waterlog, and the pump begins to short cycle. We inspect the bladder, the switch and the tank connections. Proper sizing adds years to a pump.

    Low Water Pressure Repair in Kissimmee

    Weak pressure on a well is not always the pump. A clogged sediment filter, a pressure switch set too low or a leak in the yard line can all cause it. In some cases the pump is fine and the well just cannot keep up with demand. We test each one.

    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Kissimmee

    Submersible pumps hang inside the well casing on drop pipe, often 100 feet or more below the wellhead. The usual signs are no water at all, weak pressure or a hum with no flow. Testing starts at the surface. When a pull is needed, a hoist brings the pipe up safely so we can replace the motor, the impellers or the check valve.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Kissimmee

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Most switches fail from pitted contacts, a plugged sensing tube or insects in the housing. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank, typically 40 and 60 psi. One of the cheaper well repairs.
