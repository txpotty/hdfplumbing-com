    Hardness Testing at the Tap in Kissimmee

    We test with a titration kit, counting drops until the sample changes color, because test strips are too rough for setting a valve. Raw water gets tested from an unsoftened hose bib or with the valve on bypass. Softened water comes from the kitchen tap.

    Resin Cleaning and Regeneration Tuning in Kissimmee, FL

    Iron is the most common resin fouler we see. It coats the beads orange and blocks the exchange sites. We feed a resin cleaner through the brine well, run a regeneration and retest, and if the capacity comes back we shorten the time between regenerations so the iron does not build back up.

    Control Valve and Injector Cleaning in Kissimmee, FL

    Brine draw depends on a venturi injector, a tiny nozzle and throat that pulls brine out of the salt tank as water rushes past. A few specks of grit can stop it cold. We pull the injector and screen, clean them, and replace the seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1 if they are worn.

    Brine Tank Cleanout and Inspection in Kissimmee, FL

    Brine tanks collect a gray sludge of salt fines and insoluble grit, and it builds up faster with cheap rock salt. We empty, scrub and rinse the tank. Then we look for cracks near the overflow elbow and the brine well and refill with fresh salt.

    Softener Upkeep for Restaurants and Shops in Kissimmee

    Ice machines, espresso machines, dish machines and boilers all scale up fast on hard water, and equipment warranties often require treated water. Each visit includes a hardness test, logged for your warranty file. Brine tank cleanouts come around more often on these systems.

    Resin Bed Checks and Regeneration Settings in Kissimmee

    Resin does the actual softening. The beads swap sodium for calcium and magnesium, and once they are loaded up they need brine to recharge. We compare the softened hardness against the gallons used since the last regeneration to see whether the bed still holds its rated capacity.

    Commercial Twin-Tank Maintenance in Kissimmee

    Salons, car washes, hotels and restaurants put far more gallons through a softener than any house does, and a hard-water day can mean spotted glassware or a scaled ice machine. Twin-tank and progressive-flow systems need closer checks on the meter and the tank changeover. Visits run monthly or quarterly, depending on volume.

    Salt Bridge Removal and Brine Tank Cleaning in Kissimmee, FL

    Humidity and cheap salt cause most salt bridges. A hard shell forms across the tank, and the salt underneath dissolves away, leaving a hollow. The softener keeps regenerating on schedule, but it draws plain water instead of brine, which leaves the resin without a recharge and hard water flows to every tap.
