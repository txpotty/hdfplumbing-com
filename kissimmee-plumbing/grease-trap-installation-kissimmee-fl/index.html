    In-Ground Gravity Interceptor Installation in Kissimmee, FL

    Larger restaurants and high-volume kitchens often need an outdoor gravity interceptor. These concrete or polyethylene tanks typically range from several hundred to over 1,500 gallons. We excavate, set the tank on a stable base, connect inlet and outlet piping and install manhole risers. Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and damage buried water and sewer lines. We plan excavation equipment around the ground conditions we find on site. A properly installed interceptor can go far longer between pump-outs than an indoor trap.

    Grease Trap Installation for Schools and Institutions in Kissimmee

    School cafeterias, hospitals, senior living facilities and churches with kitchens often need commercial interceptors.     Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in Kissimmee

    Hydromechanical grease traps sit under or near sinks and use baffles and flow control to separate grease from water. They are common in small cafes, coffee shops and quick-service restaurants with tight space. We install units from Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura sized by gallons per minute. Each install includes a flow control fitting and proper venting to keep the trap working as designed. These units need frequent cleaning, so we place them where staff can reach the lid easily.
