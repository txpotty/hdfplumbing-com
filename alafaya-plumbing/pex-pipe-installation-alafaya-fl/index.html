    Full Home PEX Repiping in Alafaya

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Alafaya, FL

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A is the most flexible and joins with expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and joins with crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C sits closer to B in feel. Every one is listed for potable water. Pipe, fittings and tools come from the same maker on our jobs, which keeps the warranty valid.

    PEX for Water Line Replacement in Alafaya

    Your service line runs from the meter or well to the house, usually 18 to 36 inches down. Because PEX comes on a long coil, the whole run from the meter to the foundation can go in with zero buried fittings. Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and damage buried water and sewer lines. It gets bedded in sand, not rocky backfill. Rock pressing on the pipe is the usual cause of an early failure.

    Whole-Home PEX Repiping in Alafaya

    Low pressure at every faucet, across the whole house, usually points at the pipe and not the fixtures. In an older house that usually means rust-choked galvanized or polybutylene with tired fittings, and a new faucet cartridge will not touch it. A whole-home repipe will. Every line from the main valve to each angle stop gets replaced, and the system is tested before anything gets closed up.

    Copper to PEX Conversions in Alafaya

    Pinhole leaks in copper rarely show up alone. Aggressive water, fast flow and soil contact under slabs can pit copper from the inside, and the first few pinholes are typically followed by more. Many wells in Florida produce water with a sulfur smell or high iron, which often calls for filtration to protect fixtures and appliances. A switch to PEX ends it. We use listed transition fittings anywhere PEX ties into copper that stays.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Alafaya

    PEX-B is stiffer, is cheaper by the roll, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. Since a crimp is only as good as the tool that made it, every ring gets a go/no-go gauge check, and any ring that fails gets cut off and redone. That takes seconds. Skipping it is how leaks start.
