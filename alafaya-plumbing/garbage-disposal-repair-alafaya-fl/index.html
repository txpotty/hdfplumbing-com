    Leak Detection and Repair in Alafaya, FL

    Disposal leaks come from four places: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. We dry everything, run water with a little food coloring and watch to see which joint shows color first, since water runs down the outside of the unit and hides the real source.     Flange and Inlet Leak Repair in Alafaya, FL
