    Frequently Asked Questions About Grease Trap Installation in Alafaya

    Installed prices for small indoor hydromechanical traps typically fall between $1,500 and $5,000.     Nearly every food business needs one. Codes require one wherever sinks or dish machines send greasy waste to the sewer. A few low-grease operations get waived, and the sewer authority makes that call. Ask before you sign the lease.

    For indoor traps we lean on Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura, because their parts are easy to find and their units carry PDI or ASME certification. Schier's plastic bodies do not rust, which helps in hot, wet kitchens. Brand matters less than sizing. We pick the rating first, then the brand that fits the space and budget.

    Two warranties apply here. Manufacturer coverage on the unit varies by brand and material, and plastic bodies usually get the longer terms. Our labor warranty covers the joints and the setting, and you get it in writing up front.

    In everyday talk the two names get swapped. Strictly, a grease trap is the small indoor unit rated in gallons per minute, and an interceptor is the large tank rated in gallons of capacity. Codes vary on the wording. Some plumbing codes call both of them interceptors and separate them as hydromechanical and gravity, so we use whatever terms your permit office uses.

    The common rule is to clean before grease and solids fill 25 percent of the trap's depth. For a small indoor trap in a busy kitchen, that can be weekly. Outdoor tanks go longer. Many gravity interceptors are pumped every 30 to 90 days, and your local FOG program may set a maximum interval no matter what the trap looks like.

    Size comes from flow, and flow comes from your fixtures. We add up the drain rate of each sink compartment, floor sink and dish machine, then apply the code's drain-down time to get gallons per minute. Gravity tanks use volume instead. Outdoor tanks get sized by the sewer authority's formula, which we run for you.

    Coverage comes from two places. Your trap carries a factory warranty that varies by brand and material. Our written labor warranty covers the joints we made and how we set the unit. Damage from skipped cleanings falls outside it.
