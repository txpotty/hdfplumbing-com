    Running out of hot water usually means the house outgrew the tank. A bigger tank or a higher first-hour rating handles more back-to-back showers, and sometimes a 50-gallon high-recovery unit matches a 75 in the same footprint. Bigger is not always needed.

    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Alafaya

    Electric water heaters in Alafaya usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. A bad element can be replaced.     Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Alafaya, FL

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters move heat from the surrounding air into the tank, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need room and airflow, about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they do best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. They also cool the room.
