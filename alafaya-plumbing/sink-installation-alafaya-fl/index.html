    Drop-In Sink Installation in Alafaya, FL

    Drop-in sinks are the most forgiving style. The rim hides small cutout flaws, so laminate tops take a drop-in with just a size check and the right sealant. Mounting clips snug it down. Stainless drop-ins weigh very little.

    Pedestal Sink Installation in Alafaya, FL

    Pedestal sinks hide almost nothing. Since the trap and stops are right behind the column, we center the drain and use chrome parts that look finished. The wall needs solid blocking. The basin hangs from it, not the pedestal.

    Emergency Sink Replacement in Alafaya

    A cracked bowl cannot be patched for long. When a bowl cracks or a drain rusts through, we cap the lines first so the house keeps water, then set a new sink. Stock sizes are often same-day. Odd cutouts take longer.

    Commercial Sink Installation in Alafaya, FL

    Commercial kitchens need sinks the health inspector will sign off on. A three-bay sink drains indirectly through an air gap to a floor sink, and hand sinks need tempered water near each prep area. Plan review comments come first. Mop basins and grease interceptor ties are part of it.

    Kitchen Sink Replacement in Alafaya

    Pulling the old kitchen sink is half the job. Old basket strainers fuse to the bowl, the disposal has to come off its mounting ring, and the dishwasher drain hose needs to be rerouted to the new disposal or tailpiece with a high loop. After that, the new bowl goes in. We reuse the disposal if it still runs well.

    Wall-Mount and ADA Sink Installation in Alafaya, FL

    Wall-hung lavatories need a carrier or heavy blocking. For an accessible bathroom we set the rim no higher than 34 inches, leave knee clearance underneath, and wrap or shield the hot water and drain pipes so a seated user cannot touch them. Offset drains help with knee space. Lever or sensor faucets finish it off.

    Vessel Sink Installation in Alafaya, FL

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. That raises the rim several inches, so we either pick a taller vessel faucet or mount a wall faucet, and we use a vessel drain that matches the bowl's overflow or lack of one. Bowls without an overflow need an unslotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.

    Kitchen Sink Installation in Alafaya

    Kitchen sinks come in four mounts: drop-in, undermount, farmhouse and flush-set. A cast iron farmhouse bowl can top a hundred pounds empty, so the base cabinet often gets reinforced before it goes in. We check the cabinet before the sink arrives. Disposal and dishwasher connections are part of the job.
