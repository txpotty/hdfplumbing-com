    Repeated repairs add up. A valve on its third cartridge in five years is probably worn inside, so one replacement beats more repairs. We track that on your invoice. Newer valves often come with lifetime cartridge warranties.

    Thermostatic Tub Valve Installation in Alafaya, FL

    Thermostatic valves hold the temperature you set. Unlike a pressure-balance valve, which only reacts to pressure changes, a thermostatic valve senses the actual water temperature and adjusts the mix, which matters for kids and older adults who cannot react fast to a hot spike. Many have separate heat and flow controls. They cost more than pressure-balance.

    Standard Bathtub Faucet Replacement in Alafaya

    Single-handle and three-handle tub faucets come out differently. A single-handle valve often takes new trim and a cartridge from the front, while a three-handle valve usually has to be cut out of the wall and replaced with a modern pressure-balance body. We check before quoting. Two-handle models fall in between.

    Tub Faucet Repair vs Replacement Costs in Alafaya, FL

    A cartridge costs much less than a valve. Replacement inside the wall makes sense when parts are discontinued, the valve body is cracked or pitted, or you want anti-scald protection on an old three-handle faucet that has none. You see both prices. Most people choose repair first.

    Diverter Valve Repair and Replacement in Alafaya, FL

    Water running from the spout during a shower means a failing diverter. Spout diverters are a quick swap. Middle stems need a seat too.
