    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Alafaya, FL

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. HDF Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. They work best on very hard water.
