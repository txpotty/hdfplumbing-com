    Root Intrusion Repair in Alafaya, FL

    Roots enter at the joints, where clay bells or cast iron hubs have a small gap or a failed gasket. Once in, they fill the pipe. With no joints left, there is nowhere for them to return.

    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Alafaya, FL

    Shifting soil is one of the main reasons sewer joints separate, crack or drop out of grade over the years. Cast iron scales and thins, and clay bells crack under the weight of soil and traffic.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Alafaya

    A bursting head is a steel cone, slightly wider than the old pipe, pulled through the line by a hydraulic unit or a winch cable. It cracks the old pipe outward into the soil while a string of new HDPE pipe follows right behind it. Fused joints, no gaps. This is the fix for collapsed pipe.

    Trenchless Drain Pipe Repair in Alafaya, FL

    Smaller drain lines inside the house can be lined too, including 2-inch kitchen drains and the 3-inch cast iron stacks found in many older homes.     Sectional Point Repairs in Alafaya

    A point repair is a short liner, often 3 to 5 feet long, set over one bad spot. We push it in on a packer, guided by the camera footage, and the packer inflates to press the resin-soaked sleeve against the pipe wall until it cures. It fixes a single crack or root entry, not a whole failing line. Sound lines with one bad joint are the right candidates.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Lining in Alafaya, FL

    Cast iron rusts from the inside out, and the bottom of the pipe goes first. That is called channel rot. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or carbide cutters spinning on a flexible shaft, which brings the wall back to bare metal so a liner can bond to it. Under a slab, lining beats breaking up the floor in two rooms.
