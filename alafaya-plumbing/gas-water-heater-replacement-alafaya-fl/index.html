    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Alafaya, FL

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. We never swap a natural gas tank onto propane without the right conversion. We confirm pressure with a manometer.

    Gas Water Heater Efficiency Ratings in Alafaya

    Efficiency ratings on tank heaters are listed as UEF, uniform energy factor, and a higher number means less gas per gallon heated. Basic tanks land near 0.60 UEF. High-efficiency condensing tanks reach 0.80 or more and can qualify for rebates.

    Tankless Gas Conversion in Alafaya

    Going tankless usually means a bigger gas line, since a whole-house tankless unit can draw 199,000 BTU against the 40,000 a tank burner uses. Most homes need the line upsized from the meter.
