    Foundation Leak Detection and Repair in Melbourne, FL

    A leak under a slab often shows up as a warm spot on the floor. Sometimes you hear water running.     Galvanized and Polybutylene Line Replacement in Melbourne

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside until little water gets through. Polybutylene splits instead. If your buried service is either one, patches are usually temporary, so we replace the line with PEX or Type K copper, often pulling the new pipe through the old path to limit digging. Pressure comes back right away.

    Sewer Line Underground Repair in Melbourne, FL

    Roots and settling soil cause most buried sewer failures. Age does the rest. We offer open trench repair, pipe bursting and cured-in-place lining, and the camera footage decides which one fits the depth, the damage and what sits on top of the line. All work meets Florida plumbing code.

    Storm Drain and Lateral Repair in Melbourne, FL

    Storm laterals carry roof and yard water away from the foundation. When they fail, water pools. We jet the line, camera it for crushed or separated sections and repair broken runs so runoff gets carried off instead of sitting next to the house. The outlet gets cleared too.

    Irrigation and Yard Line Repair in Melbourne

    Sprinkler supply lines sit shallow and break easily. Shovels and roots get them.     Underground Storm Drain Repair in Melbourne, FL

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Melbourne

    Polybutylene was used in a lot of homes built in the 1980s and early 1990s. It splits suddenly. The gray plastic turns brittle as chlorine in the water attacks it, with failures most common at fittings and tight bends. Replacement is the only lasting fix. We use PEX or copper.
