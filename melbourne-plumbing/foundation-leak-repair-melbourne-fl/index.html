    Foundation Leak Detection in Melbourne

    Accurate detection is the most important step in foundation leak repair. We isolate hot and cold lines and pressure test them to confirm a leak exists.     Direct Foundation Leak Repair in Melbourne, FL

    When the leak is in an accessible spot and the rest of the pipe is in good condition, direct repair is often the most affordable choice. Flooring can then be restored by your flooring contractor.
