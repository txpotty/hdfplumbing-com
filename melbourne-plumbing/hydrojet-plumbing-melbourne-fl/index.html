    For most homes, hydrojetting every year or two is enough for preventive maintenance, especially in areas with hard water or older plumbing.     Snaking involves using an auger to punch a hole through a clog, usually leaving behind some debris.     Jetting removes the roots inside the pipe, but roots will grow back if the joint or crack remains open. Regular jetting can control them for years. Pipe lining or repair is the permanent fix.
