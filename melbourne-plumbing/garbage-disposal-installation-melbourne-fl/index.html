    Disposal Installation in Melbourne, FL: How We Do It

    Switching From Batch-Feed to Continuous-Feed in Melbourne, FL

    Going from batch-feed to continuous-feed means adding a way to switch it on. That can be a wall switch, an air switch button on the sink deck or a switched outlet under the sink, and the choice depends on how hard it is to run wire to the spot you want. Air switches avoid new wiring in the wall. The drain usually stays the same.

    Dishwasher Connection With Disposal Installation in Melbourne

    Every new disposal has a plug sealing the dishwasher inlet. We knock it out with a screwdriver and hammer, fish the plug out of the chamber and connect the hose with a new clamp. A plug left in backs up the dishwasher. An air gap or high loop goes in too, which keeps sink water from running back into the dishwasher.

    Batch-Feed Units for Kitchens Without a Switch in Melbourne

    Batch-feed disposals only run when the stopper lid is in place, which adds safety for families with kids. They need no wall switch, so they are a good choice for kitchens where adding a switch would mean cutting into finished drywall or tile backsplash. Mounting and drains are standard.

    High-Capacity Disposal Installation in Melbourne, FL

    Busy kitchens get the most from a 1 HP unit, like an InSinkErator Evolution or a Waste King Legend model. We confirm the circuit can carry the motor and the drain can handle the flow, because a strong unit pushing into a flat trap arm will still clog. These units weigh more. They get supported during mounting.

    Garbage Disposal Installation Cost Factors in Melbourne, FL

    Most of the price comes from the unit and the electrical setup. A plain swap with power already in place costs the least, and new wiring, a lowered drain or a reworked double bowl each add labor as a separate line. Unit and labor are quoted together.

    High-Horsepower Disposal Installation in Melbourne

    A 3/4 or 1 HP unit is bigger and heavier than a starter model, so we measure the cabinet first. Taller units can push the discharge outlet below the wall stub, and when that happens we either lower the drain at the wall or pick a model with a higher outlet so water does not sit in the chamber. Power needs a check too. A larger motor pulls more current when it starts.

    Disposals in Remodels and New Builds in Melbourne

    On new builds and remodels in Melbourne, we work with your contractor so the disposal, the sink and the dishwasher are planned together. That usually means a dedicated circuit for the disposal, a drain stub set low enough for the trap and a dishwasher hose path that allows a proper high loop or air gap. Early planning prevents rework.

    Disposal Installation for Double Sinks in Melbourne, FL

    Double bowl sinks put the disposal on one side and a continuous waste or end outlet tee on the other. We lay out the drain so both bowls drain freely and the disposal outlet does not push water into the other bowl, which happens when the tee sits too close to the disposal discharge. Heavy-wall fittings last longer.
