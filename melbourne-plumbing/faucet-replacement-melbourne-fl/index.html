    Touchless Faucet Upgrade in Melbourne

    Swapping a standard faucet for a touchless one takes a little more planning. Touchless models need a dry spot on the cabinet wall for the control box and either batteries or an outlet within reach of the cord. Power gets checked first. We adjust the sensor range at the end.

    Outdoor Faucet and Hose Bib Replacement in Melbourne, FL

    A split hose bib often looks fine from outside. Frozen pipes are rare in most of Florida, and many homes have outdoor plumbing that would never hold up in a northern winter. The crack is usually behind the siding, inside the wall, where water left in the pipe froze or the body just wore out after years of being cranked shut. We check the wall cavity. Frost-free sillcocks with built-in vacuum breakers go in as the replacement where freezing is possible.

    Budget-Friendly Faucet Replacement in Melbourne, FL

    Not every faucet swap needs a designer model. A mid-range faucet from Moen, Delta or Pfister with a ceramic cartridge and a brass body will outlast a pricey import with a plastic waterway. We will tell you where to spend. Finish and spout style are where the price climbs, not the parts that keep it dry.

    Delta Faucet Replacement in Melbourne, FL

    Delta faucets come in a few cartridge families, and the model number tells you which. Older single-handle models use a ball and seats, while newer ones take a cartridge, which changes whether repair or replacement makes more sense. We identify the model on site. A sound body sometimes only needs parts.

    Crusty Bathroom Faucet Replacement in Melbourne, FL

    Crusty lav faucets are the most common swap we do. We pull the old centerset or widespread faucet, scrape the deck down to bare porcelain and install a new faucet with a matching pop-up drain so the finishes line up. Figure an hour. We line up the handles before we leave.

    Moen Faucet Replacement in Melbourne

    Moen's 1225 cartridge runs a huge share of single-handle Moen faucets. When it sticks, the handle gets stiff and the temperature drifts, and a cartridge that has been in place for fifteen years can be welded in by scale. Sometimes pulling it cracks the body. That is when we replace the faucet.

    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Replacement in Melbourne

    Pull-down spray heads take a beating. After a few years, the hose wears thin, the spray button sticks and the head will not dock the way it did. We swap the whole faucet. The new hose weight gets clipped where the head docks cleanly, and both supplies are new braided lines.

    Utility Sink Faucet Replacement in Melbourne

    Utility faucets in garages and laundry rooms take hard use. People hang buckets on the spout and yank hoses off the threads, which loosens the mounting and cracks the supply connections over time. We use brass-bodied replacements. The new faucet is anchored tight to the wall or the tub.
