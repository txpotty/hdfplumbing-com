    Annual Sump Pump Check-ups in Melbourne

    Regular annual maintenance is crucial for the longevity and reliability of your sump pump.     Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in Melbourne, FL

    Not every sump sits in a basement. Many homes with crawlspaces or deep window wells use small pumps to clear runoff.     Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Melbourne, FL

    The float switch is a critical component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. A stuck or improperly adjusted float switch can lead to the pump running continuously or failing to activate at all. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to support reliable operation for your home in Melbourne, FL.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Check-Ups in Melbourne, FL

    For homes equipped with pedestal sump pumps, HDF Plumbing provides dedicated maintenance services in Melbourne, FL. We focus on inspecting the external motor, confirming proper lubrication and checking the impeller for any obstructions. This type of pump, with its motor above the water, requires specific attention to its shaft and bearings to prevent premature wear and ensure reliable performance during heavy rains.

    Battery Backup Pump Maintenance in Melbourne

    Severe storms often bring power outages along with the rain. A battery backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or fails. We load test the battery, check the charger and alarm and run the backup pump on its own. Most backup batteries need replacing every three to five years.

    Submersible Sump Pump Service in Melbourne
