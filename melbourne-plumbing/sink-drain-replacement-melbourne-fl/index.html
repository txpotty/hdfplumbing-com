    Kitchen Sink Drain Replacement in Melbourne

    Kitchen drains start failing at the basket strainer. Old strainer putty dries out and the locknut rusts, so water runs down the tailpiece and looks like a trap leak. Strainer and trap get swapped as a pair. Disposal sinks get a new flange instead.

    Utility and Basement Sink Drain Replacement in Melbourne

    Basement sinks often drain to a pump. Sinks below the sewer line drain through a pump, so we check its valve and discharge line during the job. Pump trouble is easy to miss. A gurgling pit is a clue.
