    Commercial Basement Waterproofing in Melbourne

    Sump Pump Installation & Repair in Melbourne

    A sump pump lives in a pit cut below floor level and kicks on when a float rises. Stuck floats, worn motors and failed check valves cause most of the service calls we run on sumps. We test yours with a bucket. A battery backup is worth adding where power drops in storms.

    Water Damage Assessment in Melbourne, FL

    Before any repair, we figure out where the water came from and how far it spread. Moisture meters on drywall and baseboards show the wet area, and a thermal camera picks up cold spots where water is wicking behind the finish. Guessing costs money. You get photos and readings for your insurance file.

    Exterior Waterproofing Solutions in Melbourne, FL

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing, cleaning the wall, and applying a membrane with a drainage board over it. It is the most thorough fix and the most disruptive one. Expect a few days of work. Our crew handles the footing drain and its outlet, and a waterproofing partner applies the membrane.

    Clogged Drain Tile Repair in Melbourne

    Footing drains clog with silt and roots over the years, and once they stop flowing the water table at the wall rises. A camera run from the sump inlet shows us where the tile is plugged. A jetter usually opens it. If the old clay tile has collapsed, it gets replaced with new perforated pipe.

    Crack Injection for Basements in Melbourne

    Injection works on poured concrete walls, not block. We set ports along the crack, seal the face with epoxy paste, and pump in polyurethane or epoxy from the bottom port up until resin shows at the next port. Epoxy adds strength. Polyurethane is better for active leaks because it foams on contact with water.

    Supply Line Leak Repair in Melbourne

    Water lines along a basement ceiling can leak at fittings, valves or pinholes. Around Melbourne, flushing the water heater yearly and descaling tankless units helps keep scale buildup under control. We cut out the bad section and replace it with copper or PEX, and if we find more than two or three pinholes on one run, we recommend repiping that run instead of patching it again. Repiping a basement ceiling is usually a one-day job.

    Emergency Basement Leak Repair in Melbourne, FL

    A pipe that lets go in a basement can put an inch of water on the floor in under an hour. First, close the main water shutoff. Our plumber stops the leak and repairs the pipe, then restores water so the drying can start. We can also refer a water mitigation company.
