    Gas Water Heater Work in Melbourne, FL

    40 and 50 Gallon Tank Replacement in Melbourne

    Forty and fifty gallon gas tanks are the workhorse in most Florida homes, and a leak at the bottom seam means the tank is finished. Newer tanks can run taller than the old one. We measure the closet before ordering. Plan on about two to three hours.

    Large Capacity 75 Gallon Gas Heaters in Melbourne, FL

    Big families with soaking tubs often outgrow a 50 gallon tank and need a 75 gallon heater or a high-recovery 50 at around 65,000 to 75,000 BTU. Those burners need more gas. We size the gas line before installing, since an undersized line cuts recovery.

    A.O. Smith Gas Water Heater Service in Melbourne

    A.O. Smith makes tanks under its own name and also builds the State line, so parts overlap a lot between the two brands. We handle the sizing and the gas connection. Exhaust venting gets checked against their specs.

    Atmospheric Vent Upgrades in Melbourne, FL

    Flue pipes on older atmospheric heaters rust at the joints and lose their upward slope, and a loose connector can spill exhaust into the closet. We replace damaged sections and the draft hood if needed. We check draft with a match or mirror at the hood.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Melbourne, FL

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through 2 or 3-inch PVC, so they can sit far from a chimney. A standard outlet has to be within reach of the cord. We run the vent within the maker's length and elbow limits.

    Thermal Expansion Tank Installation in Melbourne

    Closed systems are the reason expansion tanks exist. A pressure regulator or backflow preventer on the main stops heated water from pushing back to the street, so pressure climbs every time the burner runs. Code commonly calls for an expansion tank. We precharge it to your house pressure.

    Power Vent Water Heater Replacement in Melbourne

    Blower motors and pressure switches are the weak points on power vent tanks. When one fails twice on a tank that is already 8 or 9 years old, replacing the whole heater is usually cheaper over time. We confirm a dedicated outlet first.

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Melbourne, FL

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. A natural gas unit does not go on propane unless the manufacturer allows a conversion. We confirm pressure with a manometer.
