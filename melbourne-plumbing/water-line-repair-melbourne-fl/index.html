    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Melbourne, FL

    A single leak on a newer copper or PEX line is usually a quick spot repair. Repeat leaks, galvanized pipe or a line older than about 40 years usually justify full replacement. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars, while a full replacement costs more but resets the clock for decades. We show you the condition of the pipe we expose so you can make the call with actual information.

    PEX Water Line Repair in Melbourne, FL

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a flexible, durable, and corrosion-resistant option more and more used in homes across Melbourne, FL. While PEX is less prone to leaks than older materials, damage can still occur from improper installation, rodent chewing, or impact. Our team is fully experienced in PEX repair, using specialized crimping tools and fittings to make strong, leak-proof connections. We make sure your PEX system continues to perform well.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Melbourne

    Many water line problems are made worse by high incoming pressure. A failed pressure reducing valve can push well over 80 psi into your plumbing, which stresses the service line and every fixture. We test static pressure with a gauge and replace worn regulators from brands like Watts and Zurn Wilkins. We also swap seized gate valves for full-port ball valves so you can shut the water off quickly in an emergency.

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Melbourne, FL

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Around Melbourne, flushing the water heater yearly and descaling tankless units helps keep scale buildup under control. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which chokes flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe seldom holds up because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and stops repeat leaks.

    Main Water Line Repair in Melbourne

    The main water line brings water from the municipal supply to your home. When this line leaks or breaks, it can cause significant water loss, foundation damage, and low water pressure. Our team specializes in diagnosing and repairing main water line issues, from pinpoint leaks to full pipe ruptures, so your home's primary water source is secure. We use advanced detection methods to find the exact location of the problem, limiting excavation and disruption to your property in Melbourne. We also check for common issues like root intrusion or shifting soil.

    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Melbourne

    Slab leaks, where water lines running beneath your home's concrete foundation leak, are a especially challenging issue. Houses from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s may have polybutylene supply pipe, which many insurers dislike and owners choose to replace. These leaks can undermine your foundation, leading to serious structural damage. We use specialized listening devices and camera inspections to locate slab leaks precisely and offer repair options including direct slab penetration, re-routing, or pipe lining. Addressing a slab leak quickly in Melbourne is crucial to prevent extensive, costly foundation damage.
