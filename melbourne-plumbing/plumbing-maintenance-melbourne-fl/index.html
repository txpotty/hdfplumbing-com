    What a Plumbing Checkup Covers in Melbourne, FL

    Sewer and Drain Camera Inspection in Melbourne, FL

    A camera shows what the sewer looks like inside. Roots, sags, cracks and grease show up on camera, especially in old clay and cast iron. You get a copy of the video. We lay out options if something needs work.

    Sump Pump Testing in Melbourne, FL

    Basements flood when the pump fails during a storm. Hurricanes and tropical storms bring flooding, power loss, and sewer backups to Florida, often overwhelming drainage systems for days. We fill the pit to test the float, check the discharge line and confirm the check valve holds. Battery backups get load tested.

    Gas Line and Pipe Material Checks in Melbourne, FL

    Gas leaks are the one item we never skip. We check visible gas piping and appliance connectors for corrosion and leaks, confirm the shutoff valves turn, and note any old flexible connectors that should be replaced before they crack. Pipe material matters too. Older pipe types get flagged with repipe choices.

    Slab Leak and Hidden Leak Checks in Melbourne

    Many homes in Florida built before the mid 1970s have cast iron drains under the slab, which often crack and corrode with age. Soil movement and hard water can wear buried lines until they leak out of sight for months. Warm floors are a clue. So is a meter that spins with everything off. Acoustic and thermal gear pinpoint it.

    Drain Camera Checks During Maintenance in Melbourne

    Sewer problems hide underground. The camera finds roots, grease, sags and offsets before they back up into the house. Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and damage buried water and sewer lines. We show you the video. Catching a cracked joint early can mean a spot repair instead of a full replacement.

    Whole-Home Plumbing Inspection in Melbourne, FL

    Room by room, fixture by fixture. Under-sink checks, toilet valve tests, fixture runs, washer hose inspection and a pressure reading cover the house. Washers and flappers get replaced that day. Larger repairs get a written price.

    Water Softener Service in Melbourne

    A softener only helps if it regenerates. Salt level, brine tank, injector and a test regeneration all get checked. Hardness gets tested after. A failed softener sends hard water through the house without warning.

    Hot Water Tank Service in Melbourne, FL

    Gas and electric tanks need different checks. On gas units we look at the burner flame, the flue draft and the gas connection for leaks, and on electric units we test the elements and the thermostats and check the wiring for heat damage. Sediment comes out either way. Scale on the tank floor wastes energy and shortens tank life.
