    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Melbourne, FL

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A is the most flexible and joins with expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and joins with crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C sits closer to B in feel. All three are code-listed for drinking water. We match the pipe to the fittings and tools from one maker so the warranty stays intact.

    PEX for Water Line Replacement in Melbourne

    Your service line runs from the meter or well to the house, usually 18 to 36 inches down. A high water table across much of Florida affects drainage, septic design, and how easily crews can dig for underground repairs. It gets bedded in sand, not rocky backfill. Sharp rock against plastic pipe is how a new line fails early.
