    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in Melbourne

    Hydromechanical grease traps sit under or near sinks and use baffles and flow control to separate grease from water. They are common in small cafes, coffee shops and quick-service restaurants with tight space. We install units from Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura sized by gallons per minute.     Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in Melbourne

    Hydro-mechanical grease interceptors, often called HGI units, are compact and designed for indoor use, typically under a sink or on the floor. These units use baffles to slow down wastewater flow, allowing grease to separate and accumulate. HDF Plumbing installs various sizes of HGI units, ensuring proper venting, leveling, and connection to your drainage system in Melbourne for maximum efficiency.
