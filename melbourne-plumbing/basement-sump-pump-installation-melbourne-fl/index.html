    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Melbourne

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the water. That keeps it dry and easy to reach, which is why they suit narrow pits under 14 inches across, and why they often outlast cheap submersibles in Melbourne homes. They are louder, though.

    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Melbourne

    Older homes often have narrow pits that will not fit a submersible. A pedestal pump fits where nothing else will, and we install it with rigid discharge, a quality check valve, and a float rod set to your pit depth so it starts before water reaches the top of the liner. They are noisy but durable.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Melbourne

    Outages and heavy rain tend to show up together. Without house power, a battery backup keeps a second pump going, and a fully charged 75 amp-hour battery can run a typical backup pump for several hours of on-and-off cycling, longer on a light night. We load test the battery.

    Primary Sump Pump Replacement in Melbourne
