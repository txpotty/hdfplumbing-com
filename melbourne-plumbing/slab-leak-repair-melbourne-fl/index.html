    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Melbourne, FL

    Hot lines leak under slabs more than cold lines. Heat is the reason. The pipe swells and shrinks against rock every time hot water flows, and a recirculation pump keeps that going constantly, thinning the wall. We repair or reroute the line and can set the recirculation pump on a timer.

    Sewer Line Slab Leak Repair in Melbourne, FL

    Drain lines under the slab leak too. You'll often notice an odor. We run a sewer camera through the line to find the cracked fitting, belly or root intrusion, then repair it by tunneling, cutting the slab or lining the pipe, depending on where the damage is and how long it runs. Health codes in Melbourne, FL cover this work.

    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Melbourne, FL

    Direct repair is often the most practical fix for a single leak. We cut a small square. With the pipe exposed, the damaged length is cut out, new pipe is joined in, and the line is pressure tested before the soil goes back in and gets compacted in layers. The concrete is patched flush with the floor.

    PEX Repiping for Slab Leaks in Melbourne, FL

    Two or three slab leaks in a few years point to the pipe, not bad luck. That is when a repipe pays off. PEX bends with the structure, won't corrode and runs overhead, so a whole-house repipe leaves nothing under the slab. It suits many Melbourne homes.

    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Melbourne, FL

    Spot repairs fit leaks in otherwise good pipe. One cut, one fix. The floor gets opened, the pipe replaced and tested, and the trench compacted and patched, ready for your flooring contractor.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Melbourne

    Cold leaks don't make warm spots. The bill gives them away. They show up as a higher bill, a meter that turns with every faucet off, or a patch of carpet that stays damp, and they soak the soil just as much as hot leaks do. Your Melbourne home is no exception. We isolate the cold line and fix it or run a new one.

    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Melbourne

    A reroute abandons the leaking pipe under the slab. The new pipe runs through the attic. For older Melbourne homes, it bypasses the whole buried run of tired copper or galvanized and removes the chance of another leak there. We cut small openings at fixture walls.

    Slab Leak Detection in Melbourne

    The first test is the water meter. We shut off everything first. A meter that keeps turning tells us to isolate hot from cold, test each side and walk the losing line with a ground mic. Thermal imaging shows hot water leaks as warm areas on the floor.
