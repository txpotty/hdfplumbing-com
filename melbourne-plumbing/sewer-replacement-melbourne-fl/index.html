    Traditional Trench Sewer Replacement in Melbourne

    An open trench gives full access to the pipe from the house to the main. A small excavator does most of it, with hand digging near other lines. The old pipe comes out, the trench bottom gets a bed of gravel or sand, and new 4-inch PVC goes in at a steady quarter inch of fall per foot, confirmed with a level on every length. Then the camera.

    Advanced Diagnostics for Sewer Issues in Melbourne, FL

    Diagnosis comes before price. The camera shows what is wrong, the locator shows where. We sometimes add a hydro-jet cleaning first, because a line full of grease can hide a crack, then camera again so we are looking at the pipe itself. Guesswork costs money.

    Commercial Sewer Replacement in Melbourne

    Bigger pipe, more flow, more rules. Commercial laterals usually start at 6 inches. We handle the permit, set up bypass pumping when the building cannot shut down, and coordinate traffic control if the tap sits in the street, keeping the outage as short as the job allows. Night shifts possible.

    Sewer Line Material Upgrades in Melbourne

    Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg all failed for different reasons. Modern PVC does not rust, flatten or let roots in at glued joints. Schedule 40 or SDR 35 PVC is what most codes call for on a lateral, and we match the grade to the depth and load over the pipe. Glued joints stay sealed.

    Trenchless Sewer Repair Options in Melbourne, FL

    Pipe bursting needs two pits. We dig a launch pit near the house and a receiving pit near the main. A cone-shaped head is pulled through the old line, breaking it outward, while fused HDPE pipe follows right behind it, so the new line takes the same path without a trench. Driveways stay put.

    Commercial Sewer Line Replacement in Melbourne

    Commercial laterals are often 6-inch pipe and carry grease or heavier flows. Restaurants usually have a grease interceptor in the line too. We schedule the work around your hours, set up temporary bypass pumping when the building needs to stay open, and coordinate with the city on any work at the tap. Weekend starts are common.

    Sewer Scope Inspections for Replacement in Melbourne

    A sewer camera on a push cable goes in through the cleanout. The footage shows each joint, crack and root intrusion. A small transmitter called a sonde sits behind the camera head, letting us mark the depth and location from the surface, which keeps the digging small. Bring questions.

    Foundation Protection during Sewer Replacement in Melbourne, FL

    Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and crack buried water and sewer lines. A sewer leaking under or beside the house keeps the soil wet in one spot, which can lead to uneven settling of the slab. When we replace a line near the foundation we dig carefully, avoid undermining footings, and tamp each layer of fill. Slow work near the house.
