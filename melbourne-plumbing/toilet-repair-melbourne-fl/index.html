    Leaking Toilet Repair in Melbourne

    Water on the floor can come from four places: the wax ring, the tank-to-bowl gasket, the supply line or sweat running off a cold tank. Each one needs a different fix. We wipe it all dry and watch where the first drop forms, which takes about ten minutes and saves pulling a toilet that did not need it. If the porcelain itself is cracked, we price a new bowl rather than trying to seal it.

    Clogged Toilet Clearing in Melbourne, FL

    Most clogs sit in the trapway, the curved channel cast into the porcelain, and a closet auger with a rubber-sleeved head clears them without scratching the bowl. Plungers miss some. If the auger comes back clean and the toilet still backs up, the blockage is further down the drain or in the main line. At that point we run a cable from the nearest cleanout or send a camera down to see it.

    Sensor and Flushometer Repair in Melbourne

    Sensor flush valves fail in their own ways, from dead batteries and dirty sensor eyes to solenoids that stick open and keep a bowl running. Before replacing parts, we test the sensor and the solenoid. Batteries are the cheap fix. If the valve flushes on its own, the sensor range or a dirty lens is usually to blame.

    Cracked Tank and Supply Line Repair in Melbourne, FL

    Braided supply lines last years, but the plastic nut on the toilet end can crack and a bulge in the braid means the inner hose is about to burst. Every visit includes a look at the supply line. Bulges get replaced on the spot. Old vinyl or solid plastic lines get swapped for braided stainless with metal nuts.

    Fill Valve Replacement in Melbourne

    Old ballcock valves with a brass arm and a big plastic float are loud and slow to refill, and they tend to stick open when scale builds up in the ports. We replace them with an adjustable modern fill valve from Fluidmaster or Korky and set the height so the critical level mark sits at least an inch above the overflow. It is a twenty-minute job. You will notice the difference the first night, because the tank stops hissing.

    Running Toilet Repair in Melbourne

    A tank that keeps hissing and refilling every few minutes is usually leaking past the flapper or overfilling into the overflow tube. It adds up fast. We check the chain, the float height, the flapper and the seat before touching a part, then replace only what failed. Most running toilets are quiet again in about thirty minutes.

    Leaking Toilet Base Repair in Melbourne, FL

    A rocking bowl breaks the wax seal a little more every time someone sits down, and eventually the leak shows up as a stain or a soft spot. On slab floors the flange is set in concrete and can crack if the closet bolts are overtightened. We check it. A cracked flange gets a stainless repair ring or a new flange before the toilet goes back down.

    Toilet Flush Repair in Melbourne, FL

    Two or three tries to get a full flush points to a flapper closing too early, a chain hooked in the wrong hole, scale in the rim jets, or a cracked overflow tube letting water bypass the bowl. You can usually see which one with the lid off. We watch a full cycle with the lid off and replace the flush valve if the overflow tube is cracked, since it cannot be patched.
