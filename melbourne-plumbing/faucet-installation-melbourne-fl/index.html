    Frequently Asked Questions About Faucet Installation in Melbourne

    Labor for a standard kitchen or bathroom faucet installation typically costs $150 to $350. Touchless faucets, wall-mount faucets and pot fillers range from $300 to $800 or more depending on the work involved. We give you a flat price up front.

    While some homeowners attempt DIY faucet installation, it can be harder than it looks. Improper installation can lead to leaks, water damage, and issues with water pressure or drainage. Hiring a professional ensures the job is done correctly, follows plumbing codes, and includes a warranty on labor, saving you potential headaches and expensive repairs down the road.

    Most standard faucet installations can be completed within 1 to 3 hours. More complex installations, such as those requiring new drilling or significant plumbing changes, may take longer. We aim to complete the job promptly while ensuring quality workmanship.

    We install all major faucet brands, including Moen, Delta, Kohler, American Standard, Pfister, and more. Our plumbers are well versed in the specific installation requirements for a wide range of manufacturers and models.

    Common signs include persistent dripping or leaking, low water pressure specifically from the faucet, visible corrosion or rust, difficulty turning handles, or an outdated appearance. Constant repairs often signal it is time for a replacement.

    If your current faucet has internal blockages or a faulty aerator, a new faucet can improve water pressure at that specific fixture. However, if the low pressure is a whole-house issue, a new faucet alone will not solve the problem.

    Moen, Delta and Kohler are solid choices with strong warranties and easy-to-find parts. Grohe and Brizo offer high-end options. We can recommend models based on your style and budget.

    Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving stubborn scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. Where water carries a lot of minerals, deposits can build up inside faucet components over time, causing reduced water flow, stubborn stains, and faster wear on internal seals and cartridges. Regular cleaning and professional installation can help reduce these effects, and new faucets are often more resilient.
