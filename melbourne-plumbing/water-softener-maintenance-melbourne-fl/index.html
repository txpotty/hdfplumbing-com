    Control Valve and Injector Cleaning in Melbourne, FL

    Brine draw depends on a venturi injector, a tiny nozzle and throat that pulls brine out of the salt tank as water rushes past. It does not take much debris to block it.     Bypass Valve and Drain Line Repairs in Melbourne, FL

    A bypass valve that does not seat fully lets raw water blend into the softened supply, and the hardness test is usually the first clue. We rebuild or replace the bypass and check the drain line for kinks. The drain line has to end above its receptor with an air gap.

    Hardness Testing at the Tap in Melbourne

    We test with a titration kit, counting drops until the sample changes color, because test strips are too rough for setting a valve. We take the raw sample from an unsoftened hose bib or with the softener on bypass. Softened water comes from the kitchen tap.

    Resin Bed Checks and Regeneration Settings in Melbourne

    Resin does the actual softening. Each bead trades sodium for calcium and magnesium until it runs out of capacity. We compare the softened hardness against the gallons used since the last regeneration to see whether the bed still holds its rated capacity.
