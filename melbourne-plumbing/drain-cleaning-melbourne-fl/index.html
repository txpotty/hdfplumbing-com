    Kitchen Drain Cleaning in Melbourne

    Grease is the big one in kitchens. Hot grease cools in the line and hardens on the pipe wall. Bits of food and soap catch on that coating until the 1-1/2 inch line is down to the size of a pencil, and we clear it with a drum machine that is safe for PVC and old cast iron alike.

    Jetting Versus Cabling in Melbourne, FL

    Cabling and jetting solve different problems. A sectional cable is built to chop and pull a clog out. A jetter scrubs the pipe wall clean and flushes the debris downstream to the city main or septic tank, so we cable first on a hard root mass and jet afterward when the line has years of buildup on it.

    Office and Retail Drains in Melbourne, FL

    Restroom batteries, mop sinks and floor sinks all clog in their own way. Paper towels are the usual suspect. Our crew handles floor sinks, mop sinks and restroom lines outside of business hours when that works better for you, and you get a written report for your maintenance file.

    Bathroom Sink, Tub and Shower Drains in Melbourne, FL

    Toothpaste and soap scum glue hair into a stringy plug. Pop-up stoppers catch it first. We pull the stopper, clean the trap and send a small cable down the branch line toward the stack, and if the stopper keeps catching hair we can adjust it or swap it for one with a simpler design. Usually under an hour.

    Bathroom Drain Cleaning in Melbourne, FL

    Hair mixed with soap scum makes a rope that wraps around the stopper linkage. Most bath clogs sit within a foot of the drain.     Hydro Jetting for Drains in Melbourne

    Jetting does what a cable cannot. Water at around 3,000 to 4,000 PSI scours the whole pipe wall. A cable punches a hole, while a jetter cleans the pipe back to near its original diameter. It works best on a kitchen or main line that keeps clogging every few months no matter how often it gets cabled.

The pipe gets cleaned all the way to the daylight outlet, and the catch basin gets emptied, so the next storm runs off instead of soaking into the soil next to the house.
