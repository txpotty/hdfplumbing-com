    Frequently Asked Questions About Insinkerator Installation in St. Augustine

    Most InSinkErator replacements cost between $300 and $800 installed, depending on the model. A Badger 5 sits at the lower end, while Evolution Excel installs land at the higher end. First-time installs that need a new outlet or flange work can cost more.

    Many homeowners can swap a disposal, but common mistakes include leaving the dishwasher knockout in place, poor flange seals and unsafe wiring. A professional install avoids leaks, tripped breakers and water damage to the cabinet.

    HDF Plumbing provides a warranty on all our labor for Insinkerator installations, typically covering the work for a specified period. The Insinkerator unit itself will come with its manufacturer's warranty, which varies by model. We stand by the quality of our work.

    Signs that your old disposal needs replacing include frequent clogging, persistent leaks from the unit's body, unusual grinding noises, or complete failure to turn on. If repairs are becoming costly or frequent, replacement is often the more cost-effective choice.

    Before our plumber arrives, please clear out the cabinet under your kitchen sink to provide easy access to the plumbing and electrical connections. If you have a new unit, have it ready. This helps us work efficiently.

    For light use, a Badger 5 or Badger 5XP works well. Families who cook a lot or want a quieter unit should consider an Evolution Compact or Excel. Homes on septic should look at the Evolution Septic Assist.

    Common signs you need a new garbage disposal include frequent clogs, persistent leaks from the unit, unusual loud noises during operation, or a complete failure to turn on. If your unit constantly jams or emits a foul odor even after cleaning, it might be time for a replacement.

    In most cases, a simple like-for-like replacement of a garbage disposal does not require a permit in St. Augustine.
