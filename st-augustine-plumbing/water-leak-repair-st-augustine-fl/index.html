    Leaks inside walls show up as bubbling paint, soft drywall, baseboard stains or a musty smell. We use moisture meters and thermal imaging to find the wet area and the leaking pipe.     Ceiling Leak Repair in St. Augustine

    A stain on the ceiling can come from an upstairs bathroom, a supply line in the attic or a water heater pan overflowing. We trace the water back to its source, which is not always right above the stain. Repairs might include resealing a tub drain, replacing a toilet wax ring or fixing an attic line. We also check for condensation sources that look like leaks.

    Pipe Burst Repair in St. Augustine, FL

    A burst pipe can cause extensive water damage very quickly, especially during sudden drops in temperature. Frozen pipes are rare in most of Florida, and many homes have outdoor plumbing that would never hold up in a northern winter. We respond quickly to emergencies, isolating the leak and performing immediate repairs using durable materials like PEX or copper. Our team is equipped to handle pipe bursts in walls, ceilings, and under floors, ensuring all affected areas are addressed and potential mold risks are reduced. We also advise on insulation improvements to prevent future freezing.

    Yard and Main Line Leak Repair in St. Augustine, FL

    The service line between the meter and your house can crack from rocky soil, roots or ground movement. We locate the leak, excavate carefully and replace the damaged section with copper or approved plastic pipe. For long or deep lines, a new trenchless line may be the smarter choice.
