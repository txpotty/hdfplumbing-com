    What Goes Into a PEX Install in St. Augustine, FL

    Full Home PEX Repiping in St. Augustine

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside out.     PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in St. Augustine

    PEX-B is stiffer, is cheaper by the roll, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. Since a crimp is only as good as the tool that made it, every ring gets a go/no-go gauge check, and any ring that fails gets cut off and redone. It is a five-second check. Skipping it causes most crimp leaks.

    Whole-Home PEX Repiping in St. Augustine

    Low pressure at every faucet, not just one, usually points at the pipe and not the fixtures.     Uponor PEX System Installation in St. Augustine

    Uponor's ProPEX system is PEX-A joined with an expansion tool and a cold-expansion ring. The fittings have a wide bore, so flow loss through each joint stays low.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in St. Augustine

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and the honest answer depends on the job. PEX-A bends tighter, recovers from a kink with a heat gun, and its expansion fittings flow more water, while PEX-B costs less and its crimp joints are just as reliable when they are gauged properly. Both last decades. In a cramped attic, we usually pick PEX-A.
