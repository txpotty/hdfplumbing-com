    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in St. Augustine, FL

    Trenching lets us fix grade. Correcting slope is where it shines. When an old line has a sag that holds water and solids, bursting would just follow the same sag, so we dig and reset the pipe on a proper bed at a proper fall. Bellies disappear.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement Options in St. Augustine, FL

    Bursting and pulling replace the pipe. Lining patches the inside of the old one. We use bursting when the old line is broken or undersized, since it puts a new pipe in place instead of reinforcing the old, and lining when the pipe is still round and mostly intact. Each has its place.
