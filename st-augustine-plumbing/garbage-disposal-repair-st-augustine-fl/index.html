    Electrical Issues and Motor Repair in St. Augustine

    A disposal that hums but will not grind has power but cannot turn, while one that is silent has no power at all. They are different problems. For a silent unit we test the breaker, reset and switch, and for a humming one we check the flywheel and motor before deciding if it is worth repairing.

    Noise and Odor Diagnosis in St. Augustine

    A rattle that stops after a few seconds is usually a spoon or a pit bouncing in the chamber. A constant high whine means something else. That whine often comes from worn motor bearings, and once bearings start to fail the noise only gets worse until the motor seizes. Most smells start at the splash guard. The guard comes out, the chamber gets cleaned and a torn guard gets replaced.
