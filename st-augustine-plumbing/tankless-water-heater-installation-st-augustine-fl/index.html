    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Services in St. Augustine, FL

    Businesses in St. Augustine, FL with steady hot water use, like salons, restaurants and small gyms, often get more out of two or three tankless units racked together than one big tank.     Commercial Tankless Water Heater Installation in St. Augustine

    Commercial hot water loads in St. Augustine run longer and harder than a house. A restaurant can run a dish machine, hand sinks and a mop sink together, and the rack must hold temperature through the rush. We rack units with a common manifold. One can be serviced while the rest run.

    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in St. Augustine

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to get hot water to a far bathroom, because the unit has to sense flow and fire first. A recirc pump on a timer or sensor keeps the hot line primed, cutting the wait at the far sink from about a minute to a few seconds. It saves water too.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in St. Augustine

    Conversions in St. Augustine usually take a full day.     Upgrading from Tank to Tankless in St. Augustine, FL

    Switching from a tank takes more than unbolting one and hanging the other. A 40-gallon tank burns about 40,000 BTU, but a whole-home tankless unit may need 150,000 to 199,000, so the meter line nearly always gets bigger. The vent changes as well. Both are on the estimate.

    Tankless vs Tank Cost Comparison in St. Augustine, FL

    Upfront, a tankless install in St. Augustine, FL costs more than a standard tank because of the unit price, the gas line upgrade and the venting changes. Over the long haul, the math narrows.
