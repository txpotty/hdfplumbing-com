    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in St. Augustine

    Standard drain snaking in St. Augustine, FL usually costs $150 to $500 depending on where the clog sits and how easy it is to reach. Main line root cutting and hydro jetting can reach $400 to $1,000 or more. Camera work is extra. You get a firm price after the tech looks at the drain and before any cable goes in.

    Rooting is safe with a trained tech and the correct cable and blade. Badly cracked or collapsed pipe can be harmed by any cleaning method. Feel matters. We stop if the pipe shows damage, and we bring in the camera to look before going any further down the line.

    Rooting uses a spinning cable with cutting blades to break up clogs and roots. Jetting works another way. Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scrub the pipe walls clean. Many tough lines do best with both, cut first to open the line and jetted second to wash out what the blades left.

    Most single-drain clogs take 1 to 3 hours from arrival to flow test. A root-packed main or a job that adds jetting can take most of a day. Access is the wild card. With no usable cleanout, we pull a toilet to get in, and that adds about an hour.

    Our trucks carry Spartan and RIDGID machines, plus jetters sized for residential and commercial lines. Both brands hold up to daily use. Several sizes of cable and cutter ride on each truck, so the tech matches the tool to the pipe rather than using one setup everywhere.

    Sewer pipes leak a little water vapor at the joints. Roots chase that moisture to the joint and work their way inside. Once in, they grow quickly. In St. Augustine, clay pipe with a joint every 2 to 3 feet and older cast iron with oakum joints are the most common targets.

    Drain snaking, also called augering, uses a flexible cable and cutting head to break up or pull out the clog. It works well on hair, wipes and roots. Jetting scours the whole pipe with high-pressure water and strips the grease, scale and sludge a cable slides past. For recurring clogs, jetting is the better bet.

    Every rooter job from HDF Plumbing carries a guarantee for a set period on the line we cleared. We go over the terms before we start. It covers the line we worked on. If that same line stops up again within the guarantee window, we come back and clear it at no extra charge.
