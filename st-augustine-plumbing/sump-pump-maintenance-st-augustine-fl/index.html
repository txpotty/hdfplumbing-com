    Submersible Sump Pump Service in St. Augustine

    Our team is skilled in maintaining submersible sump pumps, a popular choice in St. Augustine due to their quiet operation and compact design. Maintenance involves pulling the pump to inspect the watertight seals, clearing the intake screen and checking the internal components for corrosion or wear. We also verify the electrical connections are secure and free from moisture, avoiding electrical hazards and supporting longevity.

    Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in St. Augustine

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, gravel and leaves settle in the pit. That debris can get sucked into the intake screen and jam the impeller. We pump down and scoop out the basin, rinse the intake and inspect the pit liner for cracks. A clean basin helps the pump run cooler and last longer.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in St. Augustine

    The discharge line carries water away from your basement, and the check valve prevents pumped water from flowing back into the pit. We inspect these components for blockages, leaks or damage. A faulty check valve can cause the pump to short-cycle, leading to premature wear. We confirm these vital parts are functioning correctly to maintain an effective water removal system for your St. Augustine residence.

    Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in St. Augustine, FL

    The float switch is a key component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. A stuck or improperly adjusted float switch can lead to the pump running continuously or failing to activate at all. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to support reliable operation for your home in St. Augustine, FL.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in St. Augustine

    Heavy afternoon thunderstorms are common in summer, and saturated ground can push water into drain lines and slow every fixture in the house. We test your pump ahead of the rainy season by filling the pit until the float trips. We watch the pump empty the basin, listen for grinding bearings and measure how fast it cycles. If the pump struggles, you still have time to fix it before the first big storm.

    Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in St. Augustine, FL

    Not every sump sits in a basement. Many homes with crawlspaces or deep window wells use small pumps to clear runoff. We service these smaller units, clear their screens and confirm the discharge reaches daylight or an approved drain. Frozen pipes are rare in most of Florida, and many homes have outdoor plumbing that would never survive a northern winter.

    Maintenance Versus Replacement Cost in St. Augustine, FL

    Routine maintenance usually costs a fraction of replacing a burned-out pump and cleaning up a flooded basement. A new installed sump pump often runs $400 to $1,200 depending on horsepower and backup options. Most pumps last seven to ten years with care. We will tell you plainly whether yours has more years left or is ready to retire.

    Battery Backup Pump Maintenance in St. Augustine

    Severe storms often bring power outages along with the rain. A battery backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or fails. We load test the battery, check the charger and alarm and run the backup pump on its own. Most backup batteries need replacement every three to five years.
