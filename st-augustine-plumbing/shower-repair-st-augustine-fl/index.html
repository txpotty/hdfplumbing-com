    Low Shower Pressure Repair in St. Augustine, FL

    Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in St. Augustine

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Mineral grit scores the sealing faces and flattens the O-rings until the cartridge can no longer stop the flow. We swap in a new one. The new cartridge goes in with silicone grease, which keeps the handle smooth.

    Slow Shower Drain Repair in St. Augustine, FL

    Standing water at your ankles is the first sign. Hard water makes it worse, because the scale binds with soap scum and hair into a mat that sticks to the pipe wall and builds out over time.     Hot Water Pressure Problems in St. Augustine, FL

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. The problem is on the hot side, either sediment in the water heater outlet, a partly closed valve at the heater or a cartridge whose hot port is packed with scale. We test both sides. If the sinks have good hot pressure and the shower does not, the cartridge is the first suspect.
