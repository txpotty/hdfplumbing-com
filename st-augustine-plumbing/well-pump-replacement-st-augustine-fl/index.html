    Grundfos Well Pump Replacement in St. Augustine, FL

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have a built-in soft start and dry-run protection, and they run on two wires with no control box at the surface. They cost more up front. The SQE pairs with a CU 301 controller for constant pressure.

    Agricultural and Ranch Well Pumps in St. Augustine

    Irrigation and stock wells often run 3 to 10 HP pumps, sometimes on three-phase power with a motor starter and overload protection at the panel. Starters and overloads get replaced along with the pump. We schedule around watering times.

    Emergency Well Pump Replacement in St. Augustine

    Dry faucets on a well-only property mean no flushing or cooking, so we treat those as same-day calls whenever the crew and the weather allow. We keep 1/2 and 3/4 HP submersibles on the truck. Splice kits and pressure switches ride along too.

    Control Box and Pressure Switch Replacement in St. Augustine, FL

    Not every dry faucet means a dead pump. A pressure switch with pitted contacts or a plugged sensing tube can stop the pump even though nothing is wrong down the well. A new switch runs about $30 in parts. We show you the burned contacts.

    Well Pump Sizing in St. Augustine

    Sizing starts with the well log. The log, or a sounder down the casing, gives us static water level and well depth, and a draw-down test tells us roughly how many gallons per minute the well can actually give. Pumps bigger than the well can supply run dry.

    What a Well Pump Job Costs in St. Augustine, FL

    A well pump job usually runs a few thousand dollars, and the pump itself is often less than half of that. The rest is labor, wire, pipe and fittings, plus the hoist time to get it all down the hole. You see every line item.

    Jet Pump Seal and Motor Replacement in St. Augustine, FL

    Single-stage and two-stage jet pumps wear out at the seal and impeller, and a leaking shaft seal shows up as a puddle under the motor. We replace the pump and prime it properly. Then we watch the gauge through a full cycle.

    Jet Pump Suction Line Repair in St. Augustine, FL

    Foot valves and suction lines cause most jet pump trouble, not the pump. Air sneaks in through a pinhole in the suction line, the pump loses prime and the seal burns up running dry. We pressure test the line. If the well is too deep for a jet pump, we will say so and quote a submersible.
