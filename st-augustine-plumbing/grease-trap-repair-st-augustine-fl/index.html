    Grease Trap Odor Control & Repair in St. Augustine

    Grease Trap Repair vs Replacement Cost in St. Augustine, FL

    Age and material decide most repair versus replace calls. Once a steel unit has rust holes in the body, it is finished.     Baffle Repair and Replacement in St. Augustine

    Inside a concrete gravity interceptor the baffle is usually a poured or precast wall with a crossover opening. When that wall spalls, grease slips through to the second compartment.     Damaged Baffle Repair in St. Augustine

    Baffles are the walls inside the trap that slow the water down long enough for grease to float and solids to sink. A cracked or loose baffle turns the trap into a pass-through. We pump it out and pull the old baffle. On plastic units we order the factory part for that model number. Steel traps with a rusted-out baffle may need a fabricated piece, and sometimes a new trap is the cheaper answer.

    Clogged Grease Trap Repair in St. Augustine

    Hardened grease in the inlet tee is the usual culprit when a trap backs up. We pump the trap down, then jet the inlet line with a 4,000 PSI unit until water runs free. Food solids packed against the baffle get scraped out by hand. Then we look for the reason it clogged, such as a dishwasher dumping too much hot water at once or a missing sink strainer upstream. A clog that keeps coming back nearly always has a cause you can see.
