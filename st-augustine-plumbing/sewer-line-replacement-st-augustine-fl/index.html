    How Pipe Bursting Works in St. Augustine

    A winch pulls the bursting head. Because the head is a little wider than the old pipe, it cracks it into the surrounding dirt as the new HDPE follows. Only two pits get dug. The fused pipe has no joints, so roots cannot get in again.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in St. Augustine

    Cast iron rots from the inside out.     Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Replacement in St. Augustine, FL

    Orangeburg is tar-soaked wood fiber. Clay holds up better, except at the joints. When the camera shows trouble in three or more places, a full replacement is usually cheaper than chasing spot repairs.

    Pipe Bursting Under Driveways in St. Augustine, FL

    Concrete is expensive to replace. Bursting lets us run new HDPE under a driveway, patio or mature tree by pulling it through the path of the old pipe from a launch pit to a receiving pit. The HDPE is butt-fused on site into one long piece. A typical burst takes one working day. Restoration is two small holes instead of a sawcut slab.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement in St. Augustine, FL

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in St. Augustine

    A collapsed run or a long belly usually means we dig.
