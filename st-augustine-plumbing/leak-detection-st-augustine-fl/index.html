    Slab Leaks From Ground Movement in St. Augustine

    Pipe set in concrete cannot flex when the ground moves, so it cracks at elbows and where it rises through the slab. Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and crack buried water and sewer lines. Isolating the line comes first. Then acoustic listening and thermal imaging find the leak so nobody has to break the floor open to search, and you get a marked spot plus repair options before the saw comes out.

    Irrigation Leak Detection in St. Augustine, FL

    Sprinkler laterals crack and zone valves stick open, and either can lose thousands of gallons before the bill shows it. Check the yard first. Our tech tests the irrigation system separately from the house, runs each zone and traces buried lines, since sprinkler leaks are the first thing most water departments suggest checking.

    Gas Leak Detection in St. Augustine

    A faint sulfur smell near the range or furnace needs a professional check. Get out first if it is strong. Our licensed plumbers use a combustible gas detector and soap solution on fittings, appliance connectors and the meter riser, then pressure test the line to find a leak too small to smell, and we repair it to the fuel gas code before turning service back on.

    Pool and Spa Leak Detection in St. Augustine

    Heat and wind can pull a lot of water out of a pool, which makes it tricky to separate evaporation from a real leak. A bucket test settles it. We then pressure test the plumbing lines and dye test around lights, skimmers and return fittings, because a running leak can undermine the soil around the pool.

    Commercial Leak Detection in St. Augustine

    PEX and Copper Leak Detection in St. Augustine, FL

    Copper leaks are loud on a ground microphone. PEX is quieter, since plastic pipe does not carry sound well, so we lean more on thermal scans, moisture readings and tracer gas when the house is plumbed in it. We handle both every week. Pinholes in copper and failed crimp fittings in PEX top the list of what we find.
