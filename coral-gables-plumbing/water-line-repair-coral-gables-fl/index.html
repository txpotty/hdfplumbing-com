    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Coral Gables

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. Many homes in Florida built before the mid 1970s have cast iron drains under the slab, which often crack and corrode with age. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water and a meter that moves with everything off. Acoustic gear and a thermal camera tell us where it is. From there you have three choices: break the slab at the spot, reroute the line through the walls or attic, or skip the old under-slab pipe and repipe overhead with PEX. You get a price on every option.

    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Coral Gables, FL

    One leak on a newer copper or PEX line is a spot repair. Repeated leaks on galvanized pipe call for a new line.
