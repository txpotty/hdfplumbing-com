    Why Polybutylene Fails in Coral Gables

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. The inner wall of the pipe and fittings slowly turns brittle, and small cracks grow until the pipe splits. Around Coral Gables, flushing the water heater yearly and descaling tankless units helps keep mineral buildup under control. Heat makes it worse. Hot lines and fittings near the water heater fail more than anything else.
