    Leaking Grease Trap Repair in Coral Gables, FL

    A wet floor around an under-sink trap usually points to a failed lid gasket or a loose inlet coupling. Steel trap bodies also rust through at the bottom seams after enough years of hot, acidic water. We dry the area, fill the trap and watch where the water shows up. Some days the fix is a cheap gasket and ten minutes. An opened seam is another story, and we will say up front if a patch will not hold.

    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Coral Gables

    Inside a concrete gravity interceptor the baffle is usually a poured or precast wall with a crossover opening. A crumbling wall lets grease slip into the second compartment and out the outlet. We can rebuild the wall with form work and a sulfate-resistant mix or set a polyethylene baffle panel, depending on the tank. Either way, it needs a full pump-out first.

    Residential Grease Trap Repair in Coral Gables, FL

    Homes rarely need a grease trap, but some have one, usually a small plastic unit under the kitchen sink on a house with a septic system or a home bakery. Most leaks come from the lid or the threaded inlet fitting. We replace the gasket, reset the fittings with fresh thread sealant, and clear any grease plug in the outlet. On most homes this is under an hour of work.

    Grease Trap Drain Line Repair in Coral Gables

    Sometimes the trap is not the problem. The run from the sinks to the trap is where grease cools and builds up.     Preventative Grease Trap Maintenance & Repair in Coral Gables, FL

    Most trap repairs we do trace back to pumping intervals that stretched too long. Pretreatment programs commonly set the limit at 25 percent of the liquid depth for grease and solids combined. After a repair, we measure the grease cap and solids with a sludge judge and suggest a pumping schedule based on how fast your trap actually fills. A kitchen running two fryers can hit that mark in a couple of weeks.
