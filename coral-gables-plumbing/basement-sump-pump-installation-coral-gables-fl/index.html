    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Sump Pump Installation in Coral Gables

    Prices in Coral Gables, FL usually land between $800 and $2,500. Pedestal pumps cost less than cast iron submersibles, and the biggest swing comes from whether we have to excavate a new pit and whether you want a battery backup added to the same basin. Every quote is itemized.

    Hurricanes and tropical storms bring flooding, power loss, and sewer backups to Florida, often swamping drainage systems for days. Outages and storms arrive together, so we recommend a battery backup in most basements. The backup starts on its own if the main pump has no power, and it also catches the water if the primary switch sticks or the pump simply cannot keep up with a heavy inflow. It is cheap insurance.

    Newer homes across Florida mostly use PEX or CPVC with PVC drains, though copper pinhole leaks are still a common repair in older homes. Slab homes without a basement rarely need a pump. Houses with basements or below-grade rooms are another story, since they can take on water fast during heavy storms, especially where the ground sheds rain rather than soaking it up. A pit costs less than a cleanup.

    Liberty Pumps, Zoeller and Wayne are our regulars. All three use cast iron housings and reliable switches, and all three have solid parts support through local supply houses, which matters more than a fancy feature list when something breaks. We match the model to your pit.

    For some homeowners, a same-model pump swap is a fair weekend job. New pits and backup systems are a different matter, because those mistakes end in flooding, cracked slabs or electrical hazards, and fixing them costs more than paying for the install up front. Leave those to us.

    Changing out a pump in an existing pit takes about two to three hours. A new pit with slab cutting and a concrete patch takes most of a day, since the concrete needs a little time to firm up before the pump goes into service.

    Plan on 4 to 8 hours for a standard install. A new pit with slab cutting can stretch it to a full day, and tricky electrical access or a long discharge run through a finished wall adds time on top of that. We tell you up front.

    We strongly recommend professional installation. You are dealing with wiring, pipe and concrete in one job, and a bad seal on the lid, an undersized pit or a discharge that drains toward the house can leave you worse off than having no pump at all. Errors appear at the worst moment.
