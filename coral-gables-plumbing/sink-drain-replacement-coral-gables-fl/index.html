    Push-Button Drain Installation in Coral Gables, FL

    Push-button drains get rid of the lift rod.     Utility and Basement Sink Drain Replacement in Coral Gables

    Basement sinks often drain to a pump. Sinks below the sewer line drain through a pump, so we check its valve and discharge line during the job. Pumps fail quietly. A gurgling pit is a clue.

    Kitchen Sink Drain Replacement in Coral Gables

    Kitchen drains start failing at the basket strainer. The putty under a 3-1/2 inch strainer dries out, the locknut rusts, and water seeps down the outside of the tailpiece where it looks like the trap is leaking even when it is not. Strainer and trap get swapped as a pair. Disposal sinks get a new flange instead.

    Vessel and Grid Drain Replacement in Coral Gables

    Vessel sinks use a different drain. Most glass and stone vessel bowls have no overflow hole, so they need an unslotted grid or umbrella drain, and a slotted drain in one of those bowls leaks out of the slots right into the counter hole. Mismatched drains are common. Swapping it takes under an hour.

    Choosing Drain Material in Coral Gables, FL

    PVC is the default choice under most sinks. It will not rust and costs less, while thick brass is worth it where the trap shows, like under a pedestal. Some houses use black ABS instead. We match what you have, with a proper transition where they meet.

    Pop-Up Drain Assembly Replacement in Coral Gables, FL

    A stopper that drops on its own has a worn pivot. The plastic ball on the pivot rod wears flat, the nut that holds it loses its seal, and the rod no longer holds the stopper up, so the sink either will not drain or will not hold water. One new pop-up fixes every part of that. Takes about an hour.
