    Loose or Wobbly Sink Repair in Coral Gables

    A sink that moves when you lean on it is pulling on the drain. Undermount sinks hang on clips glued under the counter, and pedestal sinks hang on a bracket that can pull loose from drywall. We reset the clips and reseal the rim. Pedestals get blocking behind the wall when needed.

    P-Trap Repair and Replacement in Coral Gables, FL

    The trap holds a plug of water that keeps sewer gas out of the room. Most codes want 2 to 4 inches of trap seal, and a leaking or cut-down trap can lose it and let sewer odor into the house. We replace bad traps with a matched kit. Chrome traps that have rusted thin get swapped for PVC or brass.

    Repair or Replace? Sink Costs in Coral Gables, FL

    Repair is cheaper nine times out of ten. A trap or stopper costs a fraction of a new sink, and replacement only makes sense when the bowl is cracked, the glaze is chipped through, or the drain body has rusted into the basin. When it is close, you get both prices. Some people repair now and remodel next year.

    Low Water Pressure at the Sink in Coral Gables, FL

    Unscrew the aerator before anything else. Most single-faucet pressure problems are a packed aerator screen, and after that we look at the cartridge and the angle stop for debris. Whole-house low pressure is another call. We test the pressure regulator in that case.

    Leaking P-Trap Fix in Coral Gables

    Check the slip nuts first. Plastic slip nuts crack when they are overtightened with pliers, and the beveled washers inside them flatten until water seeps out at every joint along the trap. We replace the trap as one kit. A 1-1/4 inch kit for bath sinks, 1-1/2 inch for kitchens.

    Slow or Clogged Sink Drain Repair in Coral Gables, FL

    Slow drains almost always clog at the trap or the first few feet past the wall. In kitchens it is cold grease and food, and in bathrooms it is hair knotted on the stopper and glued with soap scum. We clear it with a hand auger. Hydro-jetting is for main lines, not sink arms.

    Outdoor and Utility Sink Repair in Coral Gables

    Outdoor kitchen sinks and garage utility tubs sit in the weather. When a rare freeze is forecast, covering outdoor valves and letting a faucet drip typically does the job to prevent damage. UV light cracks plastic traps and supply lines left outdoors, and dirt from the yard clogs the drain. We fix leaks at the hose bib too. A frost-free sillcock is worth it on exposed walls.

    Kitchen Sink Repair in Coral Gables

    Kitchen sinks take the most abuse in the house. Strainers lose their putty seal, sprayer hoses split inside the cabinet, and disposal flanges vibrate loose over the years. The wet part is the one we fix. A cracked composite or porcelain bowl is a replacement, and we will say so.
