    Slab Leak Reroutes in Jacksonville

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. Rerouting one branch overhead and leaving the bad pipe in the slab is often cheaper than both a repipe and a jackhammer repair. This works well for a first leak. If the house has had several leaks, we will walk through why a full repipe usually costs less over time.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Jacksonville

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water and long attic runs. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open the pipe end and let it shrink back over the fitting for a full-bore joint. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. Most homes are repiped with PEX-A in two to three days.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Jacksonville, FL

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside, and over decades the inside diameter closes down to a fraction of what it was. Pressure drops slowly. Rust-colored water in the morning, poor pressure upstairs and dripping threaded joints all point to failing galvanized. New PEX or copper takes its place. Most homeowners notice the pressure difference the first time they turn on the shower.

    Drywall Patching and Restoration in Jacksonville, FL

    Repiping needs small openings in walls and ceilings to reach fixtures and pull pipe. Our crew cuts neat rectangles, covers nearby surfaces and patches each opening after the pressure test. Textures get matched. We match knockdown, orange peel or smooth walls, so the patches are ready to prime and paint.

    Copper Repiping in Jacksonville, FL

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. Our copper work uses thicker Type L pipe, joined with lead-free solder or ProPress fittings, rather than the Type M found in many older houses. Copper takes a bit longer. Most copper jobs run a day longer than PEX, since each turn needs its own fitting.

    Emergency Pipe Failures and Repipes in Jacksonville

    Sometimes a house reaches the repipe point all at once, with a burst line and two more leaks right behind it. First we stop the leak and get water back on with a temporary fix, then we schedule the repipe without pressure. That gives you time. Insurance may cover the water damage from the burst even if it does not cover the repipe itself, so we document what we find with photos.

    Drain Line Repiping in Jacksonville

    Drain pipe wears out too, especially old cast iron that cracks and scales up inside. Repeat clogs, sewer smells and several slow drains at once often mean the drain pipe itself is failing. A camera shows the condition. New PVC or ABS, sloped and vented properly, replaces the bad sections and drains better than the old pipe.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Jacksonville, FL

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Because PEX flexes, it absorbs some of the shock that causes water hammer, and long runs with few fittings mean fewer places for a leak to start inside a wall. We keep PEX out of sunlight and away from water heater flues.
