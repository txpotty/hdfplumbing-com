    Slab Leaks in Older Homes in Jacksonville, FL

    Older homes in Jacksonville, FL often have soft copper or galvanized steel under the slab, and both corrode with age. Copper fails at bends and where it rubs concrete, while galvanized rusts from the inside until it splits. Our techs know where these fail. That knowledge points the search at the likeliest spots first.

    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Jacksonville, FL

    Hot water leaking under the slab warms the floor above it. A thermal camera shows that warm bloom through tile, vinyl and even thin carpet, often spreading outward from the leak point in a recognizable shape. Cold-side leaks will not show. We use it to narrow the search before the acoustic check.

    Tracer Gas Leak Detection in Jacksonville

    Plastic pipe muffles leak noise. For those, we drain the line and push in a safe hydrogen and nitrogen tracer gas, which rises through the slab and gets picked up by a sniffer at the floor surface, often through grout joints and hairline cracks. It finds leaks acoustic gear misses in Jacksonville homes.

    Emergency Slab Leak Response in Jacksonville

    Water flooding a hallway or seeping through grout needs fast attention. Shut the main. We respond quickly, isolate the leak and usually get water back to the rest of the home that day, then come back to locate and repair the line properly.

    Slab Leaks in Commercial Buildings in Jacksonville

    Commercial slabs are thicker and carry larger pipe, so leaks can move more water and be harder to hear from the surface with a standard ground microphone. Our approach changes for those. Our techs combine zone isolation, line tracing and tracer gas, and schedule testing around your operating hours in Jacksonville.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Jacksonville

    Hot lines leak under slabs more often than cold ones. Heat speeds up corrosion, and the pipe expands and contracts every time someone showers, wearing against the gravel and concrete until a pinhole opens. Warm floors and a water heater that runs nonstop are the signs. Gas or electric bills may jump too.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Jacksonville

    A reroute abandons the leaking line under the floor. New pipe runs through the walls or attic instead, which avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than opening a finished floor. We usually use PEX because it handles heat and resists corrosion. It suits tile and hardwood homes.
