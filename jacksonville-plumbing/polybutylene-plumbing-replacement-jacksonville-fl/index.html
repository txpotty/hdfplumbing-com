    PEX Pipe Installation in Jacksonville, FL

    PEX is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, resists scale and corrosion and installs faster than copper. Faster install means lower labor cost. Attic PEX stays out of UV, clear of flues and can lights and supported as the maker specifies, which protects the pipe and the warranty.

    Why Polybutylene Fails in Jacksonville

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. Over the years, the pipe and its acetal fittings get brittle from the inside, then crack along the inner wall until one splits under normal pressure. Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving heavy scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. Heat makes it worse. Hot lines and fittings near the water heater fail more than anything else.

    Finding Polybutylene Leaks in Jacksonville

    A poly leak inside a wall or ceiling can run for days before you see a stain. Watch the water meter. With every fixture off, a spinning leak indicator on the meter means water is going somewhere, and we use acoustic listening gear and thermal imaging to find it without opening walls at random. Finding it lets us make a temporary repair and plan the full replacement.

    Partial Polybutylene Replacement in Jacksonville, FL

    Full replacement is the right long-term fix, but it is not always possible right away. Budget and timing matter. Replacing the riskiest parts first, near the heater and in the attic, lowers the odds of a burst while you plan the rest. We will be honest about what is left.

    Full Home Repiping with PEX in Jacksonville

    Most poly replacements we do use PEX-A, a flexible tubing that runs long distances with few fittings. For slab houses, new PEX-A runs through the attic and drops down interior walls, so none of it sits under the floor. PEX handles a freeze better than copper. We use expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter open, so pressure at the far shower stays strong.

    The Cost of Polybutylene Replacement in Jacksonville, FL

    Price depends mostly on house size, the number of bathrooms, attic access and whether you pick PEX or copper. PEX costs less. A planned replacement usually costs less than the drywall, flooring and cabinet repairs after a single burst, and that is before any deductible or mold cleanup gets added on. Our estimate lists labor, materials, permit and patching separately.

    Polybutylene Identification and Inspection in Jacksonville

    Polybutylene is usually gray, though it can be white, blue or black, and is often stamped PB2110. Check the heater first. It also shows up under sinks, at the main and through garage walls, and we follow it into the attic to see how much of the house it feeds.

    Manufactured Home Poly Replacement in Jacksonville, FL

    Manufactured homes from the poly era often have polybutylene lines in the floor or the belly underneath. New PEX gets sized for the home and strapped to the frame. Exposed lines get checked. Frozen pipes are rare in most of Florida, and many homes have outdoor plumbing that would never survive a northern winter. Heat tape and insulation on exposed runs get inspected and replaced where needed, so the new supply lines are protected under the home.
