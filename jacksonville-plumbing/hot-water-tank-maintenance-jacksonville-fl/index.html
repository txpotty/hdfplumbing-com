    Professional Hot Water Tank Maintenance Services in Jacksonville, FL

    Anode Rod Inspection & Replacement in Jacksonville, FL

    The anode rod is your hot water tank's sacrificial protector against corrosion. Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving stubborn scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. During our maintenance, we pull and inspect the anode rod. If it's badly depleted, we recommend and perform a replacement to extend the life of your tank in Jacksonville, FL, preventing rust from eating away at the tank lining.

    Gas and Electric Water Heater Checks in Jacksonville, FL

    Whether your home in Jacksonville, FL uses a gas or electric hot water tank, our maintenance covers the specific needs of each. For gas units, we inspect burner assemblies, gas lines and vent systems for safety and efficiency. For electric units, we check heating elements, wiring and thermostats. Our detailed checks ensure all energy sources are operating correctly and safely.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Service in Jacksonville, FL

    Many modern plumbing systems include an expansion tank connected to the water heater. This tank takes up excess pressure created during the heating cycle, protecting your hot water tank and other plumbing fixtures. Our maintenance includes checking the expansion tank's air pressure and overall condition. Proper expansion tank function is essential to preventing premature wear on your hot water tank and maintaining system integrity.

    Commercial Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Jacksonville, FL

    Businesses in Jacksonville, FL rely closely on consistent hot water for their operations. Our commercial hot water tank maintenance services are tailored to the higher demands of commercial units.     Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in Jacksonville, FL

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber. We check the draft hood and vent connector for proper slope and secure joints. Dust and lint from garages can block the flame arrestor screen on newer FVIR models, which causes the pilot to drop out. A clean burner gives a stable blue flame and lower gas bills.

    Standard Hot Water Tank Flushing in Jacksonville

    Sediment buildup is a common issue in hot water tanks, especially where water hardness runs high. Our standard flushing service removes these mineral deposits, which can reduce efficiency and lead to premature tank failure. We drain the tank completely, flush out all sediment and refill it, ensuring clear water flow and improved heating performance. This routine service is crucial for extending the life of your unit.

    Maintenance Versus Replacement Costs in Jacksonville, FL

    A yearly flush costs a small fraction of a new tank, and a new installed tank often runs $1,400 to $3,000 or more. We give you an straight read on whether maintenance is still worth it. If a tank is past 10 to 12 years old, shows rust at the base or leaks from the seams, we will tell you replacement is the smarter spend. When the tank still has life, maintenance keeps it running efficiently for years.

    Sediment Flush and Descaling in Jacksonville

    Hard water leaves calcium carbonate behind every time the burner heats the tank. Over a year that layer can harden into chunks that insulate the water from the flame and cause noisy popping. We connect a hose to the drain valve, flush with full line pressure and agitate the bottom until the water runs clear. When scale is severe we use a descaling solution approved for potable systems. A clean tank heats faster and puts less stress on the bottom seam.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Jacksonville

    A standard flush and inspection usually runs $125 to $250 depending on tank size and access. Anode replacement usually adds $75 to $200 in parts and labor, and powered anodes cost more.

    Most manufacturers and plumbing experts recommend yearly hot water tank maintenance. This is especially true where water is hard, since minerals speed up sediment buildup and anode rod depletion.

    Regular, professional maintenance can frequently be a condition for keeping your manufacturer's warranty valid. Check your warranty documentation, but generally, proof of annual service can help keep your warranty intact.

    Common signs include reduced hot water supply, odd noises like rumbling or popping, rust-colored water, a sulfur or rotten egg smell, or water leaking from the tank. These indicate issues that maintenance can often resolve.

    In mineral-heavy water a magnesium rod may last three to five years. We measure it at every visit so you replace it before the tank lining begins to rust.

    Routine hot water tank maintenance, such as flushing and anode rod replacement, generally does not require a permit in Jacksonville, FL. However, if major repairs or a full replacement are needed, a permit may be required to ensure compliance with local building codes.

    Most flushes and inspections take 45 minutes to 90 minutes. Heavily scaled tanks or anode replacements in tight closets can take a bit longer.

    While some basic steps like draining a small amount of water can be done by a homeowner, professional hot water tank maintenance involves professional tools and experience to properly inspect electrical or gas components, anode rods and pressure relief valves. It is best left to a professional to ensure safety and code compliance.
