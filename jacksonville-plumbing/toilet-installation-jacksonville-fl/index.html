    Toilet Installation Work We Handle in Jacksonville, FL

    Urgent Toilet Replacement in Jacksonville

    A cracked tank or a bowl leaking at the base cannot wait for next week, especially in a house with one bathroom. We keep common two-piece toilets on the truck. In many cases we can pull the broken one and set a replacement in the same visit, with a 12 inch rough-in model in round or elongated. Close the stop until then.

    Wall-Hung Carrier Systems in Jacksonville

    In-wall carriers from Geberit and Grohe each mount a little differently, and the instructions matter. The frame height chart from the maker gets followed to the letter. Set the frame too low and the bowl ends up at child height, too high and nobody's feet touch the floor, and neither can be fixed once the wall is tiled. Measure twice here.

    Smart Toilet and Bidet Seat Installation in Jacksonville

    Bidet seats like the TOTO Washlet and Bio Bidet bolt onto most existing elongated bowls. Water comes from a T-valve at the fill line. Heated seats with a warm-air dryer need a nearby GFCI outlet, and when there is none we bring in an electrician first. Non-electric seats take about thirty minutes.

    ADA-Compliant Toilet Installation in Jacksonville

    ADA toilets put the seat 17 to 19 inches off the floor, and the centerline sits 16 to 18 inches from the side wall. It is set that way so a grab bar fits. Before anything is ordered we measure the drain, because shifting it to meet the ADA centerline means cutting into the floor. Elongated bowls are standard here.

    Toilet Brands We Install in Jacksonville

    We install Kohler, TOTO, American Standard, Gerber and most other brands sold at supply houses and home centers. Every brand has its quirks. Some one-piece TOTO models mount with a Unifit adapter, and certain Kohler toilets use a tank-to-bowl gasket that most hardware stores do not carry, so we stock both. We keep those parts on hand.

    Low-Flow WaterSense Toilet Installation in Jacksonville, FL

    WaterSense toilets are capped at 1.28 gallons per flush, and some dual-flush models drop to around 0.8 for liquid waste. That adds up fast. Replacing a single 3.5 gallon toilet in a household of four can save several thousand gallons a year, and some water utilities have offered rebates on high-efficiency models, so it is worth asking yours. Pick a strong MaP rating.

    Toilet Flange and Wax Ring Replacement in Jacksonville, FL

    The closet flange is the ring that ties the toilet to the drain, and it is the weak point on most old installs. On cast iron, the bolt slots rust out first. PVC and ABS flanges crack when someone overtightens the bolts, and a repair ring screwed over the top is often all it takes to restore a solid anchor. Seal goes on last.

    Two-Piece Toilet Installation in Jacksonville

    Two-piece toilets come with the tank and bowl separate, joined by a rubber gasket and two or three bolts. The tank goes on before the bowl is set. Even tightening, alternating sides, matters more than force, since uneven pressure either cracks the china or leaves one bolt weeping. It should not wobble.

    Toilet Installers Near You in Jacksonville, FL

    Older Jacksonville homes can hide surprises under the toilet, like a cast iron flange rusted thin or a subfloor gone soft from an old leak. Both get checked before anything is set. Soft plywood around the flange gets cut out and replaced with new subfloor, glued and screwed, so the toilet sits on something solid. It adds an hour, sometimes two.

    Looking for a plumber near me during a remodel usually means juggling a tile setter and an inspector who only comes on certain days. We fit our visit around theirs. Tell us when the grout will be cured and we will set the toilet the next morning, flange height already checked on a prior visit.
