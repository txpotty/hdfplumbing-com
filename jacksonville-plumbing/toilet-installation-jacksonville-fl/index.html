    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Installation in Jacksonville

    Labor to install a customer-supplied standard toilet typically runs $200 to $400. Including a mid-range toilet, most installs total $450 to $900, while wall-hung systems with carriers range from $1,500 to $3,500 or more. We give you a flat price up front.

    A one-piece toilet has the bowl and tank integrated into a single unit, offering a sleek look and simpler cleaning. A two-piece toilet has a separate bowl and tank, which are bolted together during installation. Both types are widely available and perform well.

    TOTO, Kohler and American Standard consistently perform well in flush tests and have good parts availability. Gerber and Mansfield are solid budget options. We help you match the brand and model to your needs.

    Common signs you might need a new toilet include frequent clogs, cracks in the porcelain bowl or tank, constant running water, a wobbly base, or a toilet that is very old and inefficient. Upgrading can save water and refresh the look of your bathroom.

    Replacing a toilet in the same location generally does not need a permit, but new bathrooms, additions and drain relocations typically do. Requirements vary by city, and we check with the local building department for your project.

    HDF Plumbing installs all major toilet brands, including Kohler, Toto, American Standard, Delta, and Gerber. We can help you choose a model that fits your budget, style preferences, and water efficiency goals.

    Generally, replacing an existing toilet with a similar model in the same location in Jacksonville does not require a permit. However, if you are relocating a toilet, adding a new toilet, or making major changes to the plumbing system, a permit may be required. We can explain local code requirements.

    A standard toilet installation, assuming no major plumbing modifications are needed, usually takes about 1 to 3 hours. More complex installations, such as those requiring new rough-ins or dealing with damaged subflooring, can take longer.
