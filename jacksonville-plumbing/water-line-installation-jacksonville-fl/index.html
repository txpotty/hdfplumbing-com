    Main Water Service Line Installation in Jacksonville

    PEX Water Line Installation in Jacksonville

    Cross-linked polyethylene, or PEX, bends around corners and tolerates a little ground movement. Constant heat and humidity are the main plumbing stress in Florida, keeping water heaters, AC drain lines, and exterior pipes working year round. We bury it on sand bedding with tracer wire alongside, because PEX is plastic and a locator cannot find it later without that wire on top of the pipe in Jacksonville. UV light weakens it.

    PEX and HDPE Water Line Installation in Jacksonville, FL

    HDPE and PEX both flex, so there are fewer fittings in the ground and better tolerance for soil movement. HDPE is especially tough on long runs, and we join it with approved compression or heat fusion fittings and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find the line years from now in Jacksonville, FL. We keep PEX out of the sun.
