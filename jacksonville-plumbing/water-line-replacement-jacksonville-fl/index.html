    Main Water Line Replacement in Jacksonville

    The main water line is the primary pipe delivering water from the municipal supply to your home. When this critical line fails due to age, shifting soil, or severe leaks, a full replacement is often the most cost-effective long-term solution. We expertly excavate, remove the old line, and install new, durable piping, ensuring proper connection to your home's plumbing system and the city's main.

    Copper Service Line Installation in Jacksonville, FL

    Type K copper is a durable option for underground water lines. It resists punctures and lasts for decades. Copper costs more than PEX but is preferred by some homeowners. We install copper with proper bedding to cushion it. We can also install sleeves where it passes through foundations.

    Main Water Line Repair vs. Replacement in Jacksonville

    Deciding between repair and full replacement depends on the extent of the damage. If your main water line in Jacksonville has multiple leaks, severe corrosion, or is nearing the end of its lifespan, replacement is often the more cost-effective long-term solution. Our technicians will perform a complete inspection, including pressure testing and possibly video inspection, to recommend the best course of action. We consider factors like the age of your home, the material of the existing pipe, and the nature of the damage before making a recommendation.

    PEX Water Line Replacement in Jacksonville

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a popular and cost-effective choice for water line replacement in Jacksonville. It's flexible, resistant to freezing, and less prone to scaling and corrosion than some traditional materials. Its flexibility allows for easier installation, often requiring fewer connections, which reduces the potential for leaks. PEX is also a good option for homes with aggressive soil conditions, offering a reliable alternative to metal pipes.
