    Type L vs. Type K Copper in Jacksonville, FL

    The thickest wall belongs to Type K, the usual choice underground. Type L is lighter and mostly goes indoors. Some codes allow L for burial, but we stick with K for service lines because a thicker wall buys more years against corrosive soil and stray rocks. Same fittings, different pipe.

    Main Water Line Replacement in Jacksonville

    Your service line runs from the meter or curb stop to the main shutoff inside the house, usually as 3/4-inch or 1-inch pipe. After a second leak in a new spot, we stop recommending patches. We dig at both ends, pull or cut out the old pipe, and run new line at the depth local code calls for, then tie into the meter and the house valve. Most homes are done in a day.

    Damage Assessment Before Replacement in Jacksonville

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Jacksonville, FL

    Pipe pulling works like this. We dig a pit at each end of the old line.     Copper Water Line Installation in Jacksonville

    Type K copper is the thick-wall grade made for burial. Street pressure does not bother it.     Polybutylene Water Line Replacement in Jacksonville, FL

    Polybutylene is the gray or blue plastic pipe installed from roughly the late 1970s to the mid 1990s. It degrades from the inside when exposed to chlorinated water. The failures tend to be sudden splits rather than slow drips, and some insurers will not write a policy on a house that still has it. Worth replacing.
