    Hard water leads to mineral buildup in pipes and appliances. Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving stubborn scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. If your old water softener is no longer properly removing minerals, or if it is over 15 years old, a replacement will protect your plumbing and improve water quality. We replace all types of water softeners, including salt-based and salt-free systems, sized for your household's water usage and installed correctly on your main water line to provide truly soft water throughout your Jacksonville home.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbing Replacement in Jacksonville

    Costs vary by job. A tank water heater replacement usually runs $1,400 to $3,000, a water softener $1,500 to $3,500, a water line $1,800 to $5,500 and a sewer line $4,000 to $15,000 or more.

    Some simple fixture swaps are DIY friendly, but water heaters, well pumps and sewer lines involve gas, electrical and code requirements. A licensed plumber ensures safety and compliance.

    Climate, freezes and soil type all affect how long plumbing lasts. Humidity causes condensation on cold lines and clogged AC condensate drains, a common source of hidden water damage. Freeze-thaw cycles can damage pipes, and corrosive or shifting soils can accelerate pipe degradation. Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and crack buried water and sewer lines. Our replacement solutions are chosen to withstand these local conditions for lasting durability.

    For water heater replacements, we primarily recommend and install reliable brands such as Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White and Navien. These manufacturers are known for their durability, energy efficiency and strong warranty coverage, providing long-term value for your Jacksonville home.

    The timeline for plumbing replacement depends on the complexity of the job. A water heater replacement usually takes 2 to 4 hours. A main water line or sewer line replacement can take 1 to 3 days. Whole-home repiping projects typically span 3 to 7 days, depending on the size of the house and accessibility.

    Frequent leaks, rusty or discolored water, low pressure, recurring backups and equipment over 10 years old are common signs. We can inspect and give you an honest assessment.

    We mainly use PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) and copper piping for water supply lines. For sewer and drain lines, we use durable PVC (polyvinyl chloride) or ABS (acrylonitrile butadiene styrene) pipes, based on the application and local codes.
