    Whole-Home Re-Piping in Jacksonville

    A whole-home repipe replaces every supply line from the main shutoff to each fixture, including the water heater connections. Plan on a few days for a typical house.     PEX Pipe Replacement in Jacksonville

    PEX bends around corners and comes in long rolls, so a typical repipe needs fewer fittings than copper would on the same layout. Northern parts of Florida do get occasional freezing nights, which can crack exposed pipes, irrigation lines, and outdoor devices. It often survives freezing where copper would crack. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings where possible, keeping flow strong at each joint. Faster install.
