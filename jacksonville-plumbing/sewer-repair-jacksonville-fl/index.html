    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Jacksonville, FL

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies often form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after years of wet weather. Nearly all homes in Florida sit on concrete slabs over sandy soil, so leaks under the slab are a frequent and costly repair. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is typically a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.

    Pipe Burst & Collapse Repair in Jacksonville, FL

    A burst or collapsed sewer pipe can lead to significant damage and unsanitary conditions. These issues can result from shifting soil, corrosion of old cast iron or clay pipes, or heavy vehicle traffic above the line. HDF Plumbing offers emergency repair services to quickly address these critical failures. We precisely locate the damaged section and provide durable repair or replacement options, often using trenchless methods where appropriate to limit disruption.
