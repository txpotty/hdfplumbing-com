    Sewer Line Leak Detection & Repair in Jacksonville, FL

    Sewer smell outside the house points to a leak. A lush stripe over the line is another clue. We confirm it on camera, look for cracks, open joints or a broken cleanout riser, and locate it on the surface to keep the excavation tight. A small crack can sometimes be fixed with a short sectional liner, so nothing in the yard gets disturbed. Larger breaks get cut out and replaced.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Jacksonville

    Orangeburg is wood fiber soaked in coal tar. Over decades it goes oval and blisters. Cast iron from the same era rusts from the inside, builds scale and eventually splits along the bottom where the sewage runs, so by the time a camera shows widespread damage, spot repairs are a poor investment. PVC goes in, usually end to end. Trenchless bursting is often an option.

    Root Intrusion Removal in Jacksonville, FL

    Roots enter through hairline cracks and loose joints, then grow into a mat that catches paper and grease. They come back fast. We clear the line with a root cutting head or a jetter with a chain flail, camera it afterward to see the condition of each joint, and show you whether a liner or a spot repair would stop the cycle for good. Mature trees in Jacksonville yards make this a common call.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Jacksonville

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. Crushed pipe cannot be lined. When the camera shows a collapse, a large offset or a belly deep enough to hold several inches of water, we open a trench, remove the old pipe and lay new Schedule 40 PVC on proper bedding at a quarter inch of fall per foot. We see this most in older neighborhoods with clay pipe. The dig is staged so the house is without a sewer for as little time as possible.

    Restaurant and Retail Sewer Repair in Jacksonville

    Restaurants, retail centers and multifamily buildings load a lateral far beyond a single house. Grease and wipes cause most of it. We jet the line, camera it, and repair the weak section with options that limit how long you are closed, including after-hours digs and bypass pumping when the business has to stay open. We also install two-way cleanouts for future maintenance.

    After-Hours Sewer Repair in Jacksonville

    Main lines like to fail on holidays. We still answer. An after-hours call gets a tech with a cable machine and a camera, which is enough to restore flow in most cases and to find out whether the trouble is a blockage or a structural break. If it is structural, we schedule the dig for the earliest safe window. We avoid digging blind at night near gas and electric lines.

    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Jacksonville

    Roots in a sewer line are not the tree's fault. A leaking joint invited them in. Warm, wet, nutrient-rich air escaping from a loose joint draws roots toward the line, and once they get in they thicken until they crack the pipe or separate the joint completely. We cut them out, then talk about the long-term fix. Lining or a joint replacement keeps them from coming back.

    Commercial Sewer Repair in Jacksonville

    Multifamily buildings and offices put several units' worth of flow through one lateral. Minor sags clog quickly under that load. We repair larger diameter lines, add two-way cleanouts so future maintenance is easy, and give managers recorded footage for owners, insurers and their own files. We can work outside business hours.
