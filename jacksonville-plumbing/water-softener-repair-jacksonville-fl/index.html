    Water Softener Leak Detection & Repair in Jacksonville, FL

    Leaks around your water softener can be caused by cracked tanks, loose fittings, faulty O-rings, or pressure issues. Even a small leak can lead to water damage and wasted water.     Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Jacksonville, FL

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates suction to draw brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. When it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying. We clean or replace the injector and screen and check the brine line for air leaks.

    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Jacksonville, FL

    While less common than traditional softeners, no-salt water conditioners also require maintenance and occasional repair. These systems work by conditioning water to prevent scale buildup without removing minerals. If you notice scale returning or reduced water pressure, your no-salt system may need attention. HDF Plumbing is experienced in diagnosing and repairing issues with these unique units in Jacksonville, FL, ensuring they continue to protect your plumbing and appliances effectively.
