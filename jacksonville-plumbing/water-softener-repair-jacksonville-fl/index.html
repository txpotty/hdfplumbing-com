    Drips around fittings waste water. We trace it to the seal, O-ring, hose or internal part that failed. We replace the failed part with silicone-lubricated O-rings rated for potable water in your Jacksonville, FL home and pressure test the connection.

    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Jacksonville, FL

    A crust of salt across the brine tank stops regeneration, and hard water shows up at every tap within a day or two. The bridge gets broken up and the tank cleaned out. Older brine tanks can crack, and we repair or replace those too. Keep the salt about half full.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Jacksonville

    Chlorine eats resin slowly, and after enough years the beads swell, break and stop exchanging the calcium and magnesium they are supposed to hold. This is common with older systems on treated city water. HDF Plumbing pulls the old resin and fills the tank with new media. The softener works like new.

    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Jacksonville, FL

    No-salt conditioners do not remove minerals, they change how scale forms, but they still need service. Scale returning or a drop in water pressure usually means the conditioning media is spent or the sediment prefilter is clogged. HDF Plumbing repairs these units in Jacksonville, FL. The media wears out in a few years.

    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Jacksonville, FL

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. A new quality softener installed typically costs $1,500 to $3,500.     Resin Testing and Replacement in Jacksonville

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Chlorine and chloramine in city water slowly break resin down until it turns to mush. We test resin capacity, then clean it with resin cleaner or replace it, and a solid tank and valve with fresh resin works like new for years. Mushy resin can clog the valve.
