    Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation in Jacksonville

    Most homes still rely on traditional tank water heaters for their hot water needs. These units store and heat a large volume of water, making them a dependable choice for families. Our installation service includes proper sizing based on your household's demand, ensuring adequate hot water even during peak usage.     Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Jacksonville

    Gas tank water heaters provide strong recovery and are common in many local homes. We install 40, 50 and 75 gallon models with proper venting and combustion air. Each install includes a new gas connector, sediment trap and shutoff valve. We check draft and test for gas leaks. Gas units are a good choice for families with high hot water demand.

    Water Heater Installation Cost in Jacksonville, FL

    Tank water heater installations usually cost less upfront than tankless systems. Gas tankless installs can cost more because of gas line upgrades and venting. Attic installs, relocations and code upgrades may add to the price. We provide a written quote that includes the unit, labor, permits and haul-away. We also compare long-term energy costs so you can make an informed choice.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Jacksonville, FL

    Tankless water heaters provide on-demand hot water, saving energy and space, and they are increasingly popular in Florida homes. Our plumbers specialize in installing these highly efficient units, making sure of proper gas line sizing or electrical service upgrades if needed. We also address venting requirements, which are often different from traditional units. While the upfront cost can be higher, the long-term energy savings and endless hot water supply make them an appealing option for many homeowners in Jacksonville. We'll help you choose the right model for your particular needs.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Jacksonville

    A standard 40 to 50 gallon tank water heater typically costs $1,600 to $3,500 installed. Tankless installations often run $3,000 to $6,500 depending on gas line and venting needs.

    Many jurisdictions require a permit for water heater replacement. We pull permits when required and make sure the install meets code.

    Yes, we stand behind our work. Our water heater installations come with a labor warranty, and the water heater unit itself will have a manufacturer's warranty, typically ranging from 6 to 12 years, depending on the model.

    We install Rheem, A.O. Smith, Bradford White, Navien, Rinnai and other trusted brands. We recommend models based on reliability, warranty and budget.

    Manufacturer warranties usually range from 6 to 12 years on the tank and parts. We also warranty our installation labor.

    The correct size water heater depends on the number of people in your household and your peak hot water demand. For tank units, a 40 to 50 gallon tank is common for 2 to 4 people, while larger families might need 60 to 80 gallons. For tankless units, it is about flow rate (GPM) and temperature rise. Our plumbers will assess your needs to recommend the right size.

    An expansion tank absorbs excess water pressure created as water heats up inside the tank. Many homes in Jacksonville, FL, especially those with closed-loop plumbing systems, are required by code to have an expansion tank to protect the water heater and plumbing system from damage.

    Most standard water heater installations, especially for replacing a similar tank unit, can be completed within 3 to 4 hours.
