    Underground Pipe Repair Methods for Jacksonville, FL Properties

    Commercial Underground Pipe Repair in Jacksonville

    Commercial properties run larger and longer buried lines. Leaks get expensive. We locate leaks under parking lots, sidewalks and landscaped common areas carefully so the concrete cuts stay small and tenants are not shut out. We can work nights.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Jacksonville

    Polybutylene was used in a lot of homes built in the 1980s and early 1990s. It fails without warning. The gray plastic turns brittle as chlorine in the water attacks it, and buried runs tend to split at fittings or where the pipe was bent tight during installation. Patching it just moves the next leak. We use PEX or copper.

    Sewer Line Underground Repair in Jacksonville, FL

    Roots and settling soil cause most buried sewer failures. Age does the rest. We offer open trench repair, pipe bursting and cured-in-place lining, picking the method after the video shows the depth and extent of the damage. All work meets Florida plumbing code.

    Pool and Outbuilding Supply Line Repair in Jacksonville, FL

    Pool auto-fills, guest houses, outdoor kitchens and detached garages often have their own buried water lines. They are hard to trace. We use tracer wire, electronic locators and acoustic gear to find leaks along the full length, and repairs are made with direct-bury rated pipe and fittings, then pressure tested before we close up. Some lines get rerouted instead.

    Underground Storm Drain Repair in Jacksonville, FL

    Buried storm drains clog with silt, leaves and roots. Heavy afternoon thunderstorms are common in summer, and saturated ground can push water into sewer lines and slow every fixture in the house. Then the yard floods. We clear blockages with a jetter, find cracks and disconnected joints on camera, then fix the broken pipe so water drains to the outlet the way it was built to. Downspout tie-ins get checked.

    Foundation Leak Detection and Repair in Jacksonville, FL

    A leak under a slab often shows up as a warm spot on the floor. Or a hissing sound. We isolate hot and cold lines with pressure tests and locate the leak with acoustic gear and thermal imaging, then repair it through a small slab cut or reroute the line overhead so the floor stays intact. Both options get a price.

    Buried Sewer Lateral Repair in Jacksonville, FL

    Buried laterals crack from roots, settling and age. Video locates the break. We excavate at the mark, cut out the damaged length and replace it with Schedule 40 PVC, joining it to the existing pipe with shielded couplings sized for the outside diameter of each material. We confirm slope before backfill.

    Irrigation and Yard Line Repair in Jacksonville

    Sprinkler supply lines sit shallow and break easily. Shovels and roots get them. A broken irrigation main can run up a water bill fast in the growing season, and we fix the main, the backflow connection and the feed to the valves. Vulnerable runs can be moved.
