    Water Line Reroute in Jacksonville

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the foundation and runs a new line through the attic or walls. This skips breaking concrete and removes the risk of future leaks under the slab. We usually use PEX for reroutes because it is flexible and resists corrosion. Drywall openings are kept small and neat for simple patching.

    Crawl Space Foundation Leak Repair in Jacksonville, FL

    Crawl space foundations can develop leaks from plumbing, groundwater, or HVAC condensation. These leaks can lead to wood rot, mold, and structural damage if not addressed quickly. Our team carefully inspects the crawl space and identifies the source of water intrusion. We then implement solutions such as pipe repair, sump pump installation, or vapor barrier replacement to dry out and protect your crawl space in Jacksonville, FL from future moisture issues.

    Foundation Leak Detection in Jacksonville

    Accurate detection is the most important step in foundation leak repair. We isolate hot and cold lines and pressure test them to verify a leak exists.
