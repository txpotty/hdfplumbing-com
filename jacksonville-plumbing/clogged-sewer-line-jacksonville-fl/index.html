    Understanding the difference between a main sewer line clog and a branch line clog is vital for proper diagnosis and repair in Jacksonville, FL. A main line clog affects all drains in your home, often causing backups in lower-level fixtures. Branch line clogs are usually isolated to a single fixture or area.
