    What Our Tankless Maintenance Covers in Jacksonville, FL

    Condensate and Neutralizer Service in Jacksonville

    Condensing tankless heaters drip acidic condensate, often around pH 3 to 4, and it needs to pass through a neutralizer before it reaches a drain. The trap gets cleaned and the media checked. Spent media gets replaced. An outdoor run of condensate line gets checked for support and protection.

    Rinnai Air Filter and Flame Rod Care in Jacksonville

    Rinnai cabinets are tight. We wash the air intake filter and check the flame rod for carbon. Recirculating Rinnai models also have a pump setting worth checking, since a wrong schedule wastes gas every hour the pump runs for no reason.

    Step-by-Step Tankless Flushing in Jacksonville

    First we shut off the gas and power, then close both isolation valves so the heater is cut off from the house plumbing. The pump pulls solution from a bucket, through the cold port, out the hot port and back again. After the cycle we rinse with clean water and pull the inlet screen. Then we check for leaks.

    Emergency Tankless Repair in Jacksonville, FL

    No hot water before work is a rough start. Nine times out of ten, a no-heat call is a dead igniter or a scaled exchanger tripping the high-limit. The truck carries igniters and descaling gear, so we can usually fix it that day.

    Inlet Screen and Air Filter Cleaning in Jacksonville

    Grit gets in. A small screen at the cold inlet catches sand and scale flakes, especially after a main break. Units with an air intake filter collect dust and lint, which can push the burner into a combustion code. We clean both on every visit.

    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Jacksonville

    Picture a bundle of thin tubes with a gas flame under them and cold water racing through at three or four gallons a minute. That is the exchanger. Minerals settle on the hottest tube walls first, and the passages get narrower. We dissolve it with 45 to 60 minutes of pump-driven descaling solution.

    Navien Condensing Tune-Ups in Jacksonville

    Navien NPE units are condensing heaters with a second heat exchanger that pulls extra heat out of the exhaust before it leaves the house. That second exchanger makes acidic condensate. We clear the condensate trap and check the neutralizer media. A-series models with built-in recirculation also get a pump and buffer tank check.

    Multi-Unit Tankless Checks in Jacksonville, FL

    Restaurants and salons often run two or more tankless heaters on a shared manifold, and one weak unit can drag the whole bank down. We check gas pressure with all units firing at once. Each heater gets its own flow and temperature reading. Then we compare them.
