    Well Pressure Tank Leak Repair vs. Replacement in Jacksonville

    When your well pressure tank shows signs of trouble, it is important to determine if a repair is practical or if full replacement is necessary. Small air leaks or issues with the air valve might be repairable. However, if the tank itself is corroded, has a ruptured diaphragm, or is waterlogged, replacement is almost always the more cost-effective and reliable option. Our technicians in Jacksonville provide an straightforward assessment, explaining the pros and cons of repair versus a complete well pressure tank replacement, so you can make an informed decision.

    Bladder Pressure Tank Replacement in Jacksonville

    Bladder tanks hold water inside a flexible butyl bag surrounded by pressurized air. When the bag ruptures, water fills the whole tank, there is no air cushion and the pump short cycles. A simple test is pressing the air valve on top, since water spitting out means the bladder has failed. We replace the tank, set the new pre-charge and verify the pump runs a full cycle. Most bladder tank swaps take about two to three hours.

    Constant Pressure System Conversion in Jacksonville, FL

    A constant pressure system pairs a variable speed drive like the Franklin SubDrive or Grundfos SQE with a smaller pressure tank. The pump speeds up or slows down to hold a set pressure, so showers do not fade when a sprinkler kicks on. These systems cost more, often $2,500 to $5,000, but they end pressure swings. They also cut down on hard starts on the motor. We explain whether your well and pump are good candidates.

    Flex-Lite Pressure Tank Replacement in Jacksonville

    Flex-Lite composite pressure tanks are an good option for those seeking a lightweight, corrosion-resistant alternative to traditional steel tanks. They are particularly well-suited for homes with aggressive water conditions that might accelerate corrosion in metal tanks. HDF Plumbing carefully replaces old or failing tanks with Flex-Lite models, ensuring a precise fit and proper connection to your existing well system. Their durable, fiberglass-wound construction provides long-lasting, trouble-free operation.

    Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Jacksonville

    The tank tee is the manifold that connects the tank, switch, gauge, relief valve and drain. Galvanized tees corrode and restrict flow, especially with sandy or mineral-heavy water. We replace them with brass or stainless steel tank tees that hold up much longer. A new liquid-filled gauge gives reliable readings, and a fresh relief valve protects against a stuck switch. These small parts make a big difference in reliability.
