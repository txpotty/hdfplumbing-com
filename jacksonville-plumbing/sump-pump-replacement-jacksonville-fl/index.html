    Which Sump Pump Suits Your Jacksonville, FL Home

    Zoeller Sump Pump Replacement in Jacksonville, FL

    Zoeller cast iron pumps, the M53 and M63 in particular, are what we pull off the truck most often because they take heat and hard cycling better than plastic housings. If you have a Zoeller now, we can usually match the discharge outlet and switch style so the swap goes quickly. Need more lift? The M98 steps up to half horsepower.

    Sizing a Submersible Sump Pump in Jacksonville

    Horsepower matters less than people think. For most houses a 1/3 HP pump is enough, provided the water only has to climb 10 to 15 feet to get out. Before we choose, we read the pump curve at your real discharge height. A pump that is too big empties the pit too fast and wears itself out.

    Zoeller Switch and Pump Repair in Jacksonville

    Zoeller units with a piggyback float plug the pump cord into the switch cord, and if that piggyback plug fails the pump never gets power. That is the first thing we test. Some calls only need a new switch, while others need the whole pump. We tell you which before we sell you anything.

    Light Commercial Sump Pump Replacement in Jacksonville, FL

    Light commercial work, think retail basements and small office buildings, usually means a 1/2 HP or larger cast iron pump with a 2-inch discharge and a panel alarm. We replace like for like or upsize. Sewage ejectors and grinder pumps we quote separately.

    Wayne Sump Pump Replacement in Jacksonville

    Wayne makes everything from a thermoplastic quarter-horse pump at the hardware store to cast iron models with vertical switches, and the cheap ones are what we replace most. Most dead Wayne pumps we see have a cooked motor or a float tangled in the power cord. We replace it with the right Wayne or another cast iron unit. Figure on a couple of hours.

    Elevator and Garage Pit Pumps in Jacksonville

    Elevator pits and parking garage sumps often require oil-sensing controls that stop the pump from sending hydraulic fluid or motor oil into the storm drain. Pumps and oil-sensing panels get replaced to code. Duplex setups with alternating pumps give you a spare if one quits. Insurers sometimes ask for proof.

    Storm-Night Sump Pump Replacement in Jacksonville, FL

    Most emergency sump calls come in at night, during the heaviest rain of the year, when the pump has been running nonstop for hours. We move those calls to the front of the line. The tech brings a replacement pump and a new check valve and can have water moving out of the pit within an hour of arrival in most cases. After that, we look at why the old one quit.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Jacksonville

    If your pump runs every 30 seconds during a storm and still loses ground, it is undersized for the water coming in. We time the cycles and measure how fast the pit refills. The rest is simple arithmetic. A half-horse or three-quarter-horse pump with a higher gallons-per-minute rating at your lift height may be the answer, sometimes paired with a 2-inch discharge.

    Local Sump Pump Replacement in Jacksonville, FL

    Groundwater levels shift with the seasons, and in a wet spring a pit can take on water every few minutes even with no rain in the forecast. We size the pump for that worst week. Average days do not flood basements.
