    Pop-Up Drain Assembly Replacement in Jacksonville, FL

    A stopper that drops on its own has a worn pivot. When the pivot ball wears and the nut stops sealing, the stopper drops or sticks and the nut drips. One new pop-up fixes every part of that. Takes about an hour.

    Choosing Drain Material in Jacksonville, FL

    PVC is the default choice under most sinks. It does not rust, it stands up to drain cleaners, and it costs less than brass, while polished 17-gauge brass makes sense under a pedestal sink or any trap you can see. ABS works the same way. We match what you have, with a proper transition where they meet.

    Utility Sink Drain Replacement in Jacksonville

    Laundry tubs see lint and paint water. Over time that wears the thin plastic drain and trap on a utility sink until it cracks, and lint from the washer standpipe often builds up in the trap so badly that it never drains at full speed again. We fit a 1-1/2 inch trap with a cleanout. A lint trap on the washer hose helps.

    Drain Replacement vs Repair Costs in Jacksonville, FL

    A single washer is cheaper today. On a drain more than fifteen years old, though, the next joint often fails within months, and paying for two or three service calls costs more than replacing the whole assembly in one visit. You see both options in writing. Your call.

    Kitchen Sink Drain Replacement in Jacksonville

    Kitchen drains start failing at the basket strainer. The putty under a 3-1/2 inch strainer dries out, the locknut rusts, and water seeps down the outside of the tailpiece where it looks like the trap is leaking even when it is not. We replace the strainer and trap together. Disposal sinks get a new flange instead.

    Bathroom Sink Drain Replacement in Jacksonville, FL

    Bathroom drains wear out at the pop-up. The pivot nut packing leaks, the drain body corrodes around the overflow holes, and the stopper stops sealing, so a sink that will not hold water usually needs a whole new pop-up assembly. Half-fixes do not hold here. The new drain matches your faucet finish.

    Pop-Up Drain Replacement in Jacksonville

    Lift-rod pop-ups are the most common bath drain. We pull the old body, clean the flange seat down to bare porcelain, reset the new flange in putty or silicone, and adjust the clevis so the lift rod opens and closes the stopper with one easy motion. Finish matches the faucet. Overflow-style drains are standard.

    Vessel and Grid Drain Replacement in Jacksonville

    Vessel sinks use a different drain. Most glass and stone vessel bowls have no overflow hole, so they need an unslotted grid or umbrella drain, and a slotted drain in one of those bowls leaks out of the slots right into the counter hole. We see the wrong drain often. Swapping it takes under an hour.
