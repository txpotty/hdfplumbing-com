    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in St. Petersburg

    Too much salt or water use usually means settings that do not match your hardness or household. An old timer head regenerates by the calendar, used or not. After retesting hardness, we reprogram the valve or swap in a metered head that regenerates only when needed. Salt bills drop.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Service in St. Petersburg, FL

    The tank looks full but makes no brine. A hard crust has formed and is keeping the salt from dissolving. We break up bridges, clean out mushy salt, inspect the float and brine well and repair the safety valve. In a hot or damp garage, switching to pellets cuts down on bridging.

    Urgent Water Softener Repair in St. Petersburg, FL

    Major leaks and total system failure call for a fast response. HDF Plumbing handles urgent softener calls in St. Petersburg, FL and puts them ahead of routine work. The unit goes into bypass so you keep water, and then we fix whatever failed. Bypass first.

    Resin Bed Problems in St. Petersburg

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads so they cannot grab hardness. Depending on the test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced. Fresh resin brings capacity back.

    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in St. Petersburg, FL

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. Installing a new quality softener usually runs $1,500 to $3,500. Sound tank and valve, repair wins. If the valve is obsolete and parts are scarce, replacing the head or the whole unit may make more sense than chasing used parts online.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in St. Petersburg, FL

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Its small ports clog with sediment and salt. Once it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying, and the softener regenerates with plain water that does nothing to the resin. We clean or replace it and check the brine line for air leaks.

    Water Softener Repair vs. Replacement in St. Petersburg, FL

    Age and parts availability decide whether a softener is worth repairing. HDF Plumbing gives honest advice on both counts. A ten-year-old unit with a sound tank and a rebuildable valve is usually worth fixing, while an obsolete head with no parts available is a different conversation. We lay out both options.
