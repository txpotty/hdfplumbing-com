    Residential Clogged Sewer Lines in St. Petersburg

    Most home sewer clogs come from three things: roots, grease and wipes. Often all three together. Grease coats the pipe, wipes catch on it and roots lock everything into a plug. We clear it with a sewer machine, then camera the line to see which one started it.

    Tree Root Invasion Removal in St. Petersburg

    Roots find sewer lines by following moisture in the soil. They only need a tiny opening.     Grease and Sludge Buildup Removal in St. Petersburg, FL

    Kitchen grease cools in the sewer line and sticks to the walls. Layer after layer builds up. HDF Plumbing jets it out with a grease nozzle in St. Petersburg, FL. The camera confirms it is gone.

    Main Line Hydro Jetting in St. Petersburg

    A jetter cleans the full diameter of the pipe, not just a hole through the middle. That matters a lot.
