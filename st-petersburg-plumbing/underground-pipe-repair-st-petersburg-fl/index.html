    Trenchless Pipe Repair in St. Petersburg

    Trenchless repair replaces or relines buried pipe without opening its full length. One or two holes, not a trench.     Polybutylene was used in a lot of homes built in the 1980s and early 1990s. It fails without warning. The gray plastic turns brittle as chlorine in the water attacks it, with failures most common at fittings and tight bends. Replacement is the only lasting fix. We use PEX or copper.
