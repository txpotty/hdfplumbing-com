    Recurring Drain Clogs and Camera Diagnosis in St. Petersburg, FL

    The same drain clogging every few weeks is a pipe problem, not bad luck. Something is catching debris. We run a small inspection camera through the line, letting you see any sag, crack or heavy scale on the screen. The choice is yours from there. A deeper cleaning, a spot repair or a new section are the usual options.

    Outdoor Drain & Storm Drain Clearing in St. Petersburg, FL

    Patio drains, driveway trench drains and downspout lines fill with leaves, grit and mud. Heavy afternoon thunderstorms are common in summer, and saturated ground can push water into drain lines and slow every fixture in the house. Then water heads for the foundation. We clear the grate and the pipe behind it with a cable or jetter, flush the line to its outlet and check that the outlet itself is not buried under mulch or soil. Clear outlets matter.

    Bathroom Drain Clog Clearing in St. Petersburg, FL

    Hair, soap scum and toothpaste bind together into a mat that sits on the pop-up stopper. Most sink clogs live there. We pull the stopper and clean it, clear the P-trap and run a small cable through the wall line if the water is still slow. A drain screen helps a lot.

    Bathroom Sink and Vanity Drain Clogs in St. Petersburg, FL

    Vanity drains clog at the pop-up stopper first. Hair wraps around the rod. We pull and clean the stopper, clean the trap, cable the drain arm and swap out worn parts while we are there. Scale buildup is common there.

    Toilet Clog Repair in St. Petersburg

    A toilet clog usually sits in the trapway, the S-shaped channel inside the bowl. Plungers often miss it. We use a closet auger with a rubber sleeve, so the cable clears the trapway and the bowl stays unmarked. We pull out a lot of wipes. If the auger finds nothing, the clog is past the toilet and we check the main.

    Preventative Drain Maintenance in St. Petersburg

    Drains that clog once a year can be cleaned once a year instead. That is the whole idea. HDF Plumbing sets up maintenance visits that cable or jet your problem lines, usually the kitchen line and the main, before buildup gets thick enough to stop the flow. Enzyme treatments between visits can help.

    Kitchen Sink Clog Repair in St. Petersburg

    Kitchen sinks clog from grease that cools and hardens on the pipe wall. Then food scraps and soap pile on. We pull the trap, run a cable through the trap arm and down the branch line to the main stack, and check the disposal for a jam or worn impeller. A heavy grease line may need a quick jet afterward.

    Tub and Shower Drain Clogs in St. Petersburg

    Slow tubs are almost always hair and soap scum. Every so often it is a lost bottle cap. We remove the strainer or overflow plate, pull the clog out with a drum auger built for small diameter drains and then check the trap if the drain is still sluggish. We take care not to scratch acrylic, fiberglass or porcelain.
