    Supply and Drain Leaks Under the Slab in St. Petersburg, FL

    A supply leak and a drain leak show different signs. Supply leaks are pressurized, so they move the meter, warm the floor on the hot side and run whether or not anyone uses water. Drain leaks only leak when water goes down, and they show up as odors, slow drains or damp floors near bathrooms. We test both kinds in your St. Petersburg, FL home.

    Pressure Testing and Isolation in St. Petersburg

    We always start with isolation. Our tech closes the main, caps or valves off the hot and cold sides, and puts a gauge on each, then watches which side loses pressure over a set time.     Listening Through Concrete in St. Petersburg
