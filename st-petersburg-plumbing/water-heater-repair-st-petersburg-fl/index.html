    Gas Control and Thermocouple Repair in St. Petersburg

    Thermocouples, flame sensors, igniters and gas control valves cause most gas heater failures. We test each one, clean the burner and pilot assembly and check draft at the vent. A pilot that lights and then goes out a few seconds later usually points to a weak thermocouple or a clogged flame arrestor screen. Most are done in under two hours.

    Electric Water Heater Element Repair in St. Petersburg

    Lukewarm water or hot water that runs out in five minutes usually means a burned-out element or a stuck thermostat. Each element gets an ohm reading. Readings tell us whether an element is open or shorted, which explains the breaker trips. Common elements and thermostats are stocked.

    Water Heater Leak Repair in St. Petersburg, FL

    Water on the floor next to the heater needs a closer look before anyone panics. Leaks at the relief valve or a supply fitting are usually cheap to fix. Water coming from the tank body or the bottom seam means the lining has failed, and no part will fix that. A drain pan helps on a slab.

    Electric Water Heater Repair in St. Petersburg

    Two elements and two thermostats run a standard electric tank, and any one of the four can quit without warning. If the water is warm but runs out quickly, the lower element is the first suspect. We test resistance with a meter. Elements and thermostats are on the truck, so the fix is usually done the same visit.

    Tank Water Heater Repair in St. Petersburg

    Most homes still run a 50-gallon tank. Thermostats, elements, anodes and relief valves make up most tank repairs. A good flush takes care of a lot of noise and slow recovery problems on its own, no parts required.

    Anode Rod Replacement in St. Petersburg, FL

    The anode rod sacrifices itself so the steel tank does not rust. In hard water it can be eaten away within a few years. After pulling the old rod, we fit a magnesium, aluminum or powered anode to suit your water and any smell. Rotten-egg odor often clears up.

    Gas Water Heater Repair in St. Petersburg, FL

    Gas units add a burner, a pilot or igniter and a gas control valve to the list of parts that can fail. Most no-heat calls trace to the thermocouple or the gas control. After any repair, every gas connection gets a leak test. Draft at the vent gets checked too.

    Sediment Flush and Noise Repair in St. Petersburg

    Popping, rumbling or crackling usually means a thick layer of sediment is trapping water and overheating the steel at the tank bottom. We drain the tank, break up scale with repeated fills and check the drain valve for blockage. Flushing restores capacity. On hard water, plan on one flush a year.
