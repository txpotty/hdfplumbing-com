    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in St. Petersburg, FL

    Resin beads break down after 10 to 15 years, faster on chlorinated city water, and the softener keeps regenerating while the water slowly turns hard again. New resin in the old tank sometimes beats a full replacement on price. It depends on the valve. New resin under a tired valve rarely lasts.

    Salt-Free Alternatives in St. Petersburg, FL

    Some homeowners do not want salt or have no drain near the main line. A TAC conditioner is the honest alternative there, and we tell people plainly that it prevents scale but will not give them the slick feel of soft water. Sodium concerns are another reason.

    Long-Term Softener Costs in St. Petersburg, FL

    Long-term cost on a softener is mostly salt and water. Compared with a timer unit, a metered valve set efficiently often uses a third less salt, which matters over a fifteen-year life. We compare brands on that basis.

    Whole-Home Softener Placement in St. Petersburg, FL

    Every tap in the house, including the shower and the water heater, gets soft water when the softener sits on the main line ahead of everything. We typically leave the hose bibs on hard water. No sense spending salt on the garden.

    Replacing Pelican Water Systems in St. Petersburg

    Pelican systems usually combine a carbon filter with either a salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner in one setup on the main line. Depending on how old it is, we swap media or the full unit. Following the install specs keeps the warranty intact.

    TAC Salt-Free Conditioners in St. Petersburg

    Template-assisted crystallization, or TAC, is the salt-free media with real independent test data behind it, and it is the only kind we install. It mounts on the main line after any filter and needs no electricity or drain. Magnets and clamp-on gadgets are a different story.

    Softener Loop Installation in St. Petersburg, FL

    Softener loops are the pair of stubbed-out lines near the garage wall or water heater where a softener ties into the main. Homes without one get a loop cut in, with shutoffs and a bypass. Outdoor spigots stay on hard water. The drain goes to a laundry box or floor drain with an air gap.

    Metered Softener Replacement in St. Petersburg

    Timer softeners regenerate every few days whether you used 50 gallons or 500. Metered valves like the Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 count gallons and regenerate only when the resin is close to spent. Salt bills usually drop in the first month.

    Local Water Softener Replacement in St. Petersburg, FL

    Local water varies. Hardness can be very different between a city supply and a private well a few miles out, and iron or chlorine changes what else the system needs. The system matches your test, not a guess.
