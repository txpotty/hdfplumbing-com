    Hidden Wall & Ceiling Leak Detection in St. Petersburg, FL

    Leaks behind walls or in ceilings can cause serious damage before they become visible, leading to stained drywall, peeling paint or a musty odor. These often stem from faulty pipe connections, old galvanized pipes or issues with shower pans and drains. Our technicians use moisture meters and infrared cameras to scan these areas, identifying elevated moisture levels and temperature differences that indicate a hidden leak, minimizing the need for extensive wall demolition.

    Water Heater and Softener Leak Detection in St. Petersburg

    Water heaters and softeners can leak at fittings, valves and drain lines. We check these units carefully and repair or replace failed parts. That often explains a mystery bill in homes that have water softeners.

    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in St. Petersburg

    Leaks in underground water lines, from your meter to your home, can go unnoticed for long periods, wasting water and causing yard erosion. Signs include oddly green patches in your lawn, persistent wet spots or a constantly running water meter. HDF Plumbing uses ground-penetrating radar and tracer gas detection methods to accurately map out and pinpoint leaks in buried pipes, allowing for targeted excavation and repair rather than digging up your entire yard.
