    Cartridge and Stem Replacement in St. Petersburg, FL

    Most dripping tub faucets need internals, not a new valve. We swap cartridges or stems and seats, greasing the O-rings so handles turn easily. Seat wrenches come out often. About an hour, usually.

    Brand Tub Faucet Replacement in St. Petersburg

    Brand matters more on tub valves than anywhere else. Cross-brand trim seldom fits. We find the logo or the casting mark first.

    Three-Handle to Single-Handle Conversion in St. Petersburg

    Three-handle faucets are worth converting. We cut out the old valve, sweat or crimp in a single-handle pressure-balance valve, and cover the old handle holes with a remodel plate so the tile stays intact around it. We usually work from behind the wall. Tile around the plate stays intact.

    Freestanding and Deck-Mount Tub Filler Installation in St. Petersburg

    Freestanding tubs need a floor-mount filler. Deck fillers mount on the tub deck. Tub-only fillers need a temperature limit.
