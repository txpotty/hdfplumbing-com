    HDF Plumbing stands behind our PEX pipe installations with a comprehensive warranty on both labor and materials.     Most whole-home PEX repipes take two to four days. Larger homes or difficult attic access can take a bit longer.

    PEX is a good fit for a wide range of climates because of its flexibility and resistance to freezing. Humidity causes condensation on cold lines and clogged AC condensate drains, a common source of hidden water damage. While rigid pipes can burst when water freezes, PEX can expand and contract, significantly reducing the risk of burst pipes during cold snaps. It also performs well in high temperatures, making it a reliable choice year-round in St. Petersburg.

    PEX must be protected from sunlight because UV light breaks down it. Outdoors it needs to be buried, sleeved or completely covered.

    Yes, PEX piping is certified safe for drinking water. It does not leach chemicals into water and is approved by the major plumbing codes for potable water systems. It is a cleaner alternative to older pipes that can corrode and affect water quality.

    Signs you might need PEX repiping include persistently low water pressure, discolored or rusty water, frequent leaks, or old galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes. These issues are typical in older St. Petersburg homes and point to a failing plumbing system.

    While PEX is simpler to install than copper, professional installation is highly recommended. Correct sizing, routing and secure connections are essential for preventing leaks and ensuring system longevity. Improper DIY installation can lead to costly water damage and code violations.
