    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in St. Petersburg

    For more extensive damage, multiple leaks, or pipes that are too old and corroded to repair reliably, pipe rerouting is a long-term solution. This involves abandoning the leaking line under the slab and installing a new pipe through the walls or attic. This method skips the problem area entirely, providing a fresh, durable plumbing line. It is a common solution for aging homes in St. Petersburg with original copper or galvanized plumbing.

    Direct Slab Penetration Repair in St. Petersburg, FL

    When a slab leak is directly accessible and localized, direct slab penetration is often the most efficient repair method.     Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in St. Petersburg

    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in St. Petersburg, FL

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat expands and contracts the pipe against concrete and gravel. Warm floors and higher gas or electric bills are common signs.
