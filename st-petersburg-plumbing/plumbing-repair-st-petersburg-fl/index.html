    Water Softener Repair in St. Petersburg, FL

    Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving stubborn scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. White crust on faucets and spotty glasses are the first signs a softener has stopped working. The usual causes are a salt bridge in the brine tank or a plugged injector screen. HDF Plumbing repairs the common brands and tests the water hardness before and after the fix so you can see the number drop. Resin lasts ten to fifteen years.

    Drain Repairs in St. Petersburg

    Slow drains that come back a week after snaking usually mean the pipe itself is damaged. Cracked cast iron or a sagging run will hold debris every time. We run a camera to find the spot, then replace that section or hydro-jet the line if buildup is the only problem. Grease is the usual buildup.

    Trenchless Pipe Repair in St. Petersburg, FL

    Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE line through the path of the old one, and cured-in-place lining builds a new pipe inside the existing pipe. Neither one needs a trench across the driveway. Counting concrete and sod replacement, the cost often lands near an excavation. A camera decides.

    Well Pump Service in St. Petersburg, FL

    Submersible pumps sit well below the water line, so diagnosis happens at the surface first. HDF Plumbing checks the pressure switch, the control box capacitor and the tank precharge, then measures amp draw on the motor. High amp draw usually means a worn motor or a seized impeller. Jet pumps are simpler, and most of the time a bad foot valve or a lost prime is to blame.

    Water Heater Valve and Element Repair in St. Petersburg

    Rumbling or popping from a tank means sediment on the bottom. A rotten-egg smell in the hot water usually means the anode rod is reacting with the water. We replace thermocouples, thermostats, elements, gas valves, anode rods and relief valves from truck stock, which covers the large majority of tank repairs we see in a year. Rust at the seams is different. That tank is done, and we will say so.

    Slab Leak Repair for St. Petersburg, FL Homes

    Nearly all homes in Florida sit on concrete slabs over sandy soil, so leaks under the slab are a frequent and costly repair. Lines under a slab take the brunt of that movement, and Florida homeowners often find out from a warm floor or a spinning meter. HDF Plumbing finds the leak with electronic equipment before any concrete is cut. Then the choice is a spot repair through the floor or a reroute that abandons the old line. Reroutes skip the jackhammer.

    Slab Leak Repair in St. Petersburg, FL

    A warm spot on tile, a meter that spins with every tap off, or a bill that doubles for no reason. Those are slab leak signs. Acoustic listening gear and a thermal camera find the spot before anyone touches the concrete. Options include a spot repair through the slab or a reroute overhead, each priced separately.

    Gas Line Leak Repair in St. Petersburg
