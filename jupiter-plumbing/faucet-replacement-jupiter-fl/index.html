    Kitchen Faucet Replacement in Jupiter

    Kitchen faucets usually fail at the hose first. The pull-down hose wears where it bends over the spout neck, and once it splits, water runs down the inside of the faucet and into the cabinet without anyone seeing it. A hose kit can fix that. If the body is also worn, the whole faucet gets replaced along with the supplies.

    Corrosion-Resistant Faucet Choices in Jupiter, FL

    Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving stubborn scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. Finish and body material decide how a faucet ages. Brass bodies with a PVD finish hold up to minerals, but cheap plated zinc tends to bubble and flake when scale gets under the plating. Stainless finishes hide spots well. We show you the options before you buy.

    Tub and Shower Trim Replacement in Jupiter, FL

    Tub and shower trim can be replaced without opening the wall. A Moen Posi-Temp or Delta Monitor valve takes a matching trim kit, which swaps the handle and the spout without touching the tile. The valve has to match. An outdated valve may need replacing instead.

    Outdoor Spigot Replacement in Jupiter, FL

    Outdoor spigots wear out from sun, hoses and hard closing. Northern parts of Florida do get occasional freezing nights, which can crack exposed pipes, irrigation lines, and backflow devices. Worn spigots get replaced, with frost-free models where needed and a vacuum breaker on each, which code calls for on every hose thread. The wall cavity gets a look too. Old leaks sometimes soak the insulation.

    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Replacement in Jupiter

    Pull-down spray heads take a beating. The hose wears, the spray button sticks, and after a few years the docking magnet or weight stops pulling the head back into place. We swap the whole faucet. The new hose weight gets clipped where the head docks cleanly, and both supplies are new braided lines.

    Water-Saving Faucet Upgrades in Jupiter

    Federal rules cap new kitchen and lav faucets at 2.2 gallons per minute. WaterSense lav faucets go lower, to 1.5 gallons per minute, and most people do not notice the difference because the aerator mixes air into the stream. It adds up over a year. We can swap a bath faucet for a WaterSense model in the same visit.

    Corroded Faucet Replacement in Jupiter

    Green crust at the base means the faucet body is corroding. Once the brass under the finish is pitted, cartridges stop sealing against it, and every repair drips again within a few weeks. Replacement is the only fix. Even with fused nuts, we get it out and install a faucet with a ceramic disc cartridge.
