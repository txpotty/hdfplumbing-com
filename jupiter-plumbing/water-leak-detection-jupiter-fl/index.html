    Water Meter Leak Testing in Jupiter

    The water meter is the first and simplest leak test. With every fixture and appliance off, a moving leak indicator means water is escaping somewhere. We read the flow rate to estimate how many gallons are being lost. Then we shut off zones one at a time to see which part of the property is losing water.

    Water Heater and Softener Leak Detection in Jupiter

    Water heaters and softeners can leak at fittings, valves and drain lines. A softener stuck in regeneration can send water down the drain continuously, and a dripping relief valve on a water heater wastes water too. We check these units carefully and repair or replace failed parts. That often explains a mystery bill in homes that have water softeners.

    Smart Leak Detection Device Installation in Jupiter

    Smart shutoff valves like Moen Flo and Phyn monitor water flow and can shut off water automatically when they detect a leak. Small sensors placed near water heaters, sinks and washers send alerts to your phone. We install and set up these systems so you get warnings before small leaks become big ones. Some insurers offer discounts for these devices.

    Toilet and Fixture Leak Detection in Jupiter, FL

    Toilets are one of the most common sources of wasted water. A worn flapper or a fill valve that never shuts off can leak silently around the clock. We use dye tests to find leaking toilets and check faucets, showers and supply lines for drips. These fixes are usually quick and save water right away.

    Irrigation Leak Detection in Jupiter

    Drip and sprinkler systems run underground across yards and often account for hidden water loss. Cracked tubing, broken emitters and stuck valves can waste huge amounts of water. We isolate the irrigation system, test each zone and trace leaking lines. Repairs restore efficient watering for your landscape.
