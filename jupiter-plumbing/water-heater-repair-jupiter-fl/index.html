    Electric Tank Repair in Jupiter, FL

    Four parts can fail on an electric tank, two elements and two thermostats. Scale from hard water buries the lower element and burns it out. After testing resistance and voltage, we replace what failed, reset the high limit and watch it heat up. A breaker that keeps tripping gets traced first.

    Tankless Water Heater Repair in Jupiter

    Scale is the main enemy of a tankless heater. Mineral buildup on the heat exchanger triggers overheating codes and shutdowns. A descaling flush, a clean inlet screen and a check of the igniter and flow sensor cover most tankless calls. All major brands.

    Pilot Repair in Jupiter

    A pilot that lights and then dies when you let go of the knob almost always means a weak thermocouple. Sometimes the cause is a clogged flame arrestor screen starving the burner of air. The thermocouple gets a millivolt test. A good reading points us to the gas valve instead.

    Electric Water Heater Element Repair in Jupiter

    Lukewarm water or hot water that runs out in five minutes usually means a burned-out element or a stuck thermostat. We check each element with an ohmmeter. A shorted element will trip the breaker, while an open one just sits there cold, and the readings tell us which one is which. Parts for most tanks ride on the truck.

    Tank Water Heater Repair in Jupiter

    Most homes still run a 50-gallon tank. Thermostats, elements, anodes and relief valves make up most tank repairs. A good flush takes care of a lot of noise and slow recovery problems on its own, no parts required.
