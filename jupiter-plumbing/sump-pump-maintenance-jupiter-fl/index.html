    Sump Pump Maintenance in Jupiter, FL: What We Check

    Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in Jupiter, FL

    Not every sump sits in a basement. Crawlspaces and deep window wells often have small pumps that clear runoff after a storm. Our plumber clears the screen, tests the float and traces the discharge to daylight or an approved drain, since a line that ends under the house only relocates the water. When a rare freeze is forecast, covering outdoor valves and letting a faucet drip typically does the job to prevent damage.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Jupiter

    Hurricanes and tropical storms bring flooding, power loss, and sewer backups to Florida, often overwhelming drainage systems for days. We test the pump a few weeks before the wet season by filling the pit with a hose until the float trips. Then we time how fast it pumps out and listen to the bearings. A struggling pump can then be replaced on a calm day instead of in the middle of a storm.

    Sump Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Jupiter

    Silt and gravel wash into a pit through the drain tile every time it rains, and over a few years that layer can bury the intake screen. Our plumber drains the basin and digs the sediment out by hand. A clean pit lets the float move freely. The pump runs cooler too.

    Elevator Pit and Garage Pumps in Jupiter

    Elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms all collect water that has nowhere to go by gravity, so a pump has to lift it out. Elevator pit pumps often need oil-detecting controls so hydraulic fluid never gets pumped out with the water. Duplex systems and high-water alarms get tested on a fixed schedule. The property manager gets a copy of every log.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Maintenance in Jupiter

    Zoeller pumps like the M53 and M63 use cast iron housings and an integral float switch, and they tend to outlast cheaper plastic units. Even so, we look for cracks in the casting and make sure the float moves freely. We carry the common Zoeller parts on the truck.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Jupiter

    The check valve sits on the discharge pipe a foot or so above the pump and keeps the water in the line from falling back into the pit. When it fails, the pump pumps the same water twice. Motors wear out faster. We test the valve, look for a weep hole between the pump and the valve, and check every joint up to the wall.

    Float Switch Inspection in Jupiter, FL

    The float switch tells the pump when to start and stop, and a float with too little travel between on and off makes the pump short cycle dozens of times an hour. Our plumber watches the float rise and drop through a complete cycle. Then we set the on and off points so the pump runs longer, less often.

    Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Jupiter, FL

    Some Wayne models draw water through a top suction intake, which helps them avoid pulling in the sludge that settles at the bottom of a pit. That intake gets cleaned, and so does the float mechanism. Loose discharge fittings show up on every brand. Easy fix.

    Neighborhood Sump Pump Maintenance Near Me in Jupiter, FL

    Our Jupiter plumbers have serviced Zoeller, Liberty, Wayne and Little Giant pumps in all kinds of pits, from old fieldstone basements to new poured walls. You get a flat price before any work starts.

    Low spots on a block flood first, and our crews know which Jupiter streets see standing water after a hard storm. It helps us judge how far a discharge line needs to run. A pump in a low lot works harder.
