    What We Fix Under the Sink in Jupiter, FL

    Garbage Disposal Repair in Jupiter

    A disposal that hums but will not spin is usually jammed. From below we turn the flywheel with a 1/4 inch hex key, hit the reset button and look for a bent impeller. A leaking unit is another matter. A cracked housing means a new disposal, usually an InSinkErator or a Waste King.

    Bathroom Sink Repair in Jupiter, FL

    Bath sinks have small parts that wear. Pop-up linkage parts sit in a damp cabinet all day, and the pivot nut is the leak we find most often on bathroom sinks. We check pedestal and wall-hung sinks the same way. Vanity sinks just hide the leak better.

    Supply Line and Angle Stop Replacement in Jupiter, FL

    Braided supply lines have a rubber core that ages. After five to ten years the core can split without warning, and old multi-turn stops often seize open. We swap both in one visit. Quarter-turn stops close in one motion.

    Burst Supply Line Repair in Jupiter

    A burst line under a sink can push out several gallons a minute. Close the angle stop, or the main if the stop fails, because every minute adds water to the subfloor. Then lay towels down. We replace the line and the stop and moisture-check the cabinet.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Jupiter

    A faucet that drips once a second wastes thousands of gallons a year. On compression faucets the fix is a new washer and seat, while cartridge and ceramic disc faucets need the cartridge or its seals replaced. Cartridges for Moen, Delta, Pfister and Kohler are on the truck. Odd brands take a day to order.

    Leaking P-Trap Fix in Jupiter

    Check the slip nuts first. Overtightened plastic slip nuts crack, and the beveled washers inside them flatten until each joint weeps. The whole trap gets replaced as a kit. A 1-1/4 inch kit for bath sinks, 1-1/2 inch for kitchens.

    Cracked or Loose Basin Repair in Jupiter

    Undermount clips let go more often than people think. When the epoxy under the counter fails, the bowl drops a quarter inch, the caulk line splits, and water from the counter runs straight down into the cabinet. We reset the bowl on new clips. Hairline cracks in porcelain near the drain mean replacement.

    Recurring Sink Clog Repair in Jupiter, FL

    Monthly clogs have a cause past the trap. We look past the wall with a longer cable or a camera for a sag or for buildup where the sink line meets the tub. Now and then it is a plugged vent. A sink that gurgles as it drains is the giveaway.
