    How Pipe Bursting Works in Jupiter

    A winch pulls the bursting head. The head is slightly bigger than the old pipe, so it splits clay or cast iron outward into the soil while fused HDPE trails behind it in one continuous piece. You get a pit at each end. With no joints in the new pipe, roots have no way back in.

    Sewer Permits and Code in Jupiter, FL

    Sewer replacement nearly always needs a permit. We file it, schedule the inspection and meet the inspector on site, so you never have to take a morning off to stand in the yard. The inspector looks at pipe size, slope, bedding and cleanouts. Skipping the permit can come back to bite you at resale.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement in Jupiter, FL

    Two holes instead of one long trench. With bursting, a steel head splits the old pipe into the soil and pulls new HDPE into the same path. Lining is the other trenchless route. A resin-soaked tube is pulled into the cleaned host pipe and cured in place, which leaves a new pipe inside the old one and works well when the original still holds its round shape.

    Upgrading Old Sewer Pipe Materials in Jupiter

    Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg all fail in their own ways. Clay joints let roots in every few feet, cast iron scales and rusts through along the bottom, and Orangeburg tar paper pipe slowly flattens into an oval. Modern plastic pipe avoids all three problems. Fused HDPE has no joints at all, so roots have nowhere to start.
