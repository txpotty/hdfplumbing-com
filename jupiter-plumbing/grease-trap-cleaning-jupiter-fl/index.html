    Why Choose HDF Plumbing for Grease Trap Cleaning in Jupiter, FL?

    A full trap lets grease out the outlet tee. It hardens in the pipe from there. We pump on a schedule that keeps the grease and solids layer well under the 25 percent mark, measured with a sludge judge at every visit rather than guessed from the calendar.

    • Prevent Backups, Pumping on schedule keeps grease from slipping past the outlet and setting up in the line.
    • Odor Control, A pumped trap stops the sour, rotten-egg smell that decaying grease gives off.
    • Prevent Blockages, Regular pumping keeps floor drains flowing during the dinner rush.

    Citywide Coverage

    We pump traps for bars and bakeries in every part of Jupiter, from downtown blocks with alley access to suburban strip centers where the interceptor sits under a parking space. Tight spots are normal. We carry long hose runs for traps that are a long way from the street.

    Experienced Team

    Each technician carries a sludge judge and writes down real depths. Nobody eyeballs the depth. Those measurements are what let us tell you, with numbers rather than a hunch, whether your current schedule is too tight or too loose for your kitchen.
