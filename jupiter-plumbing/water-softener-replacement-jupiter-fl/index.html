    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Jupiter, FL

    Softener resin can break down after 10 to 15 years, especially with chlorinated city water. When resin fails, you will see hard water symptoms even though the system is regenerating. Sometimes replacing resin and the tank is more cost effective than a whole new system. We inspect the valve and brine tank to decide. If the valve is old, a full replacement often makes more sense.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Jupiter

    Large households and homes with very hard water can benefit from a dual tank system that provides soft water all day and night. While one tank regenerates, the other stays in service. Kinetico and similar non-electric twin tank systems are popular for this reason. We size dual tank systems to your peak demand. They cost more upfront but prevent hard water bleed-through during regeneration.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Jupiter

    A standard single tank softener replacement typically costs $1,500 to $3,000 installed. Dual tank or premium systems usually run $3,000 to $5,000. Adding a new loop or drain line can add to the cost.

    Most softeners last 10 to 15 years. Resin and valves wear out sooner with very hard or chlorinated water.

    Many systems include 5 to 10 years on the valve and longer coverage on tanks. We also provide a warranty on our labor.

    We test your hardness in grains per gallon and estimate daily water usage. That determines the resin capacity needed.

    New water softeners typically come with manufacturer warranties ranging from 1 to 10 years on different components, such as the control valve and resin tank. HDF Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work for your peace of mind. Exact warranty details will be provided with your quote.

    Salt-free conditioners reduce scale buildup but do not remove hardness minerals. A true softener removes them, which is better for soap and fixtures.

    While it might seem like a straightforward task, water softener replacement involves working with water lines, drain connections and sometimes electrical components. Improper installation can lead to leaks, inefficient operation or even water damage. Hiring a professional means the job is done correctly, safely and to code.

    A new, properly sized water softener can sometimes improve water pressure if your old unit was undersized or had significant mineral buildup restricting flow. However, a water softener's main job is to remove hardness minerals, not to boost pressure. If low water pressure is your main concern, we can look into other causes.
