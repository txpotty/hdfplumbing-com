    Disposal Installation on an Existing Sink in Jupiter

    For a common 1/2 or 3/4 HP disposal, we remove the old unit if there is one and install the new one on the existing sink.     Air Switch Disposal Installation in Jupiter, FL

    An air button on the sink deck or counter turns the disposal on. The button pushes air through a tube to a control box in the cabinet, which keeps electricity away from the button and avoids a new wall switch. They are common on islands.

    First-Time Disposal Installation in Jupiter

    For kitchens that have never had a disposal, we start by checking the sink flange, drain height and electrical supply. The mount goes on, the unit gets hung and the discharge tube is connected to a new trap, with the wall stub lowered if it sits too high. Power comes from a licensed electrician when needed.

    Commercial Disposal Setup in Jupiter

    Restaurants and commercial kitchens in Jupiter need disposals built for constant use.     High-Capacity Disposal Installation in Jupiter, FL

    Busy kitchens get the most from a 1 HP unit, like an InSinkErator Evolution or a Waste King Legend model. We check that the circuit can handle the motor and that the discharge path is sized for the heavier flow, since a big unit feeding a narrow or poorly sloped trap arm still clogs. Bigger units are heavier. We support them while mounting.

    Batch-Feed Disposal Installation in Jupiter, FL

    A batch-feed disposal will not start until the lid is in, and the lid holds a magnet that closes a switch inside the unit. No wall switch is needed, which makes them handy in kitchens where running a new switch would mean opening finished walls. You grind one load at a time. Families with small kids often like the trade.

    Batch-Feed Disposal Setup in Jupiter

    The stopper lid acts as the switch on a batch-feed unit. We install the unit, then test the lid in every position to make sure the motor only runs when the lid is fully seated and stops the moment it is lifted out. That is the whole point. Families with kids and anyone nervous about a hand near the hopper tend to choose these in Jupiter homes.

    Switching From Batch-Feed to Continuous-Feed in Jupiter, FL

    A continuous-feed unit needs a switch, which a batch-feed kitchen may not have. That can be a wall switch, an air switch button on the sink deck or a switched outlet under the sink, and the choice depends on how hard it is to run wire to the spot you want. Air switches avoid new wiring in the wall. The plumbing side is usually a straight swap.
