    Signs you might need PEX repiping include persistently low water pressure, discolored or rusty water, frequent leaks, or old galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes. These issues are typical in older Jupiter homes and point to a failing plumbing system.

    While PEX is simpler to install than copper, professional installation is strongly recommended. Correct sizing, routing and secure connections are essential for preventing leaks and ensuring system longevity. Improper DIY installation can lead to costly water damage and code violations.

    PEX offers several advantages over copper, including lower material and installation costs, greater flexibility and resistance to corrosion and freezing. Unlike copper, PEX does not develop pinhole leaks over time due to water chemistry, making it a more durable choice for many Jupiter homes. It also reduces water hammer noise.

    PEX is a good fit for a wide range of climates because of its flexibility and resistance to freezing. Humidity causes condensation on cold lines and clogged AC condensate drains, a frequent source of hidden water damage. While rigid pipes can burst when water freezes, PEX can expand and contract, greatly lowering the risk of burst pipes during cold snaps.
