    Custom Shower System Installation in Jupiter, FL

    Multi-head showers depend on valve capacity.     Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Jupiter, FL

    Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common jobs. We pull the tub, move the drain from the end of the tub to where the shower pan needs it and raise the valve to shower height, usually around 48 inches off the floor. Slab homes need concrete work. No more stepping over a tub wall.

    Shower Valve and Trim Installation in Jupiter, FL

    The valve sits behind the tile for decades. We install pressure-balancing, thermostatic and diverter valves, set to the depth your trim kit needs and plumbed with integral stops when the valve has them. Trim goes on after tile. Waiting keeps the trim clean during tiling.

    Rain and Multi-Head Shower Installation in Jupiter

    Rain heads pull up to 2.5 gallons per minute each. Add a handheld and a couple of body sprays and you can be running more water than a standard 40-gallon water heater can keep hot for a long shower. We check the heater. Thermostatic valves with volume controls run the outlets.

    Shower Valve and Fixture Installation in Jupiter, FL
