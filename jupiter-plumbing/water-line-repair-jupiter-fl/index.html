    Dependable Water Line Repair Services in Jupiter, FL

    Main Water Line Repair in Jupiter

    The main water line brings water from the municipal supply to your home. When this line leaks or breaks, it can cause significant water loss, foundation damage, and low water pressure. Our team focuses on diagnosing and repairing main water line issues, from pinpoint leaks to full pipe ruptures, so your home's primary water source is secure. We use advanced detection methods to find the exact location of the problem, limiting excavation and disruption to your property in Jupiter. We also check for common issues like root intrusion or shifting soil.

    Main Water Line Replacement in Jupiter, FL

    When repairs are no longer cost-effective due to extensive damage or persistent issues, a full main water line replacement may be necessary. This often applies to older homes in Jupiter, FL with original galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes that are prone to corrosion and leaks. We offer full replacement services, upgrading your system with durable, modern materials like PEX or copper for a reliable water supply for decades to come. We handle all necessary permits and inspections.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Jupiter

    Many water line problems are made worse by high incoming pressure. A failed pressure reducing valve can push well over 80 psi into your plumbing, which stresses the service line and every fixture. We test static pressure with a gauge and replace worn regulators from brands like Watts and Zurn Wilkins. We also swap seized gate valves for full-port ball valves so you can shut the water off quickly in an emergency.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Jupiter

    If your service line runs under a driveway, pool deck or mature landscaping, trenchless replacement can save a lot of restoration cost. We dig small pits at each end and pull new HDPE or PEX pipe through using a pneumatic boring tool or pipe pulling equipment. The process usually takes one day. You keep your pavers and trees, and the new line carries a lengthy manufacturer warranty.

    PEX and Copper Water Line Repair in Jupiter

    Whether your home has modern PEX piping or traditional copper lines, our technicians are experienced in repairing both. PEX offers flexibility and corrosion resistance, while copper is known for its durability. We carry a full stock of matching fittings and tools for each material, ensuring a proper, code-compliant repair. Our experience extends to identifying common failure points for each type, such as fitting issues with PEX or pinhole leaks in older copper lines, frequent in homes with harder water.

    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Jupiter, FL

    Leaks often start at the compression fittings and couplings right next to the meter box. Shifting soil and heavy traffic over the box can loosen those joints over time. We determine whether the leak is on the utility side or the customer side, since the water provider is responsible for the meter and the pipe before it. When the leak is yours, we replace the fittings and the customer shutoff valve on the same trip.

    Main Water Line Leak Repair in Jupiter

    Most main line leaks show up as a soggy strip of yard, a sudden jump in your water bill or a meter dial that moves with every fixture off. We confirm the leak by isolating the house shutoff and watching the meter. Then we use ground microphones to find the exact spot. A single section is cut out and replaced with repair couplings or a new length of pipe, and the line is pressure tested before backfill.
