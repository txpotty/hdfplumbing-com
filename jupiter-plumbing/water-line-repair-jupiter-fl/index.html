    Commercial Water Line Repair in Jupiter, FL

    Commercial service lines are often 1 1/2 to 4 inch pipe feeding a meter bank, a backflow assembly and sometimes a fire line. A break shuts the business down. HDF Plumbing can schedule repairs early in the morning or after closing, and we bring the larger fittings and couplings on the truck. We also arrange the backflow test and inspection that most water providers require after work on the commercial side of the meter.

    Galvanized Pipe Water Line Repair in Jupiter, FL

    Galvanized steel service lines rust from the inside out. By the time one leaks, the rest of the pipe is close behind. We can clamp or replace a short section to get you through, which can hold for a while if the pipe is not too far gone. More often we recommend replacing the whole run from the meter to the house with copper or PEX. You get your flow back as a bonus.

    Main Water Line Repair in Jupiter

    Your main line is the pipe from the meter to the house, usually 3/4 inch or 1 inch, buried anywhere from a foot to several feet down depending on frost depth. A leak here shows up as a soggy strip of lawn and a meter dial that never rests. We start by closing the house shutoff and watching the dial. A moving dial with the house valve closed means an underground leak. Then a ground microphone narrows the spot, so the crew opens one small pit rather than trenching the whole run.

    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Jupiter, FL

    The meter box is where a lot of leaks start. Compression fittings there work loose over time. Before we touch anything, we figure out whether the leak is before or after the meter, because the water provider owns the meter and everything on the street side. If it is ours to fix, we fix it. That often means new fittings and a new customer shutoff valve on the same visit.

    Emergency Water Line Break Repair in Jupiter, FL

    A full break can flood the yard and leave the house dry at the same time. Shutting the water off comes first. The crew pumps the hole, finds the break and decides whether a coupling will do or whether a section needs replacing, setting up a temporary supply to the house if the job will take a while. Most breaks are fixed the same day. We photograph the damage for your insurance or HOA.

    Water Pressure Restoration in Jupiter

    Low pressure at every fixture points to the service line or the regulator, not the faucets. First step is a pressure gauge on the outside spigot. Then we check the pressure reducing valve, the house shutoff and the meter-side valve for a partly closed gate, and look at the pipe itself. Galvanized pipe can lose half its opening to rust scale. The fix might be a new valve or a new line.

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Jupiter, FL

    Galvanized service lines were common in houses built before the 1970s, and a lot of them are still in the ground. Around Jupiter, flushing the water heater yearly and descaling tankless units helps keep scale buildup under control. Rust and scale close the pipe from the inside. Rusty water at the first tap in the morning is a classic sign, along with pressure that drops when two fixtures run at once. Replacing the full run with copper or PEX clears all of it up. Usually a one-day job.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Jupiter

    High static pressure shortens the life of the service line and every fixture behind it. Most codes cap it at 80 psi. We gauge the pressure at a hose bib, and if it reads high we replace the pressure reducing valve with a Watts or Zurn Wilkins unit. Old gate valves that will not close get replaced with full-port ball valves. So the shutoff works when you need it.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Line Repair in Jupiter

    Spot repairs on an accessible line usually run $350 to $1,500. Depth drives most of that. A trenched meter-to-house replacement is typically $1,800 to $5,500, and trenchless replacement often lands between $3,000 and $7,000 because of the equipment involved. The written price comes before we start.

    Green grass in one strip is a big one. Soggy ground in dry weather is another. You may also notice pressure dropping at every fixture, a bill that climbs month after month, dirty water or the sound of water running behind a wall with nothing on. If you see two or more of these together, it is worth a meter test.

    Most replacements get Type K copper or PEX. For pulled lines we use HDPE. Copper lasts a very long time and resists punctures, and PEX flexes with the soil, tolerates freezing better and goes in faster. We recommend one based on your soil, line length, budget and what your local code allows in Jupiter.

    Type K copper, burial-rated PEX or HDPE poly. All three work well. Copper resists punctures and lasts decades, PEX tolerates ground movement and freezing, and HDPE is the standard choice when we pull a line trenchless. We recommend one after looking at your soil, line length and budget.

    Often the pipe itself is not covered. Damage it causes may be. Some homeowners carry separate service line coverage through their insurer or the water provider, and that coverage usually pays for the line repair. We provide photos and an itemized invoice for your claim.

    A full service line replacement usually needs a plumbing permit. Small repairs often do not. The rules come from your city or county, and they can differ even between neighboring towns in the same county. We pull the permit and schedule the inspection when it is required.

    A typical spot repair runs two to four hours, including the locate and backfill. Replacements take longer. Deep lines, long runs and jobs under concrete or pavers can push the job into a second or third day. You get a realistic timeline when we hand you the quote.
