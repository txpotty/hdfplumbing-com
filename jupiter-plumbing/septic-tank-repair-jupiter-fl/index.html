    Septic Tank Fixes We Handle in Jupiter, FL

    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Jupiter, FL

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones get eaten by sulfuric acid from sewer gas and eventually fall off into the tank. With no outlet baffle, sludge flows right out to the leach lines. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Commercial Septic Repair in Jupiter, FL

    Churches and small offices on septic run more water through the tank than any house, and a commercial kitchen adds grease on top of that. Repairs get sized to real daily flow instead of house numbers. Grease interceptors and lift stations are part of it. Downtime costs you, so we schedule around open hours.

    Repair Versus Replace in Jupiter, FL

    Most septic systems that fail can be repaired for a fraction of the cost of a new one. We price the repair and the replacement side by side, and we tell you which one we would pick for our own house. A new field is sometimes the right call. Often it is not.

    Drain Field Line Jetting in Jupiter

    Distribution laterals clog from the inside with solids and slime, especially after years without pumping. A jetter goes into each lateral from the D-box or an end cleanout, and the water that comes back tells us how bad it is. Roots get cut. If the soil itself has sealed, jetting will not fix it, and we say so.

    House-to-Tank Line Repair in Jupiter

    Gurgling drains right after a pump-out point to the line between the house and the tank, not the tank itself. Roots and settling ground cause most sags and breaks in that run. A camera from the house cleanout finds the bad joint, and we mark it in the yard. Then we dig one hole, not a trench. A second cleanout near the tank goes in while it is open.

    Septic Tee Repair in Jupiter

    Sanitary tees on the inlet and outlet crack or pop loose, sometimes from a careless pump-out hose. Without the outlet tee, the floating scum layer drifts into the laterals and plugs the gravel. We glue in a new Schedule 40 tee. An effluent filter goes on if the outlet has room.

    Emergency Septic Repair in Jupiter

    Sewage in the shower pan on a Sunday is an emergency, and we treat it like one. The first thing we ask is whether the tank has a riser we can reach, because digging for a buried lid adds an hour to the call. Stop using water until the tech arrives. Then we find the blockage, clear it and tell you what caused it.

    Septic Pump and Alarm Repair in Jupiter

    Pumped systems move effluent uphill or to a far field, and the pump sits in its own chamber with a float tree and an alarm. The alarm sounds because liquid climbed past the high float. Our tech meters the pump and tests each float before replacing anything. Zoeller and Liberty parts ride on the truck.
