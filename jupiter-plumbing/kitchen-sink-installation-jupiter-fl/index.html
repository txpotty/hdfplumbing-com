    Apron-Front Sinks in Jupiter, FL

    Apron sinks need a cabinet cut to fit the front panel, and the sink has to be in place before the counter is templated.     Drop-In Sink Replacement in Jupiter

    Removing an old drop-in begins by slicing through the old caulk and backing off the clips from below. Old clips are often rusted solid, so we bring a reciprocating saw and cut them off rather than prying on the counter. Laminate edges chip easily. Your new rim has to fully cover the old hole.

    Workstation Sink Installation in Jupiter, FL

    Workstation sinks have a ledge for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks, and many are deep single bowls with the drain off center. We plan the trap around that offset drain so the disposal fits and the cabinet keeps usable space on one side. Faucet reach matters too.
