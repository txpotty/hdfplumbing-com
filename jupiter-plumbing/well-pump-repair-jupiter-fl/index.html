    Well Pump Problems We Fix in Jupiter, FL

    Pressure Tank Problems in Jupiter

    Short cycling is the classic pressure tank symptom, with the pump clicking on and off every few seconds while a faucet runs. Usually the bladder has failed or the tank has lost its air charge. We check the precharge with the tank drained. It should sit about 2 psi below the cut-in pressure, so a 40/60 switch wants roughly 38 psi in the tank.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Jupiter

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Most switches fail from pitted contacts, a plugged sensing tube or insects in the housing. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank, typically 40 and 60 psi. One of the cheaper well repairs.

    Low Water Pressure Repair in Jupiter

    Weak pressure on a well is not always the pump. A clogged sediment filter, a pressure switch set too low or a leak in the yard line can all cause it. Sometimes the pump is fine and the well is simply producing less water than the house is asking for during peak use. We test each one.

    Emergency Well Pump Repair in Jupiter

    No water at all means a household on hold. HDF Plumbing handles emergency well pump calls in Jupiter at the top of the schedule. Technicians carry gauges, an amp clamp and the usual switches and capacitors, so many no-water calls are fixed on the first trip. A full pump swap takes longer.

    Constant Pressure System Repair in Jupiter, FL

    Variable speed drives like the Grundfos SQE and Franklin SubDrive keep pressure steady by changing motor speed. A fault code on the drive points us in the right direction. We troubleshoot the drive, the pressure sensor and the wiring, then repair or replace the failed part and verify the setpoint. These need a smaller tank.

    Well Pump Maintenance & Repair Cost in Jupiter, FL

    Maintenance costs far less than an emergency pull. A typical well pump repair in Jupiter runs $400 to $1,500, while a yearly checkup costs a fraction of that. HDF Plumbing prices both up front. The checkup covers tank precharge, switch contacts, amp draw and the well cap seal, which catches most problems before the pump fails in the middle of a hot week.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Jupiter, FL

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up through a suction line with an ejector. Most failures start with an air leak somewhere in the suction line. HDF Plumbing checks the foot valve, the ejector nozzle and every suction fitting, then reprimes the pump and confirms it builds pressure. A jet pump that runs nonstop without reaching pressure has almost always lost prime or sucked air through a cracked fitting.

    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Jupiter

    Submersible pumps hang inside the well casing on drop pipe, often 100 feet or more below the wellhead. The usual signs are no water at all, weak pressure or a hum with no flow. We test the motor from the surface first. If it has to come up, a pump hoist lifts the pipe safely, and we replace the burned motor, worn impellers or a bad check valve before setting it back.
