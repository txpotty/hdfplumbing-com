    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Sump Pump Installation in Jupiter

    Replacing a pump in an existing pit typically runs $500 to $1,200 including the pump and check valve. Installing a new pit through the slab generally falls between $1,500 and $3,500, and adding a battery backup adds about $600 to $1,500.

    Discharge should go to your yard, a dry well or an approved drainage area at least 10 feet from the foundation. Connecting a sump pump to the sanitary sewer is generally not allowed by local codes.

    We recommend having your sump pump inspected and maintained annually, ideally before the rainy season. Regular maintenance includes checking the float switch, cleaning debris out of the pit, testing the check valve and confirming the discharge line is clear. This helps prevent unexpected failures and lengthens the life of your pump.

    Swapping a pump in an existing pit usually takes two to three hours. A new pit with slab cutting and concrete patching typically takes most of a day.

    It is a good idea to test your sump pump every 2 to 3 months, especially before and after the rainy season. Simply pour a bucket of water into the sump pit to confirm the pump activates, drains the water and shuts off properly. Regular testing helps catch issues early.

    HDF Plumbing offers a comprehensive warranty on our basement sump pump installations, covering both parts and labor. The specific warranty period will be detailed in your service agreement. We stand behind our work and the quality of the products we install in your Jupiter home.

    A primary sump pump is your main defense against basement flooding, designed to handle everyday water removal. A secondary sump pump, often a battery-powered backup, serves as an emergency system that kicks on if the primary pump fails, loses power or is overwhelmed by excess water. Both work together for maximum protection.

    HDF Plumbing offers a warranty on our labor for basement sump pump installations, typically for one year. The sump pump itself comes with a manufacturer's warranty, which can range from 1 to 5 years depending on the brand and model. We will give you all warranty details at the time of installation.
