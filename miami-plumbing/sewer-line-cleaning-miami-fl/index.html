    Routine cleaning clears slow buildup. Grease, paper and scale. We enter through a cleanout and run a sectional cable with the right blade for the pipe size, following with hydro jetting when the buildup is heavy, and most older lines do well with cleaning every one to two years.

    Residential Sewer Line Cleaning in Miami

    Household laterals collect grease and hair. Both build up slowly. Over time, grease and hair coat the pipe and catch more debris, especially in older cast iron with a rough inner wall, and we clear it with a cable or a jetter sized for the pipe.

    Identifying Signs of Sewer Line Clogs in Miami, FL

    Several drains backing up at once is the main sign. Listen for gurgling. Sewer odors, slow drains in several rooms and water rising in the tub when the toilet flushes are all warning signs.
