    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Miami, FL

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Waste collects in the dip.     Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Miami

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. Crushed pipe cannot be lined. Older homes often need this on at least one section. The dig is staged so the house is without a sewer for as little time as possible.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Miami, FL

    Trenchless repair means we fix the pipe without opening a trench along its full length. Two access holes, sometimes one. Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE line through the old one and splits the old pipe outward, while cured-in-place lining pushes a resin-soaked felt tube into the old pipe and hardens it into a new wall. Both save driveways and mature trees. Not every line qualifies, and we will say so.
