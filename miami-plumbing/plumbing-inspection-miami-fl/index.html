    What a Plumbing Inspection Covers in Miami, FL

    Gas Connection Safety Check in Miami, FL

    During a full inspection, we look at the visible gas piping and the connectors to each appliance. We check for old uncoated brass connectors, missing shutoffs, missing sediment traps and corrosion, and we test joints with an electronic detector. Leaks get reported immediately. Most of our gas findings are in older homes, often at original appliance connectors.

    Commercial Plumbing Inspections in Miami, FL

    Commercial buildings have equipment homes do not, so our commercial inspections cover more ground. We look at backflow assemblies, grease interceptors, commercial water heaters, flush valves and the building sewer. Records matter for inspectors. The written report helps facility managers plan and budget repairs before a health inspector or the water provider writes them up.

    Water Heater and Softener Inspection in Miami

    We check the water heater's age by serial number, look for rust, leaks and sediment and test the relief valve. We confirm proper venting. Expansion tanks, drain pans and softeners get a look as well, and a quick hardness test tells us whether the softener is actually doing its job or just using salt.

    Water Heater Inspections in Miami

    Water heaters give clues long before they fail. We read the serial number for age, look for rust at the fittings and the base, check the venting and test the T and P relief valve. Tankless units get checked for scale. Gas heaters get a look at the flame, the drip leg and the vent draft, since a poorly venting heater is a safety issue.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspection in Miami, FL

    Older homes in Miami often have cast iron or clay sewer pipe that cracks, scales up or lets roots in at the joints. The camera records every defect along with its distance from the cleanout. We mark it on the ground. Knowing the depth and location means a repair estimate can be accurate, and it helps decide between a spot repair, a liner or a full replacement.

    Water Heater Safety Inspection in Miami

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. Our checks include the relief valve and its discharge line, the expansion tank and the drain pan. Gas units get a CO check. Backdrafting heaters can push combustion gases indoors, which is why we test the vent and report problems before we leave.

    Residential Plumbing Inspections in Miami

    A full home inspection covers the system from the water meter to the sewer connection. We check every visible pipe for corrosion and leaks, run each fixture for flow and drainage and test the water heater, the main shutoff and the angle stops. Pressure gets measured too. High pressure gets flagged.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Miami, FL

    Many older homes have clay, cast iron or Orangeburg sewer lines that can crack, sag or fill with roots. The camera starts at a cleanout. Every offset, belly, root intrusion and break shows up with its depth and location, and the recording is yours to keep.
