    Water Line Materials We Install in Miami, FL

    Our trucks carry Type K copper, PEX-a and HDPE. Each one fits a different job. Copper suits short runs and high pressure, PEX handles long runs with bends, and HDPE is the usual pick for pipe pulling and directional boring, where we need a tough, fused pipe that can be dragged a long way. All of it is listed for drinking water, carrying the NSF 61 listing.

    Copper Water Line Installation in Miami

    Type K copper is the thick-wall grade made for burial. Street pressure does not bother it. We bed it in sand, keep it away from sharp rock, and sleeve it where it passes through a foundation wall, because concrete and copper rubbing together for twenty years will wear through the pipe. PEX is cheaper.

    Commercial Water Line Services in Miami, FL

    Commercial services run bigger, often 1-1/2 to 2 inches, and almost always have a backflow assembly that needs testing after any work. Shutdowns get scheduled for when you are closed.     Trenchless Bores Under Driveways in Miami

    Directional boring drills a path under the obstacle and pulls the pipe back through it. It does not need the old pipe as a guide. We use it when the new line has to take a different route, say around a new addition or under a driveway poured after the house was built, leaving just two small pits in the yard. Heavy rock is the exception.

    Main Water Line Repair vs. Replacement in Miami

    One leak on a twenty-year-old copper line? A repair coupling is often the right call. Three leaks on a galvanized line from the 1950s is different, because the pipe is thin everywhere and the next leak is already forming, so the full line costs less than a string of repairs. We tell you which camp you are in and show you the pipe we cut out.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Miami, FL

    Pipe pulling works like this. We dig a pit at each end of the old line. A cable or bursting head goes through the old pipe and drags new HDPE or PEX back through the same path, breaking up the old line if it has gone brittle, so a driveway, a row of shrubs or a concrete walk can stay where it is.

    Traditional Water Line Excavation in Miami
