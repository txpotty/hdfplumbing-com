    A frequent problem with gas water heaters is a pilot light that won't stay lit or a burner that fails to ignite. Our technicians in Miami are skilled at diagnosing these components and performing the necessary repairs, whether it is cleaning, adjusting or replacing parts.     Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Miami

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust out through PVC pipe. Failures often involve the blower motor, pressure switch, hot surface igniter or condensate in the vent. We read the error codes, test each part and repair the failure. Correct vent slope and termination are checked before we leave.

    Strange noises coming from your gas water heater, such as rumbling, popping or whistling, often indicate sediment buildup in the tank or a burner problem.
