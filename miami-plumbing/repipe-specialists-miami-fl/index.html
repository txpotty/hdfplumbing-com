    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Miami

    Slab leaks are a costly and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Nearly all homes in Florida sit on concrete slabs over sandy soil, so leaks under the slab are a frequent and costly repair. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the damaged pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use modern detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Miami

    Kitec, with its orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe and brass fittings, and gray polybutylene pipe are both known for failures. Many homes built in the 1980s through early 2000s contain one or the other. We replace these systems completely so your home is easier to insure and sell later. Visible pipe at the water heater often tells us what you have.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Miami

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. We can reroute a single hot or cold branch overhead and abandon the leaking pipe in the slab. This is a good option for newer homes or a first-time leak. If the home has had multiple leaks, we will explain why a full repipe may save you money.

    While fixing individual leaks might seem cheaper in the short term, it's often a temporary solution for an aging plumbing system. If you're experiencing multiple leaks, low pressure, or discolored water, it indicates widespread pipe wear. Repiping addresses the root cause by replacing the entire system, providing a long-term, reliable solution and preventing future problems.

    Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving stubborn scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. Hard water can lead to mineral buildup, or scale, inside pipes over time. This buildup constricts water flow, reduces pressure, and can speed up corrosion, especially in older metal pipes. Repiping with materials like PEX or properly installed copper, along with considering a water softener, can mitigate these effects and extend the life of your plumbing system.
