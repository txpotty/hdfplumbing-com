    Upgrading from Tank to Tankless in Miami, FL

    Making the switch from a traditional tank water heater to a tankless system involves more than simply swapping units. It often requires changes to gas lines, electrical wiring and venting. HDF Plumbing manages all aspects of this conversion, making sure all upgrades meet current codes and your new tankless unit performs at its best, giving you a modern hot water solution in Miami, FL.

    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Miami, FL

    Calcium scale is the leading cause of tankless heater failure. Around Miami, flushing the water heater yearly and descaling tankless units helps keep mineral buildup under control. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides added protection.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Miami, FL
