    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Miami

    An air switch puts a push button on the countertop or sink deck instead of a wall switch. Pressing it sends air down a tube. That pulse trips a switch in a control box plugged in under the sink, so no live wiring sits anywhere near the water, which is the main reason people like them. We drill the counter or use the spare faucet hole.

    Standard InSinkErator Installation in Miami

    A standard install replaces the unit under an existing sink. We take the old disposal down, scrape the old putty off the flange and set a new flange with fresh putty. Then the mount ring goes on. We hang the unit, turn the lower ring until it locks and connect the discharge tube to the trap with a new slip-joint washer so nothing weeps. We connect power through a cord kit or the old hardwire and confirm the ground first.

    New Construction Disposal Installs in Miami

    New builds and gut remodels get the disposal in two trips. During rough-in, the drain stub height gets set and the electrician leaves a switched outlet in the sink cabinet. The disposal itself waits. Once the countertop and sink are in and the faucet is set, we come back to hang the unit, connect the dishwasher and test everything with the water on.

    Badger Replacement for Miami Homes

    Builder-grade kitchens almost always started with a Badger, and a worn one usually gets replaced with another. We pull the old unit first. If the upper mount is the same quick-lock design and is not pitted, we reuse it, which saves time and keeps the seal surfaces that already match your sink. Then the new Badger hangs, locks and gets tested.

    Upfront Disposal Installation Pricing in Miami, FL

    Price depends mostly on two things: the unit you pick and how much needs to change under the sink. A like-for-like swap is the low end. A first-time install that needs a new flange and a new outlet costs more, because that is real labor and sometimes an electrician's visit too. You get the number before we start.

    Evolution Series Installation in Miami

    Evolution units grind in two or three stages and carry extra sound insulation around the motor housing, which is why they sit taller and weigh more than a Badger. Clearance under the bowl matters here. Before install day we measure the drop from sink to trap arm so the unit fits. The Compact fits tight cabinets, and the Excel needs the most room.

    Dishwasher Connection and Air Gap Setup in Miami

    Nearly every InSinkErator has a dishwasher inlet with a factory knockout plug inside. We drive the plug out and fish it out of the chamber. Then the dishwasher hose goes on with a proper clamp, routed through an air gap or up into a high loop under the counter so sink water cannot siphon back into the dishwasher tub. Both appliances get run together.

    Batch Feed vs Continuous Feed in Miami

    Continuous feed is the type most kitchens have. Flip the switch and it runs until you flip it off. A batch feed unit needs its stopper locked in to run, keeping hands out of the chamber, and it needs no wall switch. We wire each one to match.
