    Digital Shower Systems in Miami, FL

    Digital shower systems like U by Moen and Kohler DTV need a little more planning. The digital valve mounts in an accessible spot, often in a vanity or a closet, and needs low-voltage wiring to the controller plus a dedicated outlet near the valve.     Shower Upgrades Without a Full Remodel in Miami

    Not every upgrade needs demolition. Replacing an old pan, converting a tub to a shower or swapping in a new valve and trim can make a big difference without tearing the bathroom down to the studs. You see each option's price. They tend to wrap up within a week.

    Custom Shower Enclosure Plumbing in Miami

    Body sprays and steam units add lines and planning. Every body spray needs a supply line, and the sizing has to be right so the farthest outlet still has good pressure. Pressure varies by house. We test static pressure before designing the layout.

    Shower Valve and Fixture Installation in Miami, FL

    Pressure-balancing valves are the baseline for any new shower. Thermostatic valves hold a set temperature and add volume control, which works well with rain heads and handhelds. Either one protects against scalding. The outlet count usually decides it.

    Custom Shower System Installation in Miami, FL

    Multi-head showers depend on valve capacity. One head works fine off a standard valve, while multiple outlets need a thermostatic valve, volume controls and larger 3/4-inch supply lines. Layout comes first. The water heater gets checked for capacity too.

    Shower Pan Installation in Miami

    Shower pans fail quietly. A pan with a pinhole in the liner or a poorly clamped drain will hold water for years while it slowly soaks the subfloor, so we flood-test every tiled pan for 24 hours before tile goes down. Prefab pans get a level check. Both kinds get slope toward the drain.

    Shower Stall Installation in Miami

    A one-piece stall needs a level, fully supported base. We set fiberglass and acrylic bases in a bed of mortar or setting compound so the floor of the unit does not flex underfoot, since flex is what cracks a base within a couple of years. Then the drain goes in. We check the flow at the head once the trim is on.

    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Miami, FL

    Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common jobs. We pull the tub, move the drain from the end of the tub to where the shower pan needs it and raise the valve to shower height, usually around 48 inches off the floor. On a slab, we cut concrete. The new shower is safer to step into.
