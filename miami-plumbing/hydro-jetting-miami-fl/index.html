    High-Pressure Water Jetting for Drain Cleaning in Miami

    High-pressure water jetting, or hydro jetting, is a non-invasive method that uses highly pressurized water to clear and clean pipes. Our equipment in Miami can generate water pressures up to 4,000 PSI, powerful enough to break apart stubborn blockages and scour pipe walls clean without chemicals.     Hydro Jetting Before Pipe Lining in Miami, FL

    Cured-in-place pipe lining needs a clean surface to adhere and seal correctly. We jet the host pipe to remove roots, scale and debris, then camera it to confirm it is ready. Any heavy obstructions are cut out before the liner is installed. Skipping this step can lead to wrinkles or poor bonding. Proper prep is part of why lined pipe can last many years.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Miami

    The cost of hydro jetting in Miami, FL can range from $300 to $800, depending on the severity of the clog, the length and diameter of the pipe, and whether camera inspection is required. More complex jobs, like removing extensive tree roots or cleaning long commercial lines, may be on the higher end. We provide a clear estimate after assessing your situation.

    Hydro jetting is very effective against a wide range of stubborn clogs that traditional snaking cannot fully clear. This includes heavy grease buildup, mineral scale, sludge, soap scum, hair clogs, and even invasive tree roots that have gotten into your sewer lines. It is designed to scour the entire pipe surface.

    Drain snaking uses a cable with an auger that punches a hole through a blockage to restore some flow. Hydro jetting, however, uses high-pressure water to completely scour the inside of the pipe, removing all buildup and restoring the pipe to a near-new condition. Hydro jetting offers a more thorough and longer-lasting clean.

    While hydro jetting fully removes existing tree roots from your sewer line, it does not prevent new roots from growing back. For a long-term solution to recurring root intrusion, we might recommend trenchless sewer repair or pipe replacement, which we can discuss during our assessment.

    The length of a hydro jetting service in Miami varies depending on the severity and location of the clog. A typical residential drain might take 1 to 2 hours, while a major sewer line or commercial job could take 3 to 4 hours or more. Our technicians work quickly to minimize disruption.

    Snaking uses a rotating cable to break through a clog. Jetting uses pressurized water to clean the entire pipe wall and wash debris away.

    For most modern and well-maintained pipes, hydro jetting is very safe. However, for very old, corroded, or severely damaged pipes, the high pressure could cause further damage. That is why HDF Plumbing always performs a camera inspection first to check your pipe's condition and confirm hydro jetting is appropriate.

    We use professional cart and trailer jetters along with rotating and root-cutting nozzles from well-known manufacturers. Color camera systems record the line before and after.
