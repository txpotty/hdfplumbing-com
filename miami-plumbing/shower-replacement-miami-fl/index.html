    Prefab Shower Replacement in Miami

    Shower Unit Replacement in Miami

    Cracked fiberglass units leak at the base first. Cracks tend to start where the base was not supported, and each shower flexes it until water slips through to the subfloor. Out comes the old one. Then we check the subfloor and set the new base on a full mortar bed.

    Old Shower Valve Replacement in Miami

    Two- and three-handle valves from decades back often leak and lack scald protection. Working from behind the wall when possible, we pull the old valve, install a new one and use a large trim plate to hide the old holes. Tile often stays put. Safety improves a lot.

    Custom Tile Shower Replacement in Miami, FL

    Custom tile replacement starts with careful demolition. We take the old tile and pan down to the studs and subfloor, replace any damaged wood and install a new waterproofed pan and walls, then set the valve and drain to the finished tile depth. The slope has to be right. A flood test comes before tile.

    Accessible Shower Replacement in Miami, FL

    Barrier-free showers need low or flat entries and controls within reach. We install the valve where someone seated can reach it, add blocking for grab bars and set a handheld on a slide bar, all following ADA guidelines when you ask for them. We keep the entry wide. We plan for a seat.

    Walk-In Shower Conversion in Miami, FL

    Walk-in conversions are one of the most requested bathroom jobs we do. The drain moves to the pan's center, the valve goes up to shower height and the slope keeps water from escaping the entry. Grab bar blocking is easy now. ADA layouts are available.
