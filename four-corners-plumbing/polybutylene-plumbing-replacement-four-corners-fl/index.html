    A full replacement takes every poly supply line out of service, not just the sections that have failed. Leaving some poly keeps the risk.     A whole-house poly replacement in Four Corners is a repipe, and it usually needs a plumbing permit. The permit is on us. Rough-in inspection happens with walls open, so the inspector can see supports, nail plates, valves and the test gauge. A permit on file also helps with insurers and buyers.

    Manufactured Home Poly Replacement in Four Corners, FL

    Manufactured homes from the poly era often have polybutylene lines in the floor or the belly underneath. We replace those lines with PEX sized for the home and secure them properly to the frame.     PEX Pipe Installation in Four Corners, FL

Attic PEX stays out of UV, clear of flues and can lights and supported as the maker specifies, which protects the pipe and the warranty.
