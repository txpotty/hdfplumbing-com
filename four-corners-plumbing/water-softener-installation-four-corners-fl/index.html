    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Four Corners

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. Instead of removing hardness, they convert it into a form that resists sticking, which reduces scale without salt or brine waste. Maintenance is light. They suit moderate hardness.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Four Corners, FL

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. HDF Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank.
