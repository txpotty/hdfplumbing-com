    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Four Corners

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. A fully collapsed section is one example. When the camera shows a collapse, a large offset or a belly deep enough to hold several inches of water, we open a trench, remove the old pipe and lay new Schedule 40 PVC on proper bedding at a quarter inch of fall per foot. Older homes often need this on at least one section. We stage the work so you lose sewer service for a few hours, not days.

    Restaurant and Retail Sewer Repair in Four Corners

    Restaurants, retail centers and multifamily buildings load a lateral far beyond a single house. Grease is the usual culprit. We jet the line, camera it, and repair the weak section with options that limit how long you are closed, including after-hours digs and bypass pumping when the business has to stay open. We also install two-way cleanouts for future maintenance.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Four Corners

    Clay and cast iron crack from settling soil, tree roots and vehicles parked over the line. Dirt falls in through the crack. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded stainless couplings rated for the transition between materials, then test the repair with water and video before closing the hole. Figure on a single day for a typical spot repair.

    Commercial Sewer Repair in Four Corners

    Multifamily buildings and offices put several units' worth of flow through one lateral. Small defects turn into big backups.     After-Hours Sewer Repair in Four Corners

    Main lines like to fail on holidays. We still answer.     Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Four Corners

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. A cable only punches a hole. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and we always jet a line before lining it. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We check the pipe on video before we jet it.
