    Basement Wall Sealing in Four Corners, FL

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Humidity causes condensation on cold lines and clogged AC condensate drains, a common source of hidden water damage. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then seal the face with a masonry waterproofer that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. The musty smell usually fades within weeks. Those weep holes empty into the interior drain if the house has one.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Four Corners

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and usually add a dehumidifier to hold humidity under 60 percent. Humidity causes condensation on cold lines and clogged AC condensate drains, a frequent source of hidden water damage. The joists stop rotting. Drier air under the floor also means less musty air upstairs.

    Foundation Crack Repair in Four Corners

    Crack injection takes about two hours per crack. We set injection ports every 8 to 12 inches along the crack, seal the surface with epoxy paste, and pump in resin from the bottom port up until it pushes out the next port. Polyurethane flexes a bit if the wall shifts, and epoxy bonds the two sides back together. The surface paste gets ground off once it cures.

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries.     Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Four Corners
