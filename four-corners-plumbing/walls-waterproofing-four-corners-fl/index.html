    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Four Corners

    Crawl space encapsulation involves sealing the crawl space entirely with heavy-duty vapor barriers and often includes a dehumidifier. This not only waterproofs the walls but also controls humidity, stopping mold growth and wood rot in the crawl space. Constant heat and humidity are the main plumbing stress in Florida, keeping water heaters, AC drain lines, and outdoor pipes working year round. An encapsulated crawl space can noticeably improve indoor air quality and energy efficiency by keeping moisture from moving up into the home.     Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Four Corners

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Four Corners, FL

    Block and stem walls often show efflorescence, which is the white mineral crust left when water passes through masonry. Irrigation and sprinklers aimed at the house are common culprits. We adjust or reroute irrigation, clean the wall and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating. Proper grading and drip line placement help keep the wall dry.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Four Corners

    Exterior foundation waterproofing involves excavating around the foundation to apply a waterproof membrane or coating directly to the wall. This is often the most reliable method for preventing water from entering the basement or crawl space. We use high-quality asphalt-modified polyurethane membranes or polymer-based coatings that resist hydrostatic pressure and soil movement. Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and crack buried water and sewer lines. Proper drainage solutions, such as French drains, are also integrated to divert water away.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Four Corners

It's an affordable way to control humidity and prevent wood rot.
