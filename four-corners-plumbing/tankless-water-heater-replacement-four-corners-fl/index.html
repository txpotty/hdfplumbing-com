    Picking a Tankless for Your Four Corners, FL House

    Tankless-to-Tankless Replacement in Four Corners

    Replacing an existing tankless with a new one is usually the simpler job, since the gas line, vent path and water connections are already there. We confirm they are sized right for the new unit. Old service valves often get replaced too.

    Whole-House Electric Tankless Replacement in Four Corners

    Whole-house electric tankless performance drops when the incoming water is cold, since the unit has to raise the temperature further at the same amperage. Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart are the common brands. A heat pump water heater is sometimes the better fit, and we will tell you.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Four Corners

    A gas tankless unit at full fire can pull 199,000 BTU, roughly five times what a 40 gallon tank burner uses. Most need at least a 3/4-inch line. We check meter capacity and pipe length against the manufacturer's sizing chart, then confirm pressure with the unit running. An undersized line causes ignition faults.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Four Corners, FL

    Electric tankless water heaters skip the gas line entirely, but a whole-house model can need 120 to 160 amps on two to four double-pole breakers. Plenty of panels do not have room. We check panel capacity and wire gauge before quoting.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Four Corners, FL

    Commercial tankless setups in restaurants and offices often use two or more units on a manifold, with a controller that splits the load. One bad unit can come out while the others keep running. Hand sinks and dish stations have to hit the temperatures health code requires.

    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Four Corners

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE or Rinnai Sensei pass 90 percent efficiency by pulling extra heat out of the exhaust. The vent is plain PVC. The condensate is acidic, so we install a neutralizer cartridge and run the line to an approved drain. Humidity causes condensation on cold lines and clogged AC condensate drains, a frequent source of hidden water damage. The savings carry through every season because hot water use does.

    Emergency Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Four Corners

    No hot water from a tankless usually shows up as an error code on the controller, and the code tells us half of what we need to know. A photo of the error code helps a lot. A leaking heat exchanger means replacement, usually done same day or next day.
