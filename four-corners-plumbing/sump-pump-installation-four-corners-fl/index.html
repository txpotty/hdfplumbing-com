    New Sump Pit Installation in Four Corners

    No pit yet? We cut one at the low corner. Washed gravel goes around the basin so water can reach it. Then we patch the slab. The pump, lid and discharge go in last.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Four Corners

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. Sizing comes from two measurements we take on site, the pit diameter and the vertical lift to the discharge. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. We usually pair that with a 1-1/2 inch discharge line.

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Four Corners, FL

    Two pumps on one riser cover both kinds of failure, a dead motor and a dead outlet. The primary runs on house power and handles the everyday inflow, while the battery unit waits above it on a separate float. People with carpet downstairs buy these. We install them in homes where a flood would ruin flooring and drywall.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Four Corners

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. Because everything comes assembled on a single riser, they fit into an 18-inch pit that would be too tight for two separate pumps and two check valves. That saves some labor. We set them in homes that want two layers without a bigger pit.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Four Corners

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi models send a text too. Rentals need these. They can go on a new pump or one that is already working.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Four Corners, FL

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get. The M53 and the M98 are the two we set most, depending on lift and inflow, and both come with mechanical float switches that hold up for years. They make matching backup units. We help you pick the model that fits the pit.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Four Corners

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. They run quieter and move more water. The pit lid sits flush. We seal the cord grommet and the lid on every install.
