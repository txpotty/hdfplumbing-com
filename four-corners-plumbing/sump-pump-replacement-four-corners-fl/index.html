    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Four Corners

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves without binding. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats suit larger pits.

    Wayne Sump Pump Replacement in Four Corners

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Replacement in Four Corners

    The cost of sump pump replacement in Four Corners, FL typically ranges from $500 to $1,500, depending on the type of pump, submersible or pedestal, its horsepower and any necessary pit changes. Battery backup systems will add to this cost, usually between $300 and $800. We provide a written, upfront quote after assessing your needs.

    Sump pumps usually have a lifespan of 7 to 10 years. Even if they appear to be working, their efficiency can decrease over time. Regular inspection and testing are crucial, and replacement is advisable as they approach the end of their expected service life.

    A primary sump pump is your main defense against basement flooding, powered by your home's electricity. A backup sump pump, usually battery-powered or water-powered, is a secondary system that kicks on if the primary pump fails, loses power or cannot keep up with heavy water inflow. Many Four Corners homes are better off with a backup system due to storm risks.

    Manufacturer warranties usually range from 3 to 5 years. We also offer a labor warranty.

    Most sump pumps last 7 to 10 years. Pumps that sit idle for long periods can fail earlier due to stuck switches.

    It is recommended to test your sump pump at least every three months, and especially before and after rainy seasons. To test, simply pour a bucket of water into the sump pit. The pump should activate, drain the water and then shut off automatically. If it does not, it is time for a professional inspection.

    We install Zoeller, Liberty and Wayne pumps. Cast iron models are preferred for durability.

    Most sump pump replacements take one to two hours. Battery backups or new discharge lines take longer.
