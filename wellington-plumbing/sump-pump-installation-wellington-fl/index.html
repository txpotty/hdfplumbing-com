    Sump Pump Replacement in Wellington, FL

    Sump pumps often sit idle for long stretches between storms, which can lead to stuck floats and seized motors.     New Sump Pit Installation in Wellington

    If your basement or crawl space has no pit, we can create one. We cut the slab, excavate through soil, hardpan or rock and set a durable basin. We add gravel around the basin to help water flow in. Then we patch the concrete and install the pump and cover. A new pit gives you a reliable place to collect and remove water.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Wellington

    Replacing a pump in an existing pit usually costs $600 to $1,500. Installing a new pit with slab cutting and excavation often runs $1,500 to $4,000. Battery backups usually add $500 to $1,500.

    Most sump pumps last 7 to 10 years. Pumps that sit idle for long periods may fail sooner, so periodic testing is important.

    A submersible sump pump sits inside the sump pit, making it quieter and more powerful, ideal for heavy use. A pedestal sump pump has its motor above the pit, making it easier to reach for maintenance and often more affordable, though it can be louder.

    Most standard sump pump installations can be completed within 3 to 5 hours. However, if a new sump pit needs to be excavated or if there are complex plumbing or electrical adjustments, the process might take a full day. We always aim for efficiency without compromising quality.

    A well-maintained sump pump usually lasts between 7 and 10 years. The lifespan can vary depending on the quality of the pump, how often it runs and how well it is maintained. Regular inspections and cleaning can help extend its life.

    Water should discharge away from the foundation to a safe area on your property. Many cities do not allow sump discharge into the sanitary sewer.

    A submersible sump pump sits entirely inside the sump pit, submerged in water, making it quieter and taking up less space. A pedestal sump pump has its motor mounted on a shaft above the pit, making it easier to access for maintenance and usually more affordable. Both types are effective, and our plumbers can help you choose the best fit for your Wellington basement.

    While DIY sump pump installation is possible, it is not recommended. Proper installation involves critical steps like correct pit excavation, discharge line routing, electrical wiring and code compliance.
