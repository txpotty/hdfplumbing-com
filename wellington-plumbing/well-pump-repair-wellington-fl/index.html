    Well Pump Pressure Tank Issues in Wellington

    The pressure tank works together with your well pump to maintain consistent water pressure and reduce pump cycling. A waterlogged tank, a ruptured diaphragm, or a faulty air valve can cause rapid pump cycling, fluctuating water pressure, or even no water at all. Our team accurately diagnoses pressure tank problems and performs necessary repairs or replacements. We ensure your tank is properly sized and charged for optimal system performance in Wellington.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Wellington

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are common causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most affordable well repairs.
