    Slab leaks, where water lines running beneath your home's concrete foundation leak, are a particularly challenging issue. These leaks can undermine your foundation, leading to major structural damage. We use specialized listening devices and camera inspections to pinpoint slab leaks precisely and offer repair options including direct slab penetration, re-routing, or pipe lining. Addressing a slab leak quickly in Wellington is crucial to prevent extensive, costly foundation damage.

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Wellington, FL

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving heavy scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which restricts flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe rarely lasts because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and ends repeat leaks.
