    Tankless vs Tank Cost Comparison in Wellington, FL

    Tankless installation costs more upfront than a standard tank because of the unit price, gas line upgrades and venting changes. Over time, lower standby energy losses and a longer expected lifespan offset part of that cost. Tankless units often last 15 to 20 years with proper descaling, compared with about 8 to 12 years for many tanks in hard water areas. We give you a side-by-side quote so you can decide what suits your budget.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Wellington

    Converting from a tank requires more than swapping units. A 40 gallon tank heater uses roughly 40,000 BTU, while a whole-home tankless unit may need up to 199,000 BTU, so the gas line usually needs to be upsized. Venting changes from a metal flue to PVC or stainless, and the unit moves to a wall location. We remove and dispose of your old tank and patch the old flue penetration.

    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Wellington, FL

    Calcium scale is the most common cause of tankless heater failure. Around Wellington, flushing the water heater yearly and descaling tankless units helps keep scale buildup under control. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides extra protection.
