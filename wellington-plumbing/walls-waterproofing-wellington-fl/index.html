    Frequently Asked Questions About Walls Waterproofing in Wellington

    Shower wall waterproofing with a membrane system usually runs $1,200 to $3,500 including demolition and backer board, not counting new tile. Exterior block wall sealing often costs $800 to $3,000 depending on size. Below-grade foundation waterproofing usually ranges from $2,500 to $8,000.

    Waterproofing prevents the moisture mold needs to grow. Existing mold should be removed and the area dried before sealing. We remove affected materials when needed and recommend remediation for larger growth.

    Interior waterproofing addresses water after it has entered the foundation, usually managing it with sealants and drainage systems inside the basement or crawl space. Exterior waterproofing prevents water from reaching the foundation walls in the first place, usually by excavating and applying membranes or coatings to the outside of the foundation.

    For extensive walls waterproofing projects involving excavation, structural changes, or major changes to drainage systems, a permit from the city of Wellington may be required. Simple crack repairs typically do not need a permit. HDF Plumbing will help you determine if a permit is necessary for your project and handle the application process if needed.

    HDF Plumbing works with trusted manufacturers known for high-quality waterproofing products. We commonly use products from brands like Xypex, Tremco, and Sika for their proven effectiveness and durability, from crystalline admixtures to elastomeric membranes and sealants. We select the best product for your foundation type and conditions.

    While minor DIY fixes like patching small cracks are possible, complete walls waterproofing is best left to professionals. It involves specialized knowledge of drainage, foundation mechanics, and proper material application. Improper DIY work can lead to ineffective results, wasted money, and worse water damage over time.

    When professionally installed using high-quality materials, walls waterproofing can last for many decades, often 20 to 50 years or even longer. The lifespan depends on the materials used, the quality of installation, and environmental factors like soil movement and water exposure. Modern membranes and sealants are designed for long-term durability.

    Walls waterproofing is a primary step in preventing future mold growth by eliminating the moisture source. However, it does not remove existing mold. If mold is already present, it will need to be professionally remediated before or along with the waterproofing process. Once the area is dry and mold-free, waterproofing will help make sure it stays that way.
