    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Wellington

    Atmospheric vent heaters rely on hot exhaust rising through a metal flue, with no fan. They are common in older homes and usually the least expensive gas option, but they only work if the vent has the right size, slope and clearance to combustibles. Old vents get checked. Corroded or undersized pipe gets replaced.

    Tankless Gas Water Heater Conversion in Wellington

    Converting from a tank to tankless is usually more than a swap. Most conversions need a bigger gas line from the meter, a new vent through the wall or roof, a condensate drain and isolation valves for future descaling. It takes most of a day. We do all of it to code.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Installation in Wellington, FL

    High-efficiency gas heaters use condensing technology to pull extra heat out of the exhaust. Because the exhaust comes out cool and wet, they vent through PVC and need a condensate drain, which changes where the heater can go and what the install involves. Energy Star models qualify for some rebates. We install Rheem, Bradford White and AO Smith models.

    Brands We Install in Wellington

    Rheem, Bradford White, AO Smith, Navien and Rinnai make up most of the gas heaters we install in Wellington homes and businesses. We compare warranties, recovery and venting across brands so the choice is easy. Budget matters too. We install all of them.

    Commercial Gas Water Heater Installation in Wellington

    Restaurants and laundromats can drain a residential tank in minutes. For businesses we install high-input heaters with bigger gas lines and dedicated vents, sometimes in banks of two or three. Some uses need 140 degrees. We work around your hours.

    Propane Water Heater Installation in Wellington

    Propane homes need heaters set up for propane, not natural gas. Different pressure and orifice size mean either a propane model or a factory conversion kit. We work with your supplier. We test all fittings.
