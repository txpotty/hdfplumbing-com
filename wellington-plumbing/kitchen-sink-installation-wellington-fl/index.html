    How We Install Kitchen Sinks in Wellington, FL: Style by Style

    Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in Wellington

    Sitting on the counter by their rim, drop-in sinks are a natural fit for laminate, butcher block and tile. It is often the quickest swap. We check the cutout size, seal the rim and tighten the clips evenly, and in most rentals and older homes the job is done in a couple of hours.

    Granite and Quartz Composite Sinks in Wellington

    Composite sinks are heavier than stainless and usually thicker at the drain, so standard strainers sometimes need a longer threaded body. We check the strainer fit first. If the included strainer is too short for the sink wall, we swap in one that fits rather than overtightening and risking a chip around the opening.

    Workstation Sink Installation in Wellington, FL

    Workstation sinks have a ledge for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks, and most are single deep bowls with an offset drain. We plan the trap around that offset drain so the disposal fits and the cabinet keeps usable space on one side. Faucet reach matters too.

    Farmhouse Sink Installation in Wellington, FL

    A fireclay apron sink sits on a frame inside the cabinet, not on the countertop. We build that frame from 2x4s or a steel bracket, set the sink dead level and leave the right reveal for the stone shop. We plan the drain early. A lot of them drain toward the back, so the trap layout has to match.

    Disposal and Dishwasher Hookup with Sink Installation in Wellington, FL

    Most new kitchen sinks get paired with a disposal and a dishwasher. We mount the disposal, run the dishwasher drain through an air gap or high loop and install a dedicated hot supply with its own shutoff. One visit costs less than three. Every connection is tested under real use.

    Restaurant Sink Installation in Wellington

    Restaurants, cafes and commissary kitchens in Wellington get their commercial sinks installed by HDF Plumbing. Hand sinks need tempered or hot and cold water. Three-compartment, prep and hand sinks go in with the floor sinks, indirect drains and grease trap tie-ins the inspector checks.

    Stainless Steel Sink Installation in Wellington

    Stainless sinks come in gauges from 16 to 22, and a lower gauge means thicker steel. Thin 22 gauge bowls flex and drum when the disposal runs, so we add sound pads or suggest an 18 gauge or heavier sink if you have not bought one yet. Top-mount and undermount plumb differently. We connect the strainers, faucet and disposal either way.

    Sink and Disposal Installed Together in Wellington, FL

    Adding a disposal costs least when the sink is already out. We mount the disposal on the new flange, set the trap height for its outlet, and knock out the dishwasher plug so the hose has somewhere to go. Power is a separate question. We check for a switched outlet in the cabinet.
