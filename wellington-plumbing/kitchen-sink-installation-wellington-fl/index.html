    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Wellington

    Undermount sinks need strong clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. We mount the bowl, seal the rim with silicone, and support it while the adhesive sets. Then we install strainers, traps and supply lines. A proper undermount install prevents gaps and leaks along the counter edge.

    Emergency Sink Replacement in Wellington

    A sudden sink failure, such as a severe crack or burst pipe, can bring your kitchen to a halt.     Disposal and Dishwasher Hookup with Sink Installation in Wellington, FL

    Most new kitchen sinks are paired with a disposal and dishwasher. We mount the disposal, connect the dishwasher drain through an air gap or high loop, and install the dishwasher supply. Doing it all at once saves time and money. We test every connection under real use.

    Stainless Steel Sink Installation in Wellington

    Stainless steel sinks are a popular choice for their durability and ease of cleaning, especially in busy kitchens. We are experienced at installing all gauges and styles of stainless steel sinks, ensuring proper support and watertight seals for years of use. Our team handles both top-mount and under-mount configurations, making sure your new sink integrates seamlessly with your countertops and cabinetry. We also connect all related plumbing, including faucets and garbage disposals.

    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Wellington

    Double bowl sinks need a continuous waste or end outlet layout to drain both sides through one trap. We plan the layout around your disposal and dishwasher connections. Proper slope keeps both sides draining freely. We use heavy-wall fittings that resist leaks.

    Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in Wellington

    Drop-in sinks rest on the countertop with a rim, making them a good choice for laminate and tile. We check the cutout size, seal the rim, and tighten mounting clips evenly. This is often the fastest and most affordable kitchen sink upgrade. It also works well in rentals and older homes.

    Farmhouse Sink Installation in Wellington, FL

    Farmhouse or apron-front sinks add a distinctive look to any kitchen, but their installation requires specific expertise due to their weight and unique mounting requirements. HDF Plumbing provides precise farmhouse sink installation, ensuring the sink is adequately supported within your cabinetry and properly aligned with your countertop. We carefully manage the plumbing connections, which can differ from standard sinks, to prevent leaks and keep efficient drainage. This focused service gives you a beautiful and functional focal point for your kitchen.
