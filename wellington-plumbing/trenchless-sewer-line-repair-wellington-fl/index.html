    Trenchless vs Traditional Sewer Repair Cost in Wellington, FL

    Point repairs often cost $1,500 to $3,500, while full lining of a residential lateral commonly runs $4,000 to $12,000. Pipe bursting typically ranges from $5,000 to $15,000.     Pipe Bursting Sewer Upgrades in Wellington

    Bursting can upsize the line. A 4-inch clay lateral can be replaced with 6-inch HDPE, which helps on lines that clog because they were undersized from the start or have been added onto with extra bathrooms. Each end only needs a pit around three by four feet. No trench in between.

    Root Intrusion Fixes in Wellington, FL

    Roots enter at the joints, where clay bells or cast iron hubs have a small gap or a failed gasket. Once inside, they branch into a mat that fills the pipe. We cut them out with a mechanical root cutter or a hydro jet, then line the pipe so those joints are sealed for good and the line stops needing a cleaning every few months. With no joints left, there is nowhere for them to return.

    Commercial Trenchless Sewer Repair in Wellington, FL

    Office and retail buildings often have a cast iron building drain under the slab that has rotted along the bottom. Lining it from a cleanout avoids saw-cutting the floor inside an occupied tenant space. We coordinate around tenant hours. A typical commercial lining job runs one to three days, depending on footage and how many branch connections need to be reinstated with a cutter.

    Trenchless Drain Pipe Repair in Wellington, FL

    Smaller drain lines inside the house can be lined too, including 2-inch kitchen drains and the 3-inch cast iron stacks found in many older homes. We work through cleanouts and roof vents, so the drywall and tile stay where they are. Branch lines get reinstated with a robotic cutter. That saves opening a finished wall.

    Cost Comparison of Trenchless vs Traditional in Wellington, FL

    Depth changes the math. A line two feet down in open lawn is often cheaper to dig than to line, but a lateral eight feet deep that runs under the driveway and a porch costs far more to excavate than to burst or line from two pits. You get both prices on paper. Then you pick.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Prep and Lining in Wellington

    Prep is most of the job. We jet the line to remove roots and grease, then camera it again to measure the exact footage before cutting the liner tube to length. A poor prep job means a wrinkled liner. Lining works best on cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their round shape.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacements in Wellington

    A bursting head is a steel cone, slightly wider than the old pipe, pulled through the line by a hydraulic unit or a winch cable. It cracks the old pipe outward into the soil while a string of new HDPE pipe follows right behind it. Fused joints, no gaps. We use it when the old line is too broken or flattened to hold a liner.
