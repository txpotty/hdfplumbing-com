    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Wellington

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg fiber pipe or vitrified clay sewer laterals. Orangeburg flattens and blisters over time, while clay cracks and separates at joints. Pipe bursting is often the best option for Orangeburg, and lining can work well for clay that still holds its shape. We identify the material on camera before recommending a method.

    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Wellington, FL

    Sewer lines in Wellington, FL can suffer damage from several factors, including shifting soils that cause pipes to crack or separate. A high water table across much of Florida affects drainage, septic design, and how deeply crews can dig for underground repairs. Tree root intrusion is another major cause, as roots seek out moisture in pipes and grow, causing blockages and structural damage. Age and corrosion, especially in older cast iron or clay pipes, also contribute to deterioration. Our trenchless solutions address these common issues effectively.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Wellington

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one while breaking apart the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene pipe, which is highly resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in older Wellington homes. This method is ideal when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.
