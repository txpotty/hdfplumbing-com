    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Wellington, FL

    Many homes have tankless heaters mounted on an exterior wall with no venting at all, since the unit exhausts directly outside. These units face sun, rain and temperature swings, which can wear out plastic covers and wiring over time. When a rare freeze is forecast, covering outdoor valves and letting a faucet drip is usually enough to prevent damage. When we replace them, we check the pipe cover, freeze protection settings and the electrical connection. We can add a protective pipe cover kit to guard the water and gas lines. Exterior models are often the simplest swap when the gas line is already sized correctly.

    Commercial Tankless Replacement in Wellington

    Restaurants, salons and small offices often use multiple tankless units linked together on a rack system. When one or more units fail, we replace them and rebalance the cascade controls so they split the load. We check gas pressure under full demand, since commercial racks can draw more than 600,000 BTU combined. Scheduling is easy to work around, so we can work after hours and keep your business open. We also set up a descaling schedule matched to your water usage.

    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Wellington

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by recovering heat from exhaust gases. They vent through low-cost PVC and produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized and drained properly. We install a neutralizer cartridge and route the condensate line to an approved drain. Humidity causes condensation on cold lines and clogged AC condensate drains, a frequent source of hidden water damage. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes. We explain the cost difference so you can decide if the efficiency gain is worth it.
