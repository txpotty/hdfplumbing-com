    Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Wellington

    Most tankless descaling visits cost $175 to $350, depending on brand and access. Adding an isolation valve kit typically runs $150 to $300 more.

    Common signs include reduced hot water flow, fluctuating water temperatures, strange noises coming from the unit, or error codes displayed on the control panel. If you notice any of these, it's a strong sign that your tankless water heater in Wellington is due for professional maintenance.
