    Atmospheric Vent Tank Upgrades in Wellington, FL

    Most older homes have atmospheric vent water heaters that rely on natural draft up a metal flue. We inspect the flue pipe for rust, proper slope and correct connector size during replacement. A loose or undersized vent can leak exhaust into the garage or closet. We replace damaged vent sections and draft hoods as needed. Then we test draft with the burner running before leaving.

    Thermal Expansion Tank Installation in Wellington

    Many water utilities install pressure regulators or backflow devices that create a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure spikes can stress your tank and fixtures. Code commonly requires a thermal expansion tank on these systems. We install a properly sized expansion tank and pre-charge it to match your water pressure. This simple add-on can help your new heater last longer.
