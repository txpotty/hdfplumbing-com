    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Replacement in Wellington

    Most gas water heater replacements in Wellington, FL cost $1,200 to $3,500. Tank size and brand set most of it, and gas line or venting upgrades can push it toward the top of that range. You get a written price first.

    Gas and venting mistakes are the dangerous part. A loose flare fitting leaks gas, and a vent connector without upward slope can spill carbon monoxide into the house while everything looks normal. It is a job for a licensed pro.

    Atmospheric vent heaters rely on hot exhaust rising up a metal flue on its own. Power vent models use a fan to push exhaust out through plastic pipe, which lets them vent sideways through a wall and often burn a bit more efficiently. A power vent needs electricity.
