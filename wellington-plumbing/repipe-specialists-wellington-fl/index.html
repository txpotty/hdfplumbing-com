    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Wellington, FL

    Many older homes in Wellington, FL still have galvanized steel pipes, which are vulnerable to corrosion, rust, and mineral buildup. This leads to low water pressure, discolored water, and eventual leaks. Our team specializes in the complete removal and replacement of these outdated systems with modern PEX or copper piping. This upgrade not only resolves existing issues but also significantly improves water quality and flow, boosting your home's value and plumbing reliability for decades to come.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Wellington

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. We can reroute a single hot or cold branch overhead and abandon the leaking pipe in the slab. This is a good option for newer homes or a first-time leak. If the home has had multiple leaks, we will explain why a full repipe may save you money.

    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Wellington

    Slab leaks are a serious and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Nearly all homes in Florida sit on concrete slabs over sandy soil, so leaks under the slab are a frequent and costly repair. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the damaged pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use modern detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Wellington

    Copper piping offers excellent durability, corrosion resistance, and a long lifespan, making it a premium choice for many homeowners in Wellington. While the upfront cost is higher than PEX, copper provides superior rigidity and resistance to UV light if exposed. Our team carefully solders each joint, ensuring a leak-proof and robust plumbing system that can withstand the test of time, including changing temperatures and local water conditions. Copper also naturally inhibits bacterial growth, contributing to cleaner water.

    Copper Repiping in Wellington, FL

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes. Joints are soldered with lead-free solder or pressed with ProPress fittings. Copper repipes typically cost more and take a bit longer than PEX.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Wellington

    PEX-A tubing is flexible, resists scale buildup and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open up the full pipe diameter. Long continuous runs mean fewer joints in walls and attics. Most homes are repiped with PEX in two to four days.
