    Gas Meter to House Line Repair in Country Club, FL

    The run from the utility meter to the house shutoff is the homeowner's pipe in most service areas, so the repair falls to a plumber and not the gas company. It is also the stretch that takes the most weather, sitting in the sun or the soil. Risers rust at grade.     Permit and Inspection Coordination in Country Club

    Corrugated Stainless Steel Tubing Repair in Country Club, FL

    CSST is the yellow-jacketed flexible tubing used in many newer homes, and it can be damaged by a nearby lightning strike if it was never bonded. Bonding means a 6 AWG copper wire from the gas piping to the electrical grounding system. Plenty of older installs lack it. Repairs use the same brand's fittings as the existing tubing, since manufacturers do not allow mixing parts. A splice that skips that rule fails the inspection.

    Appliance Connector Replacement in Country Club

    Old uncoated brass connectors, the kind made before the mid-1970s, are the subject of consumer safety warnings because the flare nut can separate from the tube. Any of those still in service should be replaced. We see them weekly. In its place goes a coated stainless connector and a new shutoff valve, plus a drip leg if the code calls for it. Bubble testing follows before the burner is lit.

    Outdoor Gas Line Repair in Country Club

    Grills, fire pits, pool heaters and patio heaters often sit at the end of a long buried line that nobody has looked at since it went in.
