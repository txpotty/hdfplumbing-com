    Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving heavy scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. Hard water can lead to mineral deposits, or limescale, building up inside faucets. This buildup can reduce water flow, cause drips, and corrode internal components over time. Regular maintenance or choosing faucets designed for hard water can help extend their lifespan.
