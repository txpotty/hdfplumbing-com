    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Country Club, FL

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. HDF Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. They work best on very hard water.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Country Club

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. Every whole-house install gets a bypass valve, and we add a pressure reducing valve if incoming pressure is too high. Soft water reaches every fixture. We can skip the hose bibs.

    Residential Water Softener Installation in Country Club
