    Standard Gas Water Heater Replacement in Country Club

    A standard 40 or 50 gallon atmospheric tank is what most homes have, and swapping one like for like is a two to three hour job when the venting and gas line are in good shape.     Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Replacement in Country Club

    Atmospheric vent heaters last 10 to 12 years on average, and the end usually shows up as rusty hot water or a slow drip from the bottom. The new tank ties into your existing gas line and flue. Closets need enough combustion air. A tank in a tight closet needs louvered doors or a ducted air supply, and we check that before lighting it.

    Bradford White Gas Water Heater Installation in Country Club

    Bradford White tanks are sold only through plumbing wholesalers, not big-box stores, and plenty of Florida plumbers install them for that reason. Parts are easy to get. Installing to their specs keeps the tank warranty intact.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Country Club, FL

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through 2 or 3-inch PVC, so they can sit far from a chimney. A standard outlet has to be within reach of the cord. Vent length and elbows stay inside the manufacturer's limits.

    A standard 40 or 50 gallon gas tank usually runs $1,800 to $3,200 installed, haul-away and basic code upgrades included. Power vent and 75 gallon tanks run $3,000 to $4,800. We give a firm price first.

    Annual flushing and an anode check every few years are the main things. Sediment is what kills most gas tanks, because it sits right over the burner and overheats the steel bottom until it cracks. Have it looked at now and then.

    Three to five hours for most swaps in Country Club. Converting to a different type of heater or making major plumbing changes can stretch it to a full day of work. You will know before we start.

    Flush the tank once a year. Draining a few gallons from the valve at the bottom clears sediment before it hardens, and checking the anode rod every three years tells you when to replace it before the tank starts to rust. Proper sizing helps too.

    Gas and venting mistakes are the dangerous part. One loose fitting can leak gas, and a poorly sloped vent can spill carbon monoxide without any obvious sign. Hire a licensed plumber.
