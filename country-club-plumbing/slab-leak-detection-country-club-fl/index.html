    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Country Club

    Hot lines leak under slabs more often than cold ones. Heat speeds up corrosion, and the pipe expands and contracts every time someone showers, rubbing against concrete and gravel until it wears through. Warm floors and a water heater that runs nonstop are the signs. Energy bills often rise.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Country Club, FL

    Cold-side leaks leave no warm spot. They are harder to see. They usually show up as a running meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards, which is why we isolate the cold line, trace its route and listen along it with acoustic gear.

    Pressure Testing and Isolation in Country Club

    Pressure isolation is always the first step. Our tech closes the main, caps or valves off the hot and cold sides, and puts a gauge on each, then watches which side loses pressure over a set time. The gauge points us to the right line. It also rules out a toilet or faucet leak masquerading as a slab leak in your Country Club, FL home.

    Non-Invasive Slab Leak Locating in Country Club, FL

    The floor gets opened last. Pressure tests, line tracing, thermal scans and acoustic listening all happen from the surface in your Country Club, FL home, so nothing gets drilled or cut during the search. Once two tools agree, we mark the spot and go over repairs, some of which avoid the slab entirely.

    Repiping After Repeat Slab Leaks in Country Club, FL

    Two slab leaks usually means more are coming. The rest of the pipe has likely aged the same way, so repiping replaces every supply line with new PEX or copper through the walls and attic, which ends the pattern of new leaks and new floor openings. A full repipe takes about two to four days.
