    Grease Trap Installation in Gainesville

    A new restaurant or a kitchen expansion usually needs a grease interceptor sized from the fixtures that drain to it. Small kitchens can get by with an under-sink trap, but bigger operations often need an in-ground interceptor of 500 gallons or more. Sizing is set by code. We work from your kitchen plan, set the unit at the right depth and vent it so it passes the pretreatment inspection on the first try.

    Commercial Water Heaters and Boilers in Gainesville, FL

    Gyms, commercial kitchens and apartment buildings burn through hot water in short, heavy peaks. We install and service high-input tank heaters, tankless racks and boiler-fed indirect systems with storage tanks. Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving stubborn scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. Mineral scale chokes output quickly. We put the heaters on a descaling schedule and install mixing valves so hand-wash sinks get tempered water at a safe temperature.

    Grease Trap and Interceptor Repair in Gainesville, FL

    Food service sites need a grease trap or interceptor to keep fats, oils and grease out of the public sewer, and pretreatment inspectors check them. Odor is the first warning. Our work includes baffle and outlet tee repair, lid replacement, inlet line jetting and a sizing check against your fixtures and flow. Slow sinks and overflow usually point to a full or damaged trap. Keeping the pumping log current helps at inspection time.

    Backflow Assembly Testing and Rebuilds in Gainesville

    Most water providers require annual tests on commercial backflow assemblies, and many send late notices that come with fines. Test early. We test reduced pressure zone, double check and pressure vacuum breaker assemblies with gauges calibrated every year and submit the results online or on paper, whichever your provider uses. If a device fails, we usually rebuild it the same day.

    Grease Interceptor and Line Service in Gainesville, FL

    Grease does not stay in the trap if the trap is full or the baffles are broken. It ends up in your line. Our crew checks the baffles and tees, replaces what is broken and jets the feed lines to strip hardened grease a cable cannot touch. A jetted line holds up much better on a busy night.

    After-Hours Commercial Plumbing in Gainesville

    Emergencies at a business cost more the longer they last, so speed matters more than the hourly rate. Water on the floor comes first. We isolate the leak, get restrooms and kitchen lines back in service and then schedule any larger repair for a slow period so you are not paying overtime on non-urgent work.     Commercial Drain Cleaning and Hydro-Jetting in Gainesville

    Commercial drains carry grease, paper towels and food waste in volumes no house ever sees. A cable just pokes a hole. We use high-pressure jetting with rotating nozzles that scrub grease off the whole circumference of 4-inch and 6-inch lines, not just the bottom. Jetting keeps the line open for months instead of days. Restaurants, schools and apartment stacks are where we see the biggest difference.
