    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Gainesville, FL

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones get eaten by sulfuric acid from sewer gas and eventually fall off into the tank. With no outlet baffle, sludge flows right out to the leach lines. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Septic Tank Leak Repair in Gainesville

    A tank that never fills to the outlet, or one that fills slowly with groundwater after rain, has a leak.     Septic Line Clog Clearing in Gainesville

    A slow toilet and a gurgle in the tub usually point to a clog between the house and the tank. We run a camera down the line first, then clear it with a jetter sized for 4-inch sewer pipe. Grease and wipes cause most of these, and what we do next depends on what comes out of the pipe. Roots mean a joint has opened.

    Leach Field Restoration in Gainesville, FL

    Before we condemn a leach field, we open the distribution box and look at how evenly it splits the flow. A tilted or crushed D-box can send everything to one trench and drown it.
