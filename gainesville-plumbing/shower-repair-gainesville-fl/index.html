    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Gainesville, FL

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Minerals fill the nozzles and the screen behind them, and the spray fades so gradually you barely notice until it is a mist. A soak can bring it back. If the cartridge is clogged too, we replace it and test the flow again.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Gainesville

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. If the head drips with the valve closed, water is slipping past the cartridge, and a new head will not change that. The fix is in the valve. Leaks at the arm threads are different, and those get new thread tape and a snug turn.

    Emergency Shower Repair in Gainesville, FL

    A shower that will not shut off is an emergency. Close the main valve where the water line enters the house, or the shower's own stops if it has them behind an access panel. Next, call us. HDF Plumbing sends a plumber with cartridges on the truck, and we can often get your water back on within the hour.

    Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in Gainesville

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Grit and scale scratch the sealing surfaces, the O-rings flatten and harden, and eventually the valve cannot fully stop the water when the handle is closed. We swap in a new one. We use genuine parts and coat the new one with silicone grease so the handle turns smoothly.

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Gainesville, FL

    Low pressure at the shower with good pressure at the sink narrows it down fast. That points to the head, the flow restrictor or the cartridge, not the main or the water heater, and it is usually something we can fix within an hour. We start with the head. Low pressure everywhere sends us to the pressure regulator and main valve instead.

    Hot Water Pressure Problems in Gainesville, FL

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. The problem is on the hot side, either sediment in the water heater outlet, a partly closed valve at the heater or a cartridge whose hot port is packed with scale. Each side gets tested. Good hot flow at the sinks points us straight at the shower cartridge.

    Shower Temperature Swing Repair in Gainesville

    Cold blasts when a toilet flushes mean the pressure-balance spool is stuck. That spool is supposed to slide back and forth to keep the hot and cold mix even when pressure drops on one side, and scale can freeze it in place. Replacing the cartridge solves it. If you cannot get the water hot enough, the anti-scald limit stop may just need adjusting.

    Same-Day Shower Leak Repair in Gainesville, FL
