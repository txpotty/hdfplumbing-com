    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Gainesville

    For significant damage or older, failing systems, traditional sewer line replacement often involves excavating a trench to access and replace the entire pipe. This method is effective for replacing severely collapsed or extensively rooted lines, especially common in older Gainesville homes with cast iron or clay pipes.     Clay and Orangeburg Pipe Replacement in Gainesville, FL

    Clay pipe joints let roots in, while Orangeburg pipe made from tar paper deforms over time. Both are prone to failure. We replace these lines with modern materials. Camera inspection shows the full extent of damage. Replacement ends recurring root problems.

    Addressing Tree Root Intrusions in Gainesville, FL

    Tree roots are a leading cause of sewer line damage, especially with older, more porous pipe materials like clay or cast iron. Roots can penetrate tiny cracks, grow inside the pipe, and eventually cause blockages or even burst the line. Our sewer replacement services specifically address root-damaged pipes, often recommending more resistant materials like PVC or ABS to stop future intrusions. We also advise on tree placement to protect your new sewer system.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Gainesville, FL

    Some lines have severe sagging, wrong slope or are too shallow for trenchless methods. In these cases, open-trench replacement is the best option. We excavate, remove the old pipe and install new PVC with proper bedding. We restore the trench after inspection. This method allows full correction of slope issues.
