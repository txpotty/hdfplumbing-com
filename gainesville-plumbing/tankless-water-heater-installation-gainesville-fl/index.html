    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Gainesville, FL

    Calcium scale is what kills most tankless units early. Around Gainesville, flushing the water heater yearly and descaling tankless units helps keep mineral buildup under control. Once scale coats the heat exchanger, efficiency drops and error codes start, and over time it can crack the exchanger and void the warranty. A yearly flush prevents it. We install isolation valves for that.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Installation in Gainesville, FL

    Rinnai has been making tankless heaters for decades, and parts are easy to get. We install both condensing and non-condensing Rinnai models, and the right one depends on where the vent can go and how much you want to spend up front. Non-condensing units vent in stainless. Condensing models use PVC vent and a condensate drain.

    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in Gainesville

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to get hot water to a far bathroom, since the burner fires only after it senses flow. A recirculation pump on a timer or a motion sensor keeps the hot line primed, so the wait at the farthest sink drops from a minute to a few seconds. Less water goes down the drain.
