    Making the switch from a traditional tank water heater to a tankless system involves more than just swapping units. It often requires changes to gas lines, electrical wiring and venting.     Rinnai, Navien and Noritz Installation in Gainesville, FL

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Gainesville

    Converting from a tank requires more than swapping units. A 40 gallon tank heater uses roughly 40,000 BTU, while a whole-home tankless unit may need up to 199,000 BTU, so the gas line usually needs to be upsized. Venting changes from a metal flue to PVC or stainless, and the unit moves to a wall location. We remove and haul away your old tank and patch the old flue penetration.

    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Gainesville, FL

    Calcium scale is the leading cause of tankless heater failure. Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving heavy scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides extra protection.
