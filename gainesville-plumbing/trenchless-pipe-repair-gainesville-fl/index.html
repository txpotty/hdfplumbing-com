    Pipe Bursting Services in Gainesville, FL

    Bursting replaces a pipe that is too far gone to line. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe. A winch pulls a bursting head through the line, cracking the old pipe outward into the soil while new HDPE follows right behind it, so the replacement pipe ends up in the same path. HDPE does not rust or leak at joints in Gainesville, FL soil.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Costs in Gainesville, FL

    Frequently Asked Questions About Trenchless Pipe Repair in Gainesville

    CIPP lining usually costs $80 to $250 per foot. Bursting is similar. Bursting tends to be $60 to $200 per foot plus pits, and most home sewer lines land between $4,000 and $15,000.

    Lining builds a new pipe inside the old one. Bursting replaces it. With CIPP, a resin-soaked liner cures against the old pipe wall, while bursting breaks the old pipe apart and pulls new HDPE into its place, leaving a completely new pipe.

    Drains that clog again a few weeks after clearing. Sewage backups too. Sewer smells, wet spots in the yard with no visible leak and patches of grass that grow greener than the rest usually point to a cracked or leaking sewer line underground.

    A properly installed CIPP liner can last 50 years or more. Often longer than the original. Since the liner resists rust, roots and chemicals, it often outlives the original pipe, especially clay or cast iron.

    Many cities want a permit for sewer replacement, trenchless included. We check the local rules and pull permits when needed.

    Lining reduces the inside diameter slightly, but the smooth surface usually improves flow. Pipe bursting can maintain or increase the diameter.
