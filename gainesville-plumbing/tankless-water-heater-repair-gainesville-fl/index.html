    Emergency Tankless Repair in Gainesville

    A tankless unit that leaks internally or shows a fault you cannot clear needs prompt attention. We prioritize no-hot-water calls and arrive with common sensors, igniters and filters. If a major component like a heat exchanger has failed, we check warranty coverage and order the part. We can often restore partial operation in the meanwhile.

    Condensing Tankless Repair in Gainesville, FL

    Condensing tankless heaters, such as many Navien models, extract extra heat from exhaust and produce condensate. Clogged condensate traps, failed neutralizers and blocked drains can shut the unit down. We clean the trap, replace the neutralizer media and verify proper drainage. PVC venting is also inspected for slope and sagging.

    Tankless Descaling and Flushing in Gainesville

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside the heat exchanger, limiting flow and causing overheating. Many units warn of this with scale codes, such as the LC code on Rinnai models. We connect a circulation pump to the service valves and run a descaling solution through the exchanger. Annual descaling is a smart move for most homes on hard water.

    No Hot Water Tankless Repair in Gainesville

    When your tankless unit fails to produce hot water, it is often due to issues with the ignition, gas supply or a clogged heat exchanger. Our technicians in Gainesville will thoroughly diagnose the root cause, whether it is a faulty igniter, a pressure sensor malfunction or mineral buildup. We carry common replacement parts for fast fixes, restoring your continuous hot water supply efficiently. We also check for proper venting, which is essential for the safe operation of your unit.

    Pilot Light & Ignition Repair in Gainesville, FL

    For gas-fired tankless water heaters, a faulty ignition system can completely stop hot water production. Our team in Gainesville, FL diagnoses issues with igniters, flame sensors and gas valves. We ensure proper gas pressure and ventilation are maintained for safe and efficient operation. Timely repair of these components prevents dangerous gas problems and restores your unit's ability to heat water on demand.
