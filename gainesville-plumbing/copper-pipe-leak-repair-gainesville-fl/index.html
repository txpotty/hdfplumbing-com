    Solder Joint Leak Repair in Gainesville, FL

    Older solder joints can fail from poor original workmanship, thermal expansion or vibration. A leaking joint usually shows a white or green crust around the fitting. We clean, reheat and resolder the joint or replace the fitting entirely if it is corroded. The result is a clean, fully sweated connection that holds pressure.

    Hot Water Copper Line Repair in Gainesville, FL

    Hot water lines see more corrosion than cold lines because heat speeds up chemical reactions. Recirculation pumps that run constantly can also wear copper from the inside. We repair leaking hot lines and can adjust or add timers to your recirculation system. That reduces wear and lowers energy use.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Repair in Gainesville

    Pinhole leaks are tiny holes that form when the inner wall of copper pipe erodes or pits. They usually appear in thin Type M pipe and on hot water lines where velocity and temperature are higher. We cut out the affected section and replace it with new Type L copper or a PEX transition. While the wall is open, we inspect nearby pipe for discoloration or thinning.

    Pinhole Leak Repair in Gainesville

    Pinhole leaks are a common issue with older copper pipes, often caused by corrosion or acidic water. Our technicians in Gainesville are skilled at accurately locating these small, elusive leaks, which can lead to significant water damage if left alone. We use precise tools to identify the exact spot and then perform a targeted repair, typically cutting out the damaged section and soldering in a new piece of copper pipe. This method creates a strong, durable seal, preventing future issues in that area.

    Copper Repair vs Repipe Cost in Gainesville, FL

    A single copper leak repair is affordable and fast. If you have had two or more pinhole leaks in a year, the copper is likely thinning throughout the system. At that point a PEX or Type L copper repipe can cost less over time than repeated repairs and water damage. We help you compare both options with real figures.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Gainesville

    The cost of copper pipe leak repair in Gainesville can vary significantly depending on the leak's location, severity and accessibility. Minor repairs, like a simple solder patch, might range from $200 to $500. More complex issues, such as a slab leak requiring specialized detection and repair, could range from $800 to $3,000 or more. We provide a detailed estimate after our initial inspection.

    Copper pipe leaks are commonly caused by corrosion, especially from hard or acidic water. Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving heavy scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. Other causes include excessive water pressure, improper installation, shifting foundations putting stress on joints and freezing temperatures that can cause pipes to burst. Pinhole leaks are a common symptom of internal corrosion.

    Common causes include aggressive water chemistry, high water velocity, excessive pressure, poor original soldering and stray electrical current from grounding issues. These factors erode the pipe wall from the inside over time.

    Most simple copper pipe leak repairs, such as fixing an accessible pinhole leak or a single joint, can be completed within one to four hours. More complex issues, like leaks under a slab or extensive re-piping, may take a full day or sometimes multiple days, depending on the scope of work.

    Signs of a hidden copper pipe leak include unexplained increases in your water bill, damp spots on walls or ceilings, musty odors, the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use or visible changes in your home's foundation or landscaping. Don't ignore these indicators.

    We usually use Type L copper, which has thicker walls than Type M and lasts longer. In some situations we use ProPress fittings or a PEX transition.

    We work with all standard types and brands of copper piping, including Type K, L and M, which are commonly found in residential and commercial plumbing systems.
