    New Construction Water Service Lines in Westchester

    New homes, guest houses and additions need a properly sized service line from the meter to the building. We review the plans, confirm meter size with your water provider and choose a line diameter that matches the fixture load.     Main Water Line Replacement in Westchester

    Over time, main water lines can corrode, crack or leak because of shifting soil, tree root intrusion or age. HDF Plumbing focuses on replacing old, failing main water lines, often upgrading from galvanized steel or polybutylene to more durable PEX or copper.     Commercial Water Service Installation in Westchester

    Restaurants, retail suites and small commercial buildings need service lines sized for higher demand and often require backflow protection at the meter.     PEX and HDPE Water Line Installation in Westchester, FL

    PEX and HDPE are flexible, which means fewer fittings in the ground and better tolerance for soil movement. HDPE is especially durable for long runs and resists cracking in expansive clay. We use approved compression or fusion fittings and add tracer wire so the line can be found with a locator later. PEX must stay out of sunlight before burial, so we store and lay it with care on every job site.
