    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Westchester

    Undermount sinks need strong clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. We mount the bowl, seal the rim with silicone, and support it while the adhesive sets. Then we install strainers, traps and supply lines. A proper undermount install helps prevent gaps and leaks along the counter edge.

    Disposal and Dishwasher Hookup with Sink Installation in Westchester, FL

    Most new kitchen sinks are paired with a disposal and dishwasher. We mount the disposal, connect the dishwasher drain through an air gap or high loop, and install the dishwasher supply. Doing it all at once saves time and money. We test every connection under real use.

    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Westchester

    Double bowl sinks need a continuous waste or end outlet layout to drain both sides through one trap. We plan the layout around your disposal and dishwasher connections. Proper slope keeps both sides draining well. We use heavy-wall fittings that resist leaks.

    Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in Westchester

    Drop-in sinks rest on the countertop with a rim, making them a good choice for laminate and tile. We check the cutout size, seal the rim, and tighten mounting clips evenly. This is often the fastest and most affordable kitchen sink upgrade. It also works well in rentals and older homes.
