    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in Westchester

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to get hot water to a far bathroom, since the burner fires only after it senses flow.     Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Westchester, FL

    Calcium scale is the most common reason tankless heaters fail. Around Westchester, flushing the water heater yearly and descaling tankless units helps keep scale buildup under control. Scale coats the heat exchanger, cuts efficiency and triggers error codes, and left alone it can crack the exchanger and end the warranty claim before it starts. A yearly flush prevents it. We install isolation valves for that.
