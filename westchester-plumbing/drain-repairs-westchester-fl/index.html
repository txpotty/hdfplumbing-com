    Broken Drain Pipe Repair in Westchester, FL

    Broken drain pipe usually comes from settling, roots or age. Sometimes it is a bad glue joint. We find the break with a camera and a locator, then cut out the damaged length and install new PVC or cast iron with transition couplings sized to both pipes. We test it before closing up.

    Leaking Drain Pipe Repair in Westchester, FL

    Drain leaks are low pressure, so they drip instead of spray. They hide well. Corrosion, shifting framing and cracked fittings are the usual causes, and we trace the leak by running each fixture and watching the joints, then cut out the damaged section and replace it with matching PVC, ABS or cast iron. Every repair is done to code.

    Laundry and Floor Drain Repair in Westchester

    A washer dumps a lot of water fast. Undersized drains spill over. We correct standpipe height, replace cracked laundry box drains and add venting where it is missing, then refill or repair floor drain traps so they hold water and block sewer gas. We fix primers that keep those seals full.

    Storm Drain Repair in Westchester, FL

    Yard and storm drains carry rain away from the foundation. Heavy afternoon thunderstorms are common in summer, and saturated ground can push water into sewer lines and slow every fixture in the house. Leaves and silt clog them. We clear the line with a jetter, camera it to look for crushed or separated sections, and repair broken pipe so the runoff reaches daylight instead of soaking the soil next to your foundation. A clean outlet matters too.

    Emergency Drain Repairs in Westchester, FL

    A drain that bursts under a sink can soak a cabinet in minutes. Shut off the water to it. We answer after hours, talk you through stopping the flow, and send someone out to cut out the broken pipe and fix it the same visit if possible. We also flag any wet drywall.

    Bathroom Drain Repair in Westchester

    Hair and soap scum are the main problem in bath drains. Hard water makes it worse. We clear sinks, tubs and showers, replace broken pop-up stoppers and worn P-traps, and fix leaking tub shoes and shower drain bodies that let water drip into the ceiling below. Most of these are one-hour jobs.

    Tub and Shower Drain Repair in Westchester, FL

    Hair, soap scum and minerals narrow bath drains over time. Gaskets fail too. We replace worn pop-up assemblies, broken tub shoes and leaking shower drain bodies, often working from inside the tub so the ceiling underneath stays intact. Any access hole is kept small and neat.

    Trap and slip-joint repairs typically cost $150 to $350. Pipe sections run higher. A section of drain pipe usually runs $400 to $1,200, and cast iron stack replacement can range from $2,000 to $6,000 with drywall, flooring and access driving most of the spread. We write it up before we start.

    Snugging a slip nut or replacing a plastic trap is a fair DIY job. Do not crank it down. If the leak comes from a cracked tailpiece, a corroded metal drain or somewhere inside the wall, it needs a plumber who can fix the pipe and look for rot nearby. Water damage under sinks spreads quietly.

    Unclogging a drain does not need one. Bigger changes might. Replacing a stack, relocating a drain or replacing a section of the building drain in Westchester may require a plumbing permit, depending on local rules and how much of the system is being changed. We pull it when one applies.

    Age and corrosion lead the list. Soil movement is close behind. Tree roots, temperature swings, heavy loads over buried pipe, plus bad installation such as skipped primer or unsupported runs, can all break a drain early. Most failures have more than one cause.

    Water coming up somewhere other than where you are draining. That is the big one. If the tub fills when the washer drains, or the toilet bubbles when the sink runs, the blockage is past the point where those fixtures join, usually in the main branch or the building drain. A camera confirms it.

    Most trap and fixture drain repairs take one to two hours. Hidden pipe adds time. Replacing drain pipe inside a wall or under a floor usually runs half a day or more with access setting the pace. We give you a time with the quote.

    A trap swap or a fixture drain fix is usually an hour or two. Bigger jobs run past a day. Replacing a section of main line can take a full day or several days, especially if permits, inspections or excavation under a slab are involved. The schedule is in the quote.

    Cleaning removes a blockage from a sound pipe. Repair fixes the pipe. A crack, a sag, a broken fitting or a missing vent will keep causing clogs no matter how often the line gets cleaned, which is why recurring clogs usually call for a fix instead of another cleaning. A camera tells you which.
