    Repair or Replace a Softener in Westchester, FL

    New softener systems often run $1,200 to $3,500 installed, while a resin rebed usually lands between $300 and $700. If the tank and valve are in good shape, keeping them makes sense. We recommend a new unit when the valve is obsolete and parts are gone, or when the tank itself is cracked.

    Resin Rebedding in Westchester

    Chlorine and chloramine in treated water slowly swell and soften resin beads, and iron or sediment can coat them until capacity drops off. We try a resin cleaner first. If the bed is spent, we empty out the old resin and refill the tank with new high-capacity media, which runs far less than a new softener.

    Yearly Maintenance Plans in Westchester, FL

    Control Valve and Timer Diagnostics in Westchester, FL

    The control valve runs every stage of regeneration, from backwash through brine draw to the final refill. We step it through each cycle by hand and watch for a motor that stalls, a drive gear that skips, a piston that drags or a drain that never shuts off. A worn piston is a common find.

    Commercial Softener Service in Westchester

    Commercial units work harder. Restaurants and laundromats can burn through a softener's capacity in a single day, so a twin-tank system keeps one tank online while the other recharges. We set visits around your business hours so the water never goes hard during a rush.

    Breaking Up Salt Bridges in Westchester

    To check for a bridge, we push a broom handle straight down through the salt. A hard stop, then a drop, means a bridge. We break the crust, pull out the mushy salt underneath with a wet-dry vac, then check the brine well, safety float, brine line and grid plate before refilling to about half full.

    Brine Tank Cleanout and Inspection in Westchester, FL

    Brine tanks collect a gray sludge of salt fines and insoluble grit, and it builds up faster with cheap rock salt. We empty, scrub and rinse the tank. Then we look for cracks around the brine well and overflow fitting and refill with fresh salt.

    Resin Bed Checks and Regeneration Settings in Westchester

    Resin does the actual softening. Each bead trades sodium for calcium and magnesium until it runs out of capacity. We compare the softened hardness against the gallons used since the last regeneration to tell whether the bed is still at full strength.
