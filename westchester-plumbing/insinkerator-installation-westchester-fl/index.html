    Evolution Upgrade Installs in Westchester, FL

    Quiet is the main thing people notice after an Evolution install. Insulation around the motor drops the noise a lot. What they notice later is fewer clogs downstream, because the multi-stage grind breaks fibrous scraps into much smaller pieces before they reach the trap and the branch line.     Batch Feed vs Continuous Feed in Westchester

    Continuous feed is the type most kitchens have. It runs while the switch is on. Batch feed only runs with its stopper turned in place, so nobody can reach into a running unit, which some families with small kids prefer and which needs no wall switch at all. Each type gets wired its own way.

    Upgrading Your InSinkErator in Westchester, FL

    Moving up from a 1/3 HP builder unit to a 3/4 HP Evolution changes more than the grind. The new unit is taller. Because of that height, the discharge can end up below the trap arm, and we measure first so we can lower the trap if needed. The circuit gets checked for the bigger motor's draw too.

    Disposal Installation Cost Comparison in Westchester, FL

    Cost differences come mostly from the unit and the prep work. The cheapest job is a Badger 5 swap with a cord kit. At the top is an Evolution Excel going into a kitchen that needs a new flange and a lower trap arm, with most jobs in between. You see horsepower and warranty length side by side.

    Dishwasher Connection and Air Gap Setup in Westchester

    Nearly every InSinkErator has a dishwasher inlet with a factory knockout plug inside. The plug gets knocked loose and pulled out of the grind chamber. Then the dishwasher hose goes on with a proper clamp, routed through an air gap or up into a high loop under the counter so sink water cannot siphon back into the dishwasher tub. Both appliances get run together.

    Disposal Installs for New Sinks in Westchester, FL

    A new sink changes the drain math. Undermount and farmhouse bowls are often deeper than the old drop-in. A deeper bowl pushes the disposal lower, and if the trap arm in the wall was set for a shallow sink, the discharge can end up below it, so we sometimes lower the wall stub. We plan that during the remodel, not after.

    Badger Series Installation in Westchester, FL

    The Badger line runs from the 1/3 HP Badger 1 up to the Badger 5XP. Every Badger shares one quick-lock mount. Because of that, a Badger-for-Badger swap can take under an hour, and the flange often stays if it is still clean. Badgers ship without a cord, so we bring the cord kit.

    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Westchester

    An air switch puts a push button on the countertop or sink deck instead of a wall switch. Pressing it sends air down a tube. A control box under the sink reads that pulse and flips the power, keeping live wiring well away from the water. We drill the counter or use the spare faucet hole.
