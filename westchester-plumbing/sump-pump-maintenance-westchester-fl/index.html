    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Westchester, FL

    The float switch is the most common reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris.     Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Maintenance in Westchester

    The cost for sump pump maintenance in Westchester, FL typically ranges from $100 to $250, depending on the pump type and its condition. This price usually includes a thorough inspection, cleaning and operational testing. Any necessary repairs or parts replacement would be an additional cost, which we would quote upfront.

    Most quality pumps last seven to ten years. Pumps that sit dry for long periods can seize sooner, which is why testing counts during dry months.

    Most visits take 45 minutes to an hour. Replacing a check valve or float on the same visit adds a little time.

    Routine maintenance usually does not require a permit. However, if a significant repair involves altering the discharge line or replacing the entire unit, especially if electrical work is involved, a local permit might be necessary. We take care of all permit requirements for you.

    The best time is a few weeks before your wettest season starts. Homes that see snow may also want a fall check before winter storms and snowmelt.

    Key signs include unusual noises like grinding or rattling, the pump running constantly, failing to turn on, visible rust or corrosion, or water pooling in your basement. Don't wait for a flood to call us.

    A standard sump pump maintenance appointment usually takes about 30 to 60 minutes. This time allows for a complete inspection, cleaning and testing of all components. If minor repairs are needed, the visit might run slightly longer.

    We recommend having your sump pump professionally maintained at least once a year. If your home is in an area prone to heavy rainfall or has a high water table, twice-yearly maintenance might be more worthwhile. Regular checks ensure it's ready for peak seasons.
