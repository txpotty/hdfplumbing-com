    Tankless Descaling and Service Work in Westchester, FL

    Home Tankless Flushing in Westchester

    Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving heavy scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. Inside a tankless heater, that water runs through a small copper or stainless coil while the burner fires, and every minute of hot water leaves a little calcium behind. A pump on the service valves pushes descaling solution through the coil for roughly an hour. Flow comes back. Temperatures stop swinging, and the unit quits shutting down on high-limit.

    Inlet Screen and Air Filter Cleaning in Westchester

    Grit gets in. A small screen at the cold inlet catches sand and scale flakes, especially after a main break. Units with an air intake filter collect dust and lint, which can push the burner into a combustion code. We clean both on every visit.

    Navien Condensing Tune-Ups in Westchester

    Navien NPE units are condensing heaters with a second heat exchanger that pulls extra heat out of the exhaust before it leaves the house. That second exchanger makes acidic condensate. The condensate trap gets cleared and the neutralizer media checked. On A-series models with the built-in recirculation pump, we test the pump and the buffer tank too.

    Rinnai Air Filter and Flame Rod Care in Westchester

    Rinnai cabinets are tight. We wash the air intake filter and check the flame rod for carbon. On recirculating models we also check the pump schedule, because a wrong setting burns gas for nothing.

    Maintenance for Every Tankless Brand in Westchester, FL

    We service Bosch, Rheem, Takagi and Navien heaters around Westchester, and each one has its own filter locations and code list in the manual. The manufacturer's steps come first, not a generic checklist. That matters for warranty claims down the road.

    Recirculation and Combi Unit Service in Westchester, FL

    Built-in recirculation pumps and buffer tanks get hot water to the tap faster, but they also push more water through the exchanger every day, so those units scale sooner. The recirc loop gets flushed with the exchanger. Next we confirm crossover valve or dedicated return, and set the pump schedule to fit.

    Yearly Tankless Inspection in Westchester

    A yearly inspection costs less than one service call for a dead heater. We soap-test the gas connections and try the pressure relief valve. The air filter comes out and the igniter gets a look. Anything that is wearing out goes on a written list with a plain price, and you decide what gets done now and what can wait until next year.

    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Westchester

    Picture a bundle of thin tubes with a gas flame under them and cold water racing through at three or four gallons a minute. That is the exchanger. Minerals settle on the hottest tube walls first, and the passages get narrower. A 45 to 60 minute circulation with descaling solution dissolves it.
