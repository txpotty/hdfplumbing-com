    PEX and Copper Leak Detection in Westchester

    On the microphone, copper leaks are easy to hear. PEX is quieter. Plastic pipe does not carry sound well, so we lean on thermal scans, moisture readings and tracer gas in homes plumbed with it, paying special attention to crimp fittings, the usual weak point. Copper pinholes from water chemistry are common too.

    Irrigation Leak Detection in Westchester

    Drip and sprinkler lines cover the yard underground, and they hide a lot of water loss. Stuck valves are common. We isolate the irrigation system from the house, run each zone and trace leaking lines, since a cracked lateral or broken emitter can lose thousands of gallons unnoticed.

    Pool Water Leak Detection in Westchester, FL

    Summer heat and wind pull a lot of water from a pool, which lets a leak blend in with evaporation. A bucket test separates the two. Then we pressure test the suction and return lines and dye test around lights, skimmers and fittings, because a leak left running can undermine the shell and deck.

    Water Meter Leak Testing in Westchester

    The meter is the first and simplest leak test. Shut everything, then watch. If the leak indicator spins with every fixture and appliance off, water is escaping somewhere, and the flow reading tells us roughly how many gallons a day are being lost. Then we shut off zones one at a time.

    Main Service Line Leaks in Westchester, FL

    The service line carries full street pressure and can leak underground for a long time. Soft spots in the lawn may be the only sign. We trace the line, listen for the leak and mark it for a targeted repair, usually suggesting replacement when the line is old galvanized or polybutylene.

    Slab Leaks and Foundations in Westchester

    A slab leak wets the soil the house sits on. That is the real danger. We listen for water escaping under the floor with acoustic gear, confirm hot-side leaks with a thermal camera and mark the spot within inches, so the repair is a small opening or a reroute rather than a broken-up floor, protecting the foundation from soil movement in Florida homes.

    Toilet and Fixture Leaks in Westchester, FL

    Toilets waste more water than most people expect. A worn flapper leaks silently. We drop dye in each tank, check fill valves and inspect faucets, showers and supply lines for drips, and most of these get fixed in under an hour with parts from the truck. The next bill shows the difference.

    Preventive Leak Audits in Westchester, FL

    Checking once a year catches small leaks early. We read the meter with everything off, test static pressure and inspect supply lines, shutoff valves and water heater fittings throughout homes and businesses in Westchester, FL. Older homes benefit most. Original braided connectors and corroded stops tend to fail together, so we flag them before they let go.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Leak Detection in Westchester

    Water leak detection in Westchester typically runs $150 to $450, depending on property size and how many systems need testing, like the house, irrigation and pool. Repair costs depend on the leak. We quote it before starting.

    Our trucks carry acoustic listening gear, thermal cameras, moisture meters, drain cameras and tracer gas kits for plastic pipe that does not carry sound. Each tool covers a different case. Two tools agreeing on a spot confirms it.

    Watch for a bill that jumps, water sounds with every fixture off, stains on walls or ceilings, peeling paint, a musty smell or warm floor spots. The meter dial tells you the most. Turn off every fixture and watch it for ten minutes.

    Smart shutoff valves such as Moen Flo and Phyn, plus wireless leak sensors that sit on the floor by the water heater, washer and sinks. They watch flow and can close the main. Alerts go to your phone.
