    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in Lehigh Acres, FL

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of protection and are required for high hazard connections like commercial kitchens, medical facilities, chemical injection and car washes. They include two independent check valves with a relief valve that dumps water if either check fails. Because the relief valve can discharge, we plan for drainage and install the assembly above grade where it can be tested. We carry trusted brands like Watts, Febco, Wilkins and Apollo.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Lehigh Acres

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are commonly required for high-hazard applications in Lehigh Acres.     Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Lehigh Acres

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules permit. We size them to avoid too much pressure loss on your system.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Backflow Preventer Installation in Lehigh Acres

    Residential pressure vacuum breakers usually cost $250 to $700 installed. Double check and RPZ assemblies for larger homes or businesses range from $800 to $3,500 or more depending on size and enclosure needs.

    Most residential installs take two to four hours including testing. Large commercial assemblies can take a full day, especially if piping must be reconfigured.

    Backflow is when water flows the wrong way through a pipe and carries contaminants into the clean water supply. It can happen from back siphonage during a pressure drop or back pressure from pumps and boilers.

    Installing a backflow preventer is a complex task that requires plumbing knowledge, tools and certification to meet local codes and properly protect your water supply. Incorrect installation can lead to ineffective protection, fines or health risks. It is always recommended to hire a licensed professional like HDF Plumbing.

    Many jurisdictions require a permit for new backflow assemblies, especially on commercial properties. We check local rules and pull the permit when needed.

    We commonly install high-quality backflow preventers from trusted brands such as Watts, Wilkins by Zurn, Apollo and Conbraco. Our plumbers are experienced with a wide range of devices and can recommend the best option for your application and budget.

    Most residential backflow preventer installations can be completed within 2 to 4 hours. More complex commercial systems or installations requiring significant pipe changes might take longer. We always aim to complete the work efficiently while keeping our quality standards high.

    An RPZ, or reduced pressure zone device, provides the highest level of protection and is used for high-hazard applications where contaminants could be toxic.
