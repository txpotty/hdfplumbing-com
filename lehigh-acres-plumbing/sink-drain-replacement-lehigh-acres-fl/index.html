    Kitchen Sink Drain Replacement in Lehigh Acres

    Kitchen drains start failing at the basket strainer. Strainer and trap get swapped as a pair. Disposal sinks get a new flange instead.

    Code Updates During Drain Replacement in Lehigh Acres

    Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving heavy scale on fixtures and inside water heaters.
