    Frequently Asked Questions About Urinal Installation in Lehigh Acres

    The cost for urinal installation in Lehigh Acres, FL can range from $400 to $1,200, depending on the type of urinal, complexity of plumbing modifications, and whether it's a new installation or a replacement. This price usually includes labor and basic materials, but highly specialized units or extensive rough-in work can increase the overall cost.

    A replacement takes two to four hours. A new install with rough-in and carrier usually takes one to two days, plus inspection time.

    A washdown urinal uses a simple flushing action to rinse the bowl, relying on gravity and the slope of the drain. A siphonic urinal creates a siphonic action during flushing, which pulls waste more forcefully down the drain, offering more effective waste removal and usually better odor control.

    New urinals and restroom changes usually require a plumbing permit. Simple like-for-like replacements usually do not, and we will confirm with your local building department.

    We guarantee our installation labor, and fixtures and flush valves carry manufacturer warranties, often several years on flushometer components.

    Constant running, cracked porcelain, persistent odor, leaks at the wall and slow drains all point to a fixture or valve that needs attention.

    Flush urinals use a water flush to remove waste, while waterless urinals use a specialized trap system with a sealant liquid to prevent odors without water. Waterless urinals offer major water savings but require specific maintenance for their sealant cartridges. Both types offer effective waste disposal.
