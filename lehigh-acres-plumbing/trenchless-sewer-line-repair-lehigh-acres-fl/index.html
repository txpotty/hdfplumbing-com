    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacements in Lehigh Acres

    A bursting head is a steel cone, slightly wider than the old pipe, pulled through the line by a hydraulic unit or a winch cable. As it moves, it splits the old clay or cast iron and shoves the pieces into the surrounding soil while a string of new HDPE pipe follows right behind it. Fused joints, no gaps. We use it when the old line is too broken or flattened to hold a liner.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Lining in Lehigh Acres, FL

    Cast iron rusts from the inside out, and the bottom of the pipe goes first. Plumbers call that channel rot, a groove that eats through the bottom. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or carbide cutters spinning on a flexible shaft, which brings the wall back to bare metal so a liner can bond to it. Under a slab, lining beats breaking up the floor in two rooms.

    Perma-Liner Lining Equipment in Lehigh Acres

    Perma-Liner makes inversion drums and epoxy systems for lining 2- to 6-inch drain lines, which covers most of what we see in houses. The equipment fits in a pickup bed. Compact gear matters on tight lots. The resin is mixed on site, so it does not start curing before the liner is in place.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Lehigh Acres, FL

    CIPP stands for cured-in-place pipe. We wet out a felt tube with epoxy resin, invert it into the cleaned line with air pressure, and hold it against the old pipe wall until the resin hardens. Cure time runs a few hours. The finished liner seals cracks and leaking joints in the original pipe.
