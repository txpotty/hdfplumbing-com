    Frequently Asked Questions About Drainage Services in Margate

    Cost depends on whether it is a clog or a construction job. Clearing a fixture or main line is a few hundred dollars at most. Jetting and sewer repairs cost more, and yard drains are priced by length and digging. You get a written quote first.

    Store-bought cleaners rarely clear a real clog. They can make things worse. The chemicals sit in the trap, heat up and can damage older pipe or soften glue joints, and if the clog does not clear, the plumber now has to open a trap full of caustic liquid. A cable is safer.

    Roots grow toward the water and nutrients inside sewer pipe. A hairline crack is enough. A small root at a cracked joint becomes a mass that traps paper and grease, especially in clay pipe.

    Most sink and toilet clogs clear in under an hour. A main line needs more time. A main line with camera work usually takes about two hours, more if the cleanout is hard to reach.

    Roots usually enter through an existing crack or joint, then widen it as they grow. Clay pipe can split over time. Roots can offset joints and, in bad cases, collapse the pipe, which then needs digging or lining.

    Call when more than one drain is slow, or when a toilet gurgles while the washer drains. That usually means the main line. In the yard, water that stands for a day or more after rain is the cue.

    Our cable machines come from Spartan Tool and RIDGID, and our cameras are RIDGID SeeSnake units with built-in locators. Jetters are trailer or cart units. Good equipment matters because a camera with a locator tells us exactly where and how deep a problem is, which keeps a sewer repair to one small excavation instead of a long trench.

    Most homes never need scheduled drain cleaning. A slow drain is the signal. Root-prone sewers and grease-heavy kitchens benefit from cleaning every one to two years.
