    Supply and Drain Leaks Under the Slab in Margate, FL

    Supply leaks and drain leaks act differently. Supply leaks are pressurized, so they move the meter, warm the floor on the hot side and run whether or not anyone uses water. Drain leaks only leak when water goes down, and they show up as odors, slow drains or damp floors near bathrooms. We test both kinds in your Margate, FL home.

    Listening Through Concrete in Margate
