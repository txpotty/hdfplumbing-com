    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Margate

    Showerheads clog from the inside out. Scale builds behind the face plate and in the flow restrictor, so the spray goes uneven, shoots sideways or drops to a trickle even when the valve is wide open. A vinegar soak can work. When it does not, we install a new head with soft rubber nozzles that you can wipe clean with a thumb.

    Shower Drain Body Repair in Margate

    Drain bodies can crack or work loose under the pan. When that happens, water goes under the shower instead of down the pipe, and the first sign is often a soft spot in the floor next to the shower or a stain in the ceiling below. We clear and test the drain. We reseal or replace the drain body as needed.

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Margate, FL

    Stains on the ceiling below are often the first clue. We use a moisture meter on the walls and a flood test on the pan to tell a leaking valve from a cracked pan, a failed grout line or a loose drain body. The source gets repaired. With slab floors, the water can travel under the flooring of nearby rooms.

    Mixing Valve Repair in Margate

    Mixing valves blend hot and cold inside the wall. A sticking cartridge or spool shows up as temperature that drifts or a handle that has to go much farther than it used to. We replace the cartridge. If the valve is too old for parts, we talk through a replacement valve and what it means for the tile.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Margate, FL

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. When the gate in a lift-style spout wears or a three-handle diverter stem loses its washer, water keeps pouring from the spout and the shower gets weak. Usually a new spout fixes it. It is a short job.

    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Margate, FL

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. Because everything is linked, a weak diverter starves the head and a leaky valve drips from the spout and the head alike. Each part gets tested. We also check the overflow gasket, a frequent hidden leak.

    Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in Margate

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Mineral grit scores the sealing faces and flattens the O-rings until the cartridge can no longer stop the flow. We swap in a new one. The new cartridge goes in with silicone grease, which keeps the handle smooth.

    Shower Temperature Swing Repair in Margate

    Cold blasts when a toilet flushes mean the pressure-balance spool is stuck. That spool is supposed to slide back and forth to keep the hot and cold mix even when pressure drops on one side, and scale can freeze it in place. Replacing the cartridge solves it. If you cannot get the water hot enough, the anti-scald limit stop may just need adjusting.
