    Backflow Preventer Testing in Margate

    Backflow preventers protect the drinking water supply from contamination, and many water providers call for annual testing for irrigation and commercial devices. Our certified testers check each valve and file results with your water utility as required. If a device fails, we repair or replace the internal parts. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where necessary. Keeping up with testing avoids fines and water shutoffs.

    Water Heater Maintenance in Margate

    Regular water heater maintenance is key to preventing sediment buildup. Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving heavy scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. Our service includes flushing the tank, inspecting the anode rod for corrosion, checking the temperature and pressure relief valve, and examining all connections and heating elements. This extends the life of your unit, improves efficiency, and helps avoid sudden cold showers or leaks.
