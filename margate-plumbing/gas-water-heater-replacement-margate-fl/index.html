    Picking a Gas Water Heater for Your Margate, FL Home

    40 and 50 Gallon Tank Replacement in Margate

    Forty and fifty gallon gas tanks are the workhorse in most Florida homes, and a leak at the bottom seam means the tank is finished. Newer tanks can run taller than the old one. We check clearance before we order. Plan on about two to three hours.

    No Hot Water Calls in Margate

    No hot water at all usually points to a dead thermocouple or a gas control valve that has failed after years of service. We check those first. An old tank with a bad valve usually gets replaced.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Upgrades in Margate, FL

    Condensing gas tanks run around 90 percent efficient or better and vent through PVC, while a standard atmospheric tank sits closer to 60 percent. They cost more and need a condensate drain and an outlet. Big households see payback sooner.

    Bradford White Gas Water Heater Installation in Margate

    Bradford White tanks are sold only through plumbing wholesalers, not big-box stores, and plenty of Florida plumbers install them for that reason. Parts are easy to get. We follow their venting and clearance specs to the letter, which keeps the warranty valid.

    Thermal Expansion Tank Installation in Margate

    Closed systems are the reason expansion tanks exist. A pressure regulator or backflow preventer on the main stops heated water from pushing back to the street, so pressure climbs every time the burner runs. Many codes require a thermal expansion tank there. We precharge it to your house pressure.

    A.O. Smith Gas Water Heater Service in Margate

    A.O. Smith makes tanks under its own name and also builds the State line, so parts overlap a lot between the two brands. We handle the sizing and the gas connection. We confirm the vent meets their specs.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Replacement in Margate

    Atmospheric vent heaters last 10 to 12 years on average, and the end usually shows up as rusty hot water or a slow drip from the bottom. We disconnect the old unit and reconnect the new one to the existing gas line and flue. Combustion air matters. A tank in a tight closet needs louvered doors or a ducted air supply, and we check that before lighting it.

    Standard Gas Water Heater Replacement in Margate

    A standard 40 or 50 gallon atmospheric tank is what most homes have, and swapping one like for like is a two to three hour job when the venting and gas line are in good shape. We drain the old tank, cap the gas, and set the new heater on a stand or pan. New flex connectors every time. We soap-test the gas joints and check the draft hood with the burner lit.
