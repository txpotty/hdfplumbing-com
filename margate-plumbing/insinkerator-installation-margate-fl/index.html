    Upgrading Your InSinkErator in Margate, FL

    Moving up from a 1/3 HP builder unit to a 3/4 HP Evolution changes more than the grind. The new unit is taller. That extra height can put the discharge outlet below the trap arm, so we measure the drop before install day and lower the trap or the arm if the numbers do not work. The circuit gets checked for the bigger motor's draw too.

    Disposal Installation Cost Comparison in Margate, FL

    Hard Water and Disposals in Margate

    Around Margate, flushing the water heater yearly and descaling tankless units helps keep scale buildup under control. Scale builds up on grind rings and drain tailpieces over the years. A scaled unit gets loud. Drainage slows too, since crust narrows the tailpiece and trap, and we check those parts before tying a new unit into them. If they are crusted badly, we swap them while we are there.

    Badger Series Installation in Margate, FL

    The Badger line runs from the 1/3 HP Badger 1 up to the Badger 5XP. All of them use the same quick-lock mount. Because of that, a Badger-for-Badger swap can take under an hour, and the flange often stays if it is still clean. No cord comes in the box, so the cord kit is on us.

    Batch Feed vs Continuous Feed in Margate

    Continuous feed is the type most kitchens have. Flip the switch and it runs until you flip it off. Batch feed only runs with its stopper turned in place, so nobody can reach into a running unit, which some families with small kids prefer and which needs no wall switch at all. Each type gets wired its own way.
