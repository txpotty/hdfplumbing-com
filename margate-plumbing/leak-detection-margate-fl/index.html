    Our Leak Detection Equipment in Margate

    Our trucks carry ground microphones and correlators, thermal cameras, line tracers, moisture meters and tracer gas kits. Every tool covers a different case.     Pool Leak Detection in Margate

    A pool losing more than a quarter inch a day beyond evaporation likely has a leak somewhere. We start with a bucket test.
