    Detailed Plumbing Inspections in Margate, FL

    Water Heater Inspections in Margate

    Water heaters give clues long before they fail. The serial number tells us the age, and we check for rust, look at the vent and test the relief valve. We check tankless units for scale. Gas heaters get a look at the flame, the drip leg and the vent draft, since a poorly venting heater is a safety issue.

    Gas Connection Safety Check in Margate, FL

    During a full inspection, we look at the visible gas piping and the connectors to each appliance. Old brass connectors, missing shutoffs or drip legs and rust all get noted, and we sweep joints with a gas detector. Any leak is reported on the spot. Most of our gas findings are in older homes, often at original appliance connectors.

    Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Margate, FL

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. Too much pressure wears parts. Pressure above 80 psi shortens the life of fixtures, supply hoses and water heaters, and we also identify the supply pipe material, from copper and CPVC to polybutylene, Kitec and galvanized.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Margate, FL

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. Shutoffs get tested too. Angle stops and the main shutoff valve often seize over the years, and we also listen for gurgling drains that point to venting problems, since small fixes like these are easy to plan once you know about them.

    Leak Detection and Slab Screening in Margate

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. We also check for warm floor spots. Meter tests, pressure loss checks and a look for damp baseboards come first, and slab homes get acoustic and thermal follow-up. Early slab leak finds save floors and foundations.

    Commercial Plumbing Inspections in Margate, FL

    Commercial buildings have equipment homes do not, so our commercial inspections cover more ground. Backflow devices, grease traps, large heaters, flushometers and the main sewer are all part of it. Records matter for inspectors. Managers use the report to fix problems before an inspector or water provider flags them.

    Pre-Purchase Plumbing Inspections in Margate

    A house can pass a general inspection and still have a sewer full of roots. Buyers use our inspection to learn the pipe type, the sewer condition and the heater's age before the option period ends. That leaves room to negotiate. Old galvanized, a damaged sewer or an aging heater can all become a repair request or a closing credit.

    Water Heater Safety Inspection in Margate

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. We test the T and P valve, confirm its discharge line runs to a safe place, look for an expansion tank where one is needed and check the drain pan. Gas units get a CO check. A heater that backdrafts can spill combustion gases into the house, so we test the draft at the vent hood and note any problems right away.
