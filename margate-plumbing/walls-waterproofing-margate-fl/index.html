    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Margate

    Hairline cracks in poured concrete walls leak more water than their size suggests. We inject them with low-pressure polyurethane foam, which expands to fill the crack from the inside face to the soil side, or with epoxy when the crack also needs structural bonding. Horizontal cracks are different. They can mean the wall is bowing from soil pressure, so we check for movement with a level before we seal anything. Vertical cracks are usually a one-visit job.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Margate

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and often add a dehumidifier to keep relative humidity below 60 percent. Salt air in coastal parts of Florida corrodes outdoor fittings and exposed metal pipe faster than in inland areas. The joists stop rotting. Drier air under the floor also cuts down on the musty smell in the house.

    Foundation Crack Repair in Margate

    Crack injection takes about two hours per crack. We set injection ports every 8 to 12 inches along the crack, seal the surface with epoxy paste, and pump in resin starting at the lowest port and working up until it pushes out the next port. Polyurethane flexes a bit if the wall shifts, and epoxy bonds the two sides back together. The surface paste gets ground off once it cures.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Margate, FL

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries. The usual cause is a sprinkler head spraying the wall instead of the plants, or soil graded toward the foundation. We fix the water first. After that the wall gets cleaned, dried and coated with a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Margate, FL

    Wet baseboards outside the shower or a stain on the ceiling below usually point to the pan. We plug the drain, fill the pan with an inch or two of water, and mark the waterline and check it a day later. A drop means a leak. Rebuilding means a mortar pre-slope of a quarter inch per foot, a new PVC liner or bonded membrane, and a two-piece clamping drain set on the liner. We flood test the new pan before any tile goes down.

    Drainage System Integration in Margate, FL

    Water has to go somewhere once a wall is sealed. Footing drains, interior drain tile and French drains in the yard all do the same job at different depths. We match the drain to where the water comes from, which might be a high water table, a yard that slopes toward the house, or a downspout emptying right at the foundation. We extend downspouts at least four feet from the wall.

    Basement Wall Sealing in Margate, FL

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Salt air in coastal parts of Florida corrodes outdoor fittings and exposed metal pipe faster than in inland areas. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then coat the face with a masonry waterproofing product that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. The musty smell usually fades within weeks. Those weep holes empty into the interior drain if the house has one.
