    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Maintenance in Pensacola

    Most Pensacola, FL homeowners pay $100 to $250 for a standard maintenance visit, depending on the pump type and how long since it was last serviced. The price includes the inspection, a pit cleanout and the water test. Parts and repairs are quoted separately before we start.

    Without a working battery or water-powered backup, the pit keeps filling during an outage and eventually spills over onto the basement floor. In a hard storm, that takes under an hour. Water-powered backups need city water pressure, so they do not work on a private well.

    Watch for a pump that runs nonstop, hums without moving water, cycles every minute or two, or grinds. A stuck float, rust on the housing or a musty smell near the pit also point to trouble.

    Seven to ten years is normal for a quality pump. Pumps that sit dry for long stretches can seize sooner, which is why we test them in dry months too.

    Zoeller, Wayne, Little Giant, Liberty and Flotec are the brands we see most in Pensacola homes, and we service all of them. Each brand has its own weak spots, and our crew knows them.

    Most maintenance and simple part swaps need no permit. Swapping the whole pump, adding wiring or rerouting the discharge can trigger a permit locally. We pull it for you when it applies.

    The check valve stops water in the discharge pipe from dropping back into the pit after the pump shuts off. Without one, the pump ends up moving the same water again and again. All that extra cycling shortens motor life and adds to the power bill.

    Grinding, rattling, nonstop running, a pump that will not start, rust on the housing or damp spots on the floor near the pit. Each of those is reason enough to book a visit before the next storm.
