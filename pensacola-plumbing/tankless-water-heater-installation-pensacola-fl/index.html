    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Pensacola, FL

    Calcium scale is the most common cause of tankless heater failure. Around Pensacola, flushing the water heater yearly and descaling tankless units helps keep mineral buildup under control. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides added protection.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Pensacola

    Converting from a tank requires more than swapping units. Venting changes from a metal flue to PVC or stainless, and the unit moves to a wall location. We remove and haul away your old tank and patch the old flue penetration.
