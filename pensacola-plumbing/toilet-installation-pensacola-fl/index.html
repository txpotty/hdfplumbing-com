    Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in Pensacola, FL

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, with the tank hidden behind an access plate. They free up floor space and make cleaning simple, but they need a wall at least 2 by 6 deep or a furred-out chase. We install Geberit, Grohe and Kohler carrier systems and set the bowl height to your preference. This work is best done during a remodel when the wall is open.

    Comfort-Height and ADA Toilet Installation in Pensacola

    Comfort-height toilets put the seat at about 17 to 19 inches, which is easier on knees and backs. For accessible bathrooms, we follow ADA guidelines for seat height, centerline distance from the side wall and grab bar clearance. We can install elongated bowls and add blocking for grab bars while the wall is open. These upgrades are popular with homeowners planning to age in place.

    Low-Flow WaterSense Toilet Installation in Pensacola, FL

    Modern WaterSense toilets use 1.28 gallons per flush or less, and dual-flush models offer an even lighter option for liquid waste. Swapping an old 3.5 gallon toilet makes a noticeable difference on your water bill. Some local water providers have offered rebates for high-efficiency toilets, so it is worth checking with yours. We help you choose a model with a high MaP flush rating.

    New Construction Toilet Installation in Pensacola

    In new homes and additions, we set toilets after the finished floor is in so the flange sits at the right height. We verify the drain is properly vented and the rough-in matches the fixture you selected. Each toilet gets a new wax or wax-free seal, a stainless supply line and a quarter-turn angle stop. We flush-test and check for leaks before the final inspection.
