    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Pensacola, FL

    A dead pump during a storm means the water is already climbing. Our trucks carry 1/3 and 1/2 HP submersibles, check valves and fittings, so in most homes we can pull the old pump and set a new one in one visit. Most swaps take about two hours. We test it with a full pit before leaving.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Pensacola

    No pit yet? We saw-cut the slab at the low corner, break out the concrete and dig down through soil, hardpan or rock to set an 18 by 22 inch basin. We backfill around the basin with washed gravel so groundwater has a path in. The slab gets patched with new concrete. Last come the pump, the lid and the discharge line.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Pensacola, FL

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. The charger keeps the battery topped off. Expect a battery swap every four to five years. Finished basements get the most out of them.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Pensacola

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. We size it from pit diameter and vertical lift. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. A 1-1/2 inch discharge line handles the flow from either size without straining the motor.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Pensacola

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi monitors go further and send a phone alert when the pump runs too long, the power drops or the water climbs past a set level. Landlords like them. They can go on a new pump or one that is already working.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Pensacola, FL

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get. We install their M53 and M98 models often. They make matching backup units. Pit size and lift decide which model we recommend.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Pensacola, FL

    Liberty builds quiet, well-made submersible pumps with good switch options. We install their 250 and 280 series often. Replacement switches are easy to get. We drill a 3/16 inch weep hole in the discharge to stop air lock.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Pensacola, FL

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. That makes service easy. Expect more motor noise than a submersible. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    Most Pensacola, FL sump pump jobs land between $600 and $1,800. That covers the pump, the check valve and labor. Digging a pit adds cost. Every line item is on the quote.

    Seven to ten years is typical. Cast iron pumps with mechanical switches tend to outlast plastic ones, and a pump that rests longer between cycles lasts longer. Cleaning the pit helps.
