    Pilot Light Repair in Pensacola

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most common gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and service or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are finished in about an hour.

    Emergency Gas Water Heater Repair in Pensacola

    Gas Valve and Thermostat Repair in Pensacola, FL

    Issues with the gas valve or thermostat can lead to uneven water temperatures or a complete lack of hot water. Our team can test these key components for proper operation, checking for electrical continuity and gas flow.     Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Pensacola

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust out through PVC pipe. Failures usually involve the blower motor, pressure switch, hot surface igniter or condensate in the vent. We read the error codes, test each part and repair the fault. Correct vent slope and termination are verified before we leave.

    Gas Control Valve Replacement in Pensacola, FL

    The gas control valve regulates temperature and shuts off gas if the pilot goes out. Newer valves from Honeywell and White-Rodgers use status lights that blink codes when something goes wrong. If the valve itself has failed, we replace it with an exact match or approved universal model. We leak test and verify temperature control afterward.

    Burner Cleaning and Repair in Pensacola, FL

    A yellow, lazy or noisy flame points to a dirty burner or poor combustion air. We remove and clean the burner, check the orifice size for natural gas or propane and adjust as needed. A clean blue flame heats faster and produces less carbon monoxide. We also check for rust flakes falling from the flue.
