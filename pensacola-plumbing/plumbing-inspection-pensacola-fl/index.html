    Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Pensacola, FL

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. High pressure ages fixtures fast. Anything over 80 psi cuts the life of hoses, fixtures and heaters, and we note the supply pipe type, from copper and CPVC to poly, Kitec and galvanized.

    Water Heater Safety Inspection in Pensacola

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. We test the T and P valve, confirm its discharge line runs to a safe place, look for an expansion tank where one is needed and check the drain pan. We check gas units for draft. Backdrafting heaters can push combustion gases indoors, which is why we test the vent and report problems before we leave.

    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Pensacola

    Many wells in Florida produce water with a sulfur smell or high iron, which often calls for treatment to protect fixtures and appliances. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. Signs include crusted aerators, clogged showerheads, slow toilet fill and heater sediment, and we can test hardness during the visit. You get options, not a pitch. Softeners, filters and scale reducers all help, and we suggest the one that matches the reading we took.
