    Picking a Tankless for Your Pensacola, FL Home

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Pensacola, FL

    Exterior-mount tankless units hang on an outside wall and vent straight out the front, so there is no vent pipe to run. Sun and weather wear on the covers and wiring. Frozen pipes are rare in most of Florida, and many homes have outdoor plumbing that would never survive a northern winter. On every outdoor swap, we check freeze protection and the pipe cover. They are usually the easiest swap.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Pensacola

    Rinnai units we pull are usually 12 to 18 years old, and the failure tends to be a leaking heat exchanger or a dead control board. A current Rinnai often mounts on the same bracket. Less drywall work that way. We match the vent type or convert to PVC on a condensing model.

    Emergency Tankless Replacement in Pensacola, FL

    A tankless that will not ignite or leaks from the heat exchanger leaves you with no hot water at all. Call (904) 925-7356 any time it quits. We stock common models for same-day replacement. A stopgap repair can sometimes bridge the gap until the right unit arrives.

    Recirculation Tankless Upgrades in Pensacola, FL

    Waiting a minute for hot water at the far bathroom wastes thousands of gallons a year in a long single-story house. Navien A models and Rinnai units with internal pumps can recirculate through a return line or a crossover valve under the farthest sink. The pump runs on a timer. Usually just the morning and evening peaks.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Pensacola, FL

    Pulling a 50 gallon tank out of a garage frees up floor space and ends standby heat loss. The catch is gas, since most tankless units need a 3/4-inch line or bigger plus new venting. Before quoting, we check the meter and pipe run, and afterward we cap the old flue and seal the roof. Expect a full day.

    Gas Line Testing on Tankless Installs in Pensacola, FL

    Every gas connection on a tankless gets a soap test and a manometer reading at full fire before we leave the house. A line that holds pressure at rest can still sag under full load. We test both.

    Like-for-Like Tankless Swaps in Pensacola

    A cracked heat exchanger or a failed control board on an older tankless is usually not worth repairing past the ten-year mark. We match the new unit to your gas line, vent type and mounting spot whenever we can. Most swaps take four to six hours.

    Tankless-to-Tankless Replacement in Pensacola

    Replacing an existing tankless with a new one is usually the simpler job, since the gas line, vent path and water connections are already there. We still check sizing against the new model. Corroded service valves get swapped while we are there.
