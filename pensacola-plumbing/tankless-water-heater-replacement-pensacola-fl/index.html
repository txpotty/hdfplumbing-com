    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Pensacola, FL

    Many homes have tankless heaters mounted on an exterior wall with no venting at all, since the unit exhausts directly outside. These units face sun, rain and temperature swings, which can break down plastic covers and wiring over time. Frozen pipes are rare in most of Florida, and many homes have outdoor plumbing that would never survive a northern winter. When we replace them, we check the pipe cover, freeze protection settings and the electrical connection. We can add a protective pipe cover kit to shield the water and gas lines. Exterior models are often the simplest swap when the gas line is already sized correctly.

    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Pensacola

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by capturing heat from exhaust gases. They vent through low-cost PVC and produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized and drained properly. We install a neutralizer cartridge and route the condensate line to an approved drain. Salt air in coastal parts of Florida corrodes outdoor fittings and exposed metal pipe faster than in inland areas. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes. We explain the cost difference so you can decide if the efficiency gain is worth it.

    The cost of tankless water heater replacement in Pensacola, FL usually ranges from $3,000 to $6,000, depending on the unit's capacity, brand, complexity of installation, such as converting from a tank model, and any needed electrical or gas line upgrades.     We install Navien, Rinnai, Noritz, Takagi and Rheem tankless heaters. Each has strengths, and we recommend based on your flow needs, venting options and budget.

    To protect the life and efficiency of your new tankless water heater, regular maintenance is key, especially in areas with hard water.
