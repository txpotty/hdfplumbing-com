    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Pensacola

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and the honest answer depends on the job. PEX-A bends tighter, recovers from a kink with a heat gun, and its expansion fittings flow more water, while PEX-B costs less and its crimp joints are just as reliable when they are gauged properly. Either one lasts for decades. For tight attics we lean toward PEX-A.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Pensacola, FL

    Think of a manifold like an electrical panel, but for water lines. What you get back is very few hidden fittings and steadier pressure when two people shower at the same time.

    Pinhole leaks in copper tend to come in clusters. Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving heavy scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. Converting to PEX stops the cycle. We use listed transition fittings anywhere PEX ties into copper that stays.
