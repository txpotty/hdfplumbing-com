    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Homestead, FL

    Exterior-mount tankless units hang on an outside wall and vent straight out the front, so there is no vent pipe to run. Covers and wiring take a beating outdoors. Frozen pipes are rare in most of Florida, and many homes have outdoor plumbing that would never survive a northern winter. We check the freeze protection settings and the pipe cover kit on every outdoor swap. Often the simplest job we do.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Homestead, FL

    Navien NPE units are condensing, and the A models have a built-in recirculation pump and buffer tank that cut down on cold-water sandwiches. Navien also makes combi units that heat the house and the water. We set up both.

    Commercial Tankless Racks in Homestead, FL

    Restaurants and salons run tankless racks hard. Four units together can pull over 600,000 BTU, which means checking gas pressure with all of them running. We work after hours when needed.

    Like-for-Like Tankless Swaps in Homestead

    A cracked heat exchanger or a failed control board on an older tankless is usually not worth repairing past the ten-year mark. The replacement gets matched to the existing gas, vent and mount where possible. Most swaps take four to six hours.

    Tank to Tankless Conversion in Homestead, FL

    Converting from a tank to tankless takes a full day in most homes. Expect a bigger gas line, a new vent run and a wall-mounted spot for the heater. The old flue gets capped.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Homestead, FL

    Electric tankless water heaters skip the gas line entirely, but a whole-house model can need 120 to 160 amps on two to four double-pole breakers. Many panels cannot spare that. We check panel capacity and wire gauge before quoting.

    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Homestead

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE or Rinnai Sensei pass 90 percent efficiency by pulling extra heat out of the exhaust. The vent is plain PVC. Acidic condensate goes through a neutralizer and then to an approved drain. Humidity causes condensation on cold lines and clogged AC condensate drains, a common source of hidden water damage. The savings carry through every season because hot water use does.

Run two showers and the dishwasher together and you are near 8 GPM. The unit is chosen from that figure.
