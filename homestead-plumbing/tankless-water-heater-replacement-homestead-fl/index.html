    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Homestead, FL

    Many homes have tankless heaters mounted on an exterior wall with no venting at all, since the unit exhausts directly outside. When we replace them, we check the pipe cover, freeze protection settings and the electrical connection. We can add a protective pipe cover kit to shield the water and gas lines. Exterior models are often the easiest swap when the gas line is already sized correctly.

    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Homestead

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by capturing heat from exhaust gases. They vent through inexpensive PVC and produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized and drained properly. We install a neutralizer cartridge and route the condensate line to an approved drain. Constant heat and humidity are the main plumbing stress in Florida, keeping water heaters, AC drain lines, and outdoor pipes working year round. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes. We explain the cost difference so you can decide if the efficiency gain is worth it.
