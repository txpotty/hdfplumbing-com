    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Homestead, FL

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. HDF Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. A metered control valve regenerates only when needed, which keeps salt and water use down. They work best on very hard water.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Homestead

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. Our RO installs go under the sink with their own faucet, and we can tie in the fridge or ice maker too. Pairs well with a softener. We can handle filter changes.

    Culligan Water Softener Installation in Homestead

    Culligan systems are common in homes around here, and we install and connect them. Each install follows the brand's specs for drain, bypass and power, then we program it to your water test. We handle all of it. Existing loops make it faster.

    Water Softener Replacement Services in Homestead, FL

    Worn-out softeners usually show it in one of two ways, hard water at the tap or a brine tank that never drops. We remove the old unit, check the loop and drain for problems and install a newer model that fits your current hardness and household size. We haul away the old one. The work is usually done in a few hours.

    Fleck Water Softener Installation in Homestead, FL

    Fleck valves, especially the 5600SXT and 7000SXT, are on a lot of the softeners we install. Their parts are easy to find and the valves can be rebuilt instead of replaced, so the system lasts. We program yours to your water. We confirm the setup before leaving.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Homestead

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. Instead of removing hardness, they convert it into a form that resists sticking, which reduces scale without salt or brine waste. Maintenance is light. They work best on moderate water.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Homestead

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. We follow the Ecodyne manual for the bypass, drain and overflow connections, then program hardness and regeneration settings for your water. Bring your own unit. Programming is part of the job.
