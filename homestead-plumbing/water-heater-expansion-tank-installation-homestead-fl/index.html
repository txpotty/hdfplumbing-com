    A dripping temperature and pressure relief valve frequently means thermal expansion is pushing pressure too high. A failed or missing expansion tank is the most common cause.

    Yes, expansion tanks need occasional maintenance to keep working correctly. It is recommended to have the air pressure checked annually to match the incoming water pressure. This preventive check helps extend the life of the tank and your water heater.

    Signs of a failing expansion tank include water dripping from the tank's air valve, rust or corrosion on the tank's exterior or a water heater that frequently cycles on and off.
