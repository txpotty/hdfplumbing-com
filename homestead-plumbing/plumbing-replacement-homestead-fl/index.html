    Many sewer line replacements take one to three days depending on length, depth and method. Trenchless options can speed up the timeline.

    We install water heaters from Bradford White, Rheem and A.O. Smith, tankless units from Rinnai and Navien, and quality softeners and well pumps from trusted manufacturers.

    Replacing old pipes with PEX or copper offers numerous benefits. PEX is flexible, corrosion-resistant and less prone to bursting in freezes, while copper is durable and long-lasting. Both improve water quality, eliminate leaks from old pipes and can significantly boost water pressure and flow throughout your Homestead home.

    Frequent leaks, rusty or discolored water, low pressure, recurring backups and equipment over 10 years old are typical signs. We can inspect and give you an honest assessment.

    While small fixes might be possible, major plumbing replacements like water heaters, sewer lines or full repipes are complex and best handled by licensed professionals.     Climate, freezes and soil type all affect how long plumbing lasts. Constant heat and humidity are the main plumbing stress in Florida, keeping water heaters, AC drain lines, and exterior pipes working year round. Freeze-thaw cycles can damage pipes, and corrosive or shifting soils can speed up pipe degradation. A high water table across much of Florida affects drainage, septic design, and how easily crews can dig for underground repairs. Our replacement solutions are chosen to withstand these local conditions for lasting durability.
