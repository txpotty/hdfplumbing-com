    Hydro-Jetting Services in Homestead, FL

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful method for clearing severe sewer line clogs, especially those caused by grease, scale, or heavy sludge buildup. This process uses highly pressurized water jets to scour the inside of your pipes, not only clearing the blockage but also cleaning the pipe walls. It is an excellent preventative maintenance tool and is particularly effective for older pipes in Homestead that may have collected years of debris. This technique is safe for most pipe materials.

    Tree Root Sewer Clogs in Homestead

    Roots from thirsty trees and shrubs seek out moisture and push into sewer pipe joints. Clay and older cast iron pipe are especially prone to root intrusion because of their many joints. We cut roots with a sewer machine fitted with root saws and then camera the line to see how much damage remains. For ongoing root problems, we can recommend a root treatment schedule or lining.

    Cleaning vs. Repairing a Sewer Line in Homestead, FL

    If the camera shows a sound pipe with a grease or root clog, cleaning is usually all you need. Cracked, offset or collapsed pipe will keep clogging no matter how often it is cleared. Cleaning typically costs a few hundred dollars, while repairs range from a spot fix to trenchless lining or full replacement. We walk you through the footage and costs so you can decide.

    Sewer Belly and Sagging Pipe Clogs in Homestead, FL

    Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and damage buried water and sewer lines. Soil that swells, shrinks or settles can cause sections of sewer pipe to sink and form a belly. Waste and water collect in the low spot and lead to repeat clogs. Our camera and locator show where the belly is and how deep it sits. We can then quote a spot repair to restore proper slope.

    Grease and Wipes Sewer Blockages in Homestead, FL

    Cooking grease cools and hardens in the main line, and so-called flushable wipes do not break down the way toilet paper does. Together they form dense plugs that a cable may only punch a hole through. Hydro jetting at high pressure scours the pipe walls clean and flushes debris downstream. We also share simple habits that help avoid another grease or wipe clog.
