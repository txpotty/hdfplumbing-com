    Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Homestead, FL

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. High pressure ages fixtures fast. Anything over 80 psi cuts the life of hoses, fixtures and heaters, and we note the supply pipe type, from copper and CPVC to poly, Kitec and galvanized.

    Preventive Maintenance Checks in Homestead, FL

    A maintenance check is a lighter version of a full inspection for owners who want to stay ahead of problems. The visit covers flushing the heater, working the shutoffs, reading pressure and checking for slow drains and small leaks. It takes about an hour.     Sewer Line Camera Inspections in Homestead, FL

    A sewer camera shows the inside of the main drain without digging.     Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Homestead, FL

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. We identify copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, polybutylene and Kitec, and we note the known failure patterns for each one. Some need replacing soon. Poly and Kitec fail suddenly and galvanized chokes off with rust, so we flag them with replacement options.

    Water Heater and Softener Inspection in Homestead

    We check the water heater's age by serial number, look for rust, leaks and sediment and test the relief valve. Venting and gas connections get checked. We also look at the expansion tank, drain pan and softener, and a hardness test shows whether the softener is working or just eating salt.

    Water Heater Safety Inspection in Homestead

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. Our checks include the relief valve and its discharge line, the expansion tank and the drain pan. Gas units get a CO check. A heater that backdrafts can spill combustion gases into the house, so we test the draft at the vent hood and note any problems right away.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbing Inspection in Homestead

    Most home plumbing inspections run $150 to $350, depending on the size and age of the house. Sewer video is an add-on. Adding a sewer camera inspection raises the total, and we tell you the full price before the visit so there is nothing new on the invoice when we finish.
