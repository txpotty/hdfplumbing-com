    Pedestal Sump Pump Check-Ups in Homestead, FL

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the water, so the wear shows up in the shaft, the bearings and the float rod. We check the rod for bends and the stops for slipping. Bearings get a listen. A motor that whines or rattles on startup is usually close to done.

    Annual Sump Pump Check-Ups in Homestead

    Once a year is the minimum. We check the motor, impeller, float and discharge line on every annual visit. After that, we fill the basin and time the drawdown, since a slow pump-out is the first hint of a tired impeller or a restricted line.

    Pit Cleaning and Liner Checks in Homestead

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, leaves and gravel settle at the bottom of the basin. That debris gets pulled into the intake and jams the impeller. We pump the basin dry, rinse the screen and check the plastic liner for cracks, since a cracked liner lets soil wash in from the sides. Takes about twenty minutes.

    Backup Pump Battery Service in Homestead

    Outages and heavy rain tend to arrive together. The backup takes over when the primary loses power or simply stops. Our plumber load tests the battery, tests the charger and alarm, then runs the backup alone with the primary unplugged. Expect a new battery every three to five years.
