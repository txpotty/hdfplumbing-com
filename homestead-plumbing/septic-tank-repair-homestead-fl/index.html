    Septic Tank Leak Repair in Homestead

    A tank that never fills to the outlet, or one that fills slowly with groundwater after rain, has a leak. After pumping, we inspect the walls and the joints with a strong light, and dye flushed down a fixture shows us where the water moves. Hairline cracks get a hydraulic cement patch. Split tanks get replaced.

    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Homestead, FL

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones get eaten by sulfuric acid from sewer gas and eventually fall off into the tank. With no outlet baffle, sludge flows right out to the leach lines. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Septic Lid and Riser Repair in Homestead

    Old concrete lids crack, and a cracked lid over a 1,000-gallon tank is a real danger to kids and pets. We replace failing lids and add risers that bring access up to the lawn. Future pumping gets cheaper. Every lid we set is screwed down or locked so a child cannot lift it.

    Septic Alarm Troubleshooting in Homestead

    A septic alarm sounding is the system's way of saying the water is too high in the pump chamber or the aerobic unit. Press the silence button and cut back on water. Our tech confirms the pump runs and looks for a float snagged on its cord. A tripped breaker is the cheapest answer.

    Leach Field Restoration in Homestead, FL

    Before we condemn a leach field, we open the distribution box and look at how evenly it splits the flow. A tilted or crushed D-box can send everything to one trench and drown it. Leveling or replacing the box often brings the field back. Some fields are past saving. For those, we explain the design and permit steps.

    Raw sewage coming up through a tub drain carries bacteria you do not want on your skin or your floors. Keep kids and pets away from it and stop all water use. Our tech clears the line or calls in a pump truck, depending on what we find. Cleanup guidance comes with it.
