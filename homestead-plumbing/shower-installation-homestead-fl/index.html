    Tiled Walk-In Shower Installation in Homestead

    Frameless glass walk-ins are popular, and the plumbing behind them has to be dead on. We install the valve, the head, an optional handheld and the drain, then set the mortar bed or foam pan to slope toward the drain at a quarter inch per foot. Then we test. The valve gets a pressure test and the pan a 24-hour flood test before tile.

    Shower Stall Installation in Homestead

    A one-piece stall needs a level, fully supported base. Fiberglass and acrylic bases go down on a mortar bed so they cannot flex, and flex is the usual reason a base cracks early. The drain connects from below. We check the flow at the head once the trim is on.

    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Homestead, FL

    Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common jobs. We pull the tub, move the drain from the end of the tub to where the shower pan needs it and raise the valve to shower height, usually around 48 inches off the floor. Slab homes need concrete work. No more stepping over a tub wall.

    Gym and Locker Room Showers in Homestead

    Gym showers run back to back from open to close. Commercial valves with metered or push-button controls keep run times in check, and the drains are sized for heavy use. Accessible stalls get handhelds on slide bars. We phase the work to keep some showers running.

    Budget Shower Upgrades in Homestead, FL

    Budget showers can still be built right. A fiberglass or acrylic unit with a quality pressure-balance valve costs far less than a tiled shower, and when it is set on a mortar bed with a sealed drain, it lasts decades. Spend on the valve. You get the full price before we start.

    Shower Upgrades Without a Full Remodel in Homestead

    Not every upgrade needs demolition. Replacing an old pan, converting a tub to a shower or swapping in a new valve and trim can make a big difference without tearing the bathroom down to the studs. You see each option's price. Most of these finish in a few days.

    Digital Shower Systems in Homestead, FL

    Digital shower systems like U by Moen and Kohler DTV need a little more planning. The digital valve mounts in an accessible spot, often in a vanity or a closet, and needs low-voltage wiring to the controller plus a dedicated outlet near the valve. An electrician handles the outlet. Our part is the plumbing.

    Emergency Shower System Replacement in Homestead, FL
