    What a Water Heater Service Visit Covers in Lauderhill, FL

    Tank Flush and Inspection in Lauderhill

    Sediment forms from minerals in the water. We flush the tank, test the relief valve, check the anode and look over each fitting. Efficiency comes back. So does recovery time.

    Flushing a Tank Water Heater in Lauderhill

    Popping and rumbling mean sediment. Calcium and magnesium settle on the bottom of the tank and form a layer that traps heat under it, so water boils in pockets beneath the scale and pops every time the burner or lower element fires. The tank drains until the water is clean. Heavy buildup may take several cycles.

    Gas Burner and Venting Checks in Lauderhill

    A blue flame is what we want to see. Yellow or lazy flames mean poor combustion, so we clean the burner and combustion chamber, check the thermocouple or flame sensor, and confirm the vent has a proper draft and tight connections. Bad venting is dangerous. We test the gas connection too.

    Anode Rod Checks in Lauderhill

    The anode rod rusts so the tank does not. It is a long metal rod screwed into the top of the tank, and once it has corroded down to bare wire, the water starts eating the steel tank lining instead. It comes out for inspection. Replacing the rod is much cheaper than replacing the heater.

    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Lauderhill

    Electric tanks fail at the lower element. Sediment piles up around the lower element, it runs hotter than it should, and eventually it burns out, which is why you suddenly get only a few minutes of hot water. We test both elements. With power off, we check thermostats and connections.

    Electric Element Service in Lauderhill, FL

    Two elements, two thermostats. A burned-out lower element is the usual cause of running short on hot water. We test with power off. We only replace what failed.

    Tankless Descaling in Lauderhill, FL

    Scale is the tankless unit's main enemy. Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving stubborn scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. A pump runs descaler through the exchanger for the recommended time, followed by a clean water flush. We clean the inlet filter. Igniter, burner and venting get a safety check.

    Tankless Water Heater Service in Lauderhill, FL

    Tankless units need descaling. Mineral scale coats the inside of the heat exchanger, restricting flow and making the unit work harder, and eventually it starts throwing error codes or cutting out in the middle of a shower. A pump circulates descaler through it. We also check the igniter, the fan and the exhaust vent for safe operation.
