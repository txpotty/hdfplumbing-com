    Floor Drain and Backwater Valve Repair in Lauderhill

    A floor drain that backs up during heavy use or a storm usually points to a blockage downstream or a failed backwater valve. After clearing the line, we run a camera to confirm it is open. Where the city sewer surcharges in storms, a backwater valve on the building drain keeps sewage from coming up through the floor drain, and it needs its flapper cleaned once a year. Dry traps smell.

    Wall Leak Solutions in Lauderhill, FL

    Damp walls without a crack often point to water soaking through porous block or concrete. Tape foil to the wall for a full day, and if the room side is wet it is condensation, not a leak. Condensation needs a dehumidifier. Seepage needs drainage outside or a channel inside.

    Clogged Drain Tile Repair in Lauderhill

    Footing drains clog with silt and roots over the years, and once they stop flowing the water table at the wall rises. A camera run from the sump inlet shows us where the tile is plugged. Jetting clears it. Crushed clay tile has to be dug up and replaced with perforated PVC or corrugated pipe in a sock.

    Groundwater Pressure Relief in Lauderhill

    High groundwater pushes up through the slab as well as in through the walls. Water rising through floor cracks or around a floor drain during wet weather is the usual sign. Under-slab drain pipe tied to a sump lowers the water level beneath the floor. It is not a quick job.
