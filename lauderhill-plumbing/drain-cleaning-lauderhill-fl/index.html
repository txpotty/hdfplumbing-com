    Hydro Jetting for Drains in Lauderhill

    Jetting does what a cable cannot. Water at around 3,000 to 4,000 PSI scours the whole pipe wall. A cable punches a hole, while a jetter cleans the pipe back to near its original diameter. It works best on a kitchen or main line that keeps clogging every few months no matter how often it gets cabled.

    Tub and Shower Drain Cleaning in Lauderhill, FL

    Showers clog slowly, then all at once. Hair builds a mat under the strainer.     Jetting Versus Cabling in Lauderhill, FL

    Cabling and jetting solve different problems. A cable cuts through a blockage. A jetter scrubs the pipe wall clean and flushes the debris downstream to the city main or septic tank, so we cable first on a hard root mass and jet afterward when the line has years of buildup on it.
