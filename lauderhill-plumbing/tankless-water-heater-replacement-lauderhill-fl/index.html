    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Lauderhill, FL

    Many homes have tankless heaters mounted on an exterior wall with no venting at all, since the unit exhausts directly outside. These units face sun, rain and temperature swings, which can break down plastic covers and wiring over time. Frozen pipes are rare in most of Florida, and many homes have outdoor plumbing that would never hold up in a northern winter. When we replace them, we check the pipe cover, freeze protection settings and the electrical connection. We can add a protective pipe cover kit to guard the water and gas lines. Exterior models are often the simplest swap when the gas line is already sized correctly.

    Like-for-Like Tankless Swaps in Lauderhill

    When your existing tankless has a cracked heat exchanger or a failed control board that is not worth repairing, a direct replacement is often the fastest fix. We match the new unit to your current gas line, venting type and mounting location whenever possible. Many Rinnai and Navien models share similar footprints, which keeps wall patching to a minimum. We also replace the old service valves if they are corroded or stuck. Most like-for-like swaps are finished in four to six hours.

    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Lauderhill

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by capturing heat from exhaust gases. They vent through inexpensive PVC and produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized and drained properly. We install a neutralizer cartridge and route the condensate line to an approved drain. Humidity causes condensation on cold lines and clogged AC condensate drains, a common source of hidden water damage. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes. We explain the cost difference so you can decide if the efficiency gain is worth the price.

    Commercial Tankless Replacement in Lauderhill

    Restaurants, salons and small offices often use multiple tankless units linked together on a rack system.
