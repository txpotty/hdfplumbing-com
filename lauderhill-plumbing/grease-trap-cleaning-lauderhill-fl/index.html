    Everything in the trap comes out, from the floating grease cap down to the sludge of food solids on the floor. Haulers call it brown grease. It is different from the yellow grease in your used fryer oil bin, which is recycled separately and should never be poured into the trap.

    It goes to a permitted receiving facility. Some facilities process it for biofuel or digesters. The rest is dewatered and disposed of under the facility's permit, and every load we haul is logged so the trail runs from your trap to the gate.

    Fryer volume drives the schedule more than seat count. Monthly pumping is common for busy restaurants. Lighter kitchens, like a coffee shop with a small food menu, can stretch to every two or three months as long as depth readings stay under the 25 percent line.
