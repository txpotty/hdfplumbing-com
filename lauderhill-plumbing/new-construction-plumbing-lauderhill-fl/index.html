    We do, mostly on custom homes. We work from your architect's plans and suggest changes that shorten runs or improve flow. Stacking baths, moving the water heater closer to the main bath and adding a recirculation line are the usual ones. Small moves on paper save real pipe.

    We handle the plumbing permit work and inspections on our scope. The general contractor normally holds the main permit. We book every plumbing inspection, meet the inspector, and clear corrections before the next trade needs the wall.

    For supply, we use PEX-A or PEX-B in most homes, sometimes with a copper main. Drains are PVC or ABS. Some builders want cast iron in walls beside bedrooms, since it is far quieter than PVC when an upstairs toilet flushes.

    We install Moen, Kohler, Delta and American Standard, plus most other major brands a builder or designer specifies. Homeowner-supplied fixtures are fine. Have them delivered before trim so we can open each box, since a missing part or wrong finish can delay trim a week.
