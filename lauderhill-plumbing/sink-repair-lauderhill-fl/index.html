    Kitchen Sink Repair in Lauderhill

    Kitchen sinks take the most abuse in the house.     Dripping Faucet Repair in Lauderhill

    A faucet that drips once a second wastes thousands of gallons a year. Older compression faucets need a washer and seat, and newer cartridge or disc faucets need the cartridge itself or its seals. We carry Moen, Delta, Pfister and Kohler cartridges. Odd brands take a day to order.

    Slow or Clogged Sink Drain Repair in Lauderhill, FL

    Slow drains almost always clog at the trap or the first few feet past the wall. Hydro-jetting is for main lines, not sink arms.

    Recurring Sink Clog Repair in Lauderhill, FL

    Monthly clogs have a cause past the trap. We run a camera or a longer cable into the wall arm and look for a sagging line or buildup where the sink ties into the tub drain. Now and then it is a plugged vent. A sink that gurgles as it drains is the giveaway.

    Repair or Replace? Sink Costs in Lauderhill, FL
