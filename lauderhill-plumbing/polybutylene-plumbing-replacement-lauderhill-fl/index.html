    Why Polybutylene Fails in Lauderhill

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. Over the years, the pipe and its acetal fittings get brittle from the inside, then crack along the inner wall until one splits under normal pressure. Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving heavy scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. Hot water speeds it up. Hot lines and fittings near the water heater fail more than anything else.

    Finding Polybutylene Leaks in Lauderhill

    A poly leak inside a wall or ceiling can run for days before you see a stain. The meter often tells first. If the meter's leak dial moves with everything off, we track the leak down with acoustic equipment and thermal imaging instead of guessing. Finding it lets us make a temporary repair and plan the full replacement.

    Polybutylene Replacement Costs in Lauderhill, FL

    Replacing polybutylene with PEX in an average home often costs $5,000 to $12,000, depending on size and bathrooms. Copper systems cost more.     Fitting Failure and Emergency Repair in Lauderhill

    Acetal plastic and copper crimp ring fittings are often the first part of a poly system to fail. A burst fitting in an attic or wall can cause major damage in minutes. After an emergency repair we schedule the full replacement, since removing the poly system is the only lasting fix.
