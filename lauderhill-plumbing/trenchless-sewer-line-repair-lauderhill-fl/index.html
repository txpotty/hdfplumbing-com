    Pipe Bursting in Lauderhill, FL

    Pipe bursting replaces a badly damaged line by pulling a bursting head through the old pipe. The head breaks the old pipe outward while new HDPE pipe follows behind it. Bursting is best for collapsed or severely offset lines that cannot be lined. It usually requires an entry pit and an exit pit.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in Lauderhill

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method where a new pipe is pulled through the old one while the old pipe is fractured outward. This creates a new, seamless and often larger diameter sewer line without the need for extensive trenching. It is best for severely damaged or collapsed pipes in Lauderhill that require full replacement rather than just relining.

    Sectional Point Repair in Lauderhill

    When only one section of a line is damaged, a point repair can fix it without lining the whole length. We position a short resin liner at the damaged spot using the camera and inflate it until it cures. Point repairs are a cost-effective fix for a single crack, hole or root entry. They are common on otherwise healthy lines.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Lauderhill

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one while breaking apart the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene pipe, which is highly resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in older Lauderhill homes. This method is ideal when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.

    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Lauderhill

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg fiber pipe or vitrified clay sewer laterals. Orangeburg flattens and blisters over time, while clay cracks and separates at joints. Pipe bursting is often the best option for Orangeburg, and lining can work well for clay that still holds its shape. We identify the material on camera before recommending a method.
