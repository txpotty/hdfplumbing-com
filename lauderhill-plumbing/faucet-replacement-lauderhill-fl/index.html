    Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving stubborn scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. Finish and body material decide how a faucet ages.     Delta faucets come in a few cartridge families, and the model number tells you which.     Corroded Faucet Replacement in Lauderhill

    Green crust at the base means the faucet body is corroding. Once the brass under the finish is pitted, cartridges stop sealing against it, and every repair drips again within a few weeks. Replacement is the only fix. We pull it, even with seized nuts, and set a new faucet with a ceramic disc cartridge.

    Utility Sink Faucet Replacement in Lauderhill

    Utility faucets in garages and laundry rooms take hard use. Buckets hung on the spout and hoses yanked off the threads loosen the faucet and eventually crack its supply joints. Brass bodies hold up best here. We mount it tight to the wall or tub deck.

    Moen Faucet Replacement in Lauderhill

    Moen's 1225 cartridge runs a huge share of single-handle Moen faucets. When it sticks, the handle gets stiff and the temperature drifts, and a cartridge that has been in place for fifteen years can be welded in by scale. Sometimes pulling it cracks the body. At that point a new faucet is the fix.
