    Choosing a Backup Battery in Spring Hill

    Battery choice decides how long the backup actually runs. Most backups use a sealed AGM or deep-cycle battery around 75 to 100 amp-hours, which covers several hours of on-and-off pumping. We ask how often your pump runs in a storm and size the battery to match. Plan on a new battery every three to five years.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Spring Hill

    If your pump runs every 30 seconds during a storm and still loses ground, it is undersized for the water coming in. First we time the pump cycles and measure the pit refill rate. The rest is simple arithmetic. A half-horse or three-quarter-horse pump with a higher gallons-per-minute rating at your lift height may be the answer, sometimes paired with a 2-inch discharge.

    Sump Basin and Pit Replacement in Spring Hill, FL

    Old clay tile or 18-inch plastic basins crack and fill with silt, and a pump in a basin that small cycles every few minutes during a heavy rain. The fix is to cut the slab, drop in a bigger poly basin with a bolted lid, and patch the concrete. More volume in the pit means fewer starts per hour. Fewer starts mean the motor lasts longer.

    Sizing a Submersible Sump Pump in Spring Hill

    Horsepower matters less than people think. For most houses a 1/3 HP pump is enough, provided the water only has to climb 10 to 15 feet to get out. We check the flow chart against your actual lift before we pick a size. A pump that is too big empties the pit too fast and wears itself out.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Spring Hill

    Stuck floats and leaking check valves cause more sump failures than dead motors do. When the check valve leaks, the water in the pipe drops back into the basin and the pump has to move it twice, which shortens its life. New valve every time. In a tight pit we use a vertical switch, and in a wide pit a tethered float.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Spring Hill

    A submersible pump sits at the bottom of the pit, sealed in an oil-filled housing, and it runs quieter than anything else you can put down there. We pull the old unit, scoop the gravel and silt out of the basin, and set the new pump on a flat brick so it does not suck up debris. The check valve gets replaced at the same time. Skip that part and the pump short cycles.

    Pedestal Pump Service in Spring Hill, FL

    Louder, yes, but a pedestal motor sits high and dry, so in a narrow pit it often outlasts a submersible by several years. Narrow pits favor pedestals. Worn units get replaced, and we reset the float rod stops so the cycle is right. If your pit is wide enough, a submersible may serve you better.

    Yard Drainage Pit Pumps in Spring Hill, FL

    Yard drainage pits and window well pumps can sit dry from fall to spring and then seize the first time a big storm fills them. We swap in a corrosion-resistant pump and clear out leaves and silt. We route the outlet away from the house. A bucket test every spring catches problems early.
