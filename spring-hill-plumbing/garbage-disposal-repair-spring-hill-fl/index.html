    Cost-Effective Disposal Repair in Spring Hill, FL

    A disposal repair that costs half of a new unit rarely makes sense, and we will say so. Plenty of repairs cost little. Resetting an overload, freeing a jam, replacing a switch or resealing a flange usually costs well under the price of a replacement and keeps a younger unit running for years.

    Flange and Inlet Leak Repair in Spring Hill, FL

    Leaks show up at the flange, dishwasher inlet, discharge tube or body. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty and replace gaskets or tubes as needed. A leak from the bottom of the unit usually means the internal seal has failed, and in that case replacement is the better option. It is not a repairable part.

    Humming Disposal Repair in Spring Hill

    Humming with no spin usually means something is stuck. Bones, pits, glass and spoons are the usual suspects. We cut power, work the flywheel free with a hex wrench on the bottom of the unit, remove the object and then test the motor and the reset button with water running.

    InSinkErator and Other Brand Repairs in Spring Hill, FL

    Every brand, from InSinkErator to Waste King and Moen, mounts differently, so a flange leak is fixed with that brand's kit. InSinkErator uses a three-bolt or Quick Lock style ring, while Waste King uses an EZ Mount twist ring that comes off without tools. We stock the common kits. That keeps most repairs to one visit.

    Electrical Issues and Motor Repair in Spring Hill

    A disposal that hums but will not grind is getting power but is stuck, while one that is silent has no power at all. The two need different fixes. For a silent unit we test the breaker, reset and switch, and for a humming one we check the flywheel and motor before deciding if it is worth repairing.

    Electrical Problems and Resetting in Spring Hill

    No hum at all usually means no power reaching the motor. We start at the red reset button on the bottom, then check the breaker, the wall switch and the outlet or junction box under the sink with a meter. It is wet under a sink. Corroded wire nuts and loose cord connections are common in that damp space, and we replace them rather than just tightening them back down.

    Jammed Disposal Repair in Spring Hill

    A jam usually comes from one hard object, like a peach pit, a chicken bone or a twist tie wrapped around the impeller. We free the flywheel from below with the hex key and pull the object with pliers, never a hand, then run it with water to make sure nothing else is stuck. We press the reset after clearing it. Check the trap arm if it jams again.

    Emergency Disposal and Sink Backup Repair in Spring Hill

    A failed disposal can push water back into the sink and dishwasher. If water is rising or reaching the floor, shut off the disposal and the dishwasher and call us, since running either one just adds more water to the cabinet. We move quickly on these.
