    Rural properties often run water heaters on propane instead of natural gas.     Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Spring Hill

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust out through PVC pipe. Failures often involve the blower motor, pressure switch, hot surface igniter or condensate in the vent. We read the error codes, test each part and repair the fault. Correct vent slope and termination are verified before we leave.
