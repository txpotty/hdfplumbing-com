    Leaky Shower Valve Repair in Spring Hill

    One drip every few seconds adds up to hundreds of gallons a year. Single-handle showers drip from a worn cartridge or O-rings, while old two-handle valves drip from a washer and a pitted seat.     Shower Handle Leak Repair in Spring Hill

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water leaking behind the trim slips into the wall cavity at the valve opening and soaks the drywall while the tile face stays dry. The trim comes off first. It is usually a loose bonnet nut or a worn O-ring.

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Spring Hill, FL

    Stains on the ceiling below are often the first clue. A meter on the walls and a flood test on the pan show whether the leak is the valve, the pan or the drain. The source gets repaired. On slab homes, these leaks can spread into the flooring of the next room before anyone sees them.

    Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Spring Hill, FL

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. We pull the strainer and run a hand auger through the trap, and if the clog sits past the trap in the branch line, we switch to a drum machine with a smaller cable. Drain cleaner usually misses the clog. A hair catcher over the strainer keeps the next one from forming.

    Same-Day Shower Leak Repair in Spring Hill, FL

    Shut off the main first if water is getting where it should not. For a sudden wall leak or a valve that will not close, the main valve near the meter is the first move. Then call us. We stop the leak, make the repair or a safe temporary fix and check for anything that needs drying.

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Spring Hill

    Showerheads clog from the inside out. Scale builds behind the face plate and in the flow restrictor, so the spray goes uneven, shoots sideways or drops to a trickle even when the valve is wide open. Soaking helps sometimes. Otherwise we put on a new head with rubber nozzles that rub clean with your thumb.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Spring Hill

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. Water that keeps coming out after the handle is off has to be getting past a worn cartridge or seat, so replacing the showerhead alone will not stop it. We fix the valve. Leaks at the arm threads are different, and those get new thread tape and a snug turn.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Spring Hill, FL

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Scale packs the nozzles and the restrictor screen behind them, and the spray drops off a little at a time until one day it feels like a garden mister. A soak can bring it back. If the cartridge is clogged too, we replace it and test the flow again.
