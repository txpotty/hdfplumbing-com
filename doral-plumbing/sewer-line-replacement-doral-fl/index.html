    Upgrading Old Sewer Pipe Materials in Doral

    Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg all fail in their own ways. Clay joints let roots in every few feet, cast iron scales and rusts through along the bottom, and Orangeburg tar paper pipe slowly flattens into an oval. Modern plastic pipe avoids all three problems. HDPE is fused into one continuous piece with no joints for roots to find.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Replacement in Doral, FL

    Orangeburg is tar-soaked wood fiber. Many mid-century houses got it, and by now most of it has blistered and flattened enough to snag waste. Clay is tougher but leaks roots. Several bad spots on one run usually make full replacement the better buy.
