    Frequently Asked Questions About Kitchen Sink Installation in Doral

    A basic drop-in kitchen sink install usually costs $300 to $600 for labor. Undermount and farmhouse installs usually run $450 to $1,200, and adding a disposal or relocating the drain adds $150 to $600.

    Top-mount (drop-in) sinks sit on top of the counter, with a visible rim, and are generally easier to install. Under-mount sinks are installed beneath the counter, creating a seamless look, but require more complex sealing and support for a secure installation.

    Many standard cabinets need reinforcement for heavy fireclay or cast iron sinks. We add a support frame so the sink is safe.

    Signs you might need a new kitchen sink include persistent leaks around the drain or edges, visible cracks or chips, severe staining that won't clean, rust spots, or a desire to upgrade your kitchen's look. If your current sink is old or damaged, replacement is often the best solution.

    Yes, HDF Plumbing stands behind all our work. We offer a warranty on our labor for kitchen sink installations, giving you peace of mind. Specific warranty details will be provided with your service agreement, covering any issues arising from our workmanship.

    When choosing a new kitchen sink, consider your kitchen's style, material durability, and whether you need features like multiple basins or a disposal. Also, think about water hardness in your area, as some materials might show mineral deposits more than others. Ensure the sink size is compatible with your cabinet and countertop.
