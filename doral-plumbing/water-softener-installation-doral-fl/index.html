    Fleck Water Softener Installation in Doral, FL

    Fleck valves, especially the 5600SXT and 7000SXT, are on a lot of the softeners we install. Their parts are easy to find and the valves can be rebuilt instead of replaced, so the system lasts. Settings match your test. We confirm the setup before leaving.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Doral

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. Compared with an older timer-based unit, a modern metered softener can use roughly half the salt and noticeably less regeneration water each month. The savings add up. We install several models.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Doral

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. Instead of removing hardness, they convert it into a form that resists sticking, which reduces scale without salt or brine waste. Maintenance is light. They suit moderate hardness.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Doral, FL

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. A carbon filter placed ahead of the softener removes those chemicals and protects the resin, since chlorine slowly breaks resin beads down over the years. You will taste it. We install twin-tank setups or dual-stage units.

    Softener Loop Installation in Doral

    No loop? A softener loop is a pair of capped stubs on the main line, usually in the garage, that lets a softener be connected without cutting pipe later. We add the loop. Outdoor taps can stay on hard water, which saves salt. Future service gets easier too.

    Water Softener Installation for New Construction in Doral, FL

    Building a home in Doral, FL? New construction is the cheapest time to add a softener loop, because the walls are open and the main can be routed past the garage or utility room without cutting finished drywall. We work with builders. We keep outdoor taps off the softener.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Doral

    Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water. We install salt-based softeners with a resin tank sized to your grains per gallon, a brine tank, a bypass valve and a drain line that ends at a code-approved air gap. Programming is the last step. We set regeneration overnight.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Doral

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. Every whole-house install gets a bypass valve, and we add a pressure reducing valve if incoming pressure is too high. Soft water reaches every fixture. We can skip the hose bibs.
