    Repiping Services We Handle in Doral, FL

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Doral, FL

    Polybutylene, a gray or blue plastic pipe installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s, breaks down with exposure to chlorine and other disinfectants in tap water. It fails without warning. Brittle pipe walls and cracked fittings mean one polybutylene leak usually signals risk across the whole house. We replace the full system with PEX or copper. Insurers often ask about it at renewal.

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Doral

    Old galvanized lines shed rust flakes that clog aerators, faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. That is often the first sign. Replacing galvanized with PEX or copper ends the rust, brings pressure back and stops the leaks that start at the threaded joints where galvanized is thinnest. Old pipe stays in walls when pulling it would wreck finished surfaces.

    Residential Repiping Plans in Doral

    Every house is piped a little differently, so a repipe starts with a walk-through. Our estimator counts fixtures, checks access, finds the main and asks about any planned remodels before choosing a route. Then we size the lines. A three-bath house with a tankless heater may need 1-inch main trunk lines to keep showers steady when the washer kicks on.

    Slab Leak Repiping in Doral

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and damage buried water and sewer lines. Instead of breaking concrete to patch one pipe, we reroute new supply lines overhead through the attic and down interior walls, then abandon the old lines in the slab. No jackhammer needed. It ends this leak and takes the rest of the slab plumbing out of the picture.

    Copper Repiping in Doral, FL

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. Our copper work uses thicker Type L pipe, joined with lead-free solder or ProPress fittings, rather than the Type M found in many older houses. Expect a slightly longer job. Most copper jobs run a day longer than PEX, since each turn needs its own fitting.

    Commercial Repiping in Doral, FL

    Commercial buildings in Doral, FL often have larger pipe, risers that serve several floors and tenants who cannot lose water for a full day. Our crew breaks commercial repipes into zones, working one riser or suite at a time while the rest of the building keeps water. Night work is possible. Materials are usually Type L copper or commercial PEX, sized to the fixture count and installed to commercial code.

    Condo, Townhome and Multi-Family Repipes in Doral

    Shared walls and HOA rules make repiping attached homes more involved. Our office works with your HOA on approvals and shutoff times, and we take steps to protect the units next door. Building-wide jobs go in phases. Big buildings get repiped by stack or wing, which lets residents stay home.

    Emergency Pipe Failures and Repipes in Doral

    Sometimes a house reaches the repipe point all at once, with a burst line and two more leaks right behind it. We stop the active leak first, make temporary repairs that get water back on and then plan the full repipe so you are not rushed into it. That gives you time. Insurance may cover the water damage from the burst even if it does not cover the repipe itself, so we document what we find with photos.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Repipe Specialists in Doral

    Most whole-home repipes in Doral, FL fall somewhere between $4,000 and $15,000. Bigger homes cost more. Square footage, fixture count, material choice and the amount of drywall work along the route set the final price. We quote it in writing.

    A repipe needs a permit, specialized tools, code knowledge and a passing pressure test. It is not a weekend job. Hidden leaks, failed inspections or a voided pipe warranty from a DIY job can easily cost more than a licensed crew would have.

    Having the repipe documented with a permit can make your home easier to sell and insure, especially when replacing polybutylene or Kitec. Inspectors look for it.

    We commonly use Uponor PEX-A with ProPEX fittings and Type L copper from domestic mills. We also use Viega ProPress fittings for copper work.

    We have to open some drywall, but the openings are small and planned. Most are about the size of a sheet of paper. Each cut is a clean square at a fixture or along the route, and we patch and texture all of them so the walls are ready for paint.

    Both work well, and the best choice depends on your house and your water. PEX is cheaper. PEX is flexible, handles a freeze better and resists scale, while copper is rigid, tolerates sunlight and has a long history, but it costs more and can pinhole in aggressive water.

    We provide a written workmanship warranty, and PEX makers such as Uponor offer long-term warranties when certified installers use their system. We explain the details before work begins.
