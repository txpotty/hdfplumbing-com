    Sewer Camera Inspection for Trenchless Repair in Doral

    Every trenchless job starts with a push camera fed in from the cleanout or a pulled toilet.     Pipe Bursting in Doral, FL

    Two pits are standard for a burst, one at the house end near the foundation and one out near the street or the city tap. The new HDPE pipe is butt-fused into one length first. The winch or hydraulic puller then drags the bursting head and the new pipe through. It goes quickly.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacements in Doral

    A bursting head is a steel cone, slightly wider than the old pipe, pulled through the line by a hydraulic unit or a winch cable. As it moves, it splits the old clay or cast iron and shoves the pieces into the surrounding soil while a string of new HDPE pipe follows right behind it. Fused joints, no gaps. This is the fix for collapsed pipe.

    Emergency Trenchless Sewer Services in Doral

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Doral

    Prep is most of the job. We jet the line to remove roots and grease, then camera it again to measure the exact footage before cutting the liner tube to length. A poor prep job means a wrinkled liner. Lining works best on cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their round shape.

    Causes of Sewer Line Failure in Doral, FL

    Shifting soil is one of the main reasons sewer joints separate, crack or drop out of grade over the years. Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and damage buried water and sewer lines. Roots are the other big one, since a hairline gap at a clay joint is enough for a feeder root to get in and then swell into a mat that catches paper. Age does the rest. Cast iron scales and thins, and clay bells crack under the weight of soil and traffic.
