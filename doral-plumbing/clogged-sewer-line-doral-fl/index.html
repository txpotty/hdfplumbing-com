    Hydro-Jetting Services in Doral, FL

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful method for clearing severe sewer line clogs, especially those caused by grease, scale, or heavy sludge buildup. This process uses highly pressurized water jets to scour the inside of your pipes, not only clearing the blockage but also cleaning the pipe walls. It is an effective preventative maintenance tool and is especially effective for older pipes in Doral that may have collected years of debris. This technique is safe for most pipe materials.

    Cleaning vs. Repairing a Sewer Line in Doral, FL

    If the camera shows a sound pipe with a grease or root clog, cleaning is usually all you need. Cracked, offset or collapsed pipe will keep clogging no matter how often it is cleared. Cleaning typically costs a few hundred dollars, while repairs range from a spot fix to trenchless lining or full replacement. We walk you through the footage and costs so you can decide.

    Sewer Belly and Sagging Pipe Clogs in Doral, FL

    Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and crack buried water and sewer lines. Soil that swells, shrinks or settles can cause sections of sewer pipe to sink and form a belly. Waste and water collect in the low spot and lead to repeat clogs. Our camera and locator show where the belly is and how deep it sits. We can then quote a spot repair to restore correct slope.

    Tree Root Removal Services in Doral

    Tree roots are a common cause of clogged sewer lines, especially in older Doral homes with mature landscaping.     Main Line Hydro Jetting in Doral

    Hydro jetting uses water at thousands of psi through a specialized nozzle to cut roots, grease and scale. It cleans the full diameter of the pipe rather than just boring a hole through the clog. We check the pipe condition with a camera first so jetting is only used on lines that can handle it. Many homeowners find that jetting buys them years before another clog.
