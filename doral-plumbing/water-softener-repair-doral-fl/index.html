    Valve, Resin and Brine Repair in Doral, FL

    Control Valve Troubleshooting & Repair in Doral, FL

    Stuck pistons, worn seals and failed drive motors cause most control valve trouble, and the symptoms are hard water, constant regeneration or a leak at the head. The valve gets tested in place. Seal kits and pistons for Fleck, Clack and Autotrol valves ride on the truck, so most rebuilds happen on the first visit.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Repair in Doral

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. Salt sits there unused, and regeneration fails. After breaking up the crust, we clear the sludge and test the float and brine well. Cracked tanks get replaced.

    Popular Water Softener Brand Repair in Doral

    GE, Whirlpool, Culligan, Kenmore, EcoPure and Morton make the popular softeners we repair most often, along with the private-label units from hardware stores. Every brand has its own valve layout and weak points. HDF Plumbing technicians know those designs, so diagnosis is quick. Make or model, the repair in your Doral garage is done right.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Doral

    Chlorine eats resin slowly, and after enough years the beads swell, break and stop exchanging the calcium and magnesium they are supposed to hold. Older systems on chlorinated city water see it most. HDF Plumbing pulls the old resin and fills the tank with new media. Capacity comes back right away.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Doral

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads and block the exchange. Depending on the test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced. Fresh resin brings capacity back.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Service in Doral, FL

    The tank looks full but makes no brine. A salt bridge has formed and stopped the salt from dissolving. Bridges get broken up, mushy salt gets cleaned out and the float, brine well and safety valve get checked. Pellet salt bridges less in warm or humid spaces, so we may suggest switching if your softener sits in a hot garage.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Doral, FL

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Its small ports clog with sediment and salt. A clogged injector leaves the brine tank full of water and the resin never gets recharged. We clean or replace it and check the brine line for air leaks.

    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Doral, FL

    A crust of salt across the brine tank stops regeneration, and hard water shows up at every tap within a day or two. We break up and remove the bridge and clean the tank. Older brine tanks can crack, and we repair or replace those too. Fill the tank halfway.
