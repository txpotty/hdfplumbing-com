    Well Pump Control Box & Electrical Repair in Fort Lauderdale, FL

    Many well pump failures stem from electrical issues within the control box or wiring. This can include tripped breakers, faulty capacitors, or corroded connections. Our licensed technicians are trained to safely diagnose and repair these complex electrical components. We make sure all wiring meets Florida electrical codes, preventing hazards and ensuring reliable pump operation in Fort Lauderdale homes.

    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Fort Lauderdale

    Submersible pumps are widely used in residential wells, designed to operate submerged within the well casing. When these pumps fail, symptoms often include no water, low water pressure, or strange noises from the well. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing issues like motor burnout, damaged impellers, or faulty check valves that can affect submersible pump performance. We use specialized equipment for safe and efficient pump extraction and reinstallation, ensuring the integrity of your well system.

    Well Pump Pressure Tank Issues in Fort Lauderdale

    The pressure tank works together with your well pump to maintain consistent water pressure and reduce pump cycling. A waterlogged tank, a ruptured diaphragm, or a faulty air valve can cause rapid pump cycling, fluctuating water pressure, or even no water at all. Our team accurately diagnoses pressure tank problems and performs necessary repairs or replacements. We make sure your tank is properly sized and charged for optimal system performance in Fort Lauderdale.

    Constant Pressure System Repair in Fort Lauderdale, FL

    Variable speed systems like the Grundfos SQE and Franklin SubDrive keep pressure steady by adjusting motor speed. When they fault out, the drive usually shows a code that guides diagnosis. We troubleshoot drives, sensors and wiring and repair or replace components. These systems need a smaller pressure tank but careful setup.

    Diagnosing Low Water Pressure in Fort Lauderdale

    Low water pressure from a well system can be a stubborn problem, often indicating issues beyond just the pump itself. This could be due to a failing pressure switch, a partially clogged well screen, a leak in the supply line, or even a pump that is no longer meeting demand. Our team conducts thorough diagnostics to pinpoint the exact cause of low pressure in your Fort Lauderdale home, delivering a targeted and effective repair solution.

Pulling and replacing a submersible pump can range from $1,800 to $5,000 or more, depending on depth, horsepower and wire. If your pump is older and already needs pulling, replacing it at the same time can save a second pull later. We lay out both options with honest numbers.
