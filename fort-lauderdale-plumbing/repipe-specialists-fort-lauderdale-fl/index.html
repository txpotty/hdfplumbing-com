    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Fort Lauderdale

    Slab leaks are a serious and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and crack buried water and sewer lines. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the damaged pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use modern detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.
