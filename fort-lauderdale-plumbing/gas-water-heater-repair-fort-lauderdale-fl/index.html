    Gas Control Valve Replacement in Fort Lauderdale, FL

    The gas control valve regulates temperature and shuts off gas if the pilot goes out. Newer valves from Honeywell and White-Rodgers use status lights that blink codes when something goes wrong. If the valve itself has failed, we replace it with an exact match or approved universal model. We leak test and confirm temperature control afterward.

    Propane Water Heater Repair in Fort Lauderdale

    Rural properties often run water heaters on propane instead of natural gas. Propane units use different orifices and regulator pressures, and incorrect conversions cause sooting and poor performance. We confirm the correct parts are installed and repair pilots, valves and burners. We also check tank regulators for proper pressure.

    Pilot Light and Ignition System Repair in Fort Lauderdale

    A frequent culprit for no hot water in gas heaters is a faulty pilot light or ignition system. Our technicians are experienced in diagnosing issues like a dirty pilot tube, thermocouple failure or a malfunctioning igniter. We carry common replacement parts on our service vehicles to make for a quick fix and restore your hot water flow promptly.

    Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Fort Lauderdale

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust out through PVC pipe. Failures usually involve the blower motor, pressure switch, hot surface igniter or condensate in the vent. We read the error codes, test each part and repair the failure. Correct vent slope and termination are verified before we leave.

    Venting and Draft Repair in Fort Lauderdale, FL

    Atmospheric gas water heaters rely on a properly sloped metal flue to carry exhaust out of the home. Disconnected sections, rusted draft hoods and poor slope can let combustion gases spill back. We repair or replace vent sections and confirm draft with a test. Proper venting is critical for your family's safety.
