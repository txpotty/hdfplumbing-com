    Composite Sink Installation in Fort Lauderdale

    Granite and quartz composite sinks resist scratches on the surface but can chip at the drain hole if a strainer is overtightened. We tighten by hand first. Most composite brands want silicone rather than putty under the strainer because putty can stain them, so we go by the maker's instructions.

    New Construction Sink Rough-In in Fort Lauderdale, FL

    Rough-in sets the drain height and the stops long before the cabinets arrive. We usually set the kitchen drain stub around 16 to 20 inches off the floor, then adjust for deep bowls or a disposal so the trap does not end up below the wall outlet. Vents go in now. Having the sink and faucet specs early saves moving pipe later.

    Top-mount Sink Installation in Fort Lauderdale

    Drop-in sinks are common in Florida homes with laminate or tile counters. The rim carries the weight. After checking the cutout, we lay silicone under the lip and draw the clips down evenly so the whole rim sits flat. Then the drain and faucet get connected and tested.

    Workstation Sink Installation in Fort Lauderdale, FL

    Workstation sinks have a ledge for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks, and many are deep single bowls with the drain off center. We plan the trap around that offset drain so the disposal fits and the cabinet keeps usable space on one side. Faucet reach matters too.

    Apron-Front Sinks in Fort Lauderdale, FL

    Apron sinks need a cabinet cut to fit the front panel, and the bowl must be set before the counter gets measured. We set the sink and frame early, so the fabricator measures from the actual bowl instead of a spec sheet. It prevents a crooked reveal. Cast iron versions can top 150 pounds, so we bring two people.

    Undermount Sinks on Stone in Fort Lauderdale, FL

    Stone shops vary on how they mount undermounts. Some epoxy threaded studs into the stone, others use a rail system, and we work with whichever one your countertop shop prefers. Either way, the bowl gets braced. The trap does not get connected until the adhesive cures.

    Stainless Sinks With Sound Pads in Fort Lauderdale

    Cheap stainless bowls are noisy when dishes hit them or the disposal runs. Better sinks come with rubber pads and an undercoating, and if yours does not, we can add a coat of spray undercoating before we set it. It is a small thing. Once the bowl is mounted, the space underneath is hard to reach, so this is a job worth doing while the sink is still on the floor.

    Same-Day Sink Replacement in Fort Lauderdale, FL
