    Pinhole Leak Repair in Fort Lauderdale

    Pinhole leaks form when the copper wall thins from the inside. Water chemistry causes most. High velocity, poor grounding and aggressive water all speed it up, and the leak starts as a tiny drip that stains drywall or a ceiling, so we cut out the affected section and replace it with new copper or a PEX transition. More pinholes often follow.

    PEX Pipe Repair and Installation in Fort Lauderdale

    PEX is flexible and handles freezing better than rigid pipe. It still fails. Most PEX leaks happen at a crimp ring that was not fully seated, an early brass fitting that dezincified or a section damaged by sunlight in an attic, and we cut out the bad fitting and replace it with a crimp, clamp or expansion connection rated for the pipe. Usually a quick fix.
