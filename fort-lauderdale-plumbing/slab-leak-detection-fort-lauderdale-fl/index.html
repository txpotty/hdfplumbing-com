    Slab Leak Reroutes in Fort Lauderdale

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the slab and runs new pipe through the walls or attic. It avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than direct access under finished floors. We usually use PEX for reroutes because it handles heat and resists hard-water corrosion. This option is popular when the slab is covered in tile or hardwood.

    Slab Leak Repair Through Direct Access in Fort Lauderdale, FL

    When a single leak is found on otherwise healthy pipe, direct access can be the most affordable repair. We cut a small opening in the floor at the marked spot, repair the pipe and pressure test it. Then we patch the concrete and leave the area ready for flooring. Accurate detection keeps that opening as small as possible.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Fort Lauderdale, FL

    Cold water slab leaks do not create warm spots, so they are more difficult to see. They often show up as a running water meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards. We isolate the cold side and use acoustic equipment to listen for the leak through the slab. Line tracers map the pipe route so we know exactly where to listen.

    Slab Leak Detection and Repair Cost Comparison in Fort Lauderdale, FL

    Slab leak detection usually costs $150 to $450. Direct access repairs usually run $1,000 to $3,000, including concrete patching but not flooring replacement. Reroutes generally cost $800 to $3,500 depending on distance and access. Full repipes cost more, often $4,000 to $15,000, but end the problem permanently.
