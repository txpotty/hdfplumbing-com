    Leak Detection and Slab Leak Checks in Fort Lauderdale

    Many homes in Florida built before the mid 1970s have cast iron drains under the slab, which commonly crack and corrode with age. Soil movement and hard water can wear buried or under-slab water lines until they leak. We check your water meter for hidden flow, test fixture valves and use acoustic and thermal tools to find leaks. Warm spots on floors, a running meter and unexplained high water bills are common signs. Finding a leak early prevents slab damage and mold.
