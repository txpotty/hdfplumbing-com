    Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Fort Lauderdale

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and damage buried water and sewer lines. We confirm it with a meter test and pressure test, then use acoustic listening gear and thermal imaging to pinpoint the leak within a foot or two. Then we decide. A spot repair through the floor makes sense for some, and an overhead reroute makes more sense for others. You get a price on each option.

    Backflow Assembly Testing and Rebuilds in Fort Lauderdale

    Most water providers require annual tests on commercial backflow assemblies, and many send late notices that come with fines. Test early.
