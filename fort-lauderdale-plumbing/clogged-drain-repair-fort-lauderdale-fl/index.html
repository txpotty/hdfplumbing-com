    Hydro-Jetting Services in Fort Lauderdale, FL

    A jetter pushes water at thousands of psi through a nozzle that sprays backward and pulls itself down the pipe. It scours the whole wall. Cables punch a hole through a clog, while jetting removes the buildup that would start the next one. We use it on repeat offenders.

    Kitchen Sink Clog Repair in Fort Lauderdale

    Kitchen sinks clog from grease that cools and hardens on the pipe wall. Food and soap stick to it. We pull the trap, send a cable through the wall line all the way to the stack, and check the disposal for a jam or worn impeller. A heavy grease line may need a quick jet afterward.

    Laundry and Utility Drain Clogs in Fort Lauderdale, FL

    A washing machine dumps up to 20 gallons in a couple of minutes. Lint rides along with it. Over months that lint packs the standpipe and the branch line behind it, which is why a clogged laundry drain usually overflows at the standpipe in the middle of a cycle. We cable it and suggest a lint trap. Some laundry lines share the kitchen branch, so we check both.

    Bathroom Drain Clog Clearing in Fort Lauderdale, FL

    Hair, soap scum and toothpaste bind together into a mat that sits on the pop-up stopper. That is where most bath sink clogs start. We pull the stopper and clean it, clear the P-trap and run a small cable through the wall line if the water is still slow. We can suggest a hair catcher.

    Main Sewer Line Clog Clearing in Fort Lauderdale, FL

    Several drains backing up at once usually means the clog is in the main. It needs a bigger machine. We find the cleanout, relieve the pressure slowly and then run a large cable with a cutting head. We camera the line once it flows.

    Bathroom Sink & Shower Clog Repair in Fort Lauderdale, FL
