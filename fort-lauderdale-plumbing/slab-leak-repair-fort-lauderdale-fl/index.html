    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Fort Lauderdale, FL

    Hot lines leak under slabs more than cold lines. Expansion wears them out. The pipe swells and shrinks against rock every time hot water flows, and a recirculation pump keeps that going constantly, thinning the wall. We repair or reroute the line and can set the recirculation pump on a timer.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Fort Lauderdale

    Cold leaks don't make warm spots. Watch the bill instead. They show up as a higher bill, a meter that turns with every faucet off, or a patch of carpet that stays damp, and they soak the soil just as much as hot leaks do. Your Fort Lauderdale home is no exception. We isolate the cold line and repair or reroute it.

    Ground Radar and Acoustic Locating in Fort Lauderdale

    Ground-penetrating radar shows what is inside the slab before we cut. Radar maps steel in the concrete. Paired with acoustic listening gear, it lets us mark a leak in copper, PEX or galvanized pipe even near busy roads, because the radar fixes the pipe route and the mic confirms the leak. We use both in Fort Lauderdale.
