    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Fort Lauderdale

    Crawl space encapsulation involves sealing the crawl space entirely with heavy-duty vapor barriers and often includes a dehumidifier. This not only waterproofs the walls but also controls humidity, stopping mold growth and wood rot in the crawl space. Humidity causes condensation on cold lines and clogged AC condensate drains, a common source of hidden water damage. An encapsulated crawl space can noticeably improve indoor air quality and energy efficiency by keeping moisture from moving up into the home. We use durable, reinforced poly sheeting for walls and floors, sealed at all seams and penetrations.

    Basement Wall Sealing in Fort Lauderdale, FL

    Basement wall sealing is a critical part of walls waterproofing. Humidity causes condensation on cold lines and clogged AC condensate drains, a common source of hidden water damage.
