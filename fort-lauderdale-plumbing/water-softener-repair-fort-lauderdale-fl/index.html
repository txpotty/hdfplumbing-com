    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Fort Lauderdale

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. Salt sits there unused, and regeneration fails. After breaking up the crust, we clear the sludge and test the float and brine well. Cracked tanks get replaced.

    Water Softener Leak Repair in Fort Lauderdale, FL

    Drips around fittings waste water. We trace it to the seal, O-ring, hose or internal part that failed. We replace the failed part with silicone-lubricated O-rings rated for potable water in your Fort Lauderdale, FL home and test it under line pressure.

    Resin Testing and Replacement in Fort Lauderdale

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Treated city water slowly breaks resin down into mush. After a capacity test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced, and a good tank and valve with new resin performs like new. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    Bypass and Fitting Leak Repair in Fort Lauderdale, FL

    Bypass valves, the O-rings where the valve meets the tank, cracked fittings and the drain line are the usual leak points. Even a small drip wastes water and can stain a garage floor or rot a cabinet. We replace O-rings or fittings and test under pressure. A cracked mineral tank usually means replacement.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Fort Lauderdale

    Hard water coming back usually means the softener has stopped regenerating. The usual causes are a dead timer motor, a bad board, a stuck meter or settings wiped by an outage. We test each part. After the repair, the valve is programmed for your household size and hardness so you have soft water by the next cycle.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Fort Lauderdale

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads so they cannot grab hardness. We test the resin, clean it with a resin cleaner or replace it. Fresh resin brings capacity back.

    Water Softener Repair vs. Replacement in Fort Lauderdale, FL

    Age and parts availability decide whether a softener is worth repairing. HDF Plumbing gives honest advice on both counts. A sound tank with a rebuildable valve is worth saving, but an obsolete head without parts usually is not. You get a clear breakdown.
