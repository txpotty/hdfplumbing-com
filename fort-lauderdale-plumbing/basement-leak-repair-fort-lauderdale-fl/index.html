    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Leak Repair in Fort Lauderdale

    Basement plumbing leak repairs in Fort Lauderdale typically cost $200 to $1,500, depending on the pipe and how hard it is to reach. Exposed pipe on an unfinished ceiling is cheap. Opening finished walls or the slab adds cost, and a multi-floor cast iron stack can go past that range.

    Some small leaks are fine to handle yourself, like tightening a washer hose or replacing a leaking supply connector. Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and damage buried water and sewer lines. Patches on cracks caused by ground movement tend to fail, since the water pushes from outside. Hydraulic cement buys time at best.

    Heavy storms dump a lot of water quickly, and the soil around the foundation cannot absorb it fast enough. Heavy afternoon thunderstorms are common in summer, and saturated ground can push water into sewer lines and slow every fixture in the house. Saturated soil pushes water against the walls and up under the slab through the cove joint, so the leak shows only when the ground is soaked. It disappears when the ground dries.

    Look for damp spots on walls or floors, a musty smell, and white chalky deposits on concrete. That white crust is mineral left after water dries. A rising water bill or rust at the water heater base points toward a plumbing leak. Smell often comes first.

    A pipe or fitting repair usually takes an hour or two. Crack injection takes about half a day. Sumps take most of a day, and perimeter drains take two to five days depending on wall length.

    Most plumbing leak repairs in a basement are done in one visit. Expect two to four hours with drywall access. Seepage repairs vary more, from half a day for an injected crack to most of a week for a full interior perimeter drain, which includes cutting and removing the slab edge, setting pipe and stone, and pouring new concrete.

    Simple pipe repairs and crack injection usually do not need a permit. Larger work is a different story. Replacing a drain stack, installing a new sump with a discharge line, adding a backwater valve, or excavating near the foundation may require a permit, and we pull it when it is needed.

    Yes, every repair carries a written workmanship warranty. Sump pumps and parts add the manufacturer's warranty.
