    Standard Toilet Replacement in Fort Lauderdale

    Two-piece toilets are what most houses have, and after twenty or thirty years the tank bolts corrode, the porcelain gets crazed and the flush never quite clears the bowl.     High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Fort Lauderdale, FL

    Toilets made before 1994 often use 3.5 gallons a flush, and some from the 1970s use five or more.     Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Fort Lauderdale

    Chair height toilets put the seat about 17 to 19 inches off the floor, roughly two inches higher than a standard bowl, and that makes a real difference for knees and hips.     One-Piece Toilet Replacement in Fort Lauderdale, FL

    One-piece toilets have no tank-to-bowl joint, so there are fewer leak points and fewer crevices to clean. They cost more and weigh more. A one-piece toilet usually takes two people to carry upstairs, and we set it without dragging it across your tile so the floor stays unscratched and the wax ring lands centered on the flange.

    Toilet Rough-In Adjustments in Fort Lauderdale, FL

If the new toilet's rough-in does not match, it either sits too far out or will not fit. An offset flange can correct an inch or two without moving the drain.
