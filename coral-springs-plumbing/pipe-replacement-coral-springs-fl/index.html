    PEX Repiping in Coral Springs

    PEX installs quicker. Flexible lines snake through walls with fewer openings, while manifold setups give you separate shutoffs. Popular choice.

    Copper Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Coral Springs, FL

    Older homes sometimes have copper that is thin from decades of use. Small leaks appear in random spots. We replace it with new Type L copper or PEX, and fixing awkward runs from past remodels. Better flow.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Coral Springs

    Polybutylene is gray or blue plastic installed from roughly the late 1970s to mid 1990s. It degrades from the inside. Failures tend to be sudden splits at fittings rather than slow drips, which can cause insurance headaches. Replace it.

    Whole-Home Re-Piping in Coral Springs

    A whole-home repipe replaces every supply line from the main shutoff to each fixture, including the water heater connections. Plan on a few days for a typical house. We run new trunk lines through the attic or crawlspace, branch down to fixtures through small wall openings, and keep water on at night whenever we can. Then patching.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Coral Springs

    PEX bends around corners and comes in long rolls, so a typical repipe needs fewer fittings than copper would on the same layout. Northern parts of Florida do get occasional freezing nights, which can crack exposed pipes, irrigation lines, and outdoor devices. PEX can stretch a little in a freeze instead of splitting. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings where possible, keeping flow strong at each joint. Faster install.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Coral Springs

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster. It resists scale and corrosion. Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving stubborn scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings or PEX-b with crimp rings depending on the job, and we run it in home runs from a manifold or in trunk-and-branch layouts. Fewer joints, fewer leaks.

    Whole-Home Repiping in Coral Springs

    Repiping sounds bigger than it is. You can usually stay in the house. We work room by room, restore water most evenings, and keep dust down with plastic sheeting and drop cloths, then patch every opening before we leave. Clean finish.
