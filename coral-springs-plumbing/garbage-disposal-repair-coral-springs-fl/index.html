    Noise and Odor Diagnosis in Coral Springs

    A rattle that stops after a few seconds is usually a spoon or a pit bouncing in the chamber. A steady whine is different.     Jam Clearing and Motor Repair in Coral Springs

    Bones, fruit pits and bottle caps lodge between the grind ring and the impeller lugs, and the motor stalls. Long stalls can overheat the motor, so we run it under load before we finish.

    Humming Disposal Repair in Coral Springs

    Humming with no spin usually means something is stuck. Bones, pits, glass and spoons are the usual suspects. We cut power, work the flywheel free with a hex wrench on the bottom of the unit, remove the object and then test the motor and the reset button with water running.
