    Black Iron Pipe Repair in Coral Springs, FL

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Vibration and settling loosen threaded joints over time, and pipe in damp garages or outside walls can rust through from the surface. Bad sections get replaced with new pipe. Outdoor runs get coated or painted after the repair so rust does not start again.

    CSST Repair and Bonding in Coral Springs, FL

    Newer homes often have corrugated stainless steel tubing running through the attic. Nails, rodents and lightning can all damage CSST, and older yellow-jacket tubing must be bonded to the home's grounding system to lower arc risk. It takes about an hour. Damaged runs get spliced with the manufacturer's fittings, and we confirm the bond on the way out. After a major storm, homes on wells or septic should be checked before normal use, since floodwater can foul both systems.

    Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in Coral Springs, FL

    A kitchen remodel or a new tankless water heater can push a gas system past what its original pipe can deliver. Tankless units need a lot of gas. A whole-house tankless heater can draw 150,000 BTU or more, and that often means upsizing the gas line all the way back toward the meter. Load calculation comes before any pipe. The new pipe gets sized so every burner in the house still has full pressure at once.

    Gas Appliance Line Installation in Coral Springs, FL

    A new range, dryer or water heater needs a gas line sized for its BTU rating and the length of the run. Undersized lines starve burners. Each new appliance gets a properly sized branch, a shutoff you can reach, a drip leg where required and a new coated connector. If the existing appliance line is leaking or damaged, we fix it on the same visit.

    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Coral Springs

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and damage buried water and sewer lines. We replace corroded sections with polyethylene pipe laid with tracer wire, using an approved transition riser where the line comes up out of the ground at the house. Above-ground runs stay in steel. We test before backfilling so any problem joint is fixed while the trench is still open.
