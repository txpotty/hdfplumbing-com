    What Goes Into a Sewer Replacement in Coral Springs, FL

    Code-Compliant Sewer Replacement in Coral Springs

    Code sets the minimum slope, the cleanout spacing and the pipe type. The inspector looks at fall, cleanouts and material before backfill. We set fall at a quarter inch per foot unless the run requires otherwise, place cleanouts at the house and at major direction changes, and use pipe and fittings listed for underground drainage. Paperwork stays on file.

    Emergency Sewer Line Replacement in Coral Springs, FL

    Sewage coming up a floor drain or tub is an emergency. Stop running water in the house right away. We open the cleanout to relieve the backup, run a camera to find the break, and if the line has collapsed we dig to it the same day, putting in a section of new pipe to get you flowing before planning the full replacement.

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Coral Springs

    Bursting works best on clay, cast iron and Orangeburg. A hydraulic rig at the receiving pit pulls the bursting head through the old line, splitting it into the soil, while a continuous length of fused HDPE follows behind which leaves a single joint-free pipe in the ground. Roots cannot get into a joint that does not exist.

    Sewer Line Repair vs. Full Replacement in Coral Springs

    We look at the whole line before recommending either. One offset joint near the house is a repair. Breaks every few feet, roots at every joint, or a belly that holds water for twenty feet tell us the line is failing everywhere, and in that case a full replacement saves you from paying to dig the same yard twice.

    Foundation Protection during Sewer Replacement in Coral Springs, FL

    Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and damage buried water and sewer lines. A sewer leaking under or beside the house keeps the soil wet in one spot, and that uneven moisture can push or settle part of a slab. When we replace a line near the foundation we dig carefully, avoid undermining footings, and tamp each layer of fill. Slow work near the house.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement Options in Coral Springs, FL

    Bursting and pulling replace the pipe. Lining patches the inside of the old one. We use bursting when the old line is broken or undersized, since it puts a new pipe in place instead of reinforcing the old, and lining when the pipe is still round and mostly intact. Each has its place.

    Advanced Diagnostics for Sewer Issues in Coral Springs, FL

    Diagnosis comes before price. Video tells us the problem and the sonde tells us the spot. We sometimes add a hydro-jet cleaning first, because a line full of grease can hide a crack, and a clean pipe gives a much more honest picture of its condition. Guesswork costs money.

    Trenchless Sewer Repair Options in Coral Springs, FL

    Pipe bursting needs two pits. One sits at the house, the other at the far end of the run. A cone-shaped head is pulled through the old line, breaking it outward, while fused HDPE pipe follows right behind it, which puts new pipe in the same spot without digging the yard. Driveways stay put.
