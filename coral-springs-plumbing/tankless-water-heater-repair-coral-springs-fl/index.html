    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Coral Springs

    Tankless heaters use flow sensors to start and thermistors to regulate temperature. A dirty inlet filter or failed sensor causes no hot water, fluctuating temperature or the unit turning off mid-shower. We clean filters, test sensor readings and replace faulty parts. Temperature becomes steady again once readings are accurate.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Repair in Coral Springs, FL

    Rinnai is a popular tankless brand, and its units can face challenges such as scaling from hard water or issues with their flame rod. Our technicians in Coral Springs, FL are adept at Rinnai tankless water heater repair, focusing on descaling services, fan motor replacements and fixing issues with gas valve assemblies.     Pilot Light & Ignition Repair in Coral Springs, FL

    For gas-fired tankless water heaters, a faulty ignition system can completely stop hot water production. Our team in Coral Springs, FL diagnoses issues with igniters, flame sensors and gas valves. We confirm proper gas pressure and ventilation are maintained for safe and efficient operation.     Ignition Failure Repair in Coral Springs, FL

    If your tankless unit will not fire, the cause may be a dirty flame rod, a bad igniter, low gas pressure or a blocked intake. We test each component and measure gas pressure at the inlet. Undersized gas lines are a frequent hidden cause when a tankless unit replaced a tank. We correct the issue so the unit lights every time.

    Emergency Tankless Repair in Coral Springs

    A tankless unit that leaks internally or shows a fault you cannot clear needs quick attention. We prioritize no-hot-water calls and arrive with common sensors, igniters and filters.     Recirculation Pump Repair in Coral Springs

    Many tankless systems use internal or external recirculation to deliver hot water more quickly.     Tankless Descaling and Flushing in Coral Springs

We connect a circulation pump to the service valves and run a descaling solution through the exchanger. Annual descaling is a smart move for most homes on hard water.
