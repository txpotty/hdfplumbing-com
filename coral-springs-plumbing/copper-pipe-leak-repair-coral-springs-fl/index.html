    Frequently Asked Questions About Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Coral Springs

    A minor, accessible pinhole repair starts around $200. Slab leaks run higher. Complex repairs like under-slab leaks can run past $2,000, with location, severity and footage of new pipe setting most of the price. You get a written number after locating.

    The pipe wall wears through from the inside. Water quality is the big factor. Aggressive water, high velocity, excess pressure, poor original soldering and stray electrical current from grounding issues all erode the copper over time until a tiny hole opens. Hot lines get it worst.

    Keep water pressure under about 80 psi. A regulator helps. Treating hard or aggressive water, insulating pipe in cold spots and limiting how long a recirculation pump runs all reduce the wear that leads to pinholes and failed joints. Find the main valve now.

    Every repair is warrantied. We write the warranty down. Our workmanship warranty covers the repair, and copper pipe and fittings carry their own manufacturer warranties on top of it. You see the terms first.

    Wet spots on walls, ceilings or floors are the obvious sign. Pressure drops count too. The sound of running water when nothing is on, a bill that jumps, or blue-green crust on exposed fittings all point to copper that is leaking or about to. Check the meter with everything off.

    If you have had repeated leaks, it is worth pricing. PEX resists that corrosion. PEX resists the corrosion and scale that cause copper pinholes and goes in quickly with fewer wall openings, so a PEX repipe often beats years of patching. We price both options.

    All standard copper used in homes and businesses. Type M, L and K. Our repairs use copper and fittings from established manufacturers that meet ASTM and NSF standards, and press fittings come from major brands built for potable water. No off-brand fittings.

    Most accessible repairs take one to two hours. Walls add time. Leaks inside walls or ceilings can take two to four hours, and slab leak repairs may take a full day depending on whether the line is repaired in place or rerouted. You hear the schedule up front.
