    Cracked Tank and Supply Line Repair in Coral Springs, FL

    A hairline crack in the tank or a bulging braided supply line can let go without warning and flood a bathroom.     Fill Valve Replacement in Coral Springs

    A noisy, slow or hissing tank usually points to a worn fill valve. Mineral scale clogs the small ports inside, which causes long refill times and phantom refills in the night. We replace old ballcock valves with modern adjustable fill valves from Fluidmaster or Korky and set the water level to the manufacturer mark. The job takes about 20 minutes and makes an older toilet noticeably quieter.

    Clogged Toilet Solutions in Coral Springs, FL

    Stubborn toilet clogs are a common nuisance in Coral Springs, FL homes, often caused by non-flushable items or excessive toilet paper. While a plunger can clear minor blockages, persistent clogs suggest a deeper issue in the trap, drain line, or even the main sewer line. Our plumbers utilize professional-grade augers and hydro-jetting equipment to safely and effectively clear even the toughest clogs without damaging your toilet or pipes. We also inspect for potential causes like root intrusion or pipe corrosion, which are sometimes seen in older homes.

    Leaking Toilet Base Repair in Coral Springs, FL

    Water around the base usually means the wax ring has failed or the toilet has been rocking on a loose flange. On slab foundations, the flange often sits on concrete and can crack when bolts are overtightened. We pull the toilet, inspect the flange, install a repair ring or new flange if needed and reset the bowl on a new seal. We also check for soft flooring so you know if any water damage needs attention.

    Pressure-Assist and Dual-Flush Repair in Coral Springs

    Pressure-assist toilets from Sloan Flushmate and dual-flush models from TOTO and American Standard use parts that hardware stores rarely stock.     Running Toilet Repair in Coral Springs
