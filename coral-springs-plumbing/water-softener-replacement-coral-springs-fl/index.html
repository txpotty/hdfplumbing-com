    Whole-Home Softener Placement in Coral Springs, FL

    Every tap in the house, including the shower and the water heater, gets soft water when the softener sits on the main line ahead of everything. We typically leave the hose bibs on hard water. No sense spending salt on the garden.

    TAC Salt-Free Conditioners in Coral Springs

    Template-assisted crystallization, or TAC, is the salt-free media with real independent test data behind it, and it is the only kind we install. The tank goes on the main line downstream of the filter, with no power or drain needed. Magnets and clamp-on gadgets are a different story.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Coral Springs

    Twin-tank systems keep one tank in service while the other regenerates, so you never get hard water during a middle-of-the-night regeneration. Kinetico's version uses water pressure, not power, to switch tanks. The price is higher.

    Fleck Valve Programming in Coral Springs, FL

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. The valve needs your hardness in grains, the number of people and the regeneration time set correctly, or it will regenerate too often or not enough. It is programmed before we pack up.

    Upsizing a Whole-Home Softener in Coral Springs

    Undersized softeners are common in homes that added a bathroom or a couple of teenagers since the original system went in. A 32,000-grain unit that regenerated weekly for a family of three may be running dry every three days now. We size for the house you have today.

    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in Coral Springs, FL

    Fleck control valves, the 5600SXT and 9100SXT in particular, are on a large share of the softeners we see in Florida homes. An old Fleck with discontinued parts usually gets replaced, valve and tank together. Newer Fleck heads drop right in.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Coral Springs, FL

    Resin beads break down after 10 to 15 years, faster on chlorinated city water, and the softener keeps regenerating while the water slowly turns hard again. Rebedding with new resin can be cheaper than a new system. It depends on the valve. An old valve on new resin is a short-lived fix.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Coral Springs

    Timer softeners regenerate every few days whether you used 50 gallons or 500. Metered valves like the Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 count gallons and regenerate only when the resin is close to spent. Salt bills usually drop in the first month.
