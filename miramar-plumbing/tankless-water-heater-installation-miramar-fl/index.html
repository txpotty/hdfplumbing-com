    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Miramar, FL

    Calcium scale is the most common reason tankless heaters fail. Many wells in Florida produce water with a sulfur smell or high iron, which often calls for filtration to protect fixtures and appliances. Scale coats the heat exchanger, cuts efficiency and triggers error codes, and left alone it can crack the exchanger and end the warranty claim before it starts. Flush it yearly. Service valves make it easy.
