    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Miramar, FL

    Calcium scale is the most common cause of tankless heater failure. Around Miramar, flushing the water heater yearly and descaling tankless units helps keep mineral buildup under control. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides added protection.
