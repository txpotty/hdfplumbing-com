    Leak Detection and Assessment in Miramar, FL

    Hidden leaks waste water and quietly damage floors and walls. Start at the meter. With all fixtures off, we watch the meter's leak indicator, then use acoustic listening gear and a thermal camera to trace the leak behind walls, under floors or in the slab.     Water Heater Inspections in Miramar
