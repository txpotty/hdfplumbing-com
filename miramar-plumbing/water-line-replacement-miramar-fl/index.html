    Water Line Materials We Install in Miramar, FL

    Our trucks carry Type K copper, PEX-a and HDPE. Each has a place.     Main Water Line Repair vs. Replacement in Miramar

    One leak on a twenty-year-old copper line? A repair coupling is often the right call. We tell you which camp you are in and show you the pipe we cut out.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Upgrades in Miramar, FL

    Street pressure varies a lot from one neighborhood to the next. Code says no more than 80 psi inside the house. A quarter turn and the water is off.

    Polybutylene Water Line Replacement in Miramar, FL

    Polybutylene is the gray or blue plastic pipe installed from roughly the late 1970s to the mid 1990s. It degrades from the inside when exposed to chlorinated water. The failures tend to be sudden splits rather than slow drips, and some home insurers refuse to cover homes that still have it. Worth replacing.
