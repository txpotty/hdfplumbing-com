    Freezing temperatures are a common cause of burst pipes, especially when uninsulated pipes are exposed to sustained cold.     Preventive measures include insulating pipes in unheated areas, maintaining proper water pressure, addressing hard water issues that can lead to mineral buildup and regular plumbing inspections. For homes in Florida, ensuring proper drainage around foundations can also prevent soil shifts that damage underground pipes.
