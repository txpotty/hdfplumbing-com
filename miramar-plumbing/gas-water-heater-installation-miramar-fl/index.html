    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Miramar

    Atmospheric vent heaters rely on hot exhaust rising through a metal flue, with no fan. They are common in older homes and usually the least expensive gas option, but they only work if the vent has the right size, slope and clearance to combustibles. Old vents get checked. Corroded or undersized pipe gets replaced.

    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Miramar

    Direct vent heaters pull combustion air from outside and send exhaust out through a sealed, concentric vent. Because the combustion is sealed, they work well in small closets where room air is limited. Most run without electricity. The vent goes through a wall with proper clearances.

    Propane Water Heater Installation in Miramar

    Propane homes need heaters set up for propane, not natural gas. Different pressure and orifice size mean either a propane model or a factory conversion kit. We work with your supplier. Every joint is leak tested.
