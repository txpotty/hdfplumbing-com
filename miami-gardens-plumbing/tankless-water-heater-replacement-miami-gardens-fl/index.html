    Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Miami Gardens

    A like-for-like gas tankless replacement typically runs $2,800 to $4,500 installed, depending on the brand and venting. Converting from a tank to tankless often costs $4,000 to $6,500 because of gas line and venting upgrades. We give you a written quote before any work starts.

    Well-maintained tankless units can last 15 to 20 years. In hard water areas without regular descaling, heat exchangers can fail far sooner. Annual flushing makes the biggest difference.

    DIY tankless water heater replacement is generally not recommended due to the complexity of gas lines, high-voltage electricity, water connections and venting requirements. Improper installation can lead to serious gas leaks, carbon monoxide poisoning, water damage or electrical hazards. It is best to hire a licensed and experienced plumber like HDF Plumbing.

    Most manufacturers offer 10 to 15 years on the heat exchanger and about 5 years on parts for residential use. We add our own labor warranty and register the unit for you.

    Most jurisdictions in Florida require a permit for water heater replacement, especially when gas piping or venting changes. We take care of permits where they are required.

    HDF Plumbing installs and replaces all major reputable brands of tankless water heaters. Our preferred brands include Rinnai, Navien, Noritz and Rheem, as they are known for their reliability, energy efficiency and long-term performance. We can also order and install other brands based on your preference or home requirements.

    With proper installation and regular maintenance, a new tankless water heater typically lasts 20 to 25 years. This is much longer than traditional tank water heaters, which usually last 10 to 15 years. Regular flushing and descaling can help extend the life of your tankless unit, especially in areas with hard water.

    A standard tankless water heater replacement, if you are upgrading from an existing tankless unit, typically takes 4 to 8 hours. If you are converting from a traditional tank water heater, the process can take longer, possibly a full day, due to extra plumbing, gas or electrical changes required for proper installation and venting.
