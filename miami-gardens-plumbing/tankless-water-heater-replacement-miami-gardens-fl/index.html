    Tankless vs. Tank Replacement Cost in Miami Gardens, FL

    Tankless costs more up front than a tank, often twice as much installed. Over 20 years it can come out close, since a tankless can outlast two tanks and skips standby loss, but the payback depends on how much hot water your house uses. You see both quotes.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Miami Gardens

    A gas tankless unit at full fire can pull 199,000 BTU, roughly five times what a 40 gallon tank burner uses. The gas line usually has to be 3/4-inch or larger.     Navien Venting and Install in Miami Gardens

    Navien allows 2-inch or 3-inch PVC venting on its condensing units, with maximum lengths that drop as you add elbows. We stay inside those limits. Warranty depends on it.

    Whole-House Electric Tankless Replacement in Miami Gardens

    Whole-house electric tankless performance drops when the incoming water is cold, since the unit has to raise the temperature further at the same amperage. We replace Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart units. Sometimes a heat pump tank serves the house better, and we will say so.

    A leaking tankless can drip inside the cabinet for weeks before anyone notices the puddle. Shut the gas and cold isolation valve now. We then find out whether it is a fitting or the heat exchanger, since one is cheap and the other is replacement.
