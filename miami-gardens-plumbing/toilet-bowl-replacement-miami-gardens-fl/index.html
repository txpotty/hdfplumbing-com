    Toilet bowl replacement usually costs $250 to $550, including the matched bowl, new seals, hardware and labor. Specialty or discontinued colors can increase parts cost. We compare that against full replacement so you know the best option.

    With the matching bowl on hand, the job usually takes 1 to 2 hours. If the bowl must be ordered, it may take a few days to arrive.

    If the leak is from a minor component like a flapper or fill valve, repair is often sufficient. However, if the toilet bowl itself is cracked, replacement is the only safe and effective solution to prevent water damage.

    In many cases, if only the bowl is damaged, such as with a crack, we can replace just the toilet bowl itself, assuming a compatible tank is available and in good condition.     While it might seem straightforward, toilet bowl replacement involves proper sealing, flange inspection, and a watertight connection to prevent leaks and costly water damage. We recommend professional installation to make sure it's done correctly and to code.

    Key signs you need a new toilet bowl include visible cracks, persistent leaks that aren't resolved by simple repairs, severe and unremovable stains, a wobbly toilet due to a damaged bowl or flange, or a bowl that is outdated and inefficient. Any of these can mean it's time for a replacement.
