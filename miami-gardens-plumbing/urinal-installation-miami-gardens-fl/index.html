    Waterless Urinal Installation in Miami Gardens

    Waterless urinals save the most water and need the most discipline. A cartridge or sealing liquid blocks sewer gas while letting urine through, and it must be replaced on schedule. Your custodial team needs training. We show them how.

    Commercial Urinal Installation in Miami Gardens

    Offices and restaurants want urinals that survive heavy use. We set vitreous china units on floor-mounted carriers, connect them with new outlet gaskets and stainless bolts, and pair them with a Sloan or Zurn flush valve sized to the bowl. We check partition spacing too. Most codes want 15 inches from centerline to the side.

    Waterless Cartridge Urinals in Miami Gardens

    Each waterless brand uses its own cartridge. Falcon, Waterless Co, Kohler and Zurn units all trap odor differently, and the cartridge or sealing liquid has to be replaced after a set number of uses, which in a busy restroom can mean every few months. We set up a schedule with you. Spare cartridges should stay on site.

    Urinal Permits and Compliance in Miami Gardens, FL

    New urinals usually need a permit. Adding a fixture means new drain, vent and supply work that most cities inspect at rough-in and again at final, and commercial jobs often need plans that show fixture counts and accessible layouts. We pull the permit. Like-for-like swaps may not need one.

    Urinal Replacement and Upgrades in Miami Gardens

    Cracked or stained urinals are worth replacing. Cracked china leaks eventually and heavy scale rarely comes clean, so a new bowl on the old carrier is usually best. Carrier bolts get inspected first. Rusted bolts get replaced.

    Flush Valve Urinal Installation in Miami Gardens, FL

    Flush valve urinals are the commercial standard. Tankless flushometers deliver a set volume from a 3/4 inch line and recover instantly for the next user. We set the volume to the bowl. Wrong settings stain bowls.

    Urinal Rough-In for New Restrooms in Miami Gardens

    Tenant improvements need rough-in before the walls close. We space urinals 30 inches apart and 15 inches from side walls, then set carriers and run all the lines. We stay in touch with the GC daily. Rough inspection comes first.

    Sensor Urinal Installation in Miami Gardens, FL

    Sensor valves stop missed flushes and double flushes. We install battery or hardwired units, set the sensing range so the valve does not fire when someone walks past, and turn on a sanitary flush cycle if the restroom sits empty for long stretches. Batteries last a few years. Hardwired units need an electrician first.
