    Types of Toilet Replacements in Miami Gardens, FL

    ADA and Commercial Toilet Replacement in Miami Gardens, FL

    Public restrooms need bowls rated for heavy use, and many need to meet ADA height and clearance rules when a fixture is replaced. Clearances get checked before we order anything. Adding a sensor flush valve during the swap takes little extra time, and they cut down on unflushed bowls.

    Commercial Flush Valve Upgrades in Miami Gardens

    Manual flush valves on older commercial toilets leak at the handle and stick open, and swapping to a sensor valve during a replacement improves hygiene without changing the rough-in. You can pick battery or hardwired sensors. We do the work after hours if needed, and we check that the new fixtures meet accessibility requirements.

    Gravity-Flush Toilet Replacement in Miami Gardens

    Gravity-flush toilets are the simplest and most forgiving, and a well-made one with a 3 inch flush valve will outflush most of the older toilets it replaces. Replacement parts are on every hardware shelf. We set it level with shims if the floor is out, then caulk the base and test several full flushes.

    Standard Toilet Replacement in Miami Gardens

    Two-piece toilets are what most houses have, and after twenty or thirty years the tank bolts corrode, the porcelain gets crazed and the flush never quite clears the bowl. That is when we swap them. The old unit comes off, the flange gets scraped clean and checked for cracks, then set the new one on a fresh wax ring with new closet bolts.

    Comfort-Height Toilet Replacement in Miami Gardens

    Going from a standard bowl to a comfort-height model raises the seat two or three inches, which matters a lot for seniors and anyone recovering from knee or hip surgery. The drain location stays the same. Pair it with a grab bar if you can, and we can anchor that into a stud during the same visit.

    Replacing Pressure-Assisted Toilets in Miami Gardens, FL

    Pressure-assisted toilets use a sealed vessel in the tank that stores air under line pressure and pushes water through the bowl hard, which helps in houses with long, low-slope drain runs. They are loud. Replacement means matching the vessel brand for parts later, and we check that house pressure is in the range the unit needs.

    High-Efficiency Toilet (HET) Upgrades in Miami Gardens, FL

    High-efficiency toilets carry a WaterSense label and a MaP score, which tells you how many grams of waste the toilet clears in one flush. Look for a score of at least 800. A high score means fewer double flushes and fewer clogs, and that is where the real water savings come from.

    Urgent Toilet Replacement in Miami Gardens, FL

    Late-night failures happen, usually a tank that splits along a seam or a bowl that cracks at the base and keeps seeping no matter how many towels go down. Closing the angle stop stops the water. A replacement can usually go in first thing the next morning, and we will tell you if the floor needs to dry before we set the new one.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Replacement in Miami Gardens

    A standard toilet replacement usually runs $400 to $800 with the new toilet and labor included, depending mostly on the model you choose. Any flange or subfloor repair is extra. Those get their own price before any work, so the base number does not move.

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall behind the toilet to the center of the drain, which you can find by measuring to the closet bolts. Most homes are 12 inches. Older homes sometimes have 10 or 14 inch rough-ins, and a toilet made for the wrong one either sticks out or will not fit.

    Many wells in Florida produce water with a sulfur smell or high iron, which often calls for filtration to protect fixtures and appliances. Hard water leaves calcium scale in the rim jets and on the flush valve seat, which slowly weakens the flush and makes flappers leak. Glazed trapways help. Our picks for hard water have glazed trapways and fill valves with fewer small ports.

    Our workmanship is guaranteed, and the toilet carries its own manufacturer warranty, which differs by brand and usually covers the china longer than the tank parts. If a leak traces back to our install, we return and fix it. Hold on to the invoice and the model number.

    Most swaps take 1.5 to 2.5 hours. Flange or subfloor repairs can add an hour.

    Our short list starts with TOTO Drake, American Standard Champion, Kohler Highline and Gerber Viper, all of which flush well and use parts you can find at most supply houses. Mansfield makes a solid budget pick. The model gets matched to your rough-in and space.
