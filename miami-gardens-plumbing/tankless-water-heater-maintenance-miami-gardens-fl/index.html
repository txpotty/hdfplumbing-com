    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Miami Gardens

    Picture a bundle of thin tubes with a gas flame under them and cold water racing through at three or four gallons a minute. That is your heat exchanger. Calcium and magnesium drop out on the hottest tube walls first and narrow the passages. A 45 to 60 minute circulation with descaling solution dissolves it.

    Flush Now or Replace the Exchanger Later in Miami Gardens, FL

    A new heat exchanger can run $600 to $1,500 or more installed, and some manufacturers will not cover one that failed from scale. A descaling visit costs a fraction of that.     Tankless Vent Inspection in Miami Gardens

    Gas tankless heaters push exhaust out through a vent that has to stay sealed from the heater all the way to the termination outside the house. On a condensing unit the PVC or polypropylene vent should pitch back toward the heater. Every joint gets checked, and we test for carbon monoxide by the heater. Wasp nests in vent caps are common.

    Annual Tankless Checkup in Miami Gardens, FL

    The annual checkup is a top-to-bottom pass on the heater. We read gas pressure at the inlet with a manometer while the burner is at full fire and compare it to the rating plate. The flue gets a look for obstructions. You get a written report with the readings, so next year's visit has something to compare against.

    Tankless Error Code Diagnosis in Miami Gardens, FL

    An error code is the heater telling you what it saw. Rinnai LC codes point to scale, Navien E003 is a failed ignition and Noritz 90 points at combustion air or exhaust trouble. First we read the code history. If a part has failed, you hear the repair and the price before we start.

    Tankless Banks for Restaurants and Apartments in Miami Gardens

    Cascade systems link several tankless units to one controller that fires them in turn. Heavy use means scale builds faster than in a single home unit. We descale each heater in the bank, check the cascade controller and confirm the load is shared evenly across every unit. Quarterly service is common.

    Multi-Unit Tankless Checks in Miami Gardens, FL

    Restaurants and salons often run two or more tankless heaters on a shared manifold, and one weak unit can drag the whole bank down. Gas pressure gets read with every unit firing together. Each heater gets its own flow and temperature reading. Then we compare them.
