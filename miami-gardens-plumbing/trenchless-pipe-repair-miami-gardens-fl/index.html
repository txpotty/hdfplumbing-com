    Pipe Bursting in Miami Gardens, FL

    Pipe bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. A bursting head is pulled through the old pipe, fracturing it outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind. The new pipe is fused into one continuous length with no joints. Bursting needs an entry and exit pit but avoids digging the full length.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Miami Gardens

    CIPP lining involves cleaning the old pipe, then inserting a flexible liner saturated with epoxy resin. The liner is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or UV light. Once hardened, it forms a smooth, jointless pipe inside the old one. Lining works well for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Miami Gardens

    CIPP lining is a popular trenchless method where we insert a resin-saturated felt tube into the existing damaged pipe. The liner is then cured in place using heat or air, creating a new, seamless pipe within the old one. This method is well suited for repairing cracks, breaks, and root intrusion without digging up your yard in Miami Gardens.

    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Miami Gardens, FL

    Pipe bursting is another trenchless method used for replacing severely damaged pipes. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward at the same time. This method is well suited for completely replacing a pipe segment with a new, stronger material, such as HDPE. It's often chosen when the existing pipe is collapsed or too damaged for lining. HDF Plumbing uses specialized bursting heads and winches for a smooth and efficient replacement, reducing the overall project time and cost for homeowners in Miami Gardens, FL.
