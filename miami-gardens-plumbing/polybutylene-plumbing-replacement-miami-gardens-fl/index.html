    How We Replace Polybutylene in Miami Gardens, FL

    PEX Pipe Installation in Miami Gardens, FL

    PEX is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, resists scale and corrosion and installs faster than copper. Labor runs lower with PEX. We keep PEX out of direct sunlight, away from water heater flues and recessed light housings and support it at the spacing the manufacturer calls for, which protects both the pipe and the warranty.

    Full Home Polybutylene Repiping in Miami Gardens

    A full replacement takes every poly supply line out of service, not just the sections that have failed. Partial jobs leave risk behind. We run new lines from the main shutoff to every sink, toilet, tub, washer, ice maker and water heater, add new quarter-turn stops and test the whole system before patching.

    Yard Service Line Replacement in Miami Gardens, FL

    Many poly-era homes also have a blue or black polybutylene service line running from the water meter to the house. These buried lines can split under the yard, raising the water bill and soaking the soil. Utilities get marked first.     Manufactured Home Poly Replacement in Miami Gardens, FL

    Manufactured homes from the poly era often have polybutylene lines in the floor or the belly underneath. New PEX gets sized for the home and strapped to the frame. We check underneath. Heat tape and insulation on exposed runs get inspected and replaced where needed, so the new supply lines are protected under the home.

    Copper Replacement Option in Miami Gardens

    Type L copper appeals to some homeowners for its long history and rigid feel. Joints are soldered or pressed. Copper costs more than PEX and takes a little longer to install, so we help you weigh the cost, your water conditions and how long you plan to stay in the house.

    Permits for Polybutylene Replacement in Miami Gardens

    A whole-house poly replacement in Miami Gardens is a repipe, and it usually needs a plumbing permit. The permit is on us.
