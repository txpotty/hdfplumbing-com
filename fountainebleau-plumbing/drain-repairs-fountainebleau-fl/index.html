    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Repairs in Fountainebleau

    Trap and slip-joint repairs typically cost $150 to $350. Pipe sections run higher. A section of drain pipe usually runs $400 to $1,200, and cast iron stack replacement can range from $2,000 to $6,000 with drywall, flooring and access driving most of the spread. We write it up before we start.

    Call us if a plunger or a small hand snake has not cleared it. Same goes for repeat clogs. Multiple slow drains, sewer smell or water coming up in a different fixture than the one you are using usually mean the problem is deeper in the line than a hand tool can reach.     A trap swap or a fixture drain fix is usually an hour or two. Bigger jobs run past a day. Replacing a section of main line can take a full day or several days, with permits and digging as the main things that stretch it out. We give you the timeline up front.

    PVC, ABS, cast iron, galvanized and copper drain all show up in Florida homes. We repair every one.     Clearing a simple clog takes an hour or two. Pipe work takes longer. Replacing a section of main line or opening a wall to swap a cast iron stack may run a day or longer with access and inspections setting the pace. The quote includes a time estimate.

    Small fixture drain repairs usually do not. Larger jobs often do. Replacing drain stacks, relocating drains or adding new lines may require one depending on the scope and your local rules. We handle permits when they apply.

    Hydro-jetting scours the pipe with water at several thousand psi. The whole inside gets cleaned. A snake only punches a hole through a clog, but a jet nozzle strips buildup off the entire pipe wall. Fragile pipe gets checked before jetting.
