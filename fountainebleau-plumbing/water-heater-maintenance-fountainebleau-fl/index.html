    Water Heater Anode Rod Inspection in Fountainebleau

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part that protects your water heater tank from corrosion. During maintenance in Fountainebleau, we inspect the condition of the anode rod and recommend replacement if it's significantly depleted. Replacing a worn anode rod can extend the life of your water heater by several years, preventing costly tank leaks.

    Standard Tank Water Heater Maintenance in Fountainebleau

    Our standard tank water heater maintenance includes flushing the tank to remove sediment buildup. Many wells in Florida produce water with a sulfur smell or high iron, which often calls for treatment to protect fixtures and appliances. We also inspect the anode rod, test the temperature and pressure relief valve, and check for any leaks or corrosion on the tank and connections. This service helps prevent premature failure and improves heating efficiency, keeping your energy bills down.

    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Fountainebleau, FL

    Tankless water heaters need specific maintenance, mainly a descaling flush to remove mineral deposits that can restrict flow and reduce efficiency. Our technicians in Fountainebleau, FL use commercial-grade solutions to clean the heat exchanger, so your unit keeps providing on-demand hot water efficiently. We also inspect the igniter, fan, and exhaust venting for proper performance and safety.

    Tank Water Heater Flushing in Fountainebleau

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium sediment at the bottom of the tank. This layer insulates the water from the burner or lower element, wasting energy and causing popping sounds. We connect a hose to the drain valve and flush the tank until the water runs clean. For heavy buildup, we may stir and flush in several cycles. A clean tank heats faster and runs quieter.

    Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in Fountainebleau, FL

    Gas water heater maintenance involves important safety inspections in Fountainebleau, FL. We check for gas leaks, proper venting of exhaust gases, and the function of the pilot light or igniter. We also clean the burner assembly and inspect the thermocouple. These checks are vital to prevent carbon monoxide exposure and keep your gas-fired appliance running safely.

    Electric Water Heater Element Service in Fountainebleau, FL
