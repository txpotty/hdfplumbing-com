    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Fountainebleau, FL

    Electric tank water heaters are common in homes without natural gas service. We install models with the correct voltage and breaker size for your electrical panel. These units do not need venting, which allows more flexible placement. We also install a drain pan when the heater is in a closet or attic. Electric models are simple, dependable and quiet.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Fountainebleau, FL

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from surrounding air, which can make them very efficient in warm garages and utility rooms. We install models from Rheem and A.O. Smith with proper condensate drainage. These units need adequate air space and clearance. They may qualify for utility rebates or tax credits. We help you decide if a hybrid is right for your home.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Fountainebleau

    Gas tank water heaters provide strong recovery and are common in many local homes. We install 40, 50 and 75 gallon models with proper venting and combustion air. Each install includes a new gas connector, sediment trap and shutoff valve. We check draft and test for gas leaks. Gas units are a good choice for families with high hot water demand.

    Expansion Tank Installation in Fountainebleau, FL

    Many homes have a pressure reducing valve or check valve that creates a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure can rise and stress pipes and relief valves. An expansion tank absorbs that extra pressure. We install and pre-charge the tank to match your water pressure. This protects your new water heater and pipes.
