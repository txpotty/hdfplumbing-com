    Toilet Rough-In Adjustments in Fountainebleau, FL

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, usually 12 inches, sometimes 10 or 14 in older homes. Measure to the closet bolts, not the baseboard.     Standard Toilet Replacement in Fountainebleau

    Two-piece toilets are what most houses have, and after twenty or thirty years the tank bolts corrode, the porcelain gets crazed and the flush never quite clears the bowl.     Water-Saving Toilet Upgrades in Fountainebleau, FL

    Older toilets are one of the largest indoor water users in a house, and replacing a 3.5 gallon model with a 1.28 gallon one cuts toilet water use by more than half. Dual-flush models go lower for liquids. We recommend models with high MaP scores so the savings do not come with double flushing.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Replacement in Fountainebleau

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, so replacing one means checking the carrier bolts, the gasket on the outlet and the condition of the wall finish around it. Some carriers need new bolts. We match the new bowl to the existing carrier whenever possible, which avoids opening the wall.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Fountainebleau

    Chair height toilets put the seat about 17 to 19 inches off the floor, roughly two inches higher than a standard bowl, and that makes a real difference for knees and hips.
