    Leak Detection and Slab Leak Checks in Fountainebleau

    Many homes in Florida built before the mid 1970s have cast iron drains under the slab, which commonly crack and corrode with age. Soil movement and hard water can wear buried or under-slab water lines until they leak. We check your water meter for hidden flow, test fixture valves and use acoustic and thermal tools to find leaks.     Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Fountainebleau, FL

    Whether gas or electric, your hot water tank benefits greatly from annual maintenance.     Backflow Preventer Testing in Fountainebleau

    Backflow preventers protect the drinking water supply from contamination, and many water providers call for annual testing for irrigation and commercial devices. Our certified testers check each valve and file results with your water utility as required. If a device fails, we repair or replace the internal parts. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where necessary. Keeping up with testing avoids fines and water shutoffs.
