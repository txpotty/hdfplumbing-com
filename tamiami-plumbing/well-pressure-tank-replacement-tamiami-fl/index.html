    Both bladder and diaphragm tanks use an air cushion to pressurize water, but they differ in how the water and air are separated. Bladder tanks have a replaceable rubber bladder that holds the water, fully isolating it from the air. Diaphragm tanks use a fixed rubber diaphragm.
