    Faucet and Fixture Repair in Tamiami

    Most dripping faucets need a cartridge, not a new faucet. We carry cartridges for Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister, and when you do want new fixtures we install them, connect new braided supply lines, and check the angle stops under the sink while we are there. Old stops fail when touched.

    Pressure Regulators and Shutoff Valves in Tamiami, FL

    Water pressure over 80 psi wears out hoses and valves early. We test static pressure with a gauge on a hose bib, and if it reads high we install or replace a pressure reducing valve and set it in the 50 to 70 psi range most fixtures are rated for. Seized main shutoffs get replaced too.

    Water Heater Repair and Replacement in Tamiami, FL

    Thermocouples, gas valves, elements and relief valves are the common water heater repairs.     Gas Lines for New Appliances in Tamiami, FL
