    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Tamiami, FL

    Converting an old tub to a walk-in shower is one of the most popular bathroom upgrades. We remove the tub, relocate or resize the drain and install a new shower valve at the right height. On slab foundations, the drain may need to be moved, which involves cutting concrete. The result is a safer, more usable shower.

    Standard Shower & Tub Installation in Tamiami

    HDF Plumbing handles the complete installation of standard shower and tub combinations. This includes setting the tub, installing the shower valve, connecting the showerhead and spout, and ensuring proper drainage. We work with various materials like fiberglass, acrylic, and cast iron, making sure the unit is level and securely sealed to prevent leaks, a common issue with older installations that were not done correctly. Our process accounts for existing plumbing layouts to minimize wall alterations.

    Curbless Shower Installation in Tamiami

    Curbless showers have no step, which makes them easier to use and gives a modern look. They require a recessed floor and careful drain placement, often with a linear drain. On a slab, we may need to lower the floor in the shower area. We work closely with your contractor to plan the slope and waterproofing.

    Standard Shower Stall Installation in Tamiami

    HDF Plumbing installs all types of standard shower stalls, from fiberglass units to acrylic enclosures. We make sure of proper leveling, sealing, and connection to your existing plumbing system. Our team handles the entire process, including securing the base and connecting the drain line, to prevent future leaks and ensure stability. We also verify that water pressure is right for your new showerhead.

    New Construction Shower Installation in Tamiami, FL

    For new homes and additions, we rough in shower valves, supply lines and drains during framing. Valves are set to depth for the planned wall finish, and drains are located for the chosen pan. We pass rough-in inspections and return to install trim after tile. Our scheduling keeps your build on track.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Tamiami

    Steam showers need a steam generator, a dedicated water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. We install the generator, plumb the supply and drain, and connect the steam head. A licensed electrician handles the circuit. Steam showers also need sealed enclosures and sloped ceilings.

    Rain and Multi-Head Shower Installation in Tamiami

    Rain showerheads, body sprays and dual heads need careful planning for valve capacity and water pressure. We install thermostatic valves with volume controls and diverters to run multiple outlets. We also confirm your water heater can keep up with higher flow. The result is a spa-like shower that works the way it should.

    Shower Valve & Fixture Installation in Tamiami, FL

    Beyond the main shower unit, we also install new shower valves, diverters, and various fixtures. This includes pressure-balancing valves to prevent scalding, thermostatic valves for consistent temperature, and different showerhead types like rain showers or handheld units. Our team makes sure all connections are watertight and that the fixtures operate smoothly, improving your showering experience. We can upgrade outdated fixtures to newer, more efficient models.
