    Emergency Sewer Cleaning in Tamiami

    Tree Root Removal in Tamiami, FL Sewers

    Roots get in through tiny gaps. Then they fill the pipe. We cut the root ball with a saw head sized for the pipe, then flush the leftovers with a jetter when the growth is heavy, and a camera shows where they are getting in.

    Sewer Cleaning Versus Sewer Repair in Tamiami, FL

    Cleaning cannot fix a broken pipe. A crack stays a crack. If your sewer backs up every few months, the camera usually shows a belly, a crack or a collapsed section, and we compare the yearly cost of cleanings to a spot repair, a liner or a replacement, with the numbers in writing.

    Scheduled Sewer Cleaning in Tamiami, FL

    History predicts the next backup. Repeat lines get a schedule. A line that has backed up before can be cleaned on a set schedule, often every year or two, so it never stops again without warning.

    Preventative Sewer Maintenance in Tamiami

    Roots come back after cutting. Usually within a year or so. Putting a root-prone main on a yearly cleaning schedule keeps the growth small enough that it never stops the line, and it costs far less than an after-hours emergency call.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Tamiami

    Jetting cleans the whole pipe wall. Cabling just opens a hole. Water at 3,000 to 4,000 PSI strips grease, scale and fine roots off the inside of the line and flushes it all toward the street, which leaves the pipe close to its original diameter.
