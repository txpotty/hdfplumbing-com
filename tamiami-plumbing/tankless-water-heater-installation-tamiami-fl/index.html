    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Tamiami, FL

    Calcium scale is what kills most tankless units early. Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving stubborn scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. Scale coats the heat exchanger, cuts efficiency and triggers error codes, and left alone it can crack the exchanger and end the warranty claim before it starts. A yearly flush prevents it. We install isolation valves for that.

    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in Tamiami

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to get hot water to a far bathroom, because the unit has to sense flow and fire first. A recirc pump on a timer or sensor keeps the hot line primed, cutting the wait at the far sink from about a minute to a few seconds. Less water goes down the drain.
