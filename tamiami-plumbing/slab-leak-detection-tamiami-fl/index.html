    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Tamiami

    A lot of newer houses are built on post-tension slabs.     Supply and Drain Leaks Under the Slab in Tamiami, FL

    Supply leaks and drain leaks act differently. Supply leaks are pressurized, so they move the meter, warm the floor on the hot side and run whether or not anyone uses water. Drain leaks only leak when water goes down, showing up as smells, sluggish drains or wet spots near bathrooms. Each needs its own test in your Tamiami, FL home.

    Slab Leaks in Older Homes in Tamiami, FL

    Older homes in Tamiami, FL often have soft copper or galvanized steel under the slab, and both corrode with age. Copper fails at bends and where it rubs concrete, while galvanized rusts from the inside until it splits. We know those failure points. That knowledge points the search at the likeliest spots first.

    Repiping After Repeat Slab Leaks in Tamiami, FL

    Two slab leaks usually means more are coming. The rest of the pipe has likely aged the same way, so repiping replaces every supply line with new PEX or copper through the walls and attic, which ends the pattern of new leaks and new floor openings. A full repipe takes about two to four days.

    Urgent Slab Leak Calls in Tamiami, FL

    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Tamiami

    Water escaping a pressurized pipe makes a hiss that carries up through concrete. A ground microphone picks it up.     Rerouting leaves the failed pipe in the slab. New pipe runs through the walls or attic instead, which avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than opening a finished floor. We usually use PEX because it handles heat and resists corrosion. It suits tile and hardwood homes.
