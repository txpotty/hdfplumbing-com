    How We Keep Water Out of Walls in Tamiami, FL

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Tamiami, FL

    Houses built before modern drainage rules often have no footing drain at all, or a clay tile drain that collapsed decades ago. Many homes in Florida built before the mid 1970s have cast iron drains under the slab, which commonly crack and corrode with age. Fieldstone and old brick foundations need breathable products. A rigid coating on soft brick traps moisture and makes the face spall off over a few seasons, so we use lime-compatible or vapor-open coatings and pair them with interior drain tile. Old mortar joints get repointed first.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Tamiami

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and usually add a dehumidifier to hold humidity under 60 percent. Humidity causes condensation on cold lines and clogged AC condensate drains, a frequent source of hidden water damage. Wood rot slows to a stop. Drier air under the floor also means less musty air upstairs.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Tamiami

    Bare dirt in a crawl space releases gallons of water vapor a day into the air under your floor. A 10 mil or heavier polyethylene barrier, overlapped a foot at every seam and taped, cuts that off and keeps the joists and subfloor dry. We run it up the foundation walls and hold it in place with termination strip. It is a cheap fix that makes a big difference.

    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Tamiami, FL

    Rubber washing machine hoses burst. We swap them for braided stainless hoses, put a drain pan under the washer with a line to a floor drain where one is available, and install a quarter-turn shutoff or an automatic leak valve on the supply box. Water heaters get a pan too. The wall behind them can take a moisture-resistant board and sealer.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Tamiami

    Many homes in Florida built before the mid 1970s have cast iron drains under the slab, which often crack and corrode with age. Any wall below grade sits in soil that gets saturated after heavy rain or snowmelt, and that water pushes against the wall with steady hydrostatic pressure. We inject cracks, coat the wall inside or out, and add drain tile wherever water collects at the footing. A sump goes in if groundwater sits high. Not every basement needs one.

    Commercial Wall Waterproofing in Tamiami

    Restaurant kitchens, gym locker rooms and salon wash stations put water on walls all day. We waterproof commercial showers, mop sink walls and restroom wet walls with membranes listed for continuous wet use, and we repair the supply and drain piping behind them if demolition turns up a leak. After-hours scheduling is available.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Tamiami, FL

    Interior drain tile handles water that already got through the wall. We cut a channel in the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and run it to a sump basin so the pump can push the water outside. The wall still gets wet. It just stops puddling on your floor. This route costs less than digging outside and works where a driveway or deck blocks the wall.

    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Tamiami, FL

    A sump pump is the part of an interior system that actually moves the water. We set a lidded basin below slab level, install a cast iron pump sized to the water coming in, and add a battery backup or water-powered backup for the night the power goes out during a storm. Check valves matter. Without one, the discharge water runs back into the pit and the pump short-cycles. The discharge line gets routed at least 10 feet from the house.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Walls Waterproofing in Tamiami

    Most jobs fall between $3,000 for interior crack sealing and $15,000 or more for exterior excavation with new footing drains. The size of the wall, how wet it gets, which method fits and how hard the wall is to get to all move the number. Access is a big one. Nothing starts until you have the quote in writing.

    Some handy homeowners do. Rolling on a liquid membrane is not hard, but most leaks start at the seams, inside corners and drain, and a pan also needs a correct quarter-inch-per-foot slope plus a 24-hour flood test. Having it professionally installed cuts the risk of hidden water damage.

    Damp proofing is usually a thin spray-on asphalt coat. It slows soil moisture but will not stop water that is pushing against the wall under pressure. Waterproofing uses thicker membranes, drainage boards and footing drains rated to stop liquid water, which is what a basement in wet soil or a high water table needs.

    Schluter Kerdi, Custom RedGard and Laticrete Hydro Ban cover most of our showers. Sheet membranes like Kerdi suit tight schedules because there is nothing to cure, while liquids like RedGard and Hydro Ban go on in two coats and need to dry between them. Each manufacturer's install steps are followed exactly.

    A shower wall job usually takes two to four days from demolition to finished membrane, counting the drying time between coats. Exterior block sealing often wraps up in a day. Bigger foundation jobs take several days, sometimes more if it rains.

    Check the basement after a heavy rain. Wet spots on the walls, mold or mildew, a musty smell in the crawl space, new foundation cracks, efflorescence on the concrete or standing water on the floor all mean water is getting in somewhere. Those signs usually get worse, not better.

    Done right with good materials, a waterproofing system lasts 20 to 50 years. The range is wide because soil movement, the water table and the quality of the original install all play a part, and a crack that opens later can let water around even a good membrane. Drain tile can silt up over time.

    Waterproofing takes away the moisture mold needs to grow. Existing mold should be removed and the area dried before we seal anything, and for larger growth, roughly anything over ten square feet, we recommend a remediation company. We remove smaller affected areas ourselves.
