    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Coconut Creek

    Submersible pumps are common in residential wells, designed to operate submerged within the well casing. When these pumps fail, symptoms often include no water, low water pressure, or strange noises from the well. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing issues like motor burnout, damaged impellers, or faulty check valves that can affect submersible pump performance. We use specialized equipment for safe and efficient pump extraction and reinstallation, ensuring the integrity of your well system.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Coconut Creek, FL

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and relay. Hurricanes and tropical storms bring flooding, power loss, and sewer backups to Florida, often overwhelming drainage systems for days. Lightning and power surges often damage these components. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection and replace failed parts. A surge protector at the control box can help reduce repeat failures.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Coconut Creek

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are common causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most affordable well repairs.

    Pressure Tank Repair in Coconut Creek, FL

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. That rapid cycling burns out pumps early. We check the air charge, inspect the bladder and recharge or replace the tank. Correct tank sizing protects the pump and gives steadier pressure at the tap.

    Well Pump Control Box & Electrical Repair in Coconut Creek, FL

    Many well pump failures trace back to electrical issues within the control box or wiring. This can include tripped breakers, faulty capacitors, or corroded connections. Our licensed technicians are trained to safely diagnose and repair these complex electrical components. We ensure all wiring meets Florida electrical codes, preventing hazards and ensuring reliable pump operation in Coconut Creek homes.
