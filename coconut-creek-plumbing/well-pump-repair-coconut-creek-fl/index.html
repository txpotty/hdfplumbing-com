    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Coconut Creek

    Submersible pumps hang inside the well casing on drop pipe, often 100 feet or more below the wellhead. The usual signs are no water at all, weak pressure or a hum with no flow.     Control Box and Surge Repair in Coconut Creek, FL

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and a relay. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection, replace what failed and recommend a surge arrestor at the box if one is missing. Arrestors are cheap.
