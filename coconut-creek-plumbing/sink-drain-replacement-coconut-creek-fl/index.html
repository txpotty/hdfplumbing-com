    Drain Replacement vs Repair Costs in Coconut Creek, FL

    Pop-Up Drain Assembly Replacement in Coconut Creek, FL

    A stopper that drops on its own has a worn pivot. When the pivot ball wears and the nut stops sealing, the stopper drops or sticks and the nut drips. A new pop-up fixes all of it. Takes about an hour.

    Utility and Basement Sink Drain Replacement in Coconut Creek

    Basement sinks often drain to a pump. If your laundry or utility sink sits below the sewer line, its drain runs to a sink pump or a sewage ejector, and we check the check valve and the discharge line when we replace the trap. Pump trouble is easy to miss. A gurgling pit is a clue.

    P-Trap Replacement in Coconut Creek

    Thin chrome traps are the ones we replace most. They rust inside and their nuts seize, so we cut them out and fit heavy-wall PVC or polished brass. Then it gets a full flow test. Total time is often under an hour.

    Leaking P-Trap Replacement in Coconut Creek

    Traps leak at the joints first. The beveled washers inside the slip nuts flatten and harden over the years, and a trap that has been taken apart several times to clear clogs often has stripped plastic threads or a cracked nut. A new trap kit costs little. Labor is most of the bill.

    Choosing Drain Material in Coconut Creek, FL

    PVC is the default choice under most sinks. It does not rust, it stands up to drain cleaners, and it costs less than brass, while polished 17-gauge brass makes sense under a pedestal sink or any trap you can see. ABS works the same way. We match what you have, with a proper transition where they meet.

    Corroded Drain Replacement in Coconut Creek, FL

    Drain cleaners speed up corrosion. Chemicals left sitting in a metal trap eat through it, so many old traps we pull have pinholes at the bottom. Plastic traps resist that damage. We recommend it for most cabinets.
