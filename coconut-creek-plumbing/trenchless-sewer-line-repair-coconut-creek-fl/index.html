    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Coconut Creek

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one while fracturing the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene pipe, which is highly resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in older Coconut Creek homes. This method is well suited when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.

    Sectional Point Repair in Coconut Creek

    When only one section of a line is damaged, a point repair can fix it without lining the whole length. We position a short resin liner at the damaged spot using the camera and inflate it until it cures. Point repairs are a cost-effective fix for a single crack, hole or root entry. They are common on otherwise healthy lines.

    Sewer Camera Inspections for Trenchless Repair in Coconut Creek

    Before any trenchless repair, a detailed sewer camera inspection is essential. Our high-definition cameras travel through your sewer lines, providing real-time video of the pipe's interior. This diagnostic step makes for a precise and lasting repair in Coconut Creek.

    Pipe Bursting in Coconut Creek, FL

    Pipe bursting replaces a badly damaged line by pulling a bursting head through the old pipe. The head breaks the old pipe outward while new HDPE pipe follows behind it. Bursting is ideal for collapsed or severely offset lines that cannot be lined. It typically requires an entry pit and an exit pit.

    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Coconut Creek, FL

    Sewer lines in Coconut Creek, FL can suffer damage from several factors, including shifting soils that cause pipes to crack or misalign. Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and crack buried water and sewer lines. Tree root intrusion is another major culprit, as roots seek out moisture in pipes and grow, causing blockages and structural damage. Age and corrosion, especially in older cast iron or clay pipes, also contribute to deterioration. Our trenchless solutions address these common issues effectively.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Coconut Creek, FL

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is excellent for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a fast and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by Florida homeowners.
