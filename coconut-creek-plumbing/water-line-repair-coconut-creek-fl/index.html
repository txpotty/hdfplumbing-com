    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Coconut Creek, FL

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Water across much of Florida comes from limestone aquifers and runs hard, leaving heavy scale on fixtures and inside water heaters. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which restricts flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe rarely lasts because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and stops repeat leaks.

    Main Water Line Leak Repair in Coconut Creek

    Most main line leaks show up as a soggy strip of yard, a sudden jump in your water bill or a meter dial that moves with every fixture off. We verify the leak by isolating the house shutoff and watching the meter. Then we use ground microphones to locate the exact spot. A single section is cut out and replaced with repair couplings or a new length of pipe, and the line is pressure tested before we backfill.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Coconut Creek

    If your service line runs under a driveway, pool deck or mature landscaping, trenchless replacement can save a lot of restoration cost. We dig small pits at each end and pull new HDPE or PEX pipe through using a pneumatic boring tool or pipe pulling equipment. The process usually takes one day. You keep your pavers and trees, and the new line carries a lengthy manufacturer warranty.

    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Coconut Creek, FL

    Leaks often start at the compression fittings and couplings right next to the meter box. Shifting soil and heavy traffic over the box can work loose those joints over time. We determine whether the leak is on the utility side or the customer side, since the water provider is responsible for the meter and the pipe before it. When the leak is yours, we replace the fittings and the customer shutoff valve on the same trip.

    Underground Leak Detection in Coconut Creek, FL

    Many water line leaks occur underground, making them difficult to detect without specialized equipment.     Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Coconut Creek
