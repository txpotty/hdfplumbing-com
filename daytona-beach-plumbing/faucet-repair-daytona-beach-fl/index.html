    What We Fix on Faucets in Daytona Beach, FL

    Dripping Faucet Solutions in Daytona Beach

    Every drip has a source, and on a faucet that has dripped for years there is usually more than one worn part. We replace what failed and check the rest. Each valve type, from compression to ceramic disc, wears in its own way, so the repair is matched to the valve inside yours.

    Bathroom Pop-Up Drain Repair in Daytona Beach

    Pop-up drains on bathroom sinks fail at the pivot ball, the clevis strap, the lift rod or the stopper itself, and a slow drain often turns out to be hair wrapped around the stopper. Pull the stopper first. We replace the pop-up assembly in a matching finish when the old one is corroded or the pivot leaks.

    Specialty Faucet Repair in Daytona Beach, FL

    Pot fillers, laundry tub faucets and wall-mount utility faucets each have their own valve styles, and many use compression stems or quarter-turn cartridges that hardware stores do not stock. The common stems ride on the truck. Filtered water faucets at the kitchen sink get new cartridges or check valves as needed.

    Compression Faucet Repair in Daytona Beach

    Two-handle faucets with separate hot and cold handles are often compression faucets, which seal by pressing a rubber washer down onto a brass seat inside the valve. Washers harden and seats get pitted. We replace the washer and dress or replace the seat with a seat wrench, and the packing gets redone if the stem leaks.

    Cartridge Faucet Repair in Daytona Beach, FL

    Single-handle faucets from Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister mostly use replaceable cartridges, and a worn one shows up as a drip, a stiff handle or water that will not hold a steady temperature. The part number depends on the brand and model. We match it. A Moen 1225 cartridge swap takes about thirty minutes.

    Faucet Handle Repair and Replacement in Daytona Beach, FL

    Stripped handle splines, cracked handle bodies, worn adapters and loose set screws make a faucet hard to turn or impossible to shut tight. Many brands sell the handle on its own. We match the handle to the stem or cartridge and the finish, and if the stem itself is stripped we replace it at the same time.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Daytona Beach

    Ceramic disc faucets last a long time, but grit from a water main repair or scale from hard water can scratch the discs or wear out the rubber inlet seals underneath. Inlet seals cost very little. If the discs are scratched, the whole cartridge gets replaced, since the two discs are lapped to fit each other and cannot be resurfaced.

    Leaky Faucet Repair in Daytona Beach

    Most drips come from the part that seals the water inside the faucet, which is a rubber washer on older two-handle faucets and a cartridge or ceramic disc on newer ones. First we identify which type of valve you have. It matters. We carry the common washers, seats and cartridges, so the fix usually happens on the first trip.
