    Rooting uses a spinning cable with cutting blades to break up clogs and roots. Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the pipe walls. Many tough lines benefit from both.

    Generally, simple rooter services to clear clogs do not require a permit in Daytona Beach. However, if the service reveals a need for pipe repair or replacement, especially for main sewer lines, a plumbing permit may be needed. We will handle all required permitting if that situation arises.

    Tree roots are naturally drawn to water and nutrients, which they find in sewer lines through tiny cracks or loose joints. As roots grow, they can expand and completely block the pipe, a common issue in Daytona Beach neighborhoods with mature trees and older pipes.

    Homes with tree roots or older clay pipe often need rooting every 12 to 24 months. Homes with newer PVC lines may go many years without it. A camera inspection can help set the right schedule.
