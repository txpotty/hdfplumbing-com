    Sump Pump Installation in Daytona Beach, FL

    A sump pump that fails during a storm usually failed months earlier: a stuck float, a seized impeller, a dead check valve or a cord unplugged for the shop vac. We dig the pit to the right depth, set a primary pump sized to the inflow and add a battery backup if you want one. Where the water exits matters most. The discharge gets carried well away from the foundation so the same water is not pumped twice.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Daytona Beach, FL

    A basement bathroom sits below the sewer line, so gravity cannot carry the waste out. An ejector pump in a sealed basin lifts it up to the main drain. We size the pump and basin to the fixtures it serves, vent the basin to code, and put a check valve and a full-port shutoff on the discharge so the pump can be pulled for service. We usually install Liberty or Zoeller pumps.

    Sump Pump and Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Daytona Beach

    Basements and crawl spaces that take on water need a sump pump, and fixtures below the sewer line need an ejector. One moves groundwater, the other moves sewage, and they are not interchangeable. Sizing comes first. We match pump capacity to how fast the pit fills, put the pump on its own circuit and run the discharge with a check valve.

    Tank and Tankless Water Heater Installs in Daytona Beach

    Bradford White and Rheem both make good tanks, and the brand matters less than the fit. We look at hot water demand, the space you have and whether gas or electric is cheaper to run at your house. Then we check the Florida code items. On a closed system with a check valve or pressure reducing valve, an expansion tank is usually required, and we set it with the heater.

    PEX Pipe Installation and Repiping in Daytona Beach

    Pinhole leaks in copper keep coming back once the pipe walls start to thin. Repiping in PEX stops the cycle. We run either a manifold with home runs to each fixture, which balances pressure, or a trunk-and-branch layout when the walls make home runs impractical. Access holes are kept small and patched, or left square and clean for your drywall contractor.
