    Softener Loop Installation in Daytona Beach, FL

    Softener loops are the pair of stubbed-out lines near the garage wall or water heater where a softener ties into the main. We can add a loop with shutoff valves and a bypass. Hose bibs stay off it. The drain goes to a laundry box or floor drain with an air gap.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Daytona Beach, FL

    Resin beads break down after 10 to 15 years, faster on chlorinated city water, and the softener keeps regenerating while the water slowly turns hard again. New resin in the old tank sometimes beats a full replacement on price. The valve's age decides it. An old valve on new resin is a short-lived fix.

    Clack Water Softener Replacement in Daytona Beach

    Clack WS1 valves are easy to program and easy to rebuild, which is why a lot of water treatment shops have switched to them. A sound tank can keep its resin and get a new valve. Sometimes the whole unit goes.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Daytona Beach

    Timer softeners regenerate every few days whether you used 50 gallons or 500. A metered valve, Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1, tracks gallons and regenerates when the resin actually needs it. Most people notice less salt in a few weeks.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Daytona Beach

    Salt-based softeners pull calcium and magnesium out of the water by trading them for sodium on a bed of resin beads, which is the only method that actually makes water soft. Old clock-timer units come out and metered valves go in. Salt use usually drops. We set the brine refill to your hardness so the system is not dumping salt down the drain every few days.

    TAC Salt-Free Conditioners in Daytona Beach

    Template-assisted crystallization, or TAC, is the salt-free media with real independent test data behind it, and it is the only kind we install. The tank goes on the main line downstream of the filter, with no power or drain needed. Magnets and clamp-on gadgets are a different story.

    Residential Water Softener Replacement in Daytona Beach

    Scale in the water heater is the expensive part of hard water. A tank water heater on hard water can lose a noticeable share of its efficiency as scale builds on the bottom, and tankless units scale up even faster. A working softener stops that.

    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in Daytona Beach, FL

    Fleck control valves, the 5600SXT and 9100SXT in particular, are on a large share of the softeners we see in Florida homes. If the valve is old enough that parts are hard to find, a new valve on a new tank is the smarter buy. Current Fleck valves fit standard tanks.
