    ADA Urinal Installation in Daytona Beach

    Waterless Urinal Installation in Daytona Beach

    Waterless urinals save the most water and need the most discipline. A cartridge or sealing liquid blocks sewer gas while letting urine through, and it must be replaced on schedule. Your custodial team needs training. We show them how.

    Residential Urinal Plumbing in Daytona Beach, FL

    Home urinals use the same rules as commercial ones. The drain is usually 2 inch, the trap is built into the china, the fixture needs a vent within code distance, and the flush valve needs either a 3/4 inch supply or a lower-flow residential valve on 1/2 inch. Your city may want a permit. We check that first.

    Urinal Installation Cost Factors in Daytona Beach, FL

    Carrier work is the biggest cost swing. Reusing a carrier and lines is cheap, while new carriers and pipe runs through finished walls cost more. Touchless valves cost more than manual. We itemize each piece.

    Waterless Cartridge Urinals in Daytona Beach

    Each waterless brand uses its own cartridge. Each brand uses its own cartridge, and in busy restrooms it may need changing every few months. A replacement schedule comes with the install. Spare cartridges should stay on site.

    ADA-Compliant Urinal Installation in Daytona Beach

    At least one urinal in a public restroom with more than one usually has to be accessible. That means a rim no higher than 17 inches above the floor, an elongated bowl projecting at least 13-1/2 inches from the wall, and a 30 by 48 inch clear floor space in front. The flush handle has to be easy to reach. We mark it all during rough-in.

    Flush Valve Urinal Installation in Daytona Beach, FL

    Flush valve urinals are the commercial standard. A flushometer from Sloan or Zurn delivers a measured volume from a 3/4 inch supply with no tank, and it recovers instantly for the next user, which matters in a stadium or school restroom. The volume matches the fixture rating. Wrong settings stain bowls.

    Drainage and Venting for Urinals in Daytona Beach, FL

    Urinal drains clog with scale, not paper. Urine leaves a hard mineral buildup inside the trap and the drain line, especially on waterless units and low-flow fixtures that send less water through, so we slope the line properly and add a cleanout where we can. A proper vent keeps odor out. We tie in within code distance.
