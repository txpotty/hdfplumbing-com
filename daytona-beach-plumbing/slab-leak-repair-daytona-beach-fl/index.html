    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Daytona Beach, FL

    When the leak is isolated and the rest of the pipe is in good shape, a spot repair through the slab is a practical option. We cut a small opening, excavate to the pipe and replace the damaged section. The trench is compacted and the concrete is patched. Flooring is then restored by your preferred contractor.

    Slab Leak Reroute in Daytona Beach

    Rerouting is often the best long-term choice, especially with older copper lines. We abandon the leaking line under the slab and run a new PEX line through the attic and walls. Openings are small and placed where patching is easiest. Reroutes eliminate future leaks in that section of pipe.

    PEX Repiping for Slab Leaks in Daytona Beach, FL

    If multiple slab leaks point to a systemic issue with your home's original plumbing, a full PEX repipe might be the most cost-effective and durable solution. PEX tubing is flexible, corrosion-resistant, and less prone to leaks caused by foundation shifts, making it a strong choice for many homes. We can replace all the water supply lines in your Daytona Beach home, providing a brand-new, leak-free plumbing system that will last for decades.

    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Daytona Beach

    For more extensive damage, multiple leaks, or pipes that are too old and corroded to repair reliably, pipe rerouting is a long-term solution. This involves abandoning the leaking line under the slab and installing a new pipe through the walls or attic. This method skips the problem area entirely, providing a fresh, durable plumbing line. It is a common solution for aging homes in Daytona Beach with original copper or galvanized plumbing.

    Slab Leak Repair Cost Comparison in Daytona Beach, FL

    Spot repairs usually have a lower upfront cost but involve breaking concrete and repairing flooring. Reroutes cost somewhat more but avoid concrete work and future leaks in that line. Whole-house repipes are the best choice when you have had multiple slab leaks or old copper throughout. We give you written prices for each option.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Daytona Beach, FL

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat expands and contracts the pipe against concrete and gravel.
