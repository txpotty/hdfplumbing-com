    Water Softener Problems We Fix in Daytona Beach, FL

    Resin Testing and Replacement in Daytona Beach

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Chlorine and chloramine in city water slowly break resin down until it turns to mush. We test resin capacity, then clean it with resin cleaner or replace it, and a solid tank and valve with fresh resin works like new for years. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Daytona Beach, FL

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Its small ports clog with sediment and salt. Once it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying, and the softener regenerates with plain water that does nothing to the resin. The injector and screen get cleaned or replaced, and the brine line gets checked.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Service in Daytona Beach, FL

    The tank looks full but makes no brine. A salt bridge has formed and stopped the salt from dissolving. Bridges get broken up, mushy salt gets cleaned out and the float, brine well and safety valve get checked. Pellet salt bridges less in warm or humid spaces, so we may suggest switching if your softener sits in a hot garage.

    Water Softener Leak Detection and Repair in Daytona Beach, FL

    Cracked tanks, loose fittings and failed O-rings are behind most softener leaks. A small drip still wastes water and damages flooring. Once we find the source, from the bypass to the drain connection, the failed seal or fitting gets replaced. Then it gets a pressure test.

    Control Valve Troubleshooting & Repair in Daytona Beach, FL

    Stuck pistons, worn seals and failed drive motors cause most control valve trouble, and the symptoms are hard water, constant regeneration or a leak at the head. We diagnose the valve on site. Seal kits and pistons for Fleck, Clack and Autotrol valves ride on the truck, so most rebuilds happen on the first visit.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Daytona Beach

    Hard water coming back usually means the softener has stopped regenerating. A failed timer motor, a bad circuit board or a stuck meter turbine are the usual causes, along with settings lost in a power outage. We test each part. Then we program the valve for your household and hardness, and soft water returns by the next cycle.

    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Daytona Beach, FL

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. A new quality softener installed typically costs $1,500 to $3,500. With a good tank and valve, repair is the better value. If the valve is obsolete and parts are scarce, replacing the head or the whole unit may make more sense than chasing used parts online.

    A softener stuck in regeneration can run water to the drain for hours or flood a utility room. We handle these calls quickly.
