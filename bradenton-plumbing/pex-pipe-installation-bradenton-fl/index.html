    Copper to PEX Conversions in Bradenton

    Copper lines can develop pinhole leaks from water chemistry, high velocity and sometimes soil contact under slabs. Once a home has had several pinhole leaks, more often follow. Converting to PEX stops the cycle and reduces the risk of hidden water damage. We use approved transition fittings wherever new PEX connects to existing copper stubs or valves.
