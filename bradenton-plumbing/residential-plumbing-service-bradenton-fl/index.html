    Residential Plumbing Service in Bradenton, FL: What We Fix

    Slab Leak Location and Repair in Bradenton

    Slab leaks hide under concrete until something gives them away. Nearly all homes in Florida sit on concrete slabs over sandy soil, so leaks under the slab are a common and costly repair. Our techs find them with ground microphones and thermal imaging, so the only concrete we break is the small patch right over the leak, not half the kitchen floor. Then you pick the fix. Some leaks get a spot repair, others a reroute overhead that skips the slab entirely.

    Sump Pumps and Basement Drainage in Bradenton

    A sump pump only gets tested when it rains hard. We install and replace cast iron submersible pumps, check float switches and replace worn check valves, and we can add a battery backup so the pit keeps draining when the power goes out in a storm. Pour a bucket in to test it.

    Leak Repair and Slab Leak Service in Bradenton

    On a slab foundation, a supply leak can run for weeks unseen. Warm spots on tile, a meter that spins with every tap off or a water bill that doubles are the usual clues. We pinpoint it before quoting. Fixes range from a direct repair to rerouting the line overhead or a full repipe when leaks keep coming back.

    Water Heater Repair and Replacement in Bradenton, FL

    Thermocouples, gas valves, elements and relief valves are the common water heater repairs. We service Rheem, Bradford White, Navien and Rinnai units, and when a tank is leaking from the shell we replace it with proper venting, an expansion tank and a drain pan, usually the same day. We haul the old tank away.

    Pipe Repair and Repiping for Older Homes in Bradenton, FL

    Galvanized steel and polybutylene are the two pipes we replace most. Newer homes across Florida mostly use PEX or CPVC with PVC drains, though copper pinhole leaks are still a common repair in older homes. We repipe with PEX or Type L copper, running new lines through the attic and walls with small access holes, and the water stays on at night for most of the job. Expect two to four days.
