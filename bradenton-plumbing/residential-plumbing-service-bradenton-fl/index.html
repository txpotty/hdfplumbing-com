    Slab Leak Detection & Repair in Bradenton

    Slab leaks are a serious concern for homes built on concrete foundations. Nearly all homes in Florida sit on concrete slabs over sandy soil, so leaks under the slab are a common and costly repair. Our technicians use advanced, non-invasive acoustic and thermal imaging technology to accurately pinpoint the exact location of the leak under your foundation. Early detection is critical to prevent significant structural damage and costly water waste. Once located, we provide efficient repair options, which may include direct access, rerouting or pipe bursting, always choosing the least disruptive method for your home.

    Sump Pump and Basement Drainage in Bradenton

    Leak Repair and Slab Leak Service in Bradenton

    Many homes sit on concrete slabs, so a hidden leak under the floor can run for weeks before you see it. Warm spots on tile, a running meter or a spike in your water bill are common clues. We use acoustic listening equipment, thermal imaging and pressure tests to pinpoint the leak. Then we repair it directly, reroute the line overhead or recommend a repipe when leaks keep returning.
