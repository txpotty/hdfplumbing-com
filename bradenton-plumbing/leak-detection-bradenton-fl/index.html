    Pool Leak Detection in Bradenton

    A pool losing more than a quarter inch a day beyond evaporation likely has a leak somewhere. We start with a bucket test. Then we pressure test the suction and return lines, listen for leaks with a hydrophone underwater, and dye test around the skimmer, lights and main drain so we can point to the exact fitting or crack.

    Our Leak Detection Equipment in Bradenton

    Our trucks carry acoustic correlators and ground microphones, thermal cameras, line tracers, moisture meters and tracer gas kits. Each tool has a job. Acoustic gear hears pressurized water escaping, thermal cameras show temperature differences from hot water lines and wet drywall, and tracer gas finds leaks that make no sound at all on plastic pipe.

    Slab Leak Signs in Bradenton, FL

    Warm floor spots, cracked tile and a meter that never stops are the common signs. So is the sound of water with everything off. We isolate hot and cold lines with pressure tests, then trace and listen through the slab, and once it is located we go over spot repair, reroute and repipe options with prices for each.

    Preventive Leak Inspections in Bradenton, FL

    Checking for leaks once a year catches the small stuff. We test static pressure, read the meter with everything off and look at supply lines, water heater fittings and shutoff valves. Older homes with galvanized pipe or original water heater connectors get the most out of this, because those parts often fail together. Short visit, big savings.

    Water Line Leak Detection in Bradenton, FL

    Supply leaks hide inside walls, above ceilings and under yards. The bill usually tells you first. We read the meter, close fixture stops one at a time and use a moisture meter to find the wet framing, then confirm with acoustic gear on pressurized lines, so the repair opening stays small in your Bradenton home.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection in Bradenton, FL

    A cracked drain line leaks wastewater into the soil, which can show up as a sewer smell in the yard, a soggy patch or slow drains. We use a camera for these. Our tech runs a camera from the cleanout to the city tap, recording cracks, offset joints and root entry in PVC, clay and cast iron, so you see the exact distance and depth of the break.

    Gas Line Leak Locating in Bradenton

    Gas leaks are found by test, not by nose. Our plumbers pressure test the system with a gauge, and check every joint with soap and a detector. We also walk the yard line, since a dead strip of grass over a buried gas pipe can signal a leak that has run for a long time. Utility coordination is included.
