    Laundry and Wet Bar Ejector Pumps in Bradenton, FL

    Below-grade laundry rooms and wet bars produce gray water that still needs to be lifted to the sewer.     Liberty Pumps Installation in Bradenton

    Liberty ejector pumps are a common pick on our jobs, with cast iron bodies and two-inch solids handling on most sewage models.     New Construction Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Bradenton, FL

    On a new build, we set the basin ahead of the slab pour. That is the cheapest time, since there is no concrete to cut, and we stub the drains, the vent and the discharge to the plans so the rough-in inspection goes smoothly. It keeps the pour on schedule.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin & Pit Installation in Bradenton

    Digging the pit means cutting the slab and excavating below it, deep enough to set the basin flush with the finished floor. We bed the basin on gravel and backfill around it so it cannot float when groundwater rises, then pour new concrete to seal the floor and lock the basin in place. A floating basin breaks pipe.
