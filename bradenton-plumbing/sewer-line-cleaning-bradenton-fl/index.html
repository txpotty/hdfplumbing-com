    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Line Cleaning in Bradenton

    Cabling a sewer line in Bradenton usually costs $200 to $500. Hydro jetting costs more. Adding a recorded camera inspection is often another $150 to $350 on top of the cleaning.

    Sectional cables, root saws and jetters. Plus a sewer camera. We use cables for most jobs, jetters for heavy buildup and a camera to see the line before and after.

    Once or twice a year for homes with older pipe or big trees. Some never need it. Homes with frequent clogs or commercial properties with heavy use may need more frequent cleaning, timed before roots or grease close the line.

    Older homes often have clay or cast iron, and some have Orangeburg fiber pipe. Most homes built in recent decades have ABS or PVC. Many homes in Florida built before the mid 1970s have cast iron drains under the slab, which often crack and corrode with age.
