    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Bradenton, FL

    Standard Tank Tune-Up in Bradenton

    A standard tune-up starts with the fuel off and the cold supply closed, then a full drain through the bottom valve until the water runs clear. Figure on half an hour or a bit more for the drain. We check the heating elements or burner, read the thermostat setting and look at every fitting on top of the tank for green crust or drips. Any weeping at the dielectric nipples goes on the report.

    Anode Rod Replacement in Bradenton, FL

    Water chemistry changes from one street to the next, and so does anode life. Three to five years is typical. A softened water supply can chew through a magnesium rod in two.     Relief Valve Testing in Bradenton

    Temperature and pressure relief valves on residential tanks are typically rated to open at 150 psi or 210 degrees, and they can stick shut after years of mineral buildup. Our tech lifts the lever, lets water flow, then watches it close. If it keeps dripping, we replace it. We also confirm the discharge pipe runs full size to within six inches of the floor or to an approved drain, which is what plumbing codes ask for.

    T&P Valve Replacement in Bradenton

    Replacing a relief valve is a short job, but it has to be done right. We match the new valve to the heater's input rating. We drain the tank below the valve opening, thread in the new one with pipe dope rated for potable water, and reconnect a discharge pipe in copper, CPVC or another approved material. A capped discharge pipe is a serious hazard.
