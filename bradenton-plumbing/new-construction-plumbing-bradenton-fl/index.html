    Frequently Asked Questions About New Construction Plumbing in Bradenton

    Most new single-family homes in the Bradenton area cost between $12,000 and $25,000 to plumb, with larger custom homes running higher. A bathroom adds a few thousand on its own. Tankless heaters and gas runs add to it. Our bid is fixed per phase, so the number you sign is the number you pay unless the plans change.

    Plumbing is usually covered under the general building permit. Where a separate plumbing permit is required, we pull it in our name as the licensed contractor. Either way, we schedule the plumbing inspections, attend them, and handle any corrections without the builder having to chase us down about it.

    Slow drains in a brand-new house are the first sign. Gurgling or a sewer smell usually means a vent or slope issue. A camera inspection through the cleanout will show a belly, an offset joint or debris left in the line, and it is worth doing before the builder's warranty period ends. Construction debris is common.

    For supply, we use PEX-A or PEX-B in most homes, sometimes with a copper main. Drains are PVC or ABS. Cast iron is still used in some multifamily buildings and in walls next to bedrooms where drain noise matters, since it is much quieter than plastic when a toilet flushes upstairs.

    The term is written into your contract before work starts. It covers our labor and the fittings we supply. Fixtures, water heaters and PEX systems carry their own manufacturer warranties, and we register water heaters in the owner's name at final so the claim process is simple later.

    PEX handles most water supply in new homes. It flexes and needs fewer fittings in the walls than copper. For drain, waste and vent we use Schedule 40 PVC or ABS, whichever your code and plans call for. Copper still shows up at water heaters.

    Expect plumbing to touch the schedule three times. Underground takes one to three days. Top-out usually takes three to five days on a single-family house, and trim takes one or two days once cabinets, counters and tile are in place. Inspections add a day each.

    Workmanship is covered under our written warranty, and the length is spelled out in your contract. Manufacturer warranties cover the fixtures themselves. PEX and water heater warranties can run ten years or longer if the matching fittings are used, so we keep each system one brand.
