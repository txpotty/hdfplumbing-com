    What Our Repipe Crews Do in Pinellas Park, FL

    PEX Repiping in Pinellas Park, FL

    PEX is flexible plastic tubing that can run long distances without a fitting, which means fewer joints hidden in your walls. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. It resists scale and chlorine, handles a freeze better than copper and costs less to install, which is why most of our repipes use it. Our PEX-A installs use expansion fittings that do not shrink the flow path. Flow stays strong at the last shower.

    Commercial Repipe Services in Pinellas Park

    Older commercial buildings in Pinellas Park often have galvanized risers, copper that has pinholed or plastic pipe that no longer meets code. We replace supply risers, branch lines and drains while keeping offices, shops or apartments running as much as possible. Shutoffs get scheduled ahead. Work is phased by zone, and we coordinate access, keys and noise limits with the property manager.

    Slab Leak Repiping in Pinellas Park

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and crack buried water and sewer lines. Instead of breaking concrete to patch one pipe, we reroute new supply lines overhead through the attic and down interior walls, then abandon the old lines in the slab. No jackhammer needed. That stops the current leak and removes the chance of the next one under the same floor.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Pinellas Park, FL

    Older homes may still have galvanized steel pipe that rusts from the inside out. Weak flow and rusty water are common. We remove or abandon the galvanized lines and replace them with PEX or copper, and your water pressure and water quality usually improve the same day the new lines go into service.

    Residential Repiping Plans in Pinellas Park

    Every house is piped a little differently, so a repipe starts with a walk-through. We count fixtures, check attic and crawlspace access, look at where the main comes in and ask about plans for future remodels before we draw a route. Then we size the lines. A three-bath house with a tankless heater may need 1-inch main trunk lines to keep showers steady when the washer kicks on.

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Pinellas Park

    Old galvanized lines shed rust flakes that clog aerators, faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. Clogged aerators are a clue. Replacing galvanized with PEX or copper ends the rust, brings pressure back and stops the leaks that start at the threaded joints where galvanized is thinnest. Old pipe stays in walls when pulling it would wreck finished surfaces.

Copper commonly costs 20 to 40 percent more than PEX, and some owners prefer it anyway for its long history. Our quotes for either include permit, fittings, patching and cleanup.
