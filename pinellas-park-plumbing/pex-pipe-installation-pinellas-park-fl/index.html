    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Pinellas Park, FL

    With PEX-A, the tech slips a ring over the pipe end and stretches both with a battery tool. The fitting is pushed in while the pipe is open, and the plastic's memory shrinks it back around the barb, sealing tighter during the first minute. In the cold, it takes longer to shrink back. Joints get time to set before we pressurize.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Pinellas Park

    PEX-B is stiffer, costs less per foot, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. Since a crimp is only as good as the tool that made it, every ring gets a go/no-go gauge check, and any ring that fails gets cut off and redone. It is a five-second check. Skipping it is how leaks start.
