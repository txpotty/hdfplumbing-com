    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Pinellas Park

    Many homes in Florida built before the mid 1970s have cast iron drains under the slab, which often crack and corrode with age.     Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Pinellas Park, FL

    A sump pump is the part of an interior system that actually moves the water. We set a lidded basin below slab level, install a cast iron pump sized for the inflow, and add a battery backup or water-powered backup for the night the power goes out during a storm. Check valves matter. A missing check valve lets the discharge line drain back into the pit and the pump short-cycles. The discharge line gets routed far enough out that it drains away from the house.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Pinellas Park

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing to carry the water away from the wall.
