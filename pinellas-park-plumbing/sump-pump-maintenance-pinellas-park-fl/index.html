    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Maintenance in Pinellas Park

    A standard sump pump inspection and cleaning typically runs $125 to $250. Replacing a float switch or check valve typically adds $75 to $250 depending on parts.

    Watch out for odd noises, such as grinding or rattling, that point to debris or a failing motor. If the pump runs constantly, fails to turn on or gives off a foul odor from the pit, it's definitely time for a professional check. Visible rust or corrosion are also red flags.

    If your primary sump pump fails during a power outage and you don't have a working battery backup or water-powered backup system, your basement is at risk of flooding. This underscores the importance of maintaining backup systems.

    We recommend having your sump pump professionally maintained at least once a year. If your home is in an area prone to heavy rainfall or has a high water table, twice-yearly maintenance might be more beneficial. Regular checks confirm it's ready for peak seasons.

    We service Zoeller, Liberty, Wayne, Little Giant, Superior and most other residential and commercial pumps. We can also recommend upgrades for pits that need more pumping power.

    Most quality pumps last seven to ten years. Pumps that sit dry for long periods can seize sooner, which is why testing matters during dry months.

    A standard sump pump maintenance appointment usually takes about 30 to 60 minutes. This time allows for a complete inspection, cleaning and testing of all components. If minor repairs are needed, the visit might run a little longer.
