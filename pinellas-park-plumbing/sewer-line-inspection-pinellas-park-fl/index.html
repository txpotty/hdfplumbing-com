    Belly and Offset Detection in Pinellas Park, FL

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds water and waste. Offsets happen when pipe sections shift out of alignment, often because of soil movement. Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and damage buried water and sewer lines. The camera shows these defects clearly, and we mark their location for targeted repair.
