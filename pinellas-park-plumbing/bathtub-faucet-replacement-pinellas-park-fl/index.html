    Thermostatic Tub Valve Installation in Pinellas Park, FL

    Thermostatic valves hold the temperature you set. They sense real water temperature, not just pressure, which helps kids and older adults avoid hot spikes. Volume and temperature often get separate handles. They cost more than pressure-balance.

    Thermostatic and Pressure-Balance Valves in Pinellas Park, FL

    Pressure-balance valves react to pressure drops. They adjust flow when a toilet flushes so the mix holds, and thermostatic valves hold an exact temperature. Either one satisfies scald rules. Thermostatic costs more.
