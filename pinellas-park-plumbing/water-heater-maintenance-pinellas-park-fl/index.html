    Tankless Water Heater Service in Pinellas Park, FL

    Tankless units need descaling.     Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Pinellas Park

    Electric tanks fail at the lower element. Sediment piles up around the lower element, it runs hotter than it should, and eventually it burns out, which is why you suddenly get only a few minutes of hot water. Both elements get tested. Thermostats and wiring get checked with the power off.
