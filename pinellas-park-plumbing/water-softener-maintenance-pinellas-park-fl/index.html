    Brine Tank Cleanout and Inspection in Pinellas Park, FL

    Yearly Water Softener Tune-Ups in Pinellas Park

    A tune-up starts with two hardness readings and ends with a full manual regeneration that we watch from start to finish. In between, we check the salt level, the brine well and the float. It takes about an hour on most homes.

    Breaking Up Salt Bridges in Pinellas Park

    To check for a bridge, we push a broom handle straight down through the salt. A hard stop, then a drop, means a bridge. We break the crust, vacuum out the salt mush below it, then check the brine well, safety float, brine line and grid plate before refilling to about half full.

    Salt-Free Conditioner Service in Pinellas Park
