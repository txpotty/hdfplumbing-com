    Annual Tankless Checkup in Pinellas Park, FL

    The annual checkup is a top-to-bottom pass on the heater. We read gas pressure at the inlet with a manometer while the burner is at full fire and compare it to the rating plate.     Same-Day Tankless Help in Pinellas Park, FL

    A blinking error code and a cold shower usually mean the heater protected itself from something. Usually it is a blocked exhaust or heavy scale. Emergency calls get common parts and a descaling kit, and most heaters run again before we leave.

    Tankless Error Code Diagnosis in Pinellas Park, FL

    An error code is the heater telling you what it saw. On a Rinnai an LC code means scale, on a Navien E003 means no ignition, and on a Noritz a 90 usually means an air or exhaust problem. We pull the code history before touching anything. If a part has failed, you hear the repair and the price before we start.

    Multi-Unit Tankless Checks in Pinellas Park, FL

    Restaurants and salons often run two or more tankless heaters on a shared manifold, and one weak unit can drag the whole bank down. Gas pressure gets read with every unit firing together. Each heater gets its own flow and temperature reading. Then we compare them.

    Flush Now or Replace the Exchanger Later in Pinellas Park, FL

    A new heat exchanger can run $600 to $1,500 or more installed, and some manufacturers will not cover one that failed from scale. Descaling costs far less. Cared for, many units run 15 to 20 years. We will also say whether a softener or a scale inhibitor makes sense for your water.

    Rinnai Tankless Service in Pinellas Park

    Rinnai units tell you a lot through the controller. A code 11 is a failed ignition, while code 12 is a flame that lit and then dropped out. We clean the Rinnai air intake filter and look over the flame rod for soot or cracks. The LC codes are scale warnings, and they usually clear once the exchanger has been flushed and the unit is reset.

    Condensate and Neutralizer Service in Pinellas Park

    Condensing tankless heaters drip acidic condensate, often around pH 3 to 4, and it needs to pass through a neutralizer before it reaches a drain. The trap gets cleaned and the media checked. We replace spent media. An outdoor run of condensate line gets checked for support and protection.

    Tankless Vent Inspection in Pinellas Park

    Gas tankless heaters push exhaust out through a vent that has to stay sealed from the heater all the way to the termination outside the house. Condensing units use PVC or polypropylene vent pipe, pitched back toward the heater so condensate drains. We check every joint and test for carbon monoxide near the unit. Wasp nests in vent caps are common.
