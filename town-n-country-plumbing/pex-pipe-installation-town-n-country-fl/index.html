    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Town 'n' Country, FL

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. All three are code-listed for drinking water. We match the pipe to the fittings and tools from one maker so the warranty stays intact.
