    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Town 'n' Country

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. They change the minerals into crystals that do not stick to pipe walls and heating elements, so scale drops off without adding sodium or using a drain line. Maintenance is light. They suit moderate hardness.

    Pelican Water Softener Installation in Town 'n' Country, FL

    Pelican sells both salt-based softeners and salt-free conditioners, often direct to homeowners.     High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Town 'n' Country

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. A new metered unit often uses far less salt and water than an older model on a timer, which shows up on both bills. The savings add up. We install several models.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Town 'n' Country, FL

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. HDF Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. Most effective option.
