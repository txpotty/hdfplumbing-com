    Pressure-Assist and Dual-Flush Repair in Town 'n' Country

    Pressure-assist toilets use a sealed vessel inside the china that compresses air with incoming water pressure, and the cartridge inside that vessel eventually wears out. You will not find most of these parts at a hardware store. We carry Flushmate cartridges and dual-flush seals for the common TOTO and American Standard models, and we check house pressure, since these units need steady line pressure to flush properly. Low pressure makes them sound weak.

    Clogged Toilet Solutions in Town 'n' Country, FL

    Paper towels, wipes, dental floss and toys cause most of the repeat clogs we clear, and none of them break down the way toilet paper does. An auger pulls them back out. If the toilet clogs again within a week, the problem is further down the line. We check the branch drain and the main next.

    Running Toilet Fixes in Town 'n' Country

    Phantom refills, the tank topping itself off for a few seconds in the middle of the night, almost always mean a slow leak at the flapper.     Leaking Toilet Repair in Town 'n' Country

    Water on the floor can come from four places: the wax ring, the tank-to-bowl gasket, the supply line or sweat running off a cold tank. Each one needs a different fix.     Weak Flush and Clog Repair in Town 'n' Country, FL

    Needing two flushes on a regular basis usually comes down to scale in the rim jets, a clogged siphon jet, a partial blockage in the trapway, or a flush valve that does not open all the way.     Fill Valve Replacement in Town 'n' Country

    Old ballcock valves with a brass arm and a big plastic float are loud and slow to refill, and they tend to stick open when scale builds up in the ports.
