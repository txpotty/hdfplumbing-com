    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Town 'n' Country

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. An attic heater needs a pan with its own drain line, a properly routed relief line and enough access to get the old tank out and the new one in. Ceilings are at stake. Relocation to a safer spot is an option.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Town 'n' Country

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Each one gets a new gas connector, a sediment trap and a shutoff valve, plus a vent connector pitched up toward the flue at a quarter inch per foot. We test draft before leaving. Gas suits busy households.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Town 'n' Country, FL

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Gas tankless models usually need a bigger gas line than the old tank had, plus a dedicated vent run through the wall or roof. Upfront cost is higher. We check gas capacity before quoting.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Town 'n' Country

    Closed plumbing systems trap the extra volume water gains when it heats. Insurance companies in Florida often ask about plumbing age and material, so permitted work by a licensed plumber can help with coverage. A small expansion tank on the cold line absorbs that extra volume, which keeps the relief valve from weeping and saves wear on faucets and fittings. We pre-charge it to your house pressure. It mounts on the cold inlet.

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Town 'n' Country, FL

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match voltage and breaker size to the panel, and since there is no vent, an electric tank can sit in spots a gas unit cannot. A drain pan goes under closet installs. Quiet and simple.

    Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation in Town 'n' Country

    Tank heaters still heat most homes' water, holding 40 to 75 gallons at temperature until you need it. We size the tank by first-hour rating, not just gallons, because that number tells you how much hot water it delivers in a busy morning. Gas or electric both work. We connect the fuel, set the vent and haul the old tank away.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Town 'n' Country, FL

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank. They need roughly 700 cubic feet of air around them and a condensate drain, so a warm garage or a large utility room usually works better than a closet. Rebates may apply. We will tell you if your space works.

    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Town 'n' Country, FL

    Water on the garage floor and no hot water usually means the tank has rusted through. First step is to close the cold water valve above the tank and shut off the gas or the breaker, which stops the leak from getting worse. We carry common sizes. Same-day replacement is normal.
