    Condensing Tankless Repair in Town 'n' Country, FL

    Condensing tankless heaters pull extra heat out of the exhaust and make acidic condensate that has to drain somewhere. We clean the trap, refill the neutralizer and check the PVC vent for sag or the wrong slope.
