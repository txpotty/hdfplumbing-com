    Larger restaurants and high-volume kitchens often need an outdoor gravity interceptor. These concrete or polyethylene tanks typically range from several hundred to over 1,500 gallons. A properly installed interceptor can go much longer between pump-outs than an indoor trap.

    Grease Trap Sizing and Placement in North Miami, FL

    Correct sizing and strategic placement are critical for a grease trap's effectiveness. Installing a unit that is too small will lead to frequent clogs and overflow, while an oversized one can be an unnecessary expense. Our plumbers meticulously calculate the required capacity based on your kitchen's water flow, fixture count, and operating hours, delivering optimal performance for your North Miami, FL business.

    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in North Miami

    Hydromechanical grease traps sit under or near sinks and use baffles and flow control to separate grease from water. They are common in small cafes, coffee shops and quick-service restaurants with limited space. We install units from Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura sized by gallons per minute. Each install includes a flow control fitting and proper venting to keep the trap working as designed. These units need frequent cleaning, so we place them where staff can reach the lid easily.
