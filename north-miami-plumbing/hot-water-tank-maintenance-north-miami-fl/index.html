    Standard Tank Flushing in North Miami

    Flushing is the one job every tank needs, gas or electric, new or old. The harder the water, the sooner it matters.     Anode Rod Replacement in North Miami, FL

    Water chemistry changes from one street to the next, and so does anode life. Most rods last three to five years. A softened water supply can chew through a magnesium rod in two.     Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in North Miami, FL

    The anode rod is a sacrificial metal rod that corrodes so the steel tank does not.     Gas Venting Tune-Up in North Miami, FL

    Atmospheric gas tanks vent by draft alone, so the vent connector needs upward slope of at least a quarter inch per foot and tight joints with three screws each.     Sediment Flush and Descaling in North Miami

    Calcium carbonate drops out of hard water every time the tank heats, and over a year it can harden into chunks the size of a thumbnail. That is what makes the popping noise. For stubborn scale we use a potable-rated descaler, then flush it clean. A clean bottom puts less heat stress on the seam.
