    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Installation in North Miami

    Labor to install a customer-supplied standard toilet typically runs $200 to $400. Including a mid-range toilet, most installs total $450 to $900, while wall-hung systems with carriers range from $1,500 to $3,500 or more. We give you a firm price up front.

    A one-piece toilet has the bowl and tank integrated into a single unit, offering a sleek look and easier cleaning. A two-piece toilet has a separate bowl and tank, which are bolted together during installation. Both types are easy to find and perform well.

    When choosing a new toilet, consider factors like bowl shape (round vs. elongated), height (standard vs. ADA-compliant), flushing system (gravity-fed vs. pressure-assisted), water efficiency (gallons per flush), and overall style to match your bathroom. We can help you navigate these choices.

    TOTO, Kohler and American Standard reliably perform well in flush tests and have good parts availability. Gerber and Mansfield are solid budget options. We help you match the brand and model to your needs.

    Installing a new toilet can offer several benefits, including improved water efficiency, reduced water bills, better flushing power, a better look, and increased comfort. Modern toilets are also designed to be more durable and simpler to clean.

    The rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, usually 12 inches but sometimes 10 or 14. Buying a toilet with the wrong rough-in means it will not fit or will sit too far from the wall.

    A standard toilet installation, assuming no major plumbing modifications are needed, usually takes about 1 to 3 hours. More complex installations, such as those requiring new rough-ins or dealing with damaged subflooring, can take longer.
