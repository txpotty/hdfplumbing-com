    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in North Miami, FL

    Electric tank water heaters are common in homes without natural gas service. We install models with the correct voltage and breaker size for your electrical panel. These units do not need venting, which allows more flexible placement. We also install a drain pan when the heater is in a closet or attic. Electric models are simple, dependable and quiet.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in North Miami

    Some local homes have water heaters in attics or interior closets. These installs require careful planning for access, drain pans and relief line routing. Attic temperatures can swing widely with the seasons, so we take extra steps to protect connections. We install drain pans with proper drain lines to protect ceilings. If relocation makes sense, we can move the heater to a safer spot.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in North Miami

    Gas tank water heaters provide strong recovery and are common in many local homes. We install 40, 50 and 75 gallon models with proper venting and combustion air. Each install includes a new gas connector, sediment trap and shutoff valve. We check draft and test for gas leaks. Gas units are a solid choice for families with high hot water demand.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in North Miami

    A standard 40 to 50 gallon tank water heater typically costs $1,600 to $3,500 installed. Tankless installations often cost $3,000 to $6,500 depending on gas line and venting needs.

    To improve efficiency, make sure your new water heater is properly sized for your home, consider a tankless model for on-demand heating, insulate hot water pipes and set the thermostat to 120°F (49°C) to prevent scalding and cut energy consumption.

    An expansion tank absorbs excess water pressure created as water heats up inside the tank. Many homes in North Miami, FL, especially those with closed-loop plumbing systems, are required by code to have an expansion tank to protect the water heater and plumbing system from damage.

    Yes, we stand behind our work. Our water heater installations come with a labor warranty, and the water heater unit itself will have a manufacturer's warranty, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years, depending on the model.

    Common signs that you might need a new water heater include a lack of hot water, rusty or discolored water coming from the hot tap, banging or rumbling noises from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit and an age of 10 to 15 years or more. If you notice any of these issues, it is best to have a professional inspection.

    If your home has a pressure reducing valve or check valve, an expansion tank is typically required. It protects your water heater and pipes from pressure spikes.
