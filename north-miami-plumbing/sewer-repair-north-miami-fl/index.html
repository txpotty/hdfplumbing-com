    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in North Miami

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg pipe, a tar-paper material that flattens and blisters over time. Cast iron from the same era corrodes from the inside and scales up until little flow remains. When a camera shows widespread failure, patching is a waste of money. We replace the line in sections or end to end with PVC, and we can usually use trenchless methods to avoid tearing up the whole yard.

    Root Intrusion Repair in North Miami, FL

    Tree and shrub roots go straight for the moisture and nutrients leaking from old sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense mats that catch paper and waste. We cut the roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro-jetter, then repair or seal the joint so they cannot come right back. For lines with many rooted joints, we will show you when lining or replacement makes more sense than repeated cleaning.

    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in North Miami

    Before any repair, we use high-resolution video camera inspection to precisely locate and assess sewer line damage.
