    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in North Miami

    Undermount sinks need strong clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. We mount the bowl, seal the rim with silicone, and support it while the adhesive sets. Then we install strainers, traps and supply lines. A proper undermount install prevents gaps and leaks along the counter edge.

    Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in North Miami

    Drop-in sinks rest on the countertop with a rim, making them a good choice for laminate and tile. We check the cutout size, seal the rim, and tighten mounting clips evenly. This is usually the fastest and most affordable kitchen sink upgrade. It also works well in rentals and older homes.

    New Construction Sink Plumbing in North Miami, FL

    For new home builds or major kitchen renovations in North Miami, FL, proper rough-in plumbing for kitchen sinks is essential. HDF Plumbing works with contractors and homeowners to install all necessary drain lines, water supply lines, and vent piping according to blueprints and local codes. We make sure the plumbing is positioned for your chosen sink and faucet, preventing costly adjustments later. Our careful approach guarantees a solid foundation for your kitchen's plumbing system.

    Workstation Sink Installation in North Miami, FL

    Workstation sinks include ledges for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks. They are often deep, single-bowl designs with offset drains. We position the drain to fit your cabinet and disposal and confirm the faucet reach. These sinks are a common pick in modern kitchen remodels.

    Under-mount Sink Installation in North Miami, FL

    Under-mount sinks provide a sleek, seamless look, making countertop cleaning easier. Their installation is more complex than top-mount sinks, requiring precise measurements and strong adhesive or clips for secure attachment to the underside of the counter. HDF Plumbing excels in under-mount sink installation, ensuring a robust bond and a perfectly sealed edge to prevent water infiltration. We confirm the cutout is smooth and the sink is level for optimal drainage.
