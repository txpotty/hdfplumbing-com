    Stainless Sinks With Sound Pads in North Miami

    Cheap stainless bowls are noisy when dishes hit them or the disposal runs. Better sinks come with rubber pads and an undercoating, and if yours does not, we can add a coat of spray undercoating before it goes in. It is a small thing. Once the bowl is mounted, the space underneath is hard to reach, so this is a job worth doing while the sink is still on the floor.

    Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in North Miami

    Sitting on the counter by their rim, drop-in sinks are a natural fit for tile, laminate and butcher block counters. It is often the quickest swap. After checking the cutout, we seal the rim and draw the clips down evenly, and in older homes and rentals it is often a two-hour job.

    Farmhouse Sink Plumbing in North Miami, FL

    Fireclay and cast iron farmhouse sinks can weigh well over 100 pounds empty, before a single drop of water goes in. A support frame goes into the cabinet and the sink is leveled before the countertop goes on. Deep bowls need a lower trap. The drain gets planned for that depth from day one.

    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in North Miami

    An undermount depends on its clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. We mount the bowl, seal the rim with silicone and brace it while the adhesive sets, which usually takes overnight before the sink can hold a full load of water. Then come the strainers and trap.

    Disposal and Dishwasher Hookup with Sink Installation in North Miami, FL

    Most new kitchen sinks get paired with a disposal and a dishwasher. We mount the disposal, run the dishwasher drain through an air gap or high loop and install a dedicated hot supply with its own shutoff. One visit costs less than three. Every connection is tested under real use.

    Restaurant Sink Installation in North Miami

    Restaurants, cafes and commissary kitchens in North Miami get their commercial sinks installed by HDF Plumbing. Hand sinks need tempered or hot and cold water. Three-compartment, prep and hand sinks go in with the floor sinks, indirect drains and grease trap tie-ins the inspector checks.

    Workstation Sink Installation in North Miami, FL

    Workstation sinks have a ledge for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks, and many are deep single bowls with the drain off center. We plan the trap around that offset drain so the disposal fits and the cabinet keeps usable space on one side. Check the faucet reach as well.

    Stainless Steel Sink Installation in North Miami

    Stainless sinks come in gauges from 16 to 22, and the lower the number, the thicker the steel. Thin 22 gauge bowls flex and drum when the disposal runs, so we add sound pads or suggest an 18 gauge or heavier sink if you have not bought one yet. Mounting style changes the plumbing. We connect the strainers, faucet and disposal either way.
