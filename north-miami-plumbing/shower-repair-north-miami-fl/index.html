    Low Shower Pressure Repair in North Miami, FL

    Low pressure at the shower with good pressure at the sink narrows it down fast. With good pressure elsewhere, the problem is in the head, the restrictor or the cartridge, and that is typically a short repair. We start with the head. Low pressure everywhere sends us to the pressure regulator and main valve instead.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in North Miami, FL

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open. The Moen 1222 runs the Posi-Temp line, and the Delta RP46074 runs the Monitor 13 and 14 series, and both wear out on a schedule you can almost set a calendar by. Both ride on our trucks. Original owners can often get the cartridge free under warranty.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in North Miami

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. If the head drips with the valve closed, water is slipping past the cartridge, and a new head will not change that. The fix is in the valve. Drips from the arm threads just need fresh tape and a snug turn.

    Mixing Valve Repair in North Miami

    Mixing valves blend hot and cold inside the wall. A sticking cartridge or spool shows up as temperature that drifts or a handle that has to go much farther than it used to. A new cartridge usually fixes it. If the valve is too old for parts, we talk through a replacement valve and what it means for the tile.

    Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in North Miami

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Mineral grit scores the sealing faces and flattens the O-rings until the cartridge can no longer stop the flow. We swap in a new one. We use genuine parts and coat the new one with silicone grease so the handle turns smoothly.

    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in North Miami, FL

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. That means a worn diverter steals pressure from the head, a clogged tub drain slows the shower and a leaky valve shows up at both the spout and the head. Each part gets tested. We also check the overflow gasket, a frequent hidden leak.

    Same-Day Shower Leak Repair in North Miami, FL

    Shut off the main first if water is getting where it should not. A sudden leak behind the wall or a valve that will not close can soak framing fast, so the main valve, usually near the meter or where the line enters the house, is your first stop. Call us next. We stop the leak, make the repair or a safe temporary fix and check for anything that needs drying.

    Shower Diverter Repair in North Miami, FL

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. When the gate in a lift-style spout wears or a three-handle diverter stem loses its washer, water keeps pouring from the spout and the shower gets weak. We replace the spout or stem. Spout replacements take about twenty minutes.
