    Whole-Home PEX Replacement in North Miami, FL

    PEX-A is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, scale resistant and easy to route. We use Uponor PEX-A with expansion fittings that do not restrict flow.     Some homeowners choose Type L copper for its long history and rigid feel. We solder with lead-free solder or use pressed fittings for faster installation. Copper costs more than PEX and takes a little longer to install. We help you weigh the options for your budget and water conditions.

    Polybutylene Identification and Inspection in North Miami

    Polybutylene is usually gray, though it can be white, blue or black, and is often stamped PB2110. You may see it at the water heater, under sinks or near the main shutoff. We inspect visible lines, check the stamps and trace runs through the attic. You get a clear answer and a written estimate for replacement.

    Manufactured Home Poly Replacement in North Miami, FL

    Manufactured homes from the poly era often have polybutylene lines in the floor or belly. We replace these lines with PEX sized for manufactured homes and secure them properly underneath. We also check heat tape and insulation on exposed lines where winter freezes occur.
