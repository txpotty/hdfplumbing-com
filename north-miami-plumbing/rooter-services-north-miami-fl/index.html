    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in North Miami

    Rooting a sink, tub or toilet typically runs $125 to $300. Main sewer rooting through a cleanout usually runs $200 to $500. With hydro jetting added, expect roughly $450 to $1,000, depending on how long the line is and how heavy the grease is. After-hours calls cost more.

    Rooting uses a spinning cable with cutting blades to break up clogs and roots. Jetting works another way. A jetter scours the walls with water at high pressure. Tough lines often get both, rooting to open the path and jetting to clean out what remains.

    Gurgling toilets, sewage odors and slow drains in several fixtures are the common signs. A tub that fills when you flush is a big one. Take that one seriously. Repeat clogs in one line usually mean a deeper problem, often roots at a joint well out in the yard.

    Chemical drain openers rarely clear a real clog and can eat at old pipe. Old cast iron and the thin brass traps found in North Miami's established homes suffer the worst of it. They also put our techs at risk. Standing water full of caustic chemicals splashes when we open the trap, so tell us if you poured anything in before we arrived.

    Snaking runs a spinning cable with a cutting head through the clog. A jetter uses water at thousands of psi to scour the inside of the pipe from wall to wall. Jetting cleans more. We usually jet recurring grease lines and scaled mains, and snake the one-off clogs.

    Clay or older cast iron lines with trees nearby usually need rooting every 12 to 24 months. Newer PVC lines may go many years without it. A camera helps set the schedule. Seeing how fast roots came back since the last cutting tells us whether to plan a yearly visit or stretch it to two years.

    Keep grease, coffee grounds and wipes out of drains. Hair strainers in tubs and showers help. Run hot water after using the disposal, and scrape plates into the trash before rinsing so less food goes down the kitchen line. Older lines with roots need scheduled rooting every 12 to 24 months.
