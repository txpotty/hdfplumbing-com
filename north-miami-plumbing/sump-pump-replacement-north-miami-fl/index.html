    Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in North Miami

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in North Miami, FL

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and often more long-lasting. We provide professional pedestal sump pump replacement in North Miami, FL, with proper alignment and secure mounting. This type is often preferred in shallower pits or where noise is not a primary concern, offering a robust solution for flood prevention.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in North Miami

    Submersible sump pumps are popular in North Miami for their quiet operation and ability to sit fully submerged in the sump pit. When replacing a submersible pump, we carefully remove the old unit, clean the pit and install a new, properly sized pump. HDF Plumbing can replace your existing combination system or install a new one, evaluating your needs for groundwater management.
