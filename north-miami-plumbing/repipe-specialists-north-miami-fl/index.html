    Understanding the cost of repiping is important for homeowners in North Miami, FL.     Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in North Miami

    Kitec, with its orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe and brass fittings, and gray polybutylene pipe are both known for failures. Many homes built in the 1980s through early 2000s contain one or the other. We replace these systems completely so your home is easier to insure and sell. Visible pipe at the water heater often tells us what you have.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in North Miami, FL

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and very durable piping material that's gaining popularity for repiping projects. HDF Plumbing specializes in PEX pipe replacement in North Miami, FL, offering a system that resists freezing, corrosion, and scale buildup. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points. PEX also noticeably dampens water hammer noise, providing a quieter plumbing system for your home.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in North Miami

    PEX-A tubing is flexible, resists scale buildup and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open up the full pipe diameter. Long continuous runs mean fewer joints in walls and attics. Most homes are repiped with PEX in two to three days.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in North Miami

    Copper piping offers excellent durability, corrosion resistance, and a long lifespan, making it a premium choice for many homeowners in North Miami. While the upfront cost is higher than PEX, copper provides superior rigidity and resistance to UV light if exposed. Our team carefully solders each joint, creating a leak-proof and robust plumbing system that can withstand the test of time, including changing temperatures and local water conditions. Copper also inherently inhibits bacterial growth, contributing to cleaner water.
