    Leak Repair with PEX in University, FL

    A single bad section is not always a reason to repipe. We can cut out a split or corroded stretch of copper, transition to PEX with a listed fitting, and have the water back on in an hour or two.     Emergency PEX Pipe Repair & Installation in University, FL

    PEX rarely fails on its own. Most failures trace back to a nail or screw through the pipe, a rodent working on an attic run, or a fitting that was not crimped fully. We carry the common sizes.     PEX for Water Line Replacement in University

    Your service line runs from the meter or well to the house, often a foot and a half to three feet underground.     PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in University, FL

    With PEX-A, the tech slips an expansion ring onto the pipe and stretches both with a battery tool. The fitting goes in while the plastic is still expanded, and the material's memory pulls it back down around the barb, sealing harder over the first minute. In the cold, it takes longer to shrink back. We let joints set before testing.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in University, FL

    Think of a manifold like an electrical panel, but for water lines. The trade-off is almost no fittings hidden in the walls and steadier pressure when two showers run at once.
