    Unclogging a drain does not need one. Bigger changes might. Replacing a stack, relocating a drain or replacing a section of the building drain in University may require a plumbing permit, depending on local rules and how much of the system is being changed. We pull it when one applies.

    We advise against them. Most only open a small hole. Harsh cleaners can soften old glue joints, eat at metal drain parts and sit in the trap if they do not clear the clog, which makes the job more dangerous for whoever opens the drain next. A drum auger works better.

    Hydro-jetting scours the pipe with water at several thousand psi. It cleans wall to wall. A snake only punches a hole through a clog, while a jetter removes grease, scale and fine roots from the full circumference of the pipe. We camera first on old or brittle pipe.

    Slow drains, gurgling and sewer smell are the big three warnings. Drips in the cabinet count too. A drain that clogs again a few weeks after it was cleared, or a stain on the ceiling below a tub or shower, means the pipe or a fitting needs repair rather than another cleaning. Catch it early.

    Most trap and fixture drain repairs take one to two hours. Hidden pipe adds time. Replacing drain pipe inside a wall or under a floor usually runs half a day or more with access setting the pace. The estimate includes the timeline.
