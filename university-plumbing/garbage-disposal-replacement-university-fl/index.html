    Frequently Asked Questions About Garbage Disposal Replacement in University

    A standard disposal replacement with the unit typically costs $275 to $650. Premium models like InSinkErator Evolution usually run $500 to $1,000 installed.

    A continuous feed disposal operates when you flip a wall switch, allowing you to add food waste while it's running. A batch feed disposal only operates when a special stopper is inserted into the drain, making it generally safer as it prevents accidental activation while hands are near the opening.

    While it's possible for a handy homeowner to replace a garbage disposal, it involves working with both electricity and plumbing, which can be hazardous if not done correctly. Improper installation can lead to leaks, electrical shorts, and voided warranties. For safety and peace of mind, hiring a professional plumber is the better choice.

    Most garbage disposal replacements can be completed within 1 to 2 hours. This timeframe includes disconnecting the old unit, installing the new one, and testing it carefully. More complex situations, such as significant plumbing modifications or electrical work, might extend the duration a bit.

    If your disposal is old, frequently leaking, or making grinding noises without grinding food, replacement is usually more cost-effective and provides a longer-lasting solution. Minor issues like a simple jam can often be repaired.

    New garbage disposals typically come with manufacturer warranties ranging from 1 to 10 years, depending on the model and brand. HDF Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation services for your peace of mind.

    Most disposals last 8 to 12 years. Hard water, heavy use and grease can shorten that life.

    To prolong the life of your new disposal, avoid putting fibrous foods like celery, corn husks, or onion skins down it. Also, do not dispose of grease, coffee grounds, or non-food items. Always run cold water before, during, and after use, and grind small amounts of citrus peels or ice cubes now and then to clean the unit.
