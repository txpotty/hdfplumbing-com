    Frequently Asked Questions About Clogged Drain Repair in University

    Most clogs cleared with a cable or auger cost $150 to $500. Bigger jobs cost more. Main sewer blockages that need jetting, or a repair dug from the yard because the pipe itself has failed, can run from $500 to $2,000 or more. We give you the number before starting.

    Most sink and tub clogs take one to two hours. Often less than that. Main line clogs that need a camera inspection and jetting may take three to four hours depending on the line. We give you a time estimate before we start.

    Call once the plunger has failed. Plunging harder seldom works. Other signs it is time are more than one drain backing up, backups in odd places and clogs that keep coming back. Those usually point past the trap.

    Clearing a clog does not need a permit. It is just maintenance. A permit may be needed if the fix turns into replacing pipe, excavating the yard or rerouting drain lines. We tell you in advance if that applies in University.

    Call when a plunger does not work. Odors are a sign too. Water backing up into other fixtures, or multiple slow drains usually mean a main line issue. Those need a bigger machine.

    Snaking uses a cable with a cutting or grabbing head. It breaks through the clog. Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scrub the pipe wall, which removes the grease, scale and roots that a cable leaves behind and often keeps a line clear far longer. We use both, depending on the pipe.
