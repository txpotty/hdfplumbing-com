    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in University

    Two independent checks and a relief valve make up an RPZ. If the zone between the checks loses its pressure margin, the relief valve opens, dumping water to the floor or a drain so nothing can pass upstream, which is why we never bury one in a pit in University.
