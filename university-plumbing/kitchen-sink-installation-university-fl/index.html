    Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in University

    Sitting on the counter by their rim, drop-in sinks are a natural fit for laminate, butcher block and tile. It is often the quickest swap. After checking the cutout, we seal the rim and draw the clips down evenly, and in older homes and rentals it is often a two-hour job.

    Undermount Sinks on Stone in University, FL

    Stone shops vary on how they mount undermounts. Some epoxy threaded studs into the stone, others use a rail system, and we work with whichever one your countertop shop prefers. Either way, the bowl gets braced. We do not connect the trap until the adhesive has cured.

    Granite and Quartz Composite Sinks in University

    Composite sinks are heavier than stainless and usually thicker at the drain, so standard strainers sometimes need a longer threaded body. Strainer fit gets checked first. If the included strainer is too short for the sink wall, we swap in one that fits rather than overtightening and risking a chip around the opening.

    Farmhouse Sink Installation in University, FL

    A fireclay apron sink sits on a frame inside the cabinet, not on the countertop. A lot of them drain toward the back, so the trap layout has to match.

    Stainless Steel Sink Installation in University

    Stainless sinks come in gauges from 16 to 22, and the lower the number, the thicker the steel. Thin 22 gauge bowls flex and drum when the disposal runs, so we add sound pads or suggest an 18 gauge or heavier sink if you have not bought one yet. Mounting style changes the plumbing. Either way, we hook up the faucet, strainers and disposal.

    Composite Sink Installation in University

    Granite and quartz composite sinks resist scratches on the surface but can chip at the drain hole if a strainer is overtightened. We tighten by hand first. Composite makers usually call for silicone instead of plumber's putty under the strainer, since some putties stain the material, and we follow that sheet.

    Sink and Disposal Installed Together in University, FL

    A new sink is the cheapest time to add a disposal. The disposal mounts to the new flange, the trap is set to its outlet height and the dishwasher plug gets knocked out for the hose. You also need power. We check for a switched outlet in the cabinet.
