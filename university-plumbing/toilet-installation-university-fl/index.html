    Types of Toilet Installation in University, FL

    Pressure-Assisted Toilet Installation in University

    Pressure-assisted toilets have a sealed vessel inside the tank that uses line pressure to push the flush. Below about 20 psi, they flush poorly. We check static pressure at a hose bib before recommending one, and we warn customers about the noise, since the flush is loud enough to hear across the house. They rarely clog.

    Bathroom Remodel Toilet Installation in University, FL

    New tile over old vinyl can raise a bathroom floor by half an inch or more. That leaves the old flange sitting low. Either an extender ring brings the flange up or a new flange goes in, and relocating a toilet on a slab means saw-cutting concrete to move the drain. Moving it on a wood floor is easier.

    Comfort-Height and ADA Toilets in University

    Aging in place usually starts in the bathroom. A grab bar and a taller bowl help a lot. While the wall is open, we add 2 by 6 blocking between the studs at grab bar height, so the bar can later be screwed into solid wood instead of drywall anchors that pull loose. We also center the bowl to ADA spacing.

    Standard Toilet Installation in University

    A standard floor-mount toilet is a two-hour job when the flange is sound. We shut off the stop, drain the tank and pull the old bowl. After scraping the old wax off, we check the flange for cracks, set new stainless bolts, and lower the new toilet straight down onto a fresh seal so it compresses evenly. The old one leaves with us.

    Smart Toilet and Bidet Seat Installation in University

    Bidet seats like the TOTO Washlet and Bio Bidet bolt onto most existing elongated bowls. Water comes from a T-valve at the fill line. Heated seats with a warm-air dryer need a nearby GFCI outlet, and when there is none we bring in an electrician first. Non-electric seats take about thirty minutes.

    Wall-Hung Carrier Systems in University

    In-wall carriers from Geberit and Grohe each mount a little differently, and the instructions matter. The frame height chart from the maker gets followed to the letter. Set the frame too low and the bowl ends up at child height, too high and nobody's feet touch the floor, and neither can be fixed once the wall is tiled. Measure twice here.

    Rough-In Toilet Installation in University, FL

    Rough-in is the plumbing that goes in before walls and floors close up. For a toilet that means a 3 inch drain with a vent, plus a cold supply stub. We set the drain 12 inches from the framed wall plus the thickness of drywall, so the finished rough-in lands right where the fixture expects it. Then the inspector sees it open.

    Urgent Toilet Replacement in University

Often the broken unit comes out and a 12 inch rough-in replacement goes in during the same trip, round or elongated. Close the stop until then.
