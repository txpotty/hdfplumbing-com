    What a Tank Service Visit Covers in Pine Hills, FL

    Anode Rod Replacement in Pine Hills, FL

    Water chemistry changes from one street to the next, and so does anode life. Most rods last three to five years. A softened water supply can chew through a magnesium rod in two. A new rod costs a fraction of a tank and often adds years.

    Expansion Tank Service in Pine Hills, FL

    A thermal expansion tank is the small steel tank on the cold line above the heater, and it gives heated water somewhere to go when a check valve or pressure reducer closes the system. Our tech checks the air charge with a tire gauge. The precharge needs to match static pressure in the house. A waterlogged expansion tank sounds solid when you knock on it, and it lets pressure spike every time the burner fires.

    Burner and Pilot Service in Pine Hills, FL

    On a gas tank, a dirty burner throws a lazy yellow flame and leaves soot on the flue baffle. The burner comes out and gets brushed and vacuumed. We also check the thermocouple or flame sensor, since a weak one lets the pilot go out every few days and leaves you resetting it on your knees. The goal is a steady blue flame.

    Rheem and Bradford White Tank Service in Pine Hills

    Rheem, Bradford White, AO Smith and State make most of the tanks in local garages, and each has quirks a tech should know before opening a drain valve. On some models the anode sits under a plastic top cap. Bradford White often uses a combo anode in the hot outlet nipple. We carry the right sockets for each brand.

    Standard Tank Flushing in Pine Hills

    Flushing is the one job every tank needs, gas or electric, new or old. Hard water makes it more urgent. We drain, partly refill and drain again until the hose runs clear, sometimes four or five rounds. Then we close the valve and check that it reseals without a drip.

    Electric Element Check in Pine Hills

    Electric tanks have two elements, usually 4,500 watts at 240 volts, and the lower one does most of the work and takes most of the scale. Each element gets an ohm reading. If the hot water quits halfway through a shower, the lower element is the first suspect. We replace it with the matching wattage and voltage.

    Anode Upgrades for Odor in Pine Hills, FL

    A rotten egg smell from the hot side only is almost always sulfate-reducing bacteria reacting with a magnesium anode. Swapping the rod usually fixes it. We put in an aluminum-zinc or powered anode and sanitize the tank. Powered anodes never wear out and draw very little electricity.

    Sediment Flush and Descaling in Pine Hills

    Calcium carbonate drops out of hard water every time the tank heats, and over a year it can harden into chunks the size of a thumbnail. That is what makes the popping noise. When a plain flush will not move them, we use a descaling solution rated for potable systems, let it sit and then flush it all out. A clean bottom puts less heat stress on the seam.
