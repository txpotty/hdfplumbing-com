    What We Fix on Faucets in Pine Hills, FL

    Compression Faucet Repair in Pine Hills

    Two-handle faucets with separate hot and cold handles are often compression faucets, which seal by pressing a rubber washer down onto a brass seat inside the valve. Washers harden and seats get pitted. We replace the washer and dress or replace the seat with a seat wrench, and the packing gets redone if the stem leaks.

    Kitchen Sprayer and Diverter Repair in Pine Hills, FL

    Spray heads on kitchen faucets get clogged with scale, and side sprayers lose pressure when the diverter inside the faucet body wears out. Diverters are cheap parts. Touchless faucets add a solenoid and a battery pack to the list, and we test those before replacing anything.

    Faucet Handle Repair and Replacement in Pine Hills, FL

    Stripped handle splines, cracked handle bodies, worn adapters and loose set screws make a faucet hard to turn or impossible to shut tight. Handles are often sold separately. We match the handle to the stem or cartridge and the finish, and if the stem itself is stripped we replace it at the same time.

    Hard Water Faucet Repair in Pine Hills

    Scale is the biggest reason faucet seals wear early, because the mineral crust scratches rubber and keeps ceramic discs from closing flat. Hard water speeds it up. We clean the valve body when the old part comes out, so the new cartridge or washer seals against a smooth surface.

    Emergency Faucet Repair in Pine Hills

    A faucet that will not shut off, or one with a cracked body spraying under the sink, needs the water off before anything else happens. Close the angle stops under the sink. If they will not turn, use the main shutoff for the house. We come fast for these.

    Cracked Faucet Body Repair in Pine Hills

    Cracked faucet bodies, usually from freezing or an old brass casting that finally gave out, cannot be repaired and need a replacement faucet. Turn off the stops under the sink. We can usually install a new faucet the same day, and we will check the supply lines and stops while the water is off.

    Bathroom Faucet Repair in Pine Hills, FL

    Bathroom sinks see less wear than kitchen sinks, but scale still builds up in cartridges, and pop-up drains break at the pivot rod under the sink. Neither one takes long. We match cartridges for widespread, centerset and single-hole faucets, and pop-up assemblies are replaced to fit the finish.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Pine Hills

    Ceramic disc faucets last a long time, but grit from a water main repair or scale from hard water can scratch the discs or wear out the rubber inlet seals underneath. Inlet seals cost very little. A scratched disc means a new cartridge, since the two discs are lapped to fit each other and cannot be resurfaced.
