    Batch Feed vs Continuous Feed in Pine Hills

    Continuous feed is the type most kitchens have. It runs while the switch is on. Batch feed only runs with its stopper turned in place, so nobody can reach into a running unit, which some families with small kids prefer and which needs no wall switch at all. Each type gets wired its own way.

    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Pine Hills

    An air switch puts a push button on the countertop or sink deck instead of a wall switch. The button pushes a puff of air down a thin tube. A control box under the sink reads that pulse and flips the power, keeping live wiring well away from the water. We drill the counter or use the spare faucet hole.

    Badger Series Installation in Pine Hills, FL

    The Badger line runs from the 1/3 HP Badger 1 up to the Badger 5XP. Every Badger shares one quick-lock mount. That shared mount is why a Badger-to-Badger swap is often a 45-minute job, since the upper ring and flange can stay put if they are not corroded. No cord comes in the box, so the cord kit is on us.

    Standard InSinkErator Installation in Pine Hills

    A standard install goes into a sink that already has a drain and power. The old disposal comes down, the flange gets scraped clean and a new one goes in on fresh putty. Then the mount ring goes on. We hang the unit, turn the lower ring until it locks and connect the discharge tube to the trap with a new slip-joint washer so nothing weeps. Power comes from a cord kit or the existing hardwire, checked for ground before the breaker goes back on.
