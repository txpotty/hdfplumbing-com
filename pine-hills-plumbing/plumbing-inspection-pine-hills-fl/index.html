    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. Our checks include the relief valve and its discharge line, the expansion tank and the drain pan.     Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Pine Hills, FL

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. We identify copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, polybutylene and Kitec, and we note the known failure patterns for each one.
