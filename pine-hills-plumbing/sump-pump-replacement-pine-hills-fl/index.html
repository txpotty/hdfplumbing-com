    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Replacement in Pine Hills

    Constant running, loud grinding, frequent cycling or failure to start are typical signs. Rust, age and a burning smell also point to trouble.

    A battery backup sump pump system is a secondary pump powered by a battery, designed to turn on automatically if your primary sump pump loses power during an outage or fails. This is important for maintaining flood protection during power outages caused by severe weather.

    A primary sump pump is your main defense against basement flooding, powered by your home's electricity. A backup sump pump, usually battery-powered or water-powered, is a secondary system that activates if the primary pump fails, loses power or cannot keep up with heavy water inflow. Many Pine Hills homes benefit from a backup system due to storm risks.

    It is a good idea to test your sump pump at least every three months, and especially before and after rainy seasons. To test, simply pour a bucket of water into the sump pit. The pump should activate, drain the water and then shut off automatically. If it does not, it is time for a professional inspection.

    To maintain your new sump pump, regularly check the pit for debris, test the pump monthly by pouring water into the pit, and make sure the discharge pipe is clear. An annual professional inspection by HDF Plumbing can also help extend its lifespan.
