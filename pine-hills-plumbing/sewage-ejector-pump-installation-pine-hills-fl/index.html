    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin & Pit Installation in Pine Hills

    Digging the pit means cutting the slab and excavating below it, deep enough to set the basin flush with the finished floor. Limestone bedrock and sinkhole activity in some areas can shift the ground and damage buried water and sewer lines. The basin sits on gravel with backfill around it so rising groundwater cannot float it, and new concrete seals the floor and locks it down. A floating basin breaks pipe.

    Basement Bathroom Ejector Systems in Pine Hills

    Basement bathrooms sit below the building sewer, so every fixture drains to a sealed pit instead of flowing by gravity. We cut the slab, trench the drain lines to the basin, set it, run a two-inch vent up through the house to the roof and pipe the discharge to the building drain with a check valve and shutoff. The slab gets repoured.

    Commercial Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Pine Hills, FL

    Businesses in Pine Hills, FL with restrooms, kitchens or break rooms below grade need ejector systems that keep up with heavier, steadier use than a house. Commercial setups often run duplex pumps with an alternating panel, so one pump can fail and the other keeps the restrooms open. Closed restrooms cost business. Each pump can carry the load alone.

    Zoeller Ejector Pump Installation in Pine Hills, FL

    Zoeller has made ejector pumps for decades, and its cast iron models are the ones we see still running in older basements. They work well for new systems and for drop-in replacements, since many Zoeller models fit existing pits and discharge piping as is. Parts are easy to find.

    Liberty Pumps Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Pine Hills, FL

    Liberty builds single and dual pump packages that arrive with the basin, pump, lid and fittings matched. Matched packages speed up the install, and Liberty's float design is less likely to snag on the basin wall, a common reason older pumps fail to start. Stuck floats are common.

    Ejector Pump Installation Cost Comparison in Pine Hills, FL

    Replacing a pump in an existing basin costs the least, often done in a few hours. A full new system with basin, floor excavation, roof vent, electrical and alarm costs more, but it is usually the only way to add a below-sewer bath besides a macerator. We price both options.

    Upflush and Macerating Toilet Systems in Pine Hills

    Macerating systems like Saniflo add a bath fixture or two without breaking the concrete floor. It sits behind the toilet, grinds waste and pumps it through a small line, often three-quarter or one inch, to a drain above, which suits a small half bath. Venting still applies.
