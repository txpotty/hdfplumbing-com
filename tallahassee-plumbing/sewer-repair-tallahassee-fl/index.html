    Hydro-jetting is a powerful cleaning method that uses high-pressure water streams to clear stubborn blockages, grease buildup and even tree roots from sewer lines.     Root Intrusion Removal in Tallahassee, FL

    Tree roots are a leading cause of sewer line blockages and damage, especially with the mature trees found in many Tallahassee neighborhoods. Roots can infiltrate tiny cracks, expand and eventually crush or clog pipes. We use specialized augers and hydro-jetting to cut through and remove roots, followed by inspection to assess pipe condition. For ongoing issues, we might recommend trenchless repair or pipe replacement to prevent future root problems.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Tallahassee

    Clay and cast iron sewer pipe crack from soil movement, heavy vehicles and simple age. A cracked section lets sewage leak into the soil and lets dirt fall into the pipe, which leads to recurring clogs. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded couplings rated for the transition. The repair is tested before we backfill so you know it holds.

    Pipe Burst & Collapse Repair in Tallahassee, FL

    A burst or collapsed sewer pipe can lead to serious damage and unsanitary conditions. These issues can result from shifting soil, corrosion of old cast iron or clay pipes, or heavy vehicle traffic above the line. HDF Plumbing offers emergency repair services to quickly address these critical failures.     Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Tallahassee

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg pipe, a tar-paper material that flattens and blisters over time.
