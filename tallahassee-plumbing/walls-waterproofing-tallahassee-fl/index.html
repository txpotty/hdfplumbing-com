    How We Keep Water Out of Walls in Tallahassee, FL

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Tallahassee

    A drip from a shower valve cartridge, a sweated copper stub or a loose drain slip nut can soak a wall cavity for months before paint bubbles. We find it with a moisture meter, a thermal camera that shows the cool wet spot, and a pressure test on the supply lines. Then we cut a clean access hole. The bad fitting or section of pipe gets replaced. We pull wet insulation and run fans until the cavity reads dry.

    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Tallahassee, FL

    Rubber washing machine hoses burst. We swap them for braided stainless hoses, put a drain pan under the washer piped to a floor drain where one is available, and install a quarter-turn shutoff or an automatic leak valve on the supply box. Water heaters get a pan too. Behind the heater we can install moisture-resistant board and seal it.

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Tallahassee

    Hairline cracks in poured concrete walls leak more water than their size suggests. We inject them with low-pressure polyurethane foam, which expands to fill the crack from the inside face to the soil side, or with epoxy when the crack also needs structural bonding. Horizontal cracks are different. A horizontal crack often signals soil pressure pushing the wall in, so we check for movement with a level before we seal anything. Most vertical cracks get sealed in one visit.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Tallahassee, FL

    Wet baseboards outside the shower or a stain on the ceiling below usually point to the pan. We plug the drain, fill the pan with an inch or two of water, and mark the level and wait 24 hours. A drop means a leak. Rebuilding means a mortar pre-slope of a quarter inch per foot, a new PVC liner or bonded membrane, and a two-piece clamping drain set on the liner. The new pan gets its own flood test before tile.

    Commercial Wall Waterproofing in Tallahassee

    Restaurant kitchens, gym locker rooms and salon wash stations put water on walls all day. We waterproof commercial showers, mop sink walls and restroom wet walls with membranes rated for constant water exposure, and we repair the supply and drain piping behind them when we find a leak during demo. After-hours scheduling is available.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Tallahassee, FL

    Houses built before modern drainage rules often have no footing drain at all, or a clay tile drain that collapsed decades ago. Many homes in Florida built before the mid 1970s have cast iron drains under the slab, which commonly crack and corrode with age. Fieldstone and old brick foundations need breathable products. A rigid coating on soft brick traps moisture and makes the face spall off over a few seasons, so we use lime-compatible or vapor-open coatings and pair them with a new interior drain. Old mortar joints get repointed first.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Tallahassee

    Bare dirt in a crawl space releases gallons of water vapor a day into the air under your floor. A 10 mil or heavier polyethylene barrier, overlapped a foot at every seam and taped, cuts that off and keeps the joists and subfloor dry. We run it up the foundation walls and hold it in place with termination strip. Cheap fix, big difference.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Tallahassee

    Tile and grout are not waterproof. Water passes through grout every time you shower, so the layer behind the tile, a membrane over cement board or a foam board system, is what actually keeps the studs dry. We tear out failed tile, install new backer, and apply Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban at the thickness the manufacturer calls for. Corners, niches and valve holes get extra fabric or preformed pieces.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Walls Waterproofing in Tallahassee

    Most jobs fall between $3,000 for interior crack sealing and $15,000 or more for exterior excavation with new footing drains. The size of the wall, how wet it gets, which method fits and how hard the wall is to get to all move the number. Access matters most. Nothing starts until you have the quote in writing.

    A shower wall job usually takes two to four days from demolition to finished membrane, including the drying time between coats. Exterior block sealing often wraps up in a day. Bigger foundation jobs take several days, sometimes more if it rains.

    It depends on the wall and the site. On exterior foundation work we use liquid polyurethane-modified asphalt, rubberized asphalt sheets, bentonite panels and crystalline coatings, picking the one that suits the soil and the wall material. Inside showers, it is Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban.

    Damp proofing is usually a thin spray-on asphalt coat. It slows soil moisture but will not stop water that is pushing against the wall under pressure. Waterproofing uses thicker membranes, drainage boards and footing drains rated to stop liquid water, and that is what a basement sitting in wet soil or a high water table calls for.

    Waterproofing takes away the moisture mold needs to grow. Existing mold should be removed and the area dried before any sealer goes on, and for larger growth, generally more than about ten square feet, we recommend a remediation company. Small patches we cut out and replace ourselves.

    Sprinklers, nine times out of ten. Spray heads and drip lines aimed at the house soak the block every day, and soil sloped toward the wall or a short downspout adds to it, so the masonry stays wet and leaves mineral deposits as it dries. Moving one sprinkler head can fix it.

    For big jobs, often yes. Excavation, structural changes and drainage changes can require a permit from the Tallahassee building department, so we check the rules before the quote goes out. We file the application when one is needed.

    Bubbling paint is a big one. Soft drywall near the floor, stained baseboards, a musty smell and white crust on block or stucco all point to moisture, and in a shower, loose tiles or cracked grout usually mean water is already behind the wall. We can confirm it with a meter in a few minutes.
