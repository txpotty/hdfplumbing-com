    Frequently Asked Questions About Underground Pipe Repair in Tallahassee

    A single repair on a buried water line typically costs $800 to $2,500 including locating and excavation. A new service runs higher. Main water service replacement typically runs $2,500 to $6,500, and sewer lateral repairs range from $1,500 to $5,000 depending on how deep the pipe sits. We write it up before digging.

    Less digging is the big one. The yard stays mostly intact. Trenchless methods leave driveways, patios and established landscaping alone, and the savings on putting the yard back often cover the higher method price. It is often faster.

    Wet spots or extra-green grass are the classic signs. So is a jump in the bill. A sudden drop in pressure, a sewage or sulfur smell in the yard, or gurgling drains can all point to a buried break in either the water service or the sewer lateral. A turning meter confirms a leak.

    Water lines get PEX or Type K copper. Both are burial rated. Sewer lines get Schedule 40 PVC, and the final choice depends on the existing pipe, the soil and what your local code allows. Couplings get sized to both pipes.

    Our labor warranty is written into the quote. Terms vary by repair. A spot repair and a full service replacement carry different coverage since each job uses different methods and parts. Manufacturer warranties stack on top.

    Main water service and sewer replacements usually do. Spot repairs vary. The Tallahassee building department typically wants a permit for any major buried line replacement, which allows an inspection before backfill. We handle the filing.

    Many utilities offer a one-time leak adjustment. Ask them about it. Most want proof that the leak was repaired promptly, and we give you an invoice and repair documentation that shows the date, location and fix. Rules differ by provider.

    A spot repair is usually a few hours. Pull-through work runs a day. A full sewer replacement with excavation can take two to five days, depending on length, depth, inspection timing and how much surface restoration the job needs. You get the timeline with the quote.
