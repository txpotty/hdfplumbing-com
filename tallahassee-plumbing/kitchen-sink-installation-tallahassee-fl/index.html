    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Tallahassee

    Undermount sinks rely on clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. The bowl is mounted, the rim sealed with silicone and the sink braced while the adhesive sets, normally overnight before it can take a full bowl of water. Strainers and traps go in after.

    Commercial Kitchen Sink Installation in Tallahassee, FL

    Three-compartment sinks, prep sinks and hand sinks carry separate health code requirements.     Stainless Steel Sink Installation in Tallahassee

    Stainless sinks come in gauges from 16 to 22, and the lower the number, the thicker the steel.     Undermount Sinks on Stone in Tallahassee, FL

    Stone shops vary on how they mount undermounts. Some epoxy threaded studs into the stone, others use a rail system, and we work with whichever one your countertop shop prefers. Either way, the bowl gets braced. We do not connect the trap until the adhesive has cured.
