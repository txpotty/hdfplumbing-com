    Sump Pump Installation in Tallahassee, FL

    A sump pump that fails during a storm usually failed months earlier: a stuck float, a seized impeller, a dead check valve or a cord unplugged for the shop vac.     Tankless Water Heater Installation in Tallahassee, FL

    Navien and Rinnai tankless units hang on a wall and free up the floor space a tank used to take. Most gas units need upsized gas piping and a new vent run in stainless or PVC. Electric models need amperage. Sizing is based on flow in gallons per minute and how far the incoming water has to be heated. Humidity causes condensation on cold lines and clogged AC condensate drains, a frequent source of hidden water damage. Every unit gets isolation valves so it can be flushed.

    PEX Repiping in Tallahassee

    Copper repipes cost more and take longer, which is why most whole-house repipes today use PEX-A or PEX-B tubing with brass or poly fittings. You also hear less of it, since PEX softens water hammer. Freeze damage is less likely too. Frozen pipes are rare in most of Florida, and many homes have outdoor plumbing that would never hold up in a northern winter. Fittings are matched to the tubing manufacturer.
