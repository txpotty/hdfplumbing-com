    Softener Loop Installation in Tallahassee, FL

    Softener loops are the pair of stubbed-out lines near the garage wall or water heater where a softener ties into the main. Homes without one get a loop cut in, with shutoffs and a bypass. Outdoor spigots stay on hard water. We run the drain to a standpipe or floor drain through an air gap.

    Upsizing a Whole-Home Softener in Tallahassee

    Undersized softeners are common in homes that added a bathroom or a couple of teenagers since the original system went in. A 32,000-grain unit that regenerated weekly for a family of three may be running dry every three days now. We size for the house you have today.

    Softener Failure Diagnosis in Tallahassee, FL

    A clogged injector, a dead motor or a wrong setting can make a good softener look finished. We check those first and test hardness before and after the unit, so you know whether it is softening at all. Obsolete valves are another story.

    Fleck Valve Programming in Tallahassee, FL

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. A Fleck needs hardness, household size and regen time entered right, otherwise it cycles too often or runs out of capacity. We set it before we leave.

    Salt-Free Alternatives in Tallahassee, FL

    Some homeowners do not want salt or have no drain near the main line. In those cases a TAC conditioner works, though we are upfront that it stops scale and does not make water feel soft. Sodium concerns are another reason.

    Salt-Based Softener Swaps in Tallahassee, FL

    Swapping a salt-based softener in Florida homes means disconnecting the old resin and brine tanks, cutting in new fittings if the loop is corroded, and setting the new valve. We pressurize and check every joint. We set an air gap on the drain line.

    Urgent Softener Problems in Tallahassee

    Not every softener failure is urgent. Water on the floor from the valve or an overflowing brine tank does count, and those calls get moved up. Bypass it until we arrive.

    TAC Salt-Free Conditioners in Tallahassee

    Template-assisted crystallization, or TAC, is the salt-free media with real independent test data behind it, and it is the only kind we install. It mounts on the main line after any filter and needs no electricity or drain. Magnets and clamp-on gadgets are a different story.
