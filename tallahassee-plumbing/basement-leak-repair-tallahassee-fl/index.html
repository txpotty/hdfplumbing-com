    Wall Leak Solutions in Tallahassee, FL

    Damp walls without a crack usually mean water moving through porous concrete or block. Tape foil to the wall for a full day, and if the room side is wet it is condensation, not a leak. Condensation needs a dehumidifier. Real seepage calls for better drainage or an interior channel.

    Foundation Crack Repair in Tallahassee

    Hairline cracks in a poured wall are normal and usually dry, but a crack that seeps after rain needs attention. Nearly all homes in Florida sit on concrete slabs over sandy soil, so leaks under the slab are a frequent and costly repair. Polyurethane injection from the inside fills a leaking crack all the way through and flexes with the wall. Most injections take an hour or two.

    Interior Drainage Systems in Tallahassee

    Where digging outside is not practical, an interior drain system does the same job from inside. A strip of slab comes out along the walls, perforated pipe goes in on stone, and it all drains to a sump. The wall gets a dimpled membrane. Seepage runs down behind it and into the pipe.

    Supply Line Leak Repair in Tallahassee

    Water lines along a basement ceiling tend to leak at fittings, valves and pinholes. Many wells in Florida produce water with a sulfur smell or high iron, which often calls for filtration to protect fixtures and appliances. Bad sections get cut out and replaced, and a run with repeated pinholes gets repiped rather than patched. Repiping a basement ceiling is usually a one-day job.

    Waterproofing for Older Florida Homes in Tallahassee, FL

    Older basements usually went in with block walls and little more than tar on the outside. Many homes in Florida built before the mid 1970s have cast iron drains under the slab, which commonly crack and corrode with age.     Water Damage Assessment in Tallahassee, FL

    Before any repair, we figure out where the water came from and how far it spread. We map the wet zone with a moisture meter and use a thermal camera to see behind finished walls. Guessing costs money. You get photos and readings for your insurance file.
