    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Tallahassee

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. An attic heater needs a pan with its own drain line, a properly routed relief line and enough access to get the old tank out and the new one in. Ceilings are at stake. If moving it makes sense, we can relocate the heater.

    Commercial Water Heater Installation in Tallahassee

    Restaurants, salons and offices burn through hot water at rates no household tank can keep up with. We install commercial tanks over 100,000 BTU and racked tankless systems, with the venting and gas supply those loads require. We schedule around your hours. Installs can happen after close.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Tallahassee

    Closed plumbing systems trap the extra volume water gains when it heats. Plumbing work in Florida follows the Florida Building Code, whose plumbing volume is based on the International Plumbing Code. A small expansion tank on the cold line absorbs that extra volume, which keeps the relief valve from weeping and saves wear on faucets and fittings. We pre-charge it to your house pressure. It mounts on the cold inlet.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Tallahassee, FL

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank. They need roughly 700 cubic feet of air around them and a condensate drain, so a warm garage or a large utility room usually works better than a closet. Rebates may apply. We will tell you if your space works.

    Gas Water Heater Installation in Tallahassee, FL

    Gas heaters recover faster than electric, often refilling a 50 gallon tank in under an hour. Our crews set the gas line, the drip leg and the vent connector, then do a draft test at the hood to prove the exhaust is leaving the house. Then we check for leaks. Expansion tanks go in where code requires.

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Tallahassee, FL

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match the voltage and breaker size to your panel, and because electric tanks need no vent, they can go in a closet, an attic or a laundry room where a gas unit could not. A drain pan goes under closet installs. Quiet and simple.

    Electric Water Heater Installation in Tallahassee, FL

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. We confirm the breaker and wire size match the new unit before connecting it, since many older homes have the wrong gauge. No venting needed. You get more spots to put it.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Tallahassee

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Every job gets a fresh connector, a drip leg, a shutoff and a vent connector with the right rise toward the chimney or flue. Then a draft test. Gas suits busy households.
