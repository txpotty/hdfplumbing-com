    Common repairs such as baffle replacement run $300 to $900, and effluent pump replacement typically costs $900 to $2,000. Leach field work can range from $5,000 to $20,000 or more. We provide a fixed price after inspection.

    To prevent future septic tank problems, limit water usage, avoid flushing non-biodegradable items, use septic-safe cleaning products, never pour grease down drains and schedule regular septic tank pumping every 3 to 5 years. Regular inspections can also catch issues early.

    For minor repairs like baffle replacement, a permit may not be required. Major repairs that involve excavation, major pipe replacement or any work on the drain field typically require permits from the local health department or building authority in Tallahassee, FL. HDF Plumbing will help you navigate the permitting process and make sure all work is compliant.

    HDF Plumbing services and repairs all major septic tank brands and types, including concrete, fiberglass and plastic tanks and components from manufacturers like Infiltrator Systems, Presby Environmental and Advanced Drainage Systems. Our technicians are experienced with many septic system designs, from conventional gravity systems to aerobic treatment units.

    Most additives are not needed for a healthy system. Regular pumping, water conservation and keeping grease and wipes out of drains do far more.

    Many households need pumping every three to five years, depending on tank size and household use. Regular pumping protects the leach field from buildup.
